A Gap Moment Codex concept: the integration of compassion as a computational element in clinical decision-making rather than as an afterthought or emotional overlay. Compassionate meta-logic operates at the level above clinical reasoning — it shapes which options are considered, which risks are weighted, and how uncertainty is held.

Synthesized from the intersection of Chodron's groundlessness, Davidson's contemplative neuroscience, and Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis. The claim: compassion is not soft — it is a higher-order logic that produces better clinical decisions under uncertainty because it expands the decision space rather than narrowing it.
