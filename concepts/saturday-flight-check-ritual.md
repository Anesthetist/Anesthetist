# Saturday Flight Check Ritual

A weekly pause-and-recalibrate practice. Half executive review, half founder morale touchstone. Grounded in three frameworks: The Gap and the Gain (Sullivan/Hardy), High Performance Habits (Burchard), and Operations (Delunna). Rituals over heroics.

## Structure (9 sections)

1. **The Airport** — Quarterly goals in one paragraph. If they don't exist, that IS the finding.
2. **The Gain** — Measure backward from a concrete prior state. What we had last Saturday vs today. Structural celebration, not performative.
3. **The Gap** — Honest delta from the ideal. Burchard's "raise necessity." The forward-looking version.
4. **Leading Edge Connections + HubSpot/Vault Gap Audit** — New people this week. Who's in HubSpot, who's not. Who has a vault note, who doesn't. Burchard's Influence habit, operationalized.
5. **Networking Check** — Who's gone quiet, who's due, who's warming.
6. **The Hard Thing** — Burchard's Courage habit. What Randy's been avoiding. Name it plainly, no softening.
7. **The One Thing** — Burchard's Productivity habit. One move this week, on the critical path.
8. **Energy Check** — Burchard's Energy habit. Sleep, breath, training, recovery. One sentence. The Pausality irony is intentional.
9. **The Mantra** — Rhythm over intensity. A compass for the week. Specific to this week's state, not a platitude.

## Framework Integration

- **Gap and the Gain:** Sections 2 and 3 are the backbone. Measure backward to stay sane on the long road.
- **High Performance Habits:** Each section maps to a Burchard habit — Clarity (Airport), Energy (Energy Check), Necessity (Gap), Productivity (One Thing), Influence (Connections), Courage (Hard Thing).
- **Operations (Delunna):** The ritual itself IS the infrastructure. Weekly cadence, every Saturday morning.

## Cadence
Weekly, Saturday morning. Automated as a scheduled task after the first manual run is approved.
