# Co-Regulation

The process by which one person's nervous system state influences and stabilizes another's — a bidirectional autonomic entrainment mediated by the social engagement system ([[polyvagal-theory]]). In clinical contexts, co-regulation means one calm clinician can shift the entire team's autonomic state.

## Definition

Co-regulation extends [[gap-moment-training]] from an individual practice to a team-level intervention. When a CRNA regulates their own nervous system, they simultaneously create a regulatory field that affects patients, colleagues, and the entire OR environment. The mechanism operates through:
- **Vocal prosody:** Calm voice tone activates vagal pathways in listeners
- **Facial expression:** Social engagement system reads safety cues from face
- **Respiratory entrainment:** Shared breathing synchronizes team autonomic rhythms
- **Mirror neuron activation:** Observing calm produces calm in the observer

## Clinical Application

The "Team Breath Before You Start" — one synchronized slow breath after surgical timeout — shifts the collective autonomic baseline. This is co-regulation operationalized as a clinical safety intervention.
