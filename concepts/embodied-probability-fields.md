A Gap Moment Codex concept: the practiced ability to hold multiple possible futures in somatic awareness simultaneously rather than collapsing prematurely to a single prediction. In clinical environments, this manifests as the anesthetist's capacity to feel the probability space of what might happen next — not as intellectual calculation but as embodied readiness across multiple contingencies.

Synthesized from the intersection of Damasio's somatic markers, Klein's recognition-primed decision making, and Taleb's non-ergodic risk frameworks during Randy's 40-book cross-domain synthesis.
