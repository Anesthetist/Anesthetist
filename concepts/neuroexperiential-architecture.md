A Gap Moment Codex concept: applying Christopher Alexander's pattern language and quality-without-a-name to the design of internal experiential states. Just as Alexander argued that certain spatial patterns reliably produce human flourishing, neuroexperiential architecture proposes that certain sequences of breath, attention, and sensory engagement reliably produce autonomic coherence.

Randy corrected ChatGPT's initial shallow reading of Alexander, insisting on deeper engagement with the idea that architectural "aliveness" has a direct neurophysiological correlate — the felt sense of a well-designed space maps to the felt sense of a well-regulated nervous system.
