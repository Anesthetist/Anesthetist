A unified metacognitive + metasomatic operating system for scaling a company. Combines Cedric Chin's Commoncog framework (cognitive operating principles for scaling) with Randy's somatic layer (the founder's nervous system as the ultimate governance instrument).

## Architecture

- **Metacognitive layer:** Deliberate practice, expertise acquisition, decision frameworks (Chin's Commoncog)
- **Metasomatic layer:** Interoceptive accuracy, autonomic regulation, embodied pattern recognition (Randy's extension)
- **Integration layer:** The founder's capacity to operate simultaneously from cognitive clarity and somatic awareness

## Scaling Implications

At each ARR stage ($0-100K, $100K-1M, $1M-10M), the founder faces different cognitive and somatic demands. The Neural Capital OS provides the operating system that adapts: learning flywheel at early stage, delegation and team regulation at growth stage, systems thinking and institutional coherence at scale.
