Wearable tactile feedback of physiological signals — using vibration, pressure, or temperature cues delivered through a wearable device to convey real-time autonomic state information to the user without requiring visual attention.

## Mechanism

Haptic biofeedback translates internal signals (heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate) into tactile patterns the user can feel. This creates an external interoceptive channel — particularly valuable in clinical environments where visual and auditory attention are consumed by patient care.

## Patent Landscape

Active patent risk from Phoeb-X (US 11,779,275 B2, active to 2042) covering multi-sensory wearable with interoceptive triggers. If SRL ships its own haptic band, FTO analysis required. Apple Watch haptic engine may provide a lower-risk delivery mechanism.

## Relevance to SRL

Haptic feedback is a potential modality for the ExterOryx Interface — providing the external sensory anchor that complements internal interoceptive focus during NeuroMinute delivery, especially in sterile clinical environments where audio is impractical.
