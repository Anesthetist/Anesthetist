Randy's description of his evening "brain exploding" experience — the integrative insight that occurs after intense daytime focused work. The cycle: concentrated domain immersion during the day → relaxation of executive control in the evening → spontaneous cross-domain synthesis → felt sense of cognitive expansion ("poof, exploding").

This is not mysticism — it maps to established neuroscience of the default mode network activating during rest periods after focused learning, producing novel associations. Randy's contribution is recognizing this as a reliable, trainable cycle rather than a random occurrence, and connecting it to Springett's consciousness development framework.
