The credential architecture for Somnistics certification. SCP demonstrates competence in detecting Gap Moments, selecting and executing NeuroMinute protocols with high fidelity, and producing measurable state deltas while maintaining task readiness and safety.

## Performance Tiers

- **Bronze** — Demonstrates basic gap moment detection and single-protocol execution
- **Silver** — Demonstrates multi-protocol selection and measurable state delta production
- **Gold** — Demonstrates return-to-task readiness under simulated clinical load
- **Platinum** — Demonstrates mastery across all five SRB-60 competency domains with consistent biometric evidence

## Advanced Credentials

- **SCI (Somnistics Certified Instructor)** — Qualified to teach Somnistics protocols and administer SRB-60
- **SCE (Somnistics Certified Examiner)** — Qualified to evaluate SRB-60 performance and certify SCPs
