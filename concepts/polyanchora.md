# Polyanchora™

A multi-anchor attentional scaffolding system that guides dynamic, rapid attention switching under pressure. PolyAnchora couples multiple sensory anchors simultaneously to strengthen interoceptive-exteroceptive awareness within 60-second [[neurominute]] windows.

## Definition

PolyAnchora™ Transitions trains fluid attentional shifting between up to seven interoceptive and exteroceptive targets within a single 60-second practice. Where [[anterocept]] provides the progressive spectrum of sensory focus, PolyAnchora provides the scaffolding for *simultaneous multi-anchor engagement* — the ability to hold and switch between multiple sensory streams under time pressure.

## Multi-Sensory Stack

- **Breath** (interoceptive anchor) — diaphragmatic awareness, rhythm
- **Haptic metronome** (somatosensory anchor) — Watch taps synchronized to breath pacing
- **Ambient spatial tone** (auditory anchor) — soundscape cues for exteroceptive awareness
- **Heartbeat** (cardiac interoceptive anchor) — pulse awareness
- **Muscle tension** (proprioceptive anchor) — postural and tension pattern awareness
- **Emotional valence** (affective anchor) — gratitude, curiosity, or calm as intentional state
- **Visual field** (exteroceptive anchor) — soft gaze or focal point awareness

## Neurophysiological Mechanism

Multi-sensory attention recruits overlapping fronto-parietal hubs, raising cortical phase-synchrony beyond single-modality practice. Cross-modal synchronization enhances working-memory capacity and attentional flexibility — the same neural infrastructure that CRNAs rely on during complex procedures.

## Clinical Application for CRNAs

- Faster lock-in on airway landmarks under OR noise and time pressure
- Fewer fixation errors through trained attentional flexibility
- Enhanced working-memory capacity via cross-modal synchronization
- Users report easier "lock-in" states under clinical pressure after PolyAnchora training
