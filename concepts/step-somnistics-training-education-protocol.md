# S.T.E.P.: Somnistics Training and Education Protocol

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Pre-publication draft (March 2026)
**Coined:** Vigil coordination session, March 22, 2026 (wargame brief → publication strategy)

## Definition

S.T.E.P. is the publishable umbrella name for the Somnistics clinical standard framework. It packages three existing canonical SRL concepts into a single citable, certifiable, and externally-facing standard:

1. **Gap Moment Training™** — the 60-second workflow-embedded intervention layer
2. **Neuro-Ouroboros™** — the 21-day progressive curriculum engine
3. **Somnistics Certification** — the three-tiered professional credential (SCP-Foundation, SCP-Practitioner, SCP-Instructor)

## Strategic Purpose

S.T.E.P. is Pausality's answer to the Mark Leonard / Veeva standardization moat. The company that defines the clinical standard for perioperative ANS regulation before a Tier 4 platform (Epic, Apple, Google) does, wins the compliance moat. Competitors must certify against S.T.E.P.'s definition of competent, not their own.
