An adaptive 5-breath AI-guided protocol designed to execute a complete autonomic state reset within 60 seconds — the NeuroMinute design space. This is the core patentable novelty claim in SRL's provisional patent application.

## Protocol Architecture

Each of the five breaths serves a distinct neurophysiological function:

- **Breath 1:** Orientation — initial vagal brake engagement, baseline HRV capture
- **Breaths 2-4:** Neuro-Ouroboros loop — recursive interoceptive coupling where each breath's biometric delta informs the next breath's parameters (pace, depth, ratio)
- **Breath 5:** Return-to-task — state consolidation and executive function re-engagement

The AI layer dynamically discovers the user's personal resonant breathing frequency rather than prescribing a fixed pace, adjusting parameters in real-time based on cardiac-autonomic feedback.

## MVP Articulation (Randy's Words)

"A fixed set of recorded layers created in a step-wise progressive fashion in 1 minute biofeedback enabled sessions (repeated 5x/day) for a 21 day sequence in 5 breaths to reembody humanity one breath at a time while improving comfort doing uncomfortable high performance mental and physical tasks, while decreasing the risk of severe mental harm from on the job experiences."

This is the clearest statement of SRL's MVP and mission. The protocol is:
- **Fixed** in early MVP (recorded layers, not yet fully adaptive)
- **Progressive** — 21-day curriculum with increasing depth
- **Frequent** — 5 repetitions per day for habituation
- **Brief** — 1 minute; fits within any workflow gap moment
- **Population-specific** — Frontline medical workers as primary cohort; universalizable

## Patent Significance

The Five-Breath protocol is the specific procedural implementation that differentiates SRL's system from prior art. The combination of (a) adaptive breath pacing, (b) closed-loop biometric feedback, (c) the Neuro-Ouroboros recursive architecture, and (d) the 60-second constraint constitutes the novel method claim.
