Randy's framework for applying exercise physiology principles (progressive overload, periodization, stress inoculation) to awareness and metacognition training. Just as muscular strength requires systematically increasing load, [[embodied-metacognition|embodied metacognition]] requires progressively increasing the complexity, frequency, and difficulty of attentional challenges.

Neither the contemplative traditions (which generally emphasize effortless practice) nor exercise science (which addresses physical capacities) combine these domains. Randy brings the CRNA's operational mindset: train under load so performance under pressure is automatic.

## The 21-Day NeuroMinute Arc

The exemplar implementation:
- **Wave 1 — Somatic Baseline**: Simple breath anchors, body scanning
- **Wave 2 — Cognitive Friction**: Thought labeling, attention toggling, interrupt drills
- **Wave 3 — Nondual Resilience**: Open awareness under mild stressors (CO2 buildup, cold)

## Five Intensification Levers

1. **Frequency**: Up to 4x/day
2. **Complexity**: Stacked sensory anchors
3. **Stressors**: Physiological challenge during practice (CO2 load, cold, interruption)
4. **Compression**: Less instruction, more raw awareness
5. **Integration**: 5-word post-session reflection
