# Clinician Durability

The capacity of a high-stakes clinician to sustain effective performance, emotional regulation, and professional engagement across a multi-decade career — without the progressive erosion of bandwidth, empathy, and clinical judgment that characterizes burnout.

## Definition

Clinician durability reframes burnout prevention from "managing stress events" to "maintaining the physiological infrastructure that enables sustained performance." The durable clinician is not someone who avoids stress but someone whose autonomic regulation capacity ([[vagal-tone]]) recovers between stressors, preventing the compounding effect of [[bandwidth-saturation-micro-events]].

## Key Determinants

- **Autonomic flexibility:** High vagal tone enables rapid recovery between cases
- **Interoceptive awareness:** Detecting one's own state drift before it causes errors
- **Sleep quality:** The foundation layer that all other durability factors rest on
- **Meaning/purpose:** Coherence with vocation prevents moral injury
- **Co-regulation capacity:** Team-level nervous system support

## Business Case

- CRNA turnover replacement cost: $80K-$150K per person
- Over 60% of healthcare workers report burnout symptoms post-pandemic
- A $15K pilot demonstrating retention improvement pays for itself 5-10x
- Framing: burnout prevention is a retention/safety ROI, not a wellness expense

## Evidence Base

- [[bandwidth-saturation-micro-events]] — cumulative micro-events as the real burnout mechanism
- [[error-archetypes-qrc]] — state drift causes errors, which causes moral injury, which accelerates burnout
- [[sleep-debt-clinical-performance]] — sleep quality as foundation
- [[ehmann-2025-mental-health-advanced-meditators]] — advanced meditators report resolution of anxiety, PTSD, addiction, and burnout symptoms; validates that sustained contemplative practice builds the autonomic infrastructure underlying career durability
- [[rayani-2025-brain-stimulation-mindfulness-ptsd]] — rTMS enhances dispositional mindfulness in PTSD patients; demonstrates that autonomic regulation capacity is augmentable in trauma-exposed populations, supporting the durability-as-infrastructure thesis
