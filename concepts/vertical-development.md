The meta-framework encompassing Kegan's developmental stages, Spiral Dynamics, and Wilber's AQAL quadrants — models that describe growth in the complexity of meaning-making, not just the accumulation of skills. Randy explored these frameworks to assess his own developmental position and to map how Somnistics relates to different levels of consciousness development.

## Relevance to SRL

Somnistics can be understood as a vertical development tool: it doesn't just teach breathing techniques (horizontal skill acquisition), it trains the capacity to observe one's own autonomic state from a meta-perspective (vertical development of self-awareness). The progression from "following a breathing protocol" to "detecting gap moments" to "holding embodied probability fields" mirrors vertical developmental stages.
