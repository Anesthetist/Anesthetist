# TransMetachora™

A three-stage cultural priming and onboarding system that reduces novelty-related cognitive load via predictive coding, then bridges into somnistics entrainment practices. Designed for cross-cultural deployment — ensuring the training works across different cultural backgrounds without triggering resistance.

## Three-Stage Priming Funnel

1. **Cultural Priming** — Localized soundscaping (traditional instruments + nature sounds + binaural beats) activates amygdala + hippocampus for emotional memory resonance. Cultural familiarity creates a "safe entry" via Porges' neuroception of safety.

2. **Sensory Alignment** — Superior colliculus engagement for multisensory integration. Synesthetic priming bridges the cultural entry into somatic awareness.

3. **Guided Transition** — Smooth handoff from culturally grounded safety into the universal somnistics training framework (breath, interoception, autonomic regulation).

## Why Cultural Priming Matters

- Novel interventions trigger threat detection (amygdala activation) in unfamiliar cultural contexts
- Cultural priming reduces this novelty response via predictive coding — the brain recognizes familiar elements and relaxes threat monitoring
- Once the threat system is quiet, the ventral vagal pathway opens for training

## Ecosystem Position

TransMetachora is the "front door" of the somnistics system — it's what meets the user first. The system maps all seven frameworks into a unified dependency chain: TransMetachora → NeuroHarmonics → ExterOryx/PolyAnchora/Multi-Phase Interoceptive Coupling.
