## Definition

The **NeuroEpistemic Layer™** is the biometric and sensory data architecture within the Somnistics framework that functions as a *governance layer of self-knowing*. It defines how an individual's nervous system knows what it knows — establishing baseline awareness and meaning construction from moment to moment.

## Core Components

The NeuroEpistemic Layer is constituted by:
- **HRV** (heart rate variability) indices — reflecting autonomic balance
- **CO₂ tolerance and end-tidal CO₂** — reflecting respiratory chemosensitivity
- **Vagal tone indices** — reflecting parasympathetic regulation capacity
- **Interoceptive tagging** — subjective body-sense data mapped against biometric signals

## Function in the Somnistics System

The NeuroEpistemic Layer serves as the *epistemic substrate* of the Somnistics ontology — the layer that determines what is *thinkable* or *feelable* in a given clinical moment. Rather than administering behavior from the outside, this layer shapes how perception, interoception, and meaning emerge within the individual sensorium.

This reframes biometric data: HRV, CO₂, and vagal indices are not merely physiological metrics — they are an **epistemic infrastructure**, governing how a clinician constructs situational reality in real time.

## Relationship to Governance

Mapped from Indy Johar's concept of "epistemic and perceptual infrastructure," the NeuroEpistemic Layer positions Somnistics as a **nervous system governance technology** — shifting the governance model from:
- *Deciding → Sensing*
- *Control → Coherence*
- *Organization → Organism*

## Clinical Significance

In the OR context, a CRNA's NeuroEpistemic Layer determines whether they are in a state that allows for fine-grained discrimination of patient cues, intuitive response calibration, and presence-based care — or whether their sensing capacity has been narrowed by sympathetic dominance and autonomic noise.

Training the NeuroEpistemic Layer means training the *conditions for knowing* — not just the content of knowledge.
