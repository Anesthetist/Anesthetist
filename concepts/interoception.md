# Interoception

The sense of the internal state of the body — awareness of heartbeat, breathing, hunger, temperature, visceral signals. Involves the anterior insula, anterior cingulate cortex, somatosensory cortices, and brainstem nuclei (NTS, PAG). Interoceptive awareness is foundational to [[gap-moment-training]]: the 30-second body scan that opens each practice resets the interoceptive signal-to-noise ratio.

## Why Missing from Traditional Medical Textbooks

- Harder to measure than observable physiology
- Field boundary problem: sits between neurology, psychology, philosophy
- Lacks clinical biomarkers until recent neuroimaging revealed insular cortex activation patterns
- Only ~15-20 years of serious research focus

## Neural Architecture

- **Posterior insula:** Raw sensory input — heartbeat, breath, gut signals
- **Anterior insula:** Complex integration of interoceptive signals with emotional and cognitive states
- **Anterior cingulate cortex:** Error monitoring and salience detection for internal signals
- **Brainstem nuclei (NTS, PAG):** Autonomic input relay

## Measurement

- **MAIA-2** (Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness) — validated self-report instrument
- **Heartbeat detection tasks** (Schandry Task) — objective accuracy measure
- **HRV coherence** — indirect measure of interoceptive-autonomic integration

## Clinical Relevance

- Linked to anesthesia awareness, panic disorder, sleep disorders, IBS, autism, alexithymia
- Interoceptive accuracy predicts emotional regulation capacity
- Training via breath awareness strengthens insula-related pathways
- The "body scan" in first 30 seconds of each [[neurominute]] resets interoceptive signal-to-noise ratio

## Evidence Base

- [[porges-2011-polyvagal-theory]] — neuroception is a form of interoceptive processing
- [[van-der-kolk-2014-body-keeps-score]] — trauma disrupts interoception; body-based interventions restore it
- Insular cortex research on posterior/anterior functional distinction
- MAIA-2 validation studies

## Clinical Observations

- CRNAs with higher interoceptive awareness show better clinical decision-making under pressure
- Interoceptive training is the foundation of the [[anterocept]] spectrum — you cannot progress to multi-modal awareness without first establishing basic body signal awareness
