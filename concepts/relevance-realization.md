# Relevance Realization

John Vervaeke's cognitive science framework describing how agents determine what is relevant in a given context — the pre-conscious, pre-propositional process by which the brain dynamically frames problems, allocates attention, and constructs meaning. In somnistics, relevance realization is the cognitive machinery that [[gap-moment-training]] trains.

## Definition

Relevance realization is not a single process but an ecology of processes (opponent processing, attentional scaling, participatory knowing) that dynamically determine what matters in a given situation. It underlies pattern recognition, clinical intuition, and the ability to notice when a state transition is needed.

## Somnistics Connection

- **Gap Moment detection** requires relevance realization: the ability to notice that *this transition moment* is salient and trainable
- **Clinical intuition** is relevance realization in action: the experienced CRNA who "just knows" something is wrong before the monitors confirm it
- **[[anterocept]] training** sharpens the interoceptive component of relevance realization — making internal signals more available to the salience detection system
- **[[hemispheric-rebalancing]]** supports relevance realization by restoring right-hemisphere broad awareness (the "master" that sees the whole context)

## Evidence Base

- [[vervaeke-relevance-realization]] — Vervaeke's academic work on the cognitive science of relevance, wisdom, and meaning-making
- [[treves-2024-mindful-brain-neural-correlates]] — decreased amygdala reactivity and altered DMN connectivity with greater trait mindfulness reflect enhanced relevance detection: less noise, more signal
- [[sezer-2022-resting-state-fmri-mindfulness]] — salience network and frontoparietal network FC changes during mindfulness map directly onto the attention control and self-awareness components of relevance realization
- Salience network neuroscience (anterior insula + ACC as the relevance detection hub)

## Clinical Observations

- CRNAs with high interoceptive awareness show better clinical relevance detection — they notice subtle changes in patient state that others miss
- Relevance realization can be impaired by sympathetic overactivation (tunnel vision) or dorsal vagal shutdown (dissociation) — another reason autonomic regulation is foundational
