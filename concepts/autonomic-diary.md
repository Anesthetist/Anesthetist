# Autonomic Diary

The narrative layer that gives [[gap-moment-training]] cumulative meaning over time.

Without it, Gap Moment data is quantitative: *"I caught my dysregulation 12 times today."* With it, the same data becomes a story: *"I'm building a different relationship with my own arousal patterns — and here's what that's making possible."*

That shift — from tracking to meaning-making — is what ties existential resilience to eudaimonic orientation. It's what makes Pausality a life practice, not a performance tool.

## Three Layers It Bridges

1. **Biometric** — HRV, respiratory rate, breath-hold duration, heart rate
2. **Behavioral** — when gap moments happened, how long, what came before and after
3. **Narrative** — the clinician's own meaning-making about their autonomic patterns over weeks and months

The narrative layer is what most biofeedback tools omit entirely.

## Design Principles

- **Voice-first** — dictation in the 60-second gaps between cases, not a form to fill out at home
- **Non-extractive** — private to the clinician; never surfaced to group leads, administrators, or anyone else
- **Longitudinal** — value compounds over time; the unit of insight is the pattern, not the event
- **Not a check-in survey** — feels like a clinical log, not a wellness app

## Anti-Fragility Mechanism

The Autonomic Diary is how Randy's observation becomes actionable data:

> "I hold my breath 8 seconds on routine cases, 15 seconds on the kid with touching tonsils, and only 4 seconds when I'm already dysregulated from the previous case."

Logged over time, that becomes the clinician's personal autonomic map — the foundation for genuine anti-fragility. Paired with [[awe-index]], it also captures when the nervous system opened rather than armored.
