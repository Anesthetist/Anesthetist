A Gap Moment Codex concept synthesized from Goldratt's Theory of Constraints, Rando's grief processing phases, and Carse's infinite game framework. The loop: identify the constraint (where grief is stuck in the body), release it through targeted breath/attention protocol, allow the grief to move to its next phase, then re-engage the infinite game of living.

This is not grief therapy — it is a somatic protocol for clinicians who encounter death and loss regularly and need to process enough to return to task without suppression or overwhelm.
