# NeuroMinutes for Meta Ray-Ban Glasses (Vagalbeats)

A Somnistics product concept: Meta's Ray-Ban smart glasses become a passive stress-detection and active regulation tool. Codename: **Vagalbeats**. Currently parked (Feb 2026).

## The Experience

> *The user feels overwhelmed. They ask for a NeuroMinute. The glasses and audio guide them — visually and sonically — to a place of safety. We use the sensors to verify they are safe (Heart Rate down, Posture up), not just to track data.*

Verification, not tracking. The system confirms safety was reached — it doesn't just report numbers.

## Why These Glasses Specifically

- **Always-on IMU** detects postural changes (slumping, "tech neck") passively — no user action required
- **Always-present audio** (open-ear speakers) delivers binaural beats without requiring separate earbuds
- **No screen interaction** — the intervention happens through body and sound, not attention
- **Socially invisible** — unlike a watch tap or AirPod, glasses don't signal "I'm doing wellness right now"

Translated through the [[language-of-the-nurse]]:

| Tech | What It Feels Like |
|------|-------------------|
| IMU Posture Gating | A gentle hand on your shoulder |
| Binaural Beats | A lullaby for your nervous system |
| NeuroMinute Protocol | The 60-Second Reset — we hold the space |

## Partnership Strategy

Outreach to Heidi in Meta Reality Labs / Developer Relations. Pitch frame: *turning the glasses into a tool for resilience and public health*. Subject line used: *"A digital 'hand on the shoulder' for the Meta Glasses?"*

Avoid: specs, feature lists. Lead with human problem.

## Design Principle

No cognitive overhead. The anti-screen intervention — delivered through posture and sound, requiring zero deliberate attention. Built for exactly the population Somnistics serves: high-demand professionals who cannot spare a moment of attention for self-care mid-shift.
