# SRL Knowledge Gap Analysis Framework

## Purpose

The most valuable function of an evidence infrastructure isn't showing what you know — it's showing what you *don't* know, and what to do about it. This framework systematically identifies the weakest links in SRL's evidence chains and converts them into prioritized research actions.

---

## Gap Type 1: Concept × Evidence Grade

**Question:** Which SRL concepts lack high-quality evidence?

| Concept | Highest Evidence Grade | Evidence Count | Gap Severity |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|-------------|
| HRV Biofeedback | 1a (meta-analysis) | 20+ | None — strong |
| Resonant Breathing Frequency | 1b (RCTs) | 10+ | Low — solid |
| Interoception (general) | 1a | 15+ | None — strong |
| Polyvagal Theory | 1b* | 10+ | Medium — *asterisk due to active debate |
| Co-Regulation | 2b | 5+ | Medium — needs RCT |
| Clinician Durability | 1b (components) | 8+ | Medium — integrated framework untested |
| Gap Moment Training | 5a (459-CRNA survey) | 3 | **HIGH** — core commercial concept lacks controlled study |
| NeuroMinute | SRL-P (field data) | 2 | **HIGH** — product concept needs formal evidence |
| Anterocept | 5b (theoretical) | 1 | **HIGH** — novel concept needs empirical validation |
| NeuroHarmonics | 5b (framework) | 2 | **HIGH** — component evidence exists but integration untested |
| Neurogating | 5b (mechanistic) | 1 | Medium — indirect evidence via sensory gating literature |
| Cognitive Variability Analysis | 2b | 3 | Medium — measurement approach needs larger N |
| SomnoAffinity | 5b | 1 | **HIGH** — novel concept needs operationalization |
| Diaphragmatic Blindness | SRL-P | 1 | **CRITICAL** — powerful finding, zero published evidence |

### Priority Actions (Concept × Evidence)

1. **URGENT: Diaphragmatic Blindness** → Design formal assessment study (n≥100 CRNAs); submit to VCU IRB. This is SRL's most vivid, marketable finding and it has zero published support. One study changes this from anecdote to evidence.

2. **URGENT: Gap Moment Training** → The VCU study is the pathway. A pre/post or waitlist-controlled design measuring HRV, self-reported regulation, and cognitive performance in CRNAs using GMT vs. control. This elevates GMT from KRL 5 to KRL 6.

3. **HIGH: NeuroMinute** → Embed as a measured intervention within the VCU study. Pre/post RMSSD measurements around individual NeuroMinute sessions provide dose-response data.

4. **HIGH: Anterocept** → Partner with a lab that does heartbeat evoked potentials (HEP) research. The Nath 2025 paper on HEP and meditation is the closest existing paradigm. Design a study comparing interoceptive prediction accuracy in trained vs. untrained clinicians.

## Gap Type 2: Concept × Population

**Question:** Which concepts are validated only for one population but claimed for others?

| Concept | Validated Populations | Claimed/Target Populations | Gap |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------------|-----|
| Gap Moment Training | CRNA (survey) | Nurses, surgeons, executives, military, families | **WIDE** — validated for CRNAs only |
| HRV Biofeedback | General adult, cardiac patients, substance use | CRNAs, executives, athletes | **Moderate** — mechanism is population-general but SRL-specific data is thin |
| Clinician Durability | Nurses (RCTs exist), physicians (surveys) | CRNAs specifically | **Moderate** — CRNA-specific durability data limited |
| NeuroMinute | CRNAs (field test) | Everyone | **WIDE** — one population |
| Co-Regulation | Mother-infant (2026 paper), therapy dyads | OR teams, corporate teams | **Moderate** — mechanism validated but team context untested |

### Priority Actions (Concept × Population)

1. **Expand GMT validation to at least one non-CRNA population.** Law students (BLIP clinic) or paramedics (Little 2026 breathwork RCT population) are fastest paths.

2. **Collect CRNA-specific HRV biofeedback data.** The general HRV literature is strong but SRL needs CRNA-specific outcomes for AANA CE and enterprise healthcare sales.

3. **Design a corporate/executive pilot.** Even n=20 would provide initial population validation. Pre/post HRV + cognitive performance + self-report regulation measures.

## Gap Type 3: Evidence × Freshness

**Question:** Which evidence chains rely on aging or historical studies?

| Concept | Oldest Key Evidence | Freshest Key Evidence | Freshness Risk |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------------|---------------|
| Polyvagal Theory | Porges 2011 | Grossman 2026 | Low (actively debated) |
| Interoception | Craig 2002, Craig 2009 | NIH Atlas 2025, PLOS 4M 2025 | Low (refreshed) |
| Somatic Marker Hypothesis | Damasio 1996 | — | **HIGH** — foundational but 30 years old; check for updates |
| HRV Biofeedback | Lehrer 2000 (resonance frequency) | JAMA 2025, meta-analysis 2026 | Low (continuously refreshed) |
| Self-Remembering | Gurdjieff (early 1900s) | — | **HIGH** — philosophical source; needs modern neuroscience bridge |
| Allostatic Load | McEwan 2007 | — | **Medium** — 19 years old; check for updated models |
| Body Keeps the Score | Van der Kolk 2014 | — | **Medium** — popular but need to track academic reception |

### Priority Actions (Freshness)

1. **Self-Remembering:** Search for 2020-2026 neuroscience literature on meta-awareness, reflexive consciousness, or dual attention. Bridge the Gurdjieffian concept to contemporary research.

2. **Somatic Marker Hypothesis:** Search for recent critiques or extensions of Damasio's framework. This is foundational to SRL's interoceptive approach and needs a current evidence refresh.

3. **Allostatic Load:** McEwan's model is widely cited but the field has evolved. Search for 2020+ updates on allostatic load measurement and interventions.

## Gap Type 4: Audience × Evidence Density

**Question:** Which target audiences have thin evidence support for SRL's value proposition?

| Audience | Evidence Notes Directly Relevant | Key Gaps |
|----------|-------------------------------|----------|
| CRNA | 30+ | Best-supported; still needs GMT RCT |
| Nurse (non-CRNA) | 10+ | Moderate; burnout RCTs exist but SRL-specific data thin |
| Surgeon | 5+ | Surgical stress HRV data exists; SRL application untested |
| Executive | 2 | **CRITICAL** — almost no evidence for corporate population |
| Military | 0 | **CRITICAL** — zero SRL-relevant military evidence collected |
| Athlete | 2 | **HIGH** — HRV biofeedback used in sports but SRL framework untested |
| Family/Consumer | 1 | **CRITICAL** — NeuroMinute Family Deck concept has no supporting data |
| Adolescent | 0 | **CRITICAL** — zero evidence; family product implies teen use |

### Priority Actions (Audience × Evidence)

1. **Executive population pilot.** Even a small study (n=20) with pre/post HRV + cognitive measures would fill the biggest revenue-relevant gap.

2. **Military partnership exploration.** DARPA, SOCOM, and the Human Performance Optimization program are natural fits. But SRL needs at least one published military-relevant data point before approaching.

3. **Adolescent safety review.** If the NeuroMinute Family Deck targets teens, SRL needs evidence of safety and appropriateness for ages 12-17. This is a regulatory and liability question, not just an evidence question.

## Gap Type 5: Contradiction Inventory

**Question:** Where does the evidence actively conflict?

| Topic | Claim A | Claim B | Status | SRL Position |
|-------|---------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Polyvagal Theory validity | PVT describes real phylogenetic hierarchy | PVT evolutionary claims are untenable | Active debate | Clinical interventions work regardless; avoid phylogenetic claims |
| Sleep quality → cognition | Quality predicts next-day processing speed | No significant sleep architecture effects on executive function | Mixed evidence | Supports CVA approach over simple metrics |
| RSA as vagal tone index | RSA is a valid non-invasive index of cardiac vagal tone | RSA is confounded by respiratory parameters | Long-standing debate | Use RSA with respiratory controls; acknowledge limitations |
| HRV as health predictor | Lower HRV predicts morbidity/mortality | HRV is modifiable and improvement tracks health gains | Complementary, not contradictory | Both claims valid; SRL focuses on modifiability |

### Priority Actions (Contradictions)

1. **Polyvagal Theory:** Monitor the Grossman-Porges exchange quarterly. Update SRL's canonical position document as the debate evolves. Consider publishing an SRL perspective piece that focuses on clinical utility rather than evolutionary mechanism.

2. **Sleep-Cognition:** Design SRL's measurement approach to capture the nuance. Don't claim simple sleep→performance links; use CVA to show individual variability patterns.

## Quarterly Gap Report Template

Every quarter, the system generates:

1. **KRL Progression Report:** Which concepts moved up or down in readiness? Why?
2. **Evidence Velocity:** How many new evidence notes were added? What grade distribution?
3. **Top 5 Gaps:** The most critical evidence weaknesses ranked by commercial impact
4. **Research Priority Queue:** Ordered list of studies/data collection efforts that would most efficiently close gaps
5. **Contradiction Update:** Any new contradictions? Any resolved?
6. **Freshness Audit:** Which evidence chains are aging and need refresh?

## The Strategic Insight

The gap analysis reveals that SRL's biggest commercial risk is not scientific validity — the underlying mechanisms (HRV biofeedback, interoception, breathwork) have strong Level 1-2 evidence. The risk is the **application gap**: SRL's *proprietary contributions* (GMT, NeuroMinute, Anterocept, Diaphragmatic Blindness) are the differentiators, but they sit at KRL 2-5 with mostly Level 5/SRL-P evidence.

The path from "interesting company with good science" to "defensible platform with predictable due diligence" runs through exactly three studies:

1. **VCU GMT + NeuroMinute Study** (CRNAs; n≥60; pre/post + control) → Elevates GMT to KRL 6, NeuroMinute to KRL 4
2. **Diaphragmatic Blindness Formal Assessment** (CRNAs; n≥100; cross-sectional) → Publishes SRL's most vivid finding
3. **Executive Population Pilot** (Corporate; n≥20; pre/post) → Opens the enterprise market with data

Three studies. That's the gap between where you are and where due diligence becomes a breeze.
