# Hormesis-Durability Model

The application of hormesis (biphasic dose-response: low/intermittent stress leads to adaptation; chronic/excessive stress leads to damage) to clinician career durability. The hypothesis: repeated, well-dosed exposure to challenge states + adequate recovery + reflective updating accelerates mastery and extends career longevity.

## Definition

The hormesis-durability model is an SRL-original synthesis of Taleb (antifragility), Mattson (hormesis), and Calabrese (dose-response). It provides the physiological basis for why [[gap-moment-training]] works: controlled micro-stressors build capacity. Distinguished from naive "stress is good" by requiring specification of dose, timing, recovery, and response class.

## Core Insight

The practical variable is not how high you spike during a hard moment, but whether you complete the recovery curve reliably and whether your baseline drifts upward over months and years. You can have elite operators with very high acute arousal who do not burn out (good recovery kinetics, stable meaning, good team safety) and operators with moderate acuity who do (persistent vigilance, social threat, sleep debt, unresolved rumination).

## Three Pillars of Durability

This model synthesizes with two complementary frameworks:
1. **Hormesis** (dose-response mechanism): well-dosed stress + recovery = accelerated mastery
2. **Broaden-and-build** (positive affect mechanism): curiosity and awe broaden cognition and build durable resources
3. **Allostatic load management** (cost-prevention mechanism): recovery kinetics prevent cumulative wear

## Integration with Antifragility

Randy explicitly synthesizes Taleb's antifragility with Vervaeke's existential resilience and hormesis: "I really want to understand this to see if one can extend their CRNA career through practicing, through practical durability practices that cause each stressor to increase the speed of mastery."

The antifragile clinician does not merely survive stressors — each well-recovered stressor leaves them stronger. But this requires the hormetic conditions: appropriate dose, adequate recovery, reflective integration.
