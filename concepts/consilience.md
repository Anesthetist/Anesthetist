E.O. Wilson's term for the unity of knowledge — the convergence of evidence from independent domains toward a single explanatory framework. When Randy was presented with seven core values extracted from his knowledge graph, he responded with one word: "consilience." The convergence IS the point.

## Relevance to SRL

Somnistics itself is a consilience engine. It produces knowledge by forcing convergence across domains that rarely speak to each other: anesthesiology + contemplative neuroscience + nursing theory + AI + pranayama. The vault is the infrastructure for tracking that convergence.
