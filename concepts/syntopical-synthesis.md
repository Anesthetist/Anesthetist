The active process of reading multiple books on the same question simultaneously and forcing them into dialogue — producing novel insights that exist in none of the individual sources. From Adler's syntopical reading method (How to Read a Book), extended by Randy into a multi-LLM-assisted knowledge construction technique.

Randy's method: assemble a curated book stack spanning multiple domains, feed them through AI synthesis, correct the AI's shallow readings, and extract the cross-domain parallels that constitute genuinely new knowledge. The synthesis is neither in the books nor in the AI — it emerges from the directed collision.
