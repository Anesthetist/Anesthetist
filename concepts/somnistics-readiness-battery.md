# Somnistics Readiness Battery (SRB-60)

The standardized, proctorable assessment battery for Somnistics certification. Built around 60-second events matching the NeuroMinute design space, the SRB-60 is Randy's "presidential fitness test of applied micro-interoception gap moment training."

## Five Competency Domains

### 1. Gap Moment Literacy
Detecting transitions and classifying likely autonomic state (hyperarousal / hypoarousal / cognitive tunnel / dyspnea loop / ready). The pattern-recognition skill — knowing when a gap moment is occurring and what state you are in.

### 2. Micro-Interoceptive Skill
Rapid sensing, labeling, and calibration of internal body signals. Tests the trainee's ability to accurately perceive and name interoceptive signals under time pressure.

### 3. Protocol Selection and Fidelity
Choosing the right NeuroMinute for the detected state and context, then executing it with high adherence to the protocol specification (correct breath ratios, proper attentional focus, appropriate modality selection).

### 4. Measured State Delta
Producing measurable physiological change within the 60-second window. Assessed via HRV shift, respiration rate change, or other biometric indicators that confirm the protocol produced its intended autonomic effect.

### 5. Return-to-Task Readiness
Regulating without sedation — demonstrating that the intervention restores executive function and task readiness rather than inducing drowsiness or dissociation. The trainee must show they can transition smoothly back to high-demand performance after a NeuroMinute.

## Performance Tiers

- **Bronze** — Demonstrates basic competency across all five domains
- **Silver** — Consistent performance with measurable state deltas
- **Gold** — Advanced skill with rapid protocol selection and high fidelity
- **Platinum** — Expert-level performance suitable for instructor candidacy
