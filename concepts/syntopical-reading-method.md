Mortimer Adler's highest level of reading from How to Read a Book: reading multiple works on the same question simultaneously, constructing a dialogue between authors who never spoke to each other, and producing a synthesis that transcends any individual source. Randy deliberately chose this method for his 12-book stack spanning decision science, trauma physiology, mystical states, expertise research, and probabilistic thinking.

Adler's five steps: (1) find relevant passages across all books, (2) bring authors to terms (establish shared vocabulary), (3) get questions clear, (4) define issues, (5) analyze the discussion. Randy extends this with AI-assisted cross-referencing: "deeply enhabfed cross referencing. Similar concepts but different wording."
