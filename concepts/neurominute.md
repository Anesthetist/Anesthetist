# NeuroMinute™

A 60-second, scripted micro-intervention combining breath, interoception cues, and multimodal sensory elements (binaural beats, voice, haptics, sound design) with a clear neurotarget and tracked biometric response. The NeuroMinute is the fundamental "Lego brick" of somnistics — measurable, low-cognitive-load, and stackable into curricula and data models.

## Definition

Each NeuroMinute is a metadata-tagged protocol unit delivered via app/Watch that targets a specific autonomic or cognitive state (e.g., vagal tone, CO₂ tolerance, insula engagement). Every session generates a tiny experiment: context → protocol → physiological response, building a longitudinal dataset on how specific nervous systems respond to specific interventions in specific environments.

## Four-Phase Structure

1. **Acknowledge the felt sense** — "Part acknowledgment" without judgment (IFS-informed)
2. **Invite the Self into watcher's seat** — invitational language, never directive
3. **Differentiate interoceptive elements** — rhythm, texture, temperature of internal signals
4. **Presence without fixing** — integration, not elimination; state transition from chaos/rigidity into the "River of Integration"

## Modular Protocol Layers

- **Breath pacing** — synchronized to cardiac oscillation (not external clock)
- **Interoceptive anchors** — heartbeat, breath texture, body temperature
- **Exteroceptive anchors** — ambient sound, haptic feedback from Watch
- **Voice tone and language precision** — never "must," "fix," or "get rid of"; somatic psychotherapy framing

## Core Breath Protocols

- **5/5/5/5 (Box Breathing)** — balanced regulation
- **10/0/10/0 (Anesthetist's Breath)** — deep vagal activation
- **3/3/3/3 (Rapid Reg)** — fast-acting under acute stress
- **4/7/8 (Parasympathetic)** — extended exhale for shutdown recovery

## Neurophysiological Targets

Designed to develop neural density along pathways associated with decreased mental health harm risk in CRNAs. Builds capacity in both top-down (prefrontal regulation) and bottom-up (brainstem/vagal) messaging to consciousness. Each NeuroMinute targets state transitions from chaos/rigidity back into Self-leadership and flow.

## Evidence Base

- [[lehrer-2000-resonance-frequency]] — breathing at resonant frequency produces measurable HRV shifts within a single session, validating the 60-second format
- [[porges-2011-polyvagal-theory]] — slow rhythmic breathing directly stimulates the ventral vagal pathway; RSA as measurable index
- [[zaccaro-2018-breathing-systematic-review]] — systematic evidence for autonomic and CNS effects of controlled breathing
- [[kerr-2013-mindfulness-body-alpha-rhythms]] — somatosensory alpha modulation measurable within brief practice windows, validating 60-second intervention format
- [[sezer-2025-meditation-autonomic-nervous-system]] — cardiac resonance and respiratory coupling effects detectable in brief meditation intervals

## Clinical Observations

- NeuroMinutes fit the clinical workflow constraint: CRNAs cannot take 10-minute breaks but can access 60-second windows between cases
- The protocol format transforms dead time into structured physiological training reps
- Progressive stacking across the [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day curriculum produces cumulative neuroplastic effects
