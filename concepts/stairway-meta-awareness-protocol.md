A five-step ascending practice that layers Tara Springett's developmental model onto Damasio's feeling-of-what-happens and Gurdjieff's self-remembering. The five steps:

1. **Orient to body** — interoceptive grounding (annamaya kosha)
2. **Name current emotion** — affective labeling (pranamaya kosha)
3. **Sense the OR soundscape** — exteroceptive field awareness (manomaya kosha)
4. **Widen to collective field** — team + patient co-regulation (vijnanamaya kosha)
5. **Hold the full stack in awareness for one breath cycle** — unified consciousness (anandamaya kosha)

Each step engages a distinct attentional network, forging resilient intero-extero loops. The protocol is designed for clinical environments where awareness must be simultaneously inward (interoceptive) and outward (team/patient/environment).

The five-step structure maps directly onto the [[kosha-architecture]] — body, emotion, environment, collective, unified awareness — providing a clinical practice protocol that operationalizes the kosha layers as a real-time awareness exercise.

Synthesized from Springett's *Stairway to Heaven* developmental model, Damasio's somatic consciousness, and Gurdjieff's [[self-remembering]].
