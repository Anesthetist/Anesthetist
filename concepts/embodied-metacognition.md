The capacity to monitor, evaluate, and modulate one's own cognitive and attentional processes *through* somatic signals rather than through abstract thought. Standard metacognition asks "what am I thinking about?" Embodied metacognition asks "what is my body telling me about how I am thinking?"

The diaphragm, heart rate variability, jaw tension, shoulder set, and interoceptive signals become the primary feedback channel for meta-awareness.

Randy's framework differs from academic "embodied cognition" (which argues cognition is shaped by the body) by making the body the *instrument of metacognitive monitoring itself*. The CRNA who notices their shoulders rising before they consciously register anxiety is practicing embodied metacognition. The meditator who detects thought-onset through a subtle shift in breathing pattern is doing the same.

## Keystone Role

This concept is the keystone of Somnistics: [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] trains it, [[neurominute|NeuroMinutes™]] exercise it, the [[stairway-meta-awareness-protocol|Stairway Meta-Awareness Protocol]] scaffolds it, and [[interoceptive-literacy|interoceptive literacy]] is its prerequisite skill.

## Origin

Randy's "I am not my thoughts" epiphany through years of Sam Harris's Waking Up app practice. The epiphany was not cognitive but somatic — it became real when it was felt, not when it was understood.

> "After doing sam harris waking up app for several years, i had an epiphany 'i am not my thoughts' and began to spend more time in the present moment, non-dual reality."
