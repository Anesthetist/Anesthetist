"Businesses regulate at the precision their founders feel."

Metasomatic entrepreneurship is Randy's extension of Cedric Chin's Commoncog framework into somatic territory. The core insight: a founder's capacity for interoceptive precision — the ability to detect and interpret their own physiological signals — directly constrains the quality of decisions, team regulation, and organizational coherence at every stage of growth.

## Thesis

If the founder cannot feel their own stress response accurately, they cannot:
- Detect demand biomarkers in customer conversations
- Calibrate team energy and prevent burnout cascades
- Make high-stakes decisions from regulation rather than reactivity
- Model the autonomic flexibility they expect from their organization

## Implications for SRL

SRL is simultaneously the product and the proof. Randy's clinical interoceptive training (28 years of CRNA practice) is the metasomatic foundation that makes the company's technical architecture possible. The product teaches what the founder already embodies.
