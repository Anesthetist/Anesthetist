English-first launch with analytics-driven expansion to additional languages and markets. Rather than attempting simultaneous global launch, use App Store analytics as low-cost global reconnaissance to identify where organic demand exists, then localize into those markets first.

This applies the minimum effective dose principle to go-to-market: launch lean, measure signal, expand where demand biomarkers appear.
