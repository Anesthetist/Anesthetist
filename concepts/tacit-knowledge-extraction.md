The pipeline from embodied skill to codified micro-practices to productized IP. This IS the SRL business model described as an epistemological process.

## The Pipeline

1. **Tacit clinical skill** -- embodied, lived expertise accumulated over 28 years
2. **Codified micro-practices** -- NeuroMinutes, Gap Moment protocols
3. **Algorithmic deployment** -- biometric feedback, adaptive delivery
4. **Community reinforcement** -- rituals, certification, peer practice
5. **IP protection** -- trademarked terms, validated instruments, published research

## Intellectual Foundations

Three traditions converge: Michael Polanyi's tacit knowledge ("we know more than we can tell"), Cedric Chin's expertise acceleration frameworks from Commoncog, and Patricia Benner's novice-to-expert nursing model.

## Economic Thesis

"Code is cheap. Interfaces are fluid. Clinical wisdom -- knowing exactly what a human nervous system needs 4 minutes after a Code Blue -- is the rarest asset on earth." (Randy, clinical-wisdom-as-asset)

If code is cheap and interfaces are fluid, value accrues to rare judgment under uncertainty. Tacit knowledge becomes infrastructure only when expressed as state variables, transition rules, decay constants, and intervention modifiers.

## Quality Standard

Randy's frustration with ChatGPT's theatrics ("Reading this output is awful") reveals his standard for extraction: plain, mechanistic, honest. No dramatization. The extraction process itself must embody the clinical precision it seeks to capture.
