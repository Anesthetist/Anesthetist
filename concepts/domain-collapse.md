# Domain Collapse

The moment a problem domain shifts from being bound by scarce human expertise to being bound only by compute. When intelligence infrastructure reaches threshold density, progress ceases to be artisanal and becomes industrial (Diamandis & Wissner-Gross, 2026).

## Definition

Domain Collapse is the pattern Diamandis and Wissner-Gross identify in *Solve Everything: Achieving Abundance by 2035*: once intelligence infrastructure reaches a threshold in any domain, the domain doesn't improve incrementally — it collapses from craft to utility, the way electricity collapsed candlemaking.

## Three Preconditions

1. **Targeting System** — Forward-looking, adversarial, payable metrics that route capital and cognition automatically
2. **Intelligence Stack** — The closed-loop harness that makes smart models reliable and safe (see [[intelligence-stack]])
3. **Trust Infrastructure** — Sufficient institutional trust for adoption at scale

When all three converge on one domain, that domain collapses.

## The Organizational Singularity Connection

The Organizational Singularity described in Ismail et al. (2026) is Domain Collapse applied to *coordination itself* — the domain of organizing human effort. The ExO 3.0 architecture (MTP + DRIVE + SHAPE) is the mechanism for making Domain Collapse happen by design, not by accident.

## SRL Relevance — Healthcare Domain Collapse

SRL's thesis is that clinical performance training is approaching Domain Collapse:
- **Current state:** Expertise-bound. Autonomic regulation skills depend on scattered clinical experience, expensive workshops, and tacit knowledge transfer (see [[tacit-knowledge-extraction]])
- **SRL's targeting system:** BOLT scores, MAIA-2, RMSSD, HRV coherence — quantified physiological outcomes
- **SRL's intelligence stack:** Pausality app + clinical LLM moat encoding 28 years of CRNA expertise
- **Trust infrastructure:** Clinical credibility (CRNA credentials), evidence-based positioning, CE accreditation

The Fiduciary Wedge (see [[fiduciary-wedge]]) means healthcare domains collapse more slowly due to irreducible clinical judgment requirements — but they still collapse. The 1-2 year regulatory lag predicted in the manuscript aligns with SRL's timeline for enterprise pilot → enterprise contract (Red Arrow 3).
