Devices and software that modulate interoceptive state, as defined by Schoeller et al. (2019). The emerging category of technology designed to sense, represent, and influence the body's internal signals.

## Category Definition

Interoceptive technology encompasses any tool that:
- Captures physiological signals related to internal body state
- Represents those signals to the user in accessible form (visual, auditory, haptic)
- Enables the user to modify their internal state through feedback loops

## SRL's Position

Somnistics is an interoceptive technology company. The product category barely exists — most competitors (Calm, Headspace) are content delivery platforms, not interoceptive technology. SRL's closed-loop biofeedback architecture places it in a different category entirely.
