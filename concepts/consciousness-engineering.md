# Consciousness Engineering

The applied practice of modulating consciousness states with precision — from wakefulness through sedation, anesthesia, and recovery. This is the unclaimed white space that positions Randy's 28-year CRNA career as a unique competitive moat: no other wellness/performance brand founder has daily, high-stakes experience *engineering* consciousness under life-or-death conditions.

## Definition

> "I don't just study consciousness — I engineer it."

CRNAs modulate consciousness daily through pharmacological agents (propofol, sevoflurane, fentanyl) titrated to precise endpoints. This experience translates directly to non-pharmacological consciousness modulation via breath, interoception, and autonomic regulation — the core of somnistics.

Nobody occupies this space:
