4-week experimental loops testing one job-to-be-done at a time. Each sprint has a single hypothesis, a defined audience segment, a measurable signal, and a decision threshold. NeuroMinute is positioned as the codified output of validated demand sprints — the product that survives the selection pressure.

From Cedric Chin's Commoncog framework, extended by Randy into the SRL context where each demand sprint is also a somatic learning loop: the founder's interoceptive accuracy improves with each sprint as pattern recognition for demand biomarkers sharpens.
