An epistemic orientation characterized by the capacity to engage with overwhelming complexity — geopolitical, technological, existential — without ideological regression, moral panic, or dissociative collapse. Distinct from psychological attachment to caregivers; this is attachment to *what is actually happening* in the world.

Randy named this quality while analyzing Dr. Pippa Malmgren's geopolitical writing, pointing to her as an exemplar: "This is an example of secure attachment with reality." The concept identifies a cognitive-somatic capacity that includes:

- Emotionally regulated tone amid volatile content
- Embodied cognition — continuous return to physical referents
- Simultaneous mentalization of multiple perspectives
- Non-dissociative sense-making (neither denies horror nor collapses into it)
- Antifragile modeling — perceiving structure of probable outcomes through chaos

This positions the *endpoint* of somnistics training — not just nervous system regulation, but the capacity to engage with complex reality from a regulated, discerning stance. A higher-order outcome beyond "calm." [[autonomic-regulation]] is necessary but not sufficient; secure attachment to reality is the functional expression of deep regulatory capacity meeting epistemic maturity.

Connects to [[relevance-realization]] (Vervaeke's framework — what matters emerges from embodied salience), [[interoceptive-literacy]] (felt-sense grounding enables reality attachment), and [[self-remembering]] (the continuous self-awareness that prevents dissociative drift).
