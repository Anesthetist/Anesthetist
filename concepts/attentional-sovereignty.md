# Attentional Sovereignty

William James said it over a century ago: the faculty of voluntarily returning a wandering attention, over and over, "is the very root of judgment, character, and will." For CRNAs, this is not philosophy — attention is literally life. Every moment of attentional drift in the OR has a potential body count.

Attentional sovereignty is the claim that *what you pay attention to is your life* — not instrumentally, but constitutively. Most resilience programs treat attention as a tool: focus better → fewer errors → last longer before burning out. Attentional sovereignty inverts that: you are not reclaiming attention so you can perform better. You are reclaiming it because **that is your life**.

## The CRNA-Specific Reframe

CRNAs already know attentional discipline in their bones. What they lack is a framework connecting the same sovereignty that keeps a pediatric airway patent to the capacity that keeps their own life from hollowing out.

The common errors of anesthesia that nobody talks about are not technical failures. They are failures of attentional sovereignty under conditions that systematically erode it.

## Why This Changes Pausality's Pitch

Most interventions position attention as performance fuel. Attentional sovereignty positions it as the substance of a human life. That shift moves [[gap-moment-training]] from a coping technique into a sovereignty practice — each 60-second gap between cases is a micro-act of choosing what your life is about right now, under high autonomic arousal, when the nervous system is screaming to armor up or check out.

This is the thesis of Randy's book, in one sentence.

## Key Source

William James, *Principles of Psychology* (1890). The most radical claim in psychology, still undefeated.
