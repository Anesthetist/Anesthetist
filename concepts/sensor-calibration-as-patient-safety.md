# Sensor Calibration as Patient Safety (not Wellness)

## The Reframe

Interoceptive and autonomic training for clinicians is typically categorized as **wellness** — optional, individual, lifestyle, adjacent to the core work of patient care. This category assignment is a strategic error for two reasons:

1. It makes the intervention politically optional inside healthcare organizations.
2. It quietly lets the patient-safety argument off the table, because wellness is not a safety variable.

The reframe: if the clinician is a [[crna-as-closed-loop-controller|closed-loop physiological controller]] whose primary sensor is their own autonomic nervous system, then **sensor calibration is a patient-safety variable**, not a wellness concern. The frame shift changes:

- **Budget category** from wellness/HR to clinical operations / patient safety
- **Evidence standard** from "nice to have" to "measurable clinical outcome"
- **Political positioning** from self-help to professional competency
- **Research justification** from employee satisfaction to safety improvement

## Why This Matters for SRL

The wellness category is crowded, commoditized, and structurally disrespected inside clinical operations. The patient-safety category has budget, political weight, and mandatory compliance machinery. The same intervention placed in the second category reaches a completely different decision-maker, with different evidence standards and a different time horizon.

This is not a marketing trick. It is a correct re-categorization that follows mechanistically from the closed-loop-controller frame.

## Supporting Literature

- [[craig-2009-interoception-awareness]] — the anterior insula as the neural substrate for the "sensor"; interoception is foundational to consciousness
- [[hrv-vagal-tone-longitudinal-burnout-predictor]] — HRV is a *prospective* predictor of clinician distress, making it a sensor-calibration variable rather than an outcome
- [[jama-2024-2025-ambient-ai-scribe-burnout-time]] — the ambient-scribe mechanism finding, read attentionally rather than temporally, is the same argument playing out in medicine as a whole
- [[dean-talbot-2018-moral-injury-not-burnout-stat]] — locates the problem in the environment, which creates the political room to reframe the intervention as structural rather than individual

## The Guardrail

This reframe does not let healthcare organizations off the hook for system-level reforms. Sensor calibration inside an unreformed moral-injury environment is exactly what it sounds like — tool use by the clinician inside a broken system. The frame accepts that *both* are needed (structural reform AND sensor calibration) and refuses the common move of treating the second as a substitute for the first.

## Implications for Product and Go-to-Market

- Wellness-category sales objections collapse ("why would we pay for meditation") when the category is patient safety.
- Evidence requirements align with existing patient-safety research machinery (IRB pathways, QI projects, Morbidity & Mortality conferences).
- CE and certification pathways (AANA Category A) are the right professional targets, because CE is the professional-competency layer, not the wellness layer. See [[aana-category-a-ce-application]].
