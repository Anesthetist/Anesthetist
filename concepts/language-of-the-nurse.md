# Language of the Nurse

The design and communication philosophy at the core of Somnistics: translate every technical concept into the warm, human, care-centered language of clinical nursing.

> "A nurse doesn't walk into a room and say, 'The SpO2 sensor indicates hypoxia.' A nurse touches the patient's arm and says, 'Let's get you some more air so you can rest easier.'"

Somnistics is the bridge between the cold technical "Meta Universe" and a **Bedside Manner**. The product doesn't track you — it cares for you.

## Translation Pairs (Operating Template)

| Tech Term | Nurse Translation |
|-----------|-------------------|
| IMU-based Posture Gating | A gentle hand on your shoulder |
| Vagal Nerve Stimulation via Binaural Beats | A lullaby for your nervous system |
| NeuroMinutes | The 60-Second Reset |

The pattern: *tech term → what it feels like to a body that's cared for → plain language explanation*.

## Strategic Value

This framing lowers adoption barriers for non-technical users, sidesteps the cold monitoring-tool vibe most wellness apps carry, and survives regulatory scrutiny by anchoring in well-being language rather than clinical claims. It also resonates specifically with the CRNA population, who already live in a care language.

## Caution

Keep "nurse" as internal philosophy. Outward-facing language stays in "well-being" and "resilience" territory — clinical care framing can trigger liability questions with partners like Meta or hospital systems.

## First Full Application

The [[neurominutes-meta-glasses]] project — translating the Vagalbeats concept (binaural beats + posture gating + HRV) into a "digital hand on the shoulder" for Meta Ray-Ban glasses. Partner pitch reframed from feature specs to: *"turning the glasses into a tool for resilience and public health."*
