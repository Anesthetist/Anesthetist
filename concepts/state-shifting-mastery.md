The practiced ability to deliberately move between autonomic states — not just from dysregulated to regulated, but between any states the clinical situation demands. State-shifting mastery is the performance outcome that Somnistics training produces: the clinician who can access calm focus, heightened alertness, compassionate presence, or analytical detachment on demand.

This is the distinction between relaxation (one direction: stressed → calm) and regulation (bidirectional: any state → target state). State-shifting mastery is the operational capability that "state control, not relaxation" points toward.
