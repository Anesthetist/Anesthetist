# Multi-Phase Interoceptive Coupling

An advanced interoceptive training method that guides users to simultaneously attend to multiple internal physiological signals (breath, heartbeat, pelvic floor, tension patterns) synchronized with controlled breathing patterns. MPIC provides the "what" — which signals to attend to — while [[neurominute]] provides the "how."

## Definition

MPIC is the coupling mechanism that trains users to hold awareness of progressively more internal signals simultaneously. Within a 60-second NeuroMinute, the first 30 seconds focus on a single interoceptive signal, then the second 30 seconds layer in additional signals. Adaptive cues (color changes, gentle chimes, voice prompts) indicate when to add the next sensory focus.

## Progression Pathway

1. **Bi-Phase Coupling (Beginner)** — Breath + heartbeat awareness. Two concurrent interoceptive targets. Entry-level for all users.

2. **Tri-Phase Coupling (Intermediate)** — Breath + pulse + proprioceptive awareness (muscle tension, postural alignment). Three concurrent targets.

3. **Multi-Phase Coupling (Advanced)** — 4-7 concurrent interoceptive targets: breath rhythm, heartbeat, muscle tension, pelvic floor, temperature gradients, visceral sensation, emotional tone.

## Within-Session Structure

- **0-30 seconds:** Single-focus interoceptive awareness (e.g., breath only)
- **30-60 seconds:** Dual/multi-focus coupling (e.g., breath + heartbeat + tension)
- **Adaptive layering:** Biofeedback integration shows real-time HRV/respiratory data alongside proprioceptive awareness cues

## Core Breath Protocols Used in MPIC

- **5/5/5/5 (Box Breathing)** — balanced regulation during coupling practice
- **10/0/10/0 (Anesthetist's Breath)** — deep vagal activation with multi-signal attention
- **3/3/3/3 (Rapid Reg)** — fast-acting coupling under acute stress
- **4/7/8 (Parasympathetic)** — extended exhale with progressive signal layering

## Neuroscientific Basis

Strengthens the insula-cingulate-brainstem integration pathway. Increases:
- **Interoceptive accuracy** — ability to detect bodily sensations with precision
- **Emotional regulation** — through coupling body signals with affective awareness
- **Autonomic control** — polyvagal network coordination across multiple signal channels
