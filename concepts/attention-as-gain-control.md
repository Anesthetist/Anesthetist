A reframing of attention from "spotlight selection" to "selective amplification + normalization under energy constraints." Drawn from convergent mathematics across 500M years of vertebrate attention circuits, chemical reaction networks, and transformer AI. The core claim: wherever a system cannot process everything, it learns to amplify a few signals and normalize the whole field. Selection is what it looks like; gain control is what it is.

Applied to Somnistics: Gap Moment Training is teaching a nervous system to solve the same optimization problem more skillfully. NeuroMinutes are "one-minute somatic gain adjustments for a nervous system under pressure." Interoceptive amplification (turning up the gain on diaphragm, HRV, cardiac pulse, localized tension) combined with somatic normalization (vagal/baroreflex cascades, network-level divisive normalization) produces apparent selection (subjective experience of calm and clarity).

This reframe distinguishes Somnistics from mindfulness/wellness ("pay attention to your breath") by positioning it as "teaching gain control under load" -- a precision-engineering framing rather than a relaxation framing.

## Key Mechanism

Gap moments reframed as local information/energy bottlenecks: too many signals (alarms, labs, surgeon, patient, internal anxiety), not enough time/energy to process everything. Gap Moment Training = teaching a nervous system to solve the same optimization problem (selective amplification + normalization under resource constraints) more skillfully. This connects GMT directly to 500M years of convergent evolution in attention architecture and to transformer AI attention mechanisms.
