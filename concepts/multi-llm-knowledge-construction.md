Randy's demonstrated method of feeding outputs from one LLM (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) into another to produce layered knowledge constructions that no single AI session could generate. This is not prompt engineering — it is a knowledge construction technique where the human serves as curator, editor, and quality gate across multiple AI reasoning passes.

## Method

1. Generate initial synthesis in LLM-A (e.g., Claude analysis)
2. Feed that output + new source material into LLM-B (e.g., ChatGPT)
3. Correct shallow readings, reject poor synthesis, endorse strong insights
4. Feed refined output into LLM-C or back into LLM-A with new questions
5. The human's judgment at each step IS the knowledge — the AIs are amplifiers

Randy explicitly stated: "this project is beyond my capabilities as a human" — positioning multi-LLM synthesis as a necessary collaborator, not just a tool.
