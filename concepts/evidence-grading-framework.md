# SRL Evidence Grading Framework

## Purpose

Every evidence link in the SRL Knowledge Vault carries a quality grade. This enables honest, defensible representation of evidence maturity to any audience — clinical partners, investors, accreditation bodies, or academic collaborators. No hand-waving. No overstating. Every claim is graded.

### Supplementary GRADE Dimensions

Each graded evidence link also carries:

**Consistency** — Does this finding align with other evidence on the same question?
- `consistent`: Multiple independent sources converge
- `mixed`: Some support, some contradiction
- `contradicted`: Strong opposing evidence exists (see contradiction tracker)
- `isolated`: Only one source; no corroboration yet

**Directness** — How directly does this evidence test the SRL concept it's linked to?
- `direct`: Study specifically tests the mechanism SRL claims
- `indirect`: Study tests a related mechanism; relevance inferred
- `extrapolated`: Study tests a different population or context; SRL applies by analogy

**Freshness** — How recent is the evidence?
- `current`: Published within 2 years
- `recent`: Published 2-5 years ago
- `aging`: Published 5-10 years ago
- `foundational`: 10+ years old but still cited as canonical
- `historical`: Primarily of historical interest; may be superseded

**Replication** — Has the finding been independently replicated?
- `replicated`: Independent teams have confirmed
- `partially-replicated`: Some aspects confirmed, others untested
- `awaiting-replication`: Single study; no independent confirmation
- `failed-replication`: Replication attempts did not confirm (flag for review)

## Part 2: Knowledge Readiness Levels (KRL)

Adapted from NASA's Technology Readiness Levels for knowledge claims. Applied to **concepts**, not individual evidence notes.

| KRL | Level Name | Description | Due Diligence Translation |
|-----|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|
| **1** | Observation | Phenomenon noticed in clinical practice or literature | "We've seen this in the field" |
| **2** | Hypothesis | Concept formally stated; testable prediction articulated | "We have a theory about why this works" |
| **3** | Proof of Concept | Initial evidence supports the claim (Level 4-5 evidence) | "Early data supports our hypothesis" |
| **4** | Controlled Validation | Lab or clinical study confirms mechanism (Level 1b-3b evidence) | "We have clinical trial data" |
| **5** | Real-World Validation | Field data confirms in operational settings | "It works in the real world, with real clinicians" |
| **6** | Multi-Site/Population | Demonstrated across multiple sites or populations | "It works across different settings" |
| **7** | Standardized | Protocol documented; trained practitioners can replicate | "Others can deliver this without us in the room" |
| **8** | Accredited | Recognized by external body (AANA, university, institutional partner) | "Third parties have validated our work" |
| **9** | Industry Standard | Widely adopted reference framework | "This is how the field does it" |

## Part 3: Compliance Integration

### For Investor Due Diligence
Present the KRL table. Investors understand TRL (Technology Readiness Levels) — KRL is immediately legible. The honest acknowledgment that some concepts are at KRL 2 while others are at KRL 5-6 demonstrates scientific integrity and a clear development roadmap.

### For Clinical Partners
Present the evidence grade table filtered by population. Hospital systems want to see Level 1-3 evidence for any intervention they'll adopt. SRL can show exactly where that evidence exists and where the pathway to it is planned.

### For AANA CE Accreditation
The controlled vocabulary with MeSH identifiers + evidence grades at Level 1a-2b = the documentation package they need. Every training module maps to graded evidence through the concept graph.

### For FDA Boundary Management
Evidence grades help SRL maintain the wellness-vs-medical boundary. Claims supported only by SRL-P or SRL-O evidence should remain framed as "general wellness" or "training and education." Claims with Level 1-3 evidence may support stronger positioning but require regulatory counsel review.

### For Patent Defense
SRL-P and SRL-O grades flag proprietary contributions. The absence of external evidence for specific SRL novel concepts (anterocept, neurogating, NeuroMinute) is actually the novelty argument — nobody else has this because SRL originated it.

## Maintenance

- **Weekly:** New evidence notes from the HPO scan are graded upon creation
- **Monthly:** KRL reassessment for any concept that received new evidence links
- **Quarterly:** Full knowledge gap analysis using the grading data
- **On contradiction:** Immediate review of affected concept KRL and evidence grades
