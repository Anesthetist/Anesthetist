# Intelligence Density

The ratio of judgment work to coordination work per person, combined with the speed of decision loops. The defining metric of the AI-native organization (Ismail, ExO 3.0, 2026).

## Definition

Intelligence Density measures how much high-value cognitive output an organization produces per human participant. High intelligence density means fewer people doing dramatically higher-value work — the coordination overhead has been absorbed by the Intelligence Stack.

## Mechanism

Organizations increase intelligence density by migrating coordination-heavy workflows to AI agents while concentrating human effort on irreducible judgment calls. The Intelligence Stack (SENSE → INTERPRET → DECIDE → ORCHESTRATE → LEARN) handles the coordination loop. Humans handle the Purpose layer and high-sigma decisions.

## SRL Relevance

SRL operates at extreme intelligence density: a founding team of three humans (Randy, Mehra, Jason) plus Vigil agent architecture producing output equivalent to a 10-15 person organization. This is both the organizational model AND the product thesis — SRL helps enterprise clients achieve intelligence density in clinician performance by replacing coordination overhead (scheduling wellness programs, tracking compliance, managing referrals) with closed-loop autonomous systems.

## Measurement

- Revenue per employee
- Decisions per human-hour
- Coordination-to-judgment ratio
- Decision loop speed (signal detection → validated action)

## Related Concepts

- Workforce Concentration (not reduction — fewer people, higher compensation, higher-leverage roles)
- Cognitive Captivity Risk (intelligence density built on a single vendor's stack is fragile)
- Domain Collapse (what happens externally when an intelligence-dense firm targets a problem domain)
