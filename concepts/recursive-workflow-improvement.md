# Recursive Workflow Improvement

Workflow-level recursive self-improvement: agents refining prompts, generating better training data, optimizing execution paths, and discovering new automation targets. The accelerant that transforms a one-time disruption into a compounding, irreversible phase transition (Ismail et al., 2026).

## Definition

This is NOT architectural self-modification in the AGI sense. It is the operational case: agents continuously improving the workflows they execute. Every cycle through the Intelligence Stack produces outputs AND improvements to the Stack itself.

The mechanism is precise:
- Agents refine their own prompts based on outcome evaluation
- Better execution generates better training data
- Pattern recognition discovers new automation targets
- The LEARN layer feeds improvements back through INTERPRET to sharpen future scenario framing

## The Compounding Gap

The gap between firms running this loop and firms that don't widens fast enough to become structurally unbridgeable — in months for information-centric sectors, years for regulated and physical-asset sectors.

This is the RSI accelerant referenced in the ExO 3.0 "R = Recursive Learning" characteristic.

## Learning Guardrails

Recursive improvement can optimize toward wrong objectives (Goodhart's Law at machine speed). Every improvement must be:
- Versioned
- Tested against compliance baselines
- A/B tested before deployment
- Automatically rolled back if it degrades performance on the eval suite
- No agent-generated optimization deploys without passing evaluation criteria defined in its Agent Specification

## Strategy Flash-Crash Problem

When every firm in a sector deploys SENSE agents monitoring the same market signals, recursive improvement creates systemic risk — sector-wide strategic herding. Design responses: signal diversification, reaction dampening, and sector-level coordination protocols.

## SRL Implementation

SRL's bot system already practices recursive workflow improvement:
- **Bot retrospectives** (retrospective.md) — post-run self-reflection
- **Pattern libraries** (patterns.md) — accumulated heuristics from prior runs
- **Learning logs** (learning-log.md) — tracking what worked and what didn't
- **Cross-bot feedback** — writer evaluates miner, coordinator tracks trends, Randy's decisions feed back to all bots
- **Vigil as LEARN layer** — orchestrating improvements across the bot system

The vault's version control protocol (git commits as lab notebook) is the audit trail for this recursive improvement.
