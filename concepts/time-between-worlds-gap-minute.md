The post-induction, pre-incision interval as a natural gap moment — a liminal space where conscious micro-rituals fit organically into clinical workflow. The practice: acknowledge impermanence (Chödrön), visualize two parallel timelines (life/death; consciousness/unconsciousness), and breathe once through each. Compresses existential insight into 60 seconds, priming calm agency during high-stakes transitions.

The phrase "time between worlds" comes from Pascal's Gurdjieff interpretation — the liminal space where consciousness can shift developmental levels. In Gurdjieff's framework, these are the "intervals" where conscious effort can change the trajectory of an entire sequence.

This is uniquely Somnistics: only a CRNA would recognize the post-induction/pre-incision moment as a natural gap moment. It is the literal clinical analogue of what Gurdjieff describes as the interval where conscious effort changes trajectory. The training insight: gap moments are not add-ons to clinical practice — they map to moments that *already exist* in the workflow. The training is about making existing liminal moments conscious, not creating new ones.

A specific practice within [[gap-moment-training]], connected to [[state-transition]] (post-induction as consciousness state transition), [[self-remembering]] (Gurdjieffian interval concept), and [[neurominute]] (deliverable as a 60-second protocol).
