# Somnistics Field Definition

The formal academic definition of somnistics as a new discipline. Somnistics is positioned analogously to how "cardiology" is both a practice and a domain — the name of the lab (SRL) and the discipline being founded.

## Formal Definition

> **Somnistics (n.)** — The interdisciplinary science and practice of training, measuring, and optimizing the body-mind system — particularly its rhythms of rest, stress, focus, and resilience — through neurophysiological awareness, technological feedback, and intentional micro-practices. It bridges neuroscience, somatic intelligence, and behavioral design to transform "gap moments" into opportunities for adaptive regulation and embodied intelligence.

## Simpler Form

> Somnistics is the science of training the nervous system.

## Disciplinary Boundaries

Somnistics integrates across:
- **Neuroscience** — polyvagal theory, insular cortex function, baroreflex physiology
- **Somatics** — embodied awareness, interoception, proprioception
- **Cognitive science** — relevance realization, hemispheric lateralization, attention training
- **Behavioral design** — micro-practice architecture, habit formation, titration to effect
- **Vedantic philosophy** — kosha (five-sheath) architecture as structural model
- **Biometric technology** — HRV, EEG, real-time wearable feedback
- **AI/ML** — adaptive personalization, anomaly detection, cognitive load classification

## Category Positioning

Somnistics is explicitly counter-positioned against:
- Generic "mindfulness apps" (Calm, Headspace) — too passive, no biometric feedback, no clinical grounding
- Corporate wellness programs — too broad, not embedded in workflow, no measurement
- Traditional breathwork — no technology integration, no progressive curriculum, no data layer

The differentiator: precision regulation for high-stakes professionals, embedded in workflow, with closed-loop biometric verification.
