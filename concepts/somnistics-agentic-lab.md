# Somnistics Agentic Research Lab

The Somnistics Agentic Research Lab is a model for converting Randy Graybeal's 28+ years of clinical tacit knowledge into scalable research outputs and commercial products — using agent swarms as the primary labor force, with Randy serving as Chairman/CEO rather than operator.

The architectural inspiration comes from Zero-Human Company (ZHC) models in which AI agents autonomously convert legacy data archives into physical prototypes (e.g., 3D-printed hardware from salvaged schematics). The Somnistics translation:

| ZHC Layer | Somnistics Translation |
|---|---|
| 6TB schematic archive | 28 years CRNA practice + AQRC error logs + research library |
| Agent swarms (Kimi/MiniMax/Grok) | Quartet agents (soul.md, agent.md, tools.md, ethics.md) |
| 3D-printed physical prototypes | Book chapters, MCP tools, Shift Architect cards, protocols |
| JouleWork (agent wages) | SaaS revenue / licensing |

## The Chairman Role

The clinician-founder's irreplaceable contributions:
1. **Vision and Permission Authority** — defining which clinical problems deserve agent attention; approving external integrations; vetoing outputs that carry professional or regulatory risk
2. **Data Curation as Strategic Asset** — choosing which frameworks get encoded (Porges, Siegel, Huberman, Keltner — not generic burnout content); which community language gets vectorized; which biometric protocols qualify as minimum effective dose
3. **Monetization Architecture** — structuring the agentic outputs (book, MCP server, app content, social content) into revenue streams the chairman doesn't have to personally deliver

## The Production Pipeline

**Step 1 — Semantic Search (Treasure Hunt)**
Agent ingests vectorized clinical archive (AQRC cases, research papers, community pain-point corpus) and surfaces relevant patterns.

**Step 2 — Framework Interpretation**
Clinical narratives → structured error taxonomies; biometric data → autonomic state classifications; contemplative frameworks → 60-second actionable protocols.

**Step 3 — Content Generation (Code as Creator)**
Agents draft book chapters, MCP tools, Shift Architect cards — parametrically from case studies and research.

**Step 4 — Validation Swarm**
Rival agents debate clinical accuracy; ethics agent flags malpractice-adjacent language; audience-resonance agent tests against authentic CRNA community corpus.

**Step 5 — Output to Market**
Book (agent-drafted, chairman-edited for medical-legal safety); MCP Server (agent-built, chairman-validates against FDA wellness guidelines); social content (agent-generated, chairman approves tone).

## The Exposure Constraint

Randy's legitimate concern about personal exposure shapes the architecture:
- **Agent-as-Brand**: outputs are "Somnistics Research Labs clinical decision support" — not "Randy's advice"
- **Aggregate Insights**: "AQRC data shows..." not "In my practice, I..."
- **Peer Review Layer**: agents flag any first-person clinical recommendations for veto

## Why This Matters for SRL

The model converts the founder's rarest asset — 28 years of pattern-matching under pressure — into a system that scales without depleting that asset. The chairman's attention is applied at maximum leverage: deciding *which problems to solve* and *what outputs to veto*, rather than doing the research or writing himself.
