Peter Kramer's concept adopted by Randy as the quality bar for all SRL writing: pointillist facts that reveal greater truths, moral judgment suspended, claims simultaneously stated and undermined by their own complexity. Randy pasted Kramer's definition directly into his ChatGPT session, signaling this as the standard.

This aligns with SRL's Communication Philosophy: descriptive not prescriptive, full spectrum before recommendation, earn the relationship through depth. Resonant nonfiction is the writing form that embodies these principles — it teaches through texture rather than instruction.
