Verbal and emotional purchase-intent signals detectable in customer conversations — the somatic markers of market demand. A founder with trained interoceptive accuracy can detect these signals in real-time: voice pitch shifts, lean-in moments, repeated language patterns, emotional activation around specific pain points.

## Application

In demand sprints and customer discovery conversations, trained attention to demand biomarkers replaces survey-based validation. The founder's body becomes a sensing instrument for product-market fit — not as mysticism, but as pattern recognition operating below conscious deliberation.

## Relationship to Somnistics

This concept extends the SRL thesis from clinical to entrepreneurial domains: if interoceptive training improves signal detection in the OR, it should also improve signal detection in the market.
