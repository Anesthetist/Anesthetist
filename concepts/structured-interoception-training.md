# Structured Interoception Training

## Definition

Randy's tri-modal protocol specification behind [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]]. Three complementary training modalities that, together, build the interoceptive infrastructure for clinical state regulation:

### Mode 1: Interoceptive Attention Training
Body-scan and signal-tracking exercises that restore the capacity to detect internal physiological states. Rebuilds the sensory channel that [[interoceptive-suppression-hypothesis|medical education suppressed]].

### Mode 2: Breath-Paced Autonomic Training
HRV-biofeedback-guided respiratory pacing at [[resonant-breathing-frequency|resonant breathing frequency]]. Trains the autonomic nervous system to shift states on demand through breath rate, depth, and rhythm.

### Mode 3: Graded Interoceptive Exposure
CO2 tolerance drills and air-hunger protocols that systematically expand the clinician's capacity to remain regulated under physiological discomfort. Applies [[hormesis-durability-model|hormetic]] principles — well-dosed stress + recovery = accelerated adaptation.

## Relationship to GMT

SIT is the *operational specification* — the "how-to" — that connects GMT concepts to clinical delivery. GMT is the brand and framework; SIT is the protocol architecture underneath.

```
Gap Moment Training™ (framework)
  └── Structured Interoception Training (protocol architecture)
       ├── Interoceptive Attention Training (Mode 1)
       ├── Breath-Paced Autonomic Training (Mode 2)
       │    └── Cardiac-Anchored Breathing
       │    └── Resonant Breathing Frequency
       └── Graded Interoceptive Exposure (Mode 3)
            └── Contemplative Progressive Overload
```

## Design Principles

- **Causal contributor to** clinician durability (Randy's explicit framing — not "prerequisite for" but "causal contributor to")
- **60-second deliverable** — each mode can be practiced in [[neurominute|NeuroMinute™]] format
- **Biofeedback-closed-loop** — training is guided by objective physiological data (HRV, RMSSD), not subjective self-report alone
- **Progressive** — difficulty titrated to the clinician's current capacity, following [[contemplative-progressive-overload]] principles
