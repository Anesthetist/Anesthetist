# Alpha-Theta Crossover

The measurable EEG moment when theta power (4-8 Hz) exceeds alpha power (8-12 Hz), marking entry into deep meditative or hypnagogic states. A validation biomarker for somnistics protocols.

## Definition

Alpha-theta crossover occurs when the dominant resting frequency shifts from alpha (relaxed alertness) to theta (deep internal processing, hypnagogic threshold). In standard neurofeedback, this is a well-established marker. Randy's contribution is applying it as a *validation biomarker* for somnistics state shifts — specifically as the "Seal" confirmation marker in the Castle-Field Vision protocol.

## SRL Application

- A concrete, measurable EEG biomarker that validates whether somnistics practices produce the intended state shifts
- Trainable via neurofeedback: practitioners can learn to reliably enter alpha-theta crossover
- Trackable via Muse S headband at 256 Hz sampling in research contexts
- Measurable with Randy's personal pipeline: Muse 2 + Mind Monitor

## Distinction from Gamma-State Binding

Alpha-theta crossover marks the **entry** into deep states. [[gamma-state-binding]] marks **peak** integration — the moment when high-frequency gamma (>30 Hz) nests within the slower alpha-theta rhythms. The progression is: relaxed alpha → alpha-theta crossover → gamma binding within theta.

## In Castle-Field Vision Protocol

When combined with pupil constriction >= 0.3mm, alpha-theta crossover indicates the practitioner has achieved the integrated vitarka-vicara state. It is the objective confirmation of subjective reports of panoramic awareness.
