An advanced biofeedback and neurotraining platform designed to enhance a person's awareness and control of their internal bodily signals by making those signals externally visible and trainable. Randy coined the term "interoceptive_simulator_v1" to describe this technology concept.

## Core Mechanism

The interoceptive simulator bridges the gap between the autonomic nervous system and conscious awareness by:

1. **Amplifying internal signals** — Real-time capture of physiological cues (HRV, respiratory patterns, heartbeat) that are normally below conscious awareness threshold
2. **Externalizing the invisible** — Displaying internal rhythms as visual, auditory, or haptic feedback (e.g., a pulsing avatar synchronized to heartbeat, sounds matching breath)
3. **Guided training protocols** — Paced breathing exercises, heartbeat awareness tasks, and interoceptive attention drills that build accuracy over time
4. **Adaptive feedback** — System adjusts signal representation based on the user's developing interoceptive skill level

## Neuroscientific Basis

Impaired interoceptive awareness is linked to anxiety, depression, and stress-related disorders. Conversely, improving interoceptive skills strengthens emotion regulation and resilience. The simulator targets the brain's interoceptive pathway — particularly the insular cortex — by creating a "mirror" of internal state that users can practice responding to.

The mechanism leverages neuroplasticity: repeated attention to internal signals activates and strengthens the neural pathways responsible for interoceptive accuracy. What fires together, wires together.

## Applications

- **CRNA/clinical training** — Pre-shift calibration and intraoperative state monitoring
- **21-Day curriculum** — Progressive interoceptive skill building from basic breath awareness to full autonomic literacy
- **High-performance professional populations** — Any domain where reading internal state under pressure is mission-critical

## Relationship to SRL IP

The Interoceptive Simulator is the technology layer that enables the Five-Breath Re-Embodiment protocol and the broader Closed-Loop Biofeedback architecture. It is the "sensing and display" component; the Adaptive Intervention Protocol is the "response" component.
