# Gap Moment Literacy

The trained capacity to recognize a between-moment as a training opportunity and choose the training action over the passive defaults of mind-wandering or scrolling. Distinct from [[interoceptive-literacy]] (body signal perception), gap moment literacy is the perceptual and behavioral skill that lets a clinician or user see a 60-second window and claim it.

## Randy's Canonical Frame

> "A gap moment is an opportunity to train, so it's a moment between moments. You have 60 seconds; you can press a button, follow the directions, and train your nervous system instead of wandering or immediately scrolling."

Gap moment literacy is not primarily state classification. It is opportunity recognition plus action choice. A person can have strong interoceptive literacy (accurate body signal perception) and still have weak gap moment literacy: they notice the moment, but they fill it with a scroll instead of a rep.

## Definition

Gap moment literacy is the trained capacity to:

1. **Recognize** a between-moment as a training opportunity rather than dead time to fill with scrolling or mind-wandering.
2. **Choose** the training action over the passive default (open the app, press the button, follow the protocol).
3. **Classify** the current autonomic state into a named attractor (hyperarousal, hypoarousal, cognitive tunnel, dyspnea loop, task-ready) so the protocol matches the state (see [[titration-to-effect]]).
4. **Assess** whether intervention is warranted; not every gap moment requires a protocol.

## The Default Alternatives

Modern defaults for between-moments are mind-wandering and doom-scrolling. Both are:

- Autonomic continuity breaks without completion. Mind-wandering lets perseverative cognition ([[brosschot-2006-perseverative-cognition]]) run. Scrolling introduces new sympathetic load.
- Missed training reps. Thirty to forty gap moments per CRNA shift ([[gap-moment-training]]) sum to substantial cumulative practice volume when claimed.
- Neither rests nor trains. Gap moment literacy replaces both with a claimed minute of directed nervous system work.

## Distinction from Interoceptive Literacy

[[interoceptive-literacy]] is the broader skill of perceiving and interpreting internal body signals. Gap moment literacy builds on interoceptive literacy but adds two layers:

1. **Temporal recognition.** Seeing the transition window as it opens.
2. **Behavioral choice.** Acting on the recognition instead of letting the window close.

A practitioner can have strong interoceptive literacy (accurate heartbeat detection, precise breath awareness) but poor gap moment literacy (fails to recognize the window or chooses the scroll over the protocol). The reverse is less common: strong gap moment literacy typically requires interoceptive literacy as a substrate.

## Assessment in the SRB-60

Domain 1 of the [[somnistics-readiness-battery]] tests gap moment literacy through timed transition-detection scenarios where the trainee must identify gap moments, choose action over default, and classify autonomic state under realistic conditions.
