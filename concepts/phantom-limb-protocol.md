# Phantom Limb Protocol

An asymmetric concept emerging from Randy's structural excavation session (Feb/Mar 2026), identified as one of the three highest-leverage ideas in a full-spectrum repository audit.

## Seed Concept

Named for the neurological phenomenon of "phantom limb" — where the brain maintains a persistent neural map of a missing limb — the **Phantom Limb Protocol** extends the co-regulation framework into a new operational domain: **remote or asynchronous physiological influence**.

The core question it answers: *Can a clinician's nervous system be deliberately pre-loaded or "imprinted" with the co-regulatory signature of a stabilizing relationship — so that the regulation effect persists when that person is absent?*

## Theoretical Basis

The concept sits at the intersection of:

1. **Co-Regulation** (Porges/polyvagal): The nervous system is fundamentally a social organ — it reads neuroceptive cues from others and adjusts its regulatory state accordingly. This is well-documented in dyadic physiological synchrony research.

2. **Physiological Synchrony**: Research on provider-patient physiological coupling (interpersonal synchrony) shows that two organisms in proximity can entrain to each other's autonomic rhythms. The Phantom Limb Protocol asks whether this entrainment can be *internalized* as a persisting regulatory resource.

3. **Interoceptive Priming**: The practice of deliberately pre-activating a specific felt-sense state (e.g., safety, groundedness) before a stressor — essentially installing a regulatory "ghost" that can be recalled during demand.

## Potential Applications

- **Pre-shift priming**: Before a high-risk case, a CRNA deliberately activates the felt sense of a stabilizing relationship (mentor, co-worker, family) as an autonomic anchor
- **Team coherence training**: A surgical team builds shared co-regulatory imprints through brief, structured pre-case synchronization rituals
- **Remote clinical coaching**: A Somnistics/Pausality coach establishes a "regulatory anchor" with a clinician in early sessions that persists as a self-regulation resource

## Why This Is Asymmetric

Most wellness interventions address what happens *during* stress. The Phantom Limb Protocol addresses what gets *installed before stress* — a regulatory infrastructure rather than a reactive intervention.

This is NOT the same as visualization. It is a physiological imprinting practice — the target is the autonomic nervous system's stored "relationship maps," not the cognitive-narrative layer.

## Status and Next Steps

This is a seedling concept. It requires:
1. Literature review: interpersonal physiological synchrony + interoceptive priming research
2. Prototype design: a Pausality/Somnistics session module that operationalizes the protocol
3. Naming and IP consideration — "Phantom Limb Protocol" is evocative; may need a more clinical name for provider-facing materials
