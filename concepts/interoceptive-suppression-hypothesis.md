# Interoceptive Suppression Hypothesis

## Definition

Medical and nursing education systematically trains clinicians to suppress their own body signals. The hidden curriculum — stoicism, emotional detachment, powering through fatigue ("bladder of steel," "don't eat," "don't sleep") — views the body as a machine to be operated, not a sensor to be tuned. This suppression reduces interoceptive sensibility, alters autonomic metacognition, causes autonomic inflexibility, and is a biological contributor to burnout. Not a character flaw — a training artifact.

## Randy's Formulation

> "Medical and nursing education has systematically excluded interoception because it views the body as a machine to be operated, not a sensor to be tuned. They train clinicians to ignore their own signals... This suppression of interoceptive data is the biological cause of burnout."

## Thesis Structure

1. **The hidden curriculum suppresses interoception** — Clinical training rewards ignoring hunger, fatigue, bladder urgency, and emotional distress signals
2. **Suppression reduces interoceptive sensibility** — Years of signal-ignoring attenuates the capacity to detect autonomic state changes (measurable via MAIA-2)
3. **Reduced sensibility causes autonomic inflexibility** — Without interoceptive feedback, the autonomic nervous system loses its capacity for fine-grained self-regulation
4. **Autonomic inflexibility manifests as burnout** — What is called "burnout" is partly the downstream consequence of a nervous system that can no longer self-regulate because its primary feedback channel has been suppressed

## Why This Matters

This hypothesis reframes burnout from an organizational/psychological problem to a biological/training problem. The intervention point shifts from "better schedules and more resilience training" to "restore the interoceptive channel that medical education shut down." This is precisely what [[structured-interoception-training]] and [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] do.

## Testable Predictions

- Clinicians with more years of training will show *lower* MAIA-2 scores (especially Noticing, Body Listening, Trusting) compared to matched non-clinical populations
- MAIA-2 scores will inversely correlate with burnout measures (MBI) in clinician populations
- Interoceptive training interventions (GMT) will improve both MAIA-2 scores and burnout metrics simultaneously
