# Kosha Architecture

The Vedantic five-sheath (pancha kosha) model translated and applied to nervous system training within the somnistics framework. Each kosha represents a layer of human experience, and the architecture provides a structural model for progressive training — each layer builds on the previous.

## Five Koshas (Translated for CRNAs)

1. **Annamaya Kosha (Physical Body)** — Physical health, ergonomics, vitality. The tangible substrate. Targeted through posture awareness, movement cues, and physical tension release.

2. **Pranamaya Kosha (Energy Body)** — Vital energy via breath, pranayama, respiratory control. The primary training surface for somnistics. [[resonant-breathing-frequency]], HRV biofeedback, and [[neurominute]] protocols all operate at this layer.

3. **Manomaya Kosha (Mental/Emotional)** — Thoughts, emotions, mental patterns. Targeted through cognitive reframing, emotional labeling, and affective interoception within [[anterocept]] Domain 2.

4. **Vijnanamaya Kosha (Wisdom/Intellect)** — Deep intellect, intuitive decision-making, clinical judgment. The "clinical intuition" layer that experienced CRNAs develop. Targeted through [[multi-phase-interoceptive-coupling]] and advanced [[polyanchora]] practices.

5. **Anandamaya Kosha (Bliss/Fulfillment)** — Purpose, joy, meaning, resilience buffer. The deepest layer, accessed through non-dual awareness states and sustained practice. Provides the existential resilience that prevents burnout.

## Structural Principle

Each layer builds on the previous: you cannot access Vijnanamaya (wisdom) without first stabilizing Pranamaya (energy/breath). This nesting architecture maps directly onto the [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day progression and the [[anterocept]] three-domain spectrum.

## Evidence Base

- [[porges-2011-polyvagal-theory]] — vagal tone (Pranamaya layer) directly supports emotional regulation (Manomaya layer) and cognitive clarity (Vijnanamaya layer)
- The kosha model provides a pre-existing structural framework that maps cleanly onto modern neuroscience: physical → energetic → emotional → cognitive → transcendent

## Clinical Observations

- CRNAs resonate with the layered architecture because it matches their clinical experience: physical stamina → breath control → emotional regulation → decision-making under pressure → finding meaning in high-stakes work
- The Moongate Training Paradigm provides progressive mastery across layers
