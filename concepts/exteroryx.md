# ExterOryx™

A neuroscientific framework for training dynamic toggling between interoceptive and exteroceptive awareness. ExterOryx specifically targets the superior colliculus and multisensory association cortices — building both embodied self-awareness and environmental situational attunement as distinct neuroplastic outcomes.

## Three-Phase Protocol

1. **Internal Anchors** — Stabilized interoceptive awareness (breath, heartbeat, tension)
2. **Merged** — Synchronized pairing of internal + external signals (breath rhythm + ambient sound)
3. **Dynamic Toggling** — Rapid attentional switching under guided intervals between internal and external channels

## Key Distinction

ExterOryx differentiates two neuroplastic outcomes that other frameworks conflate:
- **Embodied cognition** — self-awareness through body-state monitoring (insula-mediated)
- **Spatial awareness** — environmental attunement through sensory integration (superior colliculus-mediated)

Repeated toggling builds BOTH capacities independently, rather than blending them into a vague "awareness."

## Neural Targets

- Superior colliculus (multisensory integration hub)
- Multisensory association cortices
- Temporal-parietal junction (internal/external boundary)
- Insula (interoceptive anchor)
