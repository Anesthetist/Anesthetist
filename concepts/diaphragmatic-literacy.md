# Diaphragmatic Literacy

The trained skill of perceiving and controlling diaphragmatic movement with precision — the positive counterpart to [[diaphragmatic-blindness]]. Where diaphragmatic blindness describes the deficit state (inability to sense or control the diaphragm), diaphragmatic literacy is the trained capacity that Somnistics protocols aim to develop.

## Definition

Diaphragmatic literacy is the ability to:
1. **Perceive** diaphragmatic movement in real time (interoceptive awareness of the muscle)
2. **Distinguish** chest breathing from diaphragmatic breathing under varying cognitive loads
3. **Control** breath depth, rate, and ratio through deliberate diaphragmatic engagement
4. **Maintain** diaphragmatic breathing patterns under stress without conscious monitoring

## Competitive Differentiator

Randy positions diaphragmatic literacy as a tangible skill not typically taught by mainstream mindfulness apps. While Calm and Headspace offer guided breathing, they do not systematically train the proprioceptive and interoceptive discrimination required for true diaphragmatic control.

> "Emphasizing 'diaphragmatic literacy' addresses a tangible skill not typically taught by mainstream mindfulness apps."

## Relationship to Anterocept

Diaphragmatic literacy is the specific skill that Anterocept Domain 1 (Baseline Interoceptive Awareness) aims to develop. It is the entry-level competency in the Anterocept progression — the foundation upon which more complex interoceptive skills are built.
