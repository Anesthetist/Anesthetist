# State Drift

## Definition

The progressive, often undetected loss of autonomic regulation during clinical work. Attention narrows, time perception compresses, vocal tone sharpens, and recovery between arousal spikes becomes incomplete. State drift is the proximal cause of most clinical errors — not ignorance, not incompetence, but the undetected slide from optimal to suboptimal autonomic state during performance.

## Randy's Formulation

> "I've watched the error pipeline from the inside. Most events aren't caused by ignorance. They're caused by state drift."

This reframes the entire burnout and clinical error conversation from a psychological/organizational phenomenon to an autonomic one. Burnout is not a character flaw or a staffing problem alone — it is the cumulative result of undetected, unmanaged state drift across shifts, weeks, and careers.

## Mechanism

State drift operates through incomplete autonomic recovery cycles:

1. **Arousal spike** — clinical stressor activates sympathetic response
2. **Incomplete recovery** — next task begins before parasympathetic restoration completes
3. **Baseline elevation** — resting autonomic tone shifts upward across the shift
4. **Perceptual narrowing** — elevated baseline reduces peripheral awareness and interoceptive sensitivity
5. **Error vulnerability** — degraded state produces the conditions for one of six [[error-archetypes-qrc|error archetypes]]

## Clinical Significance

State drift is the problem that [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] solves. By training clinicians to detect and correct autonomic drift in real-time — using interoceptive signals rather than external checklists — GMT intervenes upstream of the error pipeline.
