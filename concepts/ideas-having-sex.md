Matt Ridley's metaphor from The Rational Optimist: innovation happens when ideas from different domains mate and produce offspring that neither parent could have generated alone. Randy directed exploration of this as a lens on his own method — SRL as a "fertility lab for ideas" where anesthesiology meets contemplative neuroscience, nursing theory meets AI signal processing, and ancient pranayama meets machine learning.

## Biological Recombination Mapping

DNA (domain knowledge) → gametes (extracted principles) → fertilization (cross-domain collision) → gestation (synthesis and refinement) → offspring (novel concepts) → natural selection (clinical validation)

This maps directly to SRL's innovation pipeline: source knowledge → extraction → syntopical synthesis → concept creation → evidence validation.
