Randy's developmental model describing the non-linear arc from ego-driven agency through crisis and disillusionment to integrated wisdom-action. The resolution of the false polarity between effectiveness and awareness.

The developmental arc, drawn from Randy's own experience and clinical observation:

1. **NPC/default mode** — seeking agency for ego gratification
2. **Agentic Fool** — building agency through confidence, willpower, personal responsibility
3. **Crisis** — "something life-altering breaks the paradigm"
4. **Disillusionment** — old drivers (achievement, status) lose meaning. "When I realized my obsession with achievement was driven by unconscious low-self esteem, achievement became less interesting for awhile."
5. **Wisdom retreat** — "nothing is interesting besides consciousness land"
6. **Integration** — agency from a place of wisdom; "a beautiful synergistic loop emerges. Your consciousness work informs greater agency, and taking action supports continued consciousness development."

This is not a straight-line progression. Randy added a winding red line to Peter Limberg's agency-consciousness quadrant, showing the actual path oscillates — moving forward in agency, then sideways into disillusionment, then gradually integrating both dimensions. "It's not a straight line, but it's worth it."

The model maps onto Kegan's developmental stages (Self-Authoring → crisis → Self-Transforming), but Randy grounds it in embodied experience rather than cognitive abstraction. [[gap-moment-training]] serves as the physiological scaffold for navigating stages 3-6. [[clinician-durability]] is the professional expression of successful integration. [[self-remembering]] is the continuous practice that sustains the integrated state.
