The deliberate construction of knowledge in distinct layers — each layer building on and transforming the one below it. In the SRL vault: evidence (Layer 0) → observations (Layer 0.5) → concepts (Layer 1) → audience translations (Layer 2) → outputs (Layer 3). Each layer adds interpretation, context, and synthesis that the lower layer lacks.

Epistemic layering is what distinguishes a knowledge graph from a database. A database stores facts. A layered epistemic system transforms facts through progressive abstraction into actionable knowledge — while maintaining provenance chains back to the original evidence.
