# Dual-Use Technology Frame

## The Core Framing

Pausality and the Gap Moment Training methodology are **built by frontline workers, for frontline workers**, with a primary beachhead in nurse anesthesia and a clear adjacency pathway to other high-stress occupations where the same autonomic physiology governs performance and survival.

Randy's corrective (March 2026): "This may be classified as a dual-use technology built for frontline workers, from frontline workers, and for frontline medical workers, but also with application of interest to the Department of Defense and people in high-stress occupations: firefighters, air traffic controllers, pilots."

## The Physiological Bridge

The shared mechanism that connects all dual-use contexts is **autonomic state regulation under sustained high-stakes load**. A CRNA managing a difficult airway, a firefighter entering a structure, a pilot in an emergency, and an air traffic controller stacking planes during a runway incursion all face the same physiological problem: threat-induced sympathetic activation degrading precisely the cognitive precision their job demands.

The interoceptive protocols that restore a CRNA's executive function between cases also work for these populations because the nervous system doesn't distinguish occupational context — only threat load and recovery access.

## Randy's Clinical Background as Proof of Origin

- CRNA since 2004 (22 years as of 2026)
- Two decades spanning pediatric anesthesia, trauma, and transplant — among the highest-complexity, highest-stakes CRNA work available
- Pediatric anesthesia on very sick children with a team: requires the same regulation skills as any life-or-death frontline role

This background provides the "built by frontline workers" credential that differentiates Somnistics from wellness tech with no clinical grounding.

## Market Segments (Primary → Adjacency)

1. **Primary:** CRNAs, CRNs, anesthesia care teams, surgical nurses
2. **Near adjacency:** Emergency medicine physicians, trauma surgeons, ICU teams
3. **Mid adjacency:** Firefighters, EMTs, paramedics (first-responder vertical)
4. **Far adjacency — high strategic value:** Air traffic controllers, military pilots, special operations personnel
5. **DoD adjacency:** Any application to military readiness, combat stress inoculation, or force preservation

## SBIR/STTR Relevance

The dual-use framing activates federal funding pathways:
- **NSF Digital Health SBIR:** Novel devices for proactive, person-centered healthcare
- **NIH:** Stress/burnout prevention in healthcare workers (NIMHD, NIAAA angles)
- **DoD SBIR:** Military readiness, autonomic resilience, stress inoculation protocols

Note: SBIR/STTR programs lapsed October 1, 2025 pending congressional reauthorization. Expected to be renewed; David McFeeters-Krone (Intellectual Assets Corp, introduced via Ron Wiener) is the recommended resource for navigating the current status and optimal agency targeting.
