Real-time, during-shift nervous system training — not before-shift preparation or after-shift recovery, but regulation deployed in the clinical windows that already exist. This is the core differentiator from every wellness program that asks clinicians to meditate before or after work.

Pitch-refined definition from NurseHack4Health: "biofeedback-guided NeuroMinutes in 30-60 second clinical windows." The key insight: clinicians already have gap moments (between cases, during handoffs, waiting for induction). In-shift mental hygiene converts these dead moments into training opportunities.
