## Core claim

> "Interoceptive knowledge is either going to compound in your favor or against you. You get to choose if you know about it." — Randy Graybeal

Interoceptive development is not optional. It is a process already running in every clinician from their first case. The only thing that is optional is **awareness of the process** and therefore **the direction of compounding**.

## The two directions

**Compounding in your favor (conscious participation):** With a framework — pranayama, self-compassion, metta, yoga nidra, any structured interoceptive practice — each clinical experience refines the sensing apparatus. Arousal becomes signal. Moral injuries are metabolized. Recovery is structured. The clinician becomes more anti-fragile with each year. The compound interest works *for* you.

**Compounding against you (unconscious subjection):** Without a framework, the same clinical experiences still shape the interoceptive system — but through scar tissue, defensive numbing, accumulated sleep debt, and unprocessed moral injury. Vagal tone erodes. The window of tolerance narrows. Self-criticism deepens. The compound interest works *against* you, and you don't even have a name for what's happening.

## Why this is the Somnistics value proposition

This framing does three things no wellness pitch can do:

1. **Respects the clinician's intelligence.** It doesn't say "you're broken" or "you need help." It says there's a process already running and you can choose to be a conscious participant.

2. **Creates urgency without fear.** The compounding is already happening. Every year without the framework is another year of compound interest running in the wrong direction. But it's never too late — the existence proof ([[graybeal-counterfactual-28-year-interoceptive-shadow]]) shows the capacity is always latent.

3. **Positions the product as infrastructure, not intervention.** Somnistics isn't selling a fix. It's selling awareness of a process plus the tools to steer it. The clinician does the compounding; Somnistics provides the vocabulary and the protocol.
