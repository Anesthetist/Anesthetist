Act-to-learn under uncertainty rather than plan-to-execute under predictability. Drawn from Sarasvathy's effectuation theory and reinforced by Cedric Chin's Commoncog article on experimental design under uncertainty, which Randy pasted in full — signaling deep resonance.

Core principles: affordable loss (bet only what you can lose), bird-in-hand (start with what you have), crazy quilt (build partnerships from who shows up), lemonade (leverage surprises), pilot-in-the-plane (focus on controllable aspects). Randy's Four Questions framework operationalizes this for SRL's stage.
