Reframing clinical vigilance through Carse's infinite game lens: the goal is not to win each case (finite game) but to keep playing — to sustain the capacity for presence, compassion, and clinical precision across an entire career. This frame transforms vigilance from hypervigilant threat-monitoring (which burns out) to sustained attentive presence (which endures).

Named resonantly with Vigil — the AI orchestrator — and with the CRNA's core professional identity as the one who watches.
