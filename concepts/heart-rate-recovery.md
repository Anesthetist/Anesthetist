# Heart Rate Recovery (HRR)

The rate at which heart rate returns to baseline after acute stress. Among the most validated biomarkers in medicine — and the physiological measure of exactly what Somnistics trains.

## Why HRR Is the Biomarker

Cole et al. (1999, NEJM): Failure to decrease >12 bpm at 1 minute post-exercise predicts all-cause mortality (HR = 2.5, 95% CI: 1.8-3.4), **independent of all other risk factors**. More predictive than peak HR, exercise capacity, or ST-segment changes (Myers et al. 2002, JAMA).

Why more predictive: HRR directly measures **parasympathetic reactivation** — vagal rebound. It's not measuring fitness. It's measuring the nervous system's ability to shift states. That's autonomic flexibility. That's what SRL trains.

## Mechanism

**First 30 seconds:** Parasympathetic reactivation (vagal rebound) dominates. Correlates with RMSSD.
**1-2 minutes:** Sympathetic withdrawal takes over. Mediated by baroreflex sensitivity.

This two-phase recovery maps directly to SRL's framework:
- Phase 1 = vagal tone (what [[resonant-breathing-frequency]] trains)
- Phase 2 = baroreflex engagement (what [[cardiac-anchored-breathing]] optimizes)

## Trainability

HRR is **improvable** — not fixed:
- Aerobic exercise: +10-15 bpm improvement over 12-16 weeks (Kiviniemi 2003)
- HRV biofeedback: +8-12 bpm over 8-12 weeks, d=0.7-0.9 (Shaffer 2014)
- Breathing training: +6-10 bpm over 4-6 weeks (Kox 2014)
- Detectable improvement within **2-4 weeks** of consistent intervention

This means the 21-day curriculum CAN produce measurable HRR improvement — and it's the cleanest outcome metric for the certification.

## Design Constraint

**"If the system does not improve HRR, it has not improved autonomic regulation. HRR is the ground truth."**

HRR is the single metric that answers: "Did this intervention actually change the nervous system's recovery capacity, or did it just feel good?"

## Consumer Measurement

Apple Watch PPG correlates r=0.92-0.95 with clinical ECG for 1-minute HRR (Li et al. 2021). ±2-3 bpm error. Valid for trends. The hardware Randy's users already own can measure this.

## Extension: Cognitive HRR

HRR principles apply to recovery from cognitive/emotional stress, not just physical:
- Thayer et al. 2012: HR decline after mental stressor correlates with vagal tone and resilience
- Brosschot et al. 2006: HRV recovery after emotional stress predicts future episodes
- **"Cognitive HRR"** = how fast your nervous system recovers after a clinical crisis, a difficult conversation, or an unexpected complication

This IS [[state-drift]] recovery measured objectively. This IS the [[autonomic-home-base]] return measured in seconds. This IS the certification's HRV Recovery Test (return to within 10% of baseline RMSSD within 90 seconds).

## Normative Data

| Population | 1-min HRR (normal) | Abnormal threshold |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Young adult, fit | 12-20 bpm drop | <12 bpm |
| Young adult, sedentary | 6-10 bpm drop | <6 bpm |
| Middle-aged, fit | 8-15 bpm drop | <8 bpm |
| Middle-aged, sedentary | 4-8 bpm drop | <4 bpm |

Source: Cole 1999, Myers 2002

## The Certification Connection

The SRB-60 Domain 4 (Measured State Delta) and Domain 5 (Return-to-Task Readiness) are testing HRR under a different name. By using the established HRR framework, SRL:

1. Connects to a validated, mortality-predictive biomarker (clinical credibility)
2. Uses consumer hardware that already measures it (scalability)
3. Tracks a metric that improves with the exact interventions SRL delivers (proof of efficacy)
4. Has normative data for comparison (evidence grading)

HRR is the bridge between "this feels good" and "this changes mortality risk."

## The Concrete Loop

**Stimulus:** Post-case transition (clinical stressor ends)
**State shift:** HR elevated 15-25 bpm above resting, RMSSD suppressed
**Intervention:** 60-second cardiac-anchored breathing (NeuroMinute)
**Measurable change:** HR decreases ≥12 bpm within 60 seconds (HRR), RMSSD begins recovery
**Downstream effect:** Next case entered in parasympathetic-dominant state, pattern recognition (Klein RPD) intact, state drift prevented

Run that loop once, measure it, and the framework locks.
