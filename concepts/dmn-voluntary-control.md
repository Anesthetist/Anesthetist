# DMN Voluntary Control

The ability to voluntarily modulate Default Mode Network activity — not suppressing it entirely, but controlling **when** it is active. Randy's framing: the DMN is valuable for creativity and self-referential processing but needs to be switched off during clinical performance. The goal is a switch, not a kill.

## Randy's Framing

> "The DMN isn't always bad. It's also involved in creative thinking and problem-solving. So, perhaps the aim isn't to completely suppress it but to learn to control when it's active."

This reframes DMN from the typical meditation literature's "quiet the monkey mind" to a more nuanced clinical position: the DMN is a tool that should be available when needed (between cases, during recovery, for creative problem-solving) and quieted when not (during active clinical performance).

## CRNA Application

For CRNAs, an overactive DMN during a case means mind-wandering and attention lapses in a surgical environment where those lapses can be fatal. But between cases, DMN activity supports pattern integration, creative problem-solving, and recovery.

The neurofeedback pathway: real-time EEG monitoring allows CRNAs to see when their DMN is active and practice techniques to quiet it when clinically necessary. This builds on years of mindfulness meditation practice as a foundation.
