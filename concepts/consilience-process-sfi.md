# Consilience Process: SFI-Inspired Cross-Domain Synthesis Protocol

Recurring process for detecting structural isomorphisms across the vault's 7 domains: neuroscience, AI systems, business strategy, consciousness studies, expertise theory, architecture, capital markets.

## Operational Definition

Consilience is the detection of structural isomorphisms. Not "both involve X" but "both exhibit the same mathematical structure." A valid finding requires: structural homology (formally identifiable pattern), generative equivalence (mechanism maps between domains), predictive yield (non-obvious testable hypothesis), constraint consistency (respects each domain's boundary conditions), novelty (new to vault), and SRL relevance.

## Four Detection Lenses (from SFI methodology)

### Lens 1: Renormalization and Hierarchical Scale
When you zoom out and remove fine-grain details, what structure remains? Do two concepts from different domains map to the same renormalized description?

Example: Interoception (vagal afferent -> CNS integration -> motor efferent) renormalizes to "recursive self-sensing loop." Market microstructure (order placement -> matching engine -> feedback to participants) renormalizes to the same loop.

### Lens 2: Phase Transitions and Universality Classes
Does each concept exhibit a phase transition? Same order of transition, same critical exponents, same universality class?

Example: Polyvagal state shift and market regime change may both be discontinuous first-order transitions driven by signal quality, with hysteresis.

### Lens 3: Power Laws and Scaling Behavior
Do multiple domains exhibit power-law scaling with the same exponents? Same exponent suggests shared constraint or principle.

### Lens 4: Information Theory and Compression
What are the information bottlenecks? Do two concepts have similar information flow topology (where high mutual information regions map to each other)?

## Recurring Synthesis Task

### Weekly (Monday 9 AM)
60 min agent-driven discovery + 30 min Randy review. Four steps:

1. Data prep: query concepts updated in past 7 days + concepts with low consilience coverage
2. Multi-lens analysis: apply all 4 lenses to high-priority pairs, produce Consilience Scorecard (0-40 integrated score)
3. Filter: discard scores below 15, promote High Potential and Promising for review
4. Deliver Consilience Candidate Memo with predictions, evidence status, domain coverage gaps

### On New Evidence Ingestion
Quick classification (which concepts? how strong?), cross-domain query (update pending scorecard scores), notify if threshold crossed. Never trigger full analysis on single evidence note.

### On Explicit Nomination
Same-day detailed Consilience Analysis Report: all four lenses in depth with citations, mapping tables, testable predictions.

## Consilience Scorecard Format

Each lens scored 0-10. Integrated score 0-40.
- High Potential: 25+
- Promising: 15-24
- Low Signal: 8-14
- False Alarm: below 8

Each scorecard must include a top prediction: "If the consilience is real, we should observe..."

## Quality Gates (all six required)

1. Structural Homology: identifiable in formal terms (state machines, networks, compression ratios, phase space)
2. Generative Equivalence: mechanism maps intelligibly between domains
3. Predictive Yield: at least one non-obvious testable prediction
4. Constraint Consistency: respects boundary conditions of each domain
5. Novelty: new to vault and not standard in published literature
6. SRL Relevance: connects to Randy's core domains

## Parallel Thinking Protocol

Apply all four lenses simultaneously across multiple domains. Not serial. Multiple domain lenses applied to the same question at once, forcing genuine integration rather than post-hoc comparison.

## Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Metadata backfill. Add domain tags, core mechanism, renormalized structure to top 30 hub concepts.
Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4): First detection cycle. Run weekly synthesis on backfilled concepts.
Phase 3 (Weeks 5-6): Review loop. Randy reviews candidate memos, calibrates quality thresholds.
Phase 4 (Weeks 7-8): Production integration. Automated triggers on evidence ingestion, scheduled tasks running.
