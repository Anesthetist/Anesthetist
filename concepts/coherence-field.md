The emergent property of a team or clinical environment where multiple individuals are in autonomic coherence simultaneously. When the CRNA, surgeon, and nursing team are individually regulated, the collective operates at a higher level of coordination, communication, and error detection than any individual could achieve alone.

Coherence field extends the individual NeuroMinute to the team level — from personal regulation to collective regulation. This is the enterprise value proposition: organizations that train coherence fields perform better, retain staff longer, and produce fewer adverse events.
