# Awe as Regulation

The deployment of awe (in Dacher Keltner's sense — perceived vastness requiring accommodation of mental schemas) as a regulatory mechanism in high-stakes clinical environments. Awe reduces self-referential processing ("small self" effect), increases prosocial orientation, and is associated with increased vagal tone and reduced inflammatory markers.

## Definition

Awe-as-regulation is an SRL-original application of Keltner's awe research to clinical environments. It reframes awe from a rare, spontaneous emotion to a deliberately cultivable regulatory stance. The clinician who can access awe — "this physiology is extraordinary," "this team coordination is remarkable," "this work matters beyond my ego" — shifts from self-referential processing (which fuels emotional exhaustion) to self-transcendent engagement (which buffers against depersonalization).

## Mechanism

Awe shifts the attentional frame from ego-centric to allocentric:
- **Self-referential reduction:** "This is about me succeeding or failing" → "This physiology is extraordinary"
- **Schema accommodation:** The clinical moment demands cognitive updating, which recruits prefrontal engagement rather than amygdala-driven reactivity
- **Vagal activation:** Awe is associated with parasympathetic dominance and reduced inflammatory cytokines (Stellar et al., 2015)
- **Prosocial orientation:** Awe increases cooperation and collective awareness — relevant to OR team dynamics

## Clinical Deployment

Awe is deployable in OR settings through attention redirection:
- Noticing the complexity of human physiology during a case
- Recognizing the coordination sophistication of the surgical team
- Appreciating the weight and meaning of the work itself
- These are not sentimental gestures — they are attentional reframes that measurably shift autonomic state

## Relationship to Curiosity

Awe-as-regulation is the complement to [[curiosity-as-clinical-stance]]. Together they form a dual positive-affect intervention for [[clinician-durability]]:
- **Curiosity** reframes interoceptive arousal from threat to data (internal regulation)
- **Awe** recontextualizes the clinical moment as vast and meaningful (identity-level regulation)
