Randy's 8-layer slider system from beginner to extreme expert. Each [[neurominute|NeuroMinute™]] is adjustable across multiple axes of intensity and complexity. The architecture that makes composable NeuroMinutes possible.

A user-controllable complexity gradient within each NeuroMinute session. Users dial in their optimal challenge level for the moment, honoring [[minimum-effective-dose|minimum effective dose]] and [[titration-to-effect|titration to effect]] principles.

## The 8 Layers

1. **Audio voice guiding the breath practice** — foundational pacing
2. **Audio voice guiding awareness** — attentional direction
3. **Affirmation in the user's own voice** — personalized cognitive layer
4. **Binaural beat** — auditory entrainment
5. **Voice guidance to interoceptive element** — body-inward awareness (e.g., heartbeat, diaphragm)
6. **Voice guidance to exteroceptive element** — traditional sense awareness (sound, temperature)
7. **Combine interoceptive + exteroceptive awareness simultaneously** — dual-channel attention
8. **Add second interoceptive element** — advanced multi-channel somatic awareness

## Design Philosophy

The slider interface allows users to find their maximum threshold for the practice. A beginner uses Layer 1 only. An experienced practitioner stacks Layers 1-7. An expert runs all 8+ layers, training the kind of multi-channel awareness that mirrors CRNA clinical monitoring (simultaneously tracking vitals, surgical field, patient state, anesthetic depth).

## Randy's Voice

> "Create a spectrum of elements that can easily be added with a slider indicator on the screen from beginner to advanced to extreme expert. Allow layers to be explored by the user to dial in their optimal for that moment."
