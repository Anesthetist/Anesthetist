# Interoceptive Literacy

The ability of a healthcare provider (or any high-stakes professional) to monitor their own physiological state with clinical precision — the same measurement rigor they apply to patients, turned inward. The educational gap that the AANA Category A CE application identifies: providers monitor patients but lack skills to monitor themselves.

## Definition

Interoceptive literacy goes beyond basic interoceptive awareness. It is the capacity to:
1. **Detect** internal signals accurately (MAIA-2 "Noticing" + "Attention Regulation")
2. **Interpret** what those signals mean in context (MAIA-2 "Emotional Awareness" + "Body Listening")
3. **Respond** appropriately using trained protocols (MAIA-2 "Self-Regulation")
4. **Trust** the body as a reliable information source (MAIA-2 "Trusting")

It is the difference between knowing that "my heart is racing" and knowing that "my heart rate is 15bpm above baseline, I'm in sympathetic activation from the last case, and I need a 60-second vagal reset before the next induction."

## Measurement

MAIA-2 (Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness) 8 subscales provide the validated measurement framework. See [[maia2-pedagogical-mapping]] for curriculum integration.

- [[kerr-2013-mindfulness-body-alpha-rhythms]] — top-down alpha modulation as the neural mechanism underlying learned interoceptive literacy; body-first attention training builds alpha-gated body awareness

- [[ehmann-2025-mindfulness-cognition-ltm]] — long-term meditators demonstrate the full spectrum of interoceptive literacy: from basic noticing to complex multi-modal awareness
