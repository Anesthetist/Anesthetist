## Definition

Randy's specific combined protocol: HRV coherence breathing (measured via Polar H10 chest strap + Elite HRV app) layered simultaneously with binaural beat entrainment (gamma/theta/alpha frequencies matched to breath tempo), synchronized to the internal metronome of the heartbeat. This is the INTEGRATION concept — the vault has [[neuroharmonics]] (binaural architecture), [[resonant-breathing-frequency]] (coherence breathing), and [[cardiac-anchored-breathing]] (heartbeat-as-metronome) as separate entries, but not the combined protocol that is Randy's actual personal practice and the core SRL innovation.

## What Makes This SRL-Original

1. **Nobody else combines these modalities this way.** Existing products offer binaural beats (Brain.fm, Hemi-Sync) OR coherence breathing (HeartMath) — never layered together with real-time HRV measurement as the feedback loop.
2. **The heartbeat as internal metronome** — not an external clock. The coherence breathing is anchored to the user's own cardiac oscillation (see [[cardiac-anchored-breathing]]), which is then LAYERED with binaural entrainment.
3. **Randy practices this daily** using Polar H10 + EHRV Plus app. This is embodied knowledge from 28 years of clinical practice, not theoretical design.

## Protocol Architecture

The layering system (8 layers from beginner to extreme expert) provides the delivery mechanism:
- Layer 4: Binaural beat added to breathing pattern
- Layers 5-8: Interoceptive/exteroceptive awareness layered on top
- Each day in the 21-day curriculum pairs a specific breathing technique with a specific binaural beat frequency (e.g., Day 4: Resonant Breathing + Alpha 10 Hz for HRV coherence; Day 5: Pursed Lip Breathing + Beta 14-20 Hz for alertness)
