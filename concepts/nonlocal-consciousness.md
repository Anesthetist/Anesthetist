# Nonlocal Consciousness

The framework — advanced by Adam M. Curry and rooted in decades of research at Princeton's PEAR Lab, IONS, and the Global Consciousness Project — that consciousness is not confined to the brain but interacts with physical systems across space and time. In Curry's framing, this creates an "Intention Economy": a paradigm where focused human intention is a measurable, engineerable force that interfaces with technology and biological systems.

## Core Claims

- **Consciousness influences physical systems**: Nearly three decades of PEAR Lab experiments (1979–2007) demonstrated statistically significant correlations between human intention and the output of random event generators (REGs)
- **The effect scales collectively**: The Global Consciousness Project (GCP), started by Roger Nelson at PEAR in 1998 and now hosted by IONS, shows that shared human experiences (mass events, meditations) create detectable signatures in globally distributed random number generators
- **Intention is engineerable**: Devices like the Mind Lamp and apps like Entangled translate this research into consumer technology — making nonlocal consciousness effects measurable and trainable at the individual level

## Institutional Lineage

- **PEAR Lab** (Princeton, 1979–2007) — Founded by Robert Jahn, Dean of Engineering. The mothership for mind-matter interaction research.
- **IONS** (Institute of Noetic Sciences) — Dean Radin as Chief Scientist. Now hosts the GCP and continues PEAR's research tradition.
- **Global Consciousness Project** — Planetary-scale measurement of collective consciousness effects on random data.
- **Mind Matter Mapping Project** — Cross-disciplinary network connecting researchers worldwide. Published the Journal of Nonlocality (2002–2015).
- **Entangled** — Adam Curry's company building consciousness technology (Mind Lamp, Entangled app) based on the above research.

## SRL Relevance

The nonlocal consciousness framework reframes several Somnistics concepts:

- **Sleep as consciousness technology**: If consciousness operates nonlocally, optimizing sleep architecture isn't just about rest — it's about tuning the instrument of consciousness itself. Sleep state transitions become windows where the boundary between local and nonlocal processing shifts.
- **Anesthesia as a consciousness boundary**: 28 years of CRNA practice = 28 years of modulating the very boundary these researchers are trying to study. The transition between conscious and unconscious states under anesthesia may represent the "entanglement activation threshold" the MMMP's working groups are investigating.
- **Anterocept as nonlocal training**: If the body anticipates future stimuli (presentiment research), then Anterocept™'s progressive interoceptive training may be systematizing access to nonlocal information that the body is already processing subconsciously.
- **Intention as performance variable**: For organizations and clinicians, the quality of intentional state may be as measurable and trainable as any physiological marker — a frontier for human performance consulting.
