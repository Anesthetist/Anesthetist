A CRNA-specific adaptation of the MAIA-2 (Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness, 37-item, 8-subscale). Randy's unprompted product concept: "What if we did an MAIA 2-CRNA?"

## Design

Keep core MAIA-2 subscales intact for comparability with interoception literature. Add CRNA-specific language and context ("during a case," "while monitoring a patient"). Add 5-10 item extension module targeting Interoceptive Attention Under Load (IAUL-CRNA) and optionally Dissociation Under Threat / Freeze-Tilt.

## Four Design Goals

1. Maintain comparability with published MAIA-2 norms
2. Make items feel like CRNA clinical life
3. Inject load/risk into the construct (interoceptive awareness while responsible for another human under time pressure)
4. Use as Somnistics outcome + screening tool (pre/post for NeuroMinutes, early warning for freeze/dissociation patterns)

## IP Value

The CRNA extension module is the Somnistics-specific IP layer. Randy is not claiming to own MAIA-2; he is creating a profession-specific construct built on its foundation. Clear research pathway: validation study, publication potential, integration with the Somnistics Readiness Battery (SRB-60).
