# Tacit Knowledge Library

## Purpose
Randy's 28 years of clinical expertise are augmented by extensive deep reading across neuroscience, philosophy, mathematics, business strategy, and consciousness studies. The concepts absorbed from this reading form a substrate of tacit knowledge that shapes his pattern recognition and decision-making. This library makes that substrate explicit and accessible.

## Architecture

### Book Registry
Each book entry captures:
- **Title, Author, Domain** — basic catalog
- **Key Concepts Extracted** — the ideas Randy internalized (linked to vault concepts)
- **Cross-Domain Bridges** — where this book's ideas connect to other domains
- **Influence on Pausality** — how this book shaped product, strategy, or clinical thinking

### Concept Extraction Protocol
For each book:
1. Identify the 3-5 core concepts that shaped Randy's thinking
2. Create vault concept notes for each (if they don't already exist)
3. Link concepts to existing vault knowledge via SKOS relations
4. Note which concepts bridge to other domains (consilience fuel)

### Domain Categories (Expected)
Based on Randy's profile, likely reading domains include:
- **Neuroscience**: polyvagal theory (Porges), interoception (Craig), consciousness (Damasio, Tononi)
- **Contemplative/Vedantic**: yoga sutras, kosha architecture, pranayama, Advaita Vedanta
- **Category Theory/Mathematics**: compositional thinking, structure-preserving maps
- **Business Strategy**: Cedric Chin/Commoncog, strategy, entrepreneurship
- **Clinical/Medical**: anesthesia, trauma, critical care, resilience
- **Philosophy of Mind**: embodied cognition, enactivism, neurophenomenology
