# Wanton-Hamlet Agency Polarity

Two failure modes of human agency frame the entire Pausality intervention logic:

- **The Wanton** (Frankfurt): acts without reflection — fully driven by the salience landscape, no higher-order desire about their desires. Pure impulsivity.
- **Hamlet**: reflects without acting — paralyzed by meta-cognition, unable to step through into decisive engagement.

Both are failures. Real agency is the fluid, context-sensitive dialogue between embodied action and reflection.

## The Reflective Gap as Trainable Unit

Between Wanton and Hamlet sits the **reflective gap** — a moment of noticing what's salient rather than being consumed by it. The gap is not a destination:

- Too little → compulsion (Wanton)
- Too much → paralysis (Hamlet)

What is trainable is the **dynamic ability to size and place the gap appropriately** for the clinical moment.

[[gap-moment-training]] maps almost exactly onto this. The Apple Watch + AirPods Pro create the conditions for a contextually-sized reflective gap — "here's a gap" without demanding the CRNA freeze in it or blow past it. The device operationalizes the philosophy.

## Existential Resilience as Base Layer

Beneath the polarity is a more fundamental question: can you keep agency online at all during high arousal, conflict, and dysregulation? Can you keep meaning and personhood intact when your nervous system is attacking you?

That's existential resilience — and it can deform. The shadow is **isolationist resilience**: "nothing touches me." Agency as armor, not connection. CRNAs are professionally trained toward this. It calcifies into burnout, moral injury, and emotional flattening.

The corrective is eudaimonic orientation — resilience *for something*, not just *against something*. This is the design question that [[autonomic-diary]] and [[awe-index]] are built to answer.
