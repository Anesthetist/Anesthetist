Objective heartbeat perception accuracy as defined by Garfinkel et al. (2015) in the interoceptive trichotomy framework. One of three distinct dimensions of interoception, alongside interoceptive sensibility (self-reported) and interoceptive awareness (metacognitive correspondence between accuracy and sensibility).

## Garfinkel Trichotomy

1. **Interoceptive accuracy** — Objective performance on heartbeat detection tasks (behavioral measure)
2. **Interoceptive sensibility** — Self-reported beliefs about interoceptive ability (questionnaire measure, e.g., MAIA-2)
3. **Interoceptive awareness** — Metacognitive correspondence between accuracy and sensibility (calibration measure)

## Relevance to SRL

The SRB-60 competency domain "Micro-Interoceptive Skill" tests a form of interoceptive accuracy — the ability to rapidly sense, label, and calibrate internal state within a 60-second window. Understanding the distinction between accuracy, sensibility, and awareness is critical for valid assessment design.
