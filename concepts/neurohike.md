# NeuroHike

## Definition

A nature-based interoceptive training practice combining walking with layered breath protocols, CO2 tolerance drills, and cognitive reflection. An outdoor extension of [[neurominute|NeuroMinute™]] practices into longer-duration, embodied sessions.

## Core Elements

1. **Walking as autonomic substrate** — Movement provides a natural oscillation of arousal and recovery, creating a moving baseline for interoceptive attention
2. **Breath protocol layers** — Nasal breathing, resonant frequency pacing, and graded CO2 tolerance drills integrated into the walk
3. **Cognitive reflection** — Layered awareness exercises moving from body signals to environmental perception to insight capture
4. **Nature as co-regulator** — Natural environments provide low-demand fascination that supports parasympathetic recovery and attentional restoration

## Design Philosophy

Low-tech, accessible, embodied. No app required. No biofeedback device necessary (though compatible with wearable HRV monitors). The practice is the walk itself — the body moving through space while attending to its own signals.

## Content Format Potential

Originally prototyped as a content format where Randy records insights during actual hikes — combining the practice itself with real-time narration of interoceptive experience. A natural complement to the studio-based NeuroMinute format.
