The founder's felt-sense accuracy as a measure of product-market fit. When a founder's interoceptive calibration is high enough to reliably distinguish genuine market pull from wishful thinking, they have epistemic PMF — the embodied confidence that what the body is detecting in customer interactions maps accurately to actual demand.

## Distinction from Traditional PMF

Traditional PMF is measured retrospectively through metrics (retention, NPS, revenue growth). Epistemic PMF is a leading indicator — the founder's real-time somatic confidence that the product resonates, detectable before the metrics confirm it.
