## Source Quality Hierarchy

1. **SEC filings, FDA databases** — Verified, regulatory
2. **Crunchbase/PitchBook** — Generally reliable for funding data
3. **Major tech press** (TechCrunch, Bloomberg, WSJ) — Reliable with sourcing
4. **Industry newsletters** (Fitt Insider, Rock Health) — Good for trends, some estimates
5. **Company press releases** — Treat as marketing claims until verified
6. **Social media** — Signals only, never treat as confirmed data
7. **App store data** — Directional for traction, not precise
