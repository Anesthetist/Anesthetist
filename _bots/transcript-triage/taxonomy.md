# Transcript Triage Bot — Taxonomy

## Primary Categories

### clinical
Breathing protocols, HRV, polyvagal theory, interoception, neuroscience, meditation, autonomic regulation, vagal tone, sleep science, CRNA practice, anesthesia, pharmacology, somatic practices, embodied cognition.

**Vault layer:** concepts/, evidence/, observations/
**High-value signals:** Randy describing clinical observations, developing novel protocols, articulating mechanisms of action, connecting neuroscience to practice.

### strategy
Market positioning, go-to-market, investor preparation, competitive analysis, revenue models, fundraising, CAC/LTV, ARR planning, pricing, wedge strategy, category creation, 7 Powers, Commoncog frameworks.

**Vault layer:** observations/, concepts/market-intelligence
**High-value signals:** Randy applying frameworks to SRL specifically, developing positioning language, analyzing competitors with specifics.

### product
Pausality app, VagalBeats (predecessor), MVP features, UX design, Apple Watch integration, HealthKit, biometric feedback, protocol design, onboarding flows, subscription architecture.

**Vault layer:** observations/, outputs/
**High-value signals:** Design decisions with clinical rationale, protocol specifications, user flow decisions tied to neuroscience.

### framework
Consciousness studies, ontology, McGilchrist hemispheric lateralization, Vervaeke relevance realization, kosha architecture, category theory, IFS (Internal Family Systems), Spiral Dynamics, Ken Wilber, philosophical foundations.

**Vault layer:** concepts/, evidence/
**High-value signals:** Randy synthesizing across traditions, connecting philosophy to clinical practice, developing novel theoretical frameworks.

### personal
Kolbe, CliftonStrengths, Print, Dan Sullivan Strategic Coach, self-assessment, career reflection, grief processing, life planning, strengths optimization, founder identity.

**Vault layer:** Generally not vault-relevant unless it informs product/strategy decisions
**High-value signals:** Moments where personal insight drives product design or company direction.

### operations
Hiring, team management, meetings, task planning, ClickUp, file management, tool setup, administrative tasks, process design, weekly reviews.

**Vault layer:** Rarely vault-relevant
**High-value signals:** Only when operational decisions reveal strategic thinking (e.g., hiring criteria that reflect IP strategy).

### content
Blog posts, LinkedIn content, pitch decks, investor narratives, branding, taglines, marketing copy, website text, email campaigns, CEU course descriptions.

**Vault layer:** outputs/
**High-value signals:** Narrative frameworks that capture positioning language, pitch angles that crystallize the value proposition.

### research
Literature reviews, PubMed searches, bibliography development, evidence synthesis, study analysis, systematic review work, intelligence reports.

**Vault layer:** evidence/
**High-value signals:** Randy's interpretation of studies, connections between evidence and SRL concepts, identification of supporting or contradicting research.

## Secondary Category Rules

- A file can have up to 2 secondary categories
- Secondary categories indicate cross-domain synthesis (e.g., clinical + strategy = CRNA market positioning with clinical rationale)
- Files with 2+ secondary categories are often higher value because they represent integrative thinking

## Special Flags

- **NOVEL_CONCEPT** — File appears to contain development of a trademarked or novel SRL concept (Gap Moment Training, NeuroMinute, Anterocept, Polyanchora, etc.)
- **CLINICAL_OBSERVATION** — File contains Randy's firsthand clinical observations from 28 years of CRNA practice
- **EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS** — File contains systematic analysis of peer-reviewed evidence
- **IP_DEVELOPMENT** — File contains intellectual property development (patents, trademarks, defensibility analysis)
