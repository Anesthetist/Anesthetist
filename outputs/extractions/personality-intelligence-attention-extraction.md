**Body:** A reframing of attention from "spotlight selection" to "selective amplification + normalization under energy constraints." Drawn from convergent mathematics across 500M years of vertebrate attention circuits, chemical reaction networks, and transformer AI. The core claim: wherever a system cannot process everything, it learns to amplify a few signals and normalize the whole field. Selection is what it looks like; gain control is what it is.

Applied to Somnistics: Gap Moment Training is teaching a nervous system to solve the same optimization problem more skillfully. NeuroMinutes are "one-minute somatic gain adjustments for a nervous system under pressure." Interoceptive amplification (turning up the gain on diaphragm, HRV, cardiac pulse, localized tension) combined with somatic normalization (vagal/baroreflex cascades, network-level divisive normalization) produces apparent selection (subjective experience of calm and clarity).

This reframe distinguishes Somnistics from mindfulness/wellness ("pay attention to your breath") by positioning it as "teaching gain control under load" -- a precision-engineering framing rather than a relaxation framing.

**Randy's voice:** Randy shares the full Javier Marin article (TDS) and directs the conversation toward Somnistics application. He does not narrate the gain-control reframe in his own words, but endorses it by immediately asking "What if we did an MAIA 2-CRNA?" -- building directly on the framework. The mapping from transformer attention to interoceptive training is a co-creation between Randy's direction and ChatGPT's elaboration.

**Extraction confidence:** High. This is a core theoretical reframe that directly informs Somnistics product language, investor positioning, and clinical training design. The concept is distinct from existing vault concepts (interoception, gap-moment-training, neurogating) because it provides the mathematical/biological foundation for why gain control works.
