## A. New Concept Candidates

### A1. `broaden-and-build` (Evidence-grade concept — Fredrickson)
- **Definition:** Barbara Fredrickson's theory that positive emotions (interest, joy, awe, love) broaden momentary thought-action repertoires and over time build durable personal resources — skills, social bonds, resilience, meaning-making capacity. Distinct from hedonic positivity; this is a functional mechanism linking affect to cognitive flexibility and resource accumulation.
- **SRL relevance:** Core theoretical mechanism explaining WHY curiosity and awe protect against CRNA burnout. Curiosity broadens attention under interoceptive intensity; awe broadens identity beyond self-referential rumination. Both build durable resources that buffer allostatic load. This is the positive psychology backbone of clinician durability.
- **Randy's framing:** Randy asks specifically about "curiosity in the Keltner sense" and "the role of approaching an interoceptively intense moment with curiosity" — he treats broaden-and-build not as abstract theory but as a clinical intervention strategy for the OR.
- **Key mechanism (from transcript):** "Curiosity is not 'positive thinking.' In the affective science literature it's closer to the emotion of 'interest,' which shifts the brain from defensive narrowing to exploratory broadening."
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (broaden-and-build IS the positive affect mechanism within durability)
  - `skos:related` → `gap-moment-training` (curiosity stance during gap moments = broaden-and-build in action)
  - `skos:related` → `interoceptive-literacy` (curiosity converts interoceptive intensity from threat to data)
  - `skos:related` → `autonomic-regulation` (broadened cognition maintains prefrontal engagement under sympathetic load)

### A2. `awe-as-regulation` (SRL-original application of Keltner's awe research)
- **Definition:** The deployment of awe (in Dacher Keltner's sense — perceived vastness requiring accommodation of mental schemas) as a regulatory mechanism in high-stakes clinical environments. Awe reduces self-referential processing ("small self" effect), increases prosocial orientation, and is associated with increased vagal tone and reduced inflammatory markers.
- **Randy's framing:** Randy asks about "being of the mindset to be looking for 'awe' in the Keltner sense in terms of minimizing the risk of burnout for CRNAs."
- **Mechanism (from transcript):** "Awe shifts from 'This is about me succeeding or failing' to 'This physiology is extraordinary,' 'This team coordination is remarkable,' 'This work matters beyond my ego.' It reduces the 'small self' effect that fuels emotional exhaustion."
- **SRL relevance:** Awe-as-regulation is the complement to curiosity-as-reframing. Together they form a dual positive-affect intervention for clinician durability. Awe recontextualizes the clinical moment as vast and meaningful; curiosity reframes interoceptive arousal as information.
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:related` → `broaden-and-build` (awe is a broaden-and-build emotion)
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (awe counters depersonalization)
  - `skos:related` → `vagal-tone` (awe associated with parasympathetic dominance)
  - `skos:related` → `relevance-realization` (awe as salience recontextualization)

### A3. `curiosity-as-clinical-stance` (SRL-original concept)
- **Definition:** The deliberate adoption of curiosity as a regulatory stance during interoceptively intense clinical moments. Curiosity reframes internal arousal from threat signal to data stream, recruits prefrontal modulation, reduces anxiety sensitivity, and preserves cognitive flexibility under load. This is NOT curiosity as personality trait but as a trained, deployable clinical skill.
- **Randy's framing:** "What's the role of approaching an interoceptively intense moment with curiosity... in terms of minimizing the risk of burnout for CRNAs?"
- **Key mechanism:** "Curiosity is a re-labeling move that converts 'my body is freaking out' into 'my body is giving me data; what's it saying?' That reduces the odds of spiraling into rumination and 'I'm failing,' which are classic accelerants of emotional exhaustion."
- **Connection to Vervaeke:** Curiosity = controlled reflective gap. "Instead of 'This surge of arousal means danger,' you get 'Interesting. This is what sympathetic activation feels like in my body.'" Maps to Vervaeke's oscillation between embodied salience and reflective oversight (Wanton vs. Hamlet).
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:broader` → `gap-moment-training` (curiosity stance is a core GMT skill)
  - `skos:related` → `broaden-and-build` (curiosity IS the "interest" emotion in Fredrickson's model)
  - `skos:related` → `interoceptive-literacy` (curiosity requires and enhances interoceptive literacy)
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (protects against anxiety sensitivity → PTSD pathway)

### A4. `hormesis-durability-model` (SRL-original synthesis)
- **Definition:** The application of hormesis (biphasic dose-response: low/intermittent stress → adaptation; chronic/excessive stress → damage) to clinician career durability. The hypothesis: repeated, well-dosed exposure to challenge states + adequate recovery + reflective updating accelerates mastery and extends career longevity. Distinguished from naive "stress is good" by requiring specification of dose, timing, recovery, and response class.
- **Randy's voice:** "I really want to understand this to see if one can extend their CRNA career through practicing, through practical durability practices that cause each stressor to increase the speed of mastery."
- **Key literature anchors (from transcript, need verification):**
  - Mattson (2008). "Hormesis defined." Ageing Research Reviews. PMID: 18191812
  - Calabrese & Baldwin (2002). "Defining hormesis." Human & Experimental Toxicology. PMID: 12417417
  - McEwen (1998). "Protective and damaging effects of stress mediators." NEJM. PMID: 9535661
  - McEwen & Stellar (1993). "Stress and the individual." Archives of Internal Medicine. PMID: 8368228
- **Integration with antifragility:** Randy explicitly asks to integrate Taleb's antifragility with Vervaeke's existential resilience and hormesis into "the practice of clinician durability in a way that's not been explored yet."
- **Red-team insight (from transcript):** "The practical variable is not how high you spike during a hard moment, but whether you complete the recovery curve reliably and whether your baseline drifts upward over months and years."
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:broader` → `clinician-durability` (hormesis is the dose-response theory underlying durability)
  - `skos:related` → `autonomic-regulation` (recovery kinetics are the measurable variable)
  - `skos:related` → `titration-to-effect` (hormesis IS titration applied to stress exposure)
  - `skos:related` → `minimum-effective-dose` (hormesis requires MED thinking)

### A5. `allostatic-load` (Standard scientific concept — McEwen)
- **Definition:** The cumulative physiological wear resulting when stress-response systems are activated too frequently, too intensely, or fail to shut off efficiently. Four pathways: repeated stressors, failure to habituate, failure to shut off, inadequate response leading to compensatory overactivation. The "cost" side of allostasis (stability through change).
- **Randy's framing:** "I don't understand the idea of hormesis or allostatic load well enough. Quiz me until I understand it exactly."
- **SRL relevance:** Allostatic load is the enemy that clinician durability combats. CRNA career longevity depends on managing allostatic load through recovery practices, social safety, and meaning-making. It operationalizes what "burnout" actually IS at the physiological level.
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (allostatic load is what durability prevents)
  - `skos:related` → `autonomic-regulation` (regulation manages the recovery curve that prevents load accumulation)
  - `skos:related` → `hormesis-durability-model` (hormesis and allostatic load are complementary frameworks)
  - `skos:related` → `vagal-tone` (HRV is a marker of allostatic load vs. recovery capacity)

### A7. `existential-resilience` (Vervaeke concept applied to CRNA practice)
- **Definition:** The capacity to retain agency in high states of arousal, conflict, and dysregulation. Vervaeke's formulation: the dynamic oscillation between embodied salience (being in the moment) and reflective oversight (stepping back), calibrated contextually. Neither pure impulsivity ("Wanton") nor pure reflection ("Hamlet") — the dialectic between them.
- **Randy's context:** Randy shares Vervaeke's social media post directly and asks for clinical mapping.
- **Clinical mapping (from transcript):** Three failure modes under interoceptive intensity:
  1. Wanton mode: impulsive reactivity, defensive tone, cognitive narrowing
  2. Hamlet mode: over-reflective rumination, paralysis, disconnection from action
  3. Dynamic coupling: curiosity-calibrated oscillation between action and awareness = agency
- **Relationships:**
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (existential resilience IS durability at the philosophical level)
  - `skos:related` → `gap-moment-training` (GMT trains the dynamic coupling Vervaeke describes)
  - `skos:related` → `curiosity-as-clinical-stance` (curiosity is the mechanism of reflective gap calibration)
  - `skos:related` → `self-remembering` (Vervaeke's "non-dual witnessing while acting")

## B. Enrichments to Existing Concepts

### B1. `clinician-durability` — Major enrichment
- **New content:** Clinician durability now has three theoretical pillars from this extraction:
  1. **Broaden-and-build** (positive affect mechanism): curiosity and awe broaden cognition and build durable resources
  2. **Hormesis** (dose-response mechanism): well-dosed stress + recovery = accelerated mastery
  3. **Allostatic load management** (cost-prevention mechanism): recovery kinetics prevent cumulative wear
- **Antifragility integration:** Randy explicitly synthesizes Taleb + Vervaeke + IPNB + Sam Harris (Waking Up) into durability. Key insight: "You can have elite operators with very high acute arousal who do not burn out (good recovery kinetics, stable meaning, good team safety) and operators with moderate acuity who do (persistent vigilance, social threat, sleep debt, unresolved rumination)."
- **IPNB + Waking Up influence:** Randy states: "I think IPNB and Sam Harris Waking Up app have had a huge influence on my happiness as a clinician." This is a first-person durability testimony — the founder's own practice.

### B2. `gap-moment-training` — Enrichment
- **New connection:** GMT is now grounded in Vervaeke's reflective gap theory. The gap moment IS the space between Wanton and Hamlet — the calibrated reflective opening that preserves agency under arousal.
- **Curiosity stance:** Curiosity is the deployable skill that opens the gap moment without falling into rumination.

### B3. `interoception` / `interoceptive-literacy` — Enrichment
- **Dual interpretation model:** Interoception can be either threat amplifier or control signal. "When clinicians begin to fear their own arousal (anxiety sensitivity), stress responses intensify and generalize." Curiosity converts interoceptive signals from threat to information.
- **Literature anchor:** Allan et al., 2023 (anxiety sensitivity → PTSD symptom severity). Falconer et al., 2012 (cognitive control → reduced re-experiencing).
