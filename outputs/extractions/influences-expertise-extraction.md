## I. New Concept Candidates

### 1. `interpersonal-neurobiology-integration`
**Type:** concept (foundational theory)
**Definition:** Dan Siegel's framework that health = integration — the linking of differentiated neural, bodily, and relational elements into a flexible, adaptive whole. Vertical (brainstem-limbic-cortex), horizontal (left-right hemisphere), and interpersonal (self-other) integration all contribute.
**Randy's use:** Not adopted wholesale but used as the *architectural principle* for SRL. Randy maps Siegel's integration levels directly onto CRNA expertise development: novice = left-dominant rule-following; expert = cortico-limbic coupling; super-expert = whole-brain integration with vagal regulation. The "integration = health" formula becomes "integration = calm vigilance" in clinical translation.
**Source:** `dr-dan-siegel-ipnb.md`, `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 168-189, 262-268), `ipnb-reflection-and-practices.md` (lines 38-58)
**SKOS relations:**

- related: `co-regulation`, `polyvagal-theory`, `attractor-basin-dynamics`
**Status:** draft
**Do not confuse with:** `co-regulation` (which is one *type* of integration, not the whole framework)

### 2. `attractor-basin-dynamics`
**Type:** concept (SRL-original synthesis)
**Definition:** The nervous system develops stable "attractor basins" — habitual states it returns to under perturbation, like valleys in a landscape. Decades of regulated exposure to critical events sculpt deep basins for calm vigilance. Novices have shallow basins; small perturbations push them into sympathetic overdrive or freeze. Super-experts live inside wide, deep ventral-vagal basins where even large perturbations keep them within the zone of prosodic control and precise action.
**Randy's voice:** "I've never heard the term 'attractor basin'" — then immediately grasped its relevance to what he has been observing for decades in OR performance.
**Source:** `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 220-280)
**SKOS relations:**
- broader: `state-transition`
- related: `interpersonal-neurobiology-integration`, `polyvagal-theory`, `clinician-durability`
- narrower: `autonomic-home-base` (existing concept — the *trained* version of a deep attractor basin)
**Status:** draft

### 3. `state-drift`
**Type:** concept (SRL-original framing)
**Definition:** The progressive, often undetected loss of autonomic regulation during clinical work — attention narrows, time compresses, tone sharpens, recovery between arousal spikes becomes incomplete. State drift is the proximal cause of most clinical errors, not ignorance. Randy's formulation: "Most events aren't caused by ignorance. They're caused by state drift."
**Randy's voice (direct):** "I've watched the error pipeline from the inside. Most events aren't caused by ignorance. They're caused by state drift."
**Source:** `ipnb-reflection-and-practices.md` (lines 193-231), `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 554-600)

This suppression of interoceptive data is a proximal biological contributor to burnout.

## II. Enrichments to Existing Concepts

### `co-regulation` (existing)
**Enrichment:** The IPNB transcripts add mechanistic specificity. Co-regulation in the OR operates through: (1) prosodic vocal tone broadcasting safety via the ventral vagal social engagement system, (2) facial expressivity transmitting calm, (3) HRV synchrony between team members during critical phases, (4) shared attractor basins where multiple nervous systems synchronize around collective safety. Randy's QRC experience showed that when one CRNA regulates, the entire team's autonomic field shifts.
**Source:** `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 200-268, 456-461)

### `clinician-durability` (existing)
**Enrichment:** The interoception-for-high-performers file provides a testable operational definition: clinician durability = maintenance of (a) low burnout (MBI), (b) low perceived stress (PSS), (c) low trauma symptoms (PCL-5), (d) high autonomic flexibility (RMSSD/baroreflex), and (e) preserved cognitive control under acute stress. The interoceptive suppression hypothesis positions SRL's intervention at the *biological* rather than merely organizational level.
**Source:** `interoception-for-high-performers.md` (lines 87-103)

### `polyvagal-theory` (existing)
**Enrichment:** Randy applies PVT not as clinical therapy but as an *operating system taxonomy for clinician states*: ventral vagal = calm voice, precise communication, situational awareness; sympathetic mobilization = rapid induction, emergency response; dorsal vagal shutdown = emotional blunting, fatigue, burnout risk. Super-experts operate predominantly from the ventral vagal platform, maintaining physiological safety even while mobilizing for crisis.
**Source:** `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 196-206)

### `embodied-clinical-intelligence` (existing)
**Enrichment:** The CRNA expertise file provides the neuroscience substrate. ECI is encoded across autonomic, limbic, and cortical networks integrating perception, emotion, and action. At the super-expert level, this becomes "neuro-integrative mastery" — the nervous system itself functions as a precision instrument for sensing, predicting, and co-regulating complex systems. "Tacit mastery is embodied neurointegration — the living architecture of calm vigilance."
**Source:** `crna-expertise-and-challenges.md` (lines 170-178, 330-332)

### `gap-moment-training` (existing)
**Enrichment:** The criticality model file reframes GMT not as "mindfulness" or "wellness" but as "state access" — the ability to enter a metastable operating regime on demand in under 60 seconds. The competitive positioning: "Most regulation products promise comfort. You promise readiness. Most apps aim to calm people down. You help them stay functional while activated."
**Source:** `criticality-interoception-readiness-model.md` (lines 74-138)

### `maia-2-crna` (existing)
**Enrichment:** Randy maps MAIA-2 subscales directly to specific vigilance functions: Attention Regulation + Noticing = sustained focus under pressure; Self-Regulation = rapid autonomic recovery; Emotional Awareness + Not-Worrying = calm affect during crisis; Body Listening + Trusting = intuitive decision-making. Also: Randy shared his own MAIA-2 scores (Noticing 3.3, Not-Distracting 4.5, Not-Worrying 5.0, Attention Regulation 5.0, Emotional Awareness 3.8, Self-Regulation 4.5, Body Listening 3.7, Trusting 4.0) — these are Client 1 in the athlete questionnaire file.
