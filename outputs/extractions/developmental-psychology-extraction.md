## A. New Concept Candidates

### A1. `secure-attachment-to-reality`
- **Definition:** An epistemic orientation characterized by the capacity to metabolize overwhelming complexity — geopolitical, technological, existential — without ideological regression, moral panic, or dissociative collapse. Distinct from the psychological "attachment" to caregivers; this is attachment to *what is actually happening* in the world.
- **Randy's voice:** "This is an example of secure attachment with reality." (Said in reference to Dr. Pippa Malmgren's geopolitical writing — pointing to it as an exemplar, not defining it abstractly. Randy recognizes the quality in others.)
- **Key attributes (per ChatGPT's elaboration, to be validated):**
  - Emotionally regulated tone amid volatile content
  - Embodied cognition — keeps returning to physical referents
  - Mentalizes multiple perspectives simultaneously
  - Non-dissociative sense-making (neither denies horror nor collapses into it)
  - Antifragile modeling — sees structure of probable outcomes through chaos
- **SRL relevance:** Positions the *endpoint* of somnistics training — not just nervous system regulation, but the capacity to engage with complex reality from a regulated, discerning stance. A higher-order outcome beyond "calm."
- **Relationship to existing concepts:**
  - `skos:broader` → `autonomic-regulation` (regulation is necessary but not sufficient)
  - `skos:related` → `interoceptive-literacy` (felt-sense grounding enables reality attachment)
  - `skos:related` → `relevance-realization` (Vervaeke's framework — what matters emerges from embodied salience)
  - `skos:related` → `gap-moment-literacy` (gap moments as micro-training for reality-attachment capacity)
  - `skos:related` → `hemispheric-rebalancing` (McGilchrist — right-hemisphere engagement with complexity vs. left-hemisphere reductive certainty)

### A2. `agency-consciousness-integration`
- **Definition:** The developmental process of integrating high-agency effectiveness with contemplative/wisdom-oriented consciousness, following a predictable non-linear arc: ego-driven agency → crisis/disillusionment → wisdom retreat → integration of both. The resolution of the false polarity between effectiveness and awareness.
- **Randy's voice (direct quotes):**
  - "We don't magically wake up to being high agency sages."
  - "Most of us follow a predictable developmental pattern that I've seen time and time again, including in my own life."
  - "At a certain point in our development, this stops working. Usually something life altering happening that breaks our paradigm."
  - "When I realized my obsession with achievement was driven by unconscious low-self esteem, achievement became less interesting for awhile."
  - "After hanging out in 'nothing is interesting besides consciousness land,' previous high agency folks start to recognize that they are holding a false polarity between wisdom and effectiveness."
  - "A beautiful synergistic loop emerges. Your consciousness work informs greater agency, and taking action supports continued consciousness development."
- **Developmental arc (Randy's model):**
  1. NPC/default mode → seeking agency for ego gratification
  2. Agentic Fool → building agency through confidence, willpower, personal responsibility
  3. Crisis → "something life-altering breaks the paradigm"
  4. Disillusionment → old drivers (achievement, status) lose meaning
  5. Wisdom retreat → "nothing is interesting besides consciousness land"
  6. Integration → agency from a place of wisdom; synergistic loop
- **SRL relevance:** This IS Randy's personal developmental arc, disclosed openly. It maps the founder's journey and implicitly frames somnistics as tools for navigating this arc — especially the crisis-to-integration transition. Gap Moment Training could serve as the physiological scaffold for stages 3-6.
- **Relationship to existing concepts:**
  - `skos:broader` → `state-transition` (macro-developmental state transitions)
  - `skos:related` → `gap-moment-training` (GMT as micro-practice for the macro developmental shift)
  - `skos:related` → `kosha-architecture` (the arc maps to progressive kosha activation)
  - `skos:related` → `clinician-durability` (the agency-consciousness loop IS clinical durability at scale)
  - `skos:related` → `self-remembering` (the integrated state requires continuous self-awareness)

### A3. `kegan-developmental-stages`
- **Definition:** Robert Kegan's constructive-developmental framework describing how adults progressively transform their relationship to meaning-making: Socialized Mind (Stage 3) → Self-Authoring Mind (Stage 4) → Self-Transforming Mind (Stage 5). Each transition involves making "subject" into "object" — what previously controlled you becomes something you can observe and choose.
- **Source context:** ChatGPT maps Randy's agency arc to Kegan; Randy engages with the mapping without correction (implicit validation).
- **Kegan stages as mapped in transcripts:**
  - **Socialized → Self-Authoring (3→4):** Agency built on external standards, ego validation. Prefrontal-striatal dopaminergic loops dominate.
  - **Self-Authoring crisis:** Ego cracks under complex feedback. Cook-Greuter's Self-Aware stage. DMN becomes more salient; ACC + anterior insula heighten interoceptive error signals.
  - **Self-Transforming (Stage 5):** Holding multiple meaning-systems simultaneously. Flexible switching between DMN, Executive Control, and Salience networks. Higher vagal tone enables calm engagement.
- **SRL relevance:** Provides developmental psychology scaffolding for somnistics' user journey model. Kegan 4→5 transition maps to the shift from "doing breathing exercises" to "becoming someone who naturally self-regulates." This is the theoretical grounding for why GMT works differently at different developmental stages.
- **Relationship to existing concepts:**
  - `skos:related` → `agency-consciousness-integration` (Kegan provides the academic frame)
  - `skos:related` → `state-transition` (developmental transitions AS state transitions)
  - `skos:related` → `kosha-architecture` (progressive deepening parallels kosha layers)
