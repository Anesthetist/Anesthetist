# Somnistics Research Labs — User Research Analysis

## Introduction

This document summarizes the insights and strategic recommendations based on our initial user research within the CRNA community, specifically through the private CRNA-only Facebook group 'TNAG.' This group represents approximately 20% of the CRNA workforce. The goal is to leverage these findings to inform the development and marketing strategy for our MVP, targeting Apple Watch and iPhone users.

## Key Insights from the Survey

### 1. Engagement Metrics

- **Participation Rate:** Strong response indicates interest in tailored solutions for CRNAs.
- **Completion Rate:** High rates suggest the survey resonated well with respondents.

### 2. Common Pain Points Identified

- Stress management, burnout prevention, PTSD tools are top priorities.
- Clear gap in accessible, physiology-based tools for CRNAs.

### 3. Tech Readiness

- Many respondents already use wearable tech like Apple Watch.
- Interest in biofeedback exercises suggests strong receptivity to tech-based solutions.

### 4. Demographic Nuances

- Senior CRNAs seek tools they wish existed earlier in their careers.
- Younger CRNAs are more interested in data-driven and engaging features.

## Actionable Marketing Strategies

### 1. Refine Messaging

- Position Somnistics as a peer-led innovator: "Built by CRNAs, for CRNAs."
- Use "Imagine if..." storytelling to highlight the vision of proactive stress management.

### 2. Community-Centric Engagement

- Re-engage TNAG members with anonymized survey insights and MVP updates.
- Host Facebook Live sessions to foster dialogue and community input.

### 3. Apple Watch as a Gateway

- Emphasize seamless integration with Apple devices.
- Highlight benefits like personalized biofeedback exercises.

### 4. Early Access Program

- Launch an exclusive beta program for TNAG members with incentives.
- Offer free trials or premium features for early adopters.

### 5. Mentorship Loop

- Target senior CRNAs with messaging focused on equipping the next generation.

## Next Steps for Marketing-Product Synergy

### 1. Collaborate with Design/Product Teams

- Share survey insights to prioritize MVP features such as biofeedback exercises and wearable tech integration.

### 2. Create Engaging Content

- Develop explainer videos showcasing the MVP in action.
- Use real-life scenarios and testimonials for authenticity.

### 3. Launch a Beta Campaign

- Open beta sign-ups for CRNAs to gather further feedback and refine the product.

### 4. Leverage Data for Funding and Partnerships

- Highlight survey results to potential investors.
- Explore partnerships with Apple or mental health organizations.

## Final Recommendation: Building Momentum

By centering the narrative around community-driven innovation, we position Somnistics as a leader in addressing CRNA-specific challenges. This is an opportunity to create both a product and a movement that empowers CRNAs to take ownership of their mental health and resilience.
