# CRNA Ecology — Brief & Deck (April 2026)

## What This Deliverable Is

A paired long-form research brief and 28-slide .pptx presentation answering a diagnostic question rather than a descriptive one:

> **Not:** What is the CRNA ecosystem?
>
> **But:** What is the state of the art of ecology for CRNAs — what evidence do we have that we've discovered our ecology, and where are we still blind?

Framed through James Bridle's *Ways of Being*:

> "Every discipline discovers its own ecology in time, as it shifts inexorably from the walled gardens of specialized research towards a greater engagement with the wider world."

## Structure (Four Acts)

**Act I — The ecology we can see.** External ecosystem: species (CRNA / MDA / CAA / SRNA / resident / OR nurse / surgeon / anesthesia tech), biomes (AMC / community / CAH / VA / ASC / OBA / locum), energy flows (billing modifiers, TEFRA-7, stipend economy, drug shortages), territorial dynamics (AANA vs ASA), path dependency (Magaw 1906 → ABA 1938 → 2026 scope fight), and climate change (PE rollup aftermath, six climate forces).

**Act II — The ecology we can't yet see.** Interior ecosystem: the CRNA as a [[crna-as-closed-loop-controller|closed-loop physiological controller]]; interoception neuroscience (Craig, Critchley, Feldman Barrett, Farb); emotion (moral injury, second-victim burden, HRV as burnout predictor, ambient-scribe attention-mechanism reframe); breath (Feldman preBötC→LC, Balban/Huberman cyclic sighing, resonance breathing); and self-knowledge as clinical instrument.

**Act III — Evidence of discovery.** What the CRNA literature has (fragmentary external facts, generic MBSR burnout studies, QI-dominated DNP capstones) and what it hasn't (integrated ecosystem view, interoception neuroscience, HRV as sensor calibration, moral injury frame, second-victim structures, breath neuroscience, closed-loop controller model). Nagelhout 7th ed. (2022) has no interior-ecology chapter.

**Act IV — The frontier.** Six markers of a mature CRNA ecology: curricula for external + interior ecosystems, research treating CRNA self-regulation as a patient-safety variable, professional norms treating the CRNA nervous system as infrastructure, economic reforms beyond fungible billing units, and an integrative literature that maps external + interior ecology as one question. The slot exists and is mostly unfilled.

## Six Load-Bearing Findings

These are the findings the brief and deck are built around, each now represented as its own vault note:

1. **[[anesthesia-stipend-phase-change-2015-2025]]** — Hospital stipends: 2015 (~40–50%) → 2025 (>95%). Phase change, not cycle. The CRNA's most under-used lever.
2. **[[pe-anesthesia-rollup-aftermath-2018-2026]]** — USAP / NAPA / MEDNAX / Envision. Debt-financed rollups collapsing on the No Surprises Act + payer pushback + wage inflation. Employment terms downstream of capital structure.
3. **[[tefra-7-medical-direction-compliance-substrate]]** — The seven-rule CMS compliance substrate that produces ACT-model rigidity.
4. **[[nurse-anesthesia-historical-primacy-magaw-1906]]** — Nurse anesthesia predates physician anesthesiology as a formal specialty by two generations. Scope politics is apex-predator territorial marking, not historical deference.
5. **[[ambient-scribe-attention-mechanism-reframe]]** — JAMA burnout 51.9%→38.8% vs. ~16 min/shift saved. The mechanism is attention, not time. Interoceptive argument embedded in medicine's own data.
6. **[[crna-as-closed-loop-controller]]** — Mechanistic premise: the CRNA is a closed-loop controller whose primary sensor is their own autonomic nervous system. Sensor fidelity is a patient-safety variable, not a wellness concern.
