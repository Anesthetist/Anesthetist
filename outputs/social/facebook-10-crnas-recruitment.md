# Facebook Post — CRNA Groups

---

**The monitor reads 142. Not the patient's. Yours.**

Your hands are steady. They always are. The tube slides home. The vent cycles. The case is saved.

But your heart rate is still 97 when the next one rolls in.

Nobody taught us what to do in the sixty seconds between cases. We learned to manage every patient's nervous system with pharmacological precision — and nothing about managing our own.

I've been building a tool that changes that.

**60 seconds. Your Apple Watch. Your AirPods. Between cases.**

Breathing synced to your heartbeat — not a timer. You see your heart rate drop in real time. Not because you believe in it. Because the vagus nerve doesn't care what you believe.

I need **10 CRNAs** willing to try this for 21 days.

3 sessions a day. 60 seconds each. Your watch measures the change.

No cost. No commitment beyond the 21 days. You keep the data.

I want to know: does your heart rate recovery improve? Does the drift you can't feel at hour 10 become visible? Does having a word for it — *state drift* — change how you move through a shift?

If you've ever finished a 12-hour day and couldn't remember the drive home, this is for you.

DM me. 10 spots.

— Randy Graybeal, DNAP, CRNA
28 years. 10,000 cases. Building the thing I wish I'd had.
