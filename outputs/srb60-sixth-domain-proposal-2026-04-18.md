# SRB-60 Sixth Domain Proposal

Randy asked for expertise to help decide a 6th competency domain for the [[somnistics-readiness-battery]] and to lock tier pass criteria.

## The Three Candidate Domains

| Candidate | What it measures | Best fit |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ Tolerance via BOLT | Central chemoreceptor adaptation to breath-hold; indirect measure of autonomic flexibility | Already fits inside Domain 2 (Micro-Interoceptive Skill); not a separate domain |
| Co-Regulation | Capacity to regulate alongside a second nervous system | Better fit as Platinum instructor-track criterion, not a universal domain |
| **Stressor Recovery** | **Return-to-baseline after an induced stressor; time to reset; completeness of autonomic recovery** | **Universal domain; the clinically most predictive metric** |

## Recommended 6th Domain: **Stressor Recovery**

### Why this one

1. **The five current domains all measure within a session.** Recognize, sense, select, execute, return. Stressor Recovery is what happens between sessions, and it is the metric that predicts clinical outcome. [[awtry-2025-surgeon-autonomic-complications]] showed across 793 cases that clinician autonomic state at transition into a procedure predicted major surgical complications. The transition is where the outcome is set.

2. **It closes the loop the vault has already opened.** [[neural-transition-failure]] names the mechanism. [[gap-moment-training]] is the intervention. Stressor Recovery is the measurable outcome of the training. Without it, the battery tests skill under benign conditions and never checks whether the skill holds after a real stressor.

3. **Co-regulation and BOLT don't need a domain.** Co-regulation is an advanced teacher-track skill that fits naturally inside Platinum tier criteria. BOLT is a specific test that fits inside Domain 2.

### What Stressor Recovery tests

- An induced stressor under controlled conditions (cold pressor, breath hold to urge-to-breathe, cognitive load task, or standardized video clip).
- Measurement of: HR peak, HR return-to-baseline time, HRV recovery, respiratory rate stabilization, subjective arrival-at-baseline.
- Protocol execution during the recovery window (a NeuroMinute applied at the detected transition).
- Delta between unaided recovery and aided recovery.

### How it maps onto existing vault concepts

- Primary mechanism: [[neural-transition-failure]] detection and interruption.
- Primary training: any [[neurominute]] appropriate to the detected post-stressor state.
- Primary evidence: [[brosschot-2006-perseverative-cognition]] on carry-over, [[awtry-2025-surgeon-autonomic-complications]] on outcomes, [[bordini-2025-cognitive-errors-difficult-airway]] on transition failures.

## Revised SRB-60 Structure

The six competency domains:

1. **Gap Moment Literacy.** Recognize the window, choose the training action over defaults, classify autonomic state.
2. **Micro-Interoceptive Skill.** Rapid sensing, labeling, and calibration of internal signals. (Includes CO₂ tolerance via BOLT as a sub-test.)
3. **Protocol Selection and Fidelity.** Match protocol to state, execute with high adherence.
4. **Measured State Delta.** Produce measurable physiological change within the 60-second window.
5. **Return-to-Task Readiness.** Regulate without sedation; restore executive function.
6. **Stressor Recovery.** Return to baseline after an induced stressor; demonstrate aided recovery is faster or more complete than unaided.

## Tier Pass Criteria (Proposal)

| Tier | Pass Criterion |
|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Basic competency on at least 5 of 6 domains at minimum threshold. No domain at zero. Indicates foundational competency. |
| **Silver** | Consistent performance on all 6 domains with measurable state deltas across a rolling 10-session sample. |
| **Gold** | Rapid protocol selection (<10s) and high fidelity (≥90%) across all 6 domains. Stressor Recovery shows aided recovery at least 25% faster than unaided baseline. |
| **Platinum** | Gold-level across all 6 domains plus demonstrable co-regulation capacity (can deliberately shift a second person's HRV under controlled conditions). Instructor-candidacy threshold. |

## Platinum Disqualifiers

Any one of these disqualifies from Platinum regardless of other performance:

- Fidelity below 90% on any of the 6 domains in the certifying window.
- Measured State Delta less than 1 SD from pre-state on more than 20% of attempts.
- Stressor Recovery shows aided recovery equal to or slower than unaided baseline (the training must demonstrably help).
- Unable to co-regulate a second nervous system under controlled conditions.
