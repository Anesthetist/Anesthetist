Train your nervous system like you train your car.

The Problem

State drift — your nervous system degrades over a session without you feeling it. Lap times get inconsistent. Reaction time slows. You don't notice until it costs you.

The Tool

60-second breathing protocols synced to your heartbeat via Apple Watch. 3x a day. 21 days. Your pit stop for your nervous system.

The Edge

Drivers with higher heart rate variability show more consistent lap times and faster recovery after incidents. Train that.
