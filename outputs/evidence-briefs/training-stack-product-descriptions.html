Randy's daily autonomic training practice requires five separate apps, each targeting a distinct layer of the nervous system: biofeedback measurement, auditory entrainment, identity anchoring, guided progressive breathing, and deep parasympathetic recovery. Together they form a coherent stack. Separately they represent friction, context-switching, and a product architecture that has not yet been unified. This document maps each app in detail -- what it does, what the evidence supports, what hardware it requires, and precisely what Pausality should absorb from it.
Layer 1: EHRV+ Layer 2: Binaural Beats Layer 3: ThinkUp Layer 4: Pausality Layer 5: Yoga Nidra / NSDR Integration Vision
The Design Constraint
If the user needs five apps to train their autonomic nervous system, Pausality has not yet shipped its architecture. Every app on this list is a feature Pausality is missing. The roadmap writes itself from the gap.

HRV biofeedback training with real-time coherence visualization
Hardware
Polar H10 chest strap (ECG-grade). Also supports other BLE HR monitors.
Pricing
Free basic. Premium subscription for biofeedback features.
Rating
4.7 / 5 (App Store)
Developer
Elite HRV
Key Features
  • Morning readiness score (60-second HRV reading)
  • Real-time HRV biofeedback during breathing sessions
  • Resonance frequency breathing pacer (adjustable rate)
  • Coherence score visualization
  • Time-domain and frequency-domain HRV metrics (RMSSD, SDNN, LF, HF, LF/HF)

Evidence Base
Used in published research (EHRV research page lists studies). Gold-standard HRV accuracy with artifact removal. The Polar H10 provides clinical-grade R-R intervals that Apple Watch cannot match for frequency-domain analysis.
SRL Relevance
This is where Randy finds his resonant frequency and confirms coherence in real-time. The Polar H10 provides clinical-grade R-R intervals that Apple Watch cannot match. The coherence visualization -- watching the HRV wave smooth from chaotic to sinusoidal -- is the biofeedback loop that teaches the nervous system what "right" feels like.
What Pausality Should Absorb
Resonance frequency finder. Real-time coherence visualization. The visual biofeedback loop that shows the HRV wave smoothing in real-time. This is the transition from open-loop (Pausality measures but does not adapt) to closed-loop (Pausality sees your HRV and adjusts the session in real-time).
02
Layer 2 · Auditory Entrainment
Binaural Beats Generator
Customizable binaural beat generator across all frequency bands
Hardware
AirPods Pro 3 (air conduction required for true binaural effect)
Pricing
Free to $9.99 (varies by app)
Rating
4.5 - 4.8 / 5 (typical)
Representative Apps
Binaural Beats Generator, Brain.fm
Key Features
  • Selectable frequency bands: Delta (1-4 Hz), Theta (4-8 Hz), Alpha (8-12 Hz), Beta (12-30 Hz), Gamma (30-100 Hz)
  • Adjustable carrier frequency
  • Background mode (plays under other apps)
  • Timer functionality
  • Some apps include isochronal tones option
Evidence Base
Mixed. Ingendoh 2023 systematic review: 5 of 14 EEG studies support frequency-specific entrainment. Subjective effects (anxiety reduction d=0.3-0.5, relaxation) are more consistent than objective EEG changes. Combination with breathwork enhances HRV outcomes: Zhang 2021 found SDNN +22% combined versus +11% breathing alone. The evidence supports attentional anchoring more reliably than cortical frequency entrainment.
SRL Relevance
Randy layers binaural beats under his breathing practice. Through AirPods Pro 3, the interaural phase difference is preserved -- this is genuine binaural processing. Through bone conduction (Shokz), it is not: bone conduction delivers monaural beats plus cranial vibration instead. The functional value is attentional anchoring, not brainwave entrainment.
What Pausality Should Absorb
An adaptive audio layer -- NeuroHarmonics™ -- reframed as attentional anchoring, not cortical entrainment. Background audio that supports breath focus without overclaiming frequency-specific cortical effects. The scientific positioning matters: "auditory focus support" rather than "brainwave entrainment."

Layer 3 · Identity Anchoring
ThinkUp (Positive Affirmations)
Self-voiced affirmation recording and playback -- the identity layer
Hardware
AirPods or any headphones
Pricing
Free (3 affirmations) / $7.99/mo / $39.99/yr / $99.99 lifetime
Rating
4.7 / 5 (7,464 ratings)
Developer
ThinkUp
Key Features
  • 300+ expert-curated affirmations across 10+ life goal categories
  • Record affirmations in your own voice
  • Add personal images and background music
  • 21-day practice recommendation
  • Apple Watch + Apple Health integration
  • Daily notification reminders
  • Background music options (meditation, healing)
Evidence Base
Self-affirmation theory (Steele 1988) demonstrates that affirming personal values reduces stress response (cortisol) and defensive processing. Self-referential speech activates mPFC and PCC -- default mode network hubs. Hearing your own voice activates superior temporal sulcus self-recognition circuits differently than hearing others. No published RCTs specifically on the ThinkUp app, but the underlying mechanisms are well-documented in social and cognitive neuroscience.
SRL Relevance
The identity and intention layer. Randy uses self-voiced affirmations as autonomic anchors. The phrase "I know how to return to center" recorded in his own voice activates self-referential processing that reinforces the autonomic home base concept. This is not affirmation culture -- it is using the neuroscience of self-voice recognition to anchor regulatory intent.
What Pausality Should Absorb
User-recorded anchor phrases (5-10 seconds) that play at key transition points in NeuroMinute™ sessions. Not a full affirmation library -- a single, powerful, self-voiced anchor that bridges the identity layer to the autonomic layer. The recording is brief, the placement is precise, the mechanism is self-referential activation at the moment of state transition.

  • Three progressive tiers: Foundation, Awareness, Mastery
  • Real-time HR and SpO2 monitoring via Apple Watch
  • HR monitoring via AirPods Pro 3
  • Post-session recaps (HR journey, recovery speed, SpO2)
  • 60-second optimized session format (NeuroMinute™)

Evidence Base
Built on resonant breathing literature (Laborde 2022 meta-analysis, 223 studies), HRV biofeedback (Lehrer 2000), and vagal tone training (Thayer & Lane 2009). The 21-day progressive curriculum applies contemplative progressive overload -- the same principle that governs strength adaptation applied to autonomic flexibility.
Current Limitations (Honest Assessment)
  • Clock-based metronome pacing (not yet cardiac-anchored)
  • Measures HRV but does not use it to adapt sessions in real-time (open-loop, not closed-loop)
  • Static audio (no adaptive soundscaping yet)
  • No resonance frequency finder
  • No self-voiced affirmation integration
  • No deep recovery / Yoga Nidra mode
SRL Relevance
Pausality is the hub -- the central product everything else converges into. Its current limitations are its roadmap. Each of the other four apps in this stack represents a feature gap that, once closed, makes the external app unnecessary. The 60-second NeuroMinute™ format is the minimum effective dose constraint that disciplines everything else: whatever Pausality absorbs must work within (or extend from) this format.

Layer 5 · Deep Parasympathetic Recovery
Yoga Nidra / NSDR
Guided deep relaxation -- the recovery counterpart to activation training
Hardware
Any headphones
Pricing
Free to $9.99/mo (varies by app)
Rating
4.8 - 4.9 / 5 (typical)
Representative Apps
Deep Rest, I AM Being, Insight Timer
Key Features
  • Guided body scan and progressive relaxation
  • 10-45 minute session options
  • NSDR protocols (Huberman-influenced in newer apps)
  • Traditional Yoga Nidra lineage options (Satyananda tradition)
  • Sleep transition support
Evidence Base
Yoga Nidra reduces cortisol, improves sleep quality (PSQI), and decreases anxiety (d=0.5-0.7). Huberman popularized NSDR as a recovery protocol. The mechanism: complete parasympathetic saturation -- the deepest autonomic recovery state available without sleep. Distinct from meditation (which maintains awareness) and sleep (which loses awareness). Yoga Nidra holds awareness at the edge of sleep -- the hypnagogic threshold where recovery is deepest and consciousness is preserved.
SRL Relevance
The recovery counterpart to the activation training in Layers 1 through 4. Without deep recovery, progressive overload produces damage, not adaptation -- this is the hormesis constraint. Stress + rest = adaptation. This is the "rest" half of that equation, and the stack is incomplete without it.
What Pausality Should Absorb
A "Deep Recovery" mode -- 10-20 minute guided Yoga Nidra / NSDR sessions that complete the training cycle. Could be Days 7, 14, and 21 in the curriculum (weekly recovery gates), or a standalone mode accessible anytime. The recovery sessions break the 60-second NeuroMinute™ format intentionally: recovery requires time, and trying to compress it would violate the physiology.
