Scope Conflict Load
68% of new CRNAs report "significant stress" from scope-of-practice conflicts (AANA 2020). This is not abstract — it manifests as chronic sympathetic activation, measurable in HRV suppression and cortisol elevation. Financial stress from educational debt compounds the allostatic load (Lee 2021).

The Physiological Mechanism

Chronic burnout produces a blunted stress response — hypo-responsive HPA axis, flattened cortisol curves, suppressed heart rate variability. This is not a system that can be trained hormetially. By the time a CRNA presents with clinical burnout, the autonomic system has lost the flexibility that makes stress adaptation possible. Intervention after burnout is remediation. Intervention before burnout is inoculation.
