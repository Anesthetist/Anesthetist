## Definition

Interoceptive neuroscience studies the sensing, interpretation, and integration of signals from the body's interior — visceral, proprioceptive, nociceptive, and thermoceptive. It investigates how the brain constructs a model of the body's physiological state, how this model drives emotion, decision-making, and selfhood, and how interoceptive training changes these processes. This is SRL's intellectual center of gravity.

## Sub-Questions (Chapter Outline)

1. **Craig's interoceptive pathway** — Lamina I spinothalamic → posterior VMb thalamus → posterior insula → anterior insula. The "interoceptive cortex" as a map of the body's physiological condition. Craig (2002, 2009): the sentient self.

2. **Three dimensions of interoception** — Accuracy (behavioral task performance), sensibility (self-reported awareness), awareness (metacognitive correspondence). Garfinkel et al. (2015): the three-dimension framework. Why they don't always correlate.

3. **MAIA and MAIA-2** — Mehling's Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness. 8 subscales. Development, validation, cross-cultural work. SRL's MAIA-2-CRNA adaptation. How MAIA-2 maps to training targets.

4. **Interoception and emotion** — Barrett's constructed emotion: interoceptive inference → affect → emotion concept. Critchley & Garfinkel: interoception as the basis of emotional experience. Bridge to D06.

5. **Interoception and decision-making** — Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis. Werner et al.: gut feelings. Iowa Gambling Task studies. How interoceptive accuracy predicts better decisions under uncertainty.

6. **Heartbeat evoked potentials (HEP)** — Neural signatures of cardiac interoception. HEP amplitude as neural interoceptive processing marker. Pollatos & Schandry work. Bridge to D02.

7. **Interoceptive training** — Can interoceptive accuracy be improved? Mehling's MABT (Mindful Awareness in Body-Oriented Therapy). Price & Hooven (2018). How body scan, breath focus, and biofeedback change interoceptive processing.

8. **Interoceptive inference (predictive processing)** — Seth & Friston: interoception as active inference. The brain predicts body states; interoceptive signals are prediction errors. How this reframes SRL's intervention: training better body-state prediction. Bridge to D12.

9. **Clinical populations** — Interoceptive deficits in alexithymia, eating disorders, anxiety, depression, PTSD, autism. What these deficits teach about the role of interoception in health.

10. **The interoceptive turn** — How interoception went from niche physiological concept to central construct in affective science, psychiatry, and embodied cognition (2010-2026). The field's trajectory and open questions.
