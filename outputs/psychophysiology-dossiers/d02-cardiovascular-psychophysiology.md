## Definition

Cardiovascular psychophysiology studies heart-brain interactions — how cardiac activity both reflects and influences psychological states. Core constructs: heart rate variability (HRV), respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA), baroreflex sensitivity (BRS), cardiac output, and blood pressure variability. This discipline provides SRL's primary measurement language (RMSSD, HRV coherence, resonant frequency) and the physiological basis of HRV biofeedback.

---

## Sub-Questions (Chapter Outline)

1. **HRV: what it is and what it means** — Time-domain (RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50), frequency-domain (HF, LF, VLF, LF/HF ratio controversy), nonlinear (DFA, sample entropy). What each metric actually indexes. The Task Force (1996) standards.

2. **Respiratory sinus arrhythmia** — RSA as vagal index. Respiratory confounds (Grossman critique). Paced breathing controls. Why RSA is the "cleanest" vagal marker — and why it's not perfect.

3. **Baroreflex physiology** — Arterial baroreceptors. Baroreflex gain/sensitivity. Cardiovagal vs. sympathetic baroreflex. How resonant breathing frequency maximizes baroreflex stimulation (Lehrer's mechanism).

4. **HRV biofeedback mechanism** — Lehrer, Vaschillo & Vaschillo (2000): resonant frequency biofeedback. The baroreflex-resonance mechanism. Phase relationships between HR oscillations and breathing. Why ~0.1 Hz (6 breaths/min) is the typical resonant frequency.

5. **HRV coherence** — HeartMath's coherence construct. McCraty's work. Scientific vs. marketing claims. What "coherence" actually measures (narrow-band HRV power at ~0.1 Hz) vs. what it's claimed to mean.

6. **Cardiac interoception** — Heartbeat detection tasks (Schandry, Whitehead). Heartbeat evoked potentials (HEP). How cardiac signals reach consciousness. Bridge to D04 (Interoceptive).

7. **Blood pressure variability** — Mayer waves (~0.1 Hz). Systolic, diastolic, and mean arterial pressure variability. Relevance to the resonant breathing mechanism.

8. **Cardiovascular reactivity and recovery** — Stress reactivity paradigms. Heart rate recovery (HRR) as prognostic marker. Vagal rebound. Why recovery is more informative than reactivity.

9. **Cardiovascular psychophysiology of aging** — HRV declines with age. Baroreflex sensitivity declines. Clinical implications for different age demographics (CRNA career span, Don Katz 8th-decade archetype).

10. **Wearable cardiovascular monitoring** — PPG vs. ECG accuracy. Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin HRV measurement validity. What's research-grade vs. consumer-grade.
