## Definition

Clinical psychophysiology is the applied discipline of using physiological measurements (HRV, EEG, EMG, skin conductance, respiration) for assessment and intervention in clinical populations. It encompasses biofeedback (teaching voluntary control of physiological processes), neurofeedback (EEG-based brain training), and applied psychophysiology more broadly. The field's professional home is the Association for Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback (AAPB). SRL operates within this discipline.

## Sub-Questions (Chapter Outline)

1. **History of biofeedback** — Miller (1969) operant conditioning of autonomic responses. Green & Green at the Menninger Foundation. Sterman and SMR neurofeedback. How biofeedback moved from laboratory curiosity to clinical tool.

2. **Theoretical foundations** — Learning theory (operant conditioning of physiological responses). Schwartz's biopsychosocial model. Systems theory applied to self-regulation. Why biofeedback works: interoceptive learning, enhanced body-state awareness, autonomic reconditioning.

3. **HRV biofeedback** — Lehrer protocol. Session structure: baseline → paced breathing → resonant frequency identification → home practice. Dosing: 10-20 sessions typical. Evidence base by condition: anxiety, depression, PTSD, chronic pain, performance. Mechanisms: baroreflex, vagal, interoceptive.

4. **Neurofeedback modalities** — Traditional frequency-band training (alpha, theta, beta, SMR). LORETA neurofeedback. Infra-low frequency (ILF). Alpha-theta crossover (Peniston protocol). Evidence hierarchy: strongest for ADHD, moderate for anxiety/depression, limited for other conditions.

5. **Respiratory biofeedback** — Capnometry-assisted respiratory training (CART). End-tidal CO₂ monitoring. Meuret protocol for panic disorder. How respiratory biofeedback relates to SRL's breathing interventions.

6. **Psychophysiological assessment** — Stress profiling. Autonomic balance assessment. Surface EMG. Skin conductance. How to read a psychophysiological assessment report. What constitutes an evidence-based psychophysiological evaluation.

7. **The evidence base** — Yucha & Montgomery (2008): evidence-based practice in biofeedback. Tan et al. (2016): HRV biofeedback efficacy. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Where the evidence is level 1 vs. level 3-4.

8. **AAPB, BCIA, and professional standards** — Board Certification in Biofeedback (BCB). Certification in neurofeedback (BCN). Professional practice guidelines. How SRL's credentialing should relate to BCIA standards.

9. **Technology and tools** — HeartMath, Thought Technology, NeuroOptimal, Muse, Cygnet. Consumer vs. clinical grade. Where SRL's Pausality technology sits in the landscape.

10. **Future of clinical psychophysiology** — Closed-loop biofeedback (real-time adaptive). Digital therapeutics (FDA clearance pathways). Integration with wearables. Personalized medicine and psychophysiology. Where the field is headed (2025-2030).
