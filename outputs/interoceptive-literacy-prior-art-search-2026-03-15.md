## 3. Adjacent Concepts in the Literature

The following established terms occupy nearby conceptual territory but are distinct from "Interoceptive Literacy" as defined by SRL:

### 3a. "Body Literacy" (Price & Hooven, 2018)
- **Source:** Price, C.J. & Hooven, C. (2018). "Interoceptive Awareness Skills for Emotion Regulation: Theory and Approach of Mindful Awareness in Body-Oriented Therapy (MABT)." *Frontiers in Psychology*, 9, 798.
- **Definition:** "The ability to identify and articulate sensory experience." Taught as the first phase of MABT.
- **Distinction:** "Body literacy" in MABT refers specifically to the ability to *name* sensations. It is a single skill within a therapeutic protocol, not a progressive multi-stage competency framework. It does not include the interpret-respond-trust progression that SRL's "Interoceptive Literacy" defines.

### 3b. "Interoceptive Accuracy / Sensibility / Awareness" (Garfinkel et al., 2015)
- **Source:** Garfinkel, S.N. et al. (2015). "Knowing your own heart: Distinguishing interoceptive accuracy from interoceptive sensibility." *Biological Psychology*, 104, 65-74.
- **Definition:** A three-dimensional measurement taxonomy — accuracy (objective performance), sensibility (self-report), awareness (metacognitive correspondence).
- **Distinction:** This is a measurement framework, not a literacy or skill-development model. It describes what can be measured, not a pathway for how to train or develop the capacity.

### 3c. "Psychosomatic Competence" (Fazekas et al., 2020, 2022)
- **Source:** Fazekas, C. et al. (2020). *Wiener klinische Wochenschrift*, 134, 581-592. [DOI: 10.1007/s00508-020-01670-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-020-01670-5)
- **Definition:** "A set of mind- and body-related abilities which foster an adequate interpretation of interoceptive signals to drive health-related behavior." Includes 6 subscales: interoceptive awareness, mentalization, body-related cognitive congruence, body-related health literacy, general self-regulation, stress experience/regulation.
- **Distinction:** Closest conceptual neighbor. However, "psychosomatic competence" is a composite construct measured by inventory, not a literacy metaphor implying progressive skill development from novice to expert. Their "body-related health literacy" subscale refers to health literacy about one's body, not interoceptive skill per se.

### 3d. "Interoceptive Awareness" (Mehling et al., 2012)
- **Source:** Mehling, W.E. et al. (2012). "The Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness (MAIA)." *PLoS ONE*, 7(11), e48230.
- **Definition:** A multidimensional construct measured across 8 subscales (Noticing, Not-Distracting, Not-Worrying, Attention Regulation, Emotional Awareness, Self-Regulation, Body Listening, Trusting).
- **Distinction:** MAIA measures dimensions of awareness. It does not frame interoception as a "literacy" — a progressive, trainable skill analogous to reading — nor does it propose a novice-to-expert developmental pathway.

### 3e. Kelly Mahler's "Interoception Curriculum" (2019)
- **Source:** Mahler, K. (2019). *The Interoception Curriculum.* Self-published.
- **Definition:** A pedagogical framework for teaching interoceptive skills in 5 stages: noticing, naming, linking to feelings, understanding impact, managing.
- **Distinction:** Mahler's work is the closest to a progressive skill model but is framed as a curriculum (pedagogical tool), not a "literacy" (cognitive capacity). She does not use the term "interoceptive literacy." Her framework is oriented toward children and individuals with developmental differences, not clinicians or high-stakes professionals.

## 5. Conclusion

**"Interoceptive Literacy" as a named, defined concept does not appear in the indexed academic literature as of March 2026.**

No peer-reviewed paper, dissertation, thesis, book chapter, or grey literature source was found that:
1. Uses the exact two-word phrase "interoceptive literacy" as a defined term
2. Proposes a progressive, trainable skill model for interoception framed as a "literacy"
3. Defines a novice-to-expert developmental pathway for interoceptive capacity using the literacy metaphor

The closest constructs in the literature are:
- **"Body literacy"** (Price & Hooven, 2018) — a single skill (naming sensations), not a progressive framework
- **"Psychosomatic competence"** (Fazekas et al., 2020) — a composite inventory, not a literacy model
- **Mahler's Interoception Curriculum** (2019) — a progressive pedagogical tool, but not termed or framed as "literacy"

Randy Graybeal / Somnistics Research Labs' definition — "the progressive, trainable capacity to notice, interpret, and skillfully respond to internal bodily signals, framed as a literacy that can be developed from basic awareness through to expert fluency" — appears to be an **original coinage and conceptual contribution** that synthesizes elements from existing frameworks (Mehling's MAIA dimensions, Price & Hooven's body literacy, Mahler's progressive stages) into a novel construct with a distinctive framing.
