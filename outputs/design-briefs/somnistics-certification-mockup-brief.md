# Somnistics Certification — Design Brief

## What this is

A professional certification for healthcare clinicians (starting with CRNAs) in managing the human nervous system as an instrument of clinical performance. Six literature-grounded competencies. Built on the Pausality delivery platform.

The credential this leads to: **Somnistics Certified — Clinical Psychophysiology for Practitioners**.

## Positioning

**Preparation, not recovery.** Traditional wellness certifications train relaxation. This one trains autonomic flexibility, interoceptive literacy, and state regulation under clinical load. The outcome is measurable: HRV trajectory, MAIA-2 delta, BOLT score, burnout scale.

**Earn it through biometric verification, not attendance.** This is the first certification where every competency is assessed with a measurable physiological or psychometric threshold.

## The six competencies

Design should present these as a unified stack, each with four elements: **what**, **why it matters clinically**, **floor threshold**, **assessment tool**.

### 1. Interoceptive Literacy
- **What:** Detect internal signals, label them, trust them.
- **Why:** You cannot regulate what you cannot perceive.
- **Floor:** MAIA-2 Body Listening ≥ 3.0; heartbeat-detection accuracy above chance.
- **Canon:** Craig 2002; Garfinkel 2015; Mehling 2018; Khalsa 2018.

### 2. Attention Regulation
- **What:** Direct and sustain attention to the body under cognitive load.
- **Why:** Regulation requires a stable platform. Divided attention collapses it.
- **Floor:** Hold body-focused attention 60 seconds through one distraction event.
- **Canon:** Lutz 2008; Goyal 2014; Creswell 2017; Davidson & Goleman *Altered Traits*.

### 3. Voluntary Breath Control
- **What:** Diaphragmatic mechanics, CO₂ tolerance, resonant-frequency breathing.
- **Why:** The breath is the only autonomic input under conscious control. This is the primary lever.
- **Floor:** Diaphragmatic breath at 6 bpm for 5 minutes; BOLT ≥ 25 s.
- **Canon:** Lehrer 2013; Zaccaro 2018; Russo 2017; McKeown (BOLT).

### 4. Autonomic State Literacy
- **What:** Name your state in real time. Ventral vagal, sympathetic, dorsal vagal.
- **Why:** The right intervention is state-dependent. Relaxation techniques during dorsal shutdown can deepen collapse.
- **Floor:** Classify own state across 10 clinical moments with >70% concordance against HRV.
- **Canon:** Porges 2011; Thayer & Lane 2000; Thayer 2012; Grossman 2023.

### 5. Closed-Loop Feedback
- **What:** Own your trajectory. Training without measurement is ritual.
- **Why:** Clinical credibility requires measurable outcomes.
- **Floor:** Eight-week trajectory on one metric — RMSSD, BOLT, or MAIA-2 delta.
- **Canon:** Schwartz & Andrasik; Yucha & Montgomery 2008; Tan 2016; Lehrer 2020.

### 6. Safety and Contraindication Literacy
- **What:** Recognize and abort high-risk situations. Hyperventilation, retention risks, vasovagal cascade.
- **Why:** This is the domain that separates a credential from a wellness hobby.
- **Floor:** Pass a scenario test on 5 high-risk situations.
- **Canon:** Meuret 2008 (CART); breathing pattern disorder literature; Buteyko safety reviews.

## The irreducible chain

One-line framing for the hero section:

**Sense → Sustain → Lever → Name → Verify → Do no harm.**

Six steps. Six competencies. Each one gates the next.

- Abstract wave / topographic line patterns (thin, elegant)

### Voice
- Confident, direct, clinical
- Medical authority without clinical coldness
- Performance-training framing (not wellness)
- Skeptic-friendly ("Your watch will prove it")

### Phrases to use
Chaos to Coherence · Gap Moment Training · Vagus nerve activation · Interoception · Micro-preparation · Biometrically verified · Autonomic flexibility

### Phrases to avoid
Mindfulness · Relax / Relaxation · Wellness · Meditation · Calm

## Suggested page structure

A three-to-five page deck, in this order:

1. **Hero / credential pitch.** Headline, subhead, the six-step chain as a visual spine, one CTA.
2. **The six competencies.** One card per competency: icon, floor threshold, assessment tool, literature anchor.
3. **Assessment architecture.** How each competency is measured. MAIA-2, HRV, BOLT, scenario testing. The biometric-verification story.
4. **Delivery and trajectory.** Eight-week structure. What changes week by week. The measurement artifacts a certified practitioner walks away with.
5. **Who this is for.** CRNAs first; expandable to physicians, NPs, first responders.

## What the mockup should communicate in one glance

A clinician who can regulate themselves is a better clinician. This is the first certification that teaches regulation as a measurable, teachable skill stack — and verifies it through physiology, not attendance.
