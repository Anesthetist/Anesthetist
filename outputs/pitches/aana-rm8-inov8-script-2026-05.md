# AANA Rm8 iNov8 Pitch. Somnistics

## Format notes

- Six-minute spoken pitch. Conversational pace runs roughly 130 words per minute. Target 750 to 800 words to leave room for pauses.
- Three slides. Wonder question opener. Six-competency stack. Compound curve.
- Open and close with a wonder question. The pause after the opening question is part of the pitch.
- Voice: clinical authority without clinical coldness. Descriptive not prescriptive.

## Slides

1. **Slide 1.** White background. One line of text: *Without looking, how long since your last full exhale?*
2. **Slide 2.** The six-competency stack as a single visual. Interoceptive literacy. Attention regulation. Voluntary breath control. Autonomic state literacy. Closed-loop feedback. Safety and contraindication.
3. **Slide 3.** Two curves over a 28-year career. One flat. One compounding. Label: *Same clinician. Different instrument.*

---

## Script

**[Slide 1 up. Pause two seconds before speaking.]**

Without looking at a watch: how long has it been since you took a full exhale?

[Pause four seconds. Let the room try to answer.]

Most clinicians cannot answer. That gap is what I am here to talk about.

**[The lost signal]**

I am a CRNA. Twenty-eight years in high-acuity perioperative practice. I have monitored other people's nervous systems for hundreds of thousands of patient hours. Heart rate. Blood pressure. End-tidal CO₂. Depth of anesthesia.

My own nervous system, across those same hours, went unobserved. Not from negligence. From a kind of attentional anesthesia that clinical training installs. Attention outward. Signal inward muted.

This is not a personal failure. The literature describes interoceptive suppression as a documented pattern in clinical populations. Clinicians lose the signal. Burnout follows. Career trajectory bends.

**[The stake]**

Healthcare burnout has documented costs in the profession and in patient outcomes. Today's tools work downstream of the missing competency. Mindfulness apps. Employee assistance programs. Wellness offsites. None of them install it.

There is no certification anywhere in healthcare that teaches a clinician to manage their own physiological instrument. That gap is the opportunity.

**[The solution]**

Somnistics teaches wonder as a clinical skill.

Wonder is not a mood. It is a restored willingness to ask questions about your own physiology. Which nostril is open. How long a comfortable exhale runs. Whether your body softened or braced when you walked into this room.

A clinician who can wonder about these things can measure them. A clinician who can measure them can train them. We deliver the training through 60-second daily protocols with biometric verification on the wrist they already wear.

**[Slide 2. Six competencies.]**

We operationalize wonder across six competencies, each with a numeric floor drawn from peer-reviewed literature.

Interoceptive literacy. Attention regulation. Voluntary breath control. Autonomic state literacy. Closed-loop feedback. Safety and contraindication.

Six measurable skills. One unified instrument.

**[Slide 3. The compound curve.]**

Here is the strategic argument.

Interior skills do not add. They compound. A CRNA certified at twenty-four versus fifty-two does not get twenty-eight more years of practice. They get twenty-eight years of compound growth. Different instrument entirely.

We are not selling a wellness product. We are installing the substrate on which a clinical career compounds.

**[Defensibility]**

Three things combine here that no competitor has all of at once.

Clinical credibility. Built by a CRNA with twenty-eight years of high-acuity practice. The physiology is not imported. It is inhabited.

Pedagogical novelty. The program opens with destabilization, not instruction. A candidate who guesses at a wonder question is already more awake than one who did not know the question was pose-able. This is not a technique a competitor copies in a sprint.

Measurable outcomes. Every competency has a numeric floor. Every candidate walks away with a portfolio of their own data. Wellness programs do not ship receipts. This one does.

A fourth advantage rounds out the moat. Longitudinal compounding. Nobody else measures the twenty-year curve. We do, by design.

**[Traction]**

We are AANA Category A approved. Pausality is live in market with biometric verification through Apple Watch and AirPods Pro 3. A 14-day heart rate recovery test is enrolling now in the Heal the Healers community. Friends and Family round is at one hundred thousand of two hundred fifty thousand committed.

**[Ask]**

We are here for three things. The fifteen thousand prize. Advancement to Boston live finals in August. And a path into the AANA innovation network as the certification authority on clinical psychophysiology for nurse anesthesia.

Help us install this competency in the profession that monitors nervous systems for a living.

**[Closer. Slide 1 returns.]**

One more question before I close.

Which nostril is more open right now?

[Pause four seconds.]

Thank you.
