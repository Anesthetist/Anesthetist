The Gap

Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists manage autonomic nervous systems in operating rooms every day, yet receive zero formal training in sensing or regulating their own. At the WANA Spring Conference (March 2026), we polled ~100 CRNAs, students, and educators:

4%
Had any formal breath training (all from non-clinical sources: music, sports, yoga)
~0.5%
Recognized the word "interoception" (one hand, halfway, out of ~100 clinicians)
62%
CRNA burnout rate (AANA). 25% know a colleague lost to suicide.
0
CRNA programs with a structured interoceptive awareness curriculum

Interoception, the capacity to detect and interpret internal body signals, has 40+ years of research linking it to emotional regulation, decision quality, and burnout resilience. It does not appear in any CRNA curriculum. This is an unstudied population with a measurable, trainable deficit and an existing data collection infrastructure.

KDIS Alignment

The Brain, Learning, and Decision Sciences track investigates mental processes, behavior, and decision-making through neuroscience, cognitive science, and AI. SRL's research agenda sits at the intersection of interoceptive neuroscience (gut-brain signaling, body awareness), decision-making under stress, and biometric data science. Students gain access to a live clinical dataset, validated instruments, and a population that has never been studied for interoceptive training outcomes.

The Ask

We are seeking a faculty sponsor within the Kravis Department of Integrated Sciences to co-develop one or more of the above research projects. SRL provides the clinical population, data infrastructure, training intervention, and domain expertise. KDIS provides the neuroscience methodology, student researchers, and academic publication pathway. Every study produces co-authored, publishable findings in an area with near-zero existing literature. We welcome a conversation about next steps.
