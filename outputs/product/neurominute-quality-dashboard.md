## Quality Dimensions

Each NeuroMinute should be reviewed across six dimensions:

### 1. Script Quality
- Does the language follow SRL Communication Philosophy (descriptive, not prescriptive)?
- Is the pacing realistic for 60 seconds?
- Are instructions sensation-based ("notice the cool air") not command-based ("breathe deeply")?
- Does it include exit ramps and trauma-informed language?

### 2. Layer Mapping (8-Layer Slider)
- Is the base script written for Layer 1 (breath only)?
- Are layer-up instructions defined for Layers 2-8?
- Does each layer add exactly one new attentional dimension?
- Are the layers properly mapped to: Anterocept → SomnoAffinity → NeuroHarmonics → ExterOryx → Polyanchora → Neurogating?

### 3. Neural Targets
- Are specific brain regions, cranial nerves, and brainstem nuclei identified?
- Are the neural claims supported by evidence notes in the vault?
- Is the progression from posterior insula → anterior insula → prefrontal integration followed?

### 4. Biometric Predictions
- Are expected HR, HRV (RMSSD), RSA changes specified?
- Are the predictions grounded in the cited literature?
- Are they realistic for the technique and duration?

### 5. Evidence Links
- Does each NeuroMinute link to at least one evidence note?
- Are the evidence notes verified (DOI confirmed, status = review or canonical)?
- Is there a clear prov:wasDerivedFrom chain?

### 6. Randy's Clinical Review
- Does the script match Randy's 28 years of clinical intuition?
- Is the clinical_interpretation field populated with Randy's voice?
- Has the pharmacokinetic metaphor been applied where appropriate?
