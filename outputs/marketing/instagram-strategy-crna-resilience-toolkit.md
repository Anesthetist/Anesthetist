## Content Pillars (4 rotating themes)

### Pillar 1: Clinical Reality (Identity + Pain)
Content that makes CRNAs feel SEEN. The daily reality nobody outside the OR understands.

- "81 distractions per cesarean delivery. Every one of them hits your nervous system." (Voity 2025 data)
- "92.9% of ICU nurses report clinically significant fatigue. This isn't burnout. This is infrastructure failure."
- "The number one medication error in hospitals is a decimal error. Your nervous system affects your math."
- "You manage your patients' autonomic states for a living. When was the last time you managed your own?"

**Format:** Text-on-dark-background cards (brand navy + sage). Clean. No stock photos. The words do the work.

### Pillar 2: The Science (Credibility + Education)
Bite-sized science that teaches a framework, not an answer.

- "A JAMA Surgery study: surgeon autonomic state in the first 5 minutes predicted complications across 793 cases. The ideal state isn't calm. It's poised."
- "Your heart rate expands and contracts your experience of time on a beat-by-beat basis. You are literally a clock." (Arslanova 2023)
- "Nature just coined a word for what we build: interoceptomimetics — interventions that restore body-to-brain signaling."
- "HRV isn't a wellness metric. It's how fast your nervous system shifts from 'oh shit' to 'I've got this.'"

**Format:** Carousel slides (3-5 slides, swipe for the insight). First slide = hook. Last slide = "Follow for more" or toolkit CTA.

### Pillar 3: The Protocol (Value + Demonstration)
Show the product working. Don't tell — demonstrate.

- Video: Randy doing a 60-second NeuroMinute with Watch visible, HR dropping in real time
- "Before a trauma case. After a trauma case. Same minute. Different nervous system." (Mayo story, anonymized)
- "My teenager put on the Watch and said 'I had no idea my heart rate was that high.' After one minute: 'Did it go down?'"
- Time-lapse of HRV data across a shift with Gap Moments marked

**Format:** Reels (15-30 sec), screen recordings of Watch HR during breathing protocol, before/after HRV screenshots

### Pillar 4: The Invitation (Community + Download)
Build the tribe. Offer the toolkit.

- "If you've ever done 5 breaths before an intubation — you're already doing somnistics. You just didn't have the data."
- "We built a free toolkit for CRNAs. 5 protocols, the science behind each one, and how to use the 60 seconds between cases."
- Testimonial reposts (Mayo ARNP, teenager story, conference demos)
- "Tag a CRNA who needs this."

**Format:** Mix of cards + reels + carousel. Toolkit CTA in every 4th-5th post.
