## Somnistics Research Labs is looking for student collaborators.

### The Problem

Healthcare workers are burning out at record rates. 50 to 66 percent of nurses report burnout symptoms. Turnover costs hospitals $4,000 to $20,000 per clinician per year. The people who keep us alive in operating rooms, emergency departments, and intensive care units have no tools that work in the time they actually have.

Meditation apps tell them to find 20 quiet minutes. They have 60 seconds between cases. Nobody has built what they need.

### What We Built

Somnistics Research Labs is a Bellevue, Washington health tech company building clinical-grade nervous system regulation tools. Our first product, Pausality, is a 60-second breathing app for Apple Watch and AirPods Pro. It reads your heart rate and blood oxygen in real time and adapts each session to your body. Your physiology drives the protocol, not a timer. Measurable recovery, verified on your wrist.

Pausality is live in the App Store right now. pausality.health

### What We Have Built Beyond the App

A structured clinical knowledge graph with 956 notes, 165 validated concepts, and 472 evidence sources. Built to library science standards (Dublin Core, SKOS, PROV-O). Every claim traces to a source. Every concept links to related concepts. This is not a document folder. It is a living knowledge platform that an AI system can query, traverse, and build on.

We built this in 17 days using AI tools. An orchestrator we call Vigil operates as a digital librarian with 10 specialized bot protocols and 18 custom tools. It extracts knowledge, writes structured notes, resolves citations, scans competitive landscapes, and generates research essays from the graph automatically.

The knowledge graph is the long-term asset. It powers protocol generation for Pausality today. It will train a clinical AI tomorrow.

### What We Are Working On

Five untapped domains of clinical competency that nobody is training:

1. **Applied breath training** for clinical environments (60-second protocols with biometric feedback)
2. **Inner dialogue training** for performance under pressure (self-talk as a trainable clinical skill)
3. **Interoceptive awareness** across eight measurable dimensions (the science of sensing your own body)
4. **Emotional granularity and amplitude training** (precision in identifying and feeling emotional states)
5. **Somatic discharge and peer recovery** (physical release practices for teams after high-stress cases)

We have an IRB pilot protocol drafted. Research collaborations with VCU. A nursing theory with 13 falsifiable hypotheses. A product in the App Store. And a knowledge graph that grows every day.
