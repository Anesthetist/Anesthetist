What Changed
Embodied metacognition Keystone
From zero vault presence to keystone concept. Every other concept now traces through it. This is the hub node that connects interoception, autonomic regulation, clinical expertise, and somatic practice into a single coherent framework. It did not exist in the vault five days ago.
Polyvagal Theory reframing required Needs Surgery
Grossman 2026 with 38 co-authors. The vault's most-referenced theory needs surgery. The Neurovisceral Integration model (Thayer) is the replacement. PVT language must be systematically downgraded from theoretical framework to clinical metaphor across all notes.
NeuroHarmonics credibility risk Review Required
Only 5 of 14 studies support binaural beat entrainment. Canonical status may be premature. The evidence base is thinner than the vault currently represents. Requires honest downgrade or scope narrowing before any clinical-facing output references it.
Three concepts survived deep validation Load-Bearing
Resonant breathing frequency (meta-analytic support). HRV biofeedback (RCT-level evidence). Interoception (replicated across labs). These three are the foundation. Everything else in the vault is scaffolding around them.
Heart Rate Recovery as ground truth biomarker Game Changer
Cole 1999 NEJM: hazard ratio of 2.5 for all-cause mortality. Measurable on Apple Watch. No lab required. This changes the certification from subjective self-report to objective physiological measurement. Day 0, Day 7, Day 21.
ISH confirmed as wide-open field Blue Ocean
Interoceptive Suppression Hypothesis: zero PubMed competitors for the intersection of interoceptive suppression and burnout in clinicians. The field literally does not exist yet. First-mover advantage is real and the evidence threads are already in the vault.

Competitive Landscape Update
Jhourney $4K retreats
Premium jhana meditation retreats. 40 hours of guided practice at $4,000 per seat. SRL delivers equivalent autonomic shift measurement in 63 minutes. The question is not whether the experience is different — it is whether the biomarker moves the same way.

Fractal breathing methodology. Compelling brand, zero peer-reviewed citations. The marketing is ahead of the evidence. This is exactly the credibility gap SRL is designed to exploit — by not having one.

Dave Asprey's neurofeedback intensive. $16,000 price point. Zero published studies. The premium pricing signals confidence, but the evidence gap is a liability that grows as the field matures.

200,000+ SPECT scans. Massive dataset, significant APA criticism of diagnostic methodology. The scale is impressive; the peer-review resistance is a cautionary tale for any neuroimaging-adjacent claim.
The Three Actions
Action 1 — Get 10 CRNAs
The Facebook post is written. Ten spots. A 21-day protocol with pre/post HRR measurement. Not a sales pitch — a research invitation. Clinical peers testing a clinical hypothesis. The post goes up Tuesday morning.
Action 2 — Measure HRR

What Died
V1 landing page attempt Killed
Sloppy. Did not meet the bar. Jason's standard applies: if it does not look like it was made by someone who cares about every pixel, it communicates the opposite of clinical precision. Scrapped and restarted.
Full dark mode as default format Killed
Too harsh for extended reading. The design system works for intelligence reports and dashboards, but long-form clinical content needs a lighter touch. Dark mode remains for reports; light mode for educational material.
Overclaiming novelty Killed
"Doesn't exist" became "operationalizes an integration." The vault taught us that most of what we thought was novel is actually a new synthesis of existing work. The honest claim is stronger than the inflated one.
The system as intelligence-only Killed
A knowledge graph that does not produce action is a library, not a weapon. The vault must tilt from intelligence accumulation to action propagation. The three actions above are the proof that this shift happened.
