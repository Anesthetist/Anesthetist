## 1. Company Overview

Somnistics Research Labs is a Bellevue, Washington health technology company building clinical-grade nervous system regulation tools for high-performance professionals. Delaware C-corp. Founded by Randy Graybeal, a 28-year Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist, and Jason Fields, former VP of User Experience at Audible/Amazon and Head of UX at BBC iPlayer.

The first product is Pausality, a 60-second breathing app for Apple Watch and AirPods Pro that reads real-time heart rate and SpO2 and adapts each session to the user's body. The user's physiology drives the protocol, not a timer. The result is measurable nervous system recovery verified on the wrist, not self-reported calm.

Pausality is live in the App Store at pausality.health. It is in use by a trauma and resuscitation team at Mayo Clinic, introduced through a clinical advisor on staff there.

The company is developing Somnistics Certification, an accredited continuing education program for nurse anesthesiologists, as the enterprise distribution channel. 54,000 CRNAs need continuing education hours annually to maintain licensure.

## 5. Revenue Model

Three revenue streams:

1. **Consumer subscriptions.** Pausality at $12.99/month or $49.99/year in the App Store. Biometrically adaptive breathing sessions with real-time heart rate and HRV feedback on Apple Watch.

2. **Somnistics Certification.** Accredited continuing education for nurse anesthesiologists, sold to individuals and purchased by hospital systems for clinical teams. Recurring revenue built into a professional licensure requirement.

3. **Enterprise licensing.** Hospital systems purchase Pausality for departments bundled with Somnistics Certification and workforce resilience analytics for the Chief Nursing Officer.

Consumer subscriptions prove the product. Certification opens the door. Enterprise licensing scales the revenue.

## 6. Competitive Landscape — Three Layers

### The Core Insight

Six of the largest companies in the world launched health AI products in the last 90 days. Every one of them is a data layer. None of them is an intervention layer. Pausality is the intervention layer.

### Layer 1: Big Tech Builds the Data Layer

| Company | What they launched | What they cannot do |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| OpenAI / ChatGPT Health | Apple Health + medical records, 230M weekly queries | Clinical intervention protocols |
| Microsoft Copilot Health | Apple Health + Oura + Fitbit + 50,000 US EHRs | Guided breathing, biometric verification |
| Google Gemini Health Coach | 24/7 coaching, personal health LLM | Workflow-embedded micro-interventions |
| Samsung Brain Health | Cognitive health via watch and ring | Autonomic regulation training |
| Meta | Smartwatch + Meta AI, neural wristband | Clinical protocols |
| Amazon Health AI | One Medical to Amazon.com | Biometric-verified interventions |

### Layer 2: Neurotech Builds the Sensing Layer

| Company | Raised | What they build |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| Merge Labs | $250M seed ($850M valuation) | Brain-computer interface (OpenAI's largest check) |
| Neurable | $65M | Brain-sensing headphones |
| Elemind | $20M | AI neurostim wearable |
| Subsense | $27M | Non-surgical BCI |

They detect nervous system state. Nobody builds what happens after the sensor fires.

### Layer 3: EHRs Reduce Admin Burden, Not Physiological Stress

Epic cut documentation time 50% and burnout feelings 70%. Oracle/Cerner saved 200,000+ hours. But clinicians still absorb the physiological cost of high-acuity cases. Epic reduces friction. Pausality builds recovery.

Epic's App Market already lists SilverCloud and NeuroFlow. Pausality in that marketplace reaches two thirds of US hospitals.

## 7. Direct Competitors

### NEUROFIT — Closest Narrative Competitor
- "Nervous System Trainer" positioning
- 115,000 downloads, 2,000+ health professionals trained
- Founded by a Snapchat engineer from personal CPTSD recovery
- Polyvagal framing, somatic exercises, phone camera HRV
- 39% stress reduction by day 7 (n=16,487)
- Appears bootstrapped
- Weakness: No real-time biometric feedback. Phone camera HRV is pre/post, not closed-loop. No clinical founder. Polyvagal framing scientifically contested. No CE accreditation. No enterprise distribution.

### Climatic — Category Creator in "Daily Lung Health"
- $10M seed from Lerer Hippeau
- Mount Sinai research before product launch
- Physical product (dry powder inhaler), not software
- Weakness: Hardware dependency, no biofeedback, no outcome measurement, different mechanism (mucociliary clearance, not autonomic regulation).

### HeartMath — Incumbent
- $15M revenue, 500+ studies, HRV coherence biofeedback, 30 years in market
- Weakness: Legacy hardware, no Apple Watch integration, no adaptive protocols, no CE pathway, aging brand. Proved the category works. Did not build the next generation product.

### The Dying Category

| Company | Signal |
|---------|--------|
| Calm | Revenue down 24%, lost 500K subscribers |
| Headspace | Valuation dropped 89% ($3B to $320M) |
| Breathwrk | Sold to Peloton for $2.2M on $7.7M raised |
| Oxa | Consumer sunset, acquired by Myant |

B2C mindfulness is dying. Clinical validation is the moat.
