## II. The Competitive Landscape Has Three Layers

This is the single most important strategic insight from today's work. Every investor conversation should frame it this way:

### Layer 1: Big Tech Is Building the Health Data Layer

| Platform | Launch | What They Do | What They Don't Do |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| OpenAI / ChatGPT Health | Jan 2026 | Apple Health + medical records + 230M weekly queries | Clinical intervention protocols |
| Microsoft Copilot Health | **Mar 12, 2026** | Apple Health + Oura + Fitbit + **50K US EHRs** + Harvard Health | Guided breathing, biometric verification |
| Google Gemini Health Coach | Live | 24/7 coaching, Personal Health LLM, Large Sensor Model | Workflow-embedded micro-interventions |
| Samsung Brain Health | CES 2026 | Cognitive health via Watch/Ring, dementia detection | ANS regulation training |
| Meta "Malibu 2" | 2026 | Smartwatch + Meta AI, neural wristband | Clinical protocols |
| Amazon Health AI | Mar 2026 | From One Medical to Amazon.com, personalized assistant | Biometric-verified interventions |

**The pitch line:** "Six of the world's largest companies launched health AI products in the last 90 days. Every one of them is a data layer. None of them is an intervention layer. Pausality is the intervention layer."

### Layer 2: Neurotech Is Building the Sensing Layer

| Company | Raised | What They Build |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| Merge Labs | $250M seed ($850M valuation) | BCI — OpenAI's largest check |
| Neurable | $65M | Brain-sensing headphones, cognitive insights |
| Elemind | $20M | AI neurostim wearable |
| Subsense | $27M | Non-surgical BCI |

**The pitch line:** "They build sensors that detect nervous system state. Nobody builds what happens after the sensor fires. That's Pausality."

### Layer 3: EHRs Are Reducing Admin Burden (Not Stress)

| Platform | What They Do | Results |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Epic (38% US hospitals) | AI Charting, DAX Copilot, Art/Emmie/Penny | -50% doc time, -70% burnout feelings, -44% turnover |
| Oracle/Cerner ($28.3B) | Clinical AI Agent, voice-driven EHR | -40% doc time, 200K+ hours saved |

**The gap:** Even after cutting documentation time in half, clinicians still absorb the physiological stress of high-acuity cases. Epic reduces the friction. **Pausality builds the recovery.**

**The distribution play:** Epic's App Market already lists SilverCloud and NeuroFlow. Pausality in the Epic App Market = instant distribution to 2/3 of US hospitals.
