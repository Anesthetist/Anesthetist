# Competitor Playbook Analysis: High-Threat Deep Dive

**Date:** March 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Study the playbooks of Climatic and NEUROFIT. Copy what works. Skip what doesn't fit.

---

## Competitor 1: CLIMATIC

### The Playbook

**Category creation, not product launch.** Climatic isn't selling a breathing device. They're creating the "daily lung health" category — positioning lung care alongside sleep, nutrition, hydration, and gut health as a foundational wellness pillar. Their 10-year vision: "taking care of your lungs will be as routine as brushing your teeth."

### Leadership Team

| Person | Role | Background | Why It Matters |
|--------|------|-----------|----------------|
| Eric Kau | Co-founder, CEO | COO at Seed Health (scaled), Chewy, Amazon | Knows DTC operations, logistics, scaling from 0-1 |
| Allie Melnick | Co-founder, CCO | Brand GM + CEO within Harry's Inc | DTC brand building, subscription models, consumer positioning |

### Board / Investors

- **Lerer Hippeau** (lead) — NYC early-stage VC, media + consumer brands focus (Casper, Allbirds, Glossier)
- **BBGV** — consumer brands
- **Brand Foundry** — brand-building specialty
- **Cade Ventures, Good Friends, Space Station** — supporting investors

**Pattern:** Consumer brand investors, not health tech VCs. They're betting on the brand and category creation, not clinical outcomes.

### What They're Doing Right (Copy This)

1. **Category creation language.** "Daily lung health" is genius positioning — it creates a new budget line that didn't exist. SRL should do the same: "in-shift mental hygiene" is SRL's category creation moment. Don't compete in "wellness apps" — create "clinical autonomic regulation."

2. **Clinical research first, then product.** Climatic did the Mount Sinai study BEFORE launching the product. The research validates the category. SRL has the same opportunity: publish the Pausality pilot data (Mayo, VCU) before scaling.

3. **Operator CEO + Brand CCO.** Eric (operations) + Allie (brand) is a powerful combo. Randy (clinical/IP) + Jason (design/product) is equally strong but needs a brand/marketing counterpart.

4. **Stealth-to-launch with $10M.** They stayed quiet until the seed was closed and the research was done. Clean narrative on launch day.

5. **Analogies to established habits.** "As routine as brushing your teeth" is immediately understandable. SRL needs its own: "As automatic as checking your vitals" or "As natural as adjusting your mask."

### What They're Doing Wrong (Skip This)

1. **Physical product dependency.** L Max is a dry powder inhaler — manufacturing, supply chain, regulatory (even if non-prescription). Software scales better.

2. **Consumer brand investors.** Lerer Hippeau is great for DTC, but SRL's enterprise/clinical pathway needs health tech VCs who understand hospital procurement.

3. **No biofeedback.** No measurement of outcomes. You inhale the powder and... trust that it worked. SRL's closed-loop biofeedback proves efficacy in real-time.

4. **Different mechanism entirely.** Lung physiology (mucociliary clearance) vs. ANS regulation. They sound similar to outsiders but are fundamentally different science.

### Where They'll Be in 5 Years

Consumer lung health brand ($50-200M revenue if the category takes off). Possibly acquired by a supplement or respiratory care company (ResMed, Theravent). NOT competing with SRL for clinical autonomic regulation — parallel category, not overlapping.

## Competitor 2: NEUROFIT

### The Playbook

**Founder's trauma story + polyvagal positioning + certification funnel.** Andrew Hogue built NEUROFIT from his personal CPTSD recovery as a Snapchat engineer. The product is somatic exercises guided by phone camera HRV. The business model is B2C app + B2B health professional certification.

### Marketing Strategy (Detailed)

1. **SEO content machine.** neurofit.app/learn/ has dozens of articles targeting "somatic exercises for [condition]" — burnout, overwhelm, shutdown, anxiety, PTSD. Every article drives to app download.

2. **Polyvagal Theory popularization.** They teach Porges' framework to a consumer audience. "What Is The Polyvagal Theory?" is a gateway article. This builds credibility while creating demand for the product.

3. **Health professional certification.** The NEUROFIT Certification is a B2B2C play: train therapists/coaches, who then recommend the app to their clients. "Attract new clients 10x faster." This is a distribution hack — every certified professional becomes a sales channel.

4. **Founder's story as marketing.** Andrew's CPTSD recovery story is the origin narrative. Personal, vulnerable, credible.

5. **Substack newsletter.** Content marketing via email, building community.

6. **DoD/VA pipeline.** Seeking military partnerships for SOF stress recovery, PTSD prevention. This is a credibility + revenue play.

### What They're Doing Right (Copy This)

1. **Health professional certification program.** This is the single most replicable idea. SRL's Somnistics Certified Professional (SCP) is EXACTLY this — but with AANA accreditation and biofeedback data, not just exercises. **Priority: build the SCP certification program as a distribution channel.**

2. **Outcome data with independent biostatistician.** n=16,487, Harvard Medical School biostatistician, MMRM analysis. This is investor-grade evidence. SRL needs equivalent independent analysis of Pausality data.

3. **SEO content targeting clinical conditions.** "Somatic exercises for burnout" captures intent at the moment of need. SRL should target: "CRNA stress management," "HRV biofeedback during shift," "breathing exercises between cases."

4. **Founder trauma story as origin narrative.** Randy's story (wife's suicide during pandemic, 28 years in the OR, building the tool he wished he'd had) is equally powerful. Use it.

5. **Zero adverse events claim.** Important for clinical credibility. SRL should track and publish the same.

6. **Military/DoD pathway.** SOF stress recovery is a use case for Gap Moment Training. File this for later.

### What They're Doing Wrong (Skip This)

1. **Phone camera HRV.** Less accurate than wearable PPG. NEUROFIT's biometric data quality is fundamentally inferior to Apple Watch + Open Wearables.

2. **No real-time biofeedback during exercises.** You do the somatic exercise, then check your HRV after. SRL's closed-loop shows you the change AS IT HAPPENS.

3. **No clinical workflow integration.** Consumer app, not designed for in-shift use. 5-minute exercises don't fit between intubations.

4. **Somatic exercises, not breathwork.** Body movement exercises (shaking, tapping) vs. SRL's breath-based protocols. Hard to do somatic shaking in a sterile OR.

5. **Bootstrapped constraints.** No disclosed funding limits R&D velocity. SRL's F&F round + partnership strategy provides more runway.

### Where They'll Be in 5 Years

200-500K users. Established health professional certification program (5,000+ certified). Possible Series A for enterprise expansion. May pivot to DoD/VA as primary revenue. Not a direct clinical competitor to SRL but competing for the "nervous system regulation" narrative.

## What to Copy: The SRL Playbook (Synthesized)

### From Climatic:
- [ ] **Create the category.** "In-shift mental hygiene" or "clinical autonomic regulation" — not "wellness app"
- [ ] **Research-first narrative.** Publish Mayo/VCU pilot data before scaling
- [ ] **Habit-level analogy.** "As automatic as checking vitals before a case"

### From NEUROFIT:
- [ ] **SCP certification as distribution channel.** Train CRNAs who recommend the app to colleagues. Every certified professional = a sales channel
- [ ] **Independent outcome data.** Commission independent biostatistician analysis of Pausality HRV data
- [ ] **SEO content targeting clinical conditions.** "CRNA burnout," "HRV biofeedback during shift," "breathing exercises between cases"
- [ ] **Founder's story as origin narrative.** Randy's personal story — deployed deliberately, not accidentally
- [ ] **Health professional training funnel.** Conference workshops → certification → app adoption → enterprise pilot
- [ ] **Track and publish zero adverse events.** Clinical credibility baseline
- [ ] **DoD/VA as future revenue stream.** SOF stress recovery, military resilience — file for Series A

### SRL's Unfair Advantages Over Both:
1. **28-year CRNA founder** (neither has a clinician founder)
2. **Real-time closed-loop biofeedback** (neither does this)
3. **Patent architecture** (Five-Breath Re-Embodiment)
4. **AANA CE accreditation pathway** (neither has this)
5. **Knowledge graph with 146 concepts + evidence chains** (neither has a knowledge system)
6. **Multi-platform via Open Wearables** (neither has this partnership)
7. **Enterprise pilot sites** (Mayo Clinic, VCU, AANA)
