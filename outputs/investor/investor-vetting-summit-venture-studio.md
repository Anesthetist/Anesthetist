## Red Flags Summary

1. **Name mismatch** — "Summit Venture Group" vs. actual "Summit Venture Studio." Minor, but suggests the introducer may not be deeply embedded with the firm.

2. **Model mismatch** — SVS's entire thesis is university IP commercialization. SRL doesn't fit this model. If SVS is interested in SRL, the question is *why* — and whether they'd try to apply a model that doesn't fit.

3. **Founder control risk** — SVS's "founders by hire" approach directly conflicts with Randy's role as domain expert and co-founder. Any deal structure must preserve Randy's authority over clinical content and product direction.

4. **Ownership dilution** — "2–3x more ownership" is a red flag for founder dilution. Must understand exact terms before proceeding.

5. **Unverifiable claims** — The Duke case study is plausible but several key details couldn't be independently confirmed (Dr. Galar's identity, $6M savings figure, Duke contract).

6. **Introducer opacity** — Patrick Viernes's connection to SVS is not publicly documented.
