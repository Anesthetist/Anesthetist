Week 1: Foundation

  • Nucleus ambiguus — vagal brake engagement via resonant breathing

Neurotransmitters: ACh (nucleus basalis), GABA (insular lateral inhibition). No NE spike — observation, not effort.

Week 2: Deepening

Core mechanism: Interhemispheric integration — building the bridge between analytical and intuitive processing

  • Corpus callosum — forced bilateral oscillation via alternate nostril
  • Hippocampus — contextual binding for state-dependent memory
  • Cardiac vagal circuits — heart-brain coherence loop via ujjayi + extended exhale
  • Chemoreceptors — CO₂ tolerance recalibration (breath hold)
  • Positive valence network — gratitude cue activates ventral striatum

Key shift: From "I can control my breath" to "my breath reshapes my internal state." Anterocept™ Domain 1 → Domain 2.

  • Mirror neuron system — co-regulation breath bridges individual → team
  • Ventral vagal "safety" circuits — peripheral vision activates social engagement

Day 21 = Neuro-Ouroboros™: awareness feeds on itself. The practitioner IS the instrument.
