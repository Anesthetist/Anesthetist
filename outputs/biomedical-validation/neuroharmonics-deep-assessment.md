# NeuroHarmonics: What Survives Scrutiny

## The Problem

The vault concept `neuroharmonics` (canonical, v1.0) claims "auditory cortex + thalamus entrainment" as the mechanism for its three-layer frequency architecture. The evidence does not support this claim at the level implied by canonical status.

## What the Evidence Actually Says

### Entrainment: More False Than True

| Finding | Source | Implication |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Only 5 of 14 EEG studies show cortical entrainment to binaural beats | Ingendoh et al. 2023 (systematic review) | The core mechanism claim is not reliably supported |
| No significant difference between theta, alpha, and gamma frequencies on outcomes | Yang et al. 2025 | If frequency doesn't matter, the entrainment model is wrong |
| Isochronic tones produce stronger EEG modulation than binaural beats | Dos Anjos et al. 2024 | A different auditory method works better for the claimed mechanism |
| Brainstem shows auditory steady-state response, but cortex does not reliably follow | Multiple studies reviewed by Ingendoh | The "entrainment" is subcortical, not the cortical entrainment the concept claims |

### What IS Real (Even Without Entrainment)

| Effect | Evidence | Effect Size | Mechanism (Likely) |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Anxiety reduction | Multiple RCTs | d = 0.3-0.5 | Relaxation response from rhythmic stimulus, not frequency-specific entrainment |
| HRV coherence improvement when combined with breathwork | Zhang et al. 2021 | SDNN +22% (combined) vs +11% (breathwork alone) | Auditory anchoring enhances breath focus |
| Sleep latency reduction | Zhang et al. 2021 Sleep Med Rev | d = 0.63 | Masking + relaxation |
| Pain modulation | Siedliecki 2010, Thaut 2005 | d = 0.4-0.5 | Attentional capture + endogenous opioid modulation |
| Stress reduction when combined with meditation | Sarangdhar et al. 2019 | PSS -18% (combined) vs -10% (meditation alone) | Attentional scaffolding |

### What Produces the Effects If Not Entrainment?

**BioMistral assessment of five candidate mechanisms:**

1. **Attentional anchoring** (STRONG) — Binaural beats provide a non-invasive auditory focus point that reduces mind-wandering during breathwork. This is the most defensible mechanism for SRL's use case.

2. **Non-specific relaxation response** (STRONG) — Any rhythmic, predictable auditory stimulus activates the parasympathetic nervous system. This is not frequency-specific.

3. **Expectancy/placebo** (MODERATE) — Users who believe binaural beats work show larger effects. This is real but not a mechanism you want to rely on.

4. **Environmental masking** (MODERATE) — Beats mask distracting environmental noise, reducing cognitive load. Useful but not unique.

5. **Subthreshold neural modulation** (SPECULATIVE) — Some neural changes may occur below scalp EEG detection threshold. Possible but unverifiable with current consumer hardware.

## What This Means for NeuroHarmonics

| Layer | Current Claim | Honest Claim | Still Valuable? |
|-------|-------------|-------------|----------------|

| **Adaptive Soundscaping** | "Anterior insula + right hemisphere engagement" | "HRV-responsive audio environment — auditory context adapts to autonomic state" | **YES** — the closed-loop adaptation is genuinely novel regardless of entrainment |

## The Combination Is the Story

The strongest evidence for binaural beats is **in combination**, not standalone:

- Breathwork + binaural: SDNN +22% vs +11% breathwork alone (Zhang 2021)
- Meditation + binaural: PSS -18% vs -10% meditation alone (Sarangdhar 2019)
- HRV biofeedback + binaural: RMSSD +35% vs +20% biofeedback alone (Kox 2020)

SRL's NeuroHarmonics is ALREADY a combination protocol. The three-layer architecture combines auditory pacing with breathwork with adaptive HRV feedback. **The combination is the value proposition.** The binaural beats are one component that provides attentional scaffolding for the breathing and biofeedback layers.

Reframed this way, NeuroHarmonics is:
- Not "brainwave entrainment through sound"
- Instead: "adaptive auditory environment that enhances breathwork and biofeedback training through attentional anchoring and HRV-responsive soundscaping"

That claim is defensible. That claim is honest. And it's still valuable.

## Safety Notes

- Generally safe (Cochrane 2023: "generally safe" across 1,200+ participants)

- 5% tinnitus exacerbation in pre-existing tinnitus

- **Contraindications:** epilepsy, severe tinnitus, auditory hallucinations

## The Honest Position

NeuroHarmonics is a genuinely innovative adaptive audio system. Its three-layer architecture and HRV-responsive soundscaping are novel. The closed-loop adaptation is something competitors don't have.

But the mechanism is attentional anchoring and auditory-autonomic coupling — not brainwave entrainment. The evidence for entrainment is too thin (5/14 studies) to anchor a clinical product on.

The reframe doesn't weaken the product. It strengthens the claim by grounding it in what actually works.

**Design constraint:** "If the system does not improve outcomes beyond breathwork alone, the audio layer has no independent value. Test this."
