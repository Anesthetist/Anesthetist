# Non-Dual Awareness: Neuroscience Literature Review

**Purpose:** Map the frontier of non-dual awareness research for Somnistics Research Labs — informing advanced NeuroMinute protocols, somatic certification higher levels, consciousness-boundary modules, and the relationship between interoceptive literacy and non-dual states.

**Source:** All studies retrieved from PubMed. Citations include DOI links to original articles.

---

## 1. What Is Non-Dual Awareness?

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed, non-dual awareness (NDA) is defined in the neuroscience literature as a **basic, non-conceptual, non-propositional awareness that precedes subject-object fragmentation**. It is not a content of consciousness but rather consciousness-as-such — the knowing quality itself, stripped of the habitual dualities (self/other, inside/outside, good/bad) that structure ordinary experience.

Key operationalizations from the literature:

- **Josipovic (2019)** defines NDA as possessing "non-representational reflexivity" — consciousness knowing itself without mediation by mental representations. It is irreducible to contents, functions, or states. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/bs.pbr.2018.10.021)
- **Josipovic & Miskovic (2020)** argue NDA cannot be adequately specified by the standard two-dimensional model (arousal level + phenomenal content), proposing it as a unique kind requiring its own dimension. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.02087)
- **Josipovic (2021)** proposes an **implicit-explicit gradient** of NDA as a z-axis to the existing 2D consciousness map — the degree to which NDA is manifest in any experience, independent of global state or local content. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab031)
- **Hanley, Nakamura & Garland (2018)** developed the **Nondual Awareness Dimensional Assessment (NADA)** — trait and state measures identifying two dimensions: self-transcendence and bliss. Mindfulness practitioners scored higher; practice frequency positively associated with NDA. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/pas0000615)
- **Gamma & Metzinger (2021)** created the **Minimal Phenomenal Experience questionnaire (MPE-92M)** with 12 factors characterizing "pure awareness" in 1,403 meditators, including "Emptiness and Non-egoic Self-awareness," "Wakeful Presence," and "Witness Consciousness." [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253694)

### The Taxonomy Problem

The field uses overlapping terminology: nondual awareness, pure consciousness, consciousness-as-such, minimal phenomenal experience, objectless awareness, contentless awareness, turiya. Josipovic (2025) notes this creates ongoing confusion in research design. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1665826)

## 2. Neural Correlates

### 2.1 The Topographic Reorganization Model

**Cooper-2022-Topographic-Reorganization**
**Title:** Beyond the veil of duality — topographic reorganization model of meditation
**Journal:** Neuroscience of Consciousness
**PMID:** 36237370
**DOI:** [10.1093/nc/niac013](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niac013)
**Key Findings:** Reviewed fMRI studies across meditation types and experience levels. Found: (i) decreased posterior DMN activity, (ii) increased CEN activity, (iii) decreased connectivity within posterior DMN and between posterior/anterior DMN, (iv) increased connectivity within anterior DMN and CEN, (v) significantly impacted DMN-CEN connectivity (nonlinear). Proposes a **Topographic Reorganization Model (TRoM)**: the reorganization relates to decreased mental-self-processing and synchronization with interoceptive and exteroceptive self-processing — enabling explicit NDA experience.
**Relevance to SRL:** Direct bridge between NDA and interoceptive processing. The TRoM validates that interoceptive literacy training may be a stepping stone toward non-dual states. Critical for advanced certification design.
**Evidence Level:** Systematic review (Level I)

### 2.2 DMN Deactivation in Experienced Meditators

**Brewer-2011-DMN-Meditation-Differences**
**Title:** Meditation experience is associated with differences in default mode network activity and connectivity
**Journal:** PNAS
**PMID:** 22114193
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.1112029108](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1112029108)
**Key Findings:** Main DMN nodes (mPFC, PCC) relatively deactivated in experienced meditators across all meditation types (Concentration, Loving-Kindness, Choiceless Awareness). Stronger coupling between PCC, dACC, and dlPFC in experienced meditators — consistent with decreased mind-wandering and enhanced self-monitoring.
**Relevance to SRL:** Foundational evidence that meditation training restructures the self-referential network. Implies that even basic NeuroMinute practices are moving the needle on DMN regulation.
**Evidence Level:** Controlled neuroimaging study (Level II)

### 2.3 Josipovic's Precuneus Network Hypothesis

**Josipovic-2013-Neural-Correlates-NDA**
**Title:** Neural correlates of nondual awareness in meditation
**Journal:** Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences
**PMID:** 24033505
**DOI:** [10.1111/nyas.12261](https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.12261)
**Key Findings:** NDA during Tibetan Buddhist meditation reduces anticorrelation between intrinsic (DMN) and extrinsic (task-positive) networks. Preliminary data points to involvement of a **precuneus network** in NDA with minimized phenomenal content. NDA contextualizes experience without fragmenting it into habitual dualities.
**Relevance to SRL:** The precuneus hypothesis provides a specific neural target for understanding how Gap Moment Training moves practitioners from dual to non-dual processing.
**Evidence Level:** Neuroimaging study with expert meditators (Level II-III)

### 2.4 Dual-Origin Theory and the Hippocampus System

**Sladky-2026-Amygdala-Hippocampus-MPE**
**Title:** From hidden springs to endless oceans: exploring the complementary roles of the amygdala and hippocampus in phenomenal experience
**Journal:** Neuroscience of Consciousness
**PMID:** 41836052
**DOI:** [10.1093/nc/niag007](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niag007)
**Key Findings:** Proposes dual-origin theory: the "Amygdala-System" supports ego-centric interoceptive self-modeling; the "Hippocampus-System" supports allocentric, selfless experience. MPE may represent consciousness freed from Amygdala-System dominance. Suggests **real-time fMRI neurofeedback** could upregulate the Hippocampus-System to enable controlled study of MPE.
**Relevance to SRL:** Directly maps onto the SRL framework — interoceptive training (Amygdala-System) as foundation, with advanced protocols moving toward selfless awareness (Hippocampus-System). The neurofeedback angle is a potential future modality.
**Evidence Level:** Theoretical/review (Level V), published 2026

## 3. Autonomic/Physiological Correlates

### 3.1 Advanced Meditation and the Autonomic Nervous System

**Sezer-2025-Advanced-Meditation-ANS-Review**
**Title:** Advanced and long-term meditation and the autonomic nervous system: A review and synthesis
**Journal:** Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews
**PMID:** 40204160
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106141](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106141)
**Key Findings:** Synthesizes heterogeneous ANS findings during advanced meditation. Reports concomitant sympathetic AND parasympathetic activation ("relaxed alertness"). Identifies three explanatory factors: (1) specific meditation type, (2) level of expertise, (3) intra-individual variations. Reveals specific **HRV patterns including very low and low-frequency spectral power peaks, along with cardiac and respiratory coupling**. Shifts from broad "long-term" to precise "advanced" characterization of meditation expertise.
**Relevance to SRL:** THIS IS THE KEY PAPER. Directly informs how SRL should measure and train autonomic signatures of advanced practice. The VLF/LF spectral peaks and cardiac-respiratory coupling map directly onto resonant breathing frequency work. Validates the concept of measurable autonomic markers for progression through certification levels.
**Evidence Level:** Systematic review (Level I)

### 3.2 Arousal-Driven Non-Dual Awareness (Tantric Mahamudra)

**Kozhevnikov-2022-Arousal-Based-Meditation**
**Title:** Beyond mindfulness: Arousal-driven modulation of attentional control during arousal-based practices
**Journal:** Current Research in Neurobiology
**PMID:** 36246552
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.crneur.2022.100053](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crneur.2022.100053)
**Key Findings:** Compared Tantric Mahamudra (NDA after arousal-based Yidam/Tummo) vs. non-Tantric Mahamudra (NDA after rest). Tantric path: PNS withdrawal (HF HRV decrease), Alpha2 increase, then unique state of high cortical excitability and reduced attentional control. Non-Tantric: PNS dominance, Alpha1 increase, active monitoring. Two distinct autonomic pathways to NDA — one through arousal, one through relaxation.
**Relevance to SRL:** Reveals that NDA can be accessed through BOTH sympathetic activation and parasympathetic dominance. This validates multiple entry points — relevant for CRNAs who may need arousal-compatible pathways rather than purely relaxation-based ones. Critical for protocol design.
**Evidence Level:** Controlled EEG/ECG study with 16 expert practitioners (Level II-III)

### 3.3 Sensory Gating and Non-Dual Awareness

**Kumari-2024-NDA-Startle-Reflex**
**Title:** Non-dual awareness and sensory processing in meditators: Insights from startle reflex modulation
**Journal:** Consciousness and Cognition
**PMID:** 38981366
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.concog.2024.103722](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2024.103722)
**Key Findings:** Meditators who can enter and sustain NDA (n=18) showed significantly greater prepulse inhibition (PPI) of the acoustic startle response compared to meditators who cannot (n=14). No overall difference between meditators and non-meditators — the NDA capacity is the differentiator. Suggests enhanced sensorimotor gating.
**Relevance to SRL:** Measurable physiological marker (PPI) for NDA capacity. Links NDA to enhanced sensory filtering — directly relevant to clinical performance under stress. CRNAs with enhanced sensorimotor gating would theoretically perform better in high-stimulus environments.
**Evidence Level:** Controlled experimental study (Level II-III)

## 4. The Taxonomy of Meditation States

### 4.1 The FA-OM-NDA Continuum

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed, the field has converged on a three-stage taxonomy:

1. **Focused Attention (FA):** Sustained attention on a chosen object. Beta/gamma EEG. DMN deactivation. Effortful.
2. **Open Monitoring (OM):** Non-reactive monitoring of experience without selection. Theta EEG. Less effortful.
3. **Non-Dual Awareness (NDA):** Subject-object distinction dissolves. Alpha1 or unique patterns. Effortless.

**Travis-2010-Three-Categories**
**Title:** Focused attention, open monitoring and automatic self-transcending: Categories to organize meditations
**Journal:** Consciousness and Cognition
**PMID:** 20167507
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.concog.2010.01.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2010.01.007)
**Key Findings:** Proposes "automatic self-transcending" as a third category beyond FA and OM. FA characterized by beta/gamma; OM by theta; automatic self-transcending by alpha1. Warns against averaging different meditation types together.
**Evidence Level:** Review (Level V)

**Laukkonen-2021-Predictive-Processing**
**Title:** From many to (n)one: Meditation and the plasticity of the predictive mind
**Journal:** Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews
**PMID:** 34139248
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2021.06.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2021.06.021)
**Key Findings:** Unifying account under predictive processing: meditation progressively reduces counterfactual deep cognition. FA relinquishes object-level predictions; OM relinquishes monitoring predictions; NDA relinquishes self-predictions; cessation = all predictions fall away. Places FA → OM → NDA → cessation on a single continuum of deconstructing the predictive mind.
**Relevance to SRL:** THE THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK. Maps directly onto SRL's progression model. Gap Moment Training begins at FA, advances through OM, with advanced protocols touching NDA territory. The predictive processing lens also connects to interoceptive inference (the brain predicting its own body states).
**Evidence Level:** Theoretical review (Level V) — highly cited

**Raffone-2019-Brain-Theory-Meditation**
**Title:** Toward a brain theory of meditation
**Journal:** Progress in Brain Research
**PMID:** 30732838
**DOI:** [10.1016/bs.pbr.2018.10.028](https://doi.org/10.1016/bs.pbr.2018.10.028)
**Key Findings:** Proposes Brain Theory of Meditation (BTM): FA = down-regulation of networks; OM = gating and tuning of network coupling; compassion/LKM = state-related up-regulation. Leftward asymmetry in top-down regulation. Meditation provides a meta-function for efficient brain/mind regulation and flexible allocation of constrained brain activity resources.
**Evidence Level:** Theoretical review (Level V)

### 4.2 Jhana: Advanced Concentrative Absorption

**Yang-2024-7T-Jhana-Case-Study**
**Title:** Intensive whole-brain 7T MRI case study of volitional control of brain activity in deep absorptive meditation states
**Journal:** Cerebral Cortex
**PMID:** 37943791
**DOI:** [10.1093/cercor/bhad408](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhad408)
**Key Findings:** 4 hours of 7T fMRI from an adept jhana practitioner (27 sessions). Distinctive patterns in cortical, subcortical, brainstem, and cerebellar regions. Correlations between brain activity and phenomenological qualities of attention, jhanic qualities, and narrative processing. Demonstrates jhana practice "deconstructs consciousness."
**Relevance to SRL:** Most rigorous neuroimaging of advanced meditation to date. The brainstem and cerebellar involvement is notable — these are autonomic regulatory centers. Jhana states may represent what advanced NeuroMinute practitioners could eventually access.
**Evidence Level:** Intensive case study, 7T fMRI (Level IV — high methodological rigor)

**Yang-2025-Group-Level-7T-Jhana**
**Title:** The neuroscience of highly stable, positive, and refined states of consciousness during jhana-type advanced concentration absorption meditation
**Journal:** bioRxiv (preprint)
**PMID:** 41292740
**DOI:** [10.1101/2025.11.12.688050](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.12.688050)
**Key Findings:** FIRST GROUP-LEVEL 7T fMRI study of jhana. Across eight successive states: reproducible neural trajectories with anterior-to-posterior reorganization, flattening of cortical hierarchies, nonlinear reconfiguration of global harmonics. Tight coupling between brain metrics and equanimity, attentional stability. Neurosynth decoding associates jhana with reduced suffering-related processes and increased attentional monitoring. Jhana = "distinct, structured mode of awareness that persists despite radically reduced narrative thought and sensory content."
**Relevance to SRL:** Breakthrough paper. Group-level validation that advanced meditation produces measurable, reproducible brain states. The coupling with equanimity and attentional stability directly maps to clinician resilience outcomes SRL targets.
**Evidence Level:** Group fMRI study, 7T (Level II-III) — preprint

**Ganesan-2024-Jhana-7T-Reliability**
**Title:** Within-subject reliability of brain networks during advanced meditation
**Journal:** Human Brain Mapping
**PMID:** 38726831
**DOI:** [10.1002/hbm.26666](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.26666)
**Key Findings:** Thalamus, somatomotor, limbic, DMN, control, and temporo-parietal networks showed good within-subject reliability across all jhanas. When adjusted for phenomenological variability, attention and salience networks also showed reliable patterns. Demonstrates jhana produces consistent, measurable brain signatures.
**Evidence Level:** Intensive case study, reliability analysis (Level IV)

**Potash-2025-Jhana-Nonlinear-EEG**
**Title:** Integrated Phenomenology and Brain Connectivity Demonstrate Changes in Nonlinear Processing in Jhana Advanced Meditation
**Journal:** Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience
**PMID:** 40402036
**DOI:** [10.1162/JOCN.a.50](https://doi.org/10.1162/JOCN.a.50)
**Key Findings:** Jhana better distinguished by non-oscillatory (nonlinear) dynamics than oscillatory synchrony. Deeper jhana states show equalization of feedback and feedforward processes — a balance of internally and externally driven information processing. Combining phenomenological ratings with neural metrics improves classification.
**Evidence Level:** Intensive case study, EEG (Level IV)

**Treves-2025-Dynamic-Brain-States-Jhana**
**Title:** Dynamic brain states underlying advanced concentrative absorption meditation: A 7-T fMRI-intensive case study
**Journal:** Network Neuroscience
**PMID:** 40161981
**DOI:** [10.1162/netn_a_00432](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00432)
**Key Findings:** Three brain states identified: DMN-anticorrelated state (increased in deeper jhana), hyperconnected state (elevated thalamocortical connectivity, decreased over jhana course), and sparsely connected state. The hyperconnected state associated with sensory awareness; DMN-anticorrelated with deep absorption.
**Evidence Level:** Intensive case study, 7T fMRI (Level IV)

**Dennison-2019-Default-Consciousness-Jhana-EEG**
**Title:** The Human Default Consciousness and Its Disruption: Insights From an EEG Study of Buddhist Jhana Meditation
**Journal:** Frontiers in Human Neuroscience
**PMID:** 31249516
**DOI:** [10.3389/fnhum.2019.00178](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2019.00178)
**Key Findings:** First detailed EEG study of jhana. Subjects display "spindle" activity similar to stage 2 nREM sleep while remaining highly alert. More experienced subjects display high-voltage slow-waves. Some develop ability to consciously evoke clonic seizure-like activity under full control. Reflects profound disruption of default consciousness when the personal element is withdrawn.
**Relevance to SRL:** Sleep spindle-like activity during alert meditation is remarkable — suggests jhana accesses a consciousness-state normally only seen in sleep, but with preserved awareness. Connects to SRL's sleep quality research.
**Evidence Level:** EEG study (Level III)

**Hagerty-2013-Jhana-Reward-System**
**Title:** Case study of ecstatic meditation: fMRI and EEG evidence of self-stimulating a reward system
**Journal:** Neural Plasticity
**PMID:** 23738149
**DOI:** [10.1155/2013/653572](https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/653572)
**Key Findings:** First neural recording during jhana. External awareness dims, internal verbalizations fade, personal boundaries alter, attention highly focused, joy increases. Extreme joy associated with activation of nucleus accumbens (dopamine/opioid reward system). Novel method of self-stimulating reward system using only internal mental processes.
**Relevance to SRL:** Endogenous reward activation without external stimulation. Relevant to clinician well-being — if advanced practitioners can voluntarily activate reward circuits, this has implications for burnout prevention and intrinsic motivation.
**Evidence Level:** Case study, fMRI + EEG (Level IV)

### 4.3 Cessation Events (Nirodha)

**Chowdhury-2023-Cessation-EEG**
**Title:** Investigation of advanced mindfulness meditation "cessation" experiences using EEG spectral analysis
**Journal:** Neuropsychologia
**PMID:** 37777153
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2023.108694](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2023.108694)
**Key Findings:** First EEG study of cessation events. 37 cessation events from one meditator (23,000+ hours). Marked by large-scale **alpha-power decrease starting ~40 seconds before onset**, lowest immediately following cessation. Linear decrease in occipital and parietal regions pre-cessation. Modest theta increases in central, parietal, right temporal regions. Evidence that experienced meditators can voluntarily modulate their state of consciousness.
**Relevance to SRL:** Alpha suppression pattern pre-cessation could become a biomarker. The 40-second pre-cessation window is potentially trainable/detectable with wearable EEG.
**Evidence Level:** Intensive case study, neurophenomenological (Level IV)

**Laukkonen-2023-Cessation-Nirodha**
**Title:** Cessations of consciousness in meditation: Advancing a scientific understanding of nirodha samapatti
**Journal:** Progress in Brain Research
**PMID:** 37714573
**DOI:** [10.1016/bs.pbr.2022.12.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/bs.pbr.2022.12.007)
**Key Findings:** Some practitioners report total absence of consciousness during meditation lasting up to 7 days (nirodha samapatti). Different from sleep — practitioners impervious to external stimulation, cannot be "woken up." No time experience or tiredness; stiff rather than relaxed body. Emergence produces sudden clarity, openness, insights. Integrated into predictive processing and active inference frameworks.
**Relevance to SRL:** Cessation represents the extreme end of the contemplative spectrum. While not directly trainable in clinical populations, understanding it informs the theoretical architecture of consciousness that SRL's tools are built upon.
**Evidence Level:** Review with preliminary data (Level IV-V)

**Lieberman-2025-Neuroscience-Consciousness-Advanced-Meditation**
**Title:** Toward a neuroscience of consciousness using advanced meditation
**Journal:** Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews
**PMID:** 41380790
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106520](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106520)
**Key Findings:** Proposes advanced meditation as a "minimal model framework" for consciousness science. Jhana preserves highly abstract awareness while attenuating typical consciousness features; cessation temporarily suspends consciousness entirely. These serve as precise, replicable phenomenological anchors for identifying the neural mechanisms supporting consciousness in its most reduced forms.
**Relevance to SRL:** Positions advanced meditation as equal in scientific value to anesthesia or psychedelics for studying consciousness — legitimizes the entire contemplative science enterprise SRL draws upon.
**Evidence Level:** Theoretical review (Level V) — published 2025

## 6. Clinical/Performance Relevance

### 6.1 Computational Phenomenology and Active Inference

**Tal-2025-Active-Inference-Advanced-Meditation**
**Title:** Active inference, computational phenomenology, and advanced meditation
**Journal:** Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews
**PMID:** 41475512
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106539](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106539)
**Key Findings:** Reviews computational models of advanced meditation. Identifies **precision weighting** as the common mechanism across models — the confidence attributed to different model parameters shapes experiential shifts. Early models emphasize top-down attentional modulation toward interoception; later models target layer-specific precision re-weighting within the meditator's hierarchical generative model. NDA and cessation remain largely unaddressed by current models.
**Relevance to SRL:** Precision weighting is the computational mechanism behind interoceptive literacy. SRL's training fundamentally recalibrates how practitioners weight internal signals vs. external demands. This paper provides the formal framework.
**Evidence Level:** Systematic review (Level I)

### 6.2 Nondual Awareness and Whole-Person Health

**Mills-2020-NDA-Whole-Person**
**Title:** Nondual Awareness and the Whole Person
**Journal:** Global Advances in Health and Medicine
**PMID:** 32499967
**DOI:** [10.1177/2164956120914600](https://doi.org/10.1177/2164956120914600)
**Key Findings:** NDA characterized by experience of nonseparation, compassion, and love. Well-being in NDA does not depend on content being experienced — it is an innate attribute. NDA is foundational to realizing the whole person and optimal well-being. (Co-authored by Josipovic.)
**Relevance to SRL:** Aligns NDA with integrative health frameworks. SRL's whole-person approach (somatic + psychological + performance) can draw on NDA as the advanced tier.
**Evidence Level:** Perspective/commentary (Level V)

### 6.3 Psychedelic-Meditation Convergence

**Deshon-2024-Psilocybin-Desynchronization**
**Title:** Psilocybin desynchronizes the human brain
**Journal:** Nature
**PMID:** 39020167
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41586-024-07624-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07624-5)
**Key Findings:** Psilocybin massively disrupted FC, strongest in DMN. Driven by brain desynchronization that dissolved network distinctions. Individual differences linked to subjective experience. Persistent decrease in hippocampal-DMN connectivity lasting weeks.
**Relevance to SRL:** Meditation and psychedelics converge on DMN disruption. SRL's non-pharmacological approach achieves qualitatively similar (though likely smaller magnitude) network effects through training rather than substances.
**Evidence Level:** Longitudinal precision functional mapping (Level II)

**Meling-2024-DMT-Meditation-Retreat**

**Key Findings:** In experienced meditators, DMT-harmine produced greater mystical-type experiences, NDA, emotional breakthrough vs. placebo during meditation. Greater psychological insight at day 1. At 1 month: significantly more personally meaningful and well-being-enhancing.

### 6.6 NDA and Body-Boundary Perception

**Yokosu-2025-NDA-Body-Boundary-RCT**
**Title:** Nondual Awareness, Body Boundary Perception, and Creative Attitudes in Japanese University Students
**Journal:** Behavioral Sciences
**PMID:** 41463980
**DOI:** [10.3390/bs15121638](https://doi.org/10.3390/bs15121638)
**Key Findings:** Brief (45 min) paired martial arts exercise increased NDA (Cohen's d = -1.74) and decreased perceived body boundary salience (d = 1.25). ### 6.8 Sleep and Consciousness Boundary

**Alcaraz-Sanchez-2022-Objectless-Awareness-Sleep**
**Title:** Nothingness Is All There Is: An Exploration of Objectless Awareness During Sleep

**PMID:** 35756253
**DOI:** [10.3389/fpsyg.2022.901031](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.901031)
**Key Findings:** Micro-phenomenological interviews with 18 participants found a "nothingness phase" during sleep: minimal self (bodiless yet "somewhere"), non-modal sensations, pleasant emotions, absence of visual experience, wide/unfocused attention, awareness of the state as it unfolded. ## 7. Connection to SRL

### 7.1 How NDA Research Informs Advanced NeuroMinute Protocols

The literature establishes a clear progression model that maps onto SRL's existing architecture:

| SRL Level | Meditation Stage | Neural Signature | Autonomic Marker |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Basic NeuroMinute | Focused Attention | Beta/gamma, DMN partial deactivation | Increased HF-HRV |
| Intermediate | Open Monitoring | Theta increase, broader deactivation | Cardiac-respiratory coupling |
| Advanced | Non-Dual Awareness | Precuneus activation, DMN-CEN reorganization | VLF/LF spectral peaks, "relaxed alertness" |
| Expert | Cessation/Absorption | Alpha suppression, reward circuit activation | Sympathetic-parasympathetic co-activation |

**Key insight from Sezer & Sacchet (2025):** The ANS review reveals that advanced meditation produces specific HRV patterns including VLF and LF spectral power peaks alongside cardiac-respiratory coupling. These are MEASURABLE with SRL's existing wearable technology stack (HRV monitors, respiratory sensors). This means SRL could create objective progression markers for advanced certification levels.

### 7.2 Somatic Certification Higher Levels

The Kozhevnikov (2022) finding of two pathways to NDA — arousal-based (Tantric) and relaxation-based (mindfulness) — is critical for CRNA populations. Clinical environments are inherently arousing. An arousal-compatible pathway to autonomic regulation and eventually NDA means SRL doesn't have to ask CRNAs to "relax" in impossible contexts. Instead, the arousal itself becomes the vehicle.

### 7.3 The Consciousness-Boundary Module

The Sladky (2026) dual-origin model (Amygdala-System for ego-centric processing, Hippocampus-System for allocentric/selfless processing) provides a neuroanatomical framework for the "consciousness boundary" concept. SRL's interoceptive training engages the Amygdala-System; advanced protocols would gradually engage the Hippocampus-System, enabling the kind of perspective-taking and boundary-flexibility that characterizes expert clinical judgment.

### 7.4 Interoceptive Literacy and Non-Dual States

The Cooper (2022) TRoM explicitly links NDA to **interoceptive and exteroceptive self-processing**. The reorganization from mental-self to interoceptive-self is the bridge between SRL's current training (MAIA-2, body-based awareness) and advanced non-dual territory. This is not a leap — it is a continuum. Interoceptive literacy IS the on-ramp to non-dual awareness.

The Tal (2025) computational phenomenology review identifies **precision weighting** as the mechanism: meditation recalibrates the confidence the brain assigns to its own predictions. SRL's interoceptive training recalibrates body-signal precision. Advanced practice extends this to recalibrating self-model precision itself.

### 7.5 Measurable Biomarkers for NDA-Adjacent States

From this review, the following biomarkers are candidates for SRL's wearable/assessment stack:

1. **Prepulse inhibition enhancement** (Kumari 2024) — sensorimotor gating improvement with NDA
2. **Alpha suppression patterns** (Chowdhury 2023) — 40-second pre-cessation signature
3. **VLF/LF HRV spectral peaks** (Sezer 2025) — advanced meditation autonomic signature
4. **Cardiac-respiratory coupling** (Sezer 2025) — measurable with existing technology
5. **NADA-T/NADA-S questionnaire scores** (Hanley 2018) — validated self-report measure
6. **DMN-CEN connectivity ratios** (Cooper 2022) — requires fMRI, but potential research partnership metric
