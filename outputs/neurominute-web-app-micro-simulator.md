# The NeuroMinute Web App: A Micro-Simulator for Crisis Management

A 60-second interactive web application that densely stacks physiological and neurological interventions to train clinicians for crisis-level cognitive demands — without requiring prolonged meditation. Five integrated modules, each grounded in the vault's evidence base and temporal distortion neuroscience.

---

## Module 1: The 60-Second Interactive Simulator (Core)

The primary interface: a 60-second guided training sequence that stacks interventions at clinically precise timing.

### Audio Entrainment

Web Audio API delivers dual-layer binaural architecture:
- **0:00-0:30:** 10 Hz alpha binaural undertone → relaxed alertness. Cortical idling that supports interoceptive processing without drowsiness. Targets auditory cortex → thalamic reticular nucleus → cortical oscillatory networks.
- **0:30-1:00:** Smooth shift to 7.83 Hz Schumann resonance (high theta) → deepens integrative processing. Theta supports memory consolidation and emotional integration.

**Evidence:** Garcia-Argibay 2019 (binaural beats meta-analysis); [[neuroharmonics]] three-layer frequency architecture.

### Haptic & Visual Breath Pacing

Cross-modal binding for screen-free, eyes-open practice:
- **Haptic:** Connected smartwatch delivers 5-second breath cycle. One tap = inhale. Two taps = extended exhale. Engages somatosensory cortex (S1 wrist region → SMA breath timing integration).
- **Visual:** Minimalist pulsing circle expands (inhale) and contracts (exhale). Peripheral-vision compatible — doesn't require focal attention.

**Physiology:** 5-second cycle = 6 breaths/minute = resonant frequency range. Maximizes respiratory sinus arrhythmia. Baroreflex engagement: reduced venous return during exhale → baroreceptor unloading → reflex bradycardia → physically decelerates heart rate.

**Evidence:** Lehrer 2000 (baroreflex resonance); Gerritsen 2018 (respiratory vagal stimulation model).

## Module 2: Exteroceptive Toggling & Attentional Gate Training

Prevents the "tunnel vision" that occurs during medical crises by training dynamic toggling between internal and external awareness.

### Dual-Task Processing

At the 30-second mark, the app instructs: "Hold two things at once — your breath and the space around you." This is the [[exteroryx]] dynamic toggling operationalized as a web interface.

### The Attentional Gate Model of Time Perception

When cognitive resources are divided between a primary task (breath awareness) and temporal processing (sense of duration), perception of time is altered. The attentional gate opens wider when attention is divided, allowing more temporal pulses to accumulate — subjectively expanding the perceived duration of the practice.

**Neural targets:** Dorsal attention network (intraparietal sulcus + frontal eye fields) in BROAD mode. Simultaneously maintains interoceptive stream through insula + exteroceptive stream through TPJ/superior colliculus.

**Clinical application:** The CRNA who can hold breath awareness AND room awareness simultaneously during an intubation crisis has trained the attentional gate to stay open under load — maintaining both self-regulation and situational awareness.

**Evidence:** [[exteroryx]] (interoceptive-exteroceptive toggling); [[focal-panoramic-paradox]] (Castle-Field Vision); McGilchrist 2009 (hemispheric broad vs. narrow attention).

## Module 3: Tachypsychia & Oddball Crisis Simulation

Trains the brain to handle the extreme temporal dilation that occurs during real crises — the "slow motion" effect reported by accident survivors, combat veterans, and clinicians during codes.

### Tachypsychia

The extreme expansion of subjective time where events appear to unfold in slow motion. Driven by massive norepinephrine release → locus coeruleus activation → enhanced encoding of every perceptual detail → subjective experience of expanded time.

### The Temporal Oddball Effect (Training Mode)

The app's visual interface periodically introduces unexpected "oddballs" within the repetitive breath-pacing sequence:
- A differently colored pulse
- A novel sound within the ambient pad
- An unexpected haptic pattern

**Mechanism:** The brain overestimates the duration of novel, unexpected stimuli compared to repetitive ones (Schindel et al. 2011). Driven by predictive coding: when prediction is violated, the brain allocates more processing resources → the moment subjectively expands.

### High-Density Processing Training

Because an oddball requires more cognitive energy to represent, it stretches the user's perceived duration of that moment. By deliberately triggering this temporal illusion in a safe, 60-second container, the app trains the user to:
- Maintain cognitive composure when time begins to dilate
- Process dense information without panic
- Recognize that tachypsychia is a FEATURE (enhanced processing), not a bug (losing control)

**Evidence:** [[temporal-distortion-neurobiological-architecture]] (predictive coding framework, oddball effect); [[criticality-metastable-readiness]] (brain near critical boundary processes time differently).

**Clinical application:** When the CRNA encounters a can't-intubate-can't-oxygenate scenario and time "slows down," they've already trained with the oddball module. The slow-motion perception isn't terrifying — it's familiar. They know how to use the expanded subjective time to make better decisions.

## Module 4: Flow State & Transient Hypofrontality Induction

Engineered to help practitioners rapidly enter flow — intense focus, effortless performance, distorted time perception.

### Quieting the Inner Critic

The app's prompts are trauma-informed and stripped of performance demands:
- "Don't change it" (no performance target)
- "No forcing" (no effort demand)
- "Whatever you notice" (no evaluation)
- "If comfortable, allow..." (no command)

This facilitates **transient hypofrontality** — a temporary reduction in prefrontal cortex activity. The dorsolateral PFC (responsible for self-consciousness, self-monitoring, overthinking) quiets, allowing the implicit system to take over.

### Automaticity

When the inner critic is offline:
- Processing speed increases (no executive bottleneck)
- Pattern recognition operates without analytical interference
- Motor sequences execute with less hesitation
- The practitioner enters the [[flow-state-wuwei-crna]] — effortless action under pressure

**Neural mechanism:** Reduced dlPFC activity → disinhibition of basal ganglia procedural circuits → faster action selection. Simultaneously, anterior insula + ACC maintain state monitoring without executive override. This is the "River of Integration" (Siegel) — flowing between chaos and rigidity.

**Evidence:** [[flow-state-wuwei-crna]]; [[trauma-informed-design]] (7 principles); Csikszentmihalyi flow theory; transient hypofrontality literature (Dietrich 2003).

## Module 5: Post-Session Neural Consolidation

### Autobiographical Encoding

The final 5 seconds: pure silence and open monitoring.

> "Whatever you noticed... that's yours."

**Mechanism:** The Default Mode Network activates in constructive mode — encoding the newly achieved physiological shift into autobiographical memory. The mPFC (self-reference) + PCC (autobiographical meaning) + hippocampus (memory consolidation) work together to stamp the session into the practitioner's long-term neural architecture.

**Why this matters:** Without the consolidation pause, the state change is transient — it dissolves when the next case starts. With the 5-second consolidation window, the brain encodes: "I was able to shift my nervous system in 60 seconds. That's mine. I can do it again."

**Evidence:** Craig 2009 (global emotional moment); Siegel 2012 (integration = mental health); [[temporal-distortion-neurobiological-architecture]] (encoding deficit → time acceleration; strong encoding → expanded temporal record).

### The Temporal Connection

The temporal distortion synthesis reveals that autobiographical encoding is the mechanism that prevents lifespan temporal acceleration (Teghil & Wittmann 2025). By stamping each NeuroMinute session into memory as a distinct, novel event, the practice literally counteracts the "time flying" phenomenon of aging. Each session is an oddball in the day's routine — a moment that stands out and expands the perceived record of time.

**A practice that slows aging's subjective acceleration of time. In 60 seconds.**

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Web Audio API
- Binaural beats generated client-side (two oscillators at slightly different frequencies)
- Smooth crossfade between alpha (10 Hz) and Schumann (7.83 Hz) at 30-second mark
- Ambient pad as third oscillator layer (low-pass filtered noise)

### Haptic Integration
- Web Bluetooth API for Watch connectivity
- Fallback: visual-only pacing when Watch not connected
- Tap patterns: single pulse (inhale), double pulse (exhale), triple pulse (session complete)

### Oddball Engine
- Pseudo-random timing (not predictable, not too frequent)
- Visual: color shift on the pulsing circle (200ms duration)
- Auditory: novel tone within the ambient pad (distinct but not jarring)
- Frequency: 2-3 oddballs per 60-second session (enough to train, not enough to overwhelm)

### Privacy
- All processing on-device (Web Audio API, no server-side audio)
- No biometric data transmitted
- Session completion logged locally only
