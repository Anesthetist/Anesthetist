Question Three

Why not beta blockers?

"A steady hand is clearly required for many procedures but I can't tell if this is the point (versus, say, beta blockers)"
◆

Vagal Tone

Vault Concept · 20+ evidence links

Beta blockers impose a ceiling on arousal. Breathing training builds a floor of resilience. Opposite mechanisms. For Don's vasovagal condition, beta blockers are contraindicated — they'd lower HR further when the problem is already inappropriate bradycardia.

urn:srl:concept:vagal-tone
◇

Surgeon HRV predicts 30-day complications

Guignard et al. · JAMA Surgery · 2025 · n=793

Autonomic state in the first 5 minutes of surgery predicted major complications. Beta blockers suppress the signal. Breathing training builds the capacity. Only one improves the surgeon's actual regulatory infrastructure.

urn:srl:evidence:guignard-2025-surgeon-stress-complications

HRV biofeedback: 13 RCTs, 965 patients

Applied Psychophysiology & Biofeedback · 2026 · Meta-analysis

Significantly improved cardiac autonomic function AND reduced blood pressure in cardiovascular disease patients. No side effects. No prescription. Complementary to pharmacology, not competing with it.
