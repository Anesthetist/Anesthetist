# Weekly Competitive Briefing — March 15, 2026

## Headline Signal

**Peloton acquired Breathwrk for $2.2M.** The #1 breathing app sold for pocket change — validating that standalone breathwork apps are severely undervalued without a hardware/enterprise moat. This is both a warning (breathwork alone is not a fundable category) and an opportunity (the acquirer paid almost nothing, meaning the market doesn't yet price breathwork IP properly). Pausality's clinical + biometric + enterprise positioning is the antidote to this valuation compression.

## Critical New Signals Since March 14 Briefing

### 1. Breathwrk Acquired by Peloton — $2.2M (October 2025)
**Previously tracked as Tier 1 competitor. Now REMOVED from competitive set.**

- Peloton acquired Breathwrk from founder Max Gomez for $2.2M on October 3, 2025
- Breathwrk had raised $7.7M from Sapphire Ventures, Bessemer, DCM Ventures, BAM Ventures — investors took a loss
- Breathwork sessions now sync with Peloton app, count toward streaks
- Deeper integration planned: breathwork embedded in warm-ups, cooldowns, recovery

**What this means for Pausality:** A cautionary tale. Breathwrk had brand, downloads, and VC backing — and sold for $2.2M. The lesson: **a breathing app without clinical validation, biometric feedback, or enterprise distribution is worth almost nothing in M&A.** Pausality's moat (CRNA clinical credibility, real-time HRV biofeedback, workflow-embedded intervention) is exactly what prevented Breathwrk from commanding a real price. Use this in investor conversations: "Breathwrk sold for $2.2M. Here's why we're not Breathwrk."

### 2. NEW COMPETITOR: NEUROFIT — "Nervous System Fitness" App
**Not previously tracked. Adding to Tier 1 watchlist.**

- Founded by Andrew Hogue (ex-Snapchat engineer) after personal experience with Complex PTSD and burnout
- 60K+ users across 100+ countries
- 500+ health professionals trained in their "Nervous System Fitness" method
- Uses phone camera for biometric measurement (HRV proxy)
- Personalized somatic exercises, 3-5 minutes
- Claims 54% stress reduction within 1 week
- Enterprise clients include DirectEmployers, Black Girls Code
- Funding: Not disclosed (likely bootstrapped or small angel round)

**Why this matters:** NEUROFIT is the closest narrative competitor to Pausality. They literally use the phrase "Nervous System Trainer" — the exact positioning Pausality occupies. However: they use phone camera (not Apple Watch), exercises are 3-5 min (not 60 sec), somatic exercises (not breathwork), and no clinical workflow integration. The overlap is in language and framing, not in mechanism or market. Watch closely for funding announcements.

### 3. NEW COMPETITOR: Ohm Health — Biofeedback Breathing Lamp
**Not previously tracked. Adding to Tier 2 watchlist.**

- Richmond, VA startup, team of 4
- $737K raised across 2 rounds (Seed, June 2025)
- Product: Ohm Resonance Lamp — tabletop light + PPG sensor creating personalized breathwork routines
- $249 preorder price, mid-2026 delivery
- Includes Jay Wiles (athletic performance psychologist) on team
- Opening new capital round early 2026

**Why this matters:** Ohm targets the same "nervous system regulation through breathwork + biofeedback" thesis, but through a physical product (lamp) rather than mobile/wearable. Consumer-focused, not clinical. Low funding, small team — not an immediate threat but worth monitoring.

### 4. Oxa Breathing Wearable ACQUIRED by Myant — Exiting Consumer Market
**Previously adjacent competitor. Now REMOVED.**

- Oxa (Swiss breathing wearable, raised €12.5M Series A) acquired by Myant (Canadian smart textiles)
- Consumer product is being sunset — device support ends October 2026
- Pivoting to medical certification within Myant's Skiin product line

**What this means:** One fewer competitor in the consumer breathing wearable space. Their €12.5M+ in funding didn't translate to a standalone consumer business. Reinforces that hardware-first breathing companies struggle without clinical or enterprise distribution.

### 5. Calm Revenue Dropped 24% — Losing Subscribers
- 2025 estimated revenue: $210M (down from ~$276M in 2024)
- Lost ~500K subscribers (3.5M from 4M)
- Pursuing brand licensing as new growth strategy
- Calm Health (enterprise/clinical) now a separate strategic bet

**What this means:** The mindfulness app gold rush is over. Calm's B2C model is compressing. Their enterprise pivot (Calm Health) is the survival play. This validates Pausality's enterprise-first strategy — consumer apps are a race to the bottom without clinical differentiation.

## Narrative Watch

**"Health as an Operating System"** remains the dominant VC framing (Fitt Insider 2026 thesis). The implication: wellness companies are being valued not as standalone products but as **protocol layers** within an integrated health stack. Winners will be those that generate unique data (HRV coherence, ANS state transitions) that no other layer provides.

**"The M&A Wave Is Finally Here"** — MedCity News (Feb 2026) and STAT News both confirm digital health consolidation is accelerating. Private equity is driving deals alongside strategic acquirers. Companies with clinical validation + enterprise contracts are being acquired; consumer-only plays are being acqui-hired for pennies (see: Breathwrk).

**Key quote from STAT News:** "As consumer behaviour shifts and therapeutic markets evolve, investors are prioritising platforms that combine strong engagement, data-driven personalisation, and clear pathways to value-based growth."
