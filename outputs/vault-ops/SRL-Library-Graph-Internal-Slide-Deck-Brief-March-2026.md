## SLIDE 3: Three International Standards

This isn't "Randy's special system." It's standards-compliant knowledge architecture.

**Dublin Core (ISO 15836)** — Metadata on every note. Title, creator, date, subject tags, DOI, identifier. The same standard libraries and academic databases use worldwide.

**SKOS** — Concept relationships. Every idea linked to parents (broader), children (narrower), and siblings (related). Creates a traversable knowledge graph.

**PROV-O** — Provenance chains. Every claim traces to its source. When someone asks "what's the evidence?" the vault answers with specific citations and the chain connecting them.
