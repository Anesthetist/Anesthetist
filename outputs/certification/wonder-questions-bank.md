# Wonder Questions Bank

## What this is

Twenty-one pedagogical openers that destabilize the assumption of self-knowledge. Each opens a gap between what a clinician thinks they know about their body and what they can actually answer. The gap is the wedge where training becomes possible.

These are not quiz items. They are attention devices. A candidate who guesses is already more awake than one who did not know the question was pose-able.

## How to use

One wonder question opens:

- A NeuroMinute audio (voiceover, first 5 seconds)
- A certification module (slide 1, no visual)
- A keynote or podcast segment
- A landing-page hero
- A clinical huddle or team brief
- A sales conversation with a skeptic
- A pre-assessment diagnostic across the six competencies

The placement matrix at the end of this file tags each question for its strongest venue.

## Voice rules

- Second person throughout.
- No command language. The question is the invitation.
- No follow-up instruction. Let the silence after the question do the work.
- Clinical-metaphor allowed. Mystical framing not allowed.

## The twenty-one questions

### Group A. Self-measurement you have never taken

**Q1. What is your resting heart rate right now, without a device?**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C5 closed-loop
- Function: destabilize, measure
- Literature anchor: Schandry 1981 (heartbeat detection paradigm); Garfinkel 2015 (three-dimension interoception framework)
- Placement: pre-assessment, NeuroMinute opener for day 1, landing page

**Q2. How many sighs have you taken in the last hour?**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C3 breath
- Function: destabilize, provoke
- Literature anchor: Li 2016 (peptidergic sigh generator); Ramirez lab work on physiological sighing at roughly one per 5 to 7 minutes
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener, keynote hook, clinical huddle

**Q3. When you exhale all the way out, how long until the first demand to breathe?**
- Competency: C3 breath, C5 closed-loop
- Function: measure, destabilize
- Literature anchor: McKeown BOLT methodology; Lehrer 2013 resonant breathing mechanism
- Placement: breath module opener, pre-assessment, candidate self-test

### Group B. Body signals you have stopped noticing

**Q4. Is your tongue on the roof of your mouth right now?**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C3 breath (airway posture)
- Function: invite, destabilize
- Literature anchor: airway posture and nasal breathing literature; orofacial myofunctional therapy corpus
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener, safety module S1

**Q5. Which nostril is more open?**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C3 breath
- Function: destabilize, invite
- Literature anchor: Eccles nasal cycle literature (90 to 240 minute alternation); Keuroghlian & Kalin (nasal cycle and autonomic tone)
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener for day 8 alternate nostril, keynote hook

**Q6. Where is your weight right now? Which foot, which side of each foot?**
- Competency: C1 interoception (proprioceptive), C2 attention
- Function: invite, destabilize
- Literature anchor: proprioception literature; postural control under cognitive load
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener, module opener for attention domain

**Q7. Where are your eyes resting when they have nothing to do?**
- Competency: C2 attention, C4 state
- Function: invite, destabilize
- Literature anchor: Porges 2011 (ventral vagal visual field softening); oculomotor literature on rest gaze
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener for day 18 peripheral vision, keynote hook

### Group C. Environmental attention you do not extend

**Q8. How much time do you spend using your peripheral vision as your primary source of visual information?** (seed)
- Competency: C2 attention, C4 state
- Function: destabilize
- Literature anchor: Porges 2011 ventral vagal; visual field literature on foveal vs peripheral dominance under threat
- Placement: keynote opener, landing page hero, pre-assessment

**Q9. Without looking, in what phase is the moon?** (seed)
- Competency: C1 environmental attention
- Function: destabilize, provoke
- Literature anchor: chronobiology corpus; Wittmann on embodied time; hunter-gatherer default attention literature
- Placement: keynote opener, podcast hook, integration module

**Q10. When did you last drink water? Last pee? Last take a full exhale?**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C5 closed-loop
- Function: destabilize, measure
- Literature anchor: basic homeostatic accuracy; clinician dehydration and decision-making literature
- Placement: clinical huddle, CRNA-specific marketing

### Group D. Time perception

**Q11. Estimate 60 seconds without counting.**
- Competency: C1 interoception, C2 attention
- Function: measure, provoke
- Literature anchor: Wittmann corpus on interoceptive-temporal binding in insular cortex; project_embodied_time_perception
- Placement: pre-assessment, keynote demonstration, integration module

**Q12. How often do you feel like you are living in the present moment?** (seed)
- Competency: C4 state, C2 attention
- Function: destabilize
- Literature anchor: MAIA-2 Not-Distracting and Self-Regulation subscales; Wittmann embodied time
- Placement: landing page hero, keynote opener, baseline MAIA-2 adjunct

### Group E. Breath mechanics

**Q13. What percentage of the day are you breathing through your nose?** (seed)
- Competency: C1 interoception, C3 breath
- Function: destabilize
- Literature anchor: McKeown nasal breathing literature; Buteyko method corpus
- Placement: NeuroMinute opener for nasal-breath modules, landing page hero

**Q14. What is your longest easy inhale and exhale? Easily, not forced.** (seed)
- Competency: C3 breath, C5 closed-loop
- Function: measure, destabilize
- Literature anchor: Lehrer 2013 resonant frequency; McKeown BOLT; Zaccaro 2018 slow breathing review
- Placement: breath module opener, candidate self-test, pre-assessment

### Group F. Mechanism wonders

**Q15. Why did we evolve to have conscious control of our breath?** (seed)
- Competency: C3 breath at the mechanism layer
- Function: provoke
- Literature anchor: Bramble & Lieberman 2004 (endurance running); MacLarnon & Hewitt 1999 (thoracic innervation for speech); Porges 2011 (social engagement system)
- Placement: keynote opener, Module F1 didactic, landing page secondary hero

**Q16. Why does humming vibrate your vagus nerve?**
- Competency: C3 breath, C4 state
- Function: provoke
- Literature anchor: Maniscalco 2003 nitric oxide release during humming; vagal stimulation literature on cervical acoustic coupling
- Placement: module opener, NeuroMinute opener, keynote hook

**Q17. Why does your heart beat faster on inhalation than exhalation?**
- Competency: C3 breath, C4 state
- Function: provoke, measure
- Literature anchor: Grossman respiratory sinus arrhythmia corpus; Lehrer 2013 baroreflex mechanism
- Placement: HRV module didactic, RSA demonstration in pre-assessment

**Q18. Why do yawns spread?**
- Competency: C4 state, social engagement
- Function: provoke
- Literature anchor: Gallup yawn literature (thermoregulatory account, contested); mirror neuron corpus; social contagion and co-regulation
- Placement: keynote hook, co-regulation module opener (day 19 NeuroMinute)

### Group G. Social and clinical attention

**Q19. When you walked into this room, did you soften or brace?**
- Competency: C4 state, C1 interoception (social)
- Function: invite, destabilize
- Literature anchor: Porges 2011 neuroception
- Placement: clinical huddle, team brief, keynote opener

**Q20. What is the heart rate of the person across from you?**
- Competency: C4 state (social), C1 interoception (extended field)
- Function: provoke, invite
- Literature anchor: social HRV coupling literature; co-regulation and dyadic autonomic synchrony
- Placement: module opener for co-regulation, keynote demonstration

### Group H. Safety wedge

**Q21. What would happen in your body if you hyperventilated for two full minutes? Do you know the sequence?**
- Competency: C6 safety, C3 breath
- Function: gate (qualifying question for safety competency)
- Literature anchor: Meuret 2008 CART; respiratory pattern disorder literature; hypocapnic tetany physiology
- Placement: safety module S1 opener, pre-assessment for safety domain, qualifying question for trainer track
