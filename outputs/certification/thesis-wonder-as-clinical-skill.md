# Wonder as Clinical Skill

*Without looking at a watch: how long has it been since your last full exhale?*

Most clinicians cannot answer. That gap is where this certification begins.

## The lost signal

A CRNA monitors nervous systems for a living. Heart rate, blood pressure, end-tidal CO₂, depth of anesthesia. Thousands of data points per case. A twenty-eight-year career runs into hundreds of thousands of patient-hours where another human's autonomic state is watched with clinical precision.

The clinician's own autonomic state, across those same hours, goes unobserved. Not from negligence. From a kind of attentional anesthesia that clinical training installs. Attention outward. Signal inward muted. It protects function in the short term. It erodes regulation over a career.

The literature describes a pattern of interoceptive suppression in clinical populations. Attention is trained outward. Interoceptive signal dulls. The signal is still there. Wonder about it is not.

## What we teach

Somnistics certification teaches wonder as a clinical skill.

Wonder is not a mood. It is a restored willingness to ask questions about one's own physiology. Which nostril is open. How long a comfortable exhale can run. Whether the body softened or braced on entering the room. What happens in the cascade of two full minutes of hyperventilation.

A clinician who can wonder about these things can measure them. A clinician who can measure them can train them. A clinician who can train them carries tools into shift work, high-acuity moments, and the twenty-year trajectory of a career that used to end in burnout.

Wonder without measurement is nostalgia. Measurement without wonder is compliance. The loop between them is the skill.

## How we measure

The certification operationalizes wonder across six competencies. Each has a numeric floor drawn from the literature.

1. **Interoceptive literacy.** Detect internal signals, label them, trust them. MAIA-2 Body Listening ≥ 3.0; heartbeat detection above chance.
2. **Attention regulation.** Sustain body-focused attention under cognitive load. Breath Counting Task accuracy ≥ 0.80.
3. **Voluntary breath control.** Diaphragmatic mechanics, CO₂ tolerance, resonant-frequency breathing. BOLT ≥ 25 seconds; sustained six-breath-per-minute protocol with visible HRV amplitude rise.
4. **Autonomic state literacy.** Classify own state across ten clinical moments with ≥ 70 percent concordance against HRV.
5. **Closed-loop feedback.** Own an eight-week trajectory on a self-selected metric. Interpretation, not outcome, is the pass criterion.
6. **Safety and contraindication literacy.** Recognize and abort five high-risk scenarios.

Each floor traces to a canonical source. Each threshold produces a measurable artifact the candidate keeps.

## What certification means

A Somnistics Certified clinician does not claim calm. Calm is not a clinical target. The certified clinician claims something more specific and more defensible.

They can name their autonomic state before entering a case. They can identify the breath protocol that shifts state fastest in their own physiology. They can track a metric across months and read what it says. They can refuse to teach breath retention to a patient for whom retention is contraindicated.

They have restored the signal their training once muted.

## Why this is defensible

Three things combine here that no competitor has all of at once.

1. **Clinical credibility.** Built by a CRNA with twenty-eight years of high-acuity anesthesia practice. The physiology is not imported. It is inhabited.
2. **Pedagogical novelty.** The program opens with destabilization, not instruction. A candidate who guesses at a wonder question is already more awake than one who did not know the question was pose-able. This is not a technique a competitor can copy in a sprint.
3. **Measurable outcomes.** Every competency has a numeric floor backed by peer-reviewed literature. Every candidate walks away with a portfolio of their own data. Wellness programs do not ship receipts. This one does.

## The clinician this produces

A CRNA who has spent eight weeks inside this program does not finish the way a CE-attendance certificate finishes. They finish with their own resting HRV curve, their own BOLT trajectory, their identified resonant breathing frequency, and a documented capacity to classify their autonomic state before a case begins.

Those artifacts do not lapse when the certification cycle ends. They travel into the next case, the next shift, the next decade of practice.
