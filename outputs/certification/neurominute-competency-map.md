## What this tells us

1. **Breath dominates.** 19 of 21 days touch breath mechanics. That is the product's core. The 21-day sequence is a breathing progression with interoception, attention, and state work layered in.

2. **Closed-loop feedback is thin.** One script primary, three supporting. The certification cannot assess this competency from script exposure alone. Didactic content plus the 8-week trajectory project has to carry it.

3. **Safety is absent.** Zero days primary, one supporting (day 11 breath-hold has implicit CO₂ exposure). The certification cannot produce a credentialed clinician from the NeuroMinutes alone. Safety didactic modules and the 5-scenario exam are mandatory.

4. **Interoception and state build through the sequence.** Both start light (days 1 to 6), deepen mid-program (12 to 15), and consolidate at the end (18 to 21). The sequence logic is sound.

5. **Attention is consistent throughout.** Not domain-loaded to any one week. That is correct pedagogy; attention is a transversal skill.

## Gaps to fill with didactic content

### Closed-loop feedback curriculum

Three didactic modules needed:

- **Module F1:** How to measure HRV at home (ring, watch, or belt). What RMSSD, HF power, and coherence mean. How to distinguish signal from noise. Canonical anchor: Laborde 2017 methods guide.
- **Module F2:** How to track a metric across 8 weeks. Confounders: sleep debt, shift schedule, alcohol, menstrual cycle, training load. Canonical anchor: Schwartz and Andrasik biofeedback chapter.
- **Module F3:** How to interpret a trajectory. When a flat line is a win. When a decline signals a confound, not a failure. Canonical anchor: Yucha and Montgomery evidence levels.

### Safety and contraindication curriculum

Three didactic modules needed:

- **Module S1:** Respiratory safety. Hyperventilation physiology. Tetany recognition. When to abort. Populations at risk. Canonical anchor: Meuret 2008 CART.
- **Module S2:** Cardiovascular safety. Valsalva risks, vasovagal cascade, breath-hold contraindications. Populations at risk. Canonical anchor: standard cardiovascular physiology texts plus breathing pattern disorder literature.
- **Module S3:** Psychiatric safety. Panic provocation in anxious populations. Dissociation risk in trauma histories. When to refer out. Canonical anchor: Khalsa 2018 clinical interoception roadmap; trauma-informed care literature.

### Under-covered days for breath

Days 12 (heart-focused-gratitude) and 18 (peripheral-vision-breath) do not carry breath work as primary. This is not a gap. Those scripts carry interoception and state work that the other 19 days under-cover. Protect the variety.
