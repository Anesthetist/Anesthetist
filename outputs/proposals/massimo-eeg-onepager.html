What We Measure

Alpha / Theta Ratio

The balance between focused calm (alpha waves, 8–13 Hz) and early fatigue or drift (theta waves, 4–8 Hz). High ratio = sharp and regulated. Low ratio = your brain is signaling fatigue before you feel it.

Frontal Alpha Asymmetry

Compares left vs. right forehead activity. Left-dominant alpha = approach motivation, engagement. Right-dominant = withdrawal, avoidance. Tells us your default motivational stance before a race.

Band Power Spectrum

Full breakdown across five frequency bands: Delta (deep recovery), Theta (drowsiness/creativity), Alpha (calm focus), Beta (active thinking), Gamma (peak processing). Your brain's energy budget.

Cognitive Load Response

How your brain waves change under pressure. We compare resting state to a simulated cognitive challenge. If theta spikes under load, your brain fatigues faster than it should — and that's trainable.

What Your Alpha/Theta Ratio Tells Us
> 1.5 Focused calm. Alpha dominant. Nervous system regulated, ready for precision work. The target state for race-day.
1.0 – 1.5 Alert. Balanced alpha and theta. Normal waking state. Functional but not optimized.
0.6 – 1.0 Theta rising. Early cognitive fatigue. Lap times start getting inconsistent here — before you feel tired.
< 0.6 Theta dominant. Deep fatigue, drowsiness, or inward focus. In a race car, this is the danger zone.
The insight for racing: Theta intrusion under load is the invisible performance thief. Your lap times degrade, your reaction time slows, your peripheral vision narrows — and you don't notice because it happens below conscious awareness. The EEG makes it visible. Once it's visible, it's trainable.

The Device

Muse 2 — research-validated consumer EEG headband. 4 channels, 256 samples/second. Used in over 200 peer-reviewed studies. Weighs 36 grams. Sits comfortably on the forehead with sensors behind each ear. No gel, no preparation, no discomfort. Data processed through BrainFlow (open-source, used by universities and research labs worldwide) and MNE-Python (the gold standard for EEG analysis in neuroscience).

What You Get

Full brain activity report — your band power spectrum across all four channels, alpha/theta ratio at rest and under load, frontal asymmetry score with interpretation, and a comparison to published normative ranges. At Week 6, we run it again and overlay the results. You see exactly what changed in your brain activity after 6 weeks of training.
