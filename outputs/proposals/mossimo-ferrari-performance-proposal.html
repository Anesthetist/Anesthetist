The Challenge

Mossimo is sympathetically charged before races and has difficulty recovering after them. He's amped up, running hot, and looking to feel more comfortable — not less intense, but more controlled.

Elite motorsport demands a paradox: maximum alertness with minimum physiological waste. A driver who is sympathetically overdrive burns through autonomic reserves before the final stint. The heart rate is elevated. The grip tightens. The peripheral vision narrows. Reaction time degrades not from fatigue, but from autonomic inefficiency — the nervous system spending energy on threat response instead of channeling it into precision.

The edge isn't more adrenaline. It's better regulation of the adrenaline you already have.

The goal: Improve Mossimo's autonomic baseline (resting HRV, resting HR, blood pressure) and decrease the time-to-recovery after high-load events. Not calming him down — giving his nervous system a faster return-to-ready between stints, between sessions, between race weekends.

6-Week Program

One-minute practices, five times daily, across three progressive phases. Every session tracked via real-time Apple Watch body data.

Phase 1 — Weeks 1-2

Foundation: Finding Your Baseline

5 sessions/day × 1 minute = 5 minutes total
  • Discover personal resonant breathing frequency
  • Establish morning HRV baseline routine
  • Box breathing (5/5/5/5) for autonomic calibration
  • Pre-sleep recovery breathing protocol
  • Daily BOLT score tracking
Phase 2 — Weeks 3-4

Awareness: Sensing Under Load

5 sessions/day + 2 coached sessions/week
  • Extended exhale protocols (4/7/8) for deep recovery response
  • Rapid Reg (3/3/3/3) for acute stress windows
  • Heart rate recovery drills after physical exertion
  • Simulated race-day autonomic training
  • Interoceptive attention during athletic training
Phase 3 — Weeks 5-6

Mastery: Race-Day Integration

5 sessions/day + race-day protocol design
  • Pre-race activation protocol (sympathetic priming WITHOUT overdrive)
  • In-cockpit micro-regulation between sessions
  • Post-race recovery acceleration
  • Anesthetist's Breath (10/0/10/0) for deep vagal reset
  • Personal Gap Moment protocol for race weekends

Week-by-Week

Week 0

In-Person Assessment

2-hour session with Randy. Full autonomic portrait: HRV, HR, BP, BOLT, EEG, MAIA-2, physical markers. Personalize the program based on findings. Set up Apple Watch tracking. Install Pausality.

Week 1

Resonant Frequency Discovery

Find your personal optimal breathing rate. Establish the daily 5x1-minute routine. Morning HRV tracking begins. First coached session (virtual or in-person).

Week 2

Baseline Stabilization

Box breathing mastery. Pre-sleep protocol. Monitor HRV trend for first inflection. Second coached session. First data review.

Week 3

Load Introduction

Extended exhale protocols. Heart rate recovery drills after workouts. Simulated pre-race state induction + regulation practice. Coached session.

Week 4

Acute Stress Protocols

Rapid Reg (3/3/3/3) for acute windows. Midpoint data review with Randy. Adjust protocols based on 4 weeks of HRV data. Coached session.

Week 5

Race-Day Protocol Design

Design personalized pre-race, in-session, and post-race protocols. Anesthetist's Breath for deep recovery. Coached session. Practice race-day sequence.

Week 6

Final Assessment + Race-Day Deployment

Full re-assessment: HRV, HR, BP, BOLT, EEG, MAIA-2, physical markers. Compare all metrics to baseline. Deliver race-day protocol card. Final coached session. Ongoing support options.
