Train your nervous system
like you train your car.

Your car gets a pit stop every session. Your nervous system doesn't. Lap times get inconsistent by session 3. Reaction time slows. You don't notice until it costs you a position.

The Problem

State drift

Over a session, your autonomic nervous system gradually shifts from optimal to degraded — heart rate elevated, recovery incomplete between runs, pattern recognition slower. You can't feel it happening. But the lap times show it.

The Tool

60 seconds. Your watch. Between sessions.

Breathing synced to a guided pace. Your Apple Watch measures your heart rate dropping in real time. Not meditation. A pit stop for your nervous system — measurable, visible, fast.

The Edge

Faster recovery = more consistent laps

Heart rate recovery — how fast your HR drops after stress — is the single strongest predictor of sustained performance. Drivers with higher HRV show less lap time variance across a session. Train that number and the consistency follows.

What we'd want to explore
1

Wear the Watch during a track session. See your heart rate data across every run. Find where the drift starts — when your HR stops recovering between runs.

2

Try the 21-day protocol. 60 seconds, 3x a day. Before your next race weekend. Progressive — gets harder as your nervous system gets stronger.

3

Compare before and after. Lap time consistency. Reaction time. Heart rate recovery between runs. See if the numbers move.

The driver who recovers fastest between runs is the driver who's still sharp in the final session.
