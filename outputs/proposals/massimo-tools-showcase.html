Measurement

What We Measure

Full autonomic portrait. Not a single number — a complete map of how your nervous system performs at rest, under load, and during recovery.

♥

Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

Primary biomarker

RMSSD — the millisecond-level variation between heartbeats. The single best indicator of your nervous system's capacity to shift between activation and recovery. Higher = more adaptable.

Measured via Polar H10 chest strap + Apple Watch. Daily morning readings track your trend over the program.
↕

Blood Pressure + Orthostatic Response

Baroreflex

Seated and standing blood pressure. The drop between positions tests your baroreflex — how fast your nervous system compensates for position changes. Relevant every time you go from standing to cockpit to standing.

Auto BP cuff. 3 readings each position. Orthostatic response measured as systolic delta.
θ

EEG Brain Activity

Neural patterns

Alpha/theta ratio at rest and under cognitive load. Alpha dominance = focused calm. Theta intrusion under pressure = your brain signaling fatigue before you feel it. Frontal asymmetry reveals approach vs. withdrawal tendency.

Consumer-grade EEG (Muse 2 / Emotiv). 3 conditions: eyes closed, eyes open, simulated race load. Pre and post program.
♪

BOLT Score (CO2 Tolerance)

Respiratory

Body Oxygen Level Test. Normal exhale, then time to first involuntary breathing impulse. Tests your CO2 tolerance — a key marker of autonomic flexibility and how your body handles the buildup of metabolic stress.

Simple timed test. Elite athletes: 30-40s. Below 20s = significant room for gains. Weekly tracking.
↺

Recovery Curve

Performance metric

How fast your heart rate and HRV return to baseline after a standardized stress test. This is the number that translates directly to racing: faster autonomic recovery = faster return-to-ready between stints.

Erg test at 80% max HR for 3 minutes, then measure time-to-baseline. Pre and post program comparison.
⇂

Cardiac Efficiency

Output per beat

Joules per heartbeat at standardized athletic load. More efficient = lower heart rate at the same output = more autonomic headroom in the car. Your nervous system burning less fuel for the same performance.

Concept2 erg at fixed wattage. Power output / heart rate = efficiency index.
