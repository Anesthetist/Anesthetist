The Opportunity

MUSC has the infrastructure, the mandate, and the people. What's missing is the tool.

MUSC is already doing this work. Dr. Salgado holds the institution's first-ever Vice Chair for Wellness position. Dr. Streck leads a Level I Trauma Center where biannual burnout surveys and active wellness monitoring are standard. The Surgery Department runs wellness events and tracks resident stress. The institutional commitment exists. What's missing is a scalable, measurable, workflow-integrated wellness tool that works during the shift — not before it, not after it.

The primary barrier clinicians report isn't willingness — it's time. 64.5% say they don't have time for stress management during the workday. Traditional wellness programs ask clinicians to meditate before or after shifts. But the autonomic load happens during the shift. The gap between cases. The handoff. The thirty seconds before the next critical moment.

Pausality is designed for exactly those moments: one-minute breathing practices with real-time heart rate tracking via the wearable devices clinicians already own.

What We Propose

A 12-week pilot with MUSC's surgical residents and trauma team, measuring the impact of one-minute breathing practices on wellness, recovery, and team readiness.

Pilot Structure

Phase 1 — Weeks 1-2

Baseline & Enrollment

Setup + assessment
  • Enroll 20-40 surgical residents and trauma team members
  • Baseline wellness surveys (validated instruments: MAIA-2, perceived stress scale)
  • Baseline heart rate variability trends via personal wearables
  • Install Pausality app, pair Apple Watch or AirPods Pro
  • Brief orientation session with Randy and Jason
Phase 2 — Weeks 3-10

Active Practice Period

8 weeks, 5x daily, 1 minute each
  • Progressive 21-session breathing curriculum via Pausality
  • Participants practice 1-minute sessions during natural workflow gaps (between cases, during turnover, before/after rounds)
  • Real-time heart rate tracking during each session
  • Weekly anonymized engagement and adherence data shared with MUSC wellness leadership
  • Bi-weekly check-ins with Randy for protocol guidance
Phase 3 — Weeks 11-12

Assessment & Report

Post-program measurement + analysis
  • Repeat baseline wellness surveys (MAIA-2, perceived stress scale)
  • Compare HRV trends: pre-program vs. post-program
  • Adherence analysis: who practiced, how often, when in the shift
  • Qualitative feedback: what worked, what didn't, workflow fit
  • Final report delivered to Dr. Streck, Dr. Salgado, and MUSC wellness leadership
