# Book Launch Strategy Research: Health/Wellness/Performance Nonfiction (2026)

Research compiled March 2026. Oriented toward a 28-year CRNA with clinical credibility, a knowledge graph with 200+ linked notes, an app in market, CEU pending, no existing book platform, a Mayo Clinic user, and a Don Katz connection.

---

## 1. The Book-to-Business Pipeline

### How Bestselling Health/Wellness Authors Turned Books into Enterprises

**The Brene Brown Model: Book to Certification Empire**
- Published five #1 NYT bestsellers, then created the Dare to Lead Facilitator Certification and The Daring Way certification programs
- Certified facilitators pay for training and licensing, then deliver programs inside organizations
- Brown maintains IP control through trademarked materials and certification requirements
- Revenue streams: book royalties + certification fees + speaking fees + enterprise licensing
- Key insight: The book creates the demand; the certification scales the delivery without scaling the author

**The Deb Dana Model: Clinical Book to Training Ecosystem**
- Published *The Polyvagal Theory in Therapy* (W.W. Norton, 2018) for clinicians, then *Anchored* (Sounds True) for general audience
- Created the Foundations of Polyvagal Informed Practice training series: 40+ hours of learning materials
- Dual-channel strategy: clinical publications for therapists + general audience materials for broader public
- Sells card decks, flip charts, workbooks, exercises as ancillary products
- Key insight: Two publishers for two audiences. Norton for clinical credibility, Sounds True for consumer reach

**The Kristin Neff Model: Research to Certification Program**
- Published *Self-Compassion* (book), then co-created the Mindful Self-Compassion (MSC) program with Chris Germer
- MSC is an empirically-supported 8-week course, also available as 5-day intensive
- Co-founded the Center for Mindful Self-Compassion
- MSC Teacher Training is a 6-day residential program; certified teachers can deliver the 8-week program
- Published *Teaching the Mindful Self-Compassion Program* as a professional guide
- Key insight: The research base came first, then the book, then the program, then the teacher training, then the teaching manual

**The James Clear Model: Newsletter to Book to App**
- Wrote online at JamesClear.com for 10 years before publishing *Atomic Habits*
- Grew newsletter to 3M+ subscribers organically (no paid ads)
- Newsletter format: 3 ideas, 2 quotes, 1 question each week
- Ran solo for 5 years, used book advance to hire first employee
- Recently launched Atoms app (the official Atomic Habits app)
- Key insight: The platform came first. Clear spent a decade building audience before the book existed

**The Van der Kolk Model: Clinical Authority to Slow-Burn Bestseller**
- *The Body Keeps the Score* published 2014, didn't become a phenomenon until the pandemic (2020-2021)
- 376 weeks on the NYT bestseller list as of January 2026 (7+ years)
- No flashy launch. Succeeded on clinical authority + word-of-mouth + cultural moment
- Case studies from clinical practice + autobiographical reflections + sharp critiques of mainstream views
- Key insight: Clinical depth and genuine expertise can create a slow-burn bestseller that outlasts any launch campaign. The pandemic created the cultural moment

**The James Nestor Model: Journalist to Podcast Circuit Bestseller**
- *Breath* sold 3M+ copies, published in 44 languages
- Instant NYT bestseller driven heavily by podcast appearances
- Appeared on Tim Ferriss, Rich Roll, and dozens of health/wellness podcasts
- Also appeared on ABC Nightline, CBS Morning News, NPR
- Key insight: Podcasts were the primary engine. Health/wellness audiences live on podcasts

**The Kelly McGonigal Model: Stanford Course to Book**
- *The Upside of Stress* began as "Living Well with Stress," a Stanford course
- Academic credibility + accessible writing + practical exercises
- Key insight: Course-first, book-second. The book is a scaled version of what was already working in person

**The Gabor Mate Model: Clinical Authority + Speaking Tour**
- Four books, all Canadian bestsellers, translated into 15+ languages
- *The Myth of Normal* spent 19 weeks on NYT bestseller list
- Maintains busy international speaking schedule, generally booked 8-10 months in advance
- Four of his books appeared simultaneously on the Globe and Mail bestseller list in 2024
- Key insight: Books have 20+ year shelf lives when backed by genuine clinical authority. Speaking creates ongoing demand

### The Pattern Across All Models

1. **Clinical/research credibility came first** (not platform, not followers)
2. **The book is the gateway drug**, not the main revenue source
3. **Training/certification is where scale happens** without scaling the author
4. **Speaking creates a virtuous cycle**: speaking sells books, books create speaking demand
5. **Multiple books over time** compound authority and backlist revenue
6. **The IP ecosystem matters more than any single book**

## 2. Platform Building (Pre-Launch)

### What to Build Before the Book Exists

**Email List (Most Critical Asset)**
- Email marketing is ~40x more effective at converting than social media
- Authors truly own their email list (unlike social platform followers)
- Consistency matters more than frequency: commit to one platform for at least one year
- Lead magnet strategy: offer a useful checklist, free chapter, or assessment tool
- Target: 1,000-5,000 engaged subscribers before book launch is strong for a debut
- For Randy: A "Resilience Score" self-assessment or "5 NeuroMinute Practices" PDF would be natural lead magnets

**Substack / Newsletter**
- Establish a weekly or biweekly cadence sharing ideas from the book
- Format that works: short insight + clinical vignette + practical exercise (mirrors the NeuroMinute concept)
- Substack also functions as a portfolio that agents and publishers can evaluate
- Can migrate to paid tier later, but start free to build audience

**LinkedIn (Primary Social Platform for This Author)**
- In 2026, LinkedIn's algorithm favors specificity over promotion, quality + quantity, and short-form video
- Organic reach has declined 65% vs. 2024, but strategic commenting on 10-20 posts daily boosts profile views by 50%
- Carousel/PDF posts are the top-performing content format for engagement
- Short-form video outperforms all other visual formats (84% of marketers report increased engagement)
- For Randy: Clinical vignettes, polyvagal explainers, "what I learned in 28 years in the OR" content
- Engage actively in CRNA, anesthesia, and healthcare leadership communities

**Podcast Appearances (Not Hosting)**
- Health/wellness audiences live on podcasts. This was the primary engine for Nestor, Mate, and many others
- Target shows: Huberman Lab, Rich Roll, Tim Ferriss, Dr. Rangan Chatterjee, The Model Health Show, Liz Moody
- Also target niche clinical podcasts: CRNA-specific shows, nursing leadership, perioperative care
- Personalize pitches: reference specific episodes, the host's style, inside jokes
- Framework-based pitches work best: "One big idea wrapped in a framework"
- Wellness podcasts peak Monday mornings 7-8 AM; 55% of listeners under 35; 61% women

**Conference Speaking**
- AANA Mid-Year Assembly 2026 (Washington, D.C.) is a natural fit
- HLTH USA 2026 (Las Vegas, Nov 15-18): 12,000+ attendees, accepts 5-10% of 2,000+ speaker applications, deadline likely spring/summer 2026
- Summit Anesthesia Seminars: CRNA-owned conferences
- State CRNA association conferences
- Speaking creates book demand; books create speaking demand (virtuous cycle)

**TikTok/BookTok Assessment**
- BookTok is dominated by fiction, romance, and YA -- not the primary channel for health/science nonfiction
- However, "ScienceTok" and wellness content niches do exist
- Lower priority than LinkedIn, podcasts, and Substack for this specific author profile
- Consider later, not first

### Platform Building Timeline (Pre-Book)

| Month | Action | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 1-2 | Launch Substack, set up email capture on somnistics.com | First 100 subscribers |
| 1-3 | Begin LinkedIn posting 3x/week with clinical vignettes | 500 connections in target audience |

## 3. Publishing Path Analysis

### Traditional Publishing

**What it is:** Submit via literary agent to major/mid-size publisher. Publisher handles editing, design, distribution, some marketing.

**Pros for Randy:**
- Prestige and credibility signal (matters for CEU approval, enterprise sales, clinical audiences)
- Professional editing, design, distribution handled
- Advance payment ($5,000-$50,000 typical for debut nonfiction; $10,000-$75,000 for nonfiction with platform)
- Bookstore placement and library distribution
- Foreign rights sales potential

**Cons for Randy:**
- 18-36 months from acceptance to publication (too slow for current market timing?)
- Loss of control over pricing, cover, content edits
- 70%+ of books never earn out their advance
- Agent required (acceptance rate 1-3% from 200-500 queries/month)
- Limited control over IP reuse for CEU/app content
- Standard royalties: 10-15% hardcover, 25% ebook

**Agent Requirements (2025-2026):**
- ~75% of nonfiction deals are influenced by author credentials, professional reputation, and online presence
- Agents want: unique methodology/framework, clear target audience, competitive title analysis, marketing plan
- Sharply focused query highlighting unique selling points is critical
- For Randy: Clinical credentials are strong. Platform is the gap to fill

### Hybrid Publishing

**What it is:** Author invests in production; publisher provides editorial, design, marketing support. Author retains more rights and higher royalties.

**Pros for Randy:**
- Faster timeline (6-12 months vs. 18-36)
- Higher royalties (60-100% vs. 10-25%)
- Greater control over content, cover, pricing
- Author retains rights (critical for CEU/app content reuse)
- Professional quality without full DIY burden

**Cons for Randy:**
- Upfront cost: $5,000-$80,000 depending on services
- Less prestige signal than traditional (though this gap is narrowing)
- Quality varies enormously between hybrid publishers; must vet carefully
- No advance payment
- Distribution may be weaker than traditional

**Best hybrid publishers to evaluate:** Atmosphere Press, Greenleaf Book Group (specifically focused on business/thought leadership books), Amplify Publishing

### Self-Publishing (Amazon KDP + Distribution)

**What it is:** Author manages everything directly. Full creative and financial control.

**Pros for Randy:**
- Total control over content, timing, pricing, IP reuse
- Highest royalties (up to 70% on ebooks)
- Fastest to market
- ALLi 2025 survey: indie author median income $13,500/year (vs. $6,000-$8,000 for traditionally published)
- Can update content instantly (important for a fast-moving field)
- Full rights retention for CEU, app, enterprise licensing

**Cons for Randy:**
- No prestige signal (may matter for clinical audiences and enterprise buyers)
- Must coordinate editing, design, marketing independently
- No bookstore placement without distribution deal
- Perceived as lower quality by some institutional buyers

### Recommendation for Randy

**Hybrid publishing (Greenleaf Book Group tier) OR strategic self-publishing with professional production.**

Rationale:
1. Randy needs to retain IP rights for CEU materials, app content, and enterprise licensing -- traditional publishing restricts this
2. The 18-36 month traditional timeline conflicts with the current market window (Red Arrow #2: Consumer Traction to Enterprise Proof)
3. Clinical credibility comes from 28 years as a CRNA and the evidence base, not from a publisher's imprint
4. The book's primary job is to be a credibility engine and lead magnet for the CEU/app/enterprise ecosystem, not to generate royalty income
5. If platform builds significantly in the next 6-9 months, a traditional deal becomes more viable and could be pursued for Book 2

**Exception:** If the Don Katz connection can open doors to a specific agent or publisher who understands the ecosystem play (book + app + CEU + enterprise), traditional publishing could work -- but only with a contract that preserves derivative rights for training materials and digital content.

## 4. Launch Strategy

### What the Best Launches Look Like

**Pre-Launch Phase (6-9 Months Before Publication)**
- Build ARC (Advance Reader Copy) team of 50-100 committed readers
- Deliver ARCs 6-8 weeks before launch with clear review instructions
- Run pre-order campaign (note: Amazon pre-orders do NOT count toward Amazon Day 1 rankings, but DO count toward NYT/WSJ bestseller lists)
- Secure 10-20 podcast appearances to air during launch window
- Line up conference speaking during launch quarter
- Seed excerpts on Substack, LinkedIn, and through guest posts

**Launch Week**
- Concentrate all pre-orders to count in first week for bestseller list eligibility
- Price ebook at $0.99 for first week to drive volume (if self/hybrid published)
- Activate launch team for coordinated reviews on Amazon and Goodreads
- Target 20+ reviews in first 48 hours to trigger Amazon algorithms
- Aim for a semi-related Amazon category where #1 is achievable with 20-40 sales/day

**Post-Launch (Ongoing)**
- "Long game outperforms launch-only mindset" -- author visibility is about sustained presence, not one moment
- Continue podcast appearances monthly
- Repurpose book content weekly across channels
- Build toward bulk/enterprise sales opportunities
- Stack speaking engagements

### Launch Team Building

- Recruit from most engaged email subscribers, social followers, and professional network
- Target 50-100 members minimum (not all will follow through)
- Provide clear instructions: where to review, what hashtags, when to post
- For nonfiction: recruit journalists who cover your beat, clinical colleagues, conference contacts
- Use NetGalley or BookFunnel for digital ARC distribution

## 5. Sales Channels

### Channel Analysis

**Amazon (Primary Discovery Channel)**
- Dominates ebook and print-on-demand sales
- Category selection is strategic: find categories where #1 is achievable
- Reviews are critical currency: target 50+ reviews in first 3 months
- KDP Select (Kindle Unlimited) increases visibility but requires Amazon exclusivity
- Amazon Author Central page is a must

**Bulk/Enterprise Sales**
- Corporations distribute books at events, tradeshows, and as employee gifts (e.g., thousands of copies of *Who Moved My Cheese* distributed to employees)
- Porchlight Books specializes in bulk book orders (25+ copies)
- Volume discounts: typically 10% for 10-20 copies, deeper discounts for 25+
- For Randy: Position the book as a "required reading" for CEU program participants, enterprise pilot participants, and wellness program enrollees
- This is where the book-as-business-card model generates massive volume

**Conference/Event Sales**
- AANA conferences, Summit Anesthesia Seminars, state CRNA associations
- HLTH USA 2026 (November, Las Vegas)
- Bundle book + CEU access as a conference special
- Back-of-room sales at speaking engagements

**CEU/Training Channel**
- Book becomes required text for CEU program
- Enterprise CE providers (NetCE, Medbridge, Relias) represent partnership opportunities
- AANA accreditation pathway already in progress
- Book + CEU bundle pricing creates value perception and drives both channels

**Direct Sales (Author Website)**
- Higher margins than any other channel
- Can bundle with digital extras (assessments, video content, app access)
- Free-plus-shipping funnels: offer book for free, charge $7-10 shipping, upsell course/app
- Industry conversion rate for free book offer: 10-15%

**Corporate Book Clubs**
- Managed corporate book clubs engage hundreds/thousands of employees
- PBC Guru and similar services facilitate corporate book club programs
- Position book for healthcare organization book clubs, nursing leadership programs

**Audiobook**
- Critical channel for health/wellness content (listeners consume during commutes, exercise)
- Don Katz connection to Audible is uniquely valuable here
- Author-narrated audiobooks perform well in this category
- Consider Audible exclusive vs. wide distribution

### Revenue Potential by Channel

| Channel | Revenue per Unit | Volume Potential | Strategic Value |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Amazon (retail) | $3-7 per book | Medium-High | Discovery, reviews, credibility |
| Bulk/Enterprise | $8-15 per book | High (50-500 per order) | Enterprise pipeline opener |
| Conference sales | $15-25 per book | Low-Medium | Direct relationship, upsell |
| CEU bundle | $49-149 per bundle | Medium-High | Recurring, high margin |
| Direct + shipping funnel | $7-10 + upsell | Medium | Email capture, funnel entry |
| Audiobook | $3-7 per sale | Medium | Passive, long-tail |
| Corporate book club | $10-15 per book | Medium | Enterprise relationships |

## 6. Content Repurposing

### How the Book's Content Feeds the Ecosystem

The vault already contains 200+ linked notes. The book manuscript is, in many ways, already written as raw material. Here is how content flows:

**Book Chapter --> Multiple Outputs:**

```
Book Chapter
  |
  +--> 4-6 Substack posts (excerpts + commentary)
  +--> 8-12 LinkedIn posts (key insights, clinical vignettes)
  +--> 2-3 podcast talking points (pitch these as episode topics)
  +--> 1 CEU module (add learning objectives, assessment questions)
  +--> 1-2 app protocols (extract the practical exercises)
  +--> 3-5 short-form videos (key concepts explained in 60-90 seconds)
  +--> 1 conference presentation (deeper dive with Q&A)
```

**Repurposing Principles:**
- Every piece of content can spin out at least 3-4 other forms
- Blog post into video, video into podcast, several blog posts into a downloadable guide
- The book provides entry-level knowledge; the course provides accountability and support
- Leave hooks in the book to other products (app, CEU, assessments)

**Email as Core Channel:**
- Email is more long-term and stable than social media
- Repurpose book sections as email newsletter content
- Use email to drive to all other channels (podcast episodes, app downloads, CEU enrollment)

**The Knowledge Graph Advantage:**
- Randy's vault with 200+ linked notes, SKOS relationships, and evidence chains is a unique asset
- Each concept note can become a blog post, newsletter issue, or social post
- Evidence notes provide citations and credibility for every claim
- The graph structure itself tells you which ideas are most connected (and therefore most important to readers)

## 9. Specific Recommendations for Randy

### Given: 28-year CRNA, knowledge graph with 200+ linked notes, app in market, CEU pending, no existing book platform, Mayo Clinic user, Don Katz connection

### FIRST: Activate the Don Katz Connection (This Week)

Don Katz is a PEN America Business Visionary honoree, former contributing editor at Rolling Stone/Esquire/Outside, National Book Critics Circle Award nominee, and founder of Audible. He sits at the intersection of publishing, technology, and media. This is the single most valuable connection in Randy's network for this project.

**Ask Don for:**
- Introduction to 2-3 literary agents who handle health/wellness nonfiction
- Advice on the audiobook strategy (Audible exclusive vs. wide distribution)
- Introduction to podcast bookers or media contacts
- His honest assessment of traditional vs. hybrid for this specific project
- A potential foreword or endorsement (a Don Katz endorsement carries weight)

### SECOND: Start the Newsletter (This Month)

The platform gap is the single biggest obstacle to any publishing path. Every week without a newsletter is a week of compounding audience growth lost.

**Specifics:**
- Launch on Substack (free tier)
- Weekly cadence: one insight from the vault, one clinical vignette, one practice
- Cross-post key pieces to LinkedIn
- Add email capture to somnistics.com
- This is the lowest-cost, highest-leverage action available right now

### THIRD: Transform the Vault into a Book Proposal (Next 30 Days)

The knowledge graph is the manuscript in embryonic form. A developmental editor can help structure it.

**Book proposal elements needed:**
- Overview (the big idea and why now)
- Target audience definition
- Competitive title analysis (Body Keeps the Score, Breath, Anchored, Burnout by Emily Nagoski)
- Chapter outline with summaries
- Sample chapters (2-3)
- Author bio and platform description
- Marketing plan (this document is a starting point)

**For a traditional path:** The proposal goes to agents
**For a hybrid/self path:** The proposal becomes the developmental roadmap

### FOURTH: Secure 3-5 Podcast Appearances (Next 60 Days)

Start with niche shows (CRNA podcasts, nursing leadership, perioperative care) to develop the talking points and comfort level. Then pitch up to larger health/wellness shows.

**Pitch angle:** "A 28-year CRNA discovers that the same nervous system science that keeps patients alive on the operating table can be taught to anyone in 60 seconds -- and the research is finally catching up to what clinicians have always known."

### FIFTH: File HLTH 2026 Speaker Application (Before Deadline)

12,000+ healthcare innovation leaders. 5-10% speaker acceptance rate. Even attending (not speaking) creates enterprise connections.

**Pitch angle for HLTH:** The intersection of clinical neuroscience, consumer wearables, and enterprise resilience training -- backed by 28 years of keeping people alive under pressure.

### Strategic Advantages Randy Has That Most First-Time Authors Don't

1. **The knowledge graph.** 200+ linked notes with evidence chains is 80% of the manuscript research, already done. Most authors spend 1-2 years on research alone.

2. **The app is already in market.** Most book-to-app authors have to build the app after the book. Randy has it backward (in a good way). The book validates what the app already delivers.

3. **CEU pending.** The book becomes required text for the CEU program. This is a built-in bulk sales channel.

4. **Mayo Clinic user.** This is a credibility proof point that most debut authors would kill for. "Used at Mayo Clinic" belongs on the book cover.

5. **28 years of clinical practice.** This is not a journalist or researcher writing about something they studied. This is a practitioner who has kept people alive under the most intense pressure for nearly three decades. That is the story.

6. **Don Katz.** A personal connection to the founder of Audible, a PEN America honoree, and a veteran of the literary world. This is a door that most debut authors cannot open.

7. **Trademarked IP.** Gap Moment Training, NeuroMinute, Pausality, Anterocept, Neurogating, Polyanchora -- these are brandable, licensable, certifiable. Most authors have ideas. Randy has protected intellectual property.

### What Randy Should NOT Do

1. **Do not wait for the book to be finished before building platform.** The platform must precede the book.
2. **Do not pursue traditional publishing if it means waiting 3+ years.** The market window is now.
3. **Do not try to be on every social platform.** LinkedIn + Substack + podcasts. That's it for now.
4. **Do not write the book alone in isolation.** Hire a developmental editor from the health/wellness space.
5. **Do not treat the book as the product.** The book is the gateway. The CEU + app + enterprise ecosystem is the product.
6. **Do not undervalue the Don Katz connection.** One warm introduction is worth more than 500 cold queries.
