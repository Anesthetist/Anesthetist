# Computational Interoception: The Body as Bayesian Control System

## Source

Petzschner et al. (2021), "Computational Models of Interoception and Body Regulation" (Trends in Neurosciences, PMC8109616).

## Core Thesis

Stop treating homeostasis as dumb reflex arcs. Start treating the body as a Bayesian control system that predicts and regulates its own internal state.

## Landscape of Computational Models

1. **Interoception models**: How the brain infers internal states
2. **Body regulation models**: How it chooses actions to regulate the body
3. **Forecasting models**: How it predicts future internal states

## Mathematical Scaffolding

- Homeostasis to allostasis to active inference / reinforcement learning / predictive coding
- Ties physiology to mental health through computational frameworks

## Significance for SRL

This paper provides the theoretical backbone for Anterocept and PAS-ME architectures. If interoception is Bayesian inference about body states, then Pausality's biofeedback is essentially providing better sensory evidence to update the brain's body model. The 60-second NeuroMinute becomes a structured Bayesian update operation.

Key implication: the progressive training curriculum (Foundation to Mastery) maps to improving the precision of interoceptive priors over time.
