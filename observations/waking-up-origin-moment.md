Randy's "I am not my thoughts" experience through Sam Harris's Waking Up app onboarding. The moment [[embodied-metacognition|embodied metacognition]] became experiential rather than conceptual. Origin of the awareness layer in SRL's training architecture.

## The Moment

> "After doing sam harris waking up app for several years, i had an epiphany 'i am not my thoughts' and began to spend more time in the present moment, non-dual reality."

This was not an intellectual insight but an **embodied shift** — something that changed how Randy inhabits clinical work, not just how he thinks about it. The shift moved the center of gravity from *being inside the storm of thought* to *being the sky it moves through*.

## Significance for SRL

This experience is the origin point for the entire Somnistics awareness training architecture:

- It demonstrated that metacognitive capacity can shift through practice — not just understanding
- It showed that the shift is somatic, not cognitive — felt before understood
- It validated that app-delivered micro-practices can produce genuine contemplative development
- It became the design template: the Waking Up app's approach (normalizing mind-wandering, direct-path pointing-out instructions, progressive capacity building) directly influenced NeuroMinute design philosophy

## Pedagogical Influence

Randy reverse-engineered Harris's pedagogical approach:

> "let's do the same thing, using the same frameworks as the waking up app. Except with Somnistics Research. Subtly building capacity. he's really good at acknowledging the mind wandering in his dailies."

Key insight: Harris builds capacity for metacognition by normalizing failure ("mind-wandering is the practice"). Randy adapts this for somatic awareness: "If this feels unfamiliar, that's perfect — it's a sign you're discovering something new about your breath."
