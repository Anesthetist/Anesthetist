# Pausality B2B Clinical Sales Framework — February 2026

## Context

First organic B2B inbound signal: **Sunny Cessna, CRNA, Paceline Anesthesia** (sunny@pacelineanesthesia.com). The inquiry was not "what does this cost?" — it was "how do we use this as a team?" This is a category-defining signal: the product is being perceived as a professional tool, not an app.

**HubSpot records created:** Company: Paceline Anesthesia | Contact: Sunny Cessna | Deal: Paceline Anesthesia (Opportunity stage, Partnership type, High priority)

## Core Operating Principles (Now Canon)

1. **B2B pulled in, not pushed into.** The inbound came through peer networks organically. This validates the clinical-first positioning and the 60-second constraint.
2. **Free sessions are the perfect trial.** The first three sessions provide proof of value and behavior sampling — no additional trial construct needed.
3. **Legitimacy = clarity + calmness + confidence.** Not portals, pricing grids, or automation. Manual group codes and invoices are acceptable and expected when trust is high.
4. **CRNAs reward respect for operating reality.** Community feedback confirms: *utility > novelty*, *subtle pride > loud branding*, *effectiveness > features*. This is product-market fit signal.
5. **"You are not selling an app. You are introducing a new professional behavior."**

## The 7-Stage Sales Process

| Stage | Description | RevOps Action |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Signal** | Someone said something (peer mention, second reference) | Log who, where, context. Don't act. |
| **2 — Inquiry** | Direct low-pressure question received | Respond within 24h, no sales framing. Ask only 3 questions. |
| **3 — Discovery** | 15-minute call (contact-initiated only) | 10 min listening, 5 min framing. Leave with clarity, not collateral. |
| **4 — Trial Activation** | Group access code issued, 21-day window | Provide code + one-paragraph note. Observe usage quietly. |
| **5 — Signal Check** | Day ~21 check: did they engage? | One message if silent. If no response in 7 days → nurture, not dead. |
| **6 — Proposal** | Personalized one-page/email proposal | No comparison tables, no tiers, no ROI calculators. |
| **7 — Close** | Verbal/written yes | Manual invoice, welcome note, then silence for 30 days. |

## What the Process Protects

- The clinician always feels like they're *choosing*, never being sold to
- Every touchpoint is calm, clear, professional — no hype
- Signal to close can happen in 30 days if fit is strong
- Every stage produces usable data even if the deal doesn't close

## Metrics to Track From Day One

**Leading:** Signal-to-Inquiry conversion | Inquiry-to-Discovery rate | Trial activation rate  
**Lagging:** Trial-to-Close rate | Days in pipeline | Revenue per group  
**Qualitative:** Language used by champions during discovery | Reasons for silence at Stage 5

## Deferred Scope (Not Now)

- Enterprise dashboards or admin portals
- Automated onboarding sequences
- Tiered pricing or bundled packages
- LinkedIn-HubSpot direct integration (Surfe recommended when ready)

## Closed-Loop Feedback Mechanism (Future)

At Stage 7 + 90 days, a single question to the champion: *"If a colleague at another practice asked you about Pausality, what would you tell them?"* This answer becomes positioning, testimonial, and product roadmap in one sentence.
