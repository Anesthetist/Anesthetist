# Knowledge Graph as Intellectual Autobiography

Randy's approach to building a personal knowledge graph is not academic ontology engineering — it is autobiographical. The graph maps his lived experience across 28 years of clinical practice, his reading across neuroscience and philosophy, his entrepreneurial vision, and his personal practices (Qigong, Yiquan, breathing). The structure of connections reveals how he thinks.

## The Insight

When Randy asks an LLM to "examine a knowledge graph of my personal knowledge that I've demonstrated," he is asking for a mirror, not a filing system. The knowledge graph IS the consilience — it makes visible the convergent threads that Randy holds in embodied, tacit form.

The vault itself is simultaneously:
- A research tool (organizing evidence, tracking claims, wiring relationships)
- An intellectual autobiography (the topology of connections reveals the thinker)
- A consilience engine (making visible what converges across domains)

## Implication for Vault Architecture

This observation reframes the vault's purpose. It is not just a knowledge management system — it is an externalized representation of a mind that naturally integrates across domains. The four-layer structure (evidence → observations → concepts → outputs) mirrors the progression from raw experience to articulated knowledge that occurs within Randy's own cognition.
