# Dr. Honeycutt VCU Research Collaboration Agenda

## Context

Research correspondence with Dr. Honeycutt at Virginia Commonwealth University initiated week of March 10, 2026. Opportunity to formalize SRL's research program with academic partnership.

## Five Research Questions

### 1. High-Performance Resilience Landscape Review

What resilience training programs with published outcomes exist across military, healthcare, first responder, and elite athletic populations? Systematic review of programs that include a physiological/somatic component (not cognitive-only).

Known programs to map:
- **Military:** Comprehensive Soldier Fitness (CSF2), BioTICK (Walter Reed/DARPA), Navy SEAL box breathing, TCCC tactical breathing, POTFF (USSOCOM), Special Warfare human performance programs
- **First responder:** IAFF Peer Support, Valor Officer Safety & Wellness
- **Corporate/athletic:** HeartMath, WHOOP + coaching, BrainTap, Muse neurofeedback
- **Consciousness research:** Monroe Institute (Hemi-Sync/Gateway, military trust, progressive Focus Levels)

### 2. Gap Analysis

Which existing programs include: (a) progressive interoceptive training, (b) real-time biofeedback, (c) micro-format interventions (<5 min), (d) workflow-integrated delivery? Identify the structural gaps that SRL fills.

### 3. Measurement Harmonization

Map outcome measures across programs: MAIA-2, PSS, PCL-5, HRV metrics, cognitive performance tests. Can a common outcome battery be established for cross-program comparison?

### 4. Diaphragmatic Blindness Pilot Study

Formalize Randy's informal survey (28/30 unable to engage diaphragm) as a pilot study:
- N=50 CRNAs
- Maximum nasal inhale duration pre/post diaphragmatic coaching
- EMG confirmation of muscle recruitment patterns (accessory vs. diaphragm)
- Compare to age/sex matched non-clinical controls and trained musicians/singers
- Publishable in AANA Journal or similar

### 5. Career-Longitudinal GMT Design

Has anyone studied the same resilience intervention across career stages (student → mid-career → senior)? The GMT Career Arc (Seeding → Healing → Fortifying) predicts different MAIA-2 subscale profiles by years of experience — testable with cross-sectional or longitudinal design.

## Monroe Institute Connection

Monroe Institute (Faber, VA) is a significant node in the landscape:
- Robert Monroe's Hemi-Sync: binaural beats for hemispheric synchronization (1970s-present)
- CIA Gateway Analysis: declassified assessment of audio-guided consciousness training
- Focus Level progression (10 → 12 → 15 → 21+) maps onto Anterocept domains
- Military/intelligence community trust — Gateway was a real Army intelligence program
- What Monroe proved: audio-guided consciousness state training produces measurable effects
- What Monroe never had: biometric verification, clinical outcome measurement, 60-second format, workflow integration
