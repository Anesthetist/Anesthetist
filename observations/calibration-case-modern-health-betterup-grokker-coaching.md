# Calibration Case: Modern Health, BetterUp & Grokker — Enterprise Coaching

## Why Grouped
All three sell to enterprise HR buyers with coaching/wellness platforms. They represent the spectrum from therapy-adjacent (Modern Health) to executive coaching (BetterUp) to fitness-wellness content (Grokker).

## Commoncog Triad Analysis

### Operations
**Modern Health:** Global mental health platform in 200+ countries, 35+ languages. Therapy, coaching, digital programs, and circles (group sessions). ~700 enterprise customers including Pixar, Lyft, Electronic Arts.

**BetterUp:** AI + human coaching platform. 4,500+ coaches. Expanded from executive coaching into "whole person" development. Customers include Hilton, Mars, Snap. Prince Harry as Chief Impact Officer (brand play).

**Grokker:** Video-based enterprise wellness platform. Fitness, nutrition, mental health, sleep, financial wellness content library. Lower price point than therapy/coaching platforms. Claims $13 ROI per $1 spent on healthcare cost reduction.

### Market
**Modern Health:** $330M+ raised, $2.3B peak valuation. PEPM model. Differentiator: global coverage in languages/cultures where Calm/Headspace don't operate.

**BetterUp:** $678M raised, $4.7B peak valuation. Premium pricing ($150-300/mo per user for coaching). Executive-level positioning. Significant valuation correction in 2023 downturn.

**Grokker:** $47M raised. Much leaner than peers. "Whole person" wellness video library. Claims lower cost than point solutions.

### Capital
Combined $1B+ raised signals massive enterprise wellness TAM, but BetterUp's valuation correction (from $4.7B) signals market repricing of coaching-only platforms.

## Surprising Similarities to Pausality
1. **All sell to HR/benefits with ROI data** — identical enterprise buyer persona to MUSC opportunity
2. **"Whole person" language** — BetterUp and Grokker use holistic wellness framing that resonates with Pausality's autonomic regulation approach
3. **Grokker's $13:$1 ROI claim** — Pausality's real-time biometric data could generate even more compelling ROI evidence

## Surprising Dissimilarities to Pausality
1. **Human-delivered at scale vs. Protocol-delivered** — Modern Health and BetterUp scale by adding humans (therapists, coaches). Pausality scales by adding protocols. Radically different unit economics.
2. **Generalist vs. Specialist** — All three are "wellness for everyone." Pausality is "autonomic regulation for high-performance clinicians." Specialist positioning wins in clinical environments.
3. **BetterUp's valuation correction** — warning signal that coaching-only platforms face margin pressure. Protocol-based platforms (like Pausality) have better gross margins.
4. **No physiological measurement** — None of these platforms measure whether their interventions work at a biological level. All rely on self-report surveys. Pausality proves it with biometrics.

## Pattern Recognition (RPD Library)
- **Valuation correction in coaching** suggests market wants measurable outcomes, not just self-reported satisfaction
- **Global coverage** (Modern Health) is a moat Pausality can replicate cheaply (protocols translate; therapist networks don't)
- **Video wellness content** (Grokker) at low price points commoditizes passive wellness — creates opportunity for active, biometric-validated premium tier
- **Celebrity involvement** (BetterUp/Prince Harry) drives awareness but doesn't solve measurement problem
