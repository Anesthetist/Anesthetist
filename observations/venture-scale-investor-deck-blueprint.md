# Venture Scale Investor Deck Blueprint

## Structure (12 Slides)

1. Title and Frictionless Immediacy (logo + QR code)
2. The Problem: High-Pain B2B Need ($4,129/employee burnout cost, $322B global)
3. The Credentialed Team (placed early to answer "can they execute?")
4. The Solution and Unfair Advantage (Gap Moment Training, 30-40 daily micro-transitions)
5. Why Now? (macro tailwinds: $6.8T-$10T wellness market, Personal Health OS shift)
6. Market Sizing (B2C TAM ~$3B-$4B by 2034; B2B Corporate Wellness $4.5B medical staff + $3.7B C-suite)
7. Go-To-Market and Force Multipliers (90-day pilot programs, up to 25 users)
8. Engine Telemetry / Unit Economics
9. Total Competitive Awareness (X/Y axis: execution risk vs market potential)
10. The Ask and Hull Mechanics
11. Engineering the Exit (acquirer logos)
12. Final Approach (frictionless diligence, QR repeat)

## Critical Unit Economics

- B2B LTV:CAC ratio: 20:1 (Avg contract $25K, CAC $6K, LTV $125K); crushes minimum VC scale requirement of 4:1+
- B2C LTV:CAC: 3.6:1, payback in 8.4 months
- 1% reduction in nurse turnover yields 80-120x return on Pausality platform

## The Ask

- $2M Seed Round in Q2 2026 via SAFE Note at $12M valuation cap
- Allocation: $750K Engineering, $500K Marketing, $450K R&D, $300K Sales

## Exit Targets

Strategic acquirers: Oura ($11B), WHOOP, Microsoft (Copilot Health), Apple, Function Health ($2.5B). Sector commanding 5-10x revenue multiples for IP/Data moats.

## Key Principles

- "Few words lots of pictures" for live deck; separate text-heavy "Takeaway Deck" for due diligence
- QSBS Section 1202 reminder for angel investors (5-year clock for $15M tax-free gains)
- Team slide placed early (Slide 3) to immediately establish credibility
