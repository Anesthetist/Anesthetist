## Overview

Somnistics requires four distinct definitional registers depending on audience context. This "definition ladder" was locked in a March 2026 strategic session and serves as the canonical reference for communication, IP defense, and regulatory navigation.

## The Four-Level Definition Ladder

### Brand One-Liner (Site Hero)
> *Somnistics teaches the world to breathe on purpose — so state follows intent.*

### Plain-Language (End Users)
> *Somnistics is a way to use your breathing, on purpose, to calm down, focus up, or fall asleep — fast — and see it working on your biometrics.*

### Scientific (Clinicians / Research)
> *Somnistics is an applied framework that leverages respiratory mechanics (rate, tidal volume, I:E ratio, chemoreflex/CO₂ tolerance) to shift autonomic balance via vagal and baroreflex pathways, validated against HRV, respiratory sinus arrhythmia, and end-tidal CO₂.*

### Regulatory-Safe (Partner Decks / FDA Context)
> *Somnistics provides protocolized respiratory training and biofeedback intended to support stress management, performance, and sleep quality by promoting autonomic self-regulation.*

## What Somnistics Is vs. Is Not

**Is:**
- Protocolized, measured, closed-loop respiratory training with objective biomarkers
- Evidence-seeking, mechanism-grounded, parameterized (cadence, I:E, CO₂ exposure, sigh frequency)
- Applied neurophysiology — not "wellness," not generic breathwork

**Is Not:**
- Generic "breathwork" or meditation per se
- Making claims of disease treatment or cure
- Style-based lineages without physiological targets

## Mechanistic Kernel

- **Inputs:** respiratory cadence, depth, I:E ratio, breath-hold positions, periodic sighs, CO₂ exposure/tolerance curves
- **Pathways:** pulmonary stretch receptors → nucleus tractus solitarius; baroreflex engagement → vagal efferents; chemoreceptor signaling adjusting locus-coeruleus tone
- **Outputs:** shifts in low-frequency HRV, RSA amplitude, perceived arousal, reaction time, sleep-onset latency
- **Feedback:** HRV and ETCO₂ (when available) to personalize cadence and dose

## Strategic Value

This ladder enables consistent messaging across investor decks, CE applications, research submissions, and product copy without regulatory overreach or audience mismatch.
