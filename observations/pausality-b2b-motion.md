# Pausality B2B Motion: Inbound Signal and Sales Process

## The Signal (February 2026)

Paceline Anesthesia / Sunny Cessna came inbound through peer trust. The first ask was not commercial:

> "How do we use this as a team?"

Not "what does this cost?" — the *question itself* was the signal. B2B was pulled forward, not pushed. This is what strong product-market fit directional pressure looks like.

What it validated: clinical-first positioning, the 60-second constraint, focus on operational fit over novelty.

## What CRNAs Actually Reward

From community research (threads, direct feedback):
- Utility > novelty
- Subtle pride > loud branding  
- Experience > swag
- Operational framing ("turnover time," "readiness") > wellness tropes

## The Canon B2B Motion

1. Individual clinicians use free first three sessions
2. Group interest emerges organically — do not manufacture it
3. 15-minute discovery call (size, structure, use context — nothing more)
4. Group access via time-boxed code
5. Manual invoice
6. Observe usage and language
7. Formalize only after repeat signal

This motion preserves clinician **agency** (they always feel they're choosing), **credibility** (no sales pressure), and **speed** (no unnecessary steps). The relationship is the product.

## Seven-Stage Pipeline

Signal → Inquiry → Discovery → Trial (21 days, no check-in emails) → Signal Check (one message if silent) → Proposal (one page, not a deck) → Close (30-day quiet period after, no onboarding sequences)

The most important early metric: **trial-to-close rate**. It tells you whether the product works in group settings.

## What Is Explicitly Off the Table

Enterprise dashboards · seat-management UI · complex pricing tiers · sales funnels · swag arms race. Anything that adds cognitive overhead without adding trust is deferred.

## The Positioning Statement

> "You are not selling an app. You are introducing a new professional behavior."

## HubSpot Status

Paceline Anesthesia logged as Company + Contact (Sunny Cessna, sunny@pacelineanesthesia.com) + Deal at Opportunity stage in "Pipeline 2026." Deal note: *waiting for signal, do not chase.*

## Future Trigger

CE (Continuing Education) credit integration is the likely threshold that converts group adoption from experiment to default. Having a clear "is this CE-eligible?" answer ready for discovery calls may matter sooner than anticipated.
