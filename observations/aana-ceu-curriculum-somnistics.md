# Somnistics AANA Category A CEU Curriculum Design

Randy directed the creation of a 6.0 Class A CE credit course titled "Somnistics for CRNAs: Gap Moment Training & NeuroMinutes for Autonomic Readiness Under Clinical Load."

## Randy's Direction

> "Role: world famous curriculum designer working with the team of Jared Bruder and Jason Fields. Teach these concepts in an AANA category A CEU approved manner."

## Course Structure

- **Credit hours:** 6.0 Class A CE credits
- **Compliance standard:** AANA Category A (prior approval + assessment component + relevance to nurse anesthesia practice)
- **Collaborators:** Jared Bruder, Jason Fields

## AANA CE Content Codes

- 01.12.02 — Strategies to Limit Practitioner Stress
- 01.12.01 — Promote Practitioner Health
- 01.13 — Culture of Safety
- 01.09.02 — Evidence-Based Practice (EBP)

## Key Positioning

> "This is intentionally not a 'mindfulness class.' It is a performance-and-safety curriculum."

The curriculum emphasizes autonomic readiness as a clinical competency, not a wellness add-on. This aligns with SRL's counter-positioning against generic mindfulness and maps directly to the [[state-control-operational-capability]] observation.
