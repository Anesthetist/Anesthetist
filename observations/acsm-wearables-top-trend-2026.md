## Source

American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM), Top Fitness Trends for 2026 report. Published early 2026.

## Summary

The ACSM's annual trends survey ranked wearable technology as the #1 fitness and health trend for 2026 — marking a continuation and acceleration of the wearables movement. New clinical-grade wearables now offer ECG, SpO₂, blood pressure, and early-stage continuous glucose monitoring with sensor reliability approaching dedicated medical equipment. AI integration enables algorithms to detect precursors to illness or stress before symptoms emerge, and biofeedback features are being embedded directly into consumer devices to personalize training in real time.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The mainstream institutionalization of wearable biofeedback by ACSM creates a large and growing audience primed for SRL's physiological literacy content.
