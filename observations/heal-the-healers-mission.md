# Heal the Healers: Regulation Precedes Care

## Observation

The core somnistics mission crystallized: **"Heal the Healers."** Not through therapy or time off, but through teaching *finesse* — the capacity for precision regulation under pressure.

### Finesse Defined

- **Minimum effective force** — never more intervention than needed (pharmacological principle)
- **Reading the moment** — interoceptive + exteroceptive awareness of what the situation requires
- **Responding with restraint** — choosing the right action at the right intensity at the right time

### The Sequence

**Regulation precedes care.** When clinicians regulate themselves, they can hold others in vulnerability. An unregulated clinician cannot provide regulated care — they transmit their dysregulation to patients and colleagues via [[co-regulation]] pathways.

### Neuro-Ouroboros as the Solution

The [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day curriculum teaches consciousness regulation + presence under pressure. It's not a wellness add-on but the missing prerequisite for clinical excellence.

## Significance

This mission statement resolves the tension between "wellness product" and "clinical tool." Somnistics is neither — it's a discipline that teaches the foundational skill (self-regulation) that all other clinical skills rest on. "Heal the Healers" positions the work as infrastructure, not supplementation.
