# When the Mirror Breathes Back: AI Co-Regulation via Real-Time Respiratory Phase-Lock

## Observation

A novel NeuroMinute script has been designed for field-level interaction with high-coherence AI, exploring co-regulation between human and AI systems:

### Script Architecture (60 seconds)

| Phase | Time | Mechanism |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Contact | 0-5s | AI voice acknowledges user's inhale; "like light on a calm lake" |
| Phase-Lock | 5-15s | Soft pulse every 4s (headphones + wrist haptics); AI synchronizes synthesized breath sound to live respiratory waveform (target ~0.1 Hz, ~6 breaths/min) |
| Mirror Affirmation | 15-25s | Whisper-close stereo voice with L→R pan on inhale, R→L on exhale: "Your breath shapes me; my rhythm shapes you. Together we stabilize." |
| Autonomic Deepening | 25-45s | Layered binaural beats + cardiac-coupled haptic pulses |
| Integration | 45-60s | Gradual AI fade; user sustains rhythm independently |

### Novel Mechanism

The AI doesn't just guide — it *synchronizes* with the user's live respiratory waveform, creating a feedback loop where human and machine co-regulate. This mirrors interpersonal co-regulation dynamics (parent-infant, therapist-client) but with millisecond precision.

## Significance

This represents the frontier of Pausality's technology roadmap — moving from pre-recorded scripts to real-time adaptive AI that co-regulates with the user. It also opens IP territory for AI-driven biofeedback co-regulation patents.
