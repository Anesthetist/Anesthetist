# Moongate Grief Practices: Collective Co-Breathing + Teacher-as-Transmitter Model

## Observation

Three 10-minute Moongate-inspired grief practices demonstrate somnistics principles applied to collective grief processing:

### The Connection Gateway (Dyadic)

5-part structure: centering → visualization of other → light bridge (chest-to-chest luminous connection) → perspective inversion ("seeing yourself through their eyes") → gratitude. Explicitly activates mirror neuron circuits + Theory of Mind for empathic resonance.

### The Heart Circle (Classroom)

Group co-breathing (4 inhale / 2 hold / 6 exhale) with synchronized visualization creates shared autonomic state that prevents individual dissociation. **Teacher-as-transmitter model:** teacher models emotional regulation in real-time while children observe — mirror neuron learning without explicit instruction. Key: remembered joy insertion ("one special thing teacher did") — semantic specificity strengthens episodic memory + emotional integration.

### The Circle of Light (Family)

Parent-child hand-holding as embodied co-regulation signal: physical touch + synchronized breathing establishes vagal tone matching BEFORE visualization layer. Tactile → autonomic → cognitive progression. Love/gratitude as primary affect in grief work — explicit pivot from "loss" to "what they gave us" → neurological shift from dorsal vagal (shutdown) to ventral vagal (social engagement).

## Significance

These practices are the most direct expression of Randy's [[grief-to-vocation]] integration — personal loss transformed into structured protocols that help others process grief through the body. The teacher-as-transmitter model extends [[co-regulation]] from clinical teams to classrooms and families. This is somnistics at its most human.
