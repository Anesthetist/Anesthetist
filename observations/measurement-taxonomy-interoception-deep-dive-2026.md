### 4. Critical Scientific Context

**Interoception is NOT unitary.** Garfinkel 2015 is definitive — accuracy, sensibility, and awareness are dissociable dimensions. Cardiac interoception does not automatically generalize to respiratory or gastric. SRL should not claim HRV biofeedback improves "all interoception."

**HEP is contested.** Legrand 2022 argues HEP does not index interoceptive accuracy. But HEP-HRV coupling analysis (emerging 2025) may offer a more defensible composite biomarker.

**Questionnaires don't converge.** Desmedt et al. 2022 found current interoception questionnaires do NOT measure a common construct. MAIA-2 and BPQ measure different things. Choose instruments deliberately.

**Predictive processing is the dominant framework.** Seth 2013 and Allen 2022 formalize interoception as Bayesian inference. SRL's "Interoceptive Literacy" maps naturally to this — literacy = training the brain's predictive model of the body.
