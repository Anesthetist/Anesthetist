The longer a prospect engages with your content, the faster they close.

**SRL Relevance:** Pausality's Leading Edge content strategy should maximize consumption depth, not breadth. Weekly intelligence reports, deep-dive breathing science content, and interactive protocol tools all increase engagement time, which correlates with enterprise conversion.
