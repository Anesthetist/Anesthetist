## The Core Proposition

IFS "parts" (Protectors, Exiles, Self) are not purely psychological constructs — they have specific interoceptive signatures: characteristic patterns of body sensation, breath texture, temperature, tension, and autonomic tone that are consistent and recognizable across individuals.

If these signatures can be catalogued in a Pattern Language format (named, described, illustrated, and cross-referenced like Alexander's architectural patterns), they become:
- A **clinical vocabulary** for somatic practitioners
- A **training framework** for Somnistics interoceptive literacy curriculum
- A **biometric tagging taxonomy** for the Anterocept platform

## Proposed Pattern Language Structure

Each "interoceptive emotion pattern" entry would include:
1. **Name** (e.g., "The Armored Guard," "The Frozen Lake")
2. **Part type** (Protector/Manager, Protector/Firefighter, or Exile)
3. **Interoceptive signature** (specific sensations: chest tightness, throat constriction, jaw tension, etc.)
4. **Autonomic correlate** (HRV pattern, breath texture, skin conductance)
5. **Common trigger context** (clinical: post-difficult-case, pre-induction; relational: conflict, evaluation)
6. **NeuroMinute target modality** (what intervention approach best reaches this part-state)
