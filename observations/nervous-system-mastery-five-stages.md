# Five Stages of Nervous System Mastery (Moongate Paradigm)

## Observation

The Moongate Training Paradigm maps nervous system mastery through five progressive stages, each corresponding to a kosha layer:

| Stage | Kosha | Focus | Mastery Endpoint |
|-------|-------|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Annamaya (Physical) | Body awareness, ergonomics, pranayama; overcome diaphragmatic blindness | Proprioceptive precision in every movement |
| 2 | Pranamaya (Energy) | Breath patterns (Nadi Shodhana, box breathing), energy regulation, haptic feedback | Autonomic control under pressure |
| 3 | Manomaya (Mental) | Emotional regulation, cognitive reframing, right-hemispheric shift, mindfulness cues | Cognitive clarity in gap moments |
| 4 | Vijnanamaya (Wisdom) | Case debriefs, intuitive decision-making, alpha-theta brainwave entrainment | Pattern recognition + embodied intuition |
| 5 | Anandamaya (Bliss) | Gratitude, purpose alignment, community, joy in caregiving | Coherence with transcendent purpose |

## Progression Principle

Each stage builds on the previous — you cannot achieve cognitive clarity (Stage 3) without first establishing breath control (Stage 2), and you cannot achieve embodied intuition (Stage 4) without first establishing emotional regulation (Stage 3). This stepwise threshold model is the Moongate: each gate opens only when the prior stage is sufficiently stable.

## Mapping to Somnistics Training

- Stages 1-2 → [[anterocept]] Domain 1 (baseline interoceptive awareness)
- Stage 3 → [[anterocept]] Domain 2 (layered interoception with emotional integration)
- Stages 4-5 → [[anterocept]] Domain 3 (integrated interoceptive-exteroceptive awareness)
- The 21-day [[neuro-ouroboros]] curriculum corresponds to Stages 1-3 (foundation through mental mastery)
- Stages 4-5 require sustained practice beyond the initial curriculum

## Significance

This five-stage model is the structural foundation for all Pausality curriculum design, VagalBeats progression, and CRNA onboarding. It provides the answer to "what does mastery look like?" — bidirectional nervous system control (not just calming down) with purpose-aligned coherence.
