# Field Map: Key Researchers in Interoception, Body Awareness & Adjacent Sciences

> A comprehensive map of the scientists, clinicians, and visionaries whose work underpins and surrounds the Somnistics Research Labs mission. Organized by domain with connection to SRL's five Red Arrows.

---

## 1. Interoception Science (Core)

The foundational science of how the nervous system senses, interprets, and integrates signals from within the body. This is the bedrock of everything SRL builds.

### Sahib S. Khalsa, MD, PhD
- **Institution**: UCLA Semel Institute for Neuroscience (formerly Laureate Institute for Brain Research, University of Tulsa)
- **Role**: Associate Professor in Residence; Director of Anxiety Disorders Research
- **Key Contribution**: Organized the first Interoception Summit (2016) and authored the definitive "Interoception and Mental Health: A Roadmap" -- the field's consensus document. Developed novel interoceptive probes across cardiac, respiratory, and gastrointestinal axes. Pioneer in using an ingestible vibrating capsule to measure gastrointestinal interoception.
- **Landmark Paper**: Khalsa SS et al. (2018) "Interoception and Mental Health: A Roadmap." *Biol Psychiatry Cogn Neurosci Neuroimaging* 3(6):501-513. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpsc.2017.12.004) (Based on PubMed, PMID: 29884281)
- **SRL Connection**: Khalsa's roadmap defines the measurement framework SRL must align to. His multi-axis interoceptive assessment approach (cardiac + respiratory + GI) maps directly to SRL's concept of [[interoceptive-literacy]]. His work validates that interoceptive dysfunction is transdiagnostic -- supporting SRL's broad-spectrum positioning across anxiety, burnout, and resilience.

### Hugo D. Critchley, DPhil, MBBCh
- **Institution**: Brighton and Sussex Medical School; Sackler Centre for Consciousness Science, University of Sussex
- **Role**: Chair in Psychiatry
- **Key Contribution**: Demonstrated that the right anterior insular cortex is the neural substrate for interoceptive awareness and that its gray matter volume predicts heartbeat detection accuracy. Established the neural systems supporting interoceptive awareness.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Critchley HD et al. (2004) "Neural systems supporting interoceptive awareness." *Nature Neuroscience* 7(2):189-95. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1176) (Based on PubMed, PMID: 14730305)
  - Co-authored with Seth AK (2016) "Active interoceptive inference and the emotional brain." [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0007) (PMID: 28080966)
- **SRL Connection**: Critchley's work on insula-mediated interoceptive accuracy provides the neuroanatomical justification for why training body awareness (via resonant breathing, HRV biofeedback) can improve emotional regulation -- the core mechanism of [[gap-moment-training]].

### Sarah N. Garfinkel, PhD
- **Institution**: UCL Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience (formerly Brighton and Sussex Medical School / Sackler Centre)
- **Role**: Professor of Neuroscience and Psychiatry
- **Key Contribution**: Developed the three-dimensional model of interoception: accuracy (objective performance), sensibility (self-report), and awareness (metacognitive correspondence). Demonstrated that fear perception is gated by the cardiac cycle -- fearful faces detected more intensely at systole.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Garfinkel SN et al. (2015) "Knowing your own heart: distinguishing interoceptive accuracy from interoceptive awareness." *Biol Psychol* 104:65-74. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2014.11.004) (PMID: 25451381)
  - Garfinkel SN et al. (2014) "Fear from the heart: sensitivity to fear stimuli depends on individual heartbeats." *J Neurosci* 34(19):6573-82. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3507-13.2014) (PMID: 24806682)
  - Garfinkel SN et al. (2016) "Interoceptive dimensions across cardiac and respiratory axes." *Phil Trans R Soc B* 371(1708). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0014) (PMID: 28080971)
- **SRL Connection**: The accuracy/sensibility/awareness taxonomy is the measurement framework SRL should adopt for pre/post intervention assessment. Her cardiac-cycle gating work validates that heartbeat awareness is not abstract -- it directly modulates threat perception, which is central to clinician performance under stress.

### Wolf E. Mehling, MD
- **Institution**: UCSF Osher Center for Integrative Medicine, Department of Family and Community Medicine
- **Role**: Associate Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Created the Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness (MAIA / MAIA-2) -- the most widely used self-report instrument for interoceptive body awareness. Crucially distinguished between anxiety-driven hypervigilance toward body signals and mindful, adaptive body awareness.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Mehling WE et al. (2012) "The Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness (MAIA)." *PLoS ONE* 7(11):e48230. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0048230) (PMID: 23133619)
  - Mehling WE et al. (2018) "The Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness, Version 2 (MAIA-2)." *PLoS ONE* 13(12):e0208034. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0208034) (PMID: 30513087)
  - Mehling W (2016) "Differentiating attention styles and regulatory aspects of self-reported interoceptive sensibility." *Phil Trans R Soc B* 371(1708). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0013) (PMID: 28080970)
- **SRL Connection**: The MAIA-2 is the primary outcome measure SRL should use in all clinical trials and pilot studies. Mehling's distinction between maladaptive hypervigilance and healthy mindful awareness maps directly onto the concept of [[interoceptive-literacy]] -- SRL trains the latter, not the former. MAIA-2 is public domain (www.osher.ucsf.edu/maia).

### A.D. (Bud) Craig, PhD
- **Institution**: Barrow Neurological Institute, Phoenix, AZ
- **Role**: Senior Research Scientist (now emeritus)
- **Key Contribution**: Proposed that interoception is a distinct sensory modality with its own dedicated neuroanatomical pathway (lamina I spinothalamocortical system terminating in posterior and anterior insula). Argued the anterior insula provides the neural substrate for subjective awareness of the body and the self.
- **Landmark Paper**: Craig AD (2004) "Human feelings: why are some more aware than others?" *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 8(6):239-41. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2004.04.004) (PMID: 15165543)
- **Key Book**: *How Do You Feel? An Interoceptive Moment with Your Neurobiological Self* (Princeton, 2015)
- **SRL Connection**: Craig's neuroanatomical model provides the scientific foundation for claiming that interoceptive training is training a specific sensory pathway, not merely "relaxation." This supports SRL's positioning as a precision neuroscience company, not a wellness app.

### Martin P. Paulus, MD
- **Institution**: Laureate Institute for Brain Research (LIBR), University of Tulsa
- **Role**: Scientific Director and President
- **Key Contribution**: Pioneered the study of interoceptive dysfunction in anxiety disorders, PTSD, and substance use disorders. Used aversive breathing load paradigms (inspiratory resistance) to probe interoceptive processing during decision-making. Demonstrated attenuated insula response in methamphetamine users during interoceptive challenges.
- **Key Paper**: Stewart JL et al. (2014) "You are the danger: attenuated insula response in methamphetamine users during aversive interoceptive decision-making." *Drug Alcohol Depend* 142:110-9. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2014.06.003) (PMID: 24993186)
- **SRL Connection**: Paulus's work on interoception in substance use and anxiety provides the clinical rationale for SRL's expansion beyond burnout into broader mental health applications (Red Arrow 4: Clinical LLM Moat). His breathing-load paradigms could inform SRL's assessment protocols.

### Olga Pollatos, Prof. Dr. phil. Dr. med.
- **Institution**: Ulm University, Germany
- **Role**: Head of Clinical and Health Psychology; Vice President of Education
- **Key Contribution**: Demonstrated that interoceptive sensitivity varies with body weight (attenuated in obesity), that interoceptive accuracy benefits children's decision-making, and that body posture interventions can improve interoceptive accuracy. Bridges interoception to eating behavior, emotion regulation, and embodied cognition.
- **SRL Connection**: Pollatos's finding that interoception can be trained through physical interventions (posture, movement) supports SRL's multi-modal approach. Her work on interoception and decision-making in children opens a future pathway for SRL's family/consumer audience.

### Manos Tsakiris, PhD
- **Institution**: Royal Holloway, University of London; The Warburg Institute
- **Role**: Professor of Psychology; NOMIS Distinguished Scientist
- **Key Contribution**: Demonstrated that interoceptive signals (heartbeat awareness) modulate the sense of body ownership and self-identification. Led the INtheSELF ERC Consolidator project investigating how internal body signals shape the self. Bridges interoception to social cognition and political attitudes.
- **SRL Connection**: Tsakiris's work on the interoceptive basis of selfhood provides a deeper theoretical rationale for why interoceptive training affects identity and agency -- key to SRL's narrative for clinician empowerment and the concept of [[clinician-durability]].

### Frederike H. Petzschner, PhD
- **Institution**: Brown University (formerly ETH Zurich / University of Zurich)
- **Role**: Assistant Professor, Dept. of Cognitive and Psychological Sciences
- **Key Contribution**: Defined the computational landscape for interoception models -- from perception of internal states (interoception), to action selection (body regulation), to prediction of consequences (forecasting). Bridging computational neuroscience with clinical translation.
- **Key Paper**: Petzschner FH et al. (2021) "Computational Models of Interoception and Body Regulation." *Trends in Neurosciences* 44(1):63-76. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2020.09.012) (PMID: 33378658)
- **SRL Connection**: Petzschner's computational framework is the theoretical architecture for SRL's future algorithmic work -- modeling how breathing interventions alter interoceptive predictions to improve regulation (Red Arrow 4: Clinical LLM Moat).

### Micah Allen, PhD
- **Institution**: Aarhus University, Center of Functionally Integrative Neuroscience; Cambridge Psychiatry
- **Role**: Professor of Computational Psychiatry
- **Key Contribution**: Developed formal models of cardiac active inference showing how ascending cardiac signals entrain exteroceptive perception. Published "In the Body's Eye: The Computational Anatomy of Interoceptive Inference" and "Unravelling the Neurobiology of Interoceptive Inference."
- **SRL Connection**: Allen's cardiac active inference model provides the computational bridge between HRV data (which SRL collects) and brain-level interoceptive processing. His work on interoceptive biomarkers in computational psychiatry directly supports SRL's Red Arrow 4 ambitions.

## 2. HRV Biofeedback & Resonant Breathing

The science of training the cardiovascular system through paced breathing at its resonant frequency (~0.1 Hz / ~6 breaths per minute). This is SRL's primary intervention modality.

### Paul Lehrer, PhD
- **Institution**: Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School
- **Role**: Professor of Psychiatry (Emeritus)
- **Key Contribution**: Co-founder of the resonant frequency biofeedback field. Documented that breathing at ~0.1 Hz triggers resonance in the baroreflex system, producing high-amplitude HRV oscillations. Demonstrated clinical applications in asthma, depression, fibromyalgia, PTSD.
- **Landmark Paper**: Lehrer P (2022) "My Life in HRV Biofeedback Research." *Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback* 47(4):289-298. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-022-09535-5) (PMID: 35254592)
- **Key Book**: *Principles and Practice of Stress Management* (Guilford Press)
- **SRL Connection**: Lehrer's work is the direct scientific ancestor of SRL's breathing protocols. His findings on resonant frequency as the mechanism for clinical benefit validate [[resonant-breathing-frequency]] as the cornerstone of SRL's intervention design.

### Evgeny G. Vaschillo, PhD
- **Institution**: Rutgers University (Center of Alcohol Studies)
- **Role**: Research Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Original discoverer (in Russia, pre-collaboration with Lehrer) of the resonance characteristics of the cardiovascular baroreflex system. Demonstrated that both paced breathing and rhythmical skeletal muscle tension at 0.1 Hz trigger cardiovascular resonance. Showed that heart rate and breathing phase relationships vary with age.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Vaschillo EG et al. (2011) "Resonances in the cardiovascular system caused by rhythmical muscle tension." *Psychophysiology* 48(7):927-36. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8986.2010.01156.x) (PMID: 21143610)
  - Lehrer PM & Vaschillo EG (2020) "Heart Rate and Breathing Are Not Always in Phase During Resonance Frequency Breathing." *Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback* 45(3):145-152. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-020-09459-y) (PMID: 32285231)
  - Leganes-Fonteneau M et al. (2021) "Cardiovascular mechanisms of interoceptive awareness: Effects of resonance breathing." *Int J Psychophysiol* 169:71-87. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2021.09.003) (PMID: 34534600)
- **SRL Connection**: Vaschillo's cardiovascular resonance model IS the mechanism behind SRL's core protocol. The 2021 Leganes-Fonteneau paper directly linking resonance breathing to interoceptive accuracy is a critical evidence chain link for SRL -- it shows the mechanism by which SRL's breathing protocol improves body awareness.

### Richard Gevirtz, PhD
- **Institution**: Alliant International University, California School of Professional Psychology, San Diego
- **Role**: Distinguished Professor of Psychology
- **Key Contribution**: Independently developed HRV biofeedback clinical applications over 40+ years. Published across anxiety, chronic pain, fibromyalgia, IBS, cardiac rehab, PTSD, insomnia, and performance. Past president of the Association for Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback (AAPB).
- **Key Paper**: Gevirtz R (2013) "The Promise of Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback: Evidence-Based Applications." *Biofeedback* 41(3):110-120.
- **SRL Connection**: Gevirtz's clinical breadth demonstrates the commercial viability of HRV biofeedback across dozens of conditions -- supporting SRL's TAM expansion narrative. His role as AAPB president signals the institutional legitimacy SRL needs for Red Arrow 1 (Clinical Credibility).

### Emerging Researchers in HRV Biofeedback
- **Mateo Leganes-Fonteneau** (Rutgers) -- bridging resonance breathing and interoceptive accuracy, a critical evidence chain for SRL
- **Fred Shaffer** (Truman State University) -- comprehensive HRV metric educator, author of widely-cited HRV measurement guides

## 3. Body Awareness & Somatic Therapies

The clinical practitioners and theorists who put interoception into therapeutic practice.

### Stephen W. Porges, PhD
- **Institution**: Traumatic Stress Research Consortium, Kinsey Institute, Indiana University; Dept. of Psychiatry, UNC Chapel Hill
- **Role**: Distinguished University Scientist
- **Key Contribution**: Creator of the Polyvagal Theory -- the model that the autonomic nervous system has three hierarchical circuits (ventral vagal/social engagement, sympathetic/mobilization, dorsal vagal/immobilization) governed by phylogenetic principles. Introduced the concepts of "neuroception" (unconscious threat detection) and the Social Engagement System.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Porges SW (2007) "The polyvagal perspective." *Biol Psychol* 74(2):116-43. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2006.06.009) (PMID: 17049418)
  - Porges SW (2022) "Polyvagal Theory: A Science of Safety." *Front Integr Neurosci* 16:871227. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnint.2022.871227) (PMID: 35645742)
- **SRL Connection**: Polyvagal Theory provides the clinical narrative framework for SRL's interventions -- [[polyvagal-theory]] and [[co-regulation]] are core concepts. The ventral vagal state IS the target state of SRL's breathing protocols. NOTE: The theory faces significant scientific criticism (see [[polyvagal-theory-under-fire-2026]]), so SRL must use Porges's framework carefully, emphasizing the clinically useful aspects while acknowledging debate.

### Peter A. Levine, PhD
- **Institution**: Foundation for Human Enrichment, Boulder, CO; Somatic Experiencing Trauma Institute
- **Role**: Founder of Somatic Experiencing (SE)
- **Key Contribution**: Developed Somatic Experiencing over 45+ years as a body-oriented trauma therapy that uses interoception and proprioception as core therapeutic elements. SE directs client attention to internal sensations rather than cognitive/emotional content, using "pendulation" (oscillation between activation and settling) and "titration" (gradual exposure).
- **Key Paper**: Payne P, Levine PA, Crane-Godreau MA (2015) "Somatic experiencing: using interoception and proprioception as core elements of trauma therapy." *Front Psychol* 6:93. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00093) (PMID: 25699005)
- **Key Book**: *Waking the Tiger: Healing Trauma* (1997); *In an Unspoken Voice* (2010)
- **SRL Connection**: Levine's concept of "titration to effect" directly maps onto SRL's minimum effective dose philosophy. SE's emphasis on bottom-up processing via interoceptive attention is the therapeutic ancestor of SRL's approach. The "pendulation" concept resonates with SRL's [[state-transition]] model.

### Bessel van der Kolk, MD
- **Institution**: Boston University School of Medicine; The Trauma Center at JRI
- **Role**: Professor of Psychiatry; Medical Director
- **Key Contribution**: Author of *The Body Keeps the Score* (2014), the bestselling book that brought trauma neuroscience and body-based healing to mainstream awareness. Argued that interoceptive access is the only way to change survival-oriented brain patterns. Promoted yoga, EMDR, neurofeedback, and body-based interventions for trauma.
- **SRL Connection**: Van der Kolk's mainstream credibility created the cultural moment that makes SRL's body-based approach legible to consumers and clinicians. "The body keeps the score" is now vernacular -- SRL can build on this awareness. His endorsement of interoceptive interventions provides clinical authority for SRL's approach.

### Cynthia Price, PhD, LMT
- **Institution**: University of Washington, School of Nursing
- **Role**: Research Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Developed Mindful Awareness in Body-oriented Therapy (MABT), an interoceptive awareness training that combines manual touch, mindfulness, and body literacy. RCT evidence shows MABT improves substance use outcomes, emotion regulation (measured by RSA), craving reduction, and interoceptive awareness in women with SUD.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Price CJ et al. (2019) "Longitudinal effects of interoceptive awareness training through MABT as adjunct to women's SUD treatment: A randomized controlled trial." *Drug Alcohol Depend* 198:140-149. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2019.02.012) (PMID: 30928884)
  - Price CJ et al. (2023) "Within-Person Modulation of Neural Networks following Interoceptive Awareness Training through MABT." *Brain Sci* 13(10):1396. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci13101396) (PMID: 37891765)
- **SRL Connection**: Price's MABT is the closest clinical analog to what SRL does digitally. Her RCT evidence -- particularly the neural plasticity findings showing insula deactivation and increased sensory-prefrontal connectivity -- is the strongest existing proof that interoceptive training produces measurable brain changes. SRL should cite and build on this work.

### Pat Ogden, PhD
- **Institution**: Sensorimotor Psychotherapy Institute, Boulder, CO
- **Role**: Founder
- **Key Contribution**: Developed Sensorimotor Psychotherapy, integrating body awareness and movement with cognitive and emotional processing for trauma treatment. Emphasizes the "window of tolerance" concept (originally from Dan Siegel) and working with sensorimotor processing patterns.
- **Key Book**: *Trauma and the Body: A Sensorimotor Approach to Psychotherapy* (2006)
- **SRL Connection**: Ogden's "window of tolerance" concept maps onto SRL's autonomic state model. Her work validates the principle that body-first interventions can shift psychological states -- the same principle SRL operationalizes through breathing protocols.

## 4. Contemplative Neuroscience & Breathwork Science

The science of meditation, breathwork, and their neural correlates.

### Richard J. Davidson, PhD
- **Institution**: University of Wisconsin-Madison, Center for Healthy Minds
- **Role**: William James and Vilas Professor of Psychology and Psychiatry; Founder and Director
- **Key Contribution**: Pioneer of contemplative neuroscience. Demonstrated that meditation produces lasting changes in brain structure and function. Showed that well-being is a skill that can be trained. Established the neuroscientific framework for four pillars of well-being: awareness, connection, insight, and purpose.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Dahl CJ, Lutz A, Davidson RJ (2015) "Reconstructing and deconstructing the self: cognitive mechanisms in meditation practice." *Trends Cogn Sci* 19(9):515-23. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.07.001) (PMID: 26231761)
  - Wielgosz J et al. (2019) "Mindfulness Meditation and Psychopathology." *Annu Rev Clin Psychol* 15:285-316. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-021815-093423) (PMID: 30525995)
- **SRL Connection**: Davidson's framework of well-being as a trainable skill is SRL's ultimate scientific narrative. His Center for Healthy Minds model demonstrates how to bridge contemplative practice and rigorous neuroscience -- a model SRL should emulate. Potential future academic partner.

### Antoine Lutz, PhD
- **Institution**: Lyon Neuroscience Research Center (INSERM U1028, CNRS UMR5292), Lyon, France
- **Role**: Research Director
- **Key Contribution**: Led pioneering neuroimaging studies of expert meditators with Davidson. Developed the attentional/constructive/deconstructive classification of meditation practices. Key contributor to understanding neural correlates of focused attention and open monitoring meditation.
- **SRL Connection**: Lutz's meditation classification system (attentional, constructive, deconstructive) provides a theoretical framework for positioning SRL's breathing protocols as "attentional family" interventions with specific neural targets.

### Willoughby B. Britton, PhD
- **Institution**: Brown University, Department of Psychiatry and Human Behavior
- **Role**: Associate Professor; Director, Clinical and Affective Neuroscience Laboratory
- **Key Contribution**: Leading researcher on adverse effects of meditation. Co-authored the landmark "Mind the Hype" paper calling for rigorous methodology in mindfulness research. Studies the dark side of contemplative practice and individual variability in response.
- **Key Paper**: Van Dam NT, Britton WB et al. (2018) "Mind the Hype: A Critical Evaluation and Prescriptive Agenda for Research on Mindfulness and Meditation." *Perspect Psychol Sci* 13(1):36-61. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691617709589) (PMID: 29016274)
- **SRL Connection**: Britton's work on adverse effects is essential for SRL's safety profile. SRL must design interventions that account for individual variability and avoid the hype cycle. Her rigor provides a template for how SRL should approach clinical claims (see feedback: [[feedback_clinical_claims.md]]).

### Andrew D. Huberman, PhD
- **Institution**: Stanford University, Department of Neurobiology (and Ophthalmology)
- **Role**: Associate Professor; Host of the Huberman Lab podcast
- **Key Contribution**: Popularized the "physiological sigh" (cyclic sighing: double inhale through nose, extended exhale through mouth) as a real-time stress reduction tool. Published a Cell Reports Medicine RCT showing that 5 minutes of daily cyclic sighing outperforms mindfulness meditation for mood improvement and respiratory rate reduction.
- **Landmark Paper**: Balban MY, Huberman AD et al. (2023) "Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal." *Cell Rep Med* 4(1):100895. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895) (PMID: 36630953)
- **SRL Connection**: Huberman is the most influential popularizer of breathwork science. His physiological sigh maps onto a specific pattern within SRL's breathing protocol toolkit. His massive audience (millions of podcast listeners) has pre-educated the consumer market about breathwork -- SRL benefits from this cultural priming. However, SRL must differentiate from "Huberman-style" general wellness by emphasizing clinical-grade precision and personalization.

### Zoran Josipovic, PhD
- **Institution**: New York University, Department of Psychology
- **Role**: Research Associate Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Studies nondual awareness and minimal phenomenal experience through neuroscience of advanced meditation. Proposes that consciousness-as-such is a non-representational, nondual awareness that cannot be reduced to arousal + content.
- **Key Paper**: Josipovic Z & Miskovic V (2020) "Nondual Awareness and Minimal Phenomenal Experience." *Front Psychol* 11:2087. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.02087) (PMID: 32973628)
- **SRL Connection**: Josipovic's work on nondual awareness maps onto the furthest end of SRL's interoceptive literacy spectrum -- what advanced practitioners may experience during deep practice. Relevant for SRL's long-term vision of consciousness exploration, though not immediate product priority.

### Matthew Walker, PhD
- **Institution**: UC Berkeley, Center for Human Sleep Science
- **Role**: Professor of Neuroscience and Psychology; Director
- **Key Contribution**: Founded the Center for Human Sleep Science. Author of *Why We Sleep* (2017 NYT bestseller). Research uses fMRI, EEG, genomics, proteomics, and autonomic physiology to investigate sleep's role in memory, emotion, Alzheimer's, cancer, and cardiovascular disease.
- **SRL Connection**: Sleep quality is a key downstream outcome of SRL's interventions (HRV biofeedback improves sleep architecture). Walker's work provides the evidence base for SRL's claim that breathing protocols improve sleep. His mainstream credibility is a market validation signal.

## 5. Clinician Resilience & Burnout Science

The researchers studying why healthcare workers break down and how to sustain them. This is SRL's primary market.

### Christina Maslach, PhD
- **Institution**: UC Berkeley, Department of Psychology
- **Role**: Professor of Psychology (Emerita)
- **Key Contribution**: Defined burnout as a three-dimensional syndrome: emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and reduced personal accomplishment. Created the Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI, 1981) -- still the gold standard measurement tool worldwide.
- **SRL Connection**: The MBI is the outcome measure SRL must use for burnout intervention studies. Maslach's three-factor model structures how SRL should describe its intervention's effects: reducing emotional exhaustion (primary), improving personal accomplishment (secondary), reducing depersonalization (tertiary).

### Tait D. Shanafelt, MD
- **Institution**: Stanford Medicine
- **Role**: Chief Wellness Officer; Jeanie and Stewart Ritchie Professor of Medicine; Associate Dean
- **Key Contribution**: The most prolific physician burnout researcher. Led landmark longitudinal studies showing physician burnout peaked at 62.8% in 2021 (post-COVID) and decreased to 45.2% by 2023. Established the Chief Wellness Officer (CWO) role as an institutional response. Argues for system-level, not individual-level, solutions.
- **Key Paper**: Ripp J & Shanafelt T (2020) "The Health Care Chief Wellness Officer: What the Role Is and Is Not." *Academic Medicine* 95(9):1354-1358. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000003433) (PMID: 32324635)
- **SRL Connection**: Shanafelt is the gatekeeper for institutional burnout interventions. His emphasis on system-level solutions means SRL must position its product as an organizational tool, not just a personal wellness app. His Stanford CWO role represents the ideal enterprise buyer persona for SRL (Red Arrow 3: Enterprise Pilot to Enterprise Contract). His longitudinal data provides the market sizing evidence.

### Susan D. Scott, PhD, RN, CPPS, FAAN
- **Institution**: University of Missouri Health Care
- **Role**: Nurse Scientist; Patient Safety Expert
- **Key Contribution**: Defined the "second victim" phenomenon -- healthcare providers traumatized by adverse clinical events. Created the forYOU Team, the first institutional peer support network for second victims. Found that 85% of clinicians will not ask for help after a traumatic event, and only 15% received institutional support.
- **SRL Connection**: Scott's second victim framework identifies a specific, acute use case for SRL's interventions: post-adverse-event autonomic recovery. SRL's [[gap-moment-training]] could be deployed as part of institutional peer support programs. Her finding that 85% won't ask for help validates the need for self-directed digital tools -- exactly what SRL builds.

### Colin P. West, MD, PhD
- **Institution**: Mayo Clinic; University of Colorado School of Medicine
- **Role**: Professor of Medicine, Medical Education, and Biostatistics; Collaborator with Shanafelt
- **Key Contribution**: Co-led the Mayo Clinic burnout research program. Published on structural interventions to reduce burnout including schedule changes, reduced documentation burden, and wellness programs.
- **SRL Connection**: West's evidence on structural interventions helps SRL position its tool as a complement to (not substitute for) system-level changes -- an important messaging distinction for enterprise buyers.

### Lotte N. Dyrbye, MD, MHPE
- **Institution**: University of Colorado School of Medicine
- **Role**: Chief Well-Being Officer; Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Extensive research on medical student and physician burnout, moral injury, and the relationship between burnout and patient safety outcomes.
- **SRL Connection**: Dyrbye's work on medical trainee burnout opens a pathway for SRL into graduate medical education programs -- a potential beachhead market.

## 6. Wearable Biofeedback & Digital Health / Interoceptive Technology

The builders of interoceptive technology and SRL's commercial landscape.

### Felix Schoeller, PhD
- **Institution**: MIT Media Lab (Fluid Interfaces Group); Institute for Advanced Consciousness Studies; University of Haifa
- **Role**: Research Scientist
- **Key Contribution**: Published the definitive review of "Interoceptive Technologies for Psychiatric Interventions" (2023) -- classifying technologies for manipulating interoception and their clinical applications. Proposed a taxonomy of interoceptive modulation, conditioning, and exposure paradigms. Assembled the largest interdisciplinary team in the field including Friston, Riva, Tsakiris, Allen.

## 7. Predictive Processing / Active Inference

The theoretical framework that explains WHY interoceptive training works at the computational level.

### Karl J. Friston, FRS, FMedSci
- **Institution**: Wellcome Centre for Human Neuroimaging, UCL, London
- **Role**: Scientific Director; Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Creator of the Free Energy Principle and Active Inference framework. Proposed that all living systems minimize free energy (prediction error) to maintain their existence. Applied to interoception: emotions are the content of interoceptive predictions; autonomic reflexes are enslaved by descending predictions from generative models. The most cited neuroscientist alive.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Seth AK & Friston KJ (2016) "Active interoceptive inference and the emotional brain." *Phil Trans R Soc B* 371(1708). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0007) (PMID: 28080966)
  - Owens AP et al. (2018) "Interoceptive inference: From computational neuroscience to clinic." *Neurosci Biobehav Rev* 90:174-183. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2018.04.017) (PMID: 29694845)
- **SRL Connection**: Friston's framework provides the deepest theoretical explanation for SRL's mechanism of action: resonant breathing works by increasing the precision (confidence) of interoceptive predictions, reducing prediction errors, and thereby improving allostatic regulation. This is the language for SRL's scientific publications and patent filings. Active inference IS the computational theory behind [[neuroharmonics]].

### Anil K. Seth, PhD
- **Institution**: University of Sussex, Sackler Centre for Consciousness Science
- **Role**: Professor of Cognitive and Computational Neuroscience; Director
- **Key Contribution**: Proposed "interoceptive inference" -- that emotions arise from the brain's best predictions about internal bodily signals. Developed the "interoceptive predictive coding model of conscious presence." Authored *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness* (2021), bringing these ideas to a general audience. Coined the phrase "consciousness is a controlled hallucination."
- **Key Papers**:
  - Seth AK (2013) "Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self." *Trends Cogn Sci* 17(11):565-573. (PMID: 24126130)
  - Seth AK (2012) "An interoceptive predictive coding model of conscious presence." *Front Psychol* 2:395. (PMID: 22291673)
- **SRL Connection**: Seth's "interoceptive inference" model is the theoretical bridge between SRL's breathing interventions and emotional/cognitive outcomes. His "Being You" book has popularized the idea that the self is built on interoceptive predictions -- making SRL's message more culturally legible. Potential high-value advisory board candidate.

### Lisa Feldman Barrett, PhD
- **Institution**: Northeastern University, Department of Psychology; Massachusetts General Hospital / Harvard Medical School
- **Role**: University Distinguished Professor
- **Key Contribution**: Creator of the Theory of Constructed Emotion -- emotions are not "triggered" but actively constructed by the brain using interoceptive predictions, past experience, and conceptual knowledge. Proposed the Embodied Predictive Interoception Coding (EPIC) model. Applied to depression as a disorder of allostasis where the brain becomes "locked in" and insensitive to sensory context.
- **Landmark Papers**:
  - Barrett LF (2017) "The theory of constructed emotion: an active inference account of interoception and categorization." *Soc Cogn Affect Neurosci* 12(1):1-23. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/scan/nsw154) (PMID: 27798257)
  - Barrett LF et al. (2016) "An active inference theory of allostasis and interoception in depression." *Phil Trans R Soc B* 371(1708). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2016.0011) (PMID: 28080969)
- **Key Book**: *How Emotions Are Made: The Secret Life of the Brain* (2017)
- **SRL Connection**: Barrett's constructed emotion theory provides the scientific basis for SRL's claim that training interoceptive accuracy can change emotional experience itself -- not just "manage" emotions, but alter how they are constructed. Her EPIC model is the theoretical framework for understanding how SRL's interventions work at the brain level. Her depression-as-allostatic-dysfunction model opens clinical pathways for SRL beyond burnout.

### Andy Clark, PhD
- **Institution**: University of Sussex (formerly University of Edinburgh)
- **Role**: Professor of Cognitive Philosophy
- **Key Contribution**: Author of *Surfing Uncertainty: Prediction, Action, and the Embodied Mind* (2016) -- the accessible synthesis of predictive processing theory. Argues the mind is fundamentally a prediction machine that minimizes prediction errors across both exteroceptive and interoceptive channels. Bridges predictive processing with embodied and extended cognition.
- **SRL Connection**: Clark's work provides the philosophical framework for SRL's approach: the mind is not a computer processing inputs, but a prediction engine that constructs reality from expectations. Breathing protocols work by changing the body's prediction landscape. His accessibility makes this framework usable in SRL's investor and clinician communications.
