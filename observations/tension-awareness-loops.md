# Tension Awareness Loops: Closed-Loop Interoceptive Entrainment

## Observation

Tension awareness loops are iterative body-scan and proprioceptive feedback cycles embedded within [[neurominute]] protocols. Not just awareness of tension but **closed-loop entrainment** — detect, map, breathe into, release, verify release.

### Protocol Elements

- **Day 2-3 pattern:** Radial pulse + breath synchrony → haptic feedback loop (Watch taps confirm cardiac rhythm awareness)
- **Day 5 pattern:** Half-speed movement cue in OR → proprioceptive awareness during clinical action (noticing tension *while working*, not just during breaks)
- **Week 2 pattern:** Shock reset via long exhale + peripheral vision expansion → pairing exteroceptive (visual field widening) with interoceptive (exhale-driven vagal activation)

### Mechanism

Uses insula precision mapping of cardiac + respiratory + somatosensory signals, then layers haptic/auditory feedback to create a real-time tension detection loop. The system is:
1. **Detect** — notice where tension lives (jaw, shoulders, diaphragm, pelvic floor)
2. **Map** — interoceptive precision about the quality of tension (sharp, dull, holding, bracing)
3. **Breathe into** — direct breath awareness to the tension site
4. **Release** — micro-relaxation of the targeted area on exhale
5. **Verify** — re-scan to confirm release, building interoceptive accuracy

## Significance

Tension awareness loops are the practical implementation of [[multi-phase-interoceptive-coupling]] at the most granular level. They build the skill of detecting and releasing tension in real-time during clinical work — not just during dedicated practice sessions.
