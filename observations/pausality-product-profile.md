## Core Product Features

### Biometric Feedback Engine
- **Real-time heart rate monitoring** via Apple Watch during every session
- **Blood oxygen (SpO2) monitoring** during sessions via Apple Watch
- **AirPods Pro 3 heart rate tracking** as alternative sensor
- Heart rate shown dropping on screen in real-time during breathing sessions
- Post-session recaps: HR journey, recovery speed, SpO2 changes

### Breathing Curriculum
- **21 science-backed breathing sessions**
- Structured across 3 progressive tiers: Foundation → Awareness → Mastery
- Sequential unlocking (progressive curriculum, not open library)
- Evidence-based protocols grounded in polyvagal theory, resonant breathing, interoceptive training

### Security & Privacy
- AES-256 encryption on all biometric data
- Data linked to user: Health & Fitness, Contact Info, Identifiers, Usage Data, Sensitive Info, Diagnostics
- Used for: Analytics, Product Personalization, App Functionality

### Social/Sharing
- Rich share cards (v1.0.8) showing session results
- Heart rate journey visualization shareable

## Positioning Statement

"Stress Control for High-Performance Professionals" — the breathing app that **proves it's working** with real-time heart rate monitoring during 21 science-backed breathing techniques.

### Key Differentiator
Unlike meditation apps (Calm, Headspace) that are passive and unmeasured, Pausality provides **closed-loop biometric verification** — you see your nervous system responding in real time. This is not relaxation content; it's measurable autonomic training.

### Pausality's Structural Advantages Over All Adjacent Apps
1. **Closed-loop biometric proof** — you see your HR dropping in real time (not just a timer)
2. **Progressive curriculum** — 21 sessions unlock sequentially (Foundation → Awareness → Mastery), not an open library
3. **Clinical credibility** — built by a 28-year CRNA, grounded in polyvagal theory and peer-reviewed research
4. **Dual hardware support** — Apple Watch + AirPods Pro 3
5. **Enterprise pathway** — B2B pilot model with measurable ROI, not just consumer app revenue
6. **CE credit pathway** — transforms app usage into credentialed professional development
