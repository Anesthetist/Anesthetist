## Source

Stacker, March 2026. "Burnout and AI are redefining healthcare work in 2026."

## Summary

55% of American healthcare workers are considering leaving their jobs in the next year, with burnout as a primary factor. 60% of healthcare organizations already use AI — mostly for clinical note-taking, automated charting, and patient education — to reduce administrative burden, which affects 40% of shifts and is cited as the top threat to wellbeing. The article emphasizes that organizational and structural changes (not just individual self-care) are essential to addressing the crisis.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Validates the market urgency for SRL's **Clinician Durability** framework. The 55% attrition risk creates a massive addressable market for prehabilitative interventions. The AI-for-admin trend also signals that as cognitive load from documentation decreases, there may be new opportunities to redirect clinician attention toward interoceptive and self-regulation skills — precisely what SRL trains.
