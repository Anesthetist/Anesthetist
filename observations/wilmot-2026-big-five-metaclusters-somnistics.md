# Wilmot 2026 Big Five Metaclusters Map to Polyvagal States — Somnistics Implications

## Source

**Wilmot, M.P. et al. (2026).** *Mapping Domains of Life Success: Insights From Meta-Analytic Criterion Profile Analysis.* Psychological Bulletin. DOI: 10.1037/bul0000476  
Meta-analysis of 206 life success variables across 10 domains, mapped onto Big Five personality trait configurations using meta-analytic criterion profile analysis (MACPA). N > 2 million participants across constituent studies.

## Core Finding

**The profile pattern effect** — the *configuration* of Big Five traits, not their overall elevation — accounts for approximately 30% of variance in life success outcomes, beyond what overall trait level predicts. This means *how* traits are arranged relative to each other matters as much as how high they score.

This is a **cybernetic argument** (framed explicitly via DeYoung's Cybernetic Big Five Theory): personality is the stable operating parameters of a self-regulating, goal-directed feedback system — not a fixed set of attributes.

## The Three Metaclusters and Their Polyvagal Correspondence

| Metacluster | Trait Profile | Life Success Theme | Polyvagal State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Contentment** | ES+, OP− | Life satisfaction, well-being, intimate relationships | Ventral vagal (safe, regulated) |
| **II — Agentic Engagement** | AG−, CO+ | Performance, accomplishment, leadership, ingenuity | Functional sympathetic (mobilized, exit-oriented) |
| **III — Self-Transcendence** | ES−, AG+ | Prosocial behavior, collective goals, helping others | Dorsal-vagal brake / relational engagement |

The structural correspondence is not coincidental — both the Cybernetic Big Five framework and polyvagal theory describe the organism as a hierarchically organized, threat-responsive, goal-directed self-regulating system.

## Key Implications for Somnistics

### 1. Gap Moment Training Is the Profile Pattern Intervention
Most wellness programs target **profile level** — general elevation ("be more resilient"). They fail because they don't address *configuration*. Gap Moment Training targets **profile pattern** — the specific shift from Metacluster II back to Metacluster I. This is the scientific grounding for why generic wellness fails but GMT works.

### 2. The Deference Cluster Is a CRNA Safety Signal
Cluster 3 (AG+, EX−, OP−), labeled "Deference," has "Safety performance" as its exemplar variable. For CRNA populations, this is double-edged: deference to surgical authority may be prosocially reinforced but autonomically costly. A CRNA locked in the Deference configuration may comply when they should escalate. **This is a publishable and AANA-relevant safety finding.**

### 3. Contentment Is the Measurable Intervention Target
The paper defines:
- *Gratification* (Cluster 1) — well-being from satisfying interpersonal demands
- *Balance* (Cluster 2) — capacity to adjust to diverse roles without defensive reactivity

Both are Metacluster I outcomes. **Pausality's pre-shift preparation is a contentment-priming intervention.** The Stanford PFI self-valuation subscale directly measures entry into this metacluster.

### 4. The Awe Index Anchors to Metacluster III
The Inclusion cluster (ES−, AG+, OP+) — idealistic, diplomatic, appreciative of diversity — maps onto the Awe Index concept. Awe is the felt-sense signature of Metacluster III access.

### 5. CRNA Shift Architect Is a Profile-Calibration Tool
The CRNA Shift Architect (pre-shift preparation system) functions as a daily profile-calibration instrument — using biometric and voice-input data to assess which metacluster configuration the clinician is in at shift start, and delivering the appropriate GMT protocol to recalibrate.

## Deepest Implication

Somnistics' theoretical core is the claim that **chronic occupational demand systematically traps healthcare professionals in one archetype** (Metacluster II), and that its interventions restore **archetype fluidity** — the capacity to access all three metaclusters as context requires.
