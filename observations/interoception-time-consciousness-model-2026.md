## Source

Frontiers in Psychology, 2026. Theoretical paper.

## Summary

A new neurocognitive framework proposes that interoceptive awareness and temporal orientation interact to maintain psychological and physiological stability, providing preliminary empirical evidence for a functional feedback loop that grounds conscious experience in the body and time. The model bridges embodied cognition, interoception, and time perspective research.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Directly relevant to SRL's Anterocept concept (anticipatory interoception) and the Agentic Time Weaving concept. If interoceptive awareness grounds temporal orientation, then training interoceptive literacy could simultaneously improve clinicians' ability to maintain temporal awareness during long procedures — a key Clinician Durability skill.
