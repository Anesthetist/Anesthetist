## Source

JMIR mHealth and uHealth, January 2026. Systematic review of wearable sensor technologies.

## Summary

A comprehensive systematic review documents the state of wearable sensor technologies for health monitoring in 2026. Random forest ML models lead data processing (20% of studies), followed by deep learning (17%). Wearable form factors now include patches and e-ink/tattoos. Key challenge: heart rate accuracy degrades during vigorous activity due to motion artifacts. Blood pressure and respiratory rate can now be estimated indirectly via AI algorithms.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Validates SRL's technology roadmap. The evolution toward patch-based and tattoo-like wearables aligns with Pausality's vision of unobtrusive physiological monitoring during clinical work. The AI-driven indirect estimation of vitals supports the Closed-Loop Biofeedback and Multimodal Sensor Fusion concepts. The motion artifact limitation during vigorous activity is relevant for clinical settings where providers are physically active.
