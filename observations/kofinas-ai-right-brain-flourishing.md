# Kofinas — AI Closes the Left-Brain Loop; Right-Brain Flourishing Awaits

## Source
Demetri Kofinas (@kofinas), March 29, 2026. [https://x.com/kofinas/status/2038242071245459813](https://x.com/kofinas/status/2038242071245459813)

## Key Insight
Kofinas argues that AI is closing the loop on a multi-century process that forced humanity to become increasingly left-brained to survive in industrial and information economies. If AI handles the computable, analytical, and machine-like tasks, human competitive advantage will shift sharply toward right-brained capacities — creativity, intuition, relational depth, somatic presence, and contemplative skill. He frames the outcome as binary: extinction-level disruption or a civilizational flourishing. The relationship between humanity and AI is compared to a parasite-host dynamic, emphasizing that alignment with human values is non-negotiable for the positive path.

## SRL Relevance
This observation is a direct external validation of SRL's foundational strategic thesis. Somnistics, Gap Moment Training, and the entire Pausality product ecosystem are built on the insight that the most undervalued human skill set — autonomic regulation, interoceptive literacy, contemplative capacity — is precisely what becomes scarce and precious as AI commoditizes analytical work. His framing of the "loop closing" maps to SRL's neuro-ouroboros concept (the feedback arc between technological suppression of somatic intelligence and its re-emergence). The right-brain/left-brain axis is a bridge between SRL's clinical language and mainstream strategic discourse.
