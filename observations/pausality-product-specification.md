# Pausality Product Specification: Features, Devices, and Curriculum (March 2026)

## Source

Compiled from pausality.health homepage, FAQ, and Science pages. Crawled March 19, 2026.

## Platform

- iOS app (iPhone required)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pausality/id6743325009
- Built by Somnistics Research Labs, Inc., Seattle, WA

## Biometric Device Support

### Apple Watch / Apple Watch Ultra
- Heart rate (HR)
- Blood oxygen (SpO2)
- Respiratory rate
- Full biometrics during sessions
- Companion app on watch

### AirPods Pro 3
- Heart rate via infrared sensors in ear canal
- No Apple Watch required
- Discreet monitoring option
- Automatic detection when connected

### Device Selection Guidance (from FAQ)
- AirPods Pro 3: ideal for discreet monitoring, environments where watches are impractical, or users already wearing AirPods
- Apple Watch: best for comprehensive health tracking with HR, SpO2, and respiratory data
- Many users switch between both depending on situation

## Session Structure

- **Duration:** 60 seconds per session
- **Curriculum:** 21 scientifically-sequenced breathing techniques
- **Progression:** Foundation > Awareness > Mastery
- **Core mechanic:** Real-time biometric feedback creates a learning loop that transforms breathing from abstract relaxation into precise physiological intervention

## Core Differentiators (vs. Calm, Headspace, etc.)

1. Real-time biometric feedback (not audio-only guidance)
2. 60-second sessions (not 10-20 minute meditations)
3. Preparation framing, not recovery framing
4. Designed for gap moments in clinical workflow
5. Built by a CRNA for clinicians
6. Physiological visibility: "see your nervous system respond"

## Enterprise Features (Partners page)

- Customized onboarding
- Admin dashboards for tracking engagement
- Dedicated support

## Privacy Model

- Biometric data stays on device
- Never sold to third parties
- Only anonymized aggregate data collected to improve protocols

## Scientific Foundation (per Science page)

"Somnistics is the applied science of breath-based physiological awareness."
Protocols grounded in peer-reviewed research on:
- Interoception
- Breathing physiology
- Biometric awareness

The foundation layer is interoceptive awareness: detecting internal body signals that shape emotional and cognitive control.
