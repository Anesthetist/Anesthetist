# OODA Loop Implementation for SRL Operations

Boyd's Observe → Orient → Decide → Act loop mapped to SRL's specific operational context. Derived from Cedric Chin's operations playbook applied to Somnistics, August 2025.

## The SRL OODA Mapping

### Observe
- Daily market insights bot (Reddit, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram)
- Weekly competitive intelligence scans
- PubMed mining pipeline
- App telemetry (biometrics, session length, completion rates)
- Synchronicity metrics (signal rate, engagement patterns)
- Sales conversation documentation (phrases that make ICPs lean in or hesitate)

### Orient
- 7 Powers scorecard — which powers are strengthening?
- Category theory morphism analysis — do state transitions compose?
- Vault evidence chains — does the science support the positioning?
- Map data against strategy: are CRNAs engaging? Are gap moments being used?

### Decide
- Randy + Jason commit together on strategic pivots
- Weekly experiment selection: one cheap test per week
- "Pause rule" — if CAC > $30 for four weeks, reallocate

### Act
- Ship within 1 week, not 1 month
- Weekly sprints with measurable output
- Every Monday: what did we observe? Every Wednesday: what does it mean? Every Friday: what are we shipping?

## Chin's Core Operations Principles

1. **Operations = "repeatable actions that turn strategy into outcomes."** The plumbing of execution.
2. **Tacit knowledge in ops** — you need people with judgment in context, not just SOPs.
3. **Slack vs. efficiency** — over-optimization kills adaptability. Keep intentional slack (time, cash, trust).
4. **Metrics for decisions** — ask "what decision would this metric help me make?" before tracking anything.
5. **Cadence is king** — weekly reviews, monthly retros, quarterly priorities. Rhythm > one-off heroics.

## Intentional Slack Design

- **Time slack:** 20% of team time reserved for synchronicity-driven projects
- **Cash slack:** Maintain buffer for opportunistic experiments
- **Trust slack:** Give pod leads autonomy to make calls without approval chain

## Implementation Artifacts

- 1-week experiment board (currently in ClickUp)
- Somnistics Tacit Knowledge Library (the vault itself)
- Weekly learning review cadence
- Friday "what did we learn" documentation cycle

## Target

OODA cycle time: **1 week**. Not 1 quarter.
