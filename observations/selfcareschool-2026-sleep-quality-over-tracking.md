## Source

Self Care School, 2026. "Sleep in 2026: Less Tracking, More Rhythm."

## Summary

A practitioner-facing trend piece reporting that the 2026 wellness community is shifting away from obsessive sleep metric tracking (orthosomnia) toward protecting circadian rhythms and nervous system regulation as the primary sleep optimization strategy. The consensus is moving toward simpler, biology-aligned practices over data-overloaded quantification.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Aligns precisely with SRL's SomnoAffinity and Minimum Effective Dose concepts: the most powerful sleep interventions are often the simplest (light timing, consistency, autonomic wind-down) rather than technology-heavy tracking regimes. As a CRNA with 28 years of sleep/anesthesia expertise, Randy is positioned as a credible voice ahead of this cultural shift — teaching clinicians the physiology behind sleep rather than the metrics.
