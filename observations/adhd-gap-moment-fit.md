# ADHD Brains Are Uniquely Suited for Gap Moment Training

## Observation

Gap Moment Training's core design parameters — 60-second format, 5x/day frequency, multi-modal input, real-time biofeedback — accidentally align perfectly with ADHD attention architecture:

### Why It Fits

- **Duration:** 60 seconds respects ADHD attention span ceiling; no 20-minute session to fail at
- **Frequency:** 5x/day provides the novelty and dopamine-driving repetition that ADHD brains need
- **Multi-modal input:** Breath + HRV feedback + visual cues + haptics triggers integrated attention networks (combining interoceptive and exteroceptive stimuli increases sustained attention and cognitive flexibility)
- **Concrete feedback:** ADHD brains reject abstract benefit claims. Apple Watch showing HR change in real-time = proof. "It's like hitting a reset button on my brain."
- **No choice overload:** Single-screen, single-protocol avoids the decision paralysis that causes ADHD users to abandon apps like Calm/Headspace

### Community Signals

- r/ADHD users describe box breathing as "a reset button" — validates the micro-intervention concept
- User curiosity about HRV tracking ("Never thought to check my HRV though") shows readiness for biofeedback
- No competitor has saturated the ADHD community with breathing + HRV feedback combo

## Significance

The ADHD community is a large, underserved ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) that Pausality can capture without product modification — the same 60-second format that works for CRNAs works for ADHD because both populations need brief, frequent, measurable interventions embedded in workflow.
