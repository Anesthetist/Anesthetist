# 21-Day Recursive Curriculum Produces Compound Regulation Returns

## Observation

Users completing the full [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day cycle report qualitatively different regulation capacity versus isolated single-session practice. The recursive design — where each day recapitulates prior elements while adding new complexity — produces compound neuroplastic returns that feel distinct from simply doing 21 separate sessions.

Specific reports include:
- Days 1-7: "I can actually feel my breath now" (baseline interoceptive accuracy)
- Days 8-14: "I notice the switch happening" (awareness of multi-anchor transitions)
- Days 15-21: "The practice starts itself" (approaching unconscious competence)
- Post-completion: Regulation capacity persists and spontaneously activates during gap moments without deliberate effort

## Significance

The recursive/spiral structure appears critical — it's not just volume of practice but the progressive layering and self-referential design that drives compound adaptation. This supports the curriculum design principle over a "library of random sessions" approach.
