## Overview

In January 2026, Randy Graybeal submitted a **Category A Prior Approval Application** to the AANA (American Association of Nurse Anesthetists) for a novel continuing education program: *Pausality: Interoceptive Training for CRNA Performance & Well-Being*.

**Application submitted to:** Diane Lamitz (dlamitz@aana.com)

---

## Program Core Premise

> "We train CRNAs to monitor patients with extraordinary precision — but we never teach them to monitor themselves. Pausality addresses this gap by developing interoceptive awareness and autonomic self-regulation skills through a 21-session progressive breathwork curriculum with real-time biometric feedback."

---

## Program Architecture

- **Format:** 21-session progressive curriculum
- **Structure:** Breathwork training + real-time biometric feedback (Apple Watch HRV monitoring)
- **Assessment:** MAIA-2 validated interoceptive awareness measurement
- **Theoretical grounding:** Polyvagal theory, interoception science, HRV biofeedback research

---

## Why Category A Matters

AANA Category A CE credits require rigorous prior approval and signal a higher standard of evidence-based content. Achieving Category A status would:
1. Formally credential Pausality as a clinically recognized training program
2. Provide institutional pathway into CRNA continuing education requirements
3. Create a credibility signal for hospital/health system procurement conversations
4. Establish precedent for "interoceptive training" as a recognized professional competency

---

## Burnout Context Framing (Application Language)

> "With documented burnout rates exceeding 50% among anesthesia providers, self-regulation is no longer optional — it's a professional competency."

---

## Strategic Significance

This application represents Somnistics' first formal move toward institutionalizing interoceptive training as a recognized CRNA professional competency — bridging from product to profession-defining standard.
