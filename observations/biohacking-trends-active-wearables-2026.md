## Source

Outliyr (biohacking media), University Magazine, HealthCareReaders. Multiple trend reports, Q1 2026.

## Summary

Key biohacking trends for 2026 include the emergence of "active wearables" (Wearables 3.0) — devices that actively intervene to modulate physiological states rather than merely tracking. Neurofeedback headbands providing real-time brain wave feedback for meditation and focus are now mainstream. Smart rings have overtaken watches as preferred wearables. ~12% of professional athletes and executives reportedly use neurofeedback for flow state access. Cold exposure focus has shifted from physical to mental resilience benefits.
