# UC PMHNP — The Road Not Taken

Randy considered and actively pursued a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner degree through the University of Cincinnati. He envisioned a retreat center model — a clinic in the Pacific Northwest combining prescriptive authority with somatic therapy, psychedelic-assisted therapy, meditation, movement, breath, and consciousness training. He chose NOT to become a clinical practitioner.

## Randy's Words

"I started graduate school to become a psychiatric nurse practitioner to evaluate and prescribe, and give psychedelic-assisted therapy in my own clinic in a gorgeous retreat center in the Pacific Northwest where we invite highly talented clinicians, massage therapists, nutritionists, lifestyle designers, awareness and consciousness training and masters of meditation, movement, breath where people can come for very exclusive therapies."

## Why This Matters

The PMHNP path is the road not taken. It contextualizes WHY Somnistics exists as a training platform and technology company rather than a clinical practice. The PMHNP path would have been one practitioner serving hundreds; Somnistics can reach millions. The clinical instinct found a different expression — scalable training tools rather than individual clinical encounters.
