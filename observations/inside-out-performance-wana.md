# Inside-Out Performance: WANA Lecture Curriculum for CRNAs

## Observation

A 10-slide clinical lecture for the Washington Association of Nurse Anesthetists positions nervous system regulation as "Inside-Out Performance" — optimizing internal state FIRST to enhance external clinical performance.

### Specific Perioperative Timing Protocols

| Window | Duration | Protocol | Use Case |
|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| Pre-procedure | 3 min | Box breathing + body scan + intention setting | Before induction |
| Between cases | 90 sec | Extended exhale + micro-reset | During room turnover |
| End-of-shift | 5 min | Full vagal reset + gratitude | Preventing carry-over |
| 30-second emergency | 30 sec | Body scan validation only | Between any two tasks |

### Handling CRNA Objections

- "I forget" → Anchor to hand-washing (you never forget that — make it the same trigger)
- "No time" → 30 seconds between cases (you have that, always)
- "It's woo" → Show HRV data (measurable, not mystical)
- "I'm fine" → Over 60% of CRNAs report burnout; "fine" is the most dangerous state

### Framing

Nervous system regulation as equipment maintenance — like checking your airway cart before a case. Not an afterthought, not wellness, not self-care — it's clinical preparation. "Inside-Out Performance" captures this: the internal state determines the external outcome.

## Significance

This is the most operationally specific curriculum design in the vault — exact timing, exact objection handling, exact clinical integration points. It's the sales pitch AND the product in one lecture.
