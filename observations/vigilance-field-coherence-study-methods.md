# Somnistics Vigilance and Field-Coherence Studies

## Overview

Two complementary investigations testing Somnistics interventions, both IRB-approved and preregistered.

## Study 1: Autonomic Regulation and Vigilance (21 Days)

Evaluates whether distinct 60-second breathing protocols improve autonomic regulation and vigilance over 21 days in CRNAs.

- Draws on evidence that paced breathing at ~0.1 Hz (~6 breaths/minute) increases baroreflex sensitivity and lowers blood pressure
- HRV biofeedback reduces anxiety and depression symptoms

## Study 2: Incivility Prehabilitation (14 Days)

Tests whether a 14-day incivility prehabilitation program improves team performance under hostile conditions.

- Builds on findings that rude remarks impair diagnostic performance in NICU simulations
- "Prehabilitation" framing: training resilience BEFORE exposure to workplace incivility

## Significance

This is the first formal, preregistered research protocol for Somnistics interventions. Moves from anecdotal validation to structured clinical evidence. Study 2's incivility prehabilitation angle is particularly novel; no existing interventions specifically pre-train clinicians to maintain performance under social stress.
