# AI Attention Mechanisms as Rediscovery of Biological Attention

## Observation

AI "attention mechanisms" (transformer architecture) are mathematical re-expressions of things biology has done for ~500M years — selectively amplifying some signals and suppressing others to guide behavior. This maps directly to Somnistics' positioning.

### Category Story
*"We didn't invent attention in the OR — we're rediscovering it in the body."*

### Mapping: Transformer Attention → Somnistics Attention

| AI Attention | Somnistics Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Queries-keys-values + softmax weighting | Interoceptive signal weighting (which body signals to attend to when) |
| Multi-head attention (parallel streams) | Polyanchora — multi-anchor attention training across modalities |
| Positional encoding (sequence awareness) | Gap Moment temporal context (pre-case, between cases, post-code) |
| Self-attention (input attends to itself) | Self-remembering — consciousness monitoring its own state |
| Cross-attention (one sequence attends to another) | Co-regulation — one nervous system attending to another |
| Attention dropout (regularization) | Neurogating — selectively suppressing irrelevant signals |

### Strategic Implication

This framing positions Somnistics at the intersection of two converging fields:
1. AI/ML community discovering that attention is the key architectural primitive
2. Contemplative neuroscience demonstrating that biological attention is trainable

Somnistics is building the *training system* for the biological attention mechanism that AI researchers are trying to reverse-engineer computationally.

## Significance

This narrative is powerful for investor pitches (tech-adjacent framing), academic positioning (bridges neuroscience and AI), and marketing ("Your nervous system already has an attention mechanism — we train it").
