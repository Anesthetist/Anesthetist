## Source

InSight+ (Medical Journal of Australia digital publication), 2026. Coverage of the Grossman et al. 39-author critique paper.

## Summary

Following the publication of "Why The Polyvagal Theory is Untenable" by 39 ANS scientists, mainstream medical and practitioner media began running "Polyvagal Theory debunked" headlines. These stories are circulating in clinical, coaching, and wellness communities and generating confusion. The scientific debate is ongoing — Porges responded in the same journal issue — and the practical utility of PVT-derived clinical approaches (resonant breathing, HRV biofeedback, co-regulation) is not negated by the anatomical critiques.

## Why It Matters for SRL

SRL needs a clear, nuanced communication strategy for this controversy. The functional interventions SRL teaches (resonant breathing, HRV biofeedback, gap moments) rest on well-supported autonomic physiology independent of PVT's specific neuroanatomical claims. Proactively clarifying this distinction in SRL materials protects brand credibility and positions Randy as a scientifically sophisticated voice in the space — one who tracks the evolving evidence and builds on durable mechanisms rather than popular frameworks.
