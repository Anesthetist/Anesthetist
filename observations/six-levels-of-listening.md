# Six Levels of Listening: Co-Regulation Through Presence

## Observation

The ALA (Accomplishment Leadership Academy) 2021 framework defines six progressive levels of listening that directly map to co-regulation capacity:

| Level | Mode | Neural Correlate |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| 1 | Distracted | Low attention, energy conservation — sympathetic or dorsal vagal |
| 2 | Multi-focused | Split attention, task-switching — executive network strain |
| 3 | Non-judgmental | Full attention, monitoring own emotional state — salience network active |
| 4 | Heart-centered | Loving attention, tracking saying/feeling/meaning — ventral vagal + mirror neurons |
| 5 | Inspired | Commitment container, inspiration comes through — flow state + social engagement |
| 6 | Inspiring | Deeply present to whole person (body, mind, spirit) — full co-regulation, mutual ventral vagal activation |

### Three Dimensions of Deep Listening

- **Saying** — words and content (left-hemisphere processing)
- **Feeling** — emotions and affect (right-hemisphere + insula processing)
- **Meaning** — intention, perspective, worldview (DMN + mentalizing networks)

### Core Insight

Quality of relationships depends on quality of listening. [[co-regulation]] occurs through deep presence — Level 6 listening IS co-regulation. The listener's regulated nervous system becomes a regulatory resource for the speaker.

## Significance

This framework extends somnistics beyond individual breath practice into relational/organizational contexts. A CRNA who practices Level 4+ listening during team interactions is performing co-regulation — the same ventral vagal activation achieved through breath, now achieved through attentional quality.
