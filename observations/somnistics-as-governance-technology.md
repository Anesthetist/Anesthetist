## Framing

Somnistics can be understood not as an app or wellness platform, but as a **governance model for embodied intelligence** — a technology that shapes how perception, interoception, and meaning emerge within the individual and collective sensorium.

This framing arose from mapping Indy Johar's "From Decision to Decisioning" framework onto the Somnistics ontology (March 2026 session).

---

## The Governance Analogy

Traditional governance: *central command → decides → controls → administers behavior*

Somnistics governance model: *distributed sensing → adapts → coherence → emergence*

| Governance Concept (Indy Johar) | Somnistics Equivalent | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epistemic & perceptual infrastructure | NeuroEpistemic Layer™ (HRV, CO₂, EEG proxy + interoceptive tagging) | Defines *how* the organism knows what it knows | 1-minute NeuroMinute re-establishes sensing baseline |
| Distributed, situational intelligence systems | Gap Moment Detection Engine™ | Rather than central command, each clinician is a sensor in a learning nervous system | OR gap moments as distributed micro-feedback nodes |
| Deliberative institutions | Somnistics training curriculum | Structured environments for developing new sensing capacities | 21-session Pausality progressive curriculum |
| Algorithmic mediator | Neuroadaptive biofeedback engine | AI-guided personalization of protocol based on biometric state | Adaptive pacing of breath cadence to measured RSA |
| Cultural grammars | Interoceptive literacy curriculum | Rewriting collective assumptions about what is "thinkable" or "feelable" | Teaching CRNAs that self-monitoring is professional, not weak |

> **Governance = epistemic technology → Somnistics = nervous system governance technology**
>
> Decision → Sensing

---

## Implications

1. **Somnistics is infrastructure, not a product** — like communication networks or legal frameworks, it creates the conditions for better decisions without making decisions itself
2. **The training is governance design** — shaping what is perceptually available in the critical clinical moment
3. **Scale follows from the governance frame** — distributed adoption doesn't require central control; each trained clinician becomes a self-governing node in a regulated network
4. **"Cultural grammar" is the deepest moat** — when Somnistics' vocabulary (NeuroMinute, gap moment, finesse) becomes the language of clinical self-regulation, switching costs become existential

---

## Strategic Value

This framing positions Somnistics not as a wellness company (which is easily commoditized) but as a **epistemic infrastructure provider** — analogous to how TCP/IP governs internet communication or how accounting standards govern financial reporting. The company that defines the grammar of nervous system governance defines the field.
