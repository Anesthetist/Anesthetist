## Overview

A standardized generation framework for creating NeuroMinute scripts using Interpersonal Neurobiology (IPNB) and Internal Family Systems (IFS) as the dual theoretical backbone. This protocol ensures non-pathologizing, clinically-grounded 60-second interventions.

---

## Core Therapeutic Mechanism

**Goal:** Move the user from a "Part-blended" state (Chaos or Rigidity on the Siegel River of Integration) back into the "River of Integration" — the Self-leadership state characterized as flow.

**Approach:** Guided *differentiation* (unblending) — not fixing or suppressing the activated state, but building the capacity to be present with intensity. Presence *is* the integration.

---

## The Four-Step Script Structure

### 1. Acknowledge the Part
Gently name the "felt sense" (stress, overwhelm, numbness) without judgment. Frame it as *a Part*, not the whole person.
- *"There's a frazzled part of you. Just let it be here."*

### 2. Invite the Self
Use invitational language to call the core Self — with qualities of Curiosity and Clarity — to the "watcher's seat."
- *"Bring your attention to the rhythmic sound."*

### 3. Differentiate the Basic Elements
Guide the Self to witness the raw interoceptive signals of the activated Part:
- *"Notice the rhythm of the heart."*
- *"Notice the texture of the breath."*
- *"Notice the temperature in the hands."*

### 4. Do Not Fix
Never instruct the user to "fix," "stop," or "get rid of" the feeling. The witnessing itself is the therapeutic act.
- Replace "stop feeling shaky" with "notice the pure sensation of energy in them — not 'shaky,' but 'activated.'"

---

## Input / Output Specification

**Inputs provided to generator:**
1. `[Input State]`: Starting physiological state (e.g., "Q2 Chaos: Just finished a code, heart pounding, mind racing")
2. `[Intervention Modality]`: Primary sensory tool (e.g., "6-breaths-per-minute pacing" or "Rhythmic 3.5Hz sound")
3. `[Desired Outcome]`: Goal state (e.g., "Q4 Calm & Integrated: Re-centered and ready for next task")

**Output format:** 60-second script in 10–15 second blocks

---

## Language Guidelines

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| "Notice..." | "Stop feeling..." |
| "Become aware of..." | "You must..." |
| "Get curious about..." | "Fix..." |
| "Can you just allow..." | "Get rid of..." |

## Example (Applied)

**Input:** Q2 Chaos — Post-code, heart pounding, hands shaky. Need reset before case in 10 min.
**Modality:** Plotkin-style trance beat (3.5Hz rhythmic audio) + guided attention
**Goal:** Q1 Focused Flow

**Script:**
- (0:00–0:10): *"Acknowledge the 'frazzled' feeling. It's an activated part of you. Just let it be here. Now, bring your attention to the rhythmic sound."*
- (0:11–0:25): *"Let that beat be an anchor. Get curious. Bring your attention to your heart. Don't try to change it. Just notice its own rhythm, separate from the beat."*
- (0:26–0:40): *"Now, the hands. They may feel shaky. Can you just notice the pure sensation of energy in them? Not 'shaky,' but 'activated.' Just noticing. You are the watcher."*
- (0:41–0:55): *"You've differentiated the heart, the hands, and the sound. You are the container for all of them. This is the flow state. You are centered. You are in control."*
- (0:56–1:00): *"You are ready."*

## Clinical Significance

This protocol makes NeuroMinute scripts replicable at scale while preserving the somatic, non-pathologizing stance that distinguishes Somnistics from standard mindfulness apps. The IPNB + IFS integration grounds the approach in established therapeutic frameworks while requiring zero therapy background from the user.
