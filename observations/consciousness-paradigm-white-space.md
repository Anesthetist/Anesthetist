# Paradigm Shift White Space: Materialism Exhausted, Somnistics Bridges Science and Embodied Practice

## Observation

A growing recognition in science and philosophy (Gómez-Marín, McGilchrist, Vervaeke) that materialism is exhausted and unable to account for anomalies in consciousness research. Competing models (noetic, intersubjective, embodied) are gaining legitimacy, opening **white space for translational tools** like Pausality.

### Key Signals
1. **Consciousness research** is fragmenting away from strict materialist reductionism
2. **Right-hemisphere primacy** (McGilchrist) gaining mainstream traction
3. **Relevance Realization** (Vervaeke) provides a cognitive science framework for meaning-making
4. **Nondual awareness research** (Josipovic) being studied with 7T fMRI

### Strategic Implication for SRL
Somnistics can credibly position itself as both:
- **Evidence-based** — grounded in neuroscience, HRV, validated instruments
- **Paradigm-expanding** — bridging mainstream neuroscience with lived, embodied training

This dual positioning is rare and defensible. Most competitors are either:
- Pure science (dry, clinical, no experiential depth)
- Pure wellness/spiritual (no measurement, no rigor)

SRL occupies the bridge.

## Significance

This observation supports the "Consciousness Engineering" output concept and frames why Somnistics is positioned to lead a category rather than compete within an existing one.
