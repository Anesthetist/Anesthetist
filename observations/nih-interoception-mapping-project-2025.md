## Source

ScienceDaily / Scripps Research, October 2025. Led by Nobel Prize-winning neuroscientist Ardem Patapoutian (Scripps Research) and collaborators at the Allen Institute.

## Summary

The NIH awarded $14.2 million over five years to a team led by Ardem Patapoutian — who shared the 2021 Nobel Prize for discovering cellular sensors for touch — to build a comprehensive atlas of the interoceptive nervous system at the neuronal level. The project aims to map the "hidden sixth sense": the vast network of neurons sensing internal bodily states including circulation, digestion, and immune activity. Investigators describe interoception as "fundamental to nearly every aspect of health, but a largely unexplored frontier."

## Why It Matters for SRL

This is the single largest coordinated research investment in interoception science to date, and will generate foundational neuroanatomical data that will anchor and validate the entire field — including SRL's Interoception, Interoceptive Literacy, and Multi-Phase Interoceptive Coupling concepts. SRL should monitor outputs from this project closely as they will likely set the definitional and evidence standard for interoception-based interventions over the next five years.
