# Six Error Archetypes from Quality Review Committee

## Observation

Over 10 years as a voting member of the Anesthesia Quality Review Committee, Randy reviewed hundreds of critical incidents and identified six recurring error archetypes. The crucial insight: **most events aren't caused by ignorance — they're caused by state drift.**

### The Six Archetypes

1. **Attention Narrowing + Missed Cue** — Clinician locks onto one parameter (e.g., blood pressure) while a second critical signal (e.g., end-tidal CO₂) drifts undetected. Tunnel vision under stress.

2. **Time Compression + Rushed Sequence** — External pressure (surgeon impatience, room turnover demands) compresses the decision timeline, leading to skipped safety steps.

3. **Conflict/Incivility → Communication Degradation** — Interpersonal friction in the OR erodes the quality of clinical communication. Information stops flowing, handoffs become incomplete.

4. **Fatigue + "Small Slips" That Stack** — No single error is catastrophic, but accumulated micro-errors across a shift create a brittle system that fails when one more stressor arrives.

5. **Normalization of Deviance** — Repeated small deviations from protocol that "work fine" gradually shift the baseline until an unsafe practice becomes routine.

6. **Recovery Failures After Stress Spike** — Clinician fails to return to baseline autonomic state after an acute stressor, carrying residual sympathetic activation into the next case.

## Key Insight

> "I've watched the error pipeline from the inside... Somnistics becomes the missing upstream intervention: We train state stability before the pipeline starts flowing."

Every archetype traces back to autonomic state dysfunction — not lack of knowledge. [[gap-moment-training]] and [[neurogating]] address the root cause by maintaining regulation capacity throughout the shift.

## Significance

This observation reframes patient safety from "education and protocols" (the standard approach) to "autonomic state management" (the somnistics approach). It is the empirical foundation for positioning Gap Moment Training as a patient safety intervention, not just a wellness tool.
