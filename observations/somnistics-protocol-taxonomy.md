## Overview

Somnistics uses a three-category protocol taxonomy matching the three primary clinical regulatory goals. Each protocol is parameterized with specific cadence, ratio, duration, and delivery route specifications.

---

## Protocol 1: Down-Shift (Acute Stress Reset)

**Goal:** Rapidly reduce sympathetic activation — acute stress, pre-induction, post-critical event

**Parameters:**
- Cadence: 4:6 or 4:7 (4-second inhale, 6–7-second exhale)
- Route: Nasal, diaphragmatic
- Duration: 60–90 seconds
- Mechanism: Extended exhale lengthens vagal efferent tone via RSA; exhale-biased ratio shifts HRV toward parasympathetic dominance

**Gap Moment Application:** Pre-induction checklist, between cases, post-difficult-patient-interaction

---

## Protocol 2: Stabilize (Focus / Resonance)

**Goal:** Establish coherent autonomic baseline — sustained attention, readiness state

**Parameters:**
- Cadence: 5.5–6.0 breaths per minute (resonance frequency)
- Duration: 2–3 minutes for full resonance entrainment
- Mechanism: Maximizes RSA amplitude by matching breath rate to baroreflex oscillation; peaks LF HRV power
- Note: Individual resonance frequency varies (most fall between 4.5–7.0 bpm); can be biometrically personalized

**Gap Moment Application:** Pre-shift preparation, sustained long-case attention maintenance, recovery between demanding sequences

---

## Protocol 3: Interrupt (Acute Spike Interrupt)

**Goal:** Rapid interruption of acute sympathetic spike — unexpected critical event, perceived loss of control

**Parameters:**
- Step 1: 1–2 physiological sighs (double inhale through nose, full exhale through mouth) — fastest known stress reset (~10 seconds)
- Step 2: 4:6 breathing for 60 seconds to consolidate reset
- Mechanism: Physiological sigh re-inflates collapsed alveoli, driving CO₂ dump and rapid PNS rebound; followed by resonance entrainment

**Gap Moment Application:** After unexpected adverse event, before re-entering room after difficult case, mid-long-case tension spike

---

## Feedback Integration

- **HRV monitoring** (Apple Watch or dedicated wearable): confirms shift in LF/HF ratio during stabilize protocol
- **ETCO₂** (when available): validates CO₂ tolerance and confirms absence of hyperventilation
- **Subjective felt-sense** combined with biometric validation: core interoceptive training loop

---

## Protocol Selection Algorithm

| State | Protocol | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Acute spike (sudden stress) | Interrupt | ~70 sec |
| Moderate elevation (building tension) | Down-Shift | 60–90 sec |
| Pre-shift preparation or recovery | Stabilize | 2–3 min |
