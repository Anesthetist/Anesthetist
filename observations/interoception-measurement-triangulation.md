# Interoception Measurement Triangulation: Sensibility + Accuracy + Awareness at Scale

## Observation

A three-layer interoception measurement framework has been developed for Pausality outcome validation, based on the Garfinkel accuracy/sensibility/awareness model:

### Layer 1: Subjective Interoceptive Sensibility
- **MAIA short form** — weekly EMA micro-check-ins (3-5 items) embedded in app
- **BPQ short form** — baseline + quarterly for autonomic sensitivity shifts
- Signal: Are users reporting improved body trust, attention shifting, self-regulation?

### Layer 2: Objective Interoceptive Accuracy
- **Heartbeat Detection Game** (Apple Watch/iPhone) — users count heartbeats vs. sensor HR
- Signal: Accuracy gains = better brain-body mapping, tied to lower freeze risk
- Scalable digital adaptation of the Schandry Task

### Layer 3: Interoceptive Awareness (Metacognitive)
- **Confidence slider** after heartbeat task: "How sure are you?"
- Signal: Metacognitive insight = user's ability to gauge their own accuracy → key resilience skill

### Layer 4: Physiological Biofeedback Signatures
- Apple Watch: HRV (RMSSD), respiration rate, RSA
- Pre/post NeuroMinute deltas over time
- Signal: Faster recovery to baseline after stress = Pausality effect

### Layer 5: Implementation Metrics
- Burnout proxy: 1-2 item weekly Maslach short form
- Micro-flow states: EMA "Did this reset help?"
- Engagement: completion rates of daily 60-second practices

## Significance

This triangulation differentiates Pausality from generic wellness apps — it's publishable, CEU-approvable, and maps directly to system-level ROI (reduced burnout/turnover costs, improved safety, clinician retention). The framework is designed to be both hackathon-ready and investor-credible.
