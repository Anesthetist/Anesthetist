## Overview

This week's scan surfaces a major scientific debate around Polyvagal Theory, landmark NIH investment in interoception mapping, continued strong evidence for HRV biofeedback across clinical populations, and emerging frontiers in meditation neuroscience (brain fluid dynamics and bio-computing). The convergence of AI with neurofeedback and wearable biosensors continues to accelerate toward consumer-scale delivery.

---

## Sleep & Recovery

- **Sleep quality vs. duration debate continues.** A Penn State study (261 older adults) found sleep quality — not duration — predicted next-day processing speed, while a Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience study (17 older adults, in-home wearable EEG) found no significant sleep architecture effects on executive function. The inconsistency highlights the need for more nuanced, individualized models. → `urn:srl:evidence:frontiers-2026-sleep-quality-cognition-older-adults`
- **Circadian chronotherapy goes precision.** A new review in the Journal of Psychosomatic Research synthesizes AI-driven circadian monitoring, dynamic lighting strategies, and meal-timing interventions for performance optimization. → `urn:srl:evidence:sciencedirect-2026-circadian-chronobiological-strategies`

## Cognitive Performance

- **Meditation alters brain fluid dynamics.** A 2026 neuroimaging study found that expert meditators switching to focused attention showed reduced and more coherent cerebrospinal fluid flow — a novel biomarker beyond neural activity. → `urn:srl:evidence:psypost-2026-meditation-brain-fluid-dynamics`
- **Buddhist monk brain scans reveal meditation-type-specific complexity.** Both Samatha and Vipassana meditation produced significantly more complex, less predictable brain activity compared to resting state, with distinct signatures for each practice style.
- **Bio-Intelligence Initiative launched.** All Here announced the world's first research program linking deep meditative neural signatures to bio-inspired AI systems. → `urn:srl:observation:trend-bio-intelligence-initiative-meditation-ai-2026`
- **AI neurofeedback wins CES 2026.** NeuroFeedback 2.0 took a CES Innovation Award for AI-powered personalized cognitive training. The neurofeedback market is projected to reach $663M by 2032. → `urn:srl:observation:trend-ai-neurofeedback-ces-2026`

## Physical Performance & Wearables

- **Wearable biosensors leap forward.** A 2026 JMIR systematic review documents real-time sweat-based cortisol and lactate detection, tear-based glucose monitoring, and AI-driven predictive injury models now integrated into sports platforms. → `urn:srl:observation:trend-wearable-sensors-2026-systematic-review`
- **Breathwork + music induces paradoxical bliss.** A UCLA-affiliated study found breathwork with music produces blissful altered states with simultaneous stress-response activation and changes in emotion-processing brain blood flow. → `urn:srl:evidence:nature-2025-breathwork-music-blissful-state-brain-blood-flow`

## Cross-Cutting Themes

- **Polyvagal Theory: major scientific debate.** Grossman et al. (38 co-authors) published "Why the Polyvagal Theory is Untenable" in Clinical Neuropsychiatry. Porges issued a substantial rebuttal. Clinical interventions (Safe and Sound Protocol) continue showing efficacy despite theoretical controversy. SRL should track this closely. → `urn:srl:evidence:pmc-2026-polyvagal-theory-critique-grossman`
- **Interoception gets $14.2M NIH atlas project.** Scripps Research and the Allen Institute are building the first comprehensive map of the body's internal sensory system, led by Nobel laureate Ardem Patapoutian. → `urn:srl:evidence:patapoutian-2025-nih-interoception-atlas`
- **Maternal touch as interoceptive foundation.** A Frontiers in Neuroscience (2026) paper argues co-regulation through maternal affective touch is the developmental precursor to interoceptive accuracy. → `urn:srl:evidence:frontiers-2026-maternal-touch-interoception-development`
- **4M Framework for interoception.** PLOS Biology's Whole Person Health paper introduces Measure, Map, Monitor, Modulate — a framework strikingly parallel to SRL's methodology. → `urn:srl:evidence:plos-2025-interoception-whole-person-health-4m`
- **HRV biofeedback: three strong evidence additions.** A JAMA Psychiatry RCT showed 64% reduction in substance use with wearable HRV biofeedback; a meta-analysis of 13 RCTs confirmed autonomic improvements in cardiovascular patients; and a global coherence study mapped resonant frequency distributions and emotional state effects. → `urn:srl:evidence:jama-2025-hrv-biofeedback-substance-use-rct`, `urn:srl:evidence:springer-2026-hrv-biofeedback-cardiovascular-meta-analysis`, `urn:srl:evidence:nature-2025-hrv-coherence-frequencies-emotional-states`

## Clinician Performance

- **Burnout at 63%, costing $4.6B/year.** Mindfulness + peer support RCTs show 73.5% of participants reporting improved connections. AI scribes cut documentation time by 50%. → `urn:srl:observation:trend-clinician-burnout-ai-scribes-2026`
