# PauseLabs MVP Premiere: Friends & Family Focus Group Protocol

## Event Design

- **Setting**: Randy's home/neuroplasticity lab in Woodinville, WA — quiet exclusivity, warm backdrop
- **Participants**: 10–12 carefully curated Seattle-area CRNAs
- **Duration**: 90-minute structured session + 30-minute social time
- **Atmosphere**: Nosh, mocktails, wine/beer for after — intimate, pioneering
- **Feedback design**: Structured by Jason Fields (formerly Audible/Amazon)
- **Purpose**: Final prep feedback before broader CRNA community launch

## Session Structure (90 min)

1. **Welcome & Introduction** (10 min) — facilitator intro, app purpose, expectations
2. **App Overview** (10 min) — science-based physiological control, core features (breathing sessions, real-time HRV monitoring, 1-minute rapid reset, audio guidance, performance metrics)
3. **Guided App Exploration** (20 min) — device distribution, step-by-step walkthrough (login → biometrics integration → session library → first session completion → results review)
4. **Independent Usage** (15 min) — participants choose and complete second session; facilitators observe
5. **Focused Discussion** (25 min) — usability & design, experience & effectiveness, potential use cases
6. **Feature Feedback & Priority** (10 min) — voting exercise, suggestion collection
7. **Social Time** (30 min) — informal conversation, open exploration, one-on-one with dev team

## Key Research Dimensions

- **Usability**: navigation intuitiveness, instruction clarity, biometrics display, visual design
- **Experience**: felt physiological changes, audio guidance quality, session preference
- **Practical application**: when/where they'd use it, barriers to regular use, how they'd describe it to a friend
- **NPS**: 1–10 likelihood to recommend

## Enhancement Recommendations (from ChatGPT session)

- Rebrand from "Focus Group" to "The Quiet Launch" or "NeuroMinute Pioneer Session"
- Open with a collective 1-minute Neuro-Oroboros session (drops everyone into state before any slides)
- Color-code feedback forms: blue = usability, green = experience, gold = ideas
- Close with single-word prompt: "How do you feel now vs. when you arrived?"
- Subtle design touches: hand-written cards, warm socks, scent cues (eucalyptus/cedarwood)
