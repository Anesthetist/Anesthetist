# Somnistics Integrated Taxonomy (SIT): 6-Category Classification System

## Observation

The Somnistics Mind Map documents a 6-category taxonomy (SIT) and two core engines:

### Taxonomy Categories

- **NFR** — Neurophysiological Foundations & Regulation
- **CFG** — Cognitive-Behavioral & Flow-State Generation
- **MRS** — Mindfulness, Resilience & Self-Regulation
- **AII** — AI-Driven Interventions & Integration
- **PCA** — Performance, Clinical & Audience Applications
- **ETP** — Enterprise, Training & Partnership Programs

### Two Core Engines

- **PAS-ME** (Predictive Autonomic State Modeling Engine) — classifies autonomic state in real-time (5 states: calm, focused_flow, cognitive_strain, acute_stress, recovery)
- **PIE-Engine** (Personalized Intervention Engine) — selects and delivers the appropriate intervention based on PAS-ME classification

### Patent Status

Patent filed April 3, 2025: "AI-Guided Neuroadaptive Biometric Feedback System" — IP now public.

### Revenue Architecture

$100K target = 1,000 CRNAs × $100 (Class B CE). Funnel: CE lecture (Class A, lead gen) → contact follow-up → Pausality subscription ($9.99/mo) → enterprise pilot.

## Significance

SIT provides the classification backbone for the entire vault — every concept, evidence note, and observation should eventually be tagged with its SIT category. This taxonomy is the bridge between the vault's SKOS/Dublin Core metadata and Pausality's product architecture.
