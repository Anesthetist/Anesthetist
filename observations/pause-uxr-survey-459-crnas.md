# PAUSE UXR Survey: 459 CRNAs Validate Gap Moment Training Architecture

## Survey Overview

459 respondents, November 13, 2024. Primary audience: healthcare professionals (95.7% CRNA/Anesthesiologists). This is the largest known survey of CRNAs specifically about nervous system regulation tools.

## Demographics

- **95.7%** CRNA/Anesthesiologists — nearly pure clinical population
- **75.4%** aged 35-54 (combined) — mid-career, Phase 2 (Healing) of the [[gmt-career-arc]]
- **38.4%** report "high-stress" lifestyle
- **89.1%** iPhone users, **49.8%** Apple Watch — strong iOS ecosystem presence
- **69.7%** don't use ANY mindfulness apps — massive untapped market
- **66.6%** have NEVER paid for a wellness app

## Validation of SRL Architecture

Every major SRL design decision is validated by this data:

### 1. The 60-Second Format Is What They Want

> **72.9% want quick 1-minute mindfulness exercises**

This isn't SRL imposing a format. This is 459 CRNAs telling you what they need. The [[neurominute]] 60-second format matches market demand precisely.

> **72.8% only have 1-10 minutes available daily**

The [[minimum-effective-dose]] principle isn't a theoretical preference. It's a workflow constraint imposed by the clinical environment.

### 2. No One Is Building For Them

> **55.8% cite lack of profession-specific solutions as a market gap**

More than half explicitly say nobody is building for healthcare workers. The [[competitive-moat-latency]] observation is confirmed: Calm and Headspace built for bedrooms, not operating rooms.

> **69.7% don't use any mindfulness apps**

This isn't app fatigue. This is an unserved market. They HAVEN'T tried and abandoned competitors — they've never found anything worth trying. The market is greenfield.

### 3. They Want Measurement, Not Meditation

> **85.2% want personalized insights from biometric data**

The single highest-rated feature. Not guided meditation. Not ambient music. Personalized biometric insights. This validates [[neurogating]], [[readyscore]], and the entire Apple Watch integration thesis.

> **52.4% want measurable results and time-efficient exercises**

Measurable results. Not "feel calmer." Measurement. This is a clinical population that trusts what they can quantify. The MAIA-2 outcome battery and HRV delta tracking ARE what they're asking for.

### 4. They Don't Want Community

> **59.1% not interested in community features**

They don't want a social network. They don't want peer leaderboards. They want a precision tool they can use alone between cases. This validates the solo-practitioner design of Pausality — no social features, no group challenges, no community feed.

### 5. Pricing: One-Time > Subscription

> **70% prefer one-time purchase over subscription**
> **61.2% prefer under $5/month if subscription**
> **66.6% have never paid for a wellness app**

This creates a strategic tension: the CE certification model ($100 for 3.0 credits) may be more palatable than a subscription because it's a professional expense with a credential attached, not a "wellness app subscription." The [[somnistics-certification]] framing ($100 for SCP-Foundation including CE credits) bypasses the subscription resistance entirely.

### 6. They Want Evidence, Not Affirmation

> Users want "evidence-based approaches with measurable outcomes"
> "Strong emphasis on practical, profession-relevant solutions"
> "Desire for tangible stress reduction metrics"

The 68 evidence notes in the vault, the MAIA-2 outcome battery, the HRV recovery metrics — all built before seeing this data. The vault's evidence architecture IS what 459 CRNAs are asking for.

## Strategic Recommendations (From the Survey)

1. **Healthcare-first design** — build specifically for medical professional use cases (done: the entire vault is CRNA-centered)
2. **Quick-access features** — one-handed, quick-launch, "emergency" stress tools (done: 60-second NeuroMinute format)
3. **Leverage iOS ecosystem** — Apple Watch + iPhone integration (done: Apple-first architecture)
4. **Rethink traditional wellness model** — not meditation-only; practical, measurable, hybrid pricing with professional credentials (done: CE certification model)

## Key Insight

**459 CRNAs described exactly the product SRL is building — before they knew it existed.** The vault architecture, the 60-second format, the biometric measurement, the evidence-based framing, the iOS integration, the clinical specificity, the certification model — all independently validated by the largest known CRNA survey on this topic.

The market isn't just ready. The market is waiting.
