# Mythos x Expertise x Scale — SRL Strategy Triangle

Randy's triangulated strategy framework, developed August 2025, synthesizing three intellectual lineages into a single operating spine for Somnistics Research Labs.

## The Triangle

| Vertex | Source | SRL Function |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Mythos** | Tom Morgan / The Leading Edge | Narrative positioning — Gap Moment Training as the new literacy. Story drives adoption. |
| **Expertise** | Cedric Chin / Commoncog | Operational depth — tacit CRNA knowledge codified into NeuroMinutes. Expertise is the moat. |
| **Scale** | Salim Ismail / ExO | Scaling architecture — leveraged assets (Apple Watch), community (CRNAs), algorithms (adaptive biometrics). |

## How The Vertices Reinforce Each Other

- **Mythos ↔ Expertise:** Story without substance is hollow. Expertise without story doesn't spread. The CRNA narrative ("40,000 hours watching life hang in the balance") makes the expertise memorable and shareable.
- **Expertise ↔ Scale:** Codified tacit knowledge becomes productizable. The vault → NeuroMinute → Apple Watch pipeline turns one clinician's career into infinite delivery units.
- **Scale ↔ Mythos:** Community amplifies narrative. Each CRNA who adopts GMT becomes a storyteller. Network effects compound the mythos.

## Cross-Reference to 7 Powers

The triangle maps to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers:

| Power | Triangle Vertex | SRL Manifestation |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Scale Economies | Scale | Apple ecosystem, low marginal cost per user |
| Network Economies | Scale + Mythos | CRNA community → family → cultural spread |
| Counter-Positioning | Expertise | Autonomy vs. dependency — competitors can't copy without undermining their gamification model |
| Switching Costs | Scale | Biometric history + personalized rituals |
| Branding | Mythos | Gap Moment Training = new literacy |
| Cornered Resource | Expertise | Randy's 28-year CRNA practice + vault knowledge graph |
| Process Power | Expertise + Scale | NeuroMinute pipeline (tacit → codified → delivered → measured) |
