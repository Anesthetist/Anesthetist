Saffron Sage Living blog, 2026. "The HRV Benchmark: Why the Best CEOs are Training Their Nervous Systems for 2026."

## Summary

A growing trend piece documenting that high-performance executives and CEOs are intentionally training vagal tone and HRV as a competitive performance edge. Resonant frequency breathing (approximately 5.5s inhale/5.5s exhale) is highlighted as a go-to tool — used even for 5-minute pre-meeting regulation. HRV is being tracked longitudinally as a benchmark of nervous system fitness, not just a momentary readout.
