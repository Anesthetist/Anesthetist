# Randy Graybeal's Quantified Clinical Experience

## Observation

The clinical foundation underpinning somnistics is quantifiable:

- **28 years** anesthesia experience
- **3,000+ epidurals** performed
- **Thousands of intubations** across trauma, pediatrics, organ transplants
- **5 years** cardiothoracic ICU nursing (pre-anesthesia)
- **Chief CRNA** — team management, recruiting, complex scenario training
- **10 years** Anesthesia Quality Review Committee (equal vote with anesthesiologists)
- **Washington Association of Nurse Anesthetists** leadership roles
- **Mobile anesthesia practice** — pediatric + adult
- **5 years** studying performance physiology, breathwork, and biofeedback
- **AANA Category A CE** approval for Pausality (rare for app-based training)

## Significance

This is not a wellness founder who read some papers. This is a practitioner with 28 years of direct, high-stakes clinical experience who observed the patterns that somnistics addresses firsthand. The clinical depth is the moat — it cannot be replicated by technology companies entering the space.

> "Code is cheap. Interfaces are fluid. Clinical wisdom — knowing exactly what a human nervous system needs 4 minutes after a Code Blue — is the rarest asset on earth."
