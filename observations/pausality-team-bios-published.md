# Pausality Team Bios: Published About Page (March 2026)

## Source

About page at pausality.health/about.html, crawled March 19, 2026.

## Randy Graybeal, CRNA -- Co-Founder and CEO

Published bio: Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist with over 20 years of experience in anesthesia and perioperative care across trauma, pediatrics, and organ transplantation. Deep expertise in high-performance physiology and psychology. Co-founder of Somnistics Research Labs, developing neuroscience-driven tools for emotional regulation, flow state optimization, and nervous system resilience. Pioneering one-minute interventions that integrate biofeedback, breathwork, and somatic awareness for professionals under pressure.

**Discrepancy:** Site says "over 20 years" but vault, proposals, and Randy's own statements consistently reference "28 years of clinical experience." The site bio also does not include "MSN" credential which appears in other materials. Recommend updating to: "Randy Graybeal, MSN, CRNA -- over 28 years of clinical experience."

## Jason Fields -- Co-Founder and Head of Product

Published bio: Product and UX strategist with 25+ years of experience designing at the intersection of wellness, technology, and human experience. Spans immersive media, emerging technologies, and social impact initiatives. Bridges design innovation with behavioral science. Led award-winning experiences across mobile, web, interactive TV, and IoT platforms. International perspective from living and working across the US and UK.

## Brand Implications

The team page establishes the clinician-designer dual credibility: Randy brings clinical authority and lived experience, Jason brings product design and behavioral science. This pairing is important for both investor and enterprise audiences -- it signals the product is not just clinically sound but also well-designed.
