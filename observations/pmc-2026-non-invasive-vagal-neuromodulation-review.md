## Source

PMC / peer-reviewed review article, 2025. "Harnessing non-invasive vagal neuromodulation: HRV biofeedback and SSP for cardiovascular and autonomic regulation."

## Summary

A 2025 review article formally frames HRV biofeedback and the Safe Sound Protocol (SSP) as complementary non-invasive vagal neuromodulation tools for cardiovascular and autonomic regulation. The review synthesizes evidence for both approaches and positions them as accessible clinical-grade interventions for improving vagal tone without pharmacological or surgical intervention.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The explicit pairing of HRV biofeedback with the Safe Sound Protocol (a Polyvagal Theory–derived auditory intervention) in a peer-reviewed review is notable. It signals growing clinical recognition of non-invasive vagal modulation as a legitimate therapeutic category. SRL's NeuroHarmonics concept — using resonant auditory/vibratory stimuli alongside breathing to shift autonomic state — is well-positioned within this emerging clinical category.
