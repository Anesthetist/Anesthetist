## Positioning Insight: Create the Category

Derived from applying April Dunford's positioning framework (Lenny's Podcast) to WANA deck competitive landscape.

### The Problem
SRL currently sits in an ambiguous competitive frame:
- Against consumer wellness (Headspace/Calm): SRL looks overbuilt and clinical
- Against EAPs: SRL looks too technology-forward and individual-focused
- Against generic HR wellness: SRL looks expensive for what appears similar

### The Solution: Category Creation
**"Clinical Durability Platform"** — a new category that:
1. Signals clinical rigor (not consumer fluff)
2. Frames the outcome as durability (economic language enterprise buyers understand)
3. Implies infrastructure (platform, not app — ongoing, not one-time)
4. Has no incumbent (no price anchor, no feature comparison trap)

### Competitive Isolation
| Category | Players | SRL Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Wellness | Headspace, Calm | Neuroscience-backed, clinician-specific, measurable outcomes (MAIA-2) |
| EAP | ComPsych, Lyra | Proactive training vs. reactive counseling, embedded in workflow |
| HR Wellness | Virgin Pulse, Wellable | Clinical depth, interoception science, dual clinical/business evidence |
| **Clinical Durability** | **SRL (alone)** | **Category creator advantage** |

### Dunford Framework Application
- **Best competitive alternative:** Generic mindfulness apps (what enterprise buyers would use if SRL didn't exist)
- **Unique attributes:** Interoception neuroscience, MAIA-2 measurement, CRNA-designed by 28-year clinician, HRV biofeedback integration
- **Value:** Measurable clinician retention improvement, reduced burnout costs
- **Target customers:** Health systems with >100 clinicians and existing retention problems
- **Market category:** Clinical Durability Platform (NEW)
