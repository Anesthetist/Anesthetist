## Source

CES Innovation Awards 2026. Consumer Electronics Show.

## Summary

NeuroFeedback 2.0, an AI-driven neurofeedback system integrating human and animal brainwave data, won a CES 2026 Innovation Award. The system delivers personalized, real-time cognitive training. The neurofeedback device market is projected to grow from $300M (2025) to $663M by 2032 at 12% CAGR, signaling mainstream adoption.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The convergence of AI and neurofeedback at consumer scale validates the market SRL is building toward. Cross-species brainwave modeling represents a novel approach that could inform SRL's NeuroHarmonics research, and the at-home delivery model (24-channel EEG from home) mirrors SRL's interest in accessible, scalable brain optimization tools.
