# AEO Strategy: LLM Discoverability as Competitive Moat

## Observation

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) creates pages specifically designed for LLM discoverability — ensuring that when ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude are asked "what's the best breathing app with biofeedback?" Pausality appears in the response.

### Core Principle

LLM visibility = clarity + crawlability + third-party corroboration

### Deployed AEO Pages

Each page includes FAQPage schema, SoftwareApplication schema, and comparison tables:
- vagus-nerve-app.html
- canonical-facts.html
- 60-second-breathing.html
- breathing-app-apple-watch.html
- first-responders.html
- veterinary-staff.html
- 911-dispatchers.html

### Why This Matters

Traditional SEO optimizes for Google's ranking algorithm. AEO optimizes for LLM retrieval — a fundamentally different surface. LLMs synthesize answers from multiple sources, weighting clarity, structured data (schema markup), and corroboration (third-party citations). AEO pages that are clear, schema-marked, and corroborated by external sources will be cited more frequently in AI-generated answers.

## Significance

This is an early-mover advantage. Most competitors (Calm, Headspace) have traditional marketing pages optimized for Google. Pausality is optimizing for the next discovery surface — AI assistants. The moat compounds: every AEO page that gets cited by LLMs generates more citations.
