# 40 Years of Zen — Competitive Analysis

**Source:** [40yearsofzen.com](https://40yearsofzen.com/), [LiveHelfi review](https://livehelfi.com/blogs/all/21-40-years-of-zen-in-seven-days-neurofeedback-training)
**Founder:** Dave Asprey (Bulletproof Coffee founder)
**Price:** ~$16,000 for 5-day intensive
**Cohort size:** 4 clients per session
**Technology:** 19-electrode 3D EEG neurofeedback, OpenBCI platform, 8,000 trainable variables

## What They Do

5-day intensive neurofeedback program:
- Brain mapping assessment (baseline qEEG)
- Pod-based neurofeedback training (bulk of program)
- Customized protocols targeting Alpha, Theta, Gamma, Delta based on individual map
- Private chef meals + supplementation
- Post-program webinar access with neuroscientists
- Claims: increased IQ, EQ, executive function, self-awareness

## Their Claims
- Brain continues producing "significant changes for 3-6 months" post-program
- Increased IQ and emotional intelligence
- Enhanced executive function and self-awareness
- Improved relationship dynamics

## Evidence Base
- **Zero peer-reviewed studies referenced**
- Claims rely entirely on testimonials
- No published outcome data
- No standardized instruments (no MAIA-2, no MBI, no PSS)
- No control group, no comparison condition

## What They Do Well
1. **Premium positioning** — $16K signals exclusivity and seriousness
2. **Individualized brain mapping** — 19-electrode qEEG is clinical-grade
3. **Intensive format** — 5 days of immersion produces state changes
4. **Dave Asprey's brand** — massive biohacking audience
5. **8,000 variable training** — granular protocol customization

## SRL Differentiation

| Dimension | 40 Years of Zen | SRL/Pausality |

| **Price** | $16,000 | Subscription (fraction) |

| **Evidence** | Zero published studies | 100+ PubMed references, evidence grading |
| **Accessibility** | Must travel, 4 people at a time | App-based, unlimited scale |
| **Population** | Biohackers, executives | CRNAs, clinicians, high-consequence performers |
| **Mechanism theory** | Neurofeedback (brain training) | Autonomic regulation + interoception + embodied metacognition |
| **Post-program** | Webinars, hope it sticks | Continuous biofeedback, daily practice, progressive overload |
| **Measurable outcomes** | Self-report only | HRV, MAIA-2, MBI, PSS, sleep staging, clinical performance |
| **Regulatory** | None | FDA general wellness, AANA CE pathway |

## Strategic Observations

### What SRL Can Learn
1. **The premium tier exists** — people pay $16K for brain optimization. SRL's enterprise pricing can be more aggressive.
2. **qEEG brain mapping** — could be offered as an optional premium assessment layer (e.g., via Muse + Mind Monitor or clinical EEG partnership)
3. **"40 Years of Zen in 5 days"** — the acceleration narrative works. SRL's version: "Gap Moment mastery in 21 days, not 21 years."

### Where SRL Has Structural Advantages
1. **Evidence** — SRL has 100+ citations. Asprey has zero.
2. **Scalability** — app vs. clinic. SRL can reach 100,000 users; Asprey can reach 800/year.
3. **Clinical credibility** — CRNA founder vs. coffee entrepreneur
4. **Ongoing practice** — SRL is daily; 40YOZ is a one-time event with no reinforcement protocol
5. **Biofeedback closed loop** — SRL adapts in real-time; 40YOZ is clinic-bound
