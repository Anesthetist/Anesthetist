## Five Breakthrough Insights from 462-Episode Cross-Reference

Analysis of WANA conference deck (17 slides) against Lenny's Podcast (269 episodes, 87 topics) and Pathless Path (193 episodes, 12 themes).

### 1. Category Creation, Not Competition
SRL is not a wellness app — it's a **Clinical Durability Platform**. April Dunford's positioning framework (Lenny's) applied: this separates SRL from consumer wellness (Headspace/Calm) and legacy EAPs. No incumbent, no price anchor working against us.

### 2. PLG-to-Enterprise Bridge = CE Accreditation
Individual CRNAs earning CE credits through SRL modules create bottom-up demand → enterprise contracts. Exact motion described in Lenny's PLG cluster (23 episodes). CE pathway is simultaneously credibility mechanism + revenue + user engagement + enterprise funnel.

### 3. Dual-Output Pilot Design
Every enterprise pilot produces BOTH clinical evidence (MAIA-2, HRV, burnout inventories) AND business metrics (engagement, retention, NPS, sick-day reduction). Two assets from one investment: publishable evidence for NIH SBIR + ROI proof for next enterprise sale.

### 4. "Interoceptive Intelligence" as Unified Language
Business = gut instinct. Contemplatives = presence. Clinicians = clinical intuition. SRL can own "interoceptive intelligence" — the trainable, measurable version. Cross-validated across Annie Duke (Lenny's decision-making), Andrew Taggart (Pathless Path meaning/spirituality), and WANA neuroscience (Craig/Chen/Khalsa).

### 5. Founder-Customer Identity = Moat
Brian Chesky pattern (Lenny's founder-mode): Randy IS the customer who discovered the gap. 28 years clinical experience can't be replicated. This is investor story, enterprise credibility, and clinician authenticity simultaneously. Validated by Pathless Path's "Leaving the Default Path" cluster (44 episodes).
