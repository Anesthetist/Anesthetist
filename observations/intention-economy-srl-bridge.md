# Intention Economy ↔ SRL Bridge: Consciousness as Engineerable Performance Variable

## Observation

Adam Curry's "Intention Economy" framework and Somnistics Research Labs are approaching the same fundamental insight from complementary directions: **consciousness is not just subjective experience — it is a measurable, trainable, engineerable force that affects physical and biological systems.** The convergence points suggest significant opportunities for cross-pollination, co-research, and commercial positioning.

## Convergence Map

| Intention Economy (Curry) | SRL / Somnistics (Graybeal) |
|---|---|
| Mind Lamp / REG-based intention measurement | Anterocept™ / HRV-based interoceptive measurement |
| Gamma coherence as consciousness signature | NeuroHarmonics / cross-frequency coupling training |
| Presentiment (pre-stimulus autonomic response) | Clinical intuition developed over 28 years of CRNA practice |
| "Strange attractors" — curiosity as future-pull | Gap Moment Training — engineering state transitions toward optimal states |
| Entangled app (collective consciousness) | Pausality platform (individual → organizational performance) |
| Consumer consciousness technology | Clinical/enterprise consciousness training |
| PEAR → IONS → GCP research lineage | Polyvagal → interoception → somnistics research lineage |

## Strategic Implications

### 1. Positioning Power
Curry frames consciousness technology as consumer tech (lamps, apps). SRL frames it as clinical and enterprise performance technology. These are **complementary market positions**, not competitors. Together they cover the full spectrum from personal curiosity to professional performance.

### 2. Credibility Bridge
The consciousness research community often lacks clinical credibility. SRL has it in abundance — 28 years of managing consciousness transitions in life-or-death settings. Conversely, SRL benefits from the Intention Economy's theoretical framework, which gives scientific language to phenomena clinicians observe but can't always articulate.

### 3. The "Entanglement Activation Threshold" Question
The MMMP's key research question — what determines when consciousness connects to or disconnects from physical systems? — is the question anesthesia answers pharmacologically every day. SRL is uniquely positioned to contribute empirical data to this question from the clinical side.

### 4. Attractor-Based Performance Model
Instead of the conventional "willpower + training = performance" model, both frameworks suggest: **create conditions where people are pulled toward optimal states** (Curry's strange attractors / SRL's gap moment engineering). This is a fundamentally different — and more sustainable — approach to reducing suffering and improving human performance.

### 5. Spiritual Entrepreneurship Model
Curry demonstrates what it looks like to commercialize consciousness research: rigorous research background → consumer technology → company (Entangled). SRL is following a parallel path: clinical expertise → training methodology → platform (Pausality). Curry's trajectory is a useful reference case.
