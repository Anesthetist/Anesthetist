# Phase 1: Expert Practitioner (March–November 2023)

**Cognitive mode:** Domain-anchored inquiry
**Kegan stage:** Late 3rd Order → Early 4th Order
**Dreyfus level:** Expert clinician, Novice entrepreneur
**Bloom level:** Remember / Understand / Apply (with flashes of Analyze)
**Conversations:** 133

## Defining Quote

> "Describe the anatomy and neurophysiology of holotropic breathwork with the understanding of a lifelong researcher on the topic to an audience of anesthesiologists."

## Signature Cognitive Move

**Audience calibration** — implicit theory of mind about how different people process information differently. Already thinking about translation before knowing it was a method. This is the seed of [[consilience-by-translation]], present before it had a name.

## Signal Behaviors

- Questions are direct, interrogative, anchored in professional identity
- Cross-domain analogies emerge naturally (mouth-breathing animals as lateral reasoning)
- Using AI as a sophisticated research assistant — domain expert extending reach
- The clinical expertise is fully formed; the entrepreneurial thinking is embryonic

## Developmental Significance

This phase establishes the baseline: a practitioner with 28 years of clinical expertise using AI to extend domain knowledge. The key tell is the audience specification — Randy doesn't just ask questions, he specifies who the answer is for. This audience-awareness becomes the foundation for the entire SRL communication architecture.
