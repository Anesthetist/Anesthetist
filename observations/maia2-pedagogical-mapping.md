# MAIA-2 Subscales Mapped to Somnistics Curriculum Progression

## Observation

The MAIA-2 (Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness) 8 subscales map directly onto the [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-session curriculum, providing a validated outcome measurement framework for interoceptive development:

### MAIA-2 Subscales

1. **Noticing** — Awareness of uncomfortable, comfortable, and neutral body sensations
2. **Not-Distracting** — Tendency NOT to ignore or distract from uncomfortable sensations
3. **Not-Worrying** — Tendency NOT to catastrophize body signals
4. **Attention Regulation** — Ability to sustain and control attention to body sensations
5. **Emotional Awareness** — Awareness of the connection between body sensations and emotional states
6. **Self-Regulation** — Ability to regulate distress by attending to body sensations
7. **Body Listening** — Active listening to the body for insight
8. **Trusting** — Experience of the body as safe and trustworthy

### Curriculum-to-MAIA Mapping

| Curriculum Week | MAIA-2 Targets | Anterocept Domain |
|----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Week 1 (Awareness) | Noticing + Attention Regulation | Domain 1 |
| Week 2 (Foundation) | Body Listening + Emotional Awareness | Domain 2 |
| Week 3 (Mastery) | Self-Regulation + Trusting | Domain 3 |

### Measurement Philosophy

Not just "do people feel calmer" but "do they PERCEIVE their body more accurately AND regulate better." The outcome battery (MAIA-2 + PSS-10 + BOLT score + HRV) captures different layers:
- MAIA-2 = interoceptive awareness (subjective accuracy)
- PSS-10 = perceived stress (psychological impact)
- BOLT = CO₂ tolerance (respiratory physiology)
- HRV = autonomic flexibility (physiological outcome)

## Significance

This mapping provides the evidence framework for AANA Category A CE accreditation and positions Pausality as a measurable clinical intervention, not a wellness app. Each curriculum week has pre/post MAIA-2 subscale targets that can be tested.
