# NeuroMinute as Three-Tradition Synthesis

## Observation

From a Somnistics Lab Session, Randy defined the NeuroMinute as a distillation and synthesis of three converging traditions:

### The Three Pillars

1. **Gurdjieffian Self-Remembering** — interrupting automaticity; the conscious shock that breaks mechanical behavior patterns
2. **McGilchristian Shift** — moving from analytic abstraction (left hemisphere dominance) to embodied presence (right hemisphere primacy)
3. **Modern Somatic Regulation** — breath-based autonomic modulation with biometric feedback

### Operational Definition

> "Delivered as a rapid, guided protocol, NeuroMinutes serve as actionable micro-practices — dense, potent, and reinforced by biometric feedback — that allow users to connect mind to body, intention to breath, concept to experience."

### Southwest Edition Framing (for CRNAs/SRNAs)

Targeted to U.S. Southwest clinicians with a direct, grounded, quietly powerful tone — acknowledging their clinical mindset, culture, and lifestyle context.

## Significance

This is the canonical "what is a NeuroMinute" statement — it's not just a breathing exercise, it's a 60-second intersection of three deep intellectual traditions made clinically actionable. This framing elevates NeuroMinutes above any competitor's "guided breathing" feature.
