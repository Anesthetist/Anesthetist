## The Finding

The JAMA literature on ambient AI scribes contains a mechanistic puzzle that the field has not yet read correctly. When resolved, it is a direct argument for the interoception/attention reframe of clinician distress.

- **JAMA Network Open (2024):** Burnout among clinicians adopting ambient AI scribes dropped from **51.9% → 38.8%**.
- **JAMA (2025 follow-up):** Documentation time saved was only **~16 minutes per shift**. After-hours EHR time did not meaningfully change.

These two findings are in tension under the obvious "time savings → burnout relief" interpretation. 16 minutes is not the difference between a burned-out clinician and a non-burned-out clinician.

## The Reframe

If the time delta is small but the burnout delta is large, the mechanism cannot be time. It must be **attention**. More precisely: *attentional coherence during the patient encounter*.

A clinician documenting during the encounter is continuously bifurcating attention between the patient and the chart. This is a chronic state of interoceptive and attentional incoherence — the clinician cannot fully inhabit the room. The ambient scribe's actual gift is not saved minutes, but *restored attentional coherence during the patient encounter*. The clinician is permitted to be a whole person in the room again.

That is an **interoceptive mechanism, not a scheduling one**, and every healthcare organization treating provider distress as a staffing/hours problem is aimed at the wrong variable.

## Why This Reframe Is Load-Bearing for SRL

1. It is a quantitative bridge between the burnout literature (which rarely engages interoception) and the interoception literature (which rarely engages burnout).
2. It implies that interventions aimed at attentional/interoceptive coherence — [[gap-moment-training]], [[neurominute]], structured breath practice — should move burnout *without* moving documentation time. That's a testable prediction.
3. It validates the reframe of interoceptive training as patient safety ([[sensor-calibration-as-patient-safety]]), because attention is patient-safety-adjacent in a way wellness is not.
4. It is an argument the JAMA data is making for us. We are not importing a wellness frame; we are reading their numbers correctly.

## Caveats

- The specific 51.9% → 38.8% and ~16 min figures are reported in the ambient-scribe evaluation literature as of early 2026 — exact citations to be verified before publication use.
- The attention-mechanism interpretation is the SRL synthesis, not a conclusion published verbatim in either paper.
