## Overview

These seven principles emerged from a strategic curriculum-design session and represent the core teachable laws of Somnistics. Each is framed as a falsifiable law with anchor phrases suitable for direct clinical teaching.

---

## Principle 1: State Precedes Skill

**Law:** No amount of technical skill compensates for a dysregulated nervous system.

**What it corrects:** Overtraining protocols, "just be more resilient," treating burnout as psychological weakness.

**Teach it by:** Showing identical clinicians performing the same task in different physiological states; let learners *feel* how state changes perception, timing, and tone.

**Anchor phrase:** *"Who you are physiologically determines what you can do cognitively."*

---

## Principle 2: Finesse Beats Force Under Pressure

**Law:** Force scales failure; finesse scales precision.

**Finesse means:** Minimum effective effort; reading the situation before acting; knowing when *not* to intervene.

**Teach it by:** Comparing novice vs. expert micro-behaviors; naming restraint as a skill, not a personality trait.

**Anchor phrase:** *"Expertise is not doing more — it's knowing what not to do."*

---

## Principle 3: Regulation Is Contagious

**Law:** The most regulated nervous system in the room sets the tone.

**Key reframe:** Presence is not empathy — it is *physiological leadership*.

**Teach it by:** Role-play with the same words delivered in different internal states; observe downstream effects on others.

**Anchor phrase:** *"You don't calm people by trying — you calm them by being regulated."*

---

## Principle 4: Burnout Is a Systems Failure, Not a Moral One

**Law:** Chronic dysregulation without recovery produces burnout — regardless of character.

**What it dismantles:** Shame-based wellness narratives, "if you were stronger…," individual blame for structural load.

**Teach it by:** Framing burnout as cumulative autonomic debt; showing how elite clinicians break when recovery is missing.

**Anchor phrase:** *"Burnout is what happens when excellence is unsupported."*

---

## Principle 5: Consciousness Can Be Trained Like a Skill

**Law:** Attention, awareness, and regulation are trainable capacities — not traits.

**The Somnistics unlock:** Consciousness regulation is not mystical — it's learnable, repeatable, and refinable.

**Teach it by:** Short, repeatable micro-practices with immediate felt feedback (not theory).

**Anchor phrase:** *"What you can notice, you can regulate. What you can regulate, you can transmit."*

---

## Principle 6: Minimum Effective Dose Wins in High-Load Environments

**Law:** Training must fit inside reality — or it will not be used.

**Why Somnistics uses 60-second units:** High-stakes clinicians don't have slack time; readiness must be rapid and reliable.

**Teach it by:** Designing interventions that work in chaos; testing under real clinical constraints.

**Anchor phrase:** *"If it can't be done under pressure, it doesn't count."*

---

## Principle 7: Healing Flows Outward from Regulation

**Law:** Healers heal best when they are not fighting themselves.

**Reframes the mission:** "Heal the Healers" is not self-care — it is *care infrastructure*.

**Teach it by:** Connecting self-regulation directly to patient outcomes; making the moral case without guilt.

**Anchor phrase:** *"Regulating yourself is not selfish — it's professional responsibility."*

---

## Meta-Principle (For Curriculum Designers)

*Somnistics is not teaching content. It is teaching how to arrive in a moment.*

That's why it scales. That's why it's defensible. That's why it matters.

## Pedagogical Sequence

1. Teach from embodied authority (lived examples, pattern recognition, not slides)
2. Train perception before action: always sequence Notice → Modulate → Act
3. Use micro-practices, not lectures (each principle experienced in under 2 minutes)
4. Name what experts already know — learners will recognize it without having had language for it
