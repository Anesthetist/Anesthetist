## Overview

This week's scan surfaces a particularly rich set of findings across HRV biofeedback (three major publications including an RCT and meta-analysis), a consequential polyvagal theory debate, breakthrough neuroimaging of jhana and nondual meditation states, and a novel circadian-resetting compound. The cross-cutting theme is **convergence**: wearable sensors, AI-driven analytics, and neuroscientific understanding are increasingly meeting at the point of real-time physiological state monitoring and intervention — precisely where SRL operates.

## Sleep & Recovery

- **Sleep consistency predicts cognitive performance** — Actigraphy study shows regularity of sleep timing (not just duration) is significantly associated with academic performance. Supports circadian alignment emphasis. → `urn:srl:evidence:sleep-consistency-academic-performance-2026`
- **SleepGPT foundation model** — Nature Communications paper introduces a GPT-based model that sets new benchmarks for sleep staging, pathology classification, and spindle detection. Potential technology partner for SRL sleep analytics. → `urn:srl:evidence:sleepgpt-foundation-model-sleep-decoding-2026`
- **Mic-628 circadian drug** — Novel compound cuts jet lag recovery nearly in half in preclinical study by targeting clock-control proteins. Relevant to shift-work clinician populations. → `urn:srl:evidence:mic-628-circadian-clock-drug-2026`
- **Circadian disruption and cancer/immune risk** — Oncogene study links clock misalignment to mammary gland changes and immune suppression; 35% of Americans work irregular schedules. → `urn:srl:observation:circadian-disruption-cancer-immune-2026`

## Cognitive Performance

- **Meditation changes brain fluid dynamics** — MRI study finds focused attention reduces regurgitant CSF flow, making brain fluid movement more efficient and coherent. Novel biomarker for meditation effects. → `urn:srl:evidence:meditation-csf-fluid-dynamics-2026`
- **Flow state wearable detection** — Scientific Reports study demonstrates feasibility of detecting flow via wearable EEG, HRV, SpO2, and GSR. Alpha-theta dominance confirmed. → (already in vault: `urn:srl:evidence:flow-state-wearable-detection-2025`)
- **AI neurofeedback at CES 2026** — NeuroFeedback 2.0 wins CES Innovation Award; Sens.ai combines EEG + HRV + photobiomodulation in one device. Consumer neurofeedback reaches new maturity. → `urn:srl:observation:neurofeedback-ai-powered-2026-landscape`
- **Interoception-time consciousness model** — Frontiers paper proposes that interoceptive awareness and temporal orientation form a feedback loop grounding conscious experience. → `urn:srl:observation:interoception-time-consciousness-model-2026`

## Physical Performance

- **Recovery science shifts to nervous-system-first** — 2026 sports science consensus: sleep is foundational, then autonomic regulation, then targeted interventions. Mindfulness now included in elite recovery meta-analyses. → `urn:srl:observation:recovery-modalities-evidence-update-2026`
- **Cold exposure timing matters** — New evidence suggests frequent cold immediately after strength training may blunt adaptations. Dosing and timing are key. → `urn:srl:observation:biohacking-trends-smart-rings-cgm-2026`

## Cross-Cutting Themes

- **HRV Biofeedback triple-hit week**: (1) JAMA Network Open RCT on HRV biofeedback improving myocardial flow under mental stress; (2) RCT showing 64% reduction in substance use via wearable HRVB; (3) Meta-analysis of 13 RCTs (n=965) confirming HRVB improves SDNN and reduces blood pressure. → `urn:srl:evidence:hrv-biofeedback-mental-stress-myocardial-2026`, `urn:srl:evidence:hrv-biofeedback-substance-use-disorder-rct-2026`, `urn:srl:evidence:hrv-biofeedback-cardiovascular-meta-analysis-2026`
- **Polyvagal Theory under fire** — 39 researchers co-signed Grossman critique calling PVT "untenable"; Porges responded in same journal issue. SRL must navigate this carefully. → `urn:srl:evidence:porges-2026-polyvagal-theory-status-debate`
- **Interoception measurement advances** — New ELIA scale validated for emotion-linked interoceptive awareness across four studies. → `urn:srl:evidence:elia-scale-2026-emotion-linked-interoceptive-awareness`
- **Jhana and nondual awareness neuroimaging** — First group-level 7T fMRI study of jhana states reveals reproducible neural trajectories; separate preprint documents EEG signatures of nondual awareness. → `urn:srl:evidence:jhana-7t-fmri-consciousness-2025`, `urn:srl:evidence:nondual-awareness-brain-eeg-2025`

## Trending Conversations

- **Biohacking community**: Smart rings overtaking watches as preferred wearable; CGMs going mainstream for healthy individuals; cold exposure at "peak popularity" but nuance emerging about timing constraints. Reddit r/biohackers, r/Nootropics active on all topics.
- **Polyvagal debate**: Generating significant attention on trauma therapy and neuroscience subreddits, with clinicians and researchers divided on clinical utility vs. phylogenetic accuracy.
- **Wearable sensor maturity**: JMIR systematic review documents the breadth of wearable clinical applications now possible, including patch/tattoo form factors and AI-driven vital sign estimation.
