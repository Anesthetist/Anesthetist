# Joe Hudson — 'Presence Without Fixing' as Independent Convergence on NeuroMinute Phase 4

## Source

Joe Hudson (@FU_joehudson), X post, 2026-03-22:

> "Underrated life skill: Learning to love the part of you that feels the worst, without needing it to change. Without analyzing it, fixing it, or rushing through it. Simply staying in connection with it, knowing it will unfold. The same way you'd want someone to stay with you."

## Observation

Hudson is articulating the fourth phase of the NeuroMinute structure from a relational/emotional frame rather than a physiological one:

1. Acknowledge felt sense → "the part of you that feels the worst"
2. Invite Self into watcher's seat → "learning to love" (observer stance with warmth)
3. Differentiate interoceptive elements → implied in "without analyzing it" (he's naming the temptation to over-differentiate)
4. **Presence without fixing** → "simply staying in connection with it, knowing it will unfold"

The convergence is precise. What SRL operationalizes as a 60-second structured micro-intervention, Hudson describes as a life skill. Same destination, different entry points — one through breath and body data, the other through emotional self-relating.

## MAIA-2 Mapping

Hudson's framing maps directly to two MAIA-2 subscales:
- **Not-Worrying** — "without needing it to change, without analyzing it, fixing it, or rushing through it"
- **Trusting** — "knowing it will unfold"

These are the two subscales most people score lowest on. The capacity to stay with a body signal without needing to change it is the terminal skill in interoceptive literacy — and the hardest to train.

## SRL Implication

Hudson's language is accessible, relational, and avoids clinical framing entirely. This is a potential language bridge for audiences who resist "breathing exercises" or "body awareness training" but would resonate with "staying in connection with what you feel." The physiological mechanism is identical — the framing determines who walks through the door.
