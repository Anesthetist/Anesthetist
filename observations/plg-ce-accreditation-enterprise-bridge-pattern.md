## Pattern: CE Credits as PLG Motion

Derived from cross-referencing Lenny's Podcast PLG cluster (23 episodes) with SRL's CE accreditation pathway and enterprise sales goals.

### The Loop
1. Individual CRNA discovers SRL CE modules (organic search, conference exposure, peer referral)
2. CRNA completes modules → earns CE credits → experiences interoceptive awareness training
3. CRNA experiences personal benefit (MAIA-2 score improvement, reduced burnout symptoms)
4. CRNA recommends to department colleagues → peer cohort forms
5. Department wellness committee or CNO notices adoption → inquires about enterprise license
6. Enterprise pilot → measurable outcomes → expansion

### Why This Works
- **No enterprise sales needed for initial adoption** — CE requirement creates natural pull
- **Built-in engagement metric** — CE completion rates serve as both product metric and clinical evidence
- **Price anchoring** — Individual CE modules at $X establish value before enterprise pricing conversation
- **Evidence generation** — Aggregate individual user data (anonymized) becomes the enterprise sales deck

### Lenny's Validation
PLG episodes describe this exact motion at Slack, Dropbox, Figma, Notion — individual user adoption → team adoption → enterprise procurement. Healthcare's CE requirement is the equivalent of "the product spreads through daily work."

### Strategic Implication
Prioritize CE accreditation pathway BEFORE enterprise sales push. The CE modules become the top of the enterprise funnel.
