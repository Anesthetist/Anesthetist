# Pausality Evidence Ladder: N-of-1 → Prospective Cohort → Pragmatic RCT

## Observation

A three-stage evidence generation roadmap has been defined for Pausality, addressing the key gaps where Somnistics must create its own evidence:

### Evidence Gaps to Own

1. **Dose + time scale** — Most trials use 5-20 min sessions over weeks. Evidence for 1-minute burst *immediate* physiological shift is strong, but evidence for durable trait-level change from micro-doses is limited.
2. **Ecological validity** — Trials happen in controlled labs. Pausality operates in OR hold areas, between pediatric dental cases, during real-world high-pressure workflows.
3. **Population specificity** — Literature covers general adults/students. Pausality targets healthcare professionals with sleep debt, circadian disruption, and sustained sympathetic load.

### Three-Stage Ladder

**Stage 1: N-of-1 / Observational (In-App)**
- For every user/session: store context tag + protocol tag + ΔHR/ΔHRV
- After modest N: which protocols produce largest median ΔHRV for CRNAs vs other roles
- Time-of-day and shift effects
- Yields: data-driven recommendation engine + compelling anonymized plots

**Stage 2: Prospective Cohort (Single Unit)**
- 4-8 weeks with OR CRNAs or ICU RNs
- Measures: PSS-10, burnout scores, sleep quality at baseline + follow-up
- Usage patterns: number of 60-second pauses per shift
- Looking for: dose-response pattern (">3 pauses/shift → X reduction in perceived stress")

**Stage 3: Pragmatic RCT (Future Moat)**
- Cluster-randomized by unit or person
- Control: usual care + psychoeducation PDF
- Intervention: same + Pausality with minimal onboarding
- Primary: change in stress/burnout scores
- Secondary: absenteeism, self-reported error near-misses, HRV markers
- Even a small clean study becomes powerful enterprise sales asset

## Significance

This ladder addresses the core investor question: "Where's the evidence?" Each stage produces publishable outputs while the next stage builds. Stage 1 is happening now through in-app data collection. The ecological validity gap is actually the moat — proving it works in chaotic real-world conditions.
