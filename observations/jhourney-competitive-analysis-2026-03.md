# Jhourney Competitive Analysis

**Source:** [jhourney.io](https://www.jhourney.io/), [TIME Magazine profile](https://time.com/7007856/jhourney-meditation-jhanas-retreat-bliss/)
**Founded:** 2023 (pivoted from neurotech to retreats October 2023)
**Founders:** Stephen Zerfas (ex-Lyft SWE), Alex Gruver (ex-management consulting)
**Positioning:** "Jhana in days, not decades" — Silicon Valley engineering approach to deep meditation states

## What They Offer

- **6-day intensive retreats** — 6-8 hours daily meditation, 1-on-1 facilitator sessions
- **Online retreats** — 10-day work-compatible format
- **AI facilitator** — 24/7 available during retreat
- **Progressive curriculum** — "written and re-written like code to remove all bugs"
- 125+ pages of instructions, 14+ hours of guided meditations
- Alumni community + post-retreat facilitation

**Pricing:** $1,695 (online) to $4,000+ (in-person)

## Their Claims

- 60%+ of participants access jhana within a week (self-reported)
- 85% rate program 9/10 or 10/10
- 50-80% subjective reduction in daily suffering
- 400+ participants through 16 retreats as of TIME article
- "Less daily suffering, increased emotional awareness, less reactivity, improved relationships"

## Scientific Position

- Self-describe as "Engineers, not Dharma Teachers"
- Harvard/Mass General study found jhanas correlate with distinct neural patterns (attention, joy, equanimity)
- McGill study: deep jhana shows "more flexible and unpredictable" brain communication, increased creativity
- Early EEG research caught attention of top universities
- Pivoted FROM neurotech hardware TO retreat format (significant pivot)
- **Key paper:** Chowdhury et al. 2024 (PMID: 39681243) — multimodal neurophenomenology of jhana, fMRI + EEG

## Target Audience

Explicitly Silicon Valley tech/AI professionals: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepMind, Meta, Stripe, SpaceX, McKinsey, Bain. High-income knowledge workers seeking altered states.

## Criticism

- **Richard Davidson** (leading meditation neuroscientist): "Without real scientific evidence, we have no idea. Anyone trying to monetize this should raise red flags."
- **Tina Rasmussen** (traditional jhana teacher): Questions whether they teach authentic jhanas or "pleasurable states that fall short"
- **Vince Horn**: Accused them of "arrogantly endangering people's mental health in pursuit of capital gain"
- **Safety concern:** One participant with depression reported "very intense alarm" lasting a month. Others felt discouraged from speaking negatively.
- Buddhist scholars worry about dilution of sacred practices into disconnected self-help

## What They Do Well

1. **Democratization narrative** — making jhana accessible to non-monastics
2. **Engineering mindset** — systematic curriculum design, iterative improvement
3. **Community building** — alumni network, ongoing facilitation
4. **University research connections** — Harvard, McGill
5. **Speed claim** — jhana in days vs. traditional years/decades

## Strategic Observations

### What SRL Could Learn
1. **The jhana narrative is powerful** — "states accessible on demand" resonates with high-performers
2. **The pivot from neurotech to retreat is telling** — hardware is hard; curriculum + guidance scales better
3. **University research partnerships** — Harvard/McGill collaboration model worth studying
4. **AI facilitator concept** — 24/7 AI guidance during practice aligns with SRL's agent architecture

### Where SRL Has Structural Advantages
1. **Clinical population = clinical credibility** — CRNAs give SRL evidence authority Jhourney lacks
2. **60 seconds vs. 6 days** — radically different dose model; SRL fits in workflow, Jhourney requires life disruption
3. **Biofeedback closed loop** — SRL measures what happens; Jhourney relies on self-report
4. **Safety framework** — SRL has trauma-informed design, titration to effect, clinical expertise. Jhourney has adverse event reports.
5. **The ISH** — SRL has a testable hypothesis about WHY clinicians need this. Jhourney has "bliss."
6. **Neurotagging** — SRL tags every session with neurophysiological metadata. Jhourney has no data infrastructure.

### The Convergence Opportunity
Jhourney's jhana work and SRL's autonomic regulation work converge at a specific point: **the alpha-theta crossover and DMN voluntary control**. Jhana states involve profound DMN reorganization. SRL's nondual-clinical-awareness concept targets a more functional version of the same neural mechanism. Randy's vitarka-vicara-integration concept already bridges yogic attentional gears with EEG correlates.

The question: could SRL's progressive overload curriculum produce functional jhana-adjacent states in 21 days of 3-minute daily practice? The vault has `contemplative-progressive-overload` as the framework, and `alpha-theta-crossover` as the measurable biomarker. This would be a profoundly different claim than Jhourney's — not "bliss on demand" but "clinical-grade state access in operational windows."
