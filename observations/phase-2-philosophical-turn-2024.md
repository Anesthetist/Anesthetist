# Phase 2: Philosophical Turn (January–May 2024)

**Cognitive mode:** First-principles inquiry
**Kegan stage:** 4th Order consolidation
**Dreyfus level:** Competent entrepreneur
**Bloom level:** Analyze / Evaluate
**Conversations:** 33 (volume drops, depth explodes — 39 messages avg, 83K+ chars on single topics)

## Defining Quote

> "What reference is McGilchrist making when calling out the left hemisphere's blindness to its lack of perception of half the body as 'Berkeleyan idealism'?"

## Signature Cognitive Move

**Epistemological bridging** — connecting pranayama to respiratory physiology, Berkeley to neuroscience, Eastern kosha architecture to Western interoception research. Not eclecticism but construction of a novel epistemological position. This is the intellectual foundation that makes somnistics a discipline rather than a product.

## Signal Behaviors

- Volume drops dramatically (133 → 33) but depth per conversation explodes
- The question shifts from "what is X?" to "what is the philosophical principle underlying this clinical observation?"
- Active synthesis across domains that academia treats as separate
- The formation of [[consilience-by-translation]] as a conscious method

## Developmental Significance

This is the contraction phase of the first cognitive breathing cycle. The breadth of Phase 1 compresses into depth. The question type fundamentally changes — from "describe X" to "what principle unifies these observations?" This is the classic 4th Order move: constructing a self-authored framework rather than importing others' frameworks.
