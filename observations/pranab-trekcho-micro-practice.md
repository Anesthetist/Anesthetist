Once you develop capacity for trekcho ("resting in awareness"), deploy it as micro-hits throughout the day. The capability is there when stressed, but we forget to use it.

**SRL Relevance:** This micro-dosing of contemplative practice maps to Pausality's "pause" concept. The product opportunity is making the "micro hit" accessible to people without years of meditation training, using breathing as the entry point.
