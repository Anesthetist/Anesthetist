# Ze Maria Macedo — The Most Interesting Startups Are Building for Agents, Not Consumers

## Source
Ze Maria Macedo (@ZeMariaMacedo), 2026-04-09. [https://x.com/ZeMariaMacedo/status/2042234785494364339](https://x.com/ZeMariaMacedo/status/2042234785494364339)

## Key Insight
Argues that if you truly believe in AGI, the strategic question becomes: what does AGI need that it cannot give itself? The most interesting startups are building infrastructure, tools, and services for agents rather than for human consumers or businesses. This reframes the startup opportunity landscape around agent-native products.

## SRL Relevance
Strategic provocation for SRL's positioning. Anterocept could be framed not just as a consumer wellness app but as a clinical intelligence agent — an autonomous system that needs proprietary clinical data, CRNA expertise, and peer-reviewed evidence chains that AGI cannot self-generate. Randy's 28 years of CRNA clinical knowledge becomes an even more valuable moat when the buyer is an AI system rather than a human.

## Red Arrow
**Arrow 4: Enterprise Data → Clinical LLM Moat** — If the future buyer is agents, then proprietary clinical datasets and expert-validated knowledge graphs become the scarcest resource.
