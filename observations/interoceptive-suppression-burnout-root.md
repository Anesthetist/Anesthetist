# Medical Education Systematically Suppresses Interoception

## Observation

Medical and nursing education contains a "hidden curriculum" that deliberately teaches interoceptive suppression: ignore hunger, hold your bladder ("bladder of steel"), suppress fatigue, power through emotional distress, never show vulnerability. This systematic interoceptive down-weighting is the proximal biological mediator of clinician burnout — not just workload or moral injury.

### Mechanism

1. Training rewards suppression of body signals → reduced interoceptive sensibility
2. Reduced sensibility → altered interoceptive metacognition (can't tell how stressed you are)
3. Altered metacognition → autonomic inflexibility (reduced RMSSD, poor baroreflex sensitivity)
4. Autonomic inflexibility → increased vulnerability to burnout (MBI), perceived stress (PSS), trauma symptoms (PCL-5)

### The Irony

Clinicians are trained to be exquisitely sensitive to patients' physiological signals while becoming systematically blind to their own. This is [[interoceptive-blindness-clinicians]] at the institutional level — not individual failure but structural design.

## Significance

This reframes burnout from "workload management problem" to "biological consequence of interoceptive suppression." The [[anterocept]] training methodology is therefore not wellness supplementation but *reversal of training-induced damage*. It restores the interoceptive capacity that medical education deliberately erased.
