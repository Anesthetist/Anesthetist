### SRL connection
Khalsa's roadmap (2018) is the field's strategic document. Garfinkel's trichotomy gives SRL its measurement framework. Mehling's MAIA-2 is SRL's primary self-report instrument. Craig and Berntson provide the neuroanatomical foundation. **None of these researchers frame interoception as a "literacy" — a progressive, trainable skill set. That framing is SRL's.**

## 10. Cross-Domain Synthesis: Where SRL Sits

### What exists (and who owns it)
- **Measurement**: Garfinkel (accuracy), Mehling (MAIA-2), Maslach (MBI)
- **Theory**: Friston (active inference), Seth (interoceptive inference), Barrett (constructed emotion)
- **Neuroanatomy**: Craig (insular cortex), Berntson (circuits), Patapoutian (atlas, coming)
- **Clinical protocols**: Price (MABT), Levine (SE), Dana (polyvagal exercises) — all therapist-delivered
- **Hardware tracking**: Whoop, Oura, Muse — measure but don't train
- **Fixed-protocol biofeedback**: HeartMath, Lief — biofeedback but not adaptive
- **Breathwork popularization**: Huberman, Nestor, McKeown, Hof — demand creation without clinical rigor
- **Education/OT interoception**: Mahler — owns schools market, not clinical/enterprise

### What nobody owns (SRL's white space)
**AI-adaptive, closed-loop interoceptive training delivered as a scalable digital platform with clinical-grade evidence chains, purpose-built for high-stress professionals.**

No one combines:
1. Adaptive resonant frequency discovery (not fixed-pace)
2. Progressive interoceptive literacy framework (not just tracking)
3. Clinical credibility (CEU-accredited, evidence-linked)
4. Enterprise delivery model (in-shift, minimum effective dose)
5. The "Interoceptive Literacy" construct itself — a novel, SRL-original framing

### Advisory board targets
Based on this field map, the highest-value advisors for SRL would be:
- **Sahib Khalsa** — legitimizes the science (LIBR, NIH-funded)
- **Wolf Mehling** — MAIA-2 creator; validates measurement approach
- **Cynthia Price** — closest clinical analog; validates the digital translation
- **Tait Shanafelt** — Stanford CWO; enterprise credibility for clinician wellness
- **Kelly Mahler** — partnership for education market; 180K practitioner network
- **Amir Amedi** — haptic biofeedback innovation; validates tech approach
