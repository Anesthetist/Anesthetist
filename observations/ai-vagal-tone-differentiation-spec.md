# AI-Driven Ventral vs Dorsal Vagal Tone Differentiation

## Architecture Overview

Enhanced CRNA Resilience Training System incorporating Polyvagal HRV Differentiation into biometric tracking and AI-driven adaptation layers.

## Key Enhancements

1. **Polyvagal HRV Tracking**: Differentiates ventral vs dorsal vagal tone via RSA-based metrics and wearable integration
2. **Wearable Data Integration**: WHOOP, Oura, and Apple Watch for PPG-derived HRV tracking
3. **Autonomic Nervous System Differentiation**: AI models track HRV, respiration, and RSA suppression during stress events
4. **Closed-Loop Optimization**: AI dynamically adjusts resilience training modules based on vagal state

## Ventral vs Dorsal Differentiation Method

The AI system uses multiple signal streams to distinguish between ventral vagal engagement (social engagement, safety) and dorsal vagal activation (freeze, shutdown):

- RSA (Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia) as primary ventral vagal index
- RSA suppression patterns during stress as dorsal vagal marker
- Respiration-HRV coupling analysis
- Temporal dynamics of recovery after stressor offset

## Significance

This bridges clinical neurophysiology with real-time adaptive training, enabling the system to detect not just "low HRV" but *which branch* of the autonomic nervous system is dominating, and adapt the intervention accordingly. This directly addresses the measurement gap identified in the vault's `ventral-dorsal-measurement-gap` observation.
