# HRV Abandoned App Acquisition Strategy

## Origin Insight

In van der Kolk's 2014 book "The Body Keeps the Score," a footnote on page 415 mentions "there are 24 apps in the app store claiming to improve HRV." By end of 2024, that number exceeded 100.

## Strategy

Scout well-crafted HRV apps that haven't been updated in more than 4 years, identify abandoned apps, build a spreadsheet of last-update dates and developers, with the goal of purchasing an abandoned app.

## Rationale

- Acquire existing user bases, ratings, and App Store positioning
- Faster path to market than building from zero App Store history
- May include useful IP, design patterns, or data architectures
- Cost-effective acqui-hire or asset purchase opportunity

## Market Context

The explosion from 24 to 100+ HRV apps over a decade means significant churn; many early entrants have been abandoned by developers who moved on. These represent potential acquisition targets with established App Store presence.
