# EightOS Competitive Analysis

**Source:** https://8os.io/how-to-improve-hrv-quickly/ (January 2026)
**Founders:** Dmitry Paranyushkin, Koo Des
**Product:** SomaSync app (fractal breathing entrainer + HRV measurement), Ohm Lamp (resonant breathing device)

## Their Approach

EightOS positions as a "bodymind operating system" — Yin-Yang balance framework for autonomic regulation. Three breathing protocols:

1. **Resonant Breathing** — 5:5 ratio at ~0.1 Hz (same mechanism as SRL). 20 min/day for ~1 month for lasting effects.
2. **Deep Breathing** — 6-7s exhale, 4-5s inhale. Simpler, less effective.
3. **Fractal Breathing** — Novel: mimics natural living system dynamics with self-similar properties across time scales. 2-3 daily sessions × 2 minutes via SomaSync.

## What They Do That's Interesting

### Fractal Breathing
Rather than imposing a fixed rhythm (resonant frequency), fractal breathing trains variability patterns that mirror healthy biological systems. Based on the principle that healthy HRV has fractal (self-similar) structure — not just high magnitude. This is a genuinely different approach from fixed-rate pacing.

**SRL assessment:** Worth investigating. Could complement cardiac-anchored breathing as an advanced protocol layer. The literature on fractal HRV dynamics (Goldberger 2002, Ivanov 1999) supports the principle. However, no RCTs on "fractal breathing" as an intervention exist.

### DFA Alpha (Detrended Fluctuation Analysis)
They prioritize DFA alpha over raw HRV numbers. DFA alpha measures the fractal structure of heart rate time series:
- Alpha ~1.0 = healthy fractal correlations (long-range order)
- Alpha < 0.75 = more random (loss of fractal structure, associated with disease)
- Alpha > 1.5 = over-correlated (rigidity)

**SRL assessment:** DFA alpha is a legitimate metric used in cardiac research (Peng 1995, Goldberger 2002). It captures something RMSSD doesn't — the *structure* of variability, not just the *amount*. Could be valuable as an advanced metric in the Pausality app alongside RMSSD. Apple Watch raw data may not have sufficient resolution for reliable DFA calculation.

| Clinical population | Generic wellness | CRNA/high-stakes performance |
| Biofeedback loop | Open — app guides, doesn't adapt | Closed — adapts to real-time physiology |
| Dose optimization | "20 min/day for a month" | Minimum effective dose (60s, 3x/day) |

## Gaps in Their Approach
- No citations or evidence base presented
- No clinical population validation
- No interoceptive component — purely respiratory
- Fractal breathing is theoretically interesting but empirically untested as an intervention
- No team/co-regulation dimension
- "Yin-Yang" framing may limit credibility with clinical audiences

## What SRL Could Learn
1. **DFA alpha as a metric** — worth exploring as an advanced HRV measure
2. **Fractal breathing concept** — could inspire an advanced protocol layer after resonant frequency is mastered
3. **Their "balance, not maximization" framing** aligns with SRL's "regulation, not relaxation" — validates the positioning
