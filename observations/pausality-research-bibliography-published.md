# Pausality Published Research Bibliography: Science Page Citations (March 2026)

## Source

Science page at pausality.health/science.html, crawled March 19, 2026. These are the specific citations presented to the public as the evidence base for Pausality.

## Page Framing

"Somnistics is the applied science of breath-based physiological awareness. Our protocols are grounded in peer-reviewed research on interoception, breathing physiology, and biometric awareness."

The curriculum is described as 21 sessions targeting specific aspects of nervous system awareness, from foundational awareness to advanced techniques.

## Foundation Concept

Interoception defined as "the awareness of internal body signals that shape emotional and cognitive control." The page claims that better interoceptive awareness improves emotional awareness, decision-making under stress, and resilience to burnout.

Gap Moments: "Healthcare pros experience numerous transitions each day, optimal windows for a brief breathing reset."

## Published Citations

### 1. Breathing Fundamentals and Autonomic Balance
**Yuan, H., & Silberstein, S. D. (2016).** Vagus Nerve and Vagus Nerve Stimulation: A Comprehensive Review. *Headache*, 56(1), 71-78.
- Site framing: Controlled diaphragmatic breathing engages the parasympathetic nervous system through vagal pathway engagement

### 2. Stress Biomarkers and Slow Breathing
**Dillard, C. C., et al. (2023).** Slow Breathing Reduces Biomarkers of Stress in Response to a Virtual Reality Active Shooter Training Drill. *Healthcare*, 11(23), 2351.
- Site framing: Breathing at 5-6 breaths per minute optimizes HRV and autonomic balance through resonance

### 3. Interoceptive Accuracy and Body Awareness
**Ferentzi, E., Wilhelm, O., & Köteles, F. (2022).** What counts when heartbeats are counted? *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 26(10), 831-835.
- Site framing: Non-judgmental breath observation enhances body awareness through interoceptive accuracy development

### 4. Attention Training and Cognitive Performance
**Laborde, S., Allen, M. S., et al. (2022).** Effects of voluntary slow breathing on heart rate and heart rate variability: A systematic review and a meta-analysis. *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*.
- Site framing: Comprehensive meta-analysis of 223 studies on slow breathing and cardiovascular autonomic function

### 5. Exercise Neurobiology and Breathing Coordination
**Fincham, G. W., Strauss, C., Montero-Marin, J., & Cavanagh, K. (2023).** Effect of breathwork on stress and mental health: A meta-analysis of randomised-controlled trials. *Nature Scientific Reports*.
- Site framing: Meta-analysis of 12 RCTs (785 participants), significant effects on self-reported stress and mental health

### 6. Brief Structured Respiration
**Balban, M. Y., Neri, E., Kogon, M. M., et al. (2023).** Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. *Cell Reports Medicine*.
- Site framing: Stanford RCT showing 5 minutes of daily structured breathing (cyclic sighing) outperformed mindfulness meditation. "Directly validates Pausality's brief-session model."

### 7. Non-Invasive Vagal Stimulation
**Yuan, H., & Silberstein, S. D. (2016).** Vagus Nerve and Vagus Nerve Stimulation: Part II. *Headache*, 56(2), 259-266.
- Site framing: Nostril alternation and breathing techniques act as natural vagal supporters, enhancing decision-making capacity

## Gap Analysis: What's Cited vs. What's in the Vault

The Science page cites 7 studies (6 unique, Yuan cited twice). The SRL vault contains a much deeper evidence base. Notable gaps in the public bibliography:
- No Porges (polyvagal theory) citation despite FAQ referencing polyvagal theory
- No Zaccaro 2018 (slow breathing and autonomic function)
- No MAIA-2 validation studies (used in pilot proposals)
- No HRV-specific clinician studies (vault has multiple)
- No burnout-specific evidence (MBI, ProQOL literature)

These gaps represent opportunities to strengthen the Science page with vault evidence already cataloged.
