# Better at Regulating Patients Than Self

## Observation

> "I was better at regulating my patients' nervous systems than my own."

This is the founding insight of Pausality and the somnistics discipline. After 28 years of expertly managing patients' autonomic states — titrating medications to achieve precise hemodynamic targets, adjusting ventilator settings for optimal gas exchange, modulating depth of anesthesia in real-time — Randy recognized he had never applied this same precision to his own nervous system regulation.

> "The meditation apps kept telling me to 'let go.' I couldn't. My patient's blood pressure was crashing."

## The Irony

CRNAs are among the most sophisticated autonomic regulation experts in medicine. They:
- Monitor 5-10 physiological parameters simultaneously in patients
- Titrate interventions to measurable endpoints in real-time
- Manage autonomic state transitions dozens of times per shift

Yet they have zero training, zero tools, and zero protocols for applying this expertise to themselves. The existing wellness solutions (meditation apps, yoga classes, employee assistance programs) are generic, time-intensive, and disconnected from the clinical workflow.

## The Birth of Somnistics

This observation led directly to the core design principles:
- **60-second format** — fits between cases (not 20-minute meditation)
- **Biometric verification** — clinicians trust what they can measure
- **Clinical protocol structure** — not "feel good" affirmations but structured interventions
- **Workflow integration** — embedded in the gaps that already exist
