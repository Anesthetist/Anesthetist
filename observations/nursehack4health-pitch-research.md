# NurseHack4Health Pitch Research: Verified Burnout Statistics and HRV Biofeedback Validation

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

"Pausality transforms the 60-second gap moments between high-stress events into powerful nervous system resets through Gap Moment Training; biofeedback-guided NeuroMinutes that fit seamlessly into clinical workflows. Nurse-designed, science-backed, and CEU-accredited, we're addressing the burnout crisis one micro-intervention at a time."

## Verified Burnout Statistics (2024-2025)

- 62% of U.S. nurses report burnout (application data)
- 33.45% global prevalence of emotional exhaustion among nurses (2025 meta-analysis)
- 91.1% of nurses experienced high burnout post-COVID (2023 Greece study)
- 1 in 4 nurses plan to leave their jobs
- 16.4% national RN turnover rate in 2024 (NSI 2025 report)
- 50% of ICU staff show PTSD symptoms

## Financial Impact

- $9 billion annual cost of nurse turnover nationally
- $37,700-$58,400 cost per nurse replacement (2024-2025)
- $16,736 per nurse per year on burnout-attributed turnover costs (status quo)
- $11,592 per nurse per year WITH burnout reduction program (proven savings)
- $20,000-$31,000 lost PER DAY per hospital due to turnover (2025 study)

## Patient Safety Impact

- 10% rise in nurses intending to leave leads to 14% increase in patient deaths
- Direct correlation between nurse burnout and medical errors

## HRV Biofeedback Scientific Validation

Cited studies validating HRV biofeedback for stress reduction, autonomic regulation, and clinical performance improvement; specific to the NurseHack4Health hackathon pitch (September 2025).
