## Concept

A physical card deck product designed for families — 21-day progressive curriculum of 1-minute multisensory nervous system practices, optimized for mealtimes and parties. Conceived as a viral consumer product and recurring revenue vehicle alongside the clinical Pausality platform.

> *"Everyone enjoys just the pause together, one moment, just a neuro minute together. A family neuro minute."*

---

## Product Architecture

**Format:** 42 cards (21 Core Practice + 21 Level-Up variations)

Each card includes:
- QR code linking to 1-min guided audio/video
- Beautiful HRV-responsive visual effect
- Optional AR/holographic foil for delight

Accessories: "Pause Stand" table tent to prop card during meals

---

## Curriculum Structure: 3 Phases

| Phase | Theme | Goal | Example |
|-------|-------|------|---------|
| Week 1 — Anchor | Breath & presence | Establish shared regulation baseline | "5-Count Stomach Balloon" |
| Week 2 — Awaken | Interoception & attention | Train body awareness together | "Feel Your Fingertips Glow" |
| Week 3 — Amplify | Coherence & connection | Family nervous system synchrony | "Sync Hearts, Blink Together" |

Each phase builds one new sensory layer: breath → somatic → social → vision

---

## Key Design Principles

- **Intergenerational:** Great-grandparents to toddlers can participate
- **Progressive difficulty:** "Level Up" side for older children and adults
- **Status signal:** Positioned as "what top families do" / "what fighter pilots do"
- **Multisensory:** Binaural beats, haptic sync, visual loops
- **Party-friendly:** Ends each phase with a "Family Fusion" combo card

---

## Monetization Model

1. **Physical product:** $38–58 deck + Pause Stand + web access
2. **Subscription (MRR):**
   - Monthly drop of 3 new "challenge" cards + seasonal bonuses
   - Family dashboard with HRV sync data
   - Community submissions: "Our family's remix"
3. **B2B/B2E variant:** "Team NeuroMinute" for startups, corporate wellness, and elite groups

---

## Neuroscience Foundation

Cards covertly train:
- Heart-brain coherence (RSA and cardiac interoception)
- Insular cortex activation (body awareness)
- mPFC self-other regulation
- Polyvagal safety states and family nervous system synchrony
- Right hemisphere dominance and playful flow

Theoretical grounding: Siegel's "Wheel of Awareness," McGilchrist's hemispheric lateralization, Polyvagal Theory

---

## Strategic Fit

This product extends Somnistics' reach into the consumer market without diluting the clinical brand. The "status signal" positioning ("This is what top families do") creates cultural virality that generates awareness for the clinical Pausality product through social proof and demonstration.
