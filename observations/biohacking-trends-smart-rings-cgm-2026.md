## Source

University Magazine, 2026 biohacking trends analysis; Reddit r/biohackers community discussions.

## Summary

Smart rings have officially overtaken watches as the preferred biohacking wearable in 2026, providing lab-quality sleep, HRV, and SpO2 data without screen bulk. Continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) have moved firmly into the healthy consumer market for energy optimization. Cold exposure remains at peak popularity but new research suggests frequent cold immediately after strength training may blunt muscle adaptations. Nootropics remain central but increasingly debated on Reddit.
