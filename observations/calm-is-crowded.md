"Calm is crowded. Transition intelligence is nearly empty."

Randy's market positioning insight: every wellness app, meditation program, and stress management tool targets relaxation -- getting to calm. No one trains the moments BETWEEN states -- the transitions themselves.

## The Competitive Landscape

- **Crowded lane:** Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer, breathwork apps, meditation apps -- all sell relaxation as the destination
- **Empty lane:** State transition management -- detecting when you are still sympathetically loaded despite appearing calm, training the capacity to navigate autonomic transitions under pressure, building the skill of transition itself

## Why This Matters

The CRNA who just ran a Code Blue does not need to relax. They need to accurately assess their autonomic state, determine whether they are truly back to baseline or merely functionally competent, and manage the transition while continuing to provide care. This is not a relaxation problem. It is a transition intelligence problem.

## SRL Positioning

This observation anchors state-transition-intelligence as SRL's competitive category and distinguishes the entire product line from the wellness market.
