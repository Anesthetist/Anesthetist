# Revenue Arrow Functor: Intervention Unit → Delivery Channel → Revenue

## Observation

Category theory applied to monetization reveals a structural problem: tacit one-minute methods exist, but the morphisms from delivery channel to revenue don't. Three low-hanging revenue arrows identified:

### Three Revenue Arrows

1. **1:1 "NeuroMinute Diagnostic" Sessions** — $250-500/session, 60min, for high-stress operators. Personal assessment + protocol prescription.

2. **Paid Live Workshop for OR/ER Clinicians** — 90min Zoom, $97/seat, 40-seat target = $2-10K per workshop. "Inside-Out Performance" curriculum.

3. **"Red Zone Regulation" In-Service for Hospitals** — $3-5K per engagement, enterprise track. On-site training for clinical teams.

### Functor Mapping

Intervention_Unit → Delivery_Channel → Revenue

The functor must preserve structure: a 60-second NeuroMinute (intervention unit) delivered via workshop (channel) must produce the same autonomic shift as delivered via app. If the delivery channel breaks the intervention's mechanism, the morphism doesn't compose.

### Decision Framework

Team (Randy, Jason, Livio) needs to commit to ONE arrow for a 90-day test — speed-to-cash vs. long-term fit tradeoff. The arrow that generates revenue fastest validates the business model and funds the others.

## Significance

This reframes the business strategy question from "what should we build?" to "which morphism composes first?" The intervention units already exist. The revenue targets are defined. The missing piece is committing to a delivery channel and testing composition.
