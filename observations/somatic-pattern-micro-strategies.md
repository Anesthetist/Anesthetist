# Somatic Micro-Strategies: Jaw Clench Suppresses Grief, Eye Widening Maintains Vigilance

## Observation

Observable somatic micro-behaviors map to autonomic defensive strategies and avoided affects. These are not pathology but adaptive patterns that served survival — now available for conscious observation and choice.

### Randy's Somatic Pattern Map

| Micro-Behavior | Autonomic Strategy | Avoided Affect |
|----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Jaw clench/tension | Suppress emotional expression | Grief, vulnerability |
| Eye widening | Maintain vigilance/scanning | Surprise, overwhelm |
| Chin lift | Counteract inferiority signal | Helplessness, submission |
| Chest expansion | Project capability/strength | Smallness, inadequacy |
| Rapid speech | Fill silence, maintain control | Stillness, emptiness |

### Falsification Tests

- **Mirror test:** Release jaw → does emotional tone shift? (If yes, jaw was suppressing)
- **Voice test:** Speak from chest vs. throat → does authority quality change?
- **Partner feedback:** "Am I perceived as always composed?" (If yes, micro-strategies are working)

### Unclenching Protocols

Experiments targeting defensive patterns with empowering, non-pathologizing language. Not "fix your tension" but "what happens when you let the jaw soften?" — the somatic equivalent of [[titration-to-effect]].

## Significance

These micro-strategies are the body-level implementation of what [[tension-awareness-loops]] detect and release. They connect the abstract concept of "body holds trauma" to specific, observable, measurable patterns that [[anterocept]] training can address systematically.
