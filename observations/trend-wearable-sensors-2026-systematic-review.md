## Source

JMIR mHealth and uHealth (2026). Advancements in Wearable Sensor Technologies for Health Monitoring in Terms of Clinical Applications, Rehabilitation, and Disease Risk Assessment: Systematic Review.

## Summary

Comprehensive systematic review documenting the state of wearable sensors in 2026. Key advances include tear-based glucose sensors in smart contact lenses, sweat-based real-time detection of lactate and cortisol during physical activity, and saliva-based hormone and biomarker sensors. AI integration enables predictive injury models and personalized recovery protocols. Sports platforms now correlate physiological signals with on-field decisions using edge computing.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The expanding capability of non-invasive wearable biosensors creates new data streams for SRL's measurement approach. Real-time cortisol and lactate monitoring during training could provide objective markers for state transitions, autonomic regulation quality, and recovery optimization — extending beyond HRV alone.
