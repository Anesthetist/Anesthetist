# Glossary v1: Novel Operational Terms

## Observation

The Somnistics Research Labs Glossary v1 formally defines several novel operational terms not yet captured as standalone concepts:

### Coherence Anchors

Specific practices or stimuli (affirmations, tactile sensations, auditory cues) designed to evoke rapid neuro-cardiac-respiratory coherence. Not generic "grounding" but precisely calibrated triggers that produce measurable HRV coherence within seconds.

### Insular Activation Protocols

Exercises specifically targeting the insula to strengthen its role in integrating sensory, emotional, and interoceptive information. The insula is the hub; these protocols are the training regimen for that hub.

### Biofeedback Habit Loops

Using biofeedback to reinforce healthy neural and physiological habits during micro-regulation practices. Combines MAIA-2 pedagogy with dopaminergic reward systems — the user sees their HRV improve, which triggers reward, which reinforces practice.

### Trail Flow Awareness

Neuro-hiking practice synchronizing breath and movement while focusing on trail elements. Extends somnistics training from indoor/clinical settings into outdoor movement — parasympathetic enhancement via vagal activity + proprioceptive mapping in nature.

### Passive-Active Listening Loop

Auditory framework alternating between passive absorption of soundscapes and active engagement with breath cues. Creates flow-state dynamics within audio protocols — mirrors neural dynamics of flow (alternating DMN absorption + task-positive engagement).

### CO₂ Tolerance

Ability to handle higher CO₂ levels, improving respiratory efficiency and stress resilience. Trainable through progressive breath-hold protocols. Bridges to Wim Hof methodology but distinct in clinical application.

## Significance

These terms represent operational vocabulary that the product team needs — not high-level concepts but the working language of protocol design. They bridge the gap between concept architecture (the vault) and product engineering (Pausality/VagalBeats).
