## Source

Bio-IT World, February 2026; Oncogene journal publication; Pew Charitable Trusts circadian health report, March 2026.

## Summary

A study published in Oncogene found that internal clock misalignment changes mammary gland structure and weakens the immune system, with up to 35% of Americans working irregular schedules subject to circadian disruption. Separately, the Pew Charitable Trusts published a March 2026 report examining how circadian rhythms affect human health, highlighting immune defenses that rise and fall throughout the day.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Urgent relevance for SRL's clinician audience. CRNAs and other shift-working clinicians are disproportionately exposed to circadian disruption. This evidence strengthens the case for the CRNA Shift Architect concept and the need for chrono-intervention strategies as part of the Clinician Durability framework. The immune connection adds a new dimension beyond cognitive performance to SRL's circadian messaging.
