# Expertise Pipeline

## Consilience Observation

The developmental arc from novice to expert follows an isomorphic pipeline structure across clinical practice, product building, and entrepreneurial career design. The stages, failure modes, and breakthrough mechanisms are structurally identical — what changes is the domain-specific substrate, not the process architecture.

## Evidence Chains

### Domain 1: Clinical Mastery (SRL + Benner)
Patricia Benner's novice-to-expert model describes five stages of clinical skill acquisition: Novice → Advanced Beginner → Competent → Proficient → Expert. SRL's extension adds the crucial insight that the Proficient → Expert transition requires interoceptive development — the expert clinician doesn't just know more, they *feel* differently. Their body has become an instrument of clinical detection (anterocept).

**Key SRL data:** The 28/30 diaphragmatic blindness finding reveals that most clinicians stall at the Competent/Proficient boundary precisely because they lack the interoceptive substrate needed for the expert transition. GMT is designed to unlock this gate.

**Failure mode:** Clinicians who rely solely on cognitive/analytical skill accumulation plateau. The ones who develop embodied clinical intelligence break through.

### Domain 2: Product Craftsmanship (Lenny's Podcast Corpus)
Across 303 episodes, product leaders describe an expertise pipeline that mirrors Benner:

- **Novice PM:** Follows frameworks mechanically (PRDs, OKRs, user stories)
- **Advanced Beginner:** Recognizes patterns across products but can't yet prioritize
- **Competent:** Can run a product process reliably but misses non-obvious signals
- **Proficient:** Develops "product taste" — an intuitive sense for what will work before data confirms it
- **Expert:** Operates from embodied product intuition; can feel when something is off in a user flow the way a clinician feels when a patient is "about to turn"

**Key episodes:** Interviews with Shreyas Doshi (taste vs. execution), Julie Zhuo (design intuition development), and multiple founders describing the moment product sense became "automatic."

**Structural mapping:** "Product taste" IS interoceptive literacy applied to a different signal stream. The PM who "feels" that a feature is wrong is doing the same neural computation as the CRNA who "feels" that something is off with the patient.

### Domain 3: Entrepreneurial Pattern Recognition (Pathless Path Corpus)
Paul Millerd's exploration of non-traditional career paths reveals the same pipeline operating at the life-design level:

- **Novice:** Follows the default script (school → job → promotion)
- **Advanced Beginner:** Recognizes that the script doesn't fit but lacks alternative models
- **Competent:** Can design experiments (sabbaticals, side projects, lifestyle changes)
- **Proficient:** Develops pattern recognition across experiments — knows which opportunities to pursue
- **Expert:** Operates from embodied life wisdom; navigates uncertainty with the same confidence an expert clinician navigates a crisis

**Key insight from Pathless Path:** The guests who successfully navigated career transitions all describe a period of "learning to trust the body's signals" — eerily parallel to interoceptive literacy development.
