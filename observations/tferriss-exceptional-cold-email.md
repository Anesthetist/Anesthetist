## Source

## Key Insight
Tim Ferriss shares tactical advice on writing exceptional cold emails, featuring @MichelleKhare. Cold email remains one of the highest-leverage GTM channels for early-stage founders targeting enterprise buyers. The craft of cold outreach — conciseness, specificity, clear value proposition, and credibility signal — is especially critical when selling into conservative industries like healthcare.
