## Source

MDPI Clinical Sciences, 2025. Review article comparing breathwork techniques for chronic stress and mental health.

## Summary

Growing literature confirms that breathwork interventions significantly reduce self-reported stress, anxiety, and depressive symptoms. Cyclic sighing (exhale-focused breathing) produced greater improvement in mood (p < 0.05) and reduction in respiratory rate compared with mindfulness meditation in a month-long RCT. Slow-paced breathing at ~6 breaths/minute was particularly effective in promoting parasympathetic activity and increasing HRV. The review asks whether technique selection matters or whether any deliberate breathwork suffices.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Directly relevant to SRL's Resonant Breathing Frequency concept and the NeuroHarmonics framework. The finding that cyclic sighing outperformed mindfulness meditation supports SRL's emphasis on breath-based interventions as the primary entry point for autonomic regulation. Informs Minimum Effective Dose protocols — if basic slow breathing achieves most benefits, this validates SRL's accessible 5-Minute Miracle approach while leaving room for advanced resonant frequency training as a higher-dose option.
