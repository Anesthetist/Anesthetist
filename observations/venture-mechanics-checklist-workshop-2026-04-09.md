# Venture Mechanics — Venture Scale Business Plan Checklist

Live workshop at Bellevue College, April 9, 2026. Presented by Ron Wiener (founder/CEO Venture Mechanics, 40 years as tech startup CEO, 25 years as investor).

## Framework Summary (Sequoia-derived narrative order)

Ron's checklist sections, in order:

1. **What We Do** — 5-word test. PayPal, MailChimp, Canva examples. 6th grade level. "I've looked at 10 companies today — what do you do?"
2. **Problem** — B2B vs B2C vs B2G. Saving money or growing revenue? Luxury or want-based?
3. **Solution** — Unique insight from exclusive domain expertise. Founder-market fit. Innovator's dilemma awareness.
4. **Market Size** — Beachhead table: TAM/SAM/SOM mapped to rounds and timeframes (Pre-Seed → Seed → A → B).
5. **Customers** — Rigorous discovery documentation. Pipeline chart with impressive logos. Reference-worthy customers.
6. **Per Customer Economics** — Revenue model, willingness to pay, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio (3:1 good, 4:1+ great). Must grow 15-30% MoM for venture scale.
7. **GTM Strategy** — Force multipliers. Short sales cycles. Low CAC, high revenue per customer.
8. **Competition** — Two-axis positioning chart + Porter's Five Forces. Top 2-3 competitors known cold (funding, story, why you beat them).
9. **Technology Status** — How much R vs D remains? Ideal: all R done, IP forms barrier to fast-followers.
10. **Team** — Early placement (slide 3). Fractional is sensible. Advisory board creates momentum impression.
11. **Financials** — 5-year P&L forecast. KPIs, COGS, gross margins, renewal rates. Assumptions with ranges. Public company earnings call language.
12. **The Ask** — Round type, SAFE/convertible/priced, valuation, use of funds. Always reference the next round after this one. QSBS 1202 mention for angels.
13. **Comps & Exit** — Acquirer logos, revenue multiples, minimum revenue triggers. "Serve it on a silver platter."
14. **Why Now** — FOMO creation. Team, CAGR, regulatory tailwinds, customer ratings, acquirer interest, ROI projection. "Don't wait until it's too late."

## Key Ron-isms

- "Few words, lots of pictures. You want them listening to you, not reading."
- "Showing a little ankle — not the knee, not hello your honor."
- Team slide goes UP FRONT (slide 3) — answer "can this team pull it off?" immediately.
- "Arc — a movie script — carved in clay."
- "Crown is missing a jewel — we're a perfect fit. Acquire or compete — why won't they do that?"
- "You can afford to not have me — bid it up."
- Exit strategy = flight plan. Reverse-engineer the acquirer.
- "Know your numbers off the top of your head."
- Use Ron's name in the deck if offered.

## Venture Scale Definition (from slide)

| Factor | Lifestyle | Growth | Venture Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambition | Provide a living | Generate revenue, increasing profitability | Disrupt an industry or create a new market |
| Growth | Slow or steady | Rapid, at expense of work-life balance | Rapid, may prioritize investment over profitability |
| Funding | Self-funded, savings, loans | May seek outside investment | Relies on outside investment |
| Risk | Lower, built around owner's skills | Higher, cash flow/operational challenges | Highest, based on untested ideas/technologies |
| Exit | Ownership and control long-term | IPO or acquisition possible | Acquisition or IPO in reasonable timeframe is the goal |
