# Competitive Growth Intelligence: Pausality Go-to-Market

## Summary

Deep competitive analysis across B2C viral mechanics, B2B enterprise sales, and growth tactics from Calm, Headspace, Insight Timer, Breathwrk, Spring Health, Lyra Health, and others. Full research compiled March 2026.

## Top 5 B2C Growth Tactics (Ranked by Feasibility × Impact for Pausality)

### 1. Shareable Session Result Cards (Already shipping in v1.0.8)
- Spotify Wrapped drives 2.2M social mentions because it turns personal data into identity moments
- Pausality's biometric proof (HR drop, SpO2) is MORE shareable than meditation completion badges
- Format: 1080×1920px, dominant metric center ("↓12 BPM"), deep-link to 7-day trial
- Target: 8-12% share-to-install conversion rate

### 2. Referral Program (Dual-Sided, Product-Based Incentives)
- Headspace found NO-incentive referrals outperform paid ones (intrinsic motivation)
- Noom: 3333% sales growth partly through referral
- Structure: Referrer gets 1 week free per referral; new user gets 7-day trial (vs standard 3)
- Tiered: 1 referral = 1 week, 3 = 1 month + badge, 5+ = 3 months + early access
- Trigger at session #3 (retention drop-off point)

### 3. Short-Form Video: "Before/After Biometric" Format
- Wim Hof content: 1.3B+ combined TikTok views
- Position Pausality as "safe, science-backed version designed by clinical professional"
- Best format: Show Apple Watch HR at 92 → 3 min breathing → HR at 68. Caption: "What nervous system recovery looks like"
- 3-4 posts/week across TikTok + Reels + Shorts
- Organic CAC: $0.50-$1.50/install vs $3-5 paid

### 4. CRNA Founder-as-Influencer (Your Unique Moat)
- Healthcare professional content gets 8-12% CTR to app (vs 2% generic influencers)
- Series: "What a CRNA Knows About Breathing" (5-min YouTube shorts)
- LinkedIn: 2 clinical breakdowns/week (healthcare vertical: 18-25% engagement vs 3% general)
- App Store: First line of description should lead with 28-year CRNA credential

### 5. Healthcare Professional Referral Network
- 73% of health consumers engage with health influencers for medical advice
- Recruit therapists, RTs, cardiologists as Pausality advocates
- Give them 10 patient trial codes/month
- Track code redemption as demand biomarker

## Top 5 B2B Enterprise Tactics

### 1. Pilot-as-Research-Partnership Positioning
- Reframe $20K pilot as "clinical research partnership" not "wellness pilot"
- Health systems want to publish; offer co-authored case study rights
- Pilot → enterprise conversion: 60-80% in market

### 2. EAP Replacement Narrative
- Legacy EAPs: 3-5% utilization (93% non-usage crisis)
- Spring Health achieved 26% utilization at General Mills vs 1% legacy EAP
- Pausality message: "5x engagement, clinically measured, affordable"

### 3. CMO/Chief Wellness Officer Entry (Not HR)
- Clinical champions care about outcomes + research credibility
- Close pilot in 4-6 weeks vs 9-12 month RFP cycle
- Decision committee: typically 9+ stakeholders; clinical champion accelerates by 2-3 months

### 4. ROI Metrics That Close Deals
- Lead with: $4,129/employee annual burnout cost, $322B global cost
- Measure: PHQ-9 (depression), GAD-7 (anxiety), utilization %, absenteeism
- Benchmark: $2.39 returned per $1 spent (Modern Health); $4,138/member savings (Lyra)

### 5. Benefits Broker Channel
- Brokers are #1 contact for health systems seeking wellness solutions
- Offer 20% margin on pilot + enterprise deals
- 3-5 regional brokers = distribution layer to 50+ health systems

## Key Insight: Headspace's Counter-Intuitive Content Lock
- Tested locking 20% → 50% → 90% → 100% of content
- Each increase = double-digit lift in paid conversions
- Why: More paywalls = more familiarity with subscription model
- **Pausality implication:** Progressive unlock (Foundation → Awareness → Mastery) already does this naturally

## Key Insight: Insight Timer's Teacher Revenue Sharing
- 18,000 teachers each bringing existing audience
- 70% of new signups from word-of-mouth
- 25M users with $0 marketing spend
- **Pausality implication:** Future "Somnistics Certified Professional" network could replicate this model
