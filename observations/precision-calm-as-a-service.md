## Concept

Somnistics Research Labs frames its business model as "Precision Calm as a Service" — analogized to GE's engine-plus-flight-hours model for a Business Breakdowns-style investor/partner audience.

> *"If GE builds engines and monetizes the next 30 years of flight hours, Somnistics builds 'neural engines' for clinicians and monetizes the next 30 years of stress cycles."*

---

## The GE Analogy Mapped

| GE Component | Somnistics Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Jet engine | NeuroMinute protocol + Pausality app |
| Airframe OEM | Apple Watch / iPhone / clinical wearables |
| Flight hours | Daily gap moments (30–40/shift per CRNA) |
| Engine maintenance contract | Ongoing training, curriculum progression, biometric data |

**Key insight:** Somnistics doesn't build the hardware (the "airframe") — it builds the *engine that makes the hardware valuable* for stress performance. Apple Watch without a nervous system protocol is an activity tracker. With Somnistics, it becomes a precision regulation instrument.

---

## Business Breakdown Frame

**Episode Title:** *Somnistics Research Labs: Precision Calm as a Service*

- Nurse-led neuroscience company building precision tools for "gap moment" regulation — 60-second nervous-system resets for clinicians under extreme pressure
- Flagship product: Pausality, an Apple-ecosystem app pairing guided 60-second NeuroMinutes with real-time biometrics
- Long game: become the default operating layer for clinician regulation and resilience, starting with CRNAs, then expanding to all high-stakes operators

---

## Revenue Model Implications

- The "flight hours" = recurring engagement data at every gap moment (30–40/shift × ~250 clinical days/year per CRNA)
- **Scalability proof:** The NeuroMinute protocol is standardized and high-performance — like an engine, it works the same whether deployed by 1 or 10,000 clinicians
- **Expansion morphism:** From CRNAs → all high-stakes operators (ED nurses, pilots, first responders, military, elite athletes) without product dilution

This framing was developed in preparation for a meeting with Ramesh (March 2026) and serves as a way to communicate Somnistics' long-term monetization logic in the language of venture-scale investors who think in terms of durable recurring revenue, infrastructure lock-in, and category creation.
