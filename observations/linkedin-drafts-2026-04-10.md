# LinkedIn Drafts — 2026-04-10

## Scan Summary

HBR's February research has solidified into consensus: AI doesn't reduce work, it intensifies it — productivity gains of ~3% in time savings, with the freed hours refilled by expanded scope and intensified cognitive load. Tech layoffs crossed 165,000 with ~48% AI-attributed. A new generation of HRV wearables is pushing real-time stress notifications to consumers, raising the question of whether outsourcing interoception to a device trains the skill or atrophies it. And from yesterday's HN intel — ambient AI scribes are quietly narrowing the entire "clinician burnout" conversation to paperwork, leaving the somatic dimension unguarded. Today's physiology bridge runs through three underexplored domains: AI intensification as a nervous system event, wearable-as-outsourced-interoception, and DMN suppression during agentic flow.

## Posts Drafted

### Post A: The Hour AI Freed Up Didn't Free You
- **Domain:** AI + Embodiment
- **Audience:** tech / developers / executives
- **Hook:** The hour AI freed up? It didn't free you.
- **Bridge:** HBR's new research puts the AI time savings around 3%, but the nervous system has a rule: available capacity gets claimed. Automatically. Your shoulders don't distinguish "I freed up an hour" from "I have to fill an hour."
- **Reframe:** "AI didn't give you time. It gave the part of you that can't stop more room to run."

### Post B: Your Watch Buzzed. You Tapped It Away.
- **Domain:** Performance Physiology
- **Audience:** general / executives / tech
- **Hook:** Your watch buzzed. "Take a breath." You tapped it away and went back to the email.
- **Bridge:** Second-gen HRV wearables detect stress in real time and prompt you. But interoception works like any other sense — use it, it sharpens; outsource it, it atrophies. People are learning that their body is something a device tells them about.
- **Reframe:** "A wearable that tells you to breathe is not the same as knowing when to. One is information. The other is a skill."

### Post C: The Flow State You're Paying For
- **Domain:** Contemplative Neuroscience
- **Audience:** clinicians / tech / general
- **Hook:** Running five AI agents in parallel, you'll hit a state contemplatives train for 2,500 years to access. Then you'll pay for it for a week.
- **Bridge:** When you orchestrate multiple fast-moving tasks, the brain's narrator (default mode network) goes quiet. Buddhist traditions name this state and train for it — but always pair it with hours of return. Agentic coding doesn't.
- **Reframe:** "The brain does not give back what it doesn't get rested."
