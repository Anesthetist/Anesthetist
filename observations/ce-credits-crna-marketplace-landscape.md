# CRNA Category A CE Marketplace Landscape

## Overview

Deep dive into the comprehensive marketplace for Continuing Education credits (Category A) for CRNAs, focusing on competitive landscape, pricing strategies, most popular topics, and highest-revenue generators.

## Strategic Significance for SRL

Pausality's AANA Category A CE application (targeted Q2 2026) positions it to enter this marketplace. Understanding the competitive landscape, pricing models (subscription vs pay-per-course), and popular topics informs both pricing strategy and curriculum design.

## Key Considerations

- Category A credits are the most valuable CE tier for CRNAs
- Marketplace dominated by established players (AANA direct, Medscape, specialty-specific providers)
- Pricing models vary: subscription bundles vs individual course purchases
- Most popular topics cluster around pharmacology, regional anesthesia, and patient safety
- Pausality's differentiator: wellness/self-regulation is underrepresented in current CE offerings, creating white space

## Revenue Implications

CE credits provide a recurring revenue stream independent of app subscriptions. Enterprise contracts can bundle CE credits with platform access, increasing switching costs and LTV.
