# NeuroMinute Family Deck: 42-Card Product for Mealtime Co-Regulation

## Observation

A consumer product concept for monthly recurring revenue: a deck of cards delivering the 21-day NeuroMinute curriculum as family-friendly, multisensory 1-minute exercises.

### Product Format
- **42 Cards**: 21 Core Practice + 21 Level Up variations
- QR code on each → 1-min guided audio/video + HRV visual effect
- Premium print, optional AR/holographic foil
- Includes table tent "Pause Stand" to prop card during meals

### Deck Structure (3 Phases)

| Phase | Theme | Goal |
|-------|-------|------|
| Week 1: Sense | Noticing together | Build shared attention vocabulary |
| Week 2: Sync | Moving/breathing together | Group co-regulation |
| Week 3: Create | Inventing new practices | Family agency and play |

### Revenue Model
- Physical deck: $29-39 one-time
- Digital companion subscription: $4.99/mo (audio, tracking, family challenges)
- Gift/seasonal bundles
- School/therapy office licensing

### Viral Mechanism
- "That's what the fighter pilots do" social proof framing
- Party/mealtime social context = natural sharing moment
- Beautiful enough to leave on table as conversation starter
- AR/visual effects make practices Instagram-shareable

## Significance

This extends Pausality from B2B clinician focus into B2C family market — creating a second revenue stream while building brand awareness through organic/viral adoption. The co-regulation mechanism (families breathing together) is both scientifically grounded and emotionally compelling.
