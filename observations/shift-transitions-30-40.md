# 30-40 Gap Moments Per CRNA Shift

## Observation

In 28 years of clinical anesthesia practice across transplant, pediatric trauma, and cardiac cases, Randy has identified approximately 30-40 naturally occurring transitional moments per standard CRNA shift. These include:

- **Pre-induction pauses** — the seconds before initiating anesthesia
- **Inter-case transitions** — walking between ORs, waiting for room turnover
- **Post-critical recovery** — moments after an emergency or difficult intubation
- **Handoff windows** — transferring care to recovery or ICU
- **Administrative breaks** — charting, supply runs, hallway walks
- **Shift bookends** — arrival, departure, and meal transitions

## Significance

These transitions are currently "dead time" or windows for micro-dissociation (phone-scrolling, rumination, stress accumulation). [[gap-moment-training]] reclaims them as training reps — the insight that the training surface already exists in the workflow, requiring no additional time allocation.
