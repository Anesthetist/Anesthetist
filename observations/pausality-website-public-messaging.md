## FAQ Key Definitions

**What is Pausality?** A science-based stress management app for healthcare professionals using one-minute evidence-based breathing protocols with real-time biometric feedback during gap moments.
**Differentiator:** Real-time biometric feedback. Other apps provide audio guidance only; Pausality shows your nervous system responding in real-time.
**Gap Moments defined:** Brief transitions that occur naturally throughout the workday. Healthcare professionals experience 30-40 of these micro-transitions daily.
**Session length:** One minute each.
**Privacy:** Biometric data stays on device. Never sold to third parties. Only anonymized aggregate data collected.

## Brand Voice Characteristics

- Clinical credibility without clinical coldness
- Active voice, second person ("you," "your")
- Short declarative sentences for headlines
- Preparation framing over recovery/wellness framing
- Agency language: clinicians choose, not told what to do
- Metaphor: "play your biggest nerve like a violin"

## Discrepancies to Reconcile

- About page says Randy has "over 20 years of experience" but vault and proposals reference "28 years." Recommend updating site to 28 years.
- About page does not mention "somnistics" as the applied science; Science page does. Consider adding to About.
- Partners page does not mention pilot pricing, Health Innovation Fund alignment, or publication pathway. These are proposal-only messaging.
