## Source

Evolve Daily, 2026; Frontiers in Physiology network meta-analysis on elite soccer recovery, 2026.

## Summary

2026 sports science recovery has shifted from universal protocols to individualized, nervous-system-aware approaches. Sleep is positioned as the foundational recovery tool without which other methods provide limited benefit. Targeted mobility (15-30 min, focused on hips/thoracic spine/shoulders), active recovery (Zone 2), and mindfulness interventions are all gaining evidence. A network meta-analysis on elite soccer players compared active recovery, blood flow restriction, cold-water immersion, compression, foam rolling, mindfulness, and nutritional supplementation.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Validates SRL's hierarchy-of-needs approach to performance: sleep first, autonomic regulation second, then targeted interventions. The inclusion of mindfulness as a recovery modality in elite sports science meta-analyses legitimizes SRL's contemplative interventions for performance populations. The individualization trend aligns with SRL's Adaptive Intervention Protocol and Titration to Effect concepts.
