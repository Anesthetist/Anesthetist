## Source

InSight+ (Medical Journal of Australia), March 2026. "Polyvagal Theory debunked in comprehensive new study."

## Summary

Media coverage of the Grossman et al. (2026) critique has generated widespread discussion among clinicians who use polyvagal-informed approaches. Some experts argue the clinical utility of the framework persists even if the evolutionary neuroscience is revised — polyvagal theory gave therapists "biological language for autonomic states" that was previously missing. The debate is ongoing, with Porges publishing a detailed rebuttal in the same journal issue.

## Why It Matters for SRL

SRL must proactively develop a nuanced position on this debate. The organization already builds on polyvagal-adjacent concepts (autonomic regulation, vagal tone, state transition) but can differentiate by grounding its framework in empirically validated mechanisms (HRV biofeedback, resonant breathing, interoceptive training) rather than relying solely on PVT's evolutionary narrative. This is an opportunity to position SRL as scientifically rigorous while preserving clinically useful constructs.
