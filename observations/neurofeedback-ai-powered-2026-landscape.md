## Source

CES 2026 Innovation Awards; Myndlift 2026 Guide; Divergence Neuro blog. March 2026.

## Summary

The neurofeedback consumer market has matured significantly in 2026. CES Innovation Awards recognized NeuroFeedback 2.0, an AI-driven system integrating human and animal brainwave data for personalized cognitive training via federated learning. Top devices (Sens.ai, Myndlift, Muse, Neurosity) now combine EEG, HRV, and photobiomodulation. Remote/hybrid delivery is becoming standard, with wearable EEG and cloud platforms enabling at-home professional neurofeedback.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The convergence of EEG + HRV + photobiomodulation in single devices (e.g., Sens.ai) mirrors SRL's Multimodal Sensor Fusion concept. The shift to AI-adaptive protocols validates SRL's Neuroadaptive Training System vision. As consumer neurofeedback becomes mainstream, SRL/Pausality has a window to differentiate through clinical-grade interoceptive training protocols that go beyond simple focus/calm training.
