## Source

Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience, 2025. "Polyvagal theory: a journey from physiological observation to neural innervation and clinical insight."

## Summary

A constructive 2025 review that addresses methodological critiques of Polyvagal Theory (including anatomical specificity and RSA interpretation), while documenting its broad clinical applications across trauma, chronic pain, autism, developmental disorders, and mood disorders. Frames PVT as a clinically generative framework — whatever its precise neuroanatomical accuracy — that has catalyzed important clinical innovation.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Provides the balanced counterpoint to the Grossman critique: the clinical utility of PVT-derived approaches does not depend on its anatomical claims being perfectly correct. SRL can cite this review to reinforce that autonomic-regulation-based clinical interventions have a robust evidence base that transcends the current debate. Useful for enterprise communications to healthcare systems who may encounter the "debunked" headlines.
