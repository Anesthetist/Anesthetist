## Source

Published in *Neuroscience*, 2026. ScienceDirect.

## Summary

A new game-based cognitive training approach with EEG-based brain-computer interaction (BCI) demonstrated potential for enhancing attention in individuals with ADHD. EEG attention scores increased by an average of 15% (7.85% in controls, 21.5% in ADHD participants). Adaptive multi-task games yielded an additional 10% increase following personalization. This represents a convergence of neurofeedback, gamification, and personalized intervention design.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Relevant to SRL's Neuroadaptive Training System and Closed-Loop Biofeedback concepts. The personalization-dependent improvement (additional 10% with adaptation) supports SRL's emphasis on titration-to-effect and adaptive intervention protocols. The gamification approach could inform how SRL designs engaging neurofeedback experiences for clinician performance training.
