# CRNA Burnout Prevalence: 12-72% Range, 39% COVID Peak, 9/10 Want Peer Support

## Observation

The Living Scientific Synthesis on Interoceptive Awareness and Professional Resilience in Nurse Anesthetists documents:

- **Burnout prevalence range:** 12-72% across studies (variation reflects measurement instruments and populations)
- **COVID peak:** 39% of CRNAs reported burnout during pandemic
- **"Second victim" phenomenon:** Post-traumatic stress following adverse patient events — a recognized but poorly addressed clinical reality
- **Peer support demand:** 9 out of 10 CRNAs want formal peer support protocols — the demand exists, the supply doesn't
- **Professional resilience defined as:** Measurable capacity to withstand AND grow from stress (not just "bouncing back" but actual post-traumatic growth)
- **Decision-making under time pressure:** The synthesis positions interoceptive awareness as the mechanism for maintaining clinical judgment during split-second decisions

## Significance

These numbers are the enterprise sales deck. "$80-150K CRNA replacement cost × 39% burnout rate = massive ROI opportunity for a $100/seat intervention." The 9/10 peer support stat validates the market demand. The "second victim" framing connects to [[ptsd-prehabilitation-gap]] — SRL's intervention prevents the second victim phenomenon by building resilience before adverse events occur.
