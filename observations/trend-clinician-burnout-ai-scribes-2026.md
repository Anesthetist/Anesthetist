## Source

Multiple sources: AMA reports, MDPI meta-analysis, The Partners Group, PracticeSuite. 2025-2026.

## Summary

Nearly 63% of physicians report burnout, costing the US healthcare system $4.6B annually. A meta-analysis of 17 studies (15 RCTs) found that mindfulness, coaching, and peer support interventions improved patient experience scores and reduced depersonalization, with 73.5% reporting improved colleague/patient connections. AI scribes reduced documentation time by 50%. Flexible staffing models also showed burnout reduction.

## Why It Matters for SRL

This validates the massive unmet need SRL's clinician durability concept addresses.
