# NeuroMinute Tagline Refinement: Curse of Knowledge Problem

## Observation

External feedback (from branding advisor "Alex") revealed that Somnistics messaging suffers from the **curse of knowledge** — speaking from inside the lab rather than to the uninitiated.

### Three-Filter Test for Every Tagline Candidate

1. **Clarity** — a 14-year-old understands it
2. **Emotional payoff** — hints at the feeling after 60 seconds
3. **Believability** — no woo; grounded in observable change

### Key Insight
"Do I know about the app when I see that motto?" — a winning line must intrigue the uninitiated yet feel authentic once inside.

### Design Principle
Simplicity ≠ dumbing-down; it's surfacing the **core transformational promise** in everyday language.

## Significance

This is a critical marketing constraint: Pausality's science depth is its moat, but its messaging must be radically simple. Every piece of external communication needs to pass the three-filter test. This is especially important for scaling beyond the CRNA beachhead to broader professional audiences.
