# Patent Filed April 3, 2025: AI-Guided Neuroadaptive Biometric Feedback System

## Observation

Provisional patent filed April 3, 2025 covering the core SRL technology: an AI-guided system that integrates real-time biometric data (HRV, PWTT, facial microexpression, respiratory coherence) to deliver adaptive neurophysiological training interventions.

### Layered Claims Strategy

From the IP Protection Strategy (Q2 2025):
- **Core innovations covered:** NeuroGating™, VagalBeats, PolyAnchora™, Neuro-Oroboros™
- **Action items:** Consolidated provisional patent, freedom-to-operate analysis, global protection via PCT
- **Differentiator:** Privacy-preserving AI (on-device processing) as patent-worthy claim
- **Claims span:** Semantic biofeedback, multi-modal neurovascular synchronization, predictive adaptive feedback algorithms

### IP Asset Valuation

- $455K patents (estimated)
- $160K trademarks (estimated)
- Trade secrets: therapy metadata tagging schema, ML models, diaphragmatic literacy scoring

## Significance

The patent converts the Neuro-Ouroboros technical specification into legal protection. Combined with the trademark portfolio (11 trademarked concepts) and the trade secret ontology (this vault), SRL has a three-layer IP defense.
