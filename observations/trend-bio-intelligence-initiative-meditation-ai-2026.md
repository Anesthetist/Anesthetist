## Source

PR Newswire, March 2026. All Here initiative announcement.

## Summary

The Bio-Intelligence Initiative is positioned as the world's first research program exploring how deep meditative states of stability and silence give rise to measurable neural patterns, and how these complex neural signatures may inspire the development of biologically linked, energy-efficient, and adaptive AI systems. It bridges contemplative practice with computational neuroscience.

## Why It Matters for SRL

This represents a new frontier where meditation neuroscience meets AI development — a convergence that validates SRL's approach of treating consciousness states as measurable, trainable phenomena. The "quantified meditation" concept parallels SRL's NeuroHarmonics work, and the initiative could become a collaboration or cross-pollination opportunity.
