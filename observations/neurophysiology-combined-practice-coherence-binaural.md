# Neurophysiology of Combined Practice

## The Practice

Coherence breathing (5.5 breaths/min, equal inhale/exhale) + Polar H10 chest strap monitoring real-time HRV via EHRV app + binaural beats shifting from alpha (10 Hz) to theta (6 Hz) over a 10-minute session.

This is Randy's personal practice — the thing he actually does. This note maps what's happening across four physiological systems and their interactions.

**BioMistral synthesis (Qwen3-32B, 2026-03-22):** *3 queries. All citations require independent PubMed verification.*

## Four Systems Operating Simultaneously

### System 1: Respiratory (0.1 Hz)
- Pre-Bötzinger complex paces at 5.5 breaths/min
- Diaphragmatic excursion increases, tidal volume rises to ~700-800 mL
- CO₂ slightly retained, chemoreceptor modulation
- The breath becomes the clock for everything else

### System 2: Cardiovascular (Baroreflex Resonance)
- 0.1 Hz breathing matches the baroreflex's natural resonance frequency
- Arterial baroreceptors → nucleus tractus solitarius → dorsal motor nucleus of vagus → sinoatrial node
- Maximum HRV oscillation amplitude at this frequency — the heart swings widest
- Polar H10 measures beat-to-beat R-R intervals with clinical-grade accuracy

### System 3: Autonomic (Vagal Reactivation)
- Parasympathetic dominance increases via vagal efferents
- Sympathetic withdrawal over ~5-10 minutes
- RSA maximized — heart rate naturally couples to breathing
- RMSSD reflects short-term vagal tone at this timescale

### System 4: Auditory (Binaural Processing)
- 200 Hz left ear + 210 Hz right ear = 10 Hz alpha percept
- Shifting to 206 Hz = 6 Hz theta percept
- Beat percept generated in superior olivary complex (brainstem)
- Cortical effects inconsistent (5/14 studies per Ingendoh 2023) — but attentional anchoring is real

## The Interactions (What Makes This More Than the Sum)

### Auditory-Respiratory Coupling
The binaural beat provides an attentional anchor that stabilizes breathing rhythm. Not entrainment in the neural sense, but **attentional scaffolding** — the sound gives the mind something to rest on while the breath does the physiological work. Koelsch et al. 2017: auditory stimuli near breathing frequency increase respiratory entrainment.

### Cardiac-Gated Perception
Azevedo et al.: perception differs between systole and diastole. During this practice, RSA is maximally oscillating, which means the binaural beat is perceived DIFFERENTLY at different cardiac phases. During diastole: enhanced auditory processing (baroreceptors quiet, vagal tone high). During systole: dampened (baroreceptors firing, sympathetic pulse). The beat literally sounds different with each heartbeat.

### Interoceptive-Exteroceptive Integration
The anterior insula processes BOTH the heartbeat/breath (interoceptive) and the binaural beat (exteroceptive) simultaneously. Under Barrett/Seth predictive processing: the brain assigns precision-weighting to each signal. When both are rhythmic and predictable, they integrate rather than compete. The insula becomes a mixing board.

### The Emergent State
When respiratory, cardiac, auditory, and autonomic systems are all operating in coupled coherence:
- McCraty's "physiological coherence" — HRV and RSA synchronized
- Csikszentmihalyi's flow markers — reduced cognitive load, heightened focus
- Jhana factor parallels — one-pointed attention with absorption
- Not identical to any of these, but shares measurable features with all three
