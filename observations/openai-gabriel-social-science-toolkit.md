## Key Insight
OpenAI released GABRIEL, an open-source toolkit that turns unstructured text and images into quantitative measurements. Designed for economists, social scientists, and data scientists to study qualitative data at scale — usable even without coding experience. Includes Colab notebook for rapid prototyping.
