## Scan Summary

The Axios headline this week is unmissable: AI agents are triggering addiction-like behavior in top developers — the word "slot machine" is now being used in mainstream tech press to describe agentic coding tools. Meanwhile, a PNAS study just showed that cardiac interoception literally competes with tactile perception for neural bandwidth, giving hard neuroscience to the claim that ignoring your body costs you cognitive capacity. The physiology bridge across all three: the body is being systematically excluded from the productivity equation, and the bill is coming due.
