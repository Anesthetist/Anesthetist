AI-enabled relationship wellness, targeting couples. **SRL Signal:** Specialized AI wellness platforms targeting niche human relationship contexts (couples, professionals, athletes) are attracting growth capital. ## AANA Network — Clinical Practice & Patient Safety (Mar 13, 2026)

### Joint Commission Clarifies SRNA/RRNA Standards
The Joint Commission released an FAQ affirming their standards are NOT intended to restrict or prohibit students (SRNAs/RRNAs) in teaching cases. AANA challenged a misinterpretation of JC Hospital Standards that others had attempted to use to restrict CRNA scope. **SRL Signal:** AANA is actively defending CRNA scope and professional authority. This is the community Pausality serves. Scope protection + professional validation = high trust in evidence-based tools from CRNA-designed sources.

## Not Boring — Weekly Dose of Optimism #184 (Mar 13, 2026)

### The First Multi-Behavior Brain Upload
Eon Systems mapped the complete adult Drosophila (fruit fly) connectome — 140,000 neurons, 50 million synaptic connections — and ran it on a physics-simulated fly body, producing full sensorimotor behavior including navigation, grooming, and eating. Separately, Japanese scientists developed a technique to make brains see-through without killing them (isotonic optical clearing). German team made progress on cryopreserving and thawing mouse brains with neuronal firing and plasticity intact. **SRL Signal:** Neuroscience is having a landmark week. The fundamental science underpinning ANS regulation, vagal nerve function, and breathwork efficacy is advancing rapidly. This validates the scientific substrate of Pausality's core mechanisms.

### Replit Raises $400M at $9B Valuation, Launches Agent 4
Replit raised $400M led by Georgian Partners at a $9B valuation. Launched Agent 4 — AI-native collaborative software creation with multi-agent teamwork. **SRL Signal:** AI-native product development is accelerating. The tools for building Pausality's platform are getting dramatically more powerful.

### Massive Robotics Funding Week
Sunday Robotics $165M at $1.15B; Rhoda AI $450M Series A at $1.7B; Mind Robotics $500M. **SRL Signal:** Capital is flowing heavily into physical + AI hybrid companies. Wearable + protocol companies (like Pausality) benefit from the same investor appetite for AI-physical convergence.

### Swift Solar + Meyer Burger — Vertical Integration Thesis
Swift Solar acquired Meyer Burger assets to vertically integrate perovskite solar manufacturing. **SRL Signal (strategic analogy):** The "press your technological advantage into vertical integration" thesis directly applies to Pausality. The differentiated IP (CRNA-designed, biometrically validated protocols) should be integrated into a full platform, not licensed out as a module. Own the stack.
