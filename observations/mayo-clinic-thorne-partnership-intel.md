# Mayo Clinic Partnership Intel: Michael Thorne Connection

## The Contact

Michael Thorne, Head of PA/NP Innovation at Mayo Clinic.

## How It Happened

Thorne used the Pausality app to deal with rough night shift work in the OR, specifically after a 2 AM MVA. He felt the app made a difference. This opened dialogue for championing Pausality.

## Opportunities Identified

1. **1,500 ARNP Innovation Challenge** about to start at the Rochester site; Thorne offered to champion Pausality
2. **Mayo Research collaboration** to co-create Pausality-specific research through Mayo's research infrastructure
3. **Mayo Ventures** approach (noted: they ask for significant equity but offer extensive access/resources)
4. **Sonsiel Board** position; Thorne encouraged NurseHack4Health hackathon application (deadline end of that week)

## Strategic Significance

- First real-world clinical validation from a major academic medical center leader
- Night shift OR use case directly validates the Gap Moment Training concept
- Mayo brand association would be a massive credibility accelerator
- Research co-creation pathway could produce gold-standard evidence
