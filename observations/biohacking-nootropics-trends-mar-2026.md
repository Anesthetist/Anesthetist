## Source

Rising Trends (supplement search data), Ayuba Nutrition (biohacking 2026 roundup), Reddit biohacking community aggregation. March 2026.

## Summary

The biohacking and nootropics community is experiencing a surge in interest around pharmaceutical-grade cognitive enhancers, particularly methylene blue (search interest up +309%), alongside continued growth in adaptogens like Rhodiola rosea (+173%, 60,500 monthly searches) and L-theanine (301,000 monthly searches, +50% growth). Community discourse is also showing a maturation trend — evidence-based foundations (creatine, omega-3, magnesium) are increasingly framed as the core stack, with exotic compounds layered on top. Reddit and community platforms remain the primary engines for trend identification and critical evaluation of new findings.

## Why It Matters for SRL

The community's move toward evidence-based supplementation reflects growing demand for scientifically grounded human performance content — precisely SRL's positioning. SRL content on autonomic regulation, breathwork, and interoceptive training can fill the "non-supplemental performance optimization" niche that is underserved in these communities. The methylene blue surge also intersects with SRL's interest in mitochondrial and cognitive performance optimization.
