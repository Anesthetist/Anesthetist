# Intellectual Influence Map

## Observation

Randy does not adopt frameworks wholesale. He stress-tests each influence against 28 years of OR experience, selects what survives contact with real clinical pressure, and integrates selectively. The result is something none of these influences individually produce.

## Influence Map

### Dan Siegel / IPNB
- **What Randy took:** Integration = health formula; vertical/horizontal/interpersonal integration layers; mindsight; window of tolerance
- **What Randy left behind:** Generic mindfulness framing; therapy-oriented application
- **SRL integration:** Calm vigilance as neurointegration; extends beyond dyadic co-regulation to team/OR-level coherence

### Stephen Porges / Polyvagal Theory
- **What Randy took:** Ventral vagal as operating state; social engagement system; autonomic state hierarchy
- **What Randy left behind:** Some contested phylogenetic claims
- **SRL integration:** OR team co-regulation; prosodic safety broadcasting; clinical interventions rather than therapy

### Andrew Huberman
- **What Randy took:** Protocol-level neuroscience; vagus nerve protocols; physiological sighs; cold exposure; theta/gamma synchronization; FEED model
- **What Randy left behind:** Consumer wellness framing; supplement-heavy approach
- **SRL integration:** Evidence-backed protocol components for NeuroMinutes; mechanism literacy for clinician audiences

### Michael Gervais / Finding Mastery
- **What Randy took:** "What are you willing to risk?"; performance psychology under pressure; courage + arousal training
- **What Randy left behind:** Generic motivational framing
- **SRL integration:** Gap Moment as performance readiness (not relaxation); adds physiological measurement where Gervais stays psychological

### Brian MacKenzie & Emily Hightower / Shift Adapt
- **What Randy took:** CO2 tolerance drills; Art of Breath; nasal breathing; Skill of Stress; stress as trainable tool
- **What Randy left behind:** Athlete-only framing
- **SRL integration:** Graded Interoceptive Exposure component of [[structured-interoception-training]]

### Cedric Chin / Commoncog
- **What Randy took:** Expertise acceleration via cases; effectuation; tacit knowledge extraction methods
- **What Randy left behind:** SaaS-specific business models
- **SRL integration:** [[benner-expertise-spiral]] operationalization; case-based CEU design

### Tom Morgan / Leading Edge
- **What Randy took:** Consciousness + leadership convergence; emergence; awareness-based leadership
- **What Randy left behind:** Metaphysical speculation
- **SRL integration:** Collective nervous system coherence; "coherent fields for wise action"

### Julia Mossbridge
- **What Randy took:** Choice Compass (HRV-based decision tool); unconditional love as research frame; predictive intuition
- **What Randy left behind:** Telepathy/psi claims
- **SRL integration:** Biofeedback-assisted intuitive decision-making

### B.K.S. Iyengar
- **What Randy took:** *Light on Pranayama*; sequential attention (nose-throat-chest-abdomen); kumbhaka/CO2 tolerance; breath geometry
- **What Randy left behind:** Spiritual hierarchy framing
- **SRL integration:** Interoceptive mapping in clinical context; attentional gears (vitarka-vicara) extended with EEG correlates

## Core Pattern

The synthesis formula: **Siegel's integration model provides the architectural principle, Polyvagal Theory provides the autonomic mechanics, interoception provides the training substrate, and the OR provides the crucible.** This is [[consilience-by-translation]] in action — finding the same mechanism described in different vocabularies across domains.
