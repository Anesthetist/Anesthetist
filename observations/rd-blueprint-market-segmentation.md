# R&D Blueprint: Three-Tier Market Segmentation + NASA/DARPA/FAA Targets

## Observation

The R&D Blueprint maps SRL's five proprietary frameworks to three market tiers and identifies institutional contract targets:

### Framework-to-Market Mapping

| Framework | Primary Market |
|-----------|---------------|
| Anterocept™ | High-stress professionals (CRNAs, ICU nurses, trauma surgeons) |
| ExterOryx™ | Attentional flexibility + cognitive resilience training |
| Multi-Phase Interoceptive Coupling™ | Flow state induction + stress mitigation |
| NeuroHarmonics™ Entrainer | AI audio sync for brainwave entrainment |
| PolyAnchora™ Transitions | ANS recalibration + real-time decision-making |

### Three Market Tiers

1. **Healthcare professionals** — HRV-guided burnout prevention (CRNA wedge)
2. **Corporate executives** — AI stress mitigation (enterprise expansion)
3. **Athletes/tactical operators** — Neurophysiological adaptation (performance market)

### Institutional Contract Targets

NASA, DARPA, Special Forces, FAA, Corporate Leadership Programs — each represents a high-value, high-validation contract that compounds the clinical credibility moat.

### AI Technical Stack

LSTM + Transformer architectures for real-time neurophysiological adaptation. Apple HealthKit + WatchOS AI pipelines + iOS/Android neuroadaptive API.

## Significance

This is the commercialization roadmap that connects the vault's concept architecture to revenue. Each framework has a named market; each market has institutional validation targets. The three-tier structure mirrors the five red arrows: CRNA wedge (Arrow 1-2) → enterprise (Arrow 3) → platform/data moat (Arrow 4-5).
