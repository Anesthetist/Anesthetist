# VCU–AANA Burnout/Wellness Initiative Partnership (March 2025)

## Context

In March 2025, Somnistics Research Labs initiated exploratory conversations with two institutional partners to co-develop continuing education (CE) curriculum targeting CRNA burnout and performance sustainability:

- **Crystal Hunnicutt, DNAP, CRNA, APRN, CNE, CHSE** — Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU), potential academic co-development partner and PhD-level advisor contact
- **Rebecca Frese** — American Association of Nurse Anesthesiology (AANA), liaison to the AANA Wellness Committee

## Key Observations

### VCU Partnership (Crystal Hunnicutt)
- VCU expressed interest in co-creating CE curriculum grounded in SRL's science base
- Partnership maps directly to **Career-Longitudinal GMT Phase 1 (Seeding)**: embedding [[gap-moment-training]] and [[neurominute]] practices into SRNA education pipelines
- Crystal Hunnicutt represents a potential academic advisor pathway, bridging SRL's applied research with VCU's doctoral programs
- The academic validation channel strengthens the evidence chain by creating opportunities for controlled studies of GMT interventions in training environments

### AANA Wellness Committee (Rebecca Frese)
- The AANA Wellness Committee is actively seeking evidence-based interventions for the burnout epidemic documented in [[horvath-2021-srna-pandemic-burnout]] and [[crna-burnout-epidemiology]]
- This maps to **Career-Longitudinal GMT Phase 2 (Healing)**: mid-career practitioners using GMT to repair accumulated allostatic load
- The AANA channel represents the largest addressable market: ~70,000 members, with CE course pricing modeled at ~$100/seat
- Interest from the Wellness Committee validates that institutional gatekeepers recognize the gap between awareness-level wellness programming and skill-building interventions like GMT

### Executive Stand-Up Discussion (March 13, 2025)
- Action items identified:
  - Follow up with VCU and AANA contacts to convert interest into group downloads
  - Work with VCU and the AANA Wellness Committee to advance the CE container
- The partnership pipeline feeds directly into [[revenue-arrow-functor]] — institutional licensing as the primary revenue mechanism

## Strategic Significance

This partnership represents the first institutional validation of the Career-Longitudinal GMT model. VCU provides the **Seeding** entry point (training the next generation), while AANA provides the **Healing** channel (reaching practicing CRNAs already carrying burnout damage). Together, they create a dual-track adoption pathway that mirrors the three-phase model: habits are seeded in school, healing interventions reach mid-career practitioners, and the cumulative effect builds anti-fragility across the profession.

The CE curriculum container also serves as a forcing function for formalizing SRL's intellectual property into a deliverable product — converting vault knowledge into revenue.
