# Critical Decision Method: Systematic Extraction of Randy's Clinical Expertise

## Observation

The Strategic Master Plan (7 Powers + Commoncog + Category Theory) specifies a systematic method for extracting Randy's tacit clinical expertise into teachable organizational knowledge:

### Critical Decision Method (CDM)

1. Record 100 coaching/training sessions
2. Transcribe decision points where Randy shifts approach
3. Identify the perceptual cues, mental models, and somatic markers that triggered each shift
4. Codify into scenario-based training modules

### Naturalistic Decision Making (NDM)

Training scenarios with time pressure and incomplete information — simulating OR crisis conditions. Designed so novices encounter the same decision environments that built Randy's expertise, but in compressed/controlled form.

### Perceptual Discrimination Training

"Show novices HRV charts, have them diagnose before seeing expert analysis" — spaced repetition via scenario practice. Builds the pattern-recognition that experts have and novices lack.

### Three-Pillar Simplification

Reduce overwhelming biomarkers/concepts to 3 pillars × 3 subcomponents = 9 learnable units. Prevents cognitive overload while preserving the essential structure.

## Significance

This is the method by which Randy's 28 years of embodied expertise becomes scalable intellectual property — not through writing it down but through structured decision-point extraction. It's the bridge from [[embodied-knowledge-layers]] (tacit, personal) to [[somnistics]] (codified, teachable, licensable).
