# Near-Death Clinical Narrative — "Am I Going to Die?"

Randy's foundational clinical story — the moment that crystallized the importance of clinician regulation under extreme pressure. Origin story for the entire SRL project.

## The Moment

Three surgeons in the abdomen, three CRNAs at the head. Massive hemorrhage during delivery. The patient was awake on epidural because general anesthesia would have decreased her survival chance. Baby in the bassinet a foot away.

## Randy's Words

"She looked up at me, baby in the bassinet a foot away, looks at me and says 'Am I going to die?' I comforted her and gave her some midazolam."
