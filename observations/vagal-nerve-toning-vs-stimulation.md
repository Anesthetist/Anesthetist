# VNT vs VNS: Consumer Vagal Toning vs Medical Vagal Stimulation

## Observation

A critical regulatory and mechanistic distinction exists between Vagal Nerve Stimulation (VNS) and Vagal Nerve Toning (VNT):

### Vagal Nerve Stimulation (VNS)
- Medical device: electrical current applied directly to vagus nerve
- Surgically implanted (like pacemaker) or transcutaneous (taVNS)
- FDA-regulated medical device
- Prescribed for epilepsy, treatment-resistant depression
- Mechanism: direct electrical activation of vagal afferents

### Vagal Nerve Toning (VNT)
- Consumer wellness approach: indirect vagal activation through behavior
- Methods: slow breathing, humming, cold exposure, singing, gargling
- No surgical implant or prescription required
- Mechanism: endogenous activation of vagal pathways through physiological maneuvers
- Regulation: wellness/general wellness claims, not medical device

### Pausality's Position
Pausality is firmly in the VNT category — using breath-based protocols with biofeedback to *tone* vagal pathways endogenously. This distinction:
- Keeps Pausality on the wellness side of FDA boundaries
- Aligns with consumer-accessible delivery (app + Apple Watch)
- Still leverages the same underlying neural pathways that VNS targets

### Neurofeedback Literature Gap
The neurofeedback literature discusses overlap but lacks standardized terminology for distinguishing behavioral vs. electrical vagal modulation approaches in consumer contexts.

## Significance

This distinction is foundational for Pausality's regulatory strategy, marketing language, and clinical positioning. Every claim must stay within VNT territory — "train your vagal tone" not "stimulate your vagus nerve."
