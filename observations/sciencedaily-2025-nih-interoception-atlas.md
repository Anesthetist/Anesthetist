## Source

ScienceDaily, October 2025. Reporting on NIH Director's Transformative Research Award to Scripps Research & Allen Institute.

## Summary

The NIH awarded a $14.2M Transformative Research Award to a collaborative team from Scripps Research (led by Nobel laureate Ardem Patapoutian) and the Allen Institute (Bosiljka Tasic) to build the first comprehensive atlas of the interoceptive nervous system. Described as mapping the body's "hidden sixth sense," the project aims to systematically characterize the neural circuits through which the body monitors and signals its internal state to the brain.
