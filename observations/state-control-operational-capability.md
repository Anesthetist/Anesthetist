# Somnistics as State Control, Not Relaxation

A core positioning insight from the PubMed-tightened somnistics definition: Somnistics is not "relaxation" as an aesthetic; it is state control as an operational capability.

## The Distinction

This single sentence distinguishes Somnistics from the entire wellness/meditation app market:

- **Relaxation** implies passive, pleasant, aesthetic — something you do when you have time
- **State control** implies active, precise, functional — something you do because lives depend on it
- **Operational capability** frames regulation as a professional competency, like airway management or hemodynamic monitoring

## Language Register

The framing "operational capability" comes from military, aviation, and clinical language — signaling the target audience (CRNAs, high-stakes professionals) rather than the wellness consumer.

## Counter-Positioning

| Wellness Apps Say | Somnistics Says |
|---|---|
| "Find your calm" | "Control your state" |
| "Take a mindful moment" | "Run a 60-second protocol" |
| "Reduce stress" | "Produce measurable state deltas" |
| "Relax and unwind" | "Maintain task-ready regulation" |
