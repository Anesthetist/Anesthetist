# Calibration Case: Spring Health & Lyra Health — Enterprise Mental Health

## Why Paired
Both are venture-backed enterprise mental health platforms selling to HR/benefits buyers. They represent the high end of the market Pausality's B2B play enters.

## Commoncog Triad Analysis

### Operations
**Spring Health:** AI-powered precision mental health matching. 4,500+ enterprise customers. Proprietary assessment tool routes employees to right care level (self-serve → coaching → therapy → medication). ~10M covered lives.

**Lyra Health:** 85,000+ providers in network. Blended care model (digital + human). Measurement-based care with clinical outcomes tracking. Claims 83% symptom improvement rate.

### Market
**Spring Health:** $509M raised, $3.3B valuation. Customers include Gap, Bumble, General Mills. Positioning: "precision medicine for mental health."

**Lyra Health:** $915M raised, $5.58B valuation. Customers include Morgan Stanley, Starbucks, eBay. Positioning: "evidence-based mental health for the workforce."

Both sell to benefits decision-makers (CHRO, VP Benefits) with ROI arguments around reduced absenteeism, lower claims costs, and improved productivity.

### Capital
- Combined $1.4B+ raised — signals massive enterprise wellness TAM
- Both achieved unicorn status selling to Fortune 500 HR buyers
- Revenue model: PEPM (per-employee-per-month) licensing — predictable recurring revenue

## Surprising Similarities to Pausality
1. **Selling to the same buyer** — HR/benefits teams at hospitals and enterprises. The MUSC opportunity follows identical enterprise sales motion.
2. **ROI narrative is king** — Both win deals by showing reduced claims costs and improved outcomes. Pausality's real-time biometric data makes this narrative MORE provable, not less.
3. **EAP replacement positioning** — Both positioned against underperforming EAPs. Pausality can use identical positioning for clinician-specific wellness.

## Surprising Dissimilarities to Pausality
1. **Provider networks vs. Protocol** — Spring/Lyra built massive therapist networks. Pausality built a protocol. Radically different cost structures: their marginal cost is a provider's time; Pausality's is near-zero per additional user.
2. **Reactive vs. Proactive** — Spring/Lyra activate when someone ALREADY needs help. Pausality intervenes BEFORE the nervous system dysregulates. Prevention vs. treatment.
3. **Price points** — $8-15 PEPM for Spring/Lyra with $300+ per therapy session. Pausality at $12.99/mo is orders of magnitude cheaper — could be positioned as the front-line prevention layer before Spring/Lyra-style therapy.
4. **60 seconds vs. 60 minutes** — Pausality's micro-intervention fits INTO clinical workflow. Therapy sessions require scheduled time OFF workflow.

## Pattern Recognition (RPD Library)
- **PEPM model** works for enterprise wellness — Pausality should consider PEPM pricing for B2B
- **"Precision" language** resonates with enterprise buyers (Spring's "precision mental health")
- **Complementary positioning available**: "Pausality for prevention, [therapy platform] for treatment" — strategic partnership potential
- **$1.4B raised combined** validates the enterprise mental health market is real and large
