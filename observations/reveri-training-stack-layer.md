# Reveri — 6th Layer in the Training Stack

## The App
**Reveri** by David Spiegel, MD — Associate Chair of Psychiatry at Stanford. 45+ years of clinical hypnotherapy research. 400+ published papers. The only hypnosis app built on decades of peer-reviewed Stanford neuroscience.

- Rating: 4.5/5 (4,341 reviews)
- Pricing: $24.99/mo or $99.99/yr
- Claimed efficacy: 77% pain relief in 10 min, 84% stress reduction in 1 session, 93% improved focus

## How Randy Uses It
1-minute self-hypnosis sessions for **pre/post workout performance priming**. Not the full 10-minute sessions — the short-form exercises that map to the NeuroMinute format.

## Why It's a Distinct Layer

Hypnosis is NOT breathwork, NOT meditation, NOT affirmation. It's a distinct neural mechanism:
- Modulates dorsal anterior cingulate cortex (dACC)
- Alters the functional connectivity between executive control network and default mode network
- Reduces peripheral awareness while increasing focused attention (the opposite of open monitoring meditation)
- The suggestibility pathway is separate from the interoceptive pathway

This means Reveri adds a modality that none of the other 5 layers provide.

## Validation of SRL's Minimum Effective Dose Thesis

Spiegel proved the dose can be 1 minute. From a completely different modality (hypnosis, not breathwork), an independent Stanford researcher arrived at the same conclusion: **60 seconds is enough to produce a measurable state shift.** This is independent convergent evidence for the NeuroMinute format.

## The Pre/Post Performance Framing

Reveri frames self-hypnosis as performance preparation, not wellness. "Pre-workout" and "post-workout" sessions. This is the same positioning as GMT's "pre-flight check" — autonomic training as operational capability, not self-care.

## The Full 6-Layer Training Stack

| Layer | Source | Modality | What It Trains |
|-------|--------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | EHRV+ | HRV biofeedback | Resonant frequency, coherence |
| 2 | Echobay Heal | Binaural beats | Attentional anchoring |
| 3 | ThinkUp | Self-voiced affirmation | Identity/intention |
| 4 | Pausality | Guided breathing curriculum | Progressive autonomic training |
| 5 | Reveri | Self-hypnosis | Suggestibility, focus, dACC modulation |
| 6 | Waking Up (Kelly Boys) / Huberman NSDR | Deep recovery | Parasympathetic saturation |

## What Pausality Should Absorb
- 1-minute self-hypnosis as an alternate NeuroMinute modality
- Pre-case priming sessions (not just between cases — BEFORE the first case)
- Post-case debrief with autonomic reset
- Suggestibility/hypnotizability assessment (like Reveri's intake) → maps to SRL's interoceptive sensitivity assessment concept

## Design Constraint
**"If self-hypnosis produces a 1-minute state shift through a different neural pathway than breathwork, the NeuroMinute format should include hypnotic protocols alongside respiratory protocols. Single-modality training leaves capacity on the table."**
