## Source

PsyPost, 2026. "Neuroscientists find evidence meditation changes how fluid moves in the brain."

## Summary

When expert meditators switched from mind wandering to focused attention, the absolute flow of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) decreased — driven by a reduction in backward flow — and fluid movement became more efficient and coherent. Brain activity also became significantly more complex and less predictable during both Samatha and Vipassana meditation compared to resting state. This suggests meditation doesn't just change neural activity patterns but alters fundamental brain physiology at the fluid dynamics level.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Opens a new mechanistic window for understanding how contemplative practices alter brain function. If meditation changes CSF dynamics, this could connect to **SomnoAffinity** (sleep-dependent glymphatic clearance), **NeuroHarmonics** (brain coherence), and **State Transition** concepts. The finding that CSF flow becomes "more efficient and coherent" during meditation parallels the coherence optimization SRL trains through resonant breathing.
