# Somnistics Teaches Gain Control, Not Just Focus

## Observation

Selective amplification + normalization (gain control) appears across 500 million years of vertebrate evolution, in chemical reaction networks, and in transformer AI architectures — all solving the same problem: "processing under energy constraints." This convergent evolution reveals that attention is fundamentally about *signal weighting*, not *spotlight direction*.

### Reframing for Somnistics

Traditional framing: "focus your attention on your breath"
Somnistics framing: "learn which internal channels matter right now"

The shift from "focus" to "gain control" means:
- Not narrowing attention (which creates tunnel vision)
- But adjusting the *gain* on different signal channels (turning up interoceptive signals, turning down external noise)
- [[neurominute]] protocols train anterior insula + salience network: learning which internal channels are relevant *right now*

### Gap Moment Thesis (Gain Control Version)

Gap moments are windows where the gain-control system must re-weight signals or risk:
- **Freeze:** All channels suppressed (dorsal vagal)
- **Dissociation:** Internal channels suppressed, external amplified
- **Mis-prioritization:** Wrong channel amplified (e.g., emotional reactivity when clinical precision is needed)

## Significance

This positions somnistics as teaching the most fundamental cognitive skill — not "relaxation" or "mindfulness" but the precision weighting mechanism that underlies all adaptive behavior. It's the biological mechanism of [[relevance-realization]] operationalized.
