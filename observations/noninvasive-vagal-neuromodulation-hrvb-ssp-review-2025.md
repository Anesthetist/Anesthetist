## Source

Spandidos Publications, 2025. Review article. PMC12082064.

## Summary

Comprehensive review of non-invasive vagal neuromodulation techniques, particularly HRV biofeedback and the Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP). Highlights that HRV-B at ~6 cycles/minute synchronizes breathing with HRV feedback to boost vagal tone. Explains the mechanism by which RSA exemplifies vagal modulation of the cardiac-respiratory interaction. Emphasizes the vagus nerve's role in regulating the dynamic interplay between cardiac and respiratory functions as the basis for these interventions.

## Why It Matters for SRL

Provides a comprehensive mechanistic review that supports SRL's core intervention stack — resonant breathing frequency, HRV biofeedback, and vagal tone enhancement. The inclusion of SSP (a polyvagal-informed auditory intervention) is especially relevant given the 2026 polyvagal debate; this review focuses on the empirically validated mechanisms rather than the contested evolutionary theory. Strengthens the evidence base for SRL's Neuro-Respiratory Cardiac Coherence concept.
