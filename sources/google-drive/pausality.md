# Pausality

## What It Is
Mindfulness, breathwork, and nervous system regulation app — primarily targeting CRNAs and high-performance clinical professionals. Built on SRL's Somnistics framework.

## Positioning
Not just a breathwork app. Category formation: treating neural state transitions as trainable, measurable, protocolized phenomena. Scientific defensibility is core to institutional adoption strategy.
