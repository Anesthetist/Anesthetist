## 🎯 MARKET INTELLIGENCE

### What's Working NOW on TikTok (Jan 2026)

**Viral Trends to Ride:**
1. **"Cortisol" obsession** — cortisol cocktails, cortisol face, lowering cortisol
2. **Vagal toning** — "reset your nervous system" content exploding
3. **HRV tracking** — Apple Watch users want to understand their data
4. **"Nervous system regulation"** — mainstream term now
5. **Micro-wellness** — short practices > long meditations
6. **"That girl" / "glow up"** aesthetic tied to stress management

**Content Formats Converting:**
- Before/after transformations (HRV screenshots)
- "What I wish I knew" educational hooks
- POV videos ("POV: you just reset your nervous system in 60 seconds")
- Duets/stitches with wellness creators
- "Day in my life" with micro-practices shown
- Screen recordings of Apple Watch HRV changes

### Competitor Landscape

| App | Positioning | Weakness |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| StressWatch | AI stress monitoring | Passive tracking only, no intervention |
| Calm | Meditation/sleep | Long sessions, no biometric feedback |
| Headspace | Guided meditation | No Apple Watch HRV integration |
| Breathwrk | Breathing exercises | No real-time biofeedback |
| Welltory | HRV analytics | Complex, not actionable |

**Pausality's Unique Position:**
- **Preparation, not recovery** (category-definer)
- **60-second protocols** (fits real life)
- **Real-time biofeedback** (see it working)
- **CRNA credibility** (medical authority)
- **Gap Moment Training** (proprietary framework)

## 🎬 CONTENT PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

### Daily Output
- **TikTok:** 2-3 videos/day
- **Instagram Reels:** Same content, 2/day
- **YouTube Shorts:** Top performers, 1/day

### Content Types Mix
- 40% Educational (HRV, nervous system, techniques)
- 30% Demonstration (app in action, real-time feedback)
- 20% Social proof (testimonials, transformations)
- 10% Trend-riding (duets, stitches, trending sounds)
