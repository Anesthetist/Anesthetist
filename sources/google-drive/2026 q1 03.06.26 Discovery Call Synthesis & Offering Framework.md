## Part 2: Unified Productized Offering Framework

### "Somnistics Immersion" — Modular Enterprise Offering

**Tagline:** *Train the nervous system your team already has. Measure the shift.*

#### Tier 1: The Catalyst Session
**Format:** Half-day (3-hour) live workshop (virtual or in-person)
**What It Is:** An experiential, science-grounded session that teaches participants practical nervous system regulation techniques — breathwork, vagal tone activation, and interoceptive awareness — drawn from 28 years of high-acuity clinical practice.

**Includes:**
- Pre-session HRV baseline assessment (via Pausality app)
- 3-hour facilitated session with Randy (didactic + experiential)
- Personalized take-home practice protocol
- Post-session resource kit (digital)

**Price Point:** $5,000 - $7,500
**Audience Size:** Up to 30 participants

#### Tier 2: The 30-Day Integration
**Format:** Catalyst Session + 30-day structured engagement with app, check-ins, and outcomes measurement
**What It Is:** The full experiential pathway. After the live session, participants use Pausality daily for 30 days with guided NeuroMinutes, HRV tracking, and structured weekly touchpoints. This is the Mayo Clinic pilot model adapted for enterprise delivery.

**Program Cadence:**

| Week | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Day 1** | Catalyst Session — science, methodology, experiential practice | 3 hours (Zoom or in-person) |
| **Day 1** | Pre-program assessments: MAIA-2 (interoceptive awareness), Maslach Burnout Inventory, Professional Quality of Life Scale (ProQOL), Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-4), plus any org-specific metrics | 20-30 min |
| **Days 2-7** | Participants begin daily Pausality app sessions | Self-paced |
| **End of Week 1** | Group check-in — Q&A, troubleshooting, early observations | 1 hour (Zoom) |
| **End of Week 2** | Group check-in — deeper practice, suggestions, learning from participants | 1 hour (Zoom) |
| **End of Week 3** | Extended group session — review, refinement, participant insights | 2 hours (Zoom) |
| **Day 30** | Post-program assessments: repeat all baseline instruments | 20-30 min |
| **Day 30-35** | Outcomes report delivery + debrief call with organizational leader | 1 hour |

**Includes:**
- Everything in Tier 1
- 30-day Pausality app licenses (up to 30 users)
- Pre/post validated assessments (MAIA-2, MBI, ProQOL, PSS-4)
- 3 group check-in sessions (weeks 1, 2, 3) — 4 hours total facilitator time
- End-of-engagement outcomes report (HRV trends, assessment score changes, engagement metrics, qualitative feedback)
- Debrief call with organizational leader
- Custom dashboard for the organization (engagement, completion, biometric trends)

**Measurement Framework (from Mayo Clinic pilot design):**
- **Interoceptive awareness:** MAIA-2 score change (does interoception training move the needle?)
- **Burnout:** Maslach Burnout Inventory — emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, personal accomplishment
- **Professional quality of life:** ProQOL — compassion satisfaction/fatigue
- **Perceived stress:** PSS-4
- **Biometric:** HRV trends, resting heart rate changes, session completion rates
- **Behavioral:** App engagement frequency, gap moment utilization, protocol progression
- **Optional org-specific:** Intent to leave, sick day utilization, near-miss/safety events, patient satisfaction

**Price Point:** $15,000 - $20,000
**Audience Size:** Up to 30 participants
**Per-User App Cost:** $12/user/month (bundled into package price)

**Pricing Rationale (see benchmarks below)**

### Pricing Benchmarks & Rationale

**Market research (Mar 2026):**

| Benchmark | Rate | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Half-day corporate workshop facilitator | $5,000 - $10,000 | Industry standard (Clutch, Business Training Works) |
| Full-day executive retreat facilitation | $10,000 - $70,000 | Action Learning Associates, InnerWork Company |
| Corporate wellness per-employee per-session | $10 - $25 | Meditopia for Work, Vantage Fit |
| Medium enterprise annual wellness program | $25,000 - $50,000 | Industry surveys |
| CRNA turnover replacement cost | $80,000 - $150,000 | Mayo Clinic pilot framework (internal) |

**Why $15K-$20K for Tier 2 is defensible (per Russ: "$15K is nothing for an organization this size"):**

1. **You're not selling a workshop — you're selling a measured outcome.** The MAIA-2, MBI, ProQOL, and PSS-4 pre/post design means they get a publishable-grade before/after dataset. That's clinical validation, not "lunch and learn."
2. **7+ hours of live facilitation** across the 30-day engagement (3hr Catalyst + 1hr + 1hr + 2hr check-ins), plus assessment administration, dashboard setup, and outcomes reporting. At standard facilitator rates ($5K/half-day), the facilitation time alone is worth $10K-$15K.
3. **30 app licenses at $12/user/month = $360 in software.** This is bundled — it's a negligible fraction of the engagement price, which makes the app feel like a value-add, not the product being sold.
4. **ROI framing for healthcare orgs:** If one clinician stays instead of leaving, the org saves $80K-$150K in turnover costs. A $15K pilot that demonstrates even modest retention improvement pays for itself 5-10x.
5. **For non-healthcare orgs (like Strategic Earth):** Frame the ROI as team performance, decision quality, and reduced emotional labor — harder to quantify but Kelly's language ("embodied coherence") shows she already values this.

**Pricing guidance by org type:**

| Org Type | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------|-------|
| Small consulting firm (10-30 people) | $5,000 - $7,500 | $15,000 | Strategic Earth range |
| Membership org / community (100+ members) | $7,500 - $10,000 | $15,000 - $20,000 | MORE range — premium audience |
| Healthcare system (pilot cohort) | $7,500 - $10,000 | $20,000 - $25,000 | Mayo model — includes clinical instruments |
| Enterprise (multi-cohort, annual) | Custom | $25,000+ | Tier 3 conversation |
