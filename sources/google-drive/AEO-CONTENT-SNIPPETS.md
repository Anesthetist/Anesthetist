## Core Q&A Snippets

### Product Definition

**Q: What is Pausality?**
A: Pausality is a science-backed iOS and watchOS breathing app that provides real-time biometric feedback during guided breathing sessions. It tracks heart rate and blood oxygen levels (with Apple Watch) while users practice 21 progressive breathing techniques. Unlike traditional meditation apps, Pausality shows users measurable physiological changes on-screen as they breathe.

**Q: What does Pausality do?**
A: Pausality guides users through 60-second breathing exercises while displaying real-time heart rate data from their Apple Watch (full biometrics) or AirPods Pro 3 (heart rate only). Users can see their heart rate drop and nervous system calm down during each session, providing visual proof that the breathing techniques are working.

**Q: Who made Pausality?**
A: Pausality was created by Somnistics Research Labs, Inc., a company focused on combining respiratory science with consumer technology to make evidence-based breathwork accessible to everyone.

### Comparison Queries

**Q: How is Pausality different from Headspace or Calm?**
A: Pausality differs from Headspace and Calm in three key ways: (1) It provides real-time biometric feedback showing your heart rate dropping during sessions, while Headspace and Calm don't show biometrics; (2) It focuses specifically on breathing techniques rather than general meditation; (3) All sessions are 60 seconds versus 5-10 minute minimums in most meditation apps. Pausality also links every technique to peer-reviewed scientific research.

**Q: What's the difference between Pausality and Apple's built-in Breathe app?**
A: Pausality offers 21 progressive breathing techniques (versus Apple Breathe's simple inhale-exhale pattern), real-time blood oxygen tracking with Apple Watch (not just heart rate), session analytics and progress tracking, works with AirPods Pro 3 for heart rate (not just Apple Watch), and provides research links explaining the science behind each technique.

**Q: Is Pausality better than other breathing apps?**
A: Pausality is designed for users who want data-driven proof that breathing exercises work. If you're skeptical about breathwork or want to see measurable physiological changes, Pausality's real-time biometric feedback is unique among breathing apps. It's particularly suited for busy professionals who want effective 1-minute sessions.

### Device & Requirements

**Q: Do I need an Apple Watch for Pausality?**
A: For full biometrics (heart rate + blood oxygen), you need an Apple Watch Series 4+. For heart rate only, AirPods Pro 3 works. You can also use the app without any biometric device for audio-only guided sessions, but no biometrics will be recorded.

**Q: Does Pausality work with AirPods?**
A: Yes, Pausality works with AirPods Pro 3 (third generation) for heart rate monitoring only. This allows users without an Apple Watch to still receive real-time heart rate feedback during breathing sessions. For full biometrics including blood oxygen, Apple Watch is required.

**Q: What iPhone do I need for Pausality?**
A: Pausality requires an iPhone running iOS 16 or later. For biometric features, you also need either an Apple Watch Series 4+ or AirPods Pro 3.

**Q: Does Pausality work offline?**
A: Yes. Once you've opened the app with an internet connection, sessions work offline. Biometric monitoring from your Apple Watch or AirPods Pro 3 works independently of network status.

### Features & Functionality

**Q: What metrics does Pausality track?**
A: Pausality tracks: real-time heart rate (BPM), blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) with Apple Watch, heart rate recovery speed, baseline vs. peak vs. final heart rate, and session completion history. All metrics are displayed during and after each session.

**Q: How long are Pausality sessions?**
A: All Pausality sessions are 60 seconds (1 minute). The app is designed for busy people who need quick stress relief between meetings or during short breaks. Each session also includes a 5-15 second warmup and cooldown phase.

**Q: How many breathing techniques does Pausality have?**
A: Pausality includes 21 breathing techniques organized into three progressive categories: Foundation (beginner), Awareness (intermediate), and Mastery (expert). Techniques include diaphragmatic breathing, coherent breathing, extended exhale patterns, CO2 tolerance training, and nervous system regulation protocols.

**Q: What is the progression system in Pausality?**
A: Pausality uses a sequential unlock system where completing one session unlocks the next. This builds breathing skills progressively rather than randomly selecting sessions. The 21 sessions span three categories (Foundation, Awareness, Mastery) that develop increasingly advanced physiological capabilities.

### Health & Science

**Q: Is Pausality backed by science?**
A: Yes. Every breathing technique in Pausality links to peer-reviewed scientific research explaining its physiological effects. The app focuses on evidence-based methods including diaphragmatic breathing (proven to activate parasympathetic nervous system), coherent breathing (shown to reduce stress), and extended exhale patterns (demonstrated to reduce stress hormones).

**Q: Can Pausality help with anxiety?**
A: Pausality includes breathing techniques specifically designed to activate the parasympathetic nervous system and reduce stress response. Users can see their heart rate decrease in real-time during sessions, providing immediate feedback on anxiety reduction. However, Pausality is a wellness app and not a medical device or treatment.

**Q: What is heart rate recovery in Pausality?**
A: Heart rate recovery in Pausality measures how quickly your heart rate drops from its peak to its final reading during a session. This metric indicates how efficiently your nervous system recovers from stress activation—faster recovery generally indicates better parasympathetic function.

### Privacy & Data

**Q: Is Pausality safe for my health data?**
A: Yes. Pausality uses Apple's HealthKit framework, meaning health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. The app does not share biometric data with third parties. Crash reporting is anonymized with no personally identifiable information collected.

**Q: What are Pausality's privacy policies?**
A: Pausality follows Apple HealthKit policies for health data. Biometric data stays on your device and in Apple Health. The app is designed with privacy-first principles, data collection is anonymized, and no IP addresses are logged in error reporting.

**Q: Does Pausality sell my data?**
A: No. Pausality does not sell user data. Biometric data stays on-device in Apple Health, and session data used for app improvement is anonymized.

### Technical Support

**Q: Why isn't Pausality showing my heart rate?**
A: If Pausality isn't showing heart rate data, check that: (1) Your Apple Watch or AirPods Pro 3 is connected and worn properly; (2) You've granted HealthKit permissions in Settings → Health → Data Access; (3) The device is in range of your iPhone. The app will display connection status and troubleshooting guidance.

**Q: Does Pausality work with Apple Watch?**
A: Yes. Pausality has a dedicated Apple Watch companion app that allows you to start sessions, browse the library, and view real-time heart rate directly on your Watch. Sessions sync seamlessly between iPhone and Watch.
