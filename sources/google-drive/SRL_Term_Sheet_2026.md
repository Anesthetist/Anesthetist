## 4. Conversion Terms

### Equity Financing
SAFE automatically converts into SAFE Preferred Stock at the Conversion Price (the greater of SAFE Price or Discount Price) upon the initial closing of a qualifying Equity Financing.

### Liquidity Event
Investor is entitled to the greater of (i) the Purchase Amount (Cash-Out) or (ii) the as-converted Common Stock value at the Liquidity Price (Valuation Cap ÷ Liquidity Capitalization).

### Maturity
On or after the 3rd anniversary of the first SAFE issued with the same Valuation Cap, converts to Common Stock at the SAFE Price upon majority election of SAFE holders.

### Dissolution
Investor entitled to Cash-Out Amount; junior to debt/creditors, pari passu with other SAFEs/Preferred Stock, senior to Common Stock.

---

## 5. Investor Rights & Protections

- **Pro-Rata Rights:** Early investors benefit from anti-dilution protection through pro-rata rights in subsequent rounds.
- **No Voting Rights:** Investor holds no voting rights until conversion to Capital Stock.
- **Dividend Rights:** If Company pays a Common Stock dividend while SAFE is outstanding, Investor receives a proportional Dividend Amount.
- **Transfer Restrictions:** SAFE is non-transferable without Company consent, except to affiliates or estate successors.
- **Liquidation Priority:** Pari passu with other SAFEs/Preferred Stock; junior to debt; senior to Common Stock.
