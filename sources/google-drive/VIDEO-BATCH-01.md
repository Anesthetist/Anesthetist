- **0-3s:** Close-up of Apple Watch face, finger tapping it. Accusatory angle.
- **3-10s:** Split screen - stressed face + HRV data screenshot looking bad

- **25-35s:** Side-by-side before/after HRV screenshots. App icon appears. Clean CTA text.

- **Hook:** Calls out a product they already own (Apple Watch = immediate relevance)

- **Proof:** Real-time HRV change is undeniable visual evidence

- **CTA:** "Your watch will prove it" = zero risk, they can verify themselves
