**ChatGPT:**

This equation:

\[
\text{NPV} = M_0 \cdot g \cdot \bar{s} \cdot \bar{m}
\]

is a **compact strategic valuation tool** that breaks down the **net present value** (NPV) of a business opportunity into four intuitive, high-leverage components:

---

### 🔍 Breakdown of Terms

| Variable        | Meaning | Strategic Implication |
|----------------|---------|------------------------|
| $M_0$ | **Current Market Size** | Are you in a market with real economic weight? |
| $g$ | **Discounted Market Growth Factor** | Does the market grow fast enough to justify investment—*after* adjusting for time and risk? |
| $\bar{s}$ | **Long-Term Market Share** | Can you win—and keep—a significant piece of the pie? |
| $\bar{m}$ | **Long-Term Differential Margin** | Can you earn profits *above* your cost of capital in a defensible way? |

---

### 🧠 Why It's Powerful

Instead of diving into spreadsheets or 5-year projections, this **mental model forces strategic clarity** by isolating what truly drives long-term value creation.

- If **any component is near zero**, your NPV collapses.
- If **each is above average**, the compounding effect is enormous.
- It **bridges strategy and finance** without needing financial gymnastics.

---

### ⚡Quick Interpretation

This equation says:

> The value of your business is the size of the opportunity ($M_0$), times how fast it's growing ($g$), times how much of it you can sustainably own ($\bar{s}$), times how profitable that ownership is *above your cost of capital* ($\bar{m}$).

It encourages you to ask:

- Is this a big enough market?
- Will it grow in a meaningful way?
- Can we win a defensible share?
- Can we generate margins that matter?

---

### 🧭 Follow-up Strategic Questions

1. **(5 Whys)** Why do we believe our margin will exceed the cost of capital in the long run?
2. **(SCAMPER – Substitute)** What happens if we substitute our current market with an adjacent one—does $M_0$ or $g$ jump significantly?
3. **(Socratic Method)** What would a smart competitor challenge about our assumptions for $\bar{s}$?
4. **(Six Thinking Hats – Black Hat)** Where could this equation deceive us due to hidden dependencies between terms?
5. **(5W1H – How)** How can we actively increase each term—what are the operational levers tied to $g$, $\bar{s}$, and $\bar{m}$?
