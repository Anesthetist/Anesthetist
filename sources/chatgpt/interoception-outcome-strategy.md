Got it. If you’re asking about validated instruments to measure interoception—these are commonly used in both research and clinical settings and could plug directly into Pausality outcomes work:

⸻

1. Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness (MAIA)
	•	Format: 32-item self-report questionnaire.
	•	Domains: Noticing, Emotional Awareness, Self-Regulation, Attention Regulation, Trusting, Body Listening, etc.
	•	Strength: Captures qualitative dimensions of interoception (not just accuracy).
	•	Use case for Pausality: Detects whether users are learning to trust body signals, regulate, and shift attention—core to Gap Moment Training™.
	•	Citation: Mehling W.E. et al., PLoS ONE (2012).

⸻

2. Body Perception Questionnaire (BPQ) – Short Form
	•	Format: 46-item self-report scale (can be shortened).
	•	Domains: Autonomic reactivity, awareness of bodily processes.
	•	Strength: Links subjective interoceptive awareness to autonomic symptoms.
	•	Use case for Pausality: Good for tracking baseline autonomic sensitivity shifts across populations.
	•	Citation: Porges, 1993 (Polyvagal framework aligned).

⸻

3. Heartbeat Detection Tasks (Objective Accuracy Measures)
	•	Method: Users count their heartbeats without taking pulse; accuracy scored against actual HR.
	•	Strength: Gold-standard performance-based measure of interoceptive accuracy.
	•	Limitations: Sensitive to expectancy effects and not scalable without wearables.
	•	Use case for Pausality: Can be adapted to Apple Watch/HRV data → scalable digital heartbeat perception tasks.

⸻

4. Interoceptive Accuracy vs. Sensibility vs. Awareness Framework
	•	Accuracy: Objective tasks (heartbeat detection).
	•	Sensibility: Self-report (MAIA, BPQ).
	•	Awareness: Metacognitive insight (confidence ratings after tasks).
	•	Use case for Pausality: You could embed a layered model—objective (Watch), sensibility (EMA check-ins), awareness (confidence slider).

⸻

✅ Recommendation for Pausality at scale:
	•	Use MAIA (short form) for daily/weekly EMA micro-checks.
	•	Pair with HRV + breath metrics for objective signatures.
	•	Add a lightweight heartbeat perception mini-game using Watch/phone for accuracy.
	•	This triangulates sensibility, accuracy, and awareness → publishable, CEU-approvable, and differentiates you from generic wellness apps.
