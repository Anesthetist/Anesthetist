## 🧠 WHY A GRAPH DATABASE?

Unlike a traditional database (tables, rows, columns), a **graph database** (like Neo4j, ArangoDB, or Amazon Neptune) is optimized for **relationships**—the very thing your work is about.

- Nodes = **States, interventions, users, tags, rituals**
- Edges = **"leads to", "is linked with", "strengthens", "is contraindicated by", etc.**
- Queries = **Pattern detectors, journey mappers, and resonance pathfinders**

Graph structure allows you to map not just **what things are**, but **how they interact**—across time, people, contexts, and interventions.
