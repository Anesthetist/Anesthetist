## Contact centers (enterprise)

- AHT savings = (ΔAHT seconds × calls/day × days/mo ÷ 3600) × fully-loaded $/hr  
- FCR savings = (ΔFCR × calls/day × days/mo) × cost per repeat call  
- Attrition savings = (ΔAttrition × headcount × replacement cost) ÷ 12
