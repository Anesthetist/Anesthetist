## 🟢 **WEEK 1: ANCHOR (Breath & Presence)**

### Card 1: **Balloon Belly**
🎯 *Diaphragm awareness + vagal tone*

⏱️ _Script:_  
“Place your hands gently on your stomach.  
Close your lips.  
Breathe in through your nose slowly for 4…  
Feel your belly *inflate like a balloon*.  
Now exhale through your nose for 6…  
Let your balloon soften.  
Repeat this rhythm.  
Now smile a little… as if you’re grateful for breath.”

🌀 *Level Up:*  
Count down instead of up. Add “sigh” on exhale to engage vagus.

🔬 *Neuro Notes:*  
- Activates **diaphragmatic breath**
- Enhances **parasympathetic tone** (HRV↑)
- Increases **insula activity** (body mapping)

---

### Card 2: **Feet Like Mountains**
🎯 *Grounding + present-moment anchoring*

⏱️ _Script:_  
“Stand or sit tall. Feel your **feet** touch the floor.  
Now imagine roots growing down into the ground.  
Breathe in and silently say, _‘I am here.’_  
Breathe out, _‘I am safe.’_  
One more time.  
Feel your body *settle*. Like a mountain.  
Still. Quiet. Strong.”

🌀 *Level Up:*  
Ask: “Can you feel your pulse in your toes?”

🔬 *Neuro Notes:*  
- Activates **somatosensory cortex**
- Recruits **mPFC** via affirmation loop
- Down-regulates amygdala threat signals

## 🟡 **WEEK 2: AWAKEN (Interoception & Attention)**

### Card 3: **Fingertip Sparks**
🎯 *Sensory acuity + body awareness*

⏱️ _Script:_  
“Hold your hands in front of you, palms up.  
Now focus just on your fingertips.  
Can you feel warmth? Buzzing? Air?  
Imagine each finger glowing…  
Breathe in and *feel the glow grow*.  
Breathe out and smile at your hands.  
Your body’s always listening.”

🌀 *Level Up:*  
Ask someone else to match your hand position—mirror neurons activate.

🔬 *Neuro Notes:*  
- Enhances **insula and S1 cortex**
- Strengthens **interoceptive accuracy**
- Supports **co-regulation** through mirror systems

---

### Card 4: **Candle Eyes**
🎯 *Visual field expansion + parasympathetic drop*

⏱️ _Script:_  
“Stare softly at a point across the table, like a candle flame.  
Now without moving your eyes, see how *wide* your vision can go.  
Notice your sides… the ceiling… movement.  
This is ‘soft focus.’  
Breathe gently.  
Your brain is listening through your eyes.  
Now blink slowly… once. Twice. Let go.”

🌀 *Level Up:*  
Add a gentle hum while in peripheral vision for vagal double-down.

🔬 *Neuro Notes:*  
- Engages **right hemisphere attention**
- Lowers **sympathetic tone** (safety signaling)
- Enhances **thalamic sensory filtering**
