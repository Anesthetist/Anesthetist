3. **Social Strength** – cultivating meaningful relationships, trust, and support networks.
4. **Spiritual Strength** – developing a strong sense of purpose, values alignment, and deeper meaning in life.
5. **Family Strength** – supporting stable family relationships, communication, and resilience in family dynamics.

The primary intervention is the **Master Resilience Training (MRT)** program, rooted in positive psychology principles. MRT emphasizes skill-building in mental toughness, stress management, emotional intelligence, cognitive reframing, and practical coping strategies. Through targeted training, soldiers and families develop sustained resilience, improving their ability to function effectively and maintain psychological health under prolonged and intense stressors.
