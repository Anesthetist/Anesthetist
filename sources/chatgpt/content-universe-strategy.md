## 2. Four candidate “topic universes” for Somnistics

Think of these as four potential “beats” you could own. You can blend, but you probably want one primary.

---

### Topic Universe A  
**“Gap Moment Training for High-Stakes Clinicians”**

- **Why it’s interesting to them**
  - They already *live* in gap moments: pre-induction, between cases, before speaking with a difficult family.
  - It promises something specific: *better decisions and steadier hands in 60 seconds*.

- **Your differentiated take**
  - You define gap moments rigorously (not just “take a pause”).
  - Rooted in anesthesia vigilance, HRV, CO₂ tolerance, interoception.
  - Nurse-led, OR-tested, not wellness-theory.

- **Size / depth**
  You’d never run out of material:
  - Gap moments *before*, *during*, *after* cases
  - Gap moments in parenting, grief, leadership
  - Protocol breakdowns (“The 1-Minute Pre-Intubation Reset”)
  - Case studies (de-identified) showing what changed when someone trained this way
  - “Gap Moment Playbooks” for:
    - SRNAs
    - solo providers in rural ORs
    - charge nurses
    - night shift ICU

This topic is closest to your core IP and easiest to turn into CEUs and product on-ramps.
