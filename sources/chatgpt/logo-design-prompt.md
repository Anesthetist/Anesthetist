⸻

Somnistics Research Labs, Inc — Logo Design Prompt (Jared)

Design a primary logo for Somnistics Research Labs, Inc, a nurse-led neuroscience company that builds precision tools for gap moment training—60-second nervous system resets for clinicians under extreme pressure (OR, ICU, trauma).

Core idea: “Calm precision in the tightest window of time.” We sit at the intersection of neuroscience, breath, and liminal states—transitions between chaos and control, overwhelm and clarity. The mark should feel like a trustworthy scientific lab and a subtle nervous system ritual at the same time.

Visual direction:
	•	Style: minimal, modern, flat vector, no gradients, highly legible at tiny sizes (favicon, app icon) and on scrubs or badges.
	•	Form: abstract symbol + clean wordmark. Explore motifs like:
	•	a continuous loop / ouroboros-like ring that subtly suggests a brain hemisphere or orbit,
	•	bridging arcs suggesting left/right brain integration or two systems connecting,
	•	a precise “gap” or notch in a circle or ring to represent a critical moment of intervention,
	•	gentle waveforms / breath curves / HRV rhythm integrated into a circle or bridge.
	•	Avoid clichés: no literal brains, ECG squiggles slapped on, yoga lotuses, or cheesy medical crosses. We’re clinical and future-forward, not “wellness spa.”

Tone & personality:
	•	Keywords: precise, calm, intelligent, trustworthy, liminal, nurse-led, high-stakes, future-clinical.
	•	Should feel:
	•	as reliable as an OR monitor,
	•	as calming as one perfect breath before intubation,
	•	as forward-looking as an AI research lab.

Color palette:
	•	Base: deep midnight navy (#0F1A2B or similar) for trust, depth, and clinical seriousness.
	•	Accent: intelligent teal/cyan (#17C7C1 or similar) to signal neuro, biofeedback, and adaptive tech.
	•	Design so the mark still works in pure black and white (single-color embroidery, print, and icon use).

Typography:
	•	Wordmark: SOMNISTICS as the hero, with “Research Labs, Inc.” as a secondary line when needed.
	•	Typeface style: geometric or humanist sans serif; clean, confident, slightly technical but not cold. Think “next-gen medical device company,” not “startup toy app.”

Output requirements:
	•	Provide:
	•	primary horizontal lockup (symbol + wordmark),
	•	stacked/square version for app icon and social,
	•	monochrome version.
	•	Background: light and dark variations (on white and on deep navy).

Overall, create a logo system that could sit comfortably beside top-tier med-tech and AI research brands, while clearly signaling: “We train nervous systems for the moments that matter most.”
