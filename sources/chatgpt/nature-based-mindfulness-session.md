Designing a mindfulness and focus session that incorporates interoceptive awareness, nature-based elements, and specific breathing techniques like the anesthetist's breath sounds like a wonderful idea. Here's a structured plan for such a session:

### Session Structure

#### Duration: 
Approximately 15-20 minutes

#### Environment: 
Choose a quiet, natural setting if possible. This could be a garden, a park, or even a quiet room with natural elements like plants or natural light.

#### Preparation:
- Ensure comfortable seating, either on a chair with feet flat on the ground or on a cushion on the floor in a cross-legged position.
- Wear comfortable clothing.
- Have a timer or a gentle alarm set for the duration of the session.

#### Session Flow:

1. **Interoceptive Awareness (3-4 minutes):**
   - Begin by sitting comfortably and closing your eyes.
   - Bring your attention to the sensations within your body. Notice the subtle feelings of your heartbeat, the temperature of your skin, and any sensations in your stomach, chest, or throat.
   - If your mind wanders, gently bring it back to these internal sensations.

2. **Nature-Based Mindfulness (5 minutes):**
   - Open your eyes and focus on the natural elements around you (if indoors, focus on a plant or a natural object).
   - Observe the details - the colors, textures, and movements.
   - Engage other senses - listen to any natural sounds (like leaves rustling), feel the air on your skin, or the ground beneath you.

3. **Focus and Attention Exercise (3-4 minutes):**
   - Choose a natural element in your environment (a leaf, a flower, a rock).
   - Concentrate all your attention on this object. Notice its details, its imperfections, its beauty.
   - Every time your mind drifts, acknowledge it and gently return your focus to the object.

4. **Anesthetist's Breath (3-4 minutes):**
   - Transition to focusing on your breath.
   - Inhale slowly through your nose for a count of 70 seconds.
   - Exhale slowly through your nose for a count of 10 seconds.
   - This slow breathing will require full concentration and control, aligning your focus with your breath.

5. **Closing (1-2 minutes):**
   - Gently transition your awareness back to your surroundings.
   - Reflect on the experience, acknowledging any thoughts or feelings that arose.
   - Open your eyes slowly, taking a moment before standing up.

### Additional Tips:
- It's okay if your mind wanders; the practice is in bringing it back.
- Adjust the timing as needed to suit your comfort level.
- You can incorporate gentle background sounds of nature if it helps in focusing.

Remember, mindfulness and focus practices are highly individual, so feel free to modify this plan to better suit your needs. The key is regular practice and patience.
