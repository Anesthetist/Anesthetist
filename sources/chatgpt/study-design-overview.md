# 🧠 MUSE–IONS Meditative Practice Study  
*A Large-Scale Citizen Science Project*  

### 📌 Meeting Purpose  
To introduce and explain the **IONS–MUSE Meditative Practice Study**, a 2,000-participant citizen science project exploring how different types of meditation affect cognition and emotion.  

---

### ✅ Key Takeaways  
- **2,000 participants** will be recruited.  
- Each participant is **randomly assigned** to 1 of 5 meditation types.  
- **15 minutes per day** for **28 days** (April 30–May 27, 2025).  
- Pre- and post-assessments will measure **cognitive and emotional outcomes**.  
- After completion, participants gain **access to all 5 meditation recordings**.  

---

### 📚 Background on Meditation Research  
- **Global practice:** 200–500M people meditate worldwide.  
- **Research surge:** Rapid growth over the last 20 years, now plateauing.  
- **Proven benefits:** psychiatric disorders, chronic pain, sleep quality, physiology.  
- **Common elements:** self-observation, attention training, process focus.  
- **Mechanisms:** attention regulation, body awareness, emotion regulation, perspective shifts.  

---

### 🧠 Brain Changes from Meditation  
- **Short-term:** measurable changes after as little as 8 weeks.  
- **Long-term:** structural/functional changes in emotional, memory, and self-referential circuits.  
- **Key areas:** amygdala, prefrontal cortex, hippocampus, DMN.  
- **2016 meta-analysis:** 78 neuroimaging studies → differences across meditation types.  

---

### 🔬 IONS–MUSE Study Design  
- **Citizen science format** — accessible, scalable, participatory.  
- **Steps:** consent → screening → baseline measures → randomization → practice → final assessment.  
- **Five meditation types:**  
  1. Mindfulness  
  2. Mantra  
  3. Non-dual awareness  
  4. Embodiment  
  5. Classical music (control condition)  
- **Daily protocol:** 15 min/day × 28 days.  
- **Assessments:** questionnaires + cognitive tasks.
