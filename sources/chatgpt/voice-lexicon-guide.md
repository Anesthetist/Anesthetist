### 2. Syntax & Grammar Guidelines
- Prefer **short, rhythmically precise sentences** punctuated by the occasional long, lyrical one.  
- Use **parallel structure** (“breathe, notice, return”) to create a cadence that mimics nervous system regulation.  
- Integrate **embodied verbs** — *feel, sense, anchor, regulate, soften, return, release.*  
- Avoid abstraction unless it’s followed by embodiment. (E.g., “Consciousness is not an idea — it’s a pulse you can feel in your chest.”)  
- Use **first-person plural** (“we,” “our”) when speaking as SRL; **first-person singular** when sharing founder perspective.
