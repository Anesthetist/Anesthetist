**SOC 2—The 30-Second Primer**

- **What it is.**  An **AICPA attestation** (not a certification) that an independent CPA firm issues after auditing how a service organization designs (Type I) and **operates over time** (Type II) its controls.  
- **Why it matters.**  Proves to enterprise buyers, investors, and regulators that you systematically safeguard customer data. It’s often a **must-have for B2B SaaS deals** and short-lists you with hospital systems and payors.  
- **Scope.**  Controls are mapped to five Trust Services Criteria—**Security** (mandatory), plus optional **Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy**. You choose which are relevant.  
- **Process.**  ▸ Define policies and risk-based controls ▸ Collect evidence (logs, tickets, screenshots) ▸ Auditor tests design (Type I) and effectiveness over 3–12 months (Type II) ▸ Receive report + management letter ▸ Repeat annually.  
- **Cost & effort.**  Budget ~$15-60 k for readiness + audit, 3-6 months of prep; automate with tools like Vanta or Secureframe to cut friction.  
- **Relationship to HIPAA.**  SOC 2 focuses on **how** you implement controls; HIPAA defines **what** you must protect. Doing SOC 2 well gives you ~80 % of the technical/administrative groundwork for HIPAA compliance—critical for Pausality’s healthcare data flow.
