# Somnistics Flow System  

## **Yearly Flow (Vision & Stakes)**  
- **Anchor:** “Re-embody humanity one breath at a time.”  
- **Focus:**  
  - Big bets: IP capture, category creation (Gap-Moment Training), enterprise pilots, CRNA adoption curve.  
  - Narrative: one Hero’s Journey arc → *Chaos → Practice → Elixir*.  
- **Deliverables:**  
  - Annual Hero’s Journey update (CEO voice).  
  - Major conference appearances (AANA, HLTH, ExO).  
  - Year-end investor/advisor letter.  

---

## **Quarterly Flow (Strategic Execution)**  
- **Anchor:** *Story cycles in 4 beats* → Category, Proof, Scale, Vision.  
- **Focus:**  
  - Quarterly OKRs mapped to CAC:LTV, ARR, adoption.  
  - Alternating LinkedIn narratives (Jason: investors/advisors/enterprise; Randy: clinical/peer/community).  
  - Quarterly research drops (peer validation, ontology layers, IP filings).  
- **Deliverables:**  
  - 12-week publishing calendar.  
  - Quarterly impact report (internal + external).  
  - 1 “big splash” media or keynote per quarter.  

---

## **Weekly Flow (Operational Rhythm)**  
- **Anchor:** *Two external signals + one internal pulse*.  
- **Focus:**  
  - **CEO (Randy):** 1 clinical/peer-facing post.  
  - **CXO (Jason):** 1 investor/advisor-facing post.  
  - **CMO:** 1 channel/enterprise/community nurture touch (email, article, clip).  
- **Deliverables:**  
  - Weekly publishing schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri).  
  - Team sync → what worked, what to double-down on.  
  - One “gap-moment practice” shared internally (keeps team aligned to product ethos).  

---

## **Daily Flow (Execution + Identity)**  
- **Anchor:** *Ship something daily.*  
- **Focus:**  
  - **CEO:** Micro-ships (NeuroMinute scripts, impact filters, ontology refinements, narrative testing).  
  - **CXO:** Design/UX iteration, investor touchpoint, or founder community exchange.  
  - **CMO:** Micro-analytics pulse → what landed yesterday, adjust copy/CTAs.  
- **Deliverables:**  
  - One tangible output per role, per day.  
  - Reflection: “What’s working? How do we scale it?”  
  - Daily practice: Use Pausality yourself — embody the product.

## Why this Works (System-Level)  
- **Consistency → Compounding.** Campaigns aren’t one-off; they stack into reputation, IP, and adoption.  
- **Multi-horizon coherence.** Annual vision, quarterly arcs, weekly publishing, daily shipping all reinforce one another.  
- **Embodiment.** The team lives its own product — Gap-Moment training becomes culture, not just market pitch.
