## 1. What ADIE is, in concrete terms

**Full name:** Aligning Dimensions of Interoceptive Experience (ADIE)  
**Paper:** Quadt et al., *EClinicalMedicine*, 2021.citeturn0view0  

### Population & design

- **N = 121** autistic adults (18–65) with clinically significant anxiety.  
- Randomized **1:1** to:
  - **ADIE interoceptive training** (6 sessions)  
  - **Active “exteroceptive” control** = emotional prosody identification training.  
- Outcome: **Trait anxiety (STAI-T)** at 3 months post-treatment (T2), intention-to-treat.citeturn0view0  

### Core mechanism

ADIE is intentionally built on Garfinkel’s three “dimensions” of interoception:

1. **Interoceptive accuracy (IAc)**  
   - Objective performance on heartbeat tasks.

2. **Interoceptive sensibility (IS)**  
   - How sensitive you *believe* you are (questionnaires, self-report).

3. **Interoceptive trait prediction error (ITPE)**  
   - The *mismatch* between IAc and IS (over- or under-estimating your bodily sensitivity).  
   - Larger ITPE in autism is linked to more anxiety and emotion difficulties.citeturn0search4turn0search7turn0search12  

**ADIE’s whole point**:  
> Reduce that mismatch (ITPE) by giving people direct, calibrated experience of their own heart signals with feedback.

## 2. What happens inside an ADIE session
