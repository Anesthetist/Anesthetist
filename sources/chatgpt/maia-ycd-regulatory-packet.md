## 1. What this packet actually is

- The full MAIA‑YCD = **32 cards / 8 scales** (Noticing, Not‑Distracting, Not‑Worrying + the 5 regulatory scales).
- This packet keeps only **22 cards / 5 scales**:
  1. Attention Regulation
  2. Emotional Awareness
  3. Self‑Regulation
  4. Body Listening
  5. Trustingfileciteturn0file0  

Rationale:

- These 5 are **most likely to change** with targeted interventions and are more directly tied to **self‑regulation**.
- Internal consistency is acceptable for all five (α ≈ .69–.78). The three attentional scales (Noticing, Not‑Distracting, Not‑Worrying) are psychometrically weaker in youth but still useful in some research contexts; they’re just not part of this “regulatory” packet.fileciteturn0file0

## 2. The five regulatory dimensions (with their items)

From **Appendix A** (page 4):fileciteturn0file0  

**1. Attention Regulation**  
*Ability to sustain and control attention to body sensations* (Cards 11–17; α = .78)

- Focus on breathing without distraction  
- Focus on body even with a lot going on  
- Notice posture while talking  
- Return attention to body after distraction  
- Shift from thinking to feeling body  
- Attend to whole body even when part is in pain  
- Focus on entire body when trying  

---

**2. Emotional Awareness**  
*Awareness of the connection between body sensations and emotional states* (Cards 18–22; α = .70)

- Feel body changes when angry  
- Sense “something is wrong” in life via body  
- Notice body difference after peaceful moments  
- Feel breathing become freer when comfortable  
- Feel body changes when happy  

---

**3. Self‑Regulation**  
*Ability to regulate distress by attention to body sensations* (Cards 23–26; α = .70)

- Feel calm even with a lot going on  
- Calming down by focusing on body  
- Use breath to calm and relax  
- Calm racing mind by focusing on body/breathing  

---

**4. Body Listening**  
*Active listening to the body for insight* (Cards 27–29; α = .69)

- Listen for body clues about emotions  
- When upset, check how body feels  
- Use body sensations to choose what to do  

---

**5. Trusting**  
*Experience of one’s body as safe and trustworthy* (Cards 30–32; α = .78)

- Feel good in body  
- Feel body is a safe place  
- Trust how body feels
