## 1. Influencer Marketing That Actually Works  

90% of your reach will come from <10% of posts.  
Your job is to *find* those breakout hooks, formats, and voices.  

**Most founders screw up by:**  
1. Spending too little (you need $10–20k/month for at least 6 months).  
2. Being overly picky with creators.  
3. Quitting too early.  

The system we built:  
- **Go broad.** Test dozens of micro-influencers across TikTok, IG, X, and LinkedIn.  
- **Track the outliers.** Don’t just ask “what platform works?”—ask *which creators, hooks, and formats* work.  
- **Pay base + viral bonus.** Align incentives with upside.  
- **Exploit new accounts.** Especially on TikTok—fresh accounts can outperform established ones.  
- **Capture learnings.** Every viral video went into a living playbook. Once we had 20–30 proven angles, we hired our top creators to teach others.  

Pro tip: never write scripts for creators. Scripts kill authenticity. The creator knows their audience better than you ever will.

## 2. Brand Before Performance  

Here’s the mistake: founders think they can scale ads before their brand and creative are dialed in.  

We tried. It was a disaster. CAC skyrocketed. We ended up doing an expensive rebrand (both time + money). Painful, but necessary.  

**What worked instead:**  
- Test 10x more creatives than you think you need.  
- Get bold and weird. Most ideas fail, but a few will transform your funnel.  
- Align your funnel. If the ad promises one value prop but the landing page shows another, conversion tanks.  
- Double down on resonant use cases. Once you find a storyline that works, build a whole journey around it—ads, landing page, onboarding, upsell.  

Brand is leverage. Every dollar spent on performance marketing amplifies or dies based on how strong the brand foundation is.
