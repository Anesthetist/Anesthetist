- **Midday (10 minutes)**: Incorporate more intensive breathwork during a break in daily activities.
- **Evening (10 minutes)**: End the day with relaxation-focused breathing to enhance recovery and sleep quality.

#### Notes:
- **Safety**: Continuously monitor for signs of discomfort or overexertion.
- **Adaptability**: Adjust the intensity of the exercises based on the goalie's response and feedback.
- **Integration**: Encourage the application of these breathing techniques during training and gameplay.
- **Progress Tracking**: Record CO2 levels and subjective experiences to track improvement.

This program is designed to be flexible, allowing the goalie to engage in effective breathwork exercises without disrupting their daily routine. The focus is on gradually building CO2 tolerance and integrating these techniques into everyday life, enhancing overall respiratory efficiency and performance under stress.
