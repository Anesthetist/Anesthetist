### Using Quadratic Funding (QF) to Structure a Friends-and-Family Round

Quadratic Funding blends two pools of capital:

1. **Direct contributions** from individual backers (your friends and family).  
2. **A matching pool** supplied by you, an anchor investor, or the company treasury.

The match each project (or, in this case, *your company*) receives is proportional to *the square of the sum of the square roots of individual contributions*.  In plain language: **many modest backers unlock more match dollars than a few large checks of the same total size**.  This amplifies “broad-based conviction” while still rewarding bigger supporters.
