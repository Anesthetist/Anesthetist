## **D1. Study Rationale**

CRNAs operate at the convergence of sustained vigilance, cognitive load, and emotional containment.  
While their clinical monitoring skills are advanced, their *self-monitoring capacity*—interoceptive awareness, HRV coherence, and fatigue detection—remains largely unmeasured.  
Preliminary ethnographic data (Appendix A–C) indicate chronic sympathetic activation, moral stress, and early physical decline.  

**Goal:** Quantify and improve interoceptive accuracy and physiological regulation in CRNAs through micro-intervention training (NeuroMinute™ / Pausality).  

---

## **D2. Objectives**

1. **Primary:** Determine the relationship between interoceptive awareness (MAIA-2) and clinical vigilance performance (standardized vigilance task).  
2. **Secondary:** Assess whether 21 days of micro-intervention training improve HRV, reaction-time stability, and burnout scores (MBI-EE).  
3. **Exploratory:** Model how moral distress and financial stress correlate with physiologic dysregulation.
