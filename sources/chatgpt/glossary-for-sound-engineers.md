- **Definition:** Short, often overlooked pauses in daily life (e.g., waiting for an elevator, walking to a meeting) that can be used for intentional mindfulness practices.

### **2. Diaphragmatic Blindness**  
- **Definition:** A lack of awareness or ability to consciously control diaphragmatic (deep belly) breathing, which is crucial for regulating the nervous system.  
- **Relevance:** Many of our exercises aim to retrain users to regain control and awareness of this core breath mechanism.  
- **Analogy for Sound Engineers:** Similar to losing the ability to modulate a key frequency in an audio mix; bringing it back into focus changes the whole track’s resonance.

---

### **3. Subtle Body Training**  
- **Definition:** Practices aimed at developing awareness of subtle physiological signals, such as the pulse, heartbeat, or areas of tension in the body.  
- **Relevance:** Helps users develop interoception (internal body awareness), which is tied to stress regulation and mindfulness.
