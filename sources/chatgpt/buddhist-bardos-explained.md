The concept of Bardos originates from Tibetan Buddhism and is a core aspect of its eschatology. The term "Bardo" means "intermediate state" or "transitional state." Here's a simplified breakdown of the six Bardos and their significance:

1. **Bardo of This Life (Kyene Bardo):** 
   - This Bardo encompasses the human experience from birth to death. 
   - It's an opportunity for spiritual practice and self-realization.

2. **Bardo of Dream (Milam Bardo):**
   - This phase includes the dream state during sleep.
   - It can be utilized for spiritual practices like dream yoga.

3. **Bardo of Meditation (Samten Bardo):**
   - This is the meditative state, where one can experience deeper states of consciousness.

4. **Bardo of Dying (Chikhai Bardo):**
   - This Bardo begins at the moment of death and encompasses the dying process.
   - It's believed that during this phase, one's true nature, "the luminous mind," is revealed.

5. **Bardo of Dharmata (Chonyi Bardo):**
   - This phase begins after death and includes visions and experiences reflecting one's karmic patterns.

6. **Bardo of Existence (Sipa Bardo):**
   - This is the phase before rebirth, where one's next incarnation is determined based on past karma.

Understanding and navigating these Bardos is a significant aspect of Tibetan Buddhist practice, aiming to attain liberation from the cycle of birth and death.
