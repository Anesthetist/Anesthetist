### 🧠 1. Brief Vagal Reset  
**Target:** Enhance dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC) regulation; interrupt stress-triggered habit loops.  
**Practice:**  
- Sit or stand upright.  
- Inhale quietly for 4 sec, exhale forcefully for 6 sec (emphasize exhale).  
- Simultaneously, pull gently on one side of your index fingertip as you exhale—this stimulates afferent vagal fibers via the baroreflex.  
- Repeat 6 cycles (~60 sec).  
**Why it works:** Extended exhalation activates the parasympathetic nervous system and vagus nerve, boosting HRV and PFC engagement, countering stress-induced downward shifts.

---

### ⚙️ 2. Task Interruption + Micro‑Detail Attention  
**Target:** Disrupt automatic habit-mode mediated by dorsal striatum and reinforce cognitive control.  
**Practice:**  
- Pause your current task.  
- Pick up any pen or object.  
- For 60 seconds, focus intently on micro-details: texture, weight, temperature, tiny imperfections.  
- Name aloud one unique feature per second.  
**Why it works:** Shifting attention to novel, sensory detail halts habit-laden processing, compelling the rostrolateral PFC to re-engage.

---

### 💭 3. Precision Intention Frame  
**Target:** Strengthen goal maintenance in the frontoparietal network under stress.  
**Practice:**  
- Inhale for 3 sec, hold for 2 sec, exhale for 5 sec.  
- During the hold, mentally repeat a precise “if–then” intention tied to your task (e.g., _“If I’m distracted, I will refocus on the next smallest step.”_)  
- Continue for about 6 full breaths (~60 sec).  
**Why it works:** This combines paced breathing (reinforcing autonomic stability) with implementation intentions, priming prefrontal circuits for goal-related flexibility.
