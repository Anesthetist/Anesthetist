## 1. Background on Pupillometry

**Pupillometry** measures changes in the size of the pupil over time. These changes are largely governed by:
- **Sympathetic Activation**: Tends to cause pupil dilation.
- **Parasympathetic Activation**: Tends to cause pupil constriction.

Because the autonomic nervous system (ANS) influences both sympathetic and parasympathetic activity, pupillometry serves as a noninvasive way to track real-time fluctuations in ANS balance. Researchers have used it in various fields—ranging from neurology and psychology to physiology—to gauge stress, arousal, and even cognitive load.

---
