In your weekly business planning for both your anesthesia and pain services company and Vagalbeats, you can draw inspiration from Amazon’s approach to ensure that you focus on key input metrics. Here are 10 input metrics to consider:

### 1. **Customer Satisfaction (NPS Scores)**
   - **Why:** Tracking customer satisfaction helps ensure you are meeting or exceeding client expectations, which is crucial for repeat business and referrals.

### 2. **Service Utilization Rates**
   - **Why:** Measure the percentage of your services (anesthesia, pain management, or Vagalbeats sessions) that are being utilized to understand demand and capacity.

### 3. **Patient Outcomes**
   - **Why:** For the anesthesia and pain services, tracking patient recovery times and pain management effectiveness can help improve service quality.

### 4. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
   - **Why:** Knowing how much it costs to acquire a new customer can help you manage and optimize your marketing spend.

### 5. **Conversion Rates**
   - **Why:** Track the percentage of inquiries or leads that convert into paying customers to assess the effectiveness of your sales process.

### 6. **Operational Efficiency**
   - **Why:** Measure metrics like time per procedure (anesthesia) or session (Vagalbeats) to identify areas where you can improve efficiency.

### 7. **Employee Performance and Satisfaction**
   - **Why:** Employee satisfaction can directly impact service quality. Tracking performance and satisfaction helps ensure your team is motivated and productive.

### 8. **Revenue per Customer**
   - **Why:** Monitor how much revenue each customer generates to understand your profitability per customer and identify opportunities for upselling or cross-selling.

### 9. **Churn Rate**
   - **Why:** For both companies, knowing how many customers stop using your services over a specific period helps identify retention issues.

### 10. **Market Penetration**
   - **Why:** Measure your market share within your target markets to understand your competitive position and growth potential.

### Implementing These Metrics
To effectively implement and track these metrics, consider the following steps:

1. **Set Clear Definitions:** Ensure each metric is clearly defined and understood by your team.
2. **Regular Data Collection:** Implement systems to regularly collect data on these metrics.
3. **Weekly Review Meetings:** Establish a routine for reviewing these metrics weekly with your team.
4. **Action Plans:** Develop action plans based on metric trends to continuously improve your business performance.
