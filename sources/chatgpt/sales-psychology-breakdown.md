### 🧠 Summary

> “Good copy doesn’t sell prospects on the solution. It sells them the problem.”

The key idea is **problem priming**. Effective copy creates *felt recognition* — it helps people *see themselves* in the problem, *feel its urgency*, and *self-diagnose* the need to solve it *now*.  
Once this inner recognition is activated, your offer becomes the natural next step — the fastest, most credible path to relief.

---

### 🔬 Mechanism of Action (Psychology of Conversion)

1. **Problem awareness** → Activates salience and relevance networks (anterior cingulate, insula).  
2. **Self-diagnosis** → Engages agency and ownership (dorsolateral prefrontal cortex).  
3. **Urgency framing** → Triggers temporal discounting and motivates immediate action.  
4. **Relief pathfinding** → Reduces cognitive dissonance; the brain seeks closure.  
5. **Solution adoption** → Feels self-chosen, not imposed → intrinsic buy-in.

This sequence mirrors what you already call **Gap Moment Training™** — you’re just applying it to narrative and decision flow instead of physiology.
