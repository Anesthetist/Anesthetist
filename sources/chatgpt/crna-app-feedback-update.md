### Thread the Needle: Weaving Self-Compassion into Every CRNA Checklist

Below is a plug-and-play “overlay” you can tack onto any existing OR or personal workflow checklist.  It adds only seconds—but the neuroscience says that even a brief self-kindness cue down-regulates threat circuitry, stabilizes HRV, and keeps executive function online.

| Checklist Moment | Micro Self-Compassion Cue | Script (≤ 10 sec) | Why It Matters |
|------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **1. Pre-shift gear check** | *Name & normalize* | “Today’s cases are complex; I’m human, and I’ll meet them with skill *and* kindness toward myself.” | Sets baseline tone; frames challenges as shared human experience. |

| **5. End-of-day close-out** | *Credit & close* | Log one thing you did well; thank yourself for showing up. Physically remove badge while exhaling through pursed lips. | Reinforces gain mindset; creates clear boundary between work and home. |
