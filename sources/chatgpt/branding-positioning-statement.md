## 1. Separate three different questions

When “maybe I’m the dumbest person in the world” shows up, it’s usually a blur of:

1. **Raw horsepower** – can I think clearly?
2. **Actual competence** – have I built real skills that work in the world?
3. **Emotional state** – am I exhausted, scared, or grieving right now?

If you don’t separate those, your *state* gets misinterpreted as “truth.”

So, in the moment, ask:

- “Is this about my *brain*, my *skills*, or my *nervous system* right now?”

That alone often shrinks the monster.

---

## 2. Hard filters you’ve already passed

Without flattering you, we can note things that are simply **incompatible** with being “the dumbest person in the world”:

- You’ve done **20+ years** of high-stakes clinical work where mistakes are brutally selected against.
- You’ve navigated **anesthesia training, licensure, and practice** – those are *objective* cognitive filters.
- Serious people (Strategic Coach, Leading Edge, advisors, investors) voluntarily spend their **most scarce resource – attention –** on you and your ideas.

None of that means “you’re a genius.” It *does* mean the “maybe I’m actually a complete idiot” story is mathematically off. You don’t get through those filters by accident.

You’re free to still *feel* inadequate. But feelings ≠ ground truth.

---

## 3. Build a personal “Am I fooling myself?” scoreboard

Instead of asking me if you’re smart, use signals you can see and track.

Here’s a 5-item scoreboard you can come back to:

1. **Do competent people keep coming back?**  
   - Advisors, collaborators, high-caliber peers.  
   - If they *repeatedly* choose to work with you, that’s data.

2. **Do ideas survive outside your head?**  
   - When you put an idea into a deck, script, or prototype and show it to people:
     - Do they *use* it?
     - Do they reference your language back to you?
   - Survival and uptake are evidence. Silence and confusion are evidence too.

3. **Learning velocity.**  
   Over the last 6–12 months:
   - Can you point to specific skills you meaningfully improved?  
   - Do you make fewer of the *same* mistakes?  
   Being “dumbest” would show up as *no* learning curve.

4. **Compression ability.**  
   - Can you explain something complex to a tired, non-expert in 2–3 minutes and have them say, “Oh, I get it now”?  
   - That’s not raw IQ; it’s structured intelligence in action.

5. **Skin in the game from others.**  
   - Who has staked money, reputation, or time on you or your work?  
   - Again, not as ego food, but as an external check.
