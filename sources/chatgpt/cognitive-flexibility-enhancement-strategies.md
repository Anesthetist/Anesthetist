### The Brain Networks That Power Creativity

Creativity isn’t the domain of a single brain hemisphere. Instead, it’s the result of dynamic interactions among three key brain networks:

1. **Default Mode Network (DMN):** Often referred to as the “Imagination Network,” the DMN is activated during daydreaming, mental simulations, and imagining alternative scenarios.
   
2. **Executive Attention Network (EAN):** This network is the brain’s laser focus system, helping us solve complex problems and critically evaluate ideas.
   
3. **Salience Network (SN):** Acting as a switchboard, the SN determines which network (DMN or EAN) is most relevant in any given moment and toggles between them based on the task.

Recent research reveals that creative individuals excel at **switching efficiently** between these networks. This means balancing moments of uninhibited imagination with focused evaluation—like a pendulum swinging smoothly between divergent thinking and practical reasoning.
