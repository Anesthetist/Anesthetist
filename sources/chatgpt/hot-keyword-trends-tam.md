## 0) The “Template Forge” SOP (turn wins into repeatable process)
**Purpose:** Convert any effective asset (post, email, landing page, NeuroMinute) into a template within 24 hours.

1) **Select the win**  
- Trigger: asset hits ≥1 success metric (see Metrics below).  
- Save screenshots, links, and raw copy.

2) **Deconstruct**  
- What was the **hook → promise → proof → path**?  
- List steps you took (even tiny ones). Don’t polish—capture reality.

3) **Abstract**  
- Replace specifics with variables: `{Audience}`, `{Pain}`, `{Mechanism}`, `{Outcome}`, `{CTA}`.  
- Keep rhythm & section lengths.

4) **Package**  
- Name: `T-[type]-[primary_use]-v1` (e.g., `T-Post-60sReset-v1`).  
- Add a 3-bullet usage note: when to use, when not to, common pitfalls.

5) **Test & store**  
- Use it once the same day. If it works, bump to `v1.1`.  
- Store in your Template Library (ClickUp/Notion) with tags.

## 1) Daily Content Template (≤300 words)
**Goal:** Ship fast, build trust, drive 1 clear action.
