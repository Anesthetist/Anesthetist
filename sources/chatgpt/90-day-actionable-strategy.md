Interest rates — what banks charge to borrow money — are staying **high for a long time**.  
That means **money costs more**, so investors and companies are being very careful about where they put it.  
Everyone now wants **real results, not just big promises.**

If you run a company or project (like Somnistics), this means:
- You’ll have to prove you can **make money and save money**.
- You’ll need to **show evidence** that what you do works.
