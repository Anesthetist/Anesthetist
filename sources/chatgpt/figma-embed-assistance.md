### Integration of Key Principles:
- **Focus and Attention**: Use visual and auditory cues to direct user focus on preparation steps.
- **Priming**: The serene imagery and calming tone set a relaxed state of mind.
- **Consistency**: Simple, sequential steps to ensure users follow through with preparation.
- **Social Proof**: Mention that many others have found success with these preparation steps.
- **Positive Reinforcement**: Encouragement and positive messages to motivate users.
- **Flow State**: Creating a seamless transition to the therapy session to facilitate immersion.
- **Behavioral Economics (Nudge)**: Subtle prompts guide users towards optimal preparation behavior.
- **Emotional Intelligence**: Encouraging users to self-regulate and prepare emotionally and mentally.

This onboarding process is designed to effectively prepare users in a short amount of time, ensuring they are ready to fully benefit from the 60-second VagalBeats therapy session.
