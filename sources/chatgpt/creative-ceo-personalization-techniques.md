## 🧩 The SRL Equity Calibration Formula

### 1. Define the *Role Container*  
- **Taking equity:** Document exactly what you *will* and *will not* do. Scope, time, decision authority, limits.  
- **Giving equity:** Define the *minimum bar* for contribution. What behaviors, hours, capital, or expertise justify it?

📌 **CEO prompt:** *“If I disappeared tomorrow, could the other party still know what I owed them and what they owed me?”*

---

### 2. Assign Value, Not Fantasy  
- **Test: Replaceability.** How easily could you hire or contract this contribution?  
- **Test: Timing.** Is their contribution front-loaded (e.g., cash), or compounding (e.g., network access, expertise, IP)?  
- **Test: Conversion.** Will their input directly convert into ARR, capital raised, or protected IP?  

📌 **CEO prompt:** *“If I had to buy this same contribution on the open market, what % would I pay?”*

---

### 3. Equity = Exchange, Not Emotion  
- Only grant or accept equity equal to the *net present value* of what’s delivered.  
- Resist “friendship equity” (the #1 cause of disputes).  
- Bake in cliffs/vesting so that promises don’t become dead weight.  

📌 **CEO prompt:** *“Am I paying in equity for hope, or for delivered, measurable value?”*
