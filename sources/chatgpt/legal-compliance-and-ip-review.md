## 1. Compliance

### 1.1 Medical and Regulatory Framework
- **Scope of Services**: Clarify and document that the offerings are not “medical services.” This typically involves:
  - Clear disclaimers in marketing and product documentation.
  - Internal policies that distinguish wellness or educational tools from regulated medical devices or clinical practice.
- **FDA and Similar Agencies**: If any wearable or software component monitors vitals or gives health-related feedback, confirm it does not rise to a level that requires FDA (or comparable) oversight as a medical device.
  - If it does, consider premarket submissions (e.g., 510(k) in the U.S.) or other regulatory pathways.
- **Professional Licensure Issues**: For any content that could be construed as advice in areas such as psychology, psychiatry, or physical therapy, ensure disclaimers are placed to avoid any implication of providing licensed therapy or clinical services.

### 1.2 Data Protection and Privacy 
- **Biometric Data Collection**: If the product collects or processes user data—particularly biometrics (heart rate, EEG, etc.)—you may be subject to privacy laws (e.g., HIPAA in the U.S. if there is a covered entity involved, GDPR in the EU, or state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act).
- **Data Storage and Security**: Establish data-handling protocols (encryption at rest and in transit, secure storage standards) and incorporate them into the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- **Consent and User Agreements**: Ensure users explicitly consent to data collection and usage, especially for any sensitive data. This consent should be separate from general Terms of Service.

### 1.3 Marketing and Advertising Compliance
- **Truth in Advertising**: Claims around performance enhancement or other benefits should be carefully reviewed to avoid false or unsubstantiated advertising.
- **Disclaimers**: Prominently include disclaimers that the tool is for educational and informational use, and not intended to replace professional medical or legal advice.
