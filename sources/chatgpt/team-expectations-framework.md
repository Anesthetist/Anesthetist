**How to be successful working with Randy**

**Expectations**

1. **We measure success by shared outcomes, not individual brilliance.**  
   The team > our mission > your role > your ego. We’re building something meaningful together, and alignment matters more than heroics.

2. **You are trusted, not micromanaged.**  
   I assume you’re competent and committed. If you’re stuck, speak up early—clarity is kind. Silence gets interpreted as alignment, so please don’t default to passive agreement.

3. **Always know and teach the 'why'.**  
   Explaining *why* a decision worked builds reproducibility and trust. Win without insight? It’s luck, not leadership. We are here to build systems, not sandcastles.

4. **Dissent is a form of commitment.**  
   If you disagree, say so—especially if it’s uncomfortable. Silence erodes trust. I honor courage more than consensus.

5. **Feedback is our fuel.**  
   I expect one piece of real, constructive feedback from you monthly. I promise not to take it personally. It’s how we grow. Let’s normalize honesty.

6. **Treat everyone like a client—especially under stress.**  
   Our work touches lives. Whether it's the CEO or a new grad, extend respect, curiosity, and presence. Emotional reactivity is never professionalism.

7. **Own your impact, not just your intent.**  
   We all make mistakes. The fastest way to rebuild trust is to name it, claim it, and fix it. No hiding, no shaming.

8. **Steady leadership beats reactive leadership.**  
   Show up grounded. We use real-time biofeedback and body cues for a reason. When things go sideways, your calm becomes our collective stability.
