2. **Frame Two (10-20 seconds)**: Transition to a first-person perspective. The room fades away, and the person is now suspended in a vast star-filled sky, still lying down. The stars twinkle brightly against the dark sky. The style should be more fantastical and awe-inspiring.

3. **Frame Three (20-30 seconds)**: Switch to a side view and focus on the person's lower body starting from the toes and moving up to the lower legs. Each body part visibly relaxes as the breath leaves the body. The stars can appear to gently pulse with each exhale. The style continues to be dreamy and calming.

4. **Frame Four (30-40 seconds)**: Maintain the first-person perspective, and as they inhale again, the dark sky starts to fill with the colors of a wide, expansive rainbow. The colors flow in the same direction as the inhale, creating a synchronicity between the breath and the visual. The style should be vibrant and full of colors.

5. **Frame Five (40-50 seconds)**: Return to the side view to focus on the upper body - legs, abdomen, and chest - visibly relaxing. The rainbow maintains its vibrant presence in the backdrop. The style here can mirror that of frame three but with the vibrant colors of the rainbow adding an additional layer of calm.

6. **Frame Six (50-60 seconds)**: On the final exhale, transition back to the bird's-eye view and show the person fully relaxed on the bed. Also, switch to a first-person view to visualize the affirmation, "You are always in the right place at the right time." The style can return to soft and dreamy, but now imbued with the vibrant colors of the earlier scenes.

**For the Music Team:**

Compose a relaxing and harmonious soundtrack to accompany the illustrations. The music should begin with a soft tone, mirroring the initial stages of relaxation. As the visuals transition from the city to the star-filled sky, incorporate instruments that evoke a sense of wonder and calm, like soft piano or gentle strings. 

When the rainbow appears, the music can subtly shift to include more vibrant and uplifting tones. By the final scene, the music should reflect a state of complete relaxation and peace, perhaps with a soft, recurring motif that mirrors the rhythm of deep, calming breaths. 

Remember, the goal is to enhance the relaxing and restorative journey of the viewer with your music.
