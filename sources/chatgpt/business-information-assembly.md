## 1. Business Description

**Mission & Vision**  
Somnistics (Pausality) is on a mission to empower high-stakes healthcare professionals (starting with CRNAs) to thrive—by transforming brief “gap moments” during the day into measurable cognitive and physiological gains, thereby preventing burnout, PTSD, and performance decline.

**Problem Statement**  
- CRNAs face extraordinarily high stress, elevated risk of burnout, anxiety, PTSD symptoms, and suicide risk relative to many other medical professions.  
- Traditional wellness apps are too slow, too generalized, not physiological/biofeedback-enabled, and not tailored to the moments when intervention is most needed.  
- The absence of interventions that can be used *in the moment* (between cases, during shifts, in pre-op or post-op transitions) leaves a gap in support.

**Solution / Product & Services**  
- *Pausality App*: integrated with Apple Watch / iPhone / Apple Health; leverages breathing biofeedback + adaptive binaural audio + curated micro-practices for gap moment interventions.  
- “Pre-habilitation” vs reactive wellness: training resilience before crisis rather than after.  
- Content tailored by CRNAs for CRNAs, optimized for use in clinical workflows and high stress environments.

**Business Model & Go-to-Market**  
- Subscription model (consumer & professional tiers)  
- Expansion verticals: other high-stakes healthcare providers, enterprise wellness / hospital systems  
- Marketing via professional organizations, thought leadership, peer referrals
