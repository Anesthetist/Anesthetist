4. **Advertising**: Generating revenue by displaying ads within your product or platform. This approach is often used by companies that offer free services and have a substantial user base.

6. **Transaction Fees**: Taking a percentage of each transaction that occurs through your platform. This model is prevalent in marketplaces and payment processing services.
