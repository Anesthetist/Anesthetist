## **Existing Core Concepts**

### Somnistics  
An interdisciplinary approach to improving human performance and well-being through deliberate practice. It emphasizes exploring and enhancing different states of awareness, internal body signals (interoception), and how the heart, brain, and breathing can work together.

### VagalBeats  
A sound-driven system using specific audio techniques—like binaural beats (two slightly different sound frequencies), affirmations, and focused breathing—to activate the vagus nerve. This is designed to help the body switch from a “fight-or-flight” mode to a calmer, balanced state over time. In medical terms, it can mirror some of the benefits of a stellate ganglion block (SGB)—a treatment that helps regulate the sympathetic nervous system—but here it’s achieved non-invasively through repeated audio and breathing sessions.

### Gap Moments  
Short breaks or transitions in your day that you can use for quick, effective “micro-regulation”—simple actions like a 1-minute breathing exercise that help sharpen focus, stabilize emotions, and strengthen your ability to handle stress.

### Neuro-Hikes / Neuro-Rides / Neuro-Skiing  
Outdoor activities like hiking, biking, or skiing enhanced by a combination of rhythmic nasal breathing, bone-conduction headphones delivering audio cues, and affirmations. The goal is to synchronize movement, breath, and focused attention to boost mental clarity and physical performance.

### Neuro-Cardiac-Respiratory Coherence  
A state in which the nervous system, heart, and breathing work in smooth harmony. This promotes emotional stability, sharper thinking, and calmness.

### Diaphragmatic Blindness  
A tendency to lose awareness of your natural deep breathing (using the diaphragm) due to ongoing stress or poor posture. This can reduce your ability to calm down quickly and may affect overall well-being.
