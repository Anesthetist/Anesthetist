The better this story ties everything together, the more your business will seem insightful [source](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/a-playbook-for-fundraising).

4. **Include Key Metrics**: Include key growth metrics in the main fundraising deck to highlight traction at a high level. This could include a Loom of your product flow explaining how you made key design decisions, an appendix of interesting information that inspired your approach, and a compilation of customer testimonials [source](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/a-playbook-for-fundraising).

6. **Leverage Storytelling**: Storytelling is a critical skill for product leaders. At Productboard, they tell their product story in three acts: How the world was before Productboard, what the world looks like now that Productboard is in it, and how they plan to change the world in the future [source](https://www.productboard.com/blog/evangelizing-product-vision-and-strategy/).

7. **Test Your Pitch**: Prepare your materials to about 70% completeness and then test them before going into battle. Remember, creating a pitch deck is a process of refinement and iteration. Don't be afraid to continually refine your story and presentation based on feedback and new insights.
