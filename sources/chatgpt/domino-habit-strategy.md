### Why “hinge alarms” work  
Temporal-landmark alarms act like neural circuit breakers: they *chunk* the day into clear episodes, lower working-memory load, and trigger a brief orienting response that refreshes attention. Less cognitive drag → easier state-shifts → smoother entry to flow. Cortically, this shows up as reduced midline-DMN chatter and quicker ramp-up of task-positive networks; physiologically, HRV usually blips upward because decision-fatigue drops. For a high-stakes operator like you, that translates to steadier autonomic tone and faster readiness for “gap-moment” micro-practices.
