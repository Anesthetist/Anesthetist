---

### **3. Regulatory / Risk**
- **Wellness vs. Medical**: Apple’s App Store *will* scrutinize claims.

  - If you say “treat PTSD, cure anxiety”—you risk FDA territory.  
- **Privacy**: Explicit, transparent handling of HealthKit data is essential. Apple will reject apps that aren’t clear.

---

### **4. Go-to-Market**

- **Monetization**: Freemium is standard—baseline NeuroMinutes free, premium layered interventions & adaptive AI engine paid.

---

### **5. Pitfalls to Avoid**
- Overbuilding v1 (keep to **watch + iPhone + 1-min audios + HRV feedback**).  
- Claiming too much medically.  
- Neglecting *user delight*: aesthetics, frictionless onboarding, *and* Apple design language are key.  

---
