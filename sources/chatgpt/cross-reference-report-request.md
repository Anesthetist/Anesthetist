# SOMNISTICS CROSS-REFERENCE REPORT (Revised v2)

This document distinguishes:

- **True synonym**: Same construct, same scope.
- **Near match**: Overlapping construct, different scope or mechanism.
- **False friend**: Commonly confused, technically distinct.
- **Category neighbor**: Lives nearby in the conceptual landscape but serves a different purpose.

---

## I. INTEROCEPTION

### Your Core Usage
Interoception = trainable skill of perceiving, interpreting, and modulating internal physiological state in real time.

You use it in three layers:
1. Detection (noticing signals)
2. Interpretation (making sense of signals)
3. Modulation (changing state)

### Academic Baseline Definition
Modern definition (Craig, 2002; Critchley & Garfinkel, 2017):
Interoception refers to the sensing and processing of internal bodily signals, including cardiovascular, respiratory, gastrointestinal, and immune signals.

Often divided into:
- Interoceptive accuracy (objective detection)
- Interoceptive sensibility (self-report)
- Interoceptive awareness (metacognitive insight into accuracy)

### Cross-Reference Map

**True synonym**
- Interoceptive awareness (when used broadly)

**Near matches**
- Somatic awareness (broader, includes proprioception)
- Body awareness (non-specific)
- Visceroperception (older, organ-focused framing)

**False friends**
- Proprioception (position/movement sensing, not internal organ state)
- Mindfulness (attentional stance; may include interoception but not equivalent)

**Category neighbor**
- Alexithymia research (interoceptive deficits in emotional labeling)
- Embodied cognition

### Differentiator Statement

Somnistics does not treat interoception as passive noticing.  
It treats it as a trainable performance variable with operational targets under stress.

That is not the default stance in contemplative science.
