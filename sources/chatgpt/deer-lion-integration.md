### Rumi’s Deer & Lion: a dance of gentleness **and** power  
Rumi loved paradox.  In his imagery the **deer** and **lion** are not enemies but complimentary forces inside a single, awakened heart.  

| Symbol | Traditional Rumi nuance | Neuro-psychological lens | Everyday embodiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deer** | *Mah‐e-narin*—the moon-like, tender soul lured by Divine music. It listens, trusts, and allows itself to be led. | Parasympathetic dominance (vagal tone ↑). | **Lion** | *Asad‐e-Rumi*—the fearless heart that protects truth, hunts falsehood, and roars its devotion. | Sympathetic-parasympathetic integration: a surge of nor-adrenaline **with** pre-frontal regulation.  Courage with clarity. | Upright posture, decisive speech, willingness to set boundaries or take bold action. |

> **“Be the deer and the lion”**  
> Let your heart remain exquisitely sensitive **(deer)** *while* your will acts with uncompromising integrity **(lion)**.  
> Soft awareness without decisive action becomes passivity; raw power without tenderness becomes aggression. Rumi points to the *third way*: fluidly oscillating between receptive stillness and courageous movement, much like healthy heart-rate variability alternates between inhalation (sympathetic) and exhalation (parasympathetic).
