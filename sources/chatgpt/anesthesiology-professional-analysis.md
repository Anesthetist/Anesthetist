**Potential issues with the line “Real-time biometric feedback guides every breath.”**

1. **Implied medical-device claim.**  
   *Guides every breath* can sound like the app is providing clinical respiratory control or therapeutic guidance—territory that regulators (FDA, EU MDR) reserve for cleared medical devices. If Pausality hasn’t undergone such clearance, Apple reviewers may flag the copy for overpromising.

2. **Absolute language (“every breath”).**

3. **Scope creep vs. Apple’s Guideline 5.1.1 (health & safety).**  
   Apple allows “wellness” apps that visualize HR/HRV, but prohibits claims that could be interpreted as diagnosing or treating a health condition. *Guides every breath* edges toward treatment language.
