## WANA Conference Talk — Field Evidence

**Event:** WANA Conference, March 28, 2026
**Speaker:** Randy Graybeal, CRNA
**Title:** Inside Out: Interoception and High-Performance for CRNAs
**Audience:** ~100 CRNAs, students, and educators — nearly all doctorally prepared
**Deck:** https://gamma.app/docs/Inside-Out-Interoception-and-High-Performance-for-CRNAs-5cpi63jgnof40qp

### Audience Response Data

**"Have you heard of interoception?"** — 1 hand, halfway up (out of ~100 doctorally-prepared clinicians)

**"Has anyone had formal breath training?"** — 4 hands total:
- Trumpet player
- Singer
- Swimmer
- Yoga practitioner
- Zero from clinical training pathways

**Heart rate question:** Randy asked "I wonder if my heart rate is higher or lower than the patient's?" — audience laughed, then genuinely wondered. Demonstrates the interoceptive blind spot is universal and unexamined.

**First audience comment:** "I think you're onto something here."

### Somnistic Relevance

This is primary market validation for three vault-canonical first principles:

1. **Diaphragmatic Blindness / Interoceptive Suppression:** 99% of doctorally-prepared CRNAs have never encountered the term "interoception." Formal breath training comes exclusively from non-clinical domains (music, sports, yoga). Clinical training actively suppresses interoceptive awareness.

2. **Awareness Precedes Control:** The audience's reaction to the heart rate question — laughter followed by genuine curiosity — demonstrates the moment of awareness-before-technique. They've never been prompted to sense their own state in relation to patients.

3. **Transition Windows as Training Surface:** The audience intuitively recognized the gap between cases as an unaddressed space. The question "what do you do between cases?" produced knowing silence.

### Implications

- The interoceptive training gap is not a knowledge deficit — it's a cultural suppression pattern embedded in CRNA education
- Market opportunity is confirmed: the need is unmet, unrecognized, and immediately resonant once surfaced
- Audience composition (educators + students + practitioners) means the message can propagate through training pipelines
- "Onto something" is pre-product-market-fit language — the audience is at awareness stage, not demand stage yet
