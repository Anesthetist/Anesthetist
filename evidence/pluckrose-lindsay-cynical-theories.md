## Summary

Genealogy of applied postmodernism: how academic theories about knowledge, power, and identity moved from universities into institutional culture. Pluckrose and Lindsay trace the intellectual lineage and argue for liberal empiricism as an alternative.

## Somnistic Relevance

The epistemological landscape Somnistics enters. Healthcare institutions are shaped by these cultural currents. Understanding the intellectual forces driving DEI mandates, wellness theater, and institutional messaging helps Pausality position itself: evidence-based, measurable, physiologically grounded, not ideological.
