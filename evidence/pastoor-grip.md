Pastoor's productivity framework centers on intentional time allocation and ruthless prioritization. The system treats calendar management as a strategic discipline rather than administrative overhead.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Clinician time is non-renewable. Pastoor's framework maps directly to the minimum-effective-dose principle -- doing less but with greater intentionality. For CRNAs managing call schedules, continuing education, and side ventures, the "grip" methodology offers a structural analog to how anesthetic dosing works: precise, timed, and titrated to effect.
