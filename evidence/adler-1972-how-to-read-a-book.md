## Summary

Adler and Van Doren describe four levels of reading, from elementary through inspectional and analytical to syntopical. Syntopical reading — the highest level — requires: (1) identifying relevant passages across multiple books on a shared question, (2) translating and synthesizing — establishing your own terms rather than using each author's language, (3) formulating cross-cutting questions that multiple authors can answer, and (4) defining the issues where answers diverge and analyzing the discussion. The reader, not the author, sets the agenda. Originally published 1940; revised edition 1972 with Van Doren.
