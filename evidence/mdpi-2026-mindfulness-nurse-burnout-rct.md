## Citation

(Authors, 2025/2026). A highly effective mindfulness intervention for burnout prevention and resiliency building in nurses. *PMC* / peer-reviewed journal. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11999806/

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness-based intervention produced significant reductions in burnout as measured by the Copenhagen Burnout Inventory.
- Significant reductions in perceived stress (Perceived Stress Scale) following the intervention.
- Study specifically targeted nurses — a population with documented high burnout rates, directly analogous to CRNA/perioperative clinician populations.
- Individual-focused mindfulness interventions show incremental improvements but require concurrent organizational change to fully reverse burnout.

## Relevance to SRL

Direct evidence supporting Gap Moment Training and the Clinician Durability program as scalable mindfulness-based burnout interventions. The caveat that individual interventions require organizational support aligns with SRL's enterprise deployment model — individual tools (NeuroMinute, Gap Moment Training) are most powerful when embedded in organizational culture change. Relevant to the Somnistics Certification and GMT Career Arc frameworks.

## Methodology

- **Design:** RCT / controlled intervention
- **Population:** Nurses
- **Intervention:** Mindfulness-based curriculum
- **Measures:** Copenhagen Burnout Inventory, Perceived Stress Scale
