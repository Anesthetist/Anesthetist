## Citation
Aydemir (2026). The Mind and the Senses: Associations of Sensory Processing and Interoception With Cognitive Function in Older Adults in Nursing Homes. Geriatrics & Gerontology International. DOI: 10.1111/ggi.70277

## Key Findings

- Interoception, proprioceptive seeking, and visual over-responsiveness emerged as key associated factors of cognitive performance among nursing home residents
- Cognitive health in late life is closely intertwined with sensory functioning and environmental stimulation
- Confirms that interoceptive awareness is tightly coupled with multiple sensory modalities in predicting cognitive outcomes

## SRL Relevance
While the population (elderly nursing home residents) differs from SRL's primary target, this paper strengthens the theoretical foundation linking interoceptive awareness to cognitive function. The multi-sensory integration finding supports SRL's Polyanchora concept and the ExterOryx/interoception coupling model. The olfaction-interoception link is particularly relevant to Randy's interest in sensory awareness training as an entry point for interoceptive literacy.
