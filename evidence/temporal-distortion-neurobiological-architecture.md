# The Neurobiological Architecture of Temporal Distortion

## A Synthesis of Dopaminergic Modulation and Cognitive Mechanics

---

## 1. Neuroanatomical Foundations: The Fronto-Striatal Clock Model

The fronto-striatal network constitutes the primary neural architecture for interval timing — the brain's strategic internal clock. The striatum functions as a coincidence detector of oscillatory processes (the timing engine), while the prefrontal cortex provides executive oversight and working memory for storing and comparing intervals.

### The Dopaminergic Governor

Dopaminergic tone serves as the master regulator of internal clock speed:

- **Accelerants:** Stimulants (methamphetamine) induce massive DA release via reverse transport → speeds internal pacemaker → external durations perceived as dilated (internal count outpaces objective time)
- **Decelerants:** DA antagonists (haloperidol) target meso-striatal system → slows clock rate → subjective time contraction

### Dual-Pathway Estimation

- **Basal ganglia (striatum):** Essential for motor timing and duration discrimination. Chronic stimulant use → D2 receptor downregulation → "noisy" clock with increased variance. Mimics Parkinson's timing deficits.
- **Right prefrontal regions:** Manage the "software" of timing — attention, maintenance of temporal records for supra-second intervals.

---

## 2. Clinical Pathologies: Temporal Processing in Stimulant-Dependent Individuals

SDI provide a direct window into behavioral consequences of compromised dopaminergic systems:

| Task Type | Impairment | Time Scale |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Duration Discrimination | Increased Weber fractions (reduced precision) | Impaired at 1000ms; intact at 100ms |
| Temporal Reproduction | Significant under-reproduction; higher CV | Failure specific to 2000ms |
| Paced Motor Timing | Accelerated inter-tap intervals | Clock speed increase at 1000ms |
| Time Estimation | Massive overestimation (~91s for 53s interval) | 53-second prospective range |

### The Precision-Accuracy Gap

SDI timing is not merely "fast" but "broken" — producing noisy, erratic signals. At 1-second, significantly higher Weber fractions suggest permanent degradation of timing hardware, unreliable even during abstinence.

---

## 3. Cognitive Mediation: Attention, Memory, and Impulsivity

### The Impulsivity-Estimation Link

In 53-second prospective task, SDI overestimate by ~70% (90.8s for 53s). This is a software failure completely mediated by "non-planning impulsivity":

1. High impulsivity → boredom/distress → exclusive focus on time passage
2. Prospective focus → attentional dilation of perceived duration
3. Future rewards feel "too far away" → preference for immediate gratification

### Cognitive Independence of 1-2 Second Range

Timing deficits at 1000-2000ms remain **unmediated** by attention or working memory. This is hardware failure — direct striatal dysfunction independent of concentration ability. Confirms dual-process model: automatic timing (sub-2s) vs. controlled timing (supra-second).

---

## 4. Predictive Coding and the Temporal Oddball Effect

Brain as energy-efficient prediction engine: matching stimuli → neural conservation → time contracts. Oddball violations → prediction error → time dilates.

### Feature Selectivity

Time is dilated by changes in location, size, numerosity. NOT dilated by changes in luminance, orientation, or eye of origin. Temporal processing is hard-wired to specific higher-level perceptual features, not raw intensity.

### Key Evidence

The oddball effect is eye-insensitive (persists when switching between eyes) → higher-level perceptual judgment, not low-level adaptation. Dim oddballs still dilate time → expectation, not intensity, drives dilation.

---

## 5. Macro-Temporal: Aging and Lifespan Proportionality

### The Paul Janet Hypothesis (1877)

Subjective year length is relative to total lifespan (year = 10% of 10-year-old's life, 1.25% of 80-year-old's). Mathematically elegant but secondary.

### Cognitive Decline as Primary Mediator (Teghil & Wittmann 2025)

| Traditional Assumption | Research Finding |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| Memory load causes time acceleration | Encoding deficit: reduced ability to encode new memories predicts acceleration |
| Routine reduces "marked" events | Lower cognitive functioning is primary mediator |

When the brain's ability to stamp new memories into the timeline declines, retrospective record becomes sparse → years seem to vanish.

---

## 6. Environmental and Extreme Modulators

### Auditory-Visual Disparity

The "Filled-Duration Illusion": auditory pacemaker runs faster than visual. Visual stimuli judged at 80-90% of equivalent auditory duration. Gap is multiplicative (increases with interval length).

### Near-Death Experience Time Decoupling

Greyson NDE Scale: 74% of NDErs report "time stopped or lost all meaning" vs. 27% of non-NDErs in similar threats. The NDE state involves profound neurophysiological transition where the linear fronto-striatal clock ceases to function.

---

## Final Synthesis

> Objective time is an illusion of the biological mind. Subjective duration is a plastic metric defined by the health of our dopaminergic tone, our predictive modeling of the environment, and our capacity to anchor new memories into a coherent timeline.

Hierarchical construct:
- **Automatic Timing System** (sub-2s): dopaminergic fronto-striatal hardware, raw chronometric pulse
- **Cognitively Controlled System** (supra-second): software output mediated by impulsivity and memory encoding
- **Predictive Coding Layer**: perception of time as function of discrepancy, not duration
- **Extreme States**: linear clock can decouple entirely (NDE, deep meditation, anesthesia)
