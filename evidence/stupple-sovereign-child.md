Stupple articulates a framework for raising children as autonomous agents rather than compliant products of parental anxiety. The approach treats children's sovereignty as a developmental necessity rather than a permissive indulgence.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Sovereignty maps to the self-remembering concept -- awareness of one's own agency as distinct from conditioned response. The parenting model parallels the clinician-patient dynamic where the goal is restoring the patient's own regulatory capacity rather than creating dependency on external management.
