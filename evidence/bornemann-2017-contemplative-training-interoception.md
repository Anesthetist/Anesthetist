## Summary

Bornemann & Singer (2017) conducted a 9-month longitudinal study of contemplative training effects on interoception, part of the ReSource project — one of the largest contemplative training studies ever. Published in *Psychophysiology* 54(3):469-482. PMID: 28824501.

## Key Findings

1. Steady increase in heartbeat perception accuracy over 9 months of training
2. Decreases in alexithymia alongside accuracy gains
3. Training-specific effects (not just time)
4. Demonstrates interoception is trainable with sustained practice
5. Part of the ReSource project — one of the largest contemplative training studies ever

## SRL Relevance

Direct evidence that interoceptive accuracy improves with sustained training. Validates the core premise of [[interoceptive-literacy|Interoceptive Literacy]] as a trainable skill. 9-month timeline suggests SRL should design for long-term engagement.
