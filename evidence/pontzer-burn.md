## Summary

Pontzer's constrained energy model: total daily energy expenditure is relatively fixed regardless of activity level. The body reallocates energy, it doesn't simply add to the budget. Upends the "calories in, calories out" model.

## Somnistic Relevance

The biological constraint model. If the body's energy budget is constrained, then interventions need to work within that budget, not demand more. Supports the minimum effective dose principle: 60 seconds of breathing doesn't deplete the budget the way an hour-long wellness program does.
