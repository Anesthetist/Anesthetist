## Citation

(2026). Integrating neural, physiological, and interoceptive measures in social interaction. *Frontiers in Neuroscience*, 2026. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2026.1771470

## Key Findings

- Social behavior arises from multilevel coupling among neural activity, autonomic physiology, and interoceptive processes — not simply from brain-to-brain synchrony alone.
- Hyperscanning paradigms should incorporate interoceptive and autonomic measures to understand how partners attune to each other's internal physiological rhythms.
- Shared physiological and affective states (co-regulation) contribute directly to coordinated social action and empathic accuracy.
- Multimodal hyperscanning in naturalistic settings can clarify when and how neural, autonomic, and interoceptive levels interact — from dyads to groups.

## Relevance to SRL

Provides strong empirical scaffolding for SRL's Co-Regulation concept — demonstrating that interpersonal resonance is grounded in mutual physiological attunement, not just cognitive empathy. The multi-level coupling framework also supports SRL's Multi-Phase Interoceptive Coupling and NeuroHarmonics concepts, which emphasize the body as the primary medium for relational coherence.

## Methodology

- Conceptual/review paper proposing a framework for integrating interoceptive and autonomic measures into hyperscanning paradigms.
- Draws on neuroscience, social physiology, and interoception research literature.
- Proposes temporally sensitive analyses to capture multilevel coupling dynamics.
