## Summary

Four-generation cycle theory: High, Awakening, Unraveling, Crisis. Each roughly 20 years. We are currently in a Fourth Turning (crisis era). Historical pattern predicts institutional upheaval and rebuilding.

## Somnistic Relevance

The longest macro cycle framework in the library. If we're in a Fourth Turning, institutions are being rebuilt. That's both a risk (healthcare institutions in flux) and an opportunity (new institutions need new tools). Pausality enters at a moment when the old order's mental models about clinician wellness are being discarded.
