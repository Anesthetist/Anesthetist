## Summary

How ego and emotional attachment to positions led to catastrophic loss. The key insight: there are many ways to make money in markets but only a few ways to lose it, and they all involve personalizing the position.

## Somnistic Relevance

The loss side of performance under pressure. When a clinician personalizes an adverse outcome, the same ego-attachment mechanism kicks in. Second-victim phenomenon and trading blowups share the same psychological structure. Regulation is the intervention for both.
