Robinson examines enlightenment as a structured developmental project rather than a mystical event. The framework treats awakening as an engineering problem -- identifying prerequisites, removing obstacles, and creating conditions for perceptual shifts to stabilize.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The "enlightenment as engineering" framing aligns with Pausality's approach to consciousness -- treating awareness states as trainable capacities rather than mystical gifts. This is the same logic that makes CO2 tolerance trainable and interoceptive awareness a skill rather than a talent.
