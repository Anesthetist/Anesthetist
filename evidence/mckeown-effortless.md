## Summary

McKeown's follow-up to Essentialism. The thesis: once you've identified what matters, make it easier rather than grinding harder. Effortless state, effortless action, effortless results.

## Somnistic Relevance

The product design philosophy. Pausality's 60-second protocol is effortless by design. The intervention that requires willpower won't survive a 12-hour shift. McKeown provides the framework: make the essential thing the easiest thing.
