## Overview

Lalanza et al. (2023) present a systematic review of 143 HRVB studies across all scientific fields, published in *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*. The review identifies three distinct HRVB protocol types (Optimal RF, Individual RF, and Preset-pace), documents major methodological inconsistencies, and proposes guidelines and a checklist to improve reproducibility in future HRVB research.

## Key Findings

1. Three HRVB protocol types identified: Optimal RF (n = 37, each participant breathes at previously detected RF), Individual RF (n = 48, real-time biofeedback-guided), and Preset-pace RF (n = 51, fixed rate, usually 6 breaths/min)
2. Almost two-thirds of studies did NOT report enough information to replicate the HRVB protocol
3. No consensus exists on session duration, inhalation/exhalation ratio, breathing control method, or body position
4. Proposed a methodological checklist covering 14 critical parameters for future HRVB study reporting
5. 143 studies reviewed from 2000–2021 across clinical, sports, and wellness applications
