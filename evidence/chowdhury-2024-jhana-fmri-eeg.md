# Jhana Multimodal Neurophenomenology (Chowdhury et al. 2024)

## Summary

Combined fMRI, EEG, and phenomenological study of a 23,000+ hour jhana practitioner from the Sacchet Lab (Harvard/MGH). ACAM-J states showed decreased within-network modularity, increased global functional connectivity, desegregation of default mode and visual networks, reduced broadband EEG power, and increased Lempel-Ziv brain entropy — with entropy particularly correlated with self-reported states of bliss.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Jhana states INCREASE brain entropy (complexity) — same direction as cessation, opposite to anesthesia
- Global connectivity increases (integration up, segregation down) — validates Siegel's "integration = health" thesis
- DMN desegregation = reduced self-referential rumination during absorption
- 23,000+ hours = extreme end of progressive training; validates that states are trainable, not random
- Lempel-Ziv entropy as objective marker of advanced meditative states
