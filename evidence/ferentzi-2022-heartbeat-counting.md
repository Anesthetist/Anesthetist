# What Counts When Heartbeats Are Counted (Ferentzi 2022)

## Summary

Published in Trends in Cognitive Sciences, this review critically examines the heartbeat counting task (Schandry paradigm) — the most widely used measure of interoceptive accuracy. Addresses what these tasks actually measure, their limitations, and methodological considerations for clinical application.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Heartbeat counting tasks measure a specific dimension of interoception (cardiac interoceptive accuracy)
- Methodological considerations affect interpretation of results
- Important for MAIA-2 integration — understanding what interoceptive measures capture
- Informs design of Pausality's interoceptive training assessment
