## Summary

Options trading through the lens of managing expectations and probability. Systematic approach to risk through option structures.

## Somnistic Relevance

Options thinking as a mental model. Asymmetric risk, defined exposure, managing the range of outcomes rather than predicting the point estimate. This maps to clinical decision-making: good anesthesia is options-like (defined risk, hedged positions, clear exit criteria).
