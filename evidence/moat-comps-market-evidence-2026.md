## Key Comparable Companies by Moat Layer

### Founder/Domain Moat Comps
- **Hinge Health**: Clinician-founder credibility in AI-enabled PT. 72% growth, 26% FCF margins. Software-like economics despite being "services."
- **Whoop / Oura**: Strong hardware + data moats but founders lacked lived clinical expertise. Pausality's team has what they don't.
- **Peloton**: Instructors as brand showed human credibility + content drives retention > features. Lost it when moat was just hardware.

### Hardware Integration Comps
- **Levels Health / Supersapiens**: CGM apps with device integration moats held up better than pure software in 2025-2026. Hardware dependency became asset.
- **Oura Ring + apps**: Hardware-dependent health apps command premium pricing and higher retention than software-only.

### Enterprise Distribution Comps
- **Calm Business / Lyra Health**: Enterprise mental health plays scaled via B2B despite fierce horizontal competition.
- **Ginger.io / Spring Health**: B2B channel (employer-sponsored) became the moat, not the technology.

### Data Moat Comps
- **Tempus (genomics)**: Built oncology data moat. Data became more valuable than software.
- **Flatiron Health**: Acquired by Roche for $1.9B primarily for real-world oncology data, not software platform.

### Cautionary Tales
- **Jasper / Copy.ai**: 30%+ subscriber losses when foundation models improved. AI-wrapper fragility. Pausality avoids this by not depending on AI wrappers.
- **SaaS market**: Lost >$1T in value early 2026. Vertical healthcare plays outperformed.
