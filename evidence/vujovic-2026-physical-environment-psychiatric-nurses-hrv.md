## Summary

Study protocol for a mixed-methods investigation integrating wearable HRV sensors with environmental monitoring to assess the impact of physical environment on psychiatric nurses' well-being.

## Key Findings

- Protocol integrates wearable HRV sensors with environmental monitoring (noise, light, temperature) for psychiatric nurses
- Environmental factors positioned as stress-reducing or stress-amplifying variables
- Systems-level approach combining architecture and healthcare perspectives
- Represents emerging research paradigm: environment-person-physiology interaction mapping
