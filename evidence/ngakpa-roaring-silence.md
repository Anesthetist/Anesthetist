## Summary

Introduction to Dzogchen, the "Great Perfection" tradition of Tibetan Buddhism. The view: awareness is already perfect; practice is recognizing what's already there, not constructing something new.

## Somnistic Relevance

The nondual tradition from the Tibetan side, complementing McKenna (Western radical inquiry), Gurdjieff (Fourth Way), and Patanjali (classical yoga). Dzogchen's emphasis on natural awareness connects to the nondual clinical awareness concept: the CRNA's background awareness that registers changes before conscious analysis catches up.
