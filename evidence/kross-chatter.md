## Summary

Neuroscience of inner speech. How the voice in your head can be constructive (planning, rehearsal) or destructive (rumination, anxiety spiraling). Evidence-based strategies for shifting the balance.

## Somnistic Relevance

The cognitive layer of what Pausality addresses somatically. Kross shows the problem (runaway inner speech degrades performance). Pausality shows a physiological intervention (breath-based autonomic regulation) that interrupts the rumination circuit at the body level rather than the cognitive level. Complementary mechanisms, same target.
