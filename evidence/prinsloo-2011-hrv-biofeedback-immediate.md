# Short Duration HRV Biofeedback Improves Cognition Under Stress (Prinsloo et al. 2011)

## Summary

A brief (10-minute) HRV biofeedback session improved cognitive performance under laboratory-induced cognitive stress compared to controls. Participants showed both better task performance and lower perceived stress after biofeedback. This validates that even short-duration biofeedback has immediate cognitive benefits under pressure.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- 10-minute HRV biofeedback improves cognition under stress in a single session
- Immediate effects (not requiring weeks of practice) — supports [[minimum-effective-dose]]
- Cognitive stress specifically (not just relaxation) — matches CRNA clinical demands
- Short duration + immediate effect = validates the 60-second [[neurominute]] concept

- [[neurominute]] — if 10 minutes works, 60 seconds at resonant frequency may also
