## Summary
Preprint showing interoceptive training improved heartbeat detection accuracy, emotional awareness, and body image perception. Evidence that interoceptive ability is trainable and has downstream psychological benefits.

## Relevance to SRL
Supports the core Pausality premise that interoceptive skills can be systematically trained and that improvement cascades into emotional regulation and self-awareness — key for clinician performance arguments.

## Key Constructs
- Trainability of interoceptive accuracy
- Heartbeat detection as biomarker
- Interoception → emotional awareness pathway
