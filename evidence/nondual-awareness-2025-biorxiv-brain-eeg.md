## Citation

(2025). Non-duality in brain and experience of advanced meditators. *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.21.649758

## Key Findings

- Non-dual awareness — the dissolution of internal/external duality — is characterized by "non-representational reflexivity": the capacity to be aware without engaging in conceptual representation
- Rooted in contemplative traditions (Advaita Vedanta, Kashmiri Shaivism, Buddhist schools)
- Neural correlates of non-dual states are now being mapped in advanced meditators
- Represents a frontier in consciousness research with direct implications for understanding the nature of awareness itself

## Relevance to SRL

Connects to SRL's **Nonlocal Consciousness**, **Self-Remembering**, and **Neurogating** concepts. The "non-representational reflexivity" construct maps closely to the kind of awareness SRL cultivates through **Gap Moment Training** — being present without conceptual overlay. As SRL explores consciousness studies as part of its framework, this provides emerging empirical grounding for advanced contemplative states.

## Methodology

Neuroimaging study of advanced meditators during non-dual awareness states. Preprint on bioRxiv (April 2025). Examines neural correlates of subject-object dissolution in experienced practitioners.
