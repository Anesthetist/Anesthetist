## Citation

Winter, U., LeVan, P., Borghardt, T. L., Akin, B., Wittmann, M., Leyens, Y., & Schmidt, S. (2020). Content-free awareness: EEG-fcMRI correlates of consciousness in an expert meditator. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 10, 3064. [DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03064](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03064)

## Key Findings

- Serendipitous EEG-fMRI data from an extraordinarily qualified meditator with over **50,000 hours** of practice who reported entering content-free awareness (CFA) during scanning
- CFA = conscious awareness devoid of any specific phenomenal content — awareness of awareness itself
- Neural correlates of CFA:
  - **Sharp decrease in alpha power** (reduced default sensory processing)
  - **Increase in theta power** (deep attentional engagement)
  - **Increased functional connectivity in the dorsal attention network (DAN)** — heightened top-down attention
  - **Decreased connectivity in the posterior default mode network (DMN)** — reduced self-referential mentation
- Interpreted as a **top-down-initiated attentional state** that excludes both external sensory stimuli and internal mentation from conscious experience
- CFA may represent the minimal neural correlate of consciousness (NCC) — consciousness stripped of all content

## Relevance to SRL

- **Content-free awareness** maps to the highest stages of SRL's contemplative development arc — the point where awareness becomes aware of itself without content. This is the endpoint of [[consciousness-engineering]].
- **DAN up, DMN down** is the neural signature of expert-level attentional sovereignty. SRL's NeuroMinute training aims to progressively shift this balance — even brief practices (GMT Phase 1: Seeding) begin the process.
- **The self-time link (Wittmann 2015):** CFA involves both self-dissolution AND time-dissolution. The meditator reports no sense of duration during CFA — direct evidence for the coupling of self and time.
- **50,000 hours** is not the entry point — but this paper maps the neural territory that SRL's progressive training points toward. Phase 3 (Fortifying) in [[gmt-career-arc]] moves practitioners toward this neural configuration.
- **Alpha suppression + theta increase** during CFA parallels the EEG changes documented during resonant breathing and focused meditation practices.

## Methodology

Combined EEG-fMRI recording during meditation in a single expert practitioner (50,000+ hours). Analyzed dynamic fMRI connectivity informed by concurrent EEG power analysis. Case study design — unusual but uniquely informative for mapping CFA neural correlates.
