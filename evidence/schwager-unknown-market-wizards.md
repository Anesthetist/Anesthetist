## Summary

Interviews with highly successful but lesser-known traders. Focus on process, psychology, and risk management rather than strategy specifics. Recurring themes: emotional regulation, pattern recognition, knowing when not to trade.

## Somnistic Relevance

Trading and anesthesia as structurally isomorphic. Both require real-time decisions under uncertainty with immediate consequences. The traders who survive long-term are the ones who manage their own state, not just their positions. Direct evidence for the clinician-durability thesis applied to a parallel domain.
