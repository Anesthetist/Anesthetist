## Citation

Effects of Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback on Cardiac Autonomic Function in Patients with Cardiovascular Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*, 2026. DOI: 10.1007/s10484-025-09765-3

## Key Findings

- Meta-analysis of 13 RCTs with 965 participants
- HRVB significantly improved SDNN (standard deviation of NN intervals) — a key HRV metric
- Low-frequency power and logLF increased, indicating enhanced baroreflex sensitivity
- Both systolic and diastolic blood pressure decreased
- Effects on parasympathetic activity (HF power) and psychological outcomes remain inconclusive

## Relevance to SRL

Provides moderate-quality evidence that HRV biofeedback enhances autonomic function and reduces blood pressure in cardiovascular patients. The inconclusive findings on parasympathetic activity and psychological outcomes highlight the need for longer interventions and more nuanced measures — exactly what SRL's NeuroHarmonics protocol aims to address through individualized resonant frequency training.

## Methodology

Systematic review and meta-analysis; 13 randomized controlled trials; 965 total participants; patients with cardiovascular disease; standard HRV and blood pressure outcome measures.
