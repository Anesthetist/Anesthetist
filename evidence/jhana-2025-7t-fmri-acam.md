## Citation

(2025). The neuroscience of highly stable, positive, and refined states of consciousness during jhana-type advanced concentration absorption meditation (ACAM-J). *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.12.688050

## Key Findings

- First group-level ultra-high-field (7T) fMRI study of jhana advanced concentration absorption meditation
- Across 8 successive jhana states, researchers observed reproducible neural trajectories
- Key neural patterns: anterior-to-posterior reorganization, flattening of cortical hierarchies, nonlinear reconfiguration of global harmonics
- Tight coupling between brain metrics and subjective reports of equanimity, attentional stability, and behavior
- Demonstrates that jhana states involve systematic, reproducible changes in brain organization

## Relevance to SRL

Highly relevant to SRL's **Neurogating**, **State Transition**, and **NeuroHarmonics** concepts. The "flattening of cortical hierarchies" and "global harmonic reconfiguration" during jhana may represent an extreme form of the state transitions SRL trains clinicians to recognize and facilitate. The equanimity coupling is directly relevant to **Self-Remembering** and **Gap Moment Training**. Also connects to **Nonlocal Consciousness** concept as an empirical anchor for refined states of awareness.

## Methodology

Group-level fMRI study using ultra-high-field 7T scanner. Expert meditators practiced 8 successive jhana states. Measured neural trajectories, cortical hierarchy, global harmonics, and correlated with subjective reports. Preprint on bioRxiv (Nov 2025).
