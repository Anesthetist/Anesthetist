## Citation
Neurocognitive and Autonomic Signatures of Performance Under Motivational Stress: An Integrated Psychophysiological Analysis of Reward and Punishment in Shooting Performance. PMC12908615, 2026.

## Key Findings

- Optimal performance under high-pressure conditions requires coordinated regulation of emotional, cognitive, motor, and autonomic systems
- The brainstem-prefrontal cortex-limbic system pathway integrates autonomic regulation, cognition, emotion, and stress responses
- Higher SDNN and RMSSD (HRV metrics) and lower QTVI were significantly associated with better performance on cognitive tests tapping inhibition, updating, shifting, and psychomotor speed
- Better cognitive performance is associated with healthier resting autonomic profiles and greater autonomic adaptability
- Peripheral autonomic imbalance signals transmit to the central nervous system via brainstem-PFC-limbic pathway

## SRL Relevance
This paper provides mechanistic evidence for the core SRL claim that autonomic self-regulation directly improves cognitive performance under stress. The finding that HRV metrics (SDNN, RMSSD) predict executive function (inhibition, updating, shifting) maps directly to clinical decision-making demands on CRNAs. The brainstem-PFC-limbic integration model supports SRL's multi-phase interoceptive coupling framework. While the study population is shooters rather than clinicians, the high-pressure performance paradigm transfers well to perioperative settings.
