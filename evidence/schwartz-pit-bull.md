## Summary

Memoir of one of Wall Street's most successful day traders. Raw account of the psychological and physiological toll of high-frequency decision-making under pressure.

## Somnistic Relevance

The unfiltered version of what sustained performance under pressure does to a person. Schwartz's physical health deteriorated as his trading performance peaked. The body keeps the score of every trade. Case study in what happens without the regulation tools Pausality provides.
