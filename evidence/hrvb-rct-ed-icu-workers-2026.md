## Citation
Building resilience with heart rate variability biofeedback (HRVB) in emergency department and ICU healthcare workers: a randomized controlled trial. PubMed PMID: 41734245. Published February 2026.

## Structured Abstract

**Background:** Healthcare workers in emergency departments and intensive care units are highly vulnerable to stress and burnout. Resilience training may mitigate these effects, though most programs are resource-intensive and untested in rigorous designs.

**Methods:** Randomized controlled trial comparing HRVB (brief 30-minute introduction + self-practice of 10 minutes twice daily) against a waitlist control in ED/ICU healthcare workers.

**Results:** Resilience increased significantly in the HRVB group relative to the waitlist control from pre- to post-treatment, and gains were maintained at follow-up. The intervention proved feasible as a low-cost, brief intervention.

**Conclusion:** HRVB is effective as a brief, scalable resilience-building intervention for high-acuity healthcare workers.
