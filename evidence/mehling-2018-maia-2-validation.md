## Summary
Mehling et al. (2018) present MAIA-2, a refined 37-item self-report instrument measuring eight subscales of interoceptive awareness: Noticing, Not-Distracting, Not-Worrying, Attention Regulation, Emotional Awareness, Self-Regulation, Body Listening, and Trusting. The updated version demonstrates improved reliability over the original MAIA, with better internal consistency across subscales and sensitivity to change following mind-body interventions. Validation was conducted across multiple populations including clinical and contemplative samples.

## Key Finding for SRL
MAIA-2 is one of SRL's primary outcome measures for validating interoceptive literacy interventions. Its eight-subscale structure maps directly onto SRL's training targets — particularly Attention Regulation, Self-Regulation, and Body Listening — and its demonstrated sensitivity to change makes it the ideal pre/post instrument for NeuroMinute and Gap Moment Training protocol evaluations.
