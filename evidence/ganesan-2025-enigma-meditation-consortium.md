# ENIGMA-Meditation Consortium (Ganesan et al. 2025)

## Summary

This paper announces and describes ENIGMA-Meditation, a worldwide consortium bringing together research groups to conduct large-scale, standardized neuroimaging investigations of meditation practices. The consortium addresses critical methodological challenges that have plagued meditation neuroscience: small sample sizes, heterogeneous methods, inconsistent definitions of meditation expertise, and limited cross-tradition comparison. By establishing shared protocols and pooling neuroimaging data across sites, ENIGMA-Meditation enables mega-analyses with statistical power previously impossible in this field. For SRL, this consortium represents the methodological infrastructure that will generate the definitive evidence base for meditation-based interventions — the kind of large-scale, rigorous data that enterprise and clinical credibility require.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- ENIGMA-Meditation establishes standardized protocols for meditation neuroimaging research worldwide
- The consortium addresses sample size limitations that have constrained the field
- Cross-tradition and cross-site comparisons become possible for the first time at scale
- Standardized methodology improves replicability and clinical translatability
- Mega-analyses will provide definitive evidence for neural effects of meditation
- The consortium framework could accommodate breathwork-specific protocols in future
- Enterprise and clinical credibility increasingly require this level of methodological rigor
