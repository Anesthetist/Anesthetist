# ALI Leadership Skill: Holding Space for Healing

## Framework Summary

Whetten presents healing as the application of loving attention to stored emotional pain — drawing on the Hulnick formulation that healing is "the application of loving to the places that hurt." The model posits that the unconscious mind stores emotional traumas that were too large to process at the time of occurrence; repression is framed not as pathology but as adaptive storage — the psyche holds wounds until the individual develops sufficient capacity to revisit them.

### Core Mechanism

Healing is not something that can be forced or scheduled. It emerges spontaneously during other awareness exercises (Venting and Sorting, Making Friends With Your Fears, Owning Negative Projections) when the individual contacts stored emotional material with sufficient safety and presence. The facilitator's role is to hold space — to remain present, grounded, and compassionate — rather than to direct or accelerate the process.

### 8-Step Coaching Protocol

1. **Center yourself** — the facilitator must be grounded before attempting to hold space
2. **Connect with your own body** — establish somatic presence as the container for the work
3. **Invite the client to connect with their body** — guide attention inward to where the energy/sensation lives
4. **Trace the energy to an earlier time** — follow the somatic thread backward: "How old were they? What happened?"
5. **Connect with your heart/love** — shift from cognitive processing to heart-centered presence
6. **Bring compassion to the wounded aspect** — approach the younger self with the tenderness one would offer a frightened child
7. **Allow the emotional release** — tears, shaking, grief — without fixing, interpreting, or rushing
8. **Integration** — often followed by Compassionate Self-Forgiveness as a completion step

### Key Principles

- The process works through re-accessing stored emotional material in the presence of safety and love — essentially providing what was missing at the time of the original wound
- The facilitator must resist the urge to fix, interpret, or advise — presence IS the intervention
- Healing often involves connecting with a younger version of the self and offering the compassion that was unavailable at the time of wounding
- The approach treats emotional wounds as stored energy requiring completion, not cognitive problems requiring solutions

## Somnistic Relevance

This protocol maps directly onto the autonomic re-patterning work central to Somnistics:

- **State-dependent processing**: The requirement to access a loving/safe state BEFORE approaching stored pain parallels the polyvagal principle that ventral vagal engagement must precede trauma processing. You cannot heal in the same autonomic state that created the wound.
- **Alpha-theta crossover**: The protocol's movement from cognitive awareness (alpha) to deep emotional/somatic processing (theta) with maintained observer presence mirrors the alpha-theta training paradigm — consciousness remains present while accessing material typically below the threshold of waking awareness.
- **Co-regulation as container**: The facilitator's centered, embodied presence serves as the regulatory scaffold — interpersonal physiological synchrony enables the client to access states they cannot yet sustain independently.
- **Constraint-release-grief loop**: The movement from repression (constraint) → emotional release (tears, shaking) → integration (self-forgiveness) maps the full arc of the constraint-release cycle. The stored energy IS the constraint; the healing IS the release.
- **Diagnostic-therapeutic unity**: The moment the stored wound becomes accessible IS simultaneously the moment healing begins. There is no gap between assessment and intervention — contacting the material with loving presence is both the discovery and the remedy.
- **Trauma-informed titration**: The emphasis on not pushing or forcing — allowing healing to emerge rather than engineering it — embodies the minimum effective dose principle. The system processes what it is ready to process, no more.
