# Mindfulness in Motion with Biofeedback for Healthcare Workers (Merrigan 2024)

## Summary

An 8-week Mindfulness in Motion program with wearable sensors and mobile app reduced perceived stress and mood disturbances in 66 healthcare professionals. However, objective physiological measures (HRV, sleep) did not change significantly — highlighting the gap between subjective and objective outcomes.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Subjective improvement without objective HRV change = measurement gap that Pausality addresses
- Validates need for real-time biofeedback (not just post-hoc tracking) to close the loop
- 8-week format may be too long for sustained compliance — supports 21-day [[neuro-ouroboros]] design
- Reinforces [[hrv-deprecation-measurement-integrity]] — objective HRV is hard to move with generic mindfulness

## Concepts Derived

- [[clinician-durability]] — healthcare worker stress reduction as target
- [[neurogating]] — need for real-time (not passive) biofeedback to produce objective changes
- [[minimum-effective-dose]] — shorter, more targeted interventions may outperform 8-week programs
