## Citation

(Authors, 2025). Efficacy and Methodology of Remote Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback Interventions for Mental Health: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10484-025-09750-w

## Key Findings

- 18 studies, N = 1,352 total subjects.
- **Medium effect size** for improving depression (Hedges' g = −0.41, p = 0.026) and HRV (g = 0.443, p = 0.002) compared to control conditions.
- Remote delivery (app/digital) of HRV biofeedback is feasible and retains meaningful efficacy.
- Effects observed across depression, stress, anxiety, and sleep domains.

## Relevance to SRL

Confirms scalability of HRV biofeedback via remote delivery — critical for SRL's digital product strategy and the NeuroMinute protocol as a remotely deliverable autonomic training tool. The sleep and stress effects are especially relevant to Clinician Durability and the Somnistics certification program. A medium effect size across 1,352 participants is a robust, generalizable finding.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Studies:** 18 RCTs/controlled studies
- **N:** 1,352 subjects
- **Outcomes:** Depression, anxiety, stress, sleep, HRV
- **Delivery modality:** Remote (app, digital device)
