## Summary

How Marquet transformed the worst-performing submarine in the Navy by replacing leader-follower with leader-leader. Push authority to where the information lives.

## Somnistic Relevance

The organizational design model for Vigil and for Pausality's enterprise product. In an OR, the CRNA often has the best real-time information about patient state. Marquet's model validates pushing decision authority to the person closest to the signal. Also maps to Vigil's multi-agent architecture: each agent has domain authority, not centralized control.
