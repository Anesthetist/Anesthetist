# Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal (Balban et al., 2023)

## Summary

Published in *Cell Reports Medicine* (January 2023) from Huberman and Spiegel's labs at Stanford. A 28-day RCT (n=114) comparing three 5-min/day breathwork protocols against mindfulness meditation:

- **Cyclic sighing** (double inhale through nose, prolonged exhale through mouth)
- **Box breathing** (equal in/hold/out/hold)
- **Cyclic hyperventilation with retention** (fast deep breaths + retention)
- **Mindfulness meditation** (active control)

All four groups improved, but **cyclic sighing produced significantly greater daily mood improvement** and greater reduction in resting respiratory rate than mindfulness meditation. The exhale-dominant pattern was the differential.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- A free, 5-minute, evidence-based, circuit-specific intervention *beats* mindfulness meditation on the same clinical-durability variable (mood, arousal)
- The active ingredient is exhale dominance, not general relaxation — a specific physiological lever, not a generic wellness frame
- Mechanistically ties to [[vagal-tone]]: extended exhale engages the baroreflex and parasympathetic outflow
- Published in a peer-reviewed medical journal (Cell Reports Medicine), not a wellness outlet
- Grounds the SRL claim that a clinician has, in any moment, access to a circuit-specific lever on their own autonomic state
