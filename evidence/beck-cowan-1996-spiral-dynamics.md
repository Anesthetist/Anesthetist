## Summary

Popularization of Clare Graves' Emergent Cyclical Levels of Existence Theory (ECLET) as a color-coded developmental model of human value systems (vMEMEs). The model progresses through stages: Beige (survival) → Purple (tribal) → Red (power) → Blue (order) → Orange (achievement) → Green (communitarian) → Yellow (integrative/systemic) → Turquoise (holistic). First Tier stages (Beige–Green) each believe their worldview is the only correct one. Second Tier stages (Yellow and above) recognize and integrate all previous levels.

## SRL Relevance

Randy uses Spiral Dynamics as a lens for both self-assessment and Somnistics positioning. In a dedicated assessment session, ChatGPT placed him at Yellow (Tier 2, FlexFlow) with active access to Turquoise. The resulting strategic insight: Somnistics operates at a Second-Tier consciousness level (systems-integrative, meta-aware) but is designed to serve clinicians experiencing First-Tier stress (Orange achievement pressure, Green empathic burnout, Blue procedural rigidity). This "Tier 2 tool for Tier 1 stress" positioning differentiates Somnistics from wellness apps built at the same developmental level as their users. Randy's request to "move to the next higher level" shows he views developmental progression as relevant to product design, not just personal growth.
