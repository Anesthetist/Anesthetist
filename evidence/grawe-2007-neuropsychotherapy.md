# Grawe's Neuropsychotherapy (2007)

**Closest conceptual precursor to [[neurotagging]].**

Klaus Grawe's framework explicitly maps four basic psychological needs to neural circuits and then connects specific therapeutic techniques to those circuits:

| Basic Need | Neural Circuit | Therapy Technique |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Cognitive consistency | Prefrontal cortex | Cognitive restructuring (CBT) |
| Emotional consistency | Amygdala-hippocampal | Transference analysis (psychodynamic) |
| Social consistency | Ventral striatum / mirror neurons | Social skills training |
| Physical consistency | Insula / somatic nervous system | Body-oriented therapy |

## Why This Matters for SRL

Grawe did the conceptual work that neurotagging operationalizes. His framework says "CBT targets the prefrontal cortex." Neurotagging says "this specific 60-second cognitive reappraisal protocol targeted prefrontal-limbic connectivity, was delivered at intensity level 4, and produced a 12ms RMSSD increase."

**The gap:** Grawe's framework is theoretical — it lives in a book, not a database. It was never formalized into computable metadata, never applied at session-level granularity, and never integrated with real-time biometric data.
