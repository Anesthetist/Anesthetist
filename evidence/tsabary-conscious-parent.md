## Summary

Parenting as a mirror for the parent's own unresolved patterns. Tsabary's thesis: children don't need to be fixed; parents need to become conscious of their own reactivity.

## Somnistic Relevance

The co-regulation thesis applied to the family. A dysregulated parent produces a dysregulated child, same as a dysregulated clinician produces a dysregulated OR. Pausality's core mechanism (autonomic self-regulation) has a direct parenting application that Randy lives daily.
