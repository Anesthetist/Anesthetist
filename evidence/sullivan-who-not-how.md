## Summary

Stop asking "how do I do this?" and start asking "who can do this for me?" Sullivan's framework for entrepreneurial leverage through delegation rather than personal skill acquisition.

## Somnistic Relevance

The organizational philosophy behind Vigil. Instead of Randy doing everything, build agents and hire people who handle the domains. Also the philosophy behind the virtual post-doc team: each agent is a "who" for a domain Randy can't personally monitor.
