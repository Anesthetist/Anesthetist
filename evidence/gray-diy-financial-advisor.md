Gray presents a quantitative, evidence-based approach to personal financial management that systematically removes behavioral biases from investment decisions. The thesis: systematic rules outperform human judgment precisely because they eliminate the emotional interference that degrades decision quality.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Evidence-based finance mirrors evidence-based anesthesiology -- both involve building systematic protocols that protect decision quality from the practitioner's own cognitive and emotional noise. The DIY ethos also maps to the Who Not How inversion: knowing when to systematize vs. when to delegate.
