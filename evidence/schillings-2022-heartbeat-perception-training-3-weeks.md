## Citation

Schillings, C., Karanassios, S., & Schulte, M. (2022). The effects of a 3-week heartbeat perception training on interoceptive abilities. *Frontiers in Neuroscience*, 16, 838055. [DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2022.838055](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.838055)

## Key Findings

- Three weeks of heartbeat perception training improved interoceptive abilities
- Earlier studies had shown promising short-term effects; this study demonstrates that effects persist and build over a multi-week training period
- Validates the 21-day training window as sufficient for producing durable interoceptive change
- The training protocol involved regular practice at detecting and counting heartbeats, progressively building cardiac interoceptive accuracy

## Relevance to SRL

This is the non-Wittmann validation of the 21-day training window. The Neuro-Ouroboros curriculum is 21 sessions. The temporal literacy protocol designed this session spans 21 days. Schillings et al. provide independent evidence from a different lab that 3 weeks of heartbeat-focused interoceptive training produces lasting change.

Combined with Dobrushina 2024 (single-session haptic effects) and Meissner-Wittmann 2011 (cardiac awareness predicts temporal accuracy), this creates a convergent evidence case: heartbeat perception training works in a single session (Dobrushina), builds over 3 weeks (Schillings), and transfers to temporal accuracy (Meissner-Wittmann). The SRL protocol stacks all three: haptic heartbeat feedback in each NeuroMinute, accumulated across a 21-day curriculum, producing both interoceptive and temporal improvement.

## Methodology

Longitudinal training study. Participants practiced heartbeat perception exercises over 3 weeks. Pre/post interoceptive accuracy measured via heartbeat detection tasks.
