# Safety Culture, Burnout, and Patient Safety in Anesthesia (Wilbanks 2025)

## Summary

Adequate staffing was the strongest predictor of reduced occupational burnout and retention among nurse anesthetists. Safety culture and staffing patterns were the best predictors of patient safety scores. Authors recommend resilience training, team training, and open communication as key interventions.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Staffing is #1 predictor of burnout — but SRL can address the resilience training recommendation
- Links burnout directly to patient safety scores (not just clinician wellbeing)
- Published in AANA Journal 2025 — most current evidence for the CRNA market
- "Resilience training" explicitly recommended as intervention = direct market pull for Pausality
