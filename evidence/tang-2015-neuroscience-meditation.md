# The Neuroscience of Mindfulness Meditation (Tang, Holzel & Posner 2015)

## Summary

Published in Nature Reviews Neuroscience, this landmark review synthesizes evidence for how meditation changes the brain. Identifies four key components of meditation practice — attention regulation, body awareness (interoception), emotional regulation, and change in self-perspective — and maps each to specific neural circuits and structural changes.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Four components map cleanly to SRL framework: attention ([[polyanchora]]), body awareness ([[anterocept]]), emotional regulation ([[vagal-tone]]), self-perspective ([[self-remembering]])
- Structural brain changes (cortical thickness, white matter integrity) from weeks of practice
- Anterior cingulate cortex, insula, and prefrontal cortex consistently altered
- Dose-response relationship supports progressive training (more practice = more change)
- Published in Nature Reviews Neuroscience = highest clinical credibility tier
