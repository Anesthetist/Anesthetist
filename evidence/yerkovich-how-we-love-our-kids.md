## Summary

Five attachment-based love styles and how they shape parenting behavior. Framework for recognizing your own relational patterns and their impact on your children.

## Somnistic Relevance

Attachment patterns are autonomic patterns. The avoider, the pleaser, the controller, the vacillator, the victim each have distinct nervous system signatures. Pausality's co-regulation thesis applies directly: change the parent's autonomic pattern, change the relational dynamic.
