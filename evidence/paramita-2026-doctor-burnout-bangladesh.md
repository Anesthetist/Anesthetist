## Summary

Qualitative study exploring the structural, cultural, and organizational causes of work-related stress and burnout among doctors in Bangladesh.

## Key Findings

- Structural, cultural, and organizational factors drive burnout — not individual weakness
- Postgraduate training phase identified as a critical pressure pinch-point
- Limited mental health awareness and support available to physicians
- Reframes burnout from individual failing to systems problem
- Cross-cultural validation that burnout drivers are structural, not cultural anomalies

## Relevance to SRL

Reinforces the systems-level framing essential to SRL's positioning: clinician burnout is not a personal resilience deficit but a structural failure. The postgraduate pinch-point finding aligns with the GMT career arc concept (seeding phase). Even in very different healthcare systems, the same structural patterns emerge — supporting the universality of durability-focused interventions.
