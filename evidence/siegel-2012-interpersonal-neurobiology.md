# Interpersonal Neurobiology (Siegel 2012)

## Summary

Daniel Siegel's interpersonal neurobiology framework proposes that mental health is integration — the linkage of differentiated parts into a functional whole. The "River of Integration" model describes mental health as a flow between two banks: chaos (too much flexibility, fragmentation) and rigidity (too little flexibility, constriction). Healthy functioning flows in the middle.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- The mind emerges from the intersection of neural processes and interpersonal relationships
- Integration (linking differentiated parts) is the mechanism of mental health
- Co-regulation between nervous systems is a primary mechanism of emotional development
- The "River of Integration" model maps onto autonomic state: chaos = sympathetic overwhelm, rigidity = dorsal vagal shutdown, integration = ventral vagal flow
- Mirror neurons and resonance circuits enable interpersonal neural synchrony
