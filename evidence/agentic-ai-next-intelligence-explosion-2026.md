## Core Thesis

Intelligence explosions are social, not monolithic. Each prior step-change (primate sociality, language, writing/institutions) emerged from new forms of social aggregation, not individual cognitive upgrades. AI follows the same pattern. The singularity will be plural.

## Key Claims

### Society of thought inside reasoning models
- Frontier reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1, QwQ-32B) spontaneously simulate multi-agent debates within their chain of thought
- This "society of thought" causally accounts for accuracy gains on hard tasks
- Not trained for this behavior. Emerges from RL reward for reasoning accuracy alone
- Models are rediscovering that robust reasoning is a social process

### Design space from social science
- Team science, small-group sociology, social psychology become blueprints for next-gen AI architecture
- Current reasoning models produce a single conversation (AI town hall). Effective groups need hierarchy, specialization, structured disagreement
- Need systems supporting parallel, converging, diverging deliberation streams

### Intelligence history as social aggregation
- Primate intelligence scaled with social group size, not habitat difficulty
- Language created Tomasello's "cultural ratchet": cross-generational knowledge accumulation
- Writing/law/bureaucracy externalized social intelligence into infrastructure
- LLMs are the cultural ratchet made computationally active

### Human-AI centaurs
- Composite actors, neither purely human nor purely machine
- Configurations: one human directing many agents, one AI serving many humans, many-to-many shifting ensembles
- Agents can fork, differentiate, recombine. Recursive descent into collective deliberation

### Institutional alignment vs RLHF
- RLHF is dyadic (parent-child correction model), cannot scale to billions of agents
- Alternative: institutional alignment. Persistent role protocols (judge, attorney, jury) independent of who occupies them
- Digital equivalents of courtrooms, markets, bureaucracies needed for agent ecosystems

### Governance implications
- Constitutional structure: AI systems with distinct invested values checking and balancing each other
- Governance protocols built into agent-to-agent systems (verification, procedural delegation, collaboration scaffolds)
- Humans remain in the loop across all configurations
