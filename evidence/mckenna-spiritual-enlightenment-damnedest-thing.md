## Summary

Radical deconstruction of spiritual seeking. McKenna argues most spiritual practice is ego-reinforcement dressed as transcendence. The only method that works is "spiritual autolysis" - writing what you know to be true until nothing remains.

## Somnistic Relevance

The anti-comfort-zone pole of Randy's contemplative reading. McKenna's radical honesty maps to the Phase 4 truth-seeking turn in Randy's cognitive arc. The insistence on stripping away false frameworks connects to why Somnistics rejects breathwork-as-wellness-theater and insists on measurable physiological change.
