## Citation

Scripps Research & Allen Institute (2025). NIH Director's Transformative Research Award — Comprehensive Atlas of the Internal Sensory System. National Institutes of Health.

## Key Findings

- Nobel laureate Ardem Patapoutian (Scripps Research) and collaborators at the Allen Institute received $14.2 million over five years to create the first comprehensive atlas of interoception
- The project aims to map how internal organs communicate with the brain — the "hidden sixth sense"
- As co-investigator Jin noted: "Interoception is fundamental to nearly every aspect of health, but it remains a largely unexplored frontier of neuroscience"
- The atlas will catalog sensory neurons, their molecular profiles, and their connections to organ systems

## Relevance to SRL

This is a landmark investment that validates interoception as a major frontier. The atlas will provide foundational reference data for SRL's interoceptive literacy framework and could inform future measurement tools for concepts like anterocept and gap moment detection.

## Methodology

Multi-institutional collaboration using single-cell transcriptomics, neural circuit tracing, and functional imaging to map internal sensory pathways from organs to brain. Five-year timeline with NIH Director's Transformative Research Award funding.
