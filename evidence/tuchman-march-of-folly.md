## Summary

How governments pursue policies contrary to their own interests across four case studies. Tuchman's thesis: the pattern is systemic, not personal. Institutions generate self-destructive behavior through groupthink, sunk-cost commitment, and refusal to process contrary evidence.

## Somnistic Relevance

The institutional pathology that clinician burnout represents. Healthcare systems pursue policies (12-hour shifts, production pressure, inadequate recovery) that degrade their own workforce. Tuchman frames why: the institution can't hear the signal even when the evidence is clear. Pausality's enterprise pitch is essentially "stop marching toward folly."
