## Summary

Meta-analysis of RCTs examining whether mindfulness-based interventions can increase flow state occurrence, with pooled effect sizes and mechanistic analysis.

## Key Findings

- **Scope:** 8 RCTs, n=293 participants
- **Effect size:** MBIs had a positive effect on flow (SMD=0.777, p<0.0001) — a medium-to-large effect
- **Heterogeneity:** Low (I²=22.59%), indicating consistent effects across studies
- **Mechanisms:** Enhanced attention, present-moment awareness, reduced self-criticism
- **Dose:** Median intervention length of 7 weeks
- **Implication:** Mindfulness training is a validated pathway to increased flow — the causal link is now established via meta-analytic evidence

## SRL Relevance

Establishes the causal pathway: mindfulness training → flow state. This validates Gap Moment Training as a flow-enabling intervention, not just a stress reducer. The SMD=0.777 is a strong effect for a behavioral intervention. The 7-week median dose informs protocol design but also highlights the opportunity for minimum effective dose optimization.
