## Summary
Randomized controlled trial showing resilience increased significantly in the HRVB group among ED/ICU healthcare workers. Protocol: 30-min introduction + 10-min self-practice twice daily. Supports HRVB as a feasible, individually-delivered intervention.

## Relevance to Pausality
Gold-standard RCT evidence for our modality in our exact target population. The 10-min practice duration validates that brief, targeted HRV interventions work — our 60-second Gap Moment micro-interventions are an even more accessible format. Key citation for FDA/clinical validation pathway.
