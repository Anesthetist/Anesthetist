## Summary

The story of Navinder Sarao and the 2010 Flash Crash. How one trader's spoofing contributed to a trillion-dollar market event. The fragility of interconnected systems.

## Somnistic Relevance

Systemic fragility as a concept. Complex, tightly-coupled systems (markets, operating rooms) can cascade catastrophically from small perturbations. The Flash Crash is a financial version of an anesthesia crisis: everything looks stable until it isn't, and recovery depends on who's at the controls.
