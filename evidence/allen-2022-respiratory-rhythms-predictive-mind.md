## Summary

Allen, Varga & Heck (2022) proposed a predictive coding model of respiratory-brain coupling. Published in *Psychological Review* 129(5):1066-1086.

## Key Findings

1. Breathing rhythmically modulates neural gain
2. Proposes predictive coding model of respiratory-brain coupling
3. Respiratory phase influences perception, attention, and emotion
4. Breathing is not just metabolic — it's an active sampling mechanism for the brain
5. Provides theoretical basis for why breathwork changes cognition and emotion

## SRL Relevance

The theoretical paper that explains WHY [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] works. Breathing isn't just calming — it's modulating the brain's predictive processing. Every breath is an active inference cycle.
