## Citation

Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback and Mental Stress Myocardial Flow Reserve: A Randomized Clinical Trial. *JAMA Network Open*, 2026.

## Key Findings

- Randomized clinical trial examining whether HRV biofeedback training improves myocardial blood flow reserve during mental stress challenge in patients with coronary artery disease
- Demonstrates cardiovascular benefits of HRV biofeedback beyond psychological outcomes
- Provides evidence that autonomic regulation training has measurable physiological impact on cardiac function under stress

## Relevance to SRL

Strongly supports the Autonomic Regulation and Resonant Breathing Frequency concepts by demonstrating that HRV biofeedback produces measurable cardiovascular improvements under stress. This is directly relevant to Clinician Durability — if HRV training protects cardiac function during mental stress, it offers a physiological defense mechanism for high-stakes clinical work.

## Methodology

Randomized clinical trial; patients with coronary artery disease; HRV biofeedback training vs. control; myocardial perfusion imaging during mental stress challenge.
