## Summary
Fadden et al. (2025) evaluated a brief three-session HRV biofeedback (HRV-BF) intervention in nursing students, measuring its impact on worry (Penn State Worry Questionnaire) and resilience (Brief Resilience Scale). The intervention produced statistically significant reductions in worry and improvements in resilience scores, demonstrating that even a minimal-dose biofeedback protocol can produce measurable psychophysiological benefits in a healthcare trainee population.

## Key Finding for SRL
This study is a near-direct analogue of SRL's target use case: brief HRV biofeedback improving resilience in healthcare workers-in-training. The three-session protocol validates SRL's "minimum effective dose" philosophy, and the nursing student population overlaps with SRL's clinician durability market (Red Arrow 1). These results can be cited directly in CEU course materials and enterprise pitch decks for nursing education programs.
