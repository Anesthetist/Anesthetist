# Slow Breathing Reduces Stress Biomarkers in VR Active Shooter Training (Dillard 2023)

## Summary

Slow breathing intervention reduces salivary alpha-amylase (a biomarker of sympathetic nervous system activation) during virtual reality active shooter training scenarios. This provides objective biomarker evidence — beyond self-report — that controlled breathing works under acute stress conditions that simulate real-world high-stakes environments.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Slow breathing produces measurable reduction in stress biomarkers (not just subjective report)
- Effect holds under acute, high-fidelity stress simulation (VR active shooter)
- Salivary alpha-amylase as additional outcome metric beyond HRV
- Validates breathing intervention for first responder populations
