## Overview

The Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC) is a 25-item self-report measure of resilience encompassing personal competence, trust in one's instincts, tolerance of negative affect, positive acceptance of change, and control. A widely used 10-item short form (CD-RISC-10) captures the core construct with reduced respondent burden. The scale is licensed through Mind Garden.

## Key Findings

1. 25 items measuring five factors: personal competence/tenacity, trust in one's instincts/tolerance of negative affect, positive acceptance of change/secure relationships, control, and spiritual influences
2. CD-RISC-10 short form is widely used in research and clinical settings as a practical compromise between comprehensiveness and brevity
3. Sensitive to treatment effects, making it suitable for intervention studies and clinical trials
4. Validated across clinical populations (PTSD, depression, anxiety) and general community samples
5. Licensed through Mind Garden, requiring per-use fees for research and commercial applications

## Connection to Somnistics

The CD-RISC is more comprehensive than the BRS but requires licensing, which adds cost and administrative friction to enterprise pilots. The CD-RISC-10 represents a practical compromise — short enough for app-based use, validated enough for peer-reviewed research. If SRL pursues formal clinical trials for [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]], the CD-RISC-10 provides stronger psychometric grounding than briefer free alternatives.
