# Mind Over Machine

Hubert and Stuart Dreyfus's foundational work on the **five-stage model of skill acquisition**: Novice, Advanced Beginner, Competent, Proficient, Expert.

## Key Contributions

- Challenged the dominant AI paradigm that expertise could be reduced to rule-following
- Demonstrated that expert performance relies on intuitive pattern recognition, not analytical decomposition
- The five stages describe a progression from rigid rule-adherence (Novice) to fluid, context-sensitive intuition (Expert)
- Expert performance is characterized by "seeing" the right action rather than calculating it

## Five Stages

1. **Novice** — Follows context-free rules
2. **Advanced Beginner** — Recognizes situational elements through experience
3. **Competent** — Develops hierarchical decision procedures, feels responsible for outcomes
4. **Proficient** — Sees situations holistically, knows what to do but must decide how
5. **Expert** — Intuitively does what works; performance is fluid and non-analytical
