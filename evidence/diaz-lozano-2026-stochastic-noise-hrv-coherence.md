# Stochastic Noise Enhances HRV Coherence (Diaz-Lozano et al. 2026)

## Summary

Published in *Physiology & Behavior* (November 2025), this study demonstrated that embedding stochastic auditory noise within slow-tempo music amplified HRV coherence near the 0.1 Hz resonant frequency via stochastic resonance. The dose-response followed an inverted-U curve: intermediate noise levels optimized coherence, while too little or too much noise reduced the effect. Critically, each participant had a unique optimal noise level, supporting personalized intervention design.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Stochastic resonance can amplify HRV coherence at the 0.1 Hz resonant frequency
- Inverted-U dose-response curve — intermediate noise is optimal
- Individual variation in optimal noise level requires personalization
- Auditory stimulation can modulate cardiac coherence without explicit breathing instruction
- Music-based intervention pathway for HRV coherence enhancement
