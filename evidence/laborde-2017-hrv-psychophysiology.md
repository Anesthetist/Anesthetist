# HRV and Cardiac Vagal Tone in Psychophysiology (Laborde, Mosley & Thayer 2017)

## Summary

Practical guidelines for researchers using HRV as a measure of cardiac vagal tone. Recommends RMSSD as the primary time-domain metric (more robust than HF-HRV for short recordings). Details standardized recording conditions, artifact detection, and interpretation frameworks. Essential methodological reference for any HRV-based intervention study.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- RMSSD is the recommended primary metric for vagal tone (validates Pausality's measurement approach)
- Short recordings (1-5 minutes) adequate for RMSSD — supports 60-second pre/post measurement
- Standardized conditions essential for valid comparison — informs Pausality's measurement protocol
- Thayer's neurovisceral integration model connects HRV to prefrontal cognitive function
