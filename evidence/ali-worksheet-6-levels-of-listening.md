# ALI Worksheet: 6 Levels of Listening

## Framework Summary

This self-assessment worksheet expands the Deep Listening model (3 basic + 2 advanced levels) into a 6-level developmental scale with specific behavioral indicators at each level. Each level includes three self-rating items, creating a structured diagnostic of listening capacity.

### The 6 Levels

**Level 1 — Distracted Listening**
- Conversation does not require significant or focused attention
- Primary intention is to conserve energy while engaging as well as possible
- Paying enough attention to shift to a different level if needed

**Level 2 — Multi Focused Listening**
- Shared understanding that texting/checking devices may occur
- Attention is split across multiple streams simultaneously

**Level 3 — Focused Listening**
- Full attention directed to the speaker
- Active tracking of content and basic engagement

**Level 4 — Deep Listening**
- Shared agreement that conversation is a priority, usually with a set appointment
- Gifting full, loving attention while also monitoring own emotional state
- Actively tracking what they are saying, feeling, and meaning

**Level 5 — Inspired Listening**
- Explicit commitment container created and honored
- So present to who they are and what they want that inspiration regularly comes through
- Actively tracking saying, feeling, meaning, AND being

**Level 6 — Inspiring Listening**
- Explicit commitment container created and honored
- Deeply present to both — to their whole person: body, mind, and spirit
- So present to who they are that inspiration regularly comes through them

### Key Developmental Transitions

The critical shift occurs between Levels 3→4: from focused attention to loving attention with simultaneous self-monitoring. Level 4 introduces the dual-awareness requirement — tracking the other AND one's own emotional state. Levels 5→6 mark the shift from inspiration coming TO the listener to inspiration coming THROUGH the person being listened to — the listener's presence catalyzes the other's access to their own deeper knowing.

## Somnistic Relevance

This assessment tool maps directly onto the attentional and co-regulatory capacities central to Somnistics:

- **Listening levels as attentional sovereignty stages**: The 6 levels describe a developmental arc of attentional capacity — from fragmented (distracted) through focused through dual-aware (deep) to transpersonal (inspiring). This IS the attentional sovereignty continuum: the capacity to deliberately allocate and sustain attention at increasing levels of depth and multiplicity.
- **Level 4 as nondual clinical awareness threshold**: The simultaneous tracking of the other's experience AND one's own emotional state is precisely the dual-awareness that defines nondual clinical awareness in the clinical setting. A CRNA monitoring a patient while simultaneously tracking their own somatic and emotional state is operating at Level 4+.
- **Co-regulation capacity as listening level**: Higher listening levels correlate directly with co-regulatory capacity. At Level 6, the listener's presence is sufficiently regulated and open that the speaker accesses states they cannot reach alone — the interpersonal physiological synchrony is so deep that the listener's autonomic state directly facilitates the speaker's self-organization.
- **Self-assessment as interoceptive diagnostic**: The rating format (blanks for each indicator) creates a structured self-assessment of one's current listening/presence capacity — a metacognitive snapshot that is itself an exercise in self-remembering. Where you rate yourself reveals both your capacity AND your self-awareness of that capacity.
- **Diagnostic-therapeutic unity**: Using this worksheet IS both the assessment of listening capacity and the beginning of its development. The act of noticing which level one typically operates at brings awareness to the attentional default — and awareness of the default is the first step in upgrading it.
