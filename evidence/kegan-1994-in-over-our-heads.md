## Summary

Kegan's constructive-developmental framework describes how adults progressively transform their relationship to meaning-making through five orders of consciousness:

- **Stage 1 (Impulsive)** — perception-bound, impulse-driven
- **Stage 2 (Imperial)** — needs/interests as organizing principle
- **Stage 3 (Socialized Mind)** — shaped by expectations of others, relational embeddedness
- **Stage 4 (Self-Authoring Mind)** — internal authority, self-directed ideology, owns value systems
- **Stage 5 (Self-Transforming Mind)** — holds multiple meaning-systems simultaneously, sees through one's own ideology

Each transition involves making "subject" into "object" — what previously controlled you becomes something you can observe and choose. The 4-to-5 transition is rare and involves the capacity to hold multiple self-systems simultaneously.

## SRL Relevance

Provides the developmental psychology scaffolding for Somnistics' user journey model. The Kegan 4→5 transition maps to the shift from "doing breathing exercises" (self-authored practice) to "becoming someone who naturally self-regulates" (self-transforming capacity). This is the theoretical grounding for why Gap Moment Training works differently at different developmental stages. Randy maps his own agency-consciousness integration arc directly to Kegan stages, and ChatGPT correlates Stage 5 with higher vagal tone — the capacity for calm engagement while holding multiple meaning-systems. Neurophysiologically, the 4→5 transition involves flexible switching between Default Mode, Executive Control, and Salience networks.
