# Ambient AI Scribes: Burnout Relief Without Time Savings (JAMA 2024 + 2025)

## Summary

A pair of findings that together pose a puzzle — and resolving the puzzle points at interoception.

**JAMA Network Open (2024):** Large observational study of clinicians adopting ambient AI scribes reported burnout prevalence dropping from **51.9% → 38.8%** among adopters. A striking delta.

**JAMA (2025 follow-up):** When researchers actually measured documentation time, adopters saved only **~16 minutes per shift**. After-hours EHR time (pajama time) did not meaningfully change.

The two findings are in tension under the obvious interpretation ("AI scribes reduce burnout by saving time"). 16 minutes is not the difference between a burned-out clinician and a non-burned-out clinician.

## The Mechanistic Reframe (SRL interpretation)

If the time delta is small but the burnout delta is large, the mechanism is not time-driven. It is **attention-driven**.

When a clinician is documenting during the encounter, attention is bifurcated between the patient and the chart. This is a chronic state of interoceptive and attentional incoherence — the clinician cannot fully inhabit the room. The ambient scribe's actual gift is not saved minutes; it is **restored attentional coherence during the patient encounter**. The clinician is allowed to be a whole person in the room again.

That is an interoceptive mechanism, not a scheduling one — and every healthcare organization that treats provider distress as a staffing/hours problem is missing it.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- The JAMA dataset is doing the interoceptive-reframe argument for us. The numbers force the reader to the mechanism.
- This bridges the burnout literature (which usually doesn't engage interoception) and the interoception literature (which usually doesn't engage burnout).
- Provides a quantitative hook for the SRL thesis that attentional/interoceptive coherence is a patient-safety variable, not a wellness concern.
- Burnout interventions that fail to restore attentional coherence will not move the needle regardless of how many hours they save.

## Clinical Relevance for CRNAs

CRNAs spend a meaningful fraction of every case documenting in the anesthesia record. The attentional fragmentation hypothesis predicts that better anesthesia documentation tools will reduce CRNA burnout out of proportion to the time they save, and that interoceptive training is complementary (not redundant) to those tools.
