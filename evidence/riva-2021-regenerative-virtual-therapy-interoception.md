## Summary
Riva et al. (2021) propose "Regenerative Virtual Therapy" (RVT), a framework combining virtual reality, interoceptive modulation, and mindfulness to update maladaptive body representations. The approach is grounded in a Bayesian body matrix model, where the brain maintains predictive models of bodily states that can become pathologically rigid. By using multisensory VR environments paired with interoceptive feedback (heartbeat, breathing, thermal), RVT aims to create controlled prediction-error signals that force the brain to update these internal models.

## Key Finding for SRL
The Bayesian body matrix model provides a theoretical framework for understanding why SRL's interoceptive interventions work at a computational level — they generate calibrated prediction errors that update the brain's internal model of bodily states. The VR + interoception combination also represents a future product opportunity for SRL, and the paper's emphasis on multisensory integration validates SRL's multi-modal approach (breathing + haptics + audio) over single-channel interventions.
