## Summary
Price and Hooven (2018) describe the theory and approach of Mindful Awareness in Body-Oriented Therapy (MABT), an intervention that teaches three core interoceptive awareness skills: identifying internal body sensations, accessing or sustaining awareness of those sensations, and appraising their significance for emotional regulation. MABT has demonstrated increased MAIA scores in randomized trials, with particularly strong results in substance use disorder populations where interoceptive deficits are pronounced.

## Key Finding for SRL
MABT provides a validated, manualized model for training interoceptive awareness skills — essentially a clinical proof-of-concept for what SRL aims to deliver at scale through technology. The three-skill framework (identify, access, appraise) maps to SRL's own protocol design, and the demonstrated MAIA score improvements provide benchmark effect sizes for SRL's clinical outcome targets. The substance use disorder results also open a specific clinical market opportunity.
