# Interoception, Contemplative Practice, and Health (Farb 2019)

## Summary

This review establishes the neuroscience bridge between contemplative practice and interoceptive processing, showing that mindfulness training produces measurable neuroplastic changes in the insula (the brain's interoceptive hub) and corpus callosum (inter-hemispheric communication). These changes correlate with improved emotional regulation, body awareness, and clinical outcomes.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Contemplative practice reshapes interoceptive brain circuits (insula, ACC)
- Corpus callosum changes suggest improved inter-hemispheric integration
- Interoceptive accuracy improves with systematic training
- The changes are neuroplastic — they persist beyond the practice session
