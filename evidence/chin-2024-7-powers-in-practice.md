# 7 Powers in Practice

## Summary

Chin bridges Hamilton Helmer's theoretical 7 Powers framework with practical business histories, surfacing four key operational insights that the framework alone doesn't convey.

## Four Operational Insights

### 1. Competitive Arbitrage Shows Up Late
Uncertainty protects early challengers longer than expected.
- **Shopify:** Grew for years in a "strangely empty space" with minimal serious competition
- **AWS:** Took a **full decade** before market profitability became visible; competitors remained unaware until 2015 financial disclosure
- Knowledge gaps create protective moats; aggressive competitors may overlook markets they don't fully understand

### 2. Uncertainty as the First Moat
Jerry Neumann (VC): When markets involve genuine invention, neither deduction nor induction can predict outcomes. Incumbent firms can't model uncertain futures → they can't enter. Apple got 5 crucial years in PCs because mainframe companies couldn't forecast the market.

### 3. Moatless Companies Persist Longer Than Expected
Data General (from *Soul of a New Machine*) lacked sustainable competitive advantage yet remained viable for **two decades** after losing Power. Margin compression operates on decade-long timescales.

### 4. Building Brand Power Requires Continuous Innovation
Nike's path: Waffle trainer (1974) → Nike Air → Flyknits → Vaporfly → competitors match within months. Brand power comes from **sustained innovation that competitors must perpetually chase**, not from advertising alone.

## SRL Relevance

**Insight 1 (late arbitrage) is SRL's current advantage.** The CRNA autonomic training niche is too small and too clinical for Calm/Headspace/Lyra to notice or prioritize. Uncertainty about whether "breathing apps for nurse anesthetists" is a real market protects SRL.

**Insight 2 (uncertainty as moat) applies directly.** The intersection of polyvagal neuroscience + CRNA clinical practice + micro-dose delivery + wearable biometrics is genuinely novel. Incumbents can't model it because the category doesn't exist yet.

**Insight 3 (persistence) is a warning.** Even without strong Power, SRL can persist for years — but persistence without Power means eventual margin compression. The vault, the clinical credibility, and the CRNA network must compound into actual Power (cornered resource + brand + switching costs).

**Insight 4 (continuous innovation) is the mandate.** NeuroMinutes must keep evolving. The composable architecture (TM'd concepts as stackable layers) is the innovation engine that prevents competitors from matching a static product.
