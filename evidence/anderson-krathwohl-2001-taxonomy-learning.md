# A Taxonomy for Learning, Teaching, and Assessing (Bloom's Revised)

Anderson and Krathwohl's revision of Bloom's original 1956 taxonomy of educational objectives. The revised taxonomy introduces a two-dimensional framework: the **Knowledge Dimension** (factual, conceptual, procedural, metacognitive) crossed with the **Cognitive Process Dimension** (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create).

## Key Changes from Original Bloom

- Changed from nouns to verbs (Knowledge → Remember, Comprehension → Understand, etc.)
- Swapped the top two levels: Synthesis → Create (now highest), Evaluation → Evaluate
- Added the Knowledge Dimension as a separate axis
- Added "Metacognitive Knowledge" as a knowledge type — awareness of one's own cognition

## Revised Cognitive Process Dimension

1. **Remember** — Retrieve relevant knowledge from long-term memory
2. **Understand** — Construct meaning from instructional messages
3. **Apply** — Carry out or use a procedure in a given situation
4. **Analyze** — Break material into constituent parts; determine relationships
5. **Evaluate** — Make judgments based on criteria and standards
6. **Create** — Put elements together to form a coherent whole; reorganize into a new pattern

## Relevance to SRL

Used in the [[cognitive-development-arc]] to track the progression of Randy's cognitive operations across six developmental phases — from Remember/Understand/Apply (Phase 1) through Analyze/Evaluate (Phases 2-4) to Create and "Meta-Create" (Phases 5-6). The metacognitive knowledge dimension is particularly relevant to [[embodied-metacognition]].
