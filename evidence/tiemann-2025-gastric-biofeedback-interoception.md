## Summary

Tiemann et al. (2025) conducted the first study of EGG-based gastric biofeedback for interoception training. Published in *Physiology & Behavior* 295:115108.

## Key Findings

1. Novel EGG-based biofeedback using VR vs 2D display
2. 2D biofeedback was MORE effective than VR for improving gastric interoception
3. Gastric interoception is trainable via biofeedback
4. Improvements extended to general interoceptive awareness
5. Opens new intervention paradigm beyond cardiac biofeedback

## SRL Relevance

Proves that interoceptive training generalizes beyond the cardiac domain. If SRL expands beyond HRV to multi-organ biofeedback, gastric EGG is a validated pathway. The 2D > VR finding also validates screen-based delivery.
