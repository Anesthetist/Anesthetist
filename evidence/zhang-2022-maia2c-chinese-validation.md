# Psychometric Evaluation of MAIA-2C (Chinese Validation)

## Summary

Translation and psychometric validation of MAIA-2 into simplified Chinese (MAIA-2C) in N=627 mainland Chinese university students (18-26, mostly without formal mind-body training).

## Key Findings

- **7-factor model** (not 8) — Noticing subscale removed due to low internal consistency (α ≈ 0.58) and factor fragmentation
- **31 items** retained (6 dropped from original 37)
- Item 16 (breath attention) loads with Self-Regulation rather than Attention Regulation
- Item 15 (pain rumination) forms its own factor and was dropped
- Total scale α = 0.822; subscales 0.656-0.838
- CFA: RMSEA = 0.060, SRMR = 0.081, CFI = 0.890 (borderline)
- All MAIA-2C subscales correlate negatively with trait anxiety (STAI-T; r ≈ -0.08 to -0.35)
- Non-judging shows *negative* correlations with interoceptive subscales in this naive sample (opposite of trained practitioners)

## Relevance to Somnistics

- Validates that interoceptive factor structure differs in naive vs. trained populations
- Confirms breath is experienced as regulation before attention in beginners
- Informs measurement strategy for scaling Pausality internationally
- Supports progressive curriculum design: facets develop at different rates
