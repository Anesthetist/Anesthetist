## Summary

Translation and commentary on Patanjali's foundational text. The systematic map of consciousness states, attentional training, and the relationship between breath, mind, and awareness.

## Somnistic Relevance

The original operating manual for the breath-awareness-consciousness axis. Patanjali's eight limbs encode the same progression Somnistics operationalizes: ethical ground, posture, breath regulation, sensory withdrawal, concentration, meditation, absorption. The sutras on pranayama are the historical root of everything Pausality teaches.
