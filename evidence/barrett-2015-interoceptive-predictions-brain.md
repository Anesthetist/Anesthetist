## Overview

Barrett and Simmons (2015) introduce the Embodied Predictive Interoception Coding (EPIC) model in *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, integrating an anatomical model of corticocortical connections with Bayesian active inference principles. The paper proposes that agranular visceromotor cortices contribute to interoception by issuing interoceptive predictions, and that disruptions in these predictions could function as a common vulnerability for mental and physical illness.

## Key Findings

1. The brain continuously generates interoceptive predictions — interoception is inherently predictive, not just reactive
2. Hierarchical processing flows from brainstem to insular cortex to prefrontal cortex
3. Visceromotor predictions flow top-down; prediction errors flow bottom-up through the cortical hierarchy
4. Agranular visceromotor cortices generate predictions; granular cortices compute prediction errors
5. Disruptions in interoceptive predictions may function as a transdiagnostic vulnerability for mental and physical illness

## Connection to Somnistics

This paper explains the neuroanatomy of why interoceptive training creates lasting change — you are reshaping the brain's hierarchical prediction machinery, not just teaching a momentary coping skill. The EPIC model provides anatomical grounding for SRL's claim that [[interoceptive-literacy]] training produces durable neural reorganization, supporting the shift from acute intervention to sustained [[autonomic-regulation]] capacity.
