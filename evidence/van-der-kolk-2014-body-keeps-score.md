# The Body Keeps the Score (Van der Kolk 2014)

## Summary

Bessel van der Kolk's comprehensive work synthesizes decades of trauma research to argue that trauma fundamentally reshapes the body's alarm systems — autonomic regulation, interoception, and proprioception all become disrupted. The book demonstrates that effective trauma treatment must engage the body, not just cognition. Van der Kolk presents evidence for yoga, breathing practices, EMDR, neurofeedback, and theater/movement as therapeutic interventions. For SRL, this text provides the clinical foundation for why breathwork and interoceptive training are not merely "wellness" tools but address the same neurophysiological substrate that trauma disrupts. It validates the approach of using body-based micro-interventions to restore autonomic flexibility.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Trauma is stored somatically — the body's alarm system remains activated even when the threat has passed
- Interoceptive awareness (the ability to sense internal bodily states) is often impaired in trauma survivors and chronically stressed individuals
- Yoga and breathing practices restore interoceptive capacity and autonomic regulation
- Top-down (cognitive) approaches alone are insufficient for resolving autonomic dysregulation
- Body-based interventions restore the "window of tolerance" — the range of autonomic arousal in which a person can function effectively
- HRV is a reliable biomarker for autonomic flexibility and stress resilience
