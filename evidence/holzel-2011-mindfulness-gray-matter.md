# Mindfulness Practice Increases Gray Matter Density (Holzel et al. 2011)

## Summary

This landmark study demonstrated that 8 weeks of Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) produces measurable increases in gray matter density in brain regions associated with learning, memory (hippocampus), self-referential processing (PCC), perspective-taking (TPJ), and emotional regulation. First longitudinal neuroimaging evidence that meditation produces structural neuroplastic changes.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- 8-week practice produces measurable structural brain changes (neuroplasticity is real and fast)
- Changes in hippocampus, PCC, TPJ, cerebellum — all regions relevant to somnistics training
- Supports the [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day curriculum design — if 8 weeks changes structure, 21 days changes function
- Insular cortex changes support [[anterocept]] training efficacy

## Concepts Derived

- [[anterocept]] — insular cortex neuroplasticity validates interoceptive training
- [[neuro-ouroboros]] — structural changes emerge from systematic progressive training
- [[interoception]] — brain regions that change are the interoceptive processing network
- [[hemispheric-rebalancing]] — TPJ changes support right-hemisphere broad awareness
