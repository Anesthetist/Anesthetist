## Citation

Wittmann, M. (2015). Modulations of the experience of self and time. *Consciousness and Cognition*, 38, 172–181. [DOI: 10.1016/j.concog.2015.06.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2015.06.008)

## Key Findings

- Time perception is **embodied**: the bodily self — the continuous stream of interoceptive input — is the functional anchor of both phenomenal experience and the mental self
- Subjective time emerges through the existence of the self across time as an enduring, embodied entity
- **The Self-Time Link:** An increased awareness of oneself coincides with an increased awareness of time. Conversely, a decreased awareness of the self is associated with diminished awareness of time.
- This relationship is most clearly revealed in altered states of consciousness:
  - **Meditation:** Deep meditative states reduce self-referential processing AND time awareness simultaneously
  - **Psychedelics:** Hallucinogens dissolve ego boundaries AND distort temporal experience in parallel
  - **Flow states:** Loss of self-consciousness → loss of time awareness
  - **Psychiatric conditions:** Depersonalization and dissociation involve both self-disturbance and time disturbance
- The body is the common denominator: interoceptive signals anchor both the sense of self and the sense of time

## The Self-Time Coupling Principle

| Self-Awareness | Time Awareness | State |
|---------------|----------------|-------|
| High | High | Normal waking, anxiety, hypervigilance |
| Moderate | Moderate | Relaxed awareness, optimal performance |
| Low | Low | Flow, deep meditation, psychedelic dissolution |
| Disrupted | Disrupted | Dissociation, depersonalization, trauma response |

## Relevance to SRL

**The self-time link is one of the most important theoretical bridges for SRL:**

- **Gap Moment Training** modulates self-awareness, which by Wittmann's model simultaneously modulates temporal experience. When a CRNA enters a gap moment, they briefly heighten self-awareness (interoceptive check-in) which also heightens temporal awareness — creating the subjective sense of "more time to act."
- **The clinical paradox:** Under acute stress, CRNAs lose self-awareness (survival mode) AND lose temporal accuracy (time compression). GMT reverses both simultaneously by restoring the interoceptive anchor.
- **Flow in clinical practice:** Senior clinicians in the Fortifying phase ([[gmt-career-arc]]) report reduced self-consciousness during complex cases — Wittmann's model predicts the accompanying time distortion ("I looked up and two hours had passed").
- **NeuroMinute scripts** that direct attention to body sensations are simultaneously training temporal awareness — the self-time link means interoceptive training IS temporal training.
- **Trauma-informed design implications:** Dissociative states involve both self- and time-disturbance. Carefully titrated interoceptive practices restore both.
