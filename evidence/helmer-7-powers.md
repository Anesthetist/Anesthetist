## Summary

Framework for durable competitive advantage. Seven sources of strategic power: scale economies, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, process power.

## Somnistic Relevance

The strategic lens for evaluating Pausality's defensibility. Which of the seven powers does SRL build? Cornered resource (Randy's 28 years + vault), counter-positioning (clinical rigor vs. wellness fluff), and process power (the GMT training methodology) are the candidates. Every product decision should strengthen at least one.
