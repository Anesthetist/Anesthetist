## Overview

Carrasco-Poyatos et al. (2023) conducted an 8-week randomized controlled trial comparing HRV-guided training to standard high-intensity interval training (HIIT) in cardiac rehabilitation patients. The HRV-guided approach used daily autonomic readiness assessments to modulate exercise intensity, demonstrating that individualized dosing based on autonomic state achieved superior cardiovascular outcomes at lower training volume.

## Key Findings

1. **8-week RCT comparing HRV-guided training vs standard HIIT** in cardiac rehabilitation, providing a direct head-to-head comparison of adaptive vs fixed protocols.
2. **HRV-guided training achieved better blood pressure reduction** than traditional HIIT, demonstrating superior cardiovascular outcomes from autonomic-informed dosing.
3. **Improved heart rate recovery** in the HRV-guided group, indicating enhanced parasympathetic reactivation capacity.
4. **Better cardioprotection achieved at LOWER training volume**, establishing that less-but-smarter dosing outperforms more-but-fixed programming.
5. **Validates HRV-readiness-based exercise prescription** as a superior model for cardiac rehabilitation in aging populations.
