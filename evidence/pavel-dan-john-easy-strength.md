## Summary

Strength training for people whose primary sport isn't lifting. Never miss reps, never grind, train frequently at submaximal loads, get strong without getting beat up.

## Somnistic Relevance

The somatic companion to Kettlebell Simple & Sinister. Same minimum effective dose philosophy. "Easy strength" is the training analog to Pausality's 60-second protocol: the intervention that compounds without depleting. Strength as a skill, not a test.
