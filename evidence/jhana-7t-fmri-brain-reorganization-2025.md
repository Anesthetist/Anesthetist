## Citation

(2025). Advanced concentrative absorption meditation reorganizes functional connectivity gradients of the brain: 7T MRI and phenomenology case study of jhana meditation. *PubMed*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40215476/

## Key Findings

- Jhana meditation induces a **disruption of the hierarchical organization of the brain**, shifting functional connectivity gradients toward a more globally integrated rather than segregated state.
- Associated with decreased within-network modularity, increased global functional connectivity, and desegregation of the default mode and visual networks.
- Extreme positive affect (joy, bliss) in early jhanas correlates with activation of the **nucleus accumbens** (dopamine/opioid reward system).
- Jhana constitutes a distinct, structured mode of consciousness with reproducible trends in local brain activity, macroscale gradients, and global eigenmodes — measurable at ultra-high field (7T) fMRI.

## Methodology

- Intensive case study design using 7T fMRI — highest-field-strength MRI available for human research.
- Expert meditator with extensive jhana practice.
- Neurophenomenological approach: first-person reports correlated with third-person neuroimaging.
- Measures: functional connectivity gradients, within-network modularity, global eigenmodes.
