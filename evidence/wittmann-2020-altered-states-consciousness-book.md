## Citation

Wittmann, M. (2018/2020). *Altered States of Consciousness: Experiences Out of Time and Self.* MIT Press. ISBN: 978-0262542524. (Original German 2018; English translation 2020.)

## Summary

Wittmann's second book, extending his embodied time framework into the domain of altered states of consciousness. Explores how meditation, psychedelics, near-death experiences, extreme sports, dreaming, and floatation tanks all share a common phenomenological signature: the dissolution of normal time experience AND normal self experience simultaneously.

## Key Themes

- **Out of time, out of self:** Altered states consistently involve parallel changes in time perception and self-awareness — confirming the self-time link from a phenomenological rather than neuroscientific angle
- **Meditation as ASC:** Deep meditation is an altered state of consciousness, not merely relaxation. The temporal and self-related changes are the evidence.
- **Psychedelic-meditation convergence:** Psilocybin and deep meditation produce overlapping phenomenology — ego dissolution, time distortion, unity experiences — suggesting shared neural mechanisms
- **Near-death experiences:** The "life review" and time dilation during NDEs reflect extreme insular activation as the brain processes a massive interoceptive crisis
- **Flow as altered state:** Flow involves both self-dissolution (loss of self-consciousness) and time-dissolution (loss of time awareness) — it IS an altered state, not just "being in the zone"
- **The body always participates:** Even in seemingly "disembodied" experiences (astral projection, NDEs), the body's interoceptive processing continues — altered states are always embodied states
