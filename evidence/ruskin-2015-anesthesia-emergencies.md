# Anesthesia Emergencies, 2nd Edition (Ruskin & Rosenbaum, 2015)

## Summary

*Anesthesia Emergencies* is a comprehensive clinical reference covering the full spectrum of perioperative crises, organized in the second edition by priority during emergencies — what to assess first, what to treat first, what can wait. The book covers airway emergencies, cardiovascular crises, respiratory failures, pharmacological emergencies, equipment failures, and subspecialty-specific crises (obstetric, pediatric, cardiac, neurological).

The second edition (2015) added two significant chapters: **crisis resource management** (CRM) and **disaster medicine** — reflecting the field's growing recognition that emergency performance depends not only on clinical knowledge but on communication, team dynamics, and cognitive load management under pressure.

- **1st Edition (2011):** ISBN 978-0195396713, Oxford University Press
- **2nd Edition (2015):** ISBN 978-0199377275, Oxford University Press, 480 pages

## Role in Randy's Clinical Development

Randy used this book not as a passive reference but as an **active visualization tool** — a library of emergency scenarios to mentally rehearse (see [[blue-angels-embodied-visualization]]). The practice:

1. Select an emergency scenario from the book
2. Visualize the case proceeding normally, then introduce the emergency
3. Mentally walk through: What does failure look like? What do I notice? What's going wrong? What are my options? What do I do?
4. Visualize every physical step of the response — drawing up medications, doses, motor sequences
5. Repeat until the response feels automatic

This transforms a reference book into a **procedural memory training system.** The scenarios in the book become the training reps; the gap moments between cases become the training windows.

## Connection to SRL Framework

### Crisis Resource Management and Somnistics

The 2nd edition's CRM chapter validates a key somnistics thesis: technical knowledge alone is insufficient for emergency performance. CRM principles include:

- **Situation awareness** — maintaining a global view under pressure (relates to [[attention-as-gain-control]])
- **Decision-making under uncertainty** — choosing action paths when information is incomplete
- **Communication and teamwork** — co-regulation under stress (relates to [[co-regulation-team-breath]])
- **Cognitive load management** — preventing overload during crises (relates to [[bandwidth-saturation-micro-events]])

What CRM describes at the team and cognitive level, Gap Moment Training addresses at the autonomic level. A CRNA whose nervous system is regulated will naturally perform CRM principles better — better situation awareness, calmer communication, lower cognitive load, more accurate decision-making.

### The Error Scenario Library

The emergency scenarios in this book map to the failure modes that GMT's Error Reversal Loop drill prepares clinicians for. Each scenario is essentially an answer to the question: "What could go wrong, and what would you do?" The visualization practice answers the deeper question: "What would it *feel like* when it goes wrong, and can you stay regulated enough to execute the response?"
