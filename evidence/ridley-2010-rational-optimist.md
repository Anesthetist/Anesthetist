## Summary

Ridley's core argument: innovation accelerates when concepts recombine across domains, analogous to genetic recombination producing fitter organisms. The central metaphor — "ideas having sex" (Chapter 1) — describes how prosperity is a product of exchange and recombination, not individual genius. When ideas from different domains meet and combine, the offspring are more complex and capable than either parent. Specialization enables exchange, and exchange enables innovation at increasing rates.

## SRL Relevance

Randy initiated a dedicated exploration of this concept: "Let's just play with the idea of 'ideas having sex'" — framing Somnistics as a deliberate "fertility lab for ideas" where normally-siloed domains are brought into contact. Specific recombinations Randy has executed: anesthesiology precision + contemplative neuroscience = Gap Moment Training; nursing theory (embodied empathy) + AI signal processing = real-time biofeedback protocols; ancient pranayama + machine learning = adaptive breathing interventions. The biological recombination analogy maps directly to SRL's innovation pipeline: foundational knowledge (DNA) → specific protocols/frameworks (gametes) → interdisciplinary synthesis (fertilization) → prototyping and pilots (gestation) → new frameworks like GMT, Anterocept, PolyAnchora (offspring) → market traction and clinical outcomes (natural selection).
