# The Tacit Mental Model of Business Expertise

## Summary

Lia DiBello (NDM researcher, 20+ years, 7,000+ subjects) extracted a shared mental model that all highly successful business leaders intuitively possess: **The Triad** — three interconnected domains that expert business leaders understand both individually and in their dynamic relationships.

## The Triad

### 1. Supply / Leadership (Operations)
- Organizational design and execution
- Operational competency specific to the industry
- Internal capability and execution capacity

### 2. Demand / Strategy (Market)
- Market dynamics and competitive positioning
- Consumer behavior patterns
- Industry trends and environmental signals

### 3. Capital / Finance
- Cash flow dynamics and financial metrics
- Cost structures and returns on capital
- Access to and management of capital

## The Expert Distinction

"Competent managers tend to be very talented in only one or two of these areas; however, they often do not understand the **dynamics between these areas** as well as the 'superstars' do."

Expert leaders exhibit:
- Ability to notice early market signals
- Forward simulation of consequences **across all three domains**
- Dynamic rebalancing based on external conditions
- Recognition of interconnections others miss

## Cognitive Agility

Beyond domain knowledge: "the extent to which an individual revises his or her evaluation of a situation in response to data indicating that conditions have changed."

The opposite — **cognitive rigidity** — occurs when "the person is impervious to new data, being dominated by a rigid framework or paradigm that acts to filter out new, possibly relevant, information."

## The FutureView Profiler

DiBello's assessment tool:
- Subjects analyze real company materials (10-Ks, annual reports)
- Make predictions about company performance over 5 years
- Receive year-by-year feedback
- Heat maps reveal blind spots across the Triad

Key finding: expertise acceleration of **several months** is achievable through targeted training using this method.
