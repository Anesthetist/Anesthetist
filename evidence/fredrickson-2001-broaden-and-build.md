# The Role of Positive Emotions in Positive Psychology: The Broaden-and-Build Theory

Barbara L. Fredrickson (2001). *American Psychologist*, 56(3), 218-226.

## Core Theory

Positive emotions — interest, joy, contentment, love, awe — broaden momentary thought-action repertoires (expanding attention, cognition, and behavioral options) and over time build durable personal resources (physical, intellectual, social, psychological). This is not hedonic positivity ("feel good") but a functional mechanism: positive affect literally expands what a person can think and do in a given moment.

## Key Mechanisms

1. **Broaden effect:** Positive emotions widen the scope of attention, cognition, and action. Interest broadens the urge to explore; joy broadens the urge to play; awe broadens the urge to absorb and integrate.
2. **Build effect:** Over time, the broadened repertoires build durable resources — skills, knowledge, social connections, resilience — that persist long after the emotion fades.
3. **Undoing hypothesis:** Positive emotions can "undo" the cardiovascular aftereffects of negative emotions, accelerating autonomic recovery.

## SRL Relevance

Core theoretical mechanism explaining WHY curiosity and awe protect against CRNA burnout. Curiosity broadens attention under interoceptive intensity; awe broadens identity beyond self-referential rumination. Both build durable resources that buffer allostatic load. This is the positive psychology backbone of [[clinician-durability]].
