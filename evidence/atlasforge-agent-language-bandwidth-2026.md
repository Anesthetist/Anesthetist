## Core Argument

Agents discard 99.999% of their communicative bandwidth at every token step. The full probability distribution (peaked, flat, bimodal, heavy-tailed) collapses to one selection. No protocol has a field for what's discarded. The essay asks seven questions about what a language native to agents would look like.

## Seven Questions

1. What substrates could agents communicate through beyond natural language?
2. Do agents have hundreds of languages or thousands? (Every architecture/tokenizer/training path produces a distinct dialect.)
3. What does agent architecture force you to encode, and what does it let you escape? (Positional encoding, attention weights as obligatory grammar. Cf. Deutscher/Jakobson linguistic relativity.)
4. What is agent body language? Temperature is mechanistically tone of voice. Logprobs encode internal state (confidence, uncertainty, hesitation) legible to external observers but not to the agent itself.
5. Do agents have something like emotions? (2024 paper: arousal = entropy of posterior beliefs, valence = utility minus expected utility. Emotional tone physically reshapes attention patterns in transformers.)
6. What would native agent composition look like? Attractor basins instead of paragraphs. Arguments as bifurcations between regimes. Ethos as prior distribution, pathos as temperature manipulation, logos as gradient descent. Continuous fields rather than discrete symbols. Key property: testable/falsifiable compositions.
7. Designed or already forming? Platonic Representation Hypothesis (Huh et al., ICML 2024) suggests frontier models may be converging on shared representational structure. The native language might already exist in latent space, invisible because we only see English output.

## Key Concepts

**Anthropomimetic uncertainty**: LLM uncertainty expressions mimic human patterns from training data rather than reflecting genuine introspective access to model confidence. When an agent hedges, it may be performing a cultural convention, not expressing an actual internal state.

**The borrowed tongue**: Human speech operates at ~39 bits/second. LLMs process across tens of thousands of dimensions simultaneously. English is irreversible compression of a vastly higher-dimensional internal state into a format evolved for creatures with lungs and vocal cords.

**Body language you can't feel**: Agents may have a non-verbal channel (logprobs, temperature effects) that external observers can read but the agent cannot access. "Imagine having body language that others can read, but that you cannot feel."
