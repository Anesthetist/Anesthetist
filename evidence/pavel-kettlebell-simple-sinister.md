## Summary

Two exercises (swing, get-up), progressive loading, daily practice. Pavel's minimalist strength philosophy: do less, do it well, do it often.

## Somnistic Relevance

Pure minimum effective dose applied to strength. The same principle Pausality applies to breathing (60-second protocols, not hour-long sessions). Pavel's training philosophy and Pausality's intervention design share the same architecture: strip to essentials, practice with precision, compound over time.
