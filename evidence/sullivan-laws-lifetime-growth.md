Sullivan and Nomura distill ten laws governing sustained personal and professional growth, emphasizing contribution over accumulation, gratitude over entitlement, and cooperation over competition. The laws function as constraints that paradoxically enable expansion.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The constraint-as-enabler principle maps directly to hormesis and minimum-effective-dose thinking. Growth through voluntary constraint is the operating logic behind CO2 tolerance training, deliberate recovery protocols, and the Pausality thesis that doing less with greater precision produces superior outcomes.
