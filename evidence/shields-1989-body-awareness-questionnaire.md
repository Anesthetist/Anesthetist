## Summary

Shields, Mallory & Simon (1989) developed the Body Awareness Questionnaire (BAQ), an 18-item self-report of attentiveness to normal non-emotive body processes. Published in *Journal of Personality Assessment* 53(4):802-815.

## Key Findings

1. 18 items measuring sensitivity to body cycles, detection of body changes, anticipation of bodily reactions, illness onset
2. Cronbach's alpha 0.81-0.82
3. Measures non-emotive body awareness specifically
4. Primarily unidimensional
5. Predated MAIA by 23 years

## SRL Relevance

Useful as a pre/post measure for non-emotional body awareness changes during interoceptive training.
