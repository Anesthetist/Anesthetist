## Summary

Systematic review examining how environmental factors in the operating room (noise, lighting, music, temperature) affect operative team performance.

## Key Findings

- **Scope:** 34 studies, n=4,159 participants
- **Noise:** OR noise consistently exceeds recommended standards; linked to increased mental workload and complications
- **Music:** Positive subjective perception but no objective performance benefit demonstrated
- **Systems approach:** Authors recommend a systems approach to OR performance optimization rather than individual-level interventions alone
- **Implication:** The OR environment is a systematic source of cognitive load that degrades team performance — this is a structural problem, not an individual resilience problem

## SRL Relevance

Supports the argument that OR environmental factors create a baseline cognitive load that clinicians must buffer against. The systems approach recommendation aligns with SRL's positioning as infrastructure (not just individual wellness). The noise-complications link provides another quantified pathway from environment to patient harm.
