## Summary

The foundational Western text on conflict, mortality, honor, and the cost of wrath. Achilles' rage and its consequences.

## Somnistic Relevance

The deepest layer of the classics shelf. The Iliad is about what happens when autonomic dysregulation (Achilles' rage) drives decisions in high-stakes environments. The entire poem is a case study in unregulated emotional state producing catastrophic outcomes. Reading it alongside Meditations and the Stoics shows Randy holding both the problem (Homer) and the response (Aurelius) simultaneously.
