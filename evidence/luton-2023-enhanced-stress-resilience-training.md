## Summary

Evaluation of a 5-week Enhanced Stress Resilience Training (ESRT) program for UK surgical trainees, measuring psychological outcomes and career progression.

## Key Findings

- **Intervention:** 5-week ESRT program (mindfulness-based stress resilience training)
- **Career impact:** 13-FOLD better career progression — participants had OR 13.249 for achieving National Training Number (NTN) appointment
- **Psychological outcomes:** Less burnout, less perceived stress, more mindfulness
- **Engagement barrier:** Only 31% of participants fully engaged with the program — engagement is the key barrier to effectiveness, not the intervention itself
- **Implication:** The intervention works powerfully when people do it, but adherence/engagement is the critical bottleneck

## SRL Relevance

The 13-fold career progression finding is extraordinary and directly supports ROI arguments for enterprise sales. The 31% engagement rate is the core problem that minimum effective dose and NeuroMinute design philosophy addresses — reducing friction to increase adherence.
