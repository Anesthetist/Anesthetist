# Springett Ice→Steam Kundalini Transformation Model

## Citation

Springett, Tara. (2015). *The Stairway to Heaven: Nine Steps of Consciousness from Unawareness to Full Enlightenment.* CreateSpace Independent Publishing Platform. ISBN: 9781507761601.

## Author Context

Tara Springett — Buddhist teacher since 1994, qualified psychotherapist since 1990, 1000+ kundalini-syndrome cases treated since 2011. Practitioner-teacher authority, not academic researcher. Frameworks transmitted through teaching and clinical practice rather than peer-reviewed publication.

## The Model

Four-phase metaphor for kundalini awakening as psychophysical state transformation:

1. **Ice (Frozen):** Dense, rigid psychic material. Trauma, ego defenses, frozen emotions stored somatically. Autonomic correlate: sustained dorsal vagal or chronic sympathetic activation patterns.

2. **Melted Ice (Breakdown):** Structural collapse. Emotional flooding, kriyas (spontaneous body movements), unconscious material surfaces simultaneously at upper and lower energy centers. Autonomic correlate: dysregulation, HRV instability, interoceptive overwhelm.

3. **Water (Flow):** Smoother integration. Creative emergence. Thoughts and emotions no longer frozen but moving coherently. Autonomic correlate: increasing vagal tone, respiratory sinus arrhythmia stabilization.

4. **Steam (Sublimation):** Subtle states. Compassion, transpersonal clarity. Energy rises beyond ordinary consciousness. Autonomic correlate: high HRV coherence, alpha-theta crossover, ventral vagal dominance.

## Key Clinical Insight

Ice melts simultaneously at upper and lower areas — spiritual experiences emerge alongside unconscious material surfacing. This is not sequential but concurrent. Large chunks breaking off manifest as old memories, traumatic memories, jerks, movements. Kriyas are expected in early phases but subside.

## SRL Relevance

- **Kosha alignment:** Ice→Steam maps to annamaya (dense body) → pranamaya (energetic flooding) → manomaya (integrated flow) → vijnanamaya/anandamaya (unified field)
- **Regulation-first approach:** Understanding Ice→Steam prevents misdiagnosis of kundalini symptoms as pathology. Somnistics' breath-first approach provides titrated "melting" that prevents crisis
- **Clinical messaging:** "We're not awakening kundalini; we're titrating your existing psychic system's state transitions"
- **Minimum effective dose:** Gentle, repeated 60-second interventions = micro-melting without overwhelm
