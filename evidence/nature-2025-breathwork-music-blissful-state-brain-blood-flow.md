## Citation

EurekAlert / UCLA Health (2025). Breathwork can induce altered states of consciousness linked with changes in brain blood flow. Published August 2025.

## Key Findings

- Breathwork while listening to music induced blissful altered states of consciousness in practitioners
- Accompanied by measurable changes in blood flow to emotion-processing brain regions
- Notably, the body's stress response was simultaneously activated — suggesting a paradoxical state of physiological arousal with subjective bliss
- Dr. Jack Feldman (UCLA) involved in related research on breathing and brain mechanisms

## Relevance to SRL

The paradox of simultaneous stress activation and blissful experience is highly relevant to SRL's state-transition and consciousness research. Connects to the concept of "productive arousal" vs. "distress" — and may inform how breathwork protocols in NeuroHarmonics can be designed to navigate this paradox intentionally. Also relevant to SRL's interest in jhana and cessation experiences.

## Methodology

Neuroimaging study measuring cerebral blood flow during breathwork with music. Published August 2025.
