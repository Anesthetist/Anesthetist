## Summary

Five-component manifestation framework that positions manifestation beyond simple "law of attraction" into Buddhist-informed psychological integration:

1. **Clarify a wish** with vivid specificity
2. **Emotional goal-vibration** — the felt quality/energetic signature of the desired state
3. **Clear inner saboteurs/sub-personalities** that create unconscious resistance
4. **Right attitude and altruism** — manifestation must benefit self and others
5. **Energy-body/chakra technique** as embodied shortcut

Springett requires spiritual maturity and psychological integration as prerequisites, distinguishing her approach from popular manifestation teachings.
