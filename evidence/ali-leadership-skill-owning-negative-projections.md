# ALI Leadership Skill: Owning Your Negative Projections

## Framework Summary

Whetten presents a comprehensive 7-step protocol for working with negative projections — the judgments we place on others that are always reflections of stored self-judgments. The model integrates the HIT framework (Hook/Insecurity/Trigger) from the Rating Scales with a shadow-work process that traces reactive judgments back to their intrapersonal origin.

### Core Premise

Whatever is stored in the unconscious shadow gets projected onto others. Any judgment placed on someone else is **always** a reflection of a judgment held against oneself. This operates through three projection forms:

1. **Others** — something you judge that you do to others
2. **Self** — something you judge that you do to yourself
3. **Capacity** — something you judge that you are capable of doing under certain conditions

### 7-Step Protocol

**Step 1 — Aware: "I Just Took a HIT"**
Notice that something has changed in body or emotional state. Classify the HIT type using a diagnostic table:
- Hook (Pull): "It's not fair because..." / "I need to rescue them because..."
- Insecurity (Shake): "It's your fault because..." / "I'm not worthy because..."
- Trigger (Push): "I'm upset because..." / "It's not my fault because..."

The Victim → Owner language shift from the Rating Scales is embedded here (e.g., "I'm upset because..." → "I'm feeling upset").

**Step 2 — Aware: What's Present?**
Three-channel inquiry: Observations (video replay — what did you see/hear?), Emotions (what do you feel, where in your body?), Thinking (what level of thinking are you in per the 7 Levels?).

**Step 3 — Accept: How Can I Be Okay With This?**
Allow emotions to flow through the body without freezing, fleeing, feeding, or fighting. The master question: "How can I be okay with this?" — being okay with the present-moment experience without needing to fix or externalize it.

**Step 4 — Own: Judgments Creating Feelings**
Shift from "I'm upset because..." to "I'm upset" — owning that judgments are creating the emotional response, not external circumstances.

**Step 5 — Improve: Own Your Projections**
Identify the three projection forms for each judgment. Owning projections means acknowledging these judgments exist as stored shadow content — not that they are true or accurate.

**Step 6 — Improve: Follow the Golden Thread**
Two pathways: follow the **meanings** ("what would it mean if this was true?") or follow the **feelings** to earlier experiences. Select the self-judgment with the most emotional energy and trace it to its root.

**Step 7 — Improve: Self-Compassion**
Trust that you have always been trying to achieve a positive purpose the best ways you knew how — and that everyone else has been doing the same. This completes the cycle by replacing judgment with understanding.

## Somnistic Relevance

This protocol is a masterclass in structured interoceptive processing with direct clinical translation:

- **Somatic HIT detection as interoceptive training**: Step 1's body-state change detection IS interoceptive accuracy training. The practitioner learns to detect autonomic state shifts in real-time — the exact skill that degrades under clinician fatigue and that Somnistics aims to preserve.
- **Three-channel awareness as embodied metacognition**: Step 2's simultaneous observation/emotion/thinking inquiry develops the capacity to hold multiple streams of awareness — precisely the metacognitive architecture required for nondual clinical awareness (monitoring patient, self, and relational field simultaneously).
- **Autonomic regulation via acceptance**: Step 3's "how can I be okay with this" — allowing emotions to flow without freezing, fleeing, feeding, or fighting — maps directly to polyvagal-informed regulation. These four F's correspond to sympathetic and dorsal vagal defensive strategies; the acceptance posture invites ventral vagal engagement.
- **Projection as diagnostic mirror**: The principle that all external judgments reflect internal shadow content has profound implications for clinician self-monitoring. When a clinician finds themselves judging a patient, colleague, or system, the projection framework provides a structured protocol for using that reactivity as diagnostic data about the clinician's own autonomic and psychological state.
- **Golden thread as somatic archaeology**: Step 6's process of tracing emotional energy backward to earlier experiences parallels the alpha-theta crossover work — following felt-sense threads into pre-verbal or early developmental material while maintaining observer awareness.
- **Diagnostic-therapeutic unity**: The 7-step sequence does not separate assessment from intervention. Noticing the HIT, tracing the projection, and arriving at self-compassion is a single continuous arc — the assessment IS the processing.
