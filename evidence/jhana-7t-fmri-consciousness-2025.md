## Citation

The neuroscience of highly stable, positive, and refined states of consciousness during jhana-type advanced concentration absorption meditation (ACAM-J). *bioRxiv*, 2025. DOI: 10.1101/2025.11.12.688050

## Key Findings

- First group-level, ultra-high-field (7T) fMRI study of jhana meditation states
- Across eight successive jhana states, found reproducible neural trajectories marked by:
  - Anterior-to-posterior cortical reorganization
  - Flattening of cortical hierarchies
  - Nonlinear reconfiguration of global harmonics
  - Tight coupling between brain metrics and equanimity/attentional stability
- Demonstrates that advanced meditators can volitionally access progressively refined states of consciousness with distinct, measurable neural signatures

## Relevance to SRL

Directly supports SRL's Springett Consciousness Stairway and NeuroHarmonics concepts. The finding that jhana states produce reproducible neural signatures with progressive anterior-to-posterior reorganization provides neuroimaging evidence for the kind of state-transition architecture SRL models. The coupling between brain metrics and equanimity is especially relevant to Clinician Durability — it suggests that deep contemplative training produces measurable neural stability.

## Methodology

Group-level ultra-high-field 7T fMRI; experienced jhana practitioners; eight successive jhana states; neural trajectory analysis, cortical hierarchy measures, global harmonic reconfiguration.
