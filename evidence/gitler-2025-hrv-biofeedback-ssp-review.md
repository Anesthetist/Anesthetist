# HRV Biofeedback and SSP for Autonomic Regulation (Gitler 2025)

## Summary

Comprehensive narrative review covering both HRV biofeedback (paced breathing at resonance frequency with real-time monitoring) and the Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP — a polyvagal-theory-based auditory intervention). Both modalities improve autonomic balance, reduce inflammation, and enhance emotional regulation and resilience. Future directions explicitly include wearable HRV monitoring and AI-driven adaptive biofeedback.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- HRV biofeedback at resonance frequency + real-time monitoring = exactly Pausality's approach
- SSP (polyvagal auditory intervention) validates [[neuroharmonics]] audio approach
- Review explicitly calls for wearable monitoring + AI-driven adaptive biofeedback = SRL roadmap
- Anti-inflammatory effects add a new outcome dimension beyond autonomic/cognitive metrics
- Most comprehensive recent review linking polyvagal theory to clinical biofeedback
