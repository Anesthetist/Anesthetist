# Somatic Marker Hypothesis (Damasio 1996)

## Summary

Antonio Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis proposes that bodily states (gut feelings, heartbeat changes, muscle tension) serve as rapid decision-making signals — marking options as favorable or unfavorable before conscious deliberation reaches a conclusion. The prefrontal cortex stores learned associations between situations and their body-state outcomes, enabling the "clinical intuition" that experienced practitioners rely on.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Body signals precede and guide conscious decisions (feeling precedes thought)
- This IS what [[interoception]] trains — access to somatic markers for faster, better decisions
- Clinical intuition = well-calibrated somatic marker system
- Validates [[embodied-knowledge-layers]] — the 5 layers ARE the somatic marker system organized
