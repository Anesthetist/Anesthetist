## Citation

(2025). Efficacy and methodology of remote heart rate variability biofeedback interventions for mental health: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-025-09750-w

## Key Findings

- 18 studies with 1,352 total subjects reviewed across diverse populations
- Medium effect size for depression improvement (g = −0.41) compared to control conditions
- Medium effect size for HRV improvement (g = 0.443) compared to controls
- Demonstrates that remotely delivered HRV biofeedback is effective — not just in-clinic delivery
- Supports scalability of HRV-based interventions for mental health

## Relevance to SRL

Supports scalability of SRL's core interventions. The medium effect sizes for both depression and HRV improvement via remote delivery validate SRL's potential to deliver **NeuroHarmonics** and **Resonant Breathing Frequency** training at scale. The **Minimum Effective Dose** concept is supported by evidence that even remote, self-directed protocols produce meaningful change. Also relevant to **Clinician Durability** as a scalable wellbeing intervention.

## Methodology

Systematic review and meta-analysis. 18 RCTs, 1,352 subjects. Focus on remote/telehealth-delivered HRV biofeedback interventions. Outcomes: depression, anxiety, HRV metrics. Published in *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback* (Springer, 2025).
