## Summary

National summit consensus paper on the role of meaning, purpose, and spirituality (MPS) in clinician well-being across all career stages.

## Key Findings

- National summit consensus: meaning, purpose, and spirituality are essential for clinician well-being across all career stages
- Significant gaps in replicability and scalability of existing MPS interventions
- Organizational culture must actively support MPS alongside individual programs
- MPS interventions show promise but lack the rigorous evidence base and scalable delivery models needed for widespread adoption
