# DHT Metadata Framework for Digital Health Technology

Published in *Sensors* (2022). Divides digital health technology metadata into context-independent metadata (concept of interest, context of use) and context-dependent metadata (therapeutic area, trial design). Explicitly acknowledges that **no standards exist for capturing DHT biosensor data applicable across modalities and disease areas.**

## Relevance to SRL

**Closest conceptual neighbor to neurotagging.** Recognizes the same gap SRL identified — the absence of a universal metadata layer connecting digital interventions to biosensor outcomes. However, framed for clinical trials in a single disease (Parkinson's), doesn't address real-time adaptive intervention, doesn't handle progressive curriculum structures, and hasn't been adopted as a standard.

## Neurotagging Gap

DHT Metadata Framework validates the need. [[neurotagging]] is the implementation — broader scope (not disease-specific), real-time adaptive, curriculum-aware, and designed for behavioral micro-interventions rather than device monitoring.
