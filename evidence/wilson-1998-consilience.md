## Summary

Wilson argues that all human knowledge — from physics through biology through social sciences through arts — can and should be unified through causal explanations that bridge domains. The term "consilience" (originally coined by William Whewell in 1840) describes the convergence of evidence from independent, unrelated sources toward a single conclusion. Wilson extends this into an epistemological program: the boundaries between academic disciplines are artifacts of institutional history, not nature, and knowledge advances most rapidly at the intersections.
