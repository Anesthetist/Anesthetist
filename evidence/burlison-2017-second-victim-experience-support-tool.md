## Overview

The Second Victim Experience and Support Tool (SVEST) is a 29-item instrument designed to measure the second victim experience in healthcare workers following adverse patient events. It assesses psychological distress, physical distress, and professional/performance impact, while also capturing perceived organizational support and support preferences. A revised version (SVEST-R) was validated by Winning et al. in 2021.

## Key Findings

1. 29 items organized across 7 dimensions capturing the full spectrum of the second victim experience
2. Measures psychological distress, physical distress, and professional/performance impact following adverse events
3. Also measures perceived organizational support and individual support preferences, providing actionable data for institutional response
4. Validated specifically in healthcare workers who have experienced adverse patient events
5. SVEST-R (revised version, Winning et al. 2021) provides updated psychometric validation

## Connection to Somnistics

The SVEST measures the specific phenomenon that SRL addresses in the CRNA population — the [[clinician-durability|second victim experience]] following adverse intraoperative events. Pre/post SVEST measurement in [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] intervention studies would directly validate SRL's core clinical value proposition: that structured autonomic regulation training reduces the severity and duration of second victim responses in anesthesia providers.
