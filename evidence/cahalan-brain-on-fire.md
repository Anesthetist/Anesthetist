## Summary

Cahalan's first-person account of anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis. Her consciousness disintegrated over weeks while the medical system failed to diagnose it. One doctor's pattern recognition saved her life.

## Somnistic Relevance

A case study in consciousness disruption from the patient side. Also a case study in clinical pattern recognition: the doctor who diagnosed her saw what others missed because he maintained curiosity under uncertainty. The book embodies both sides of Pausality's thesis: consciousness is fragile, and the clinician's cognitive state determines patient outcomes.
