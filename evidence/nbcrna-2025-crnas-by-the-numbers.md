# NBCRNA 2025 Annual Report: CRNAs By The Numbers

## Key Metrics

- **32.6** — Average age at start of CRNA career
- **47.2** — CRNA overall average age
- **3,235** — Newly certified CRNAs entered the profession in 2025
- **63,800+** — Nurse anesthesia professionals holding the CRNA credential

## Market Relevance for SRL

These numbers define the Total Addressable Market for Pausality's clinician-facing products:

- **63,800+ credentialed CRNAs** is the core TAM for CRNA-specific offerings (CEU courses, NeuroMinute™, Gap Moment Training™)
- **3,235 new CRNAs/year** represents the annual pipeline — early-career clinicians in the "seeding" phase of the GMT Career Arc who are building habits and most open to new approaches
- **Average career start at 32.6** means CRNAs enter with life experience and clinical nursing background — they're not blank slates, they're already practitioners adopting a new identity
- **Average age 47.2** suggests a mid-career concentration — the demographic most susceptible to cumulative autonomic load and most in need of durability frameworks
- **~14.6 year average career duration** (47.2 minus 32.6) is the window for clinician durability interventions
