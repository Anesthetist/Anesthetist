## Summary

Practical exploration of yoga's ethical foundations - the yamas (restraints) and niyamas (observances). Maps ancient behavioral principles to modern daily practice.

## Somnistic Relevance

The ethical substrate beneath the breathing and awareness practices. Somnistics teaches autonomic regulation, but the yogic tradition insists that technique without ethical ground produces instability. The yamas and niyamas frame why someone regulates, not just how.
