## Citation

New drug resets the body clock and cuts jet lag recovery nearly in half. *ScienceDaily*, February 8, 2026. Reporting on published research.

## Key Findings

- Novel compound Mic-628 can reliably shift the body's internal clock forward
- Targets a key clock-control protein and jump-starts the gene that sets daily rhythms
- In mice with simulated jet lag, a single dose cut recovery time nearly in half
- Represents a pharmacological approach to circadian phase-shifting

## Relevance to SRL

Relevant to SRL's interest in circadian optimization and shift-work performance. For CRNAs and other clinicians who rotate through day/night shifts, a compound that accelerates circadian re-entrainment could be a significant Clinician Durability tool. This connects to the CRNA Shift Architect concept and the broader recognition that circadian disruption is a major driver of clinician performance degradation.

## Methodology

Preclinical animal study (mice); simulated jet lag protocol; single-dose administration; circadian phase-shift measurement.
