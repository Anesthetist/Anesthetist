## Summary

History of the relationship between science and religion. Spencer argues the "conflict thesis" is a myth. The two magisteria have been entangled, mutually productive, and in tension throughout history.

## Somnistic Relevance

The epistemological context for holding clinical evidence and contemplative tradition simultaneously. Randy reads neuroscience and Dzogchen, pharmacology and Gurdjieff. Spencer's work validates that these aren't contradictory pursuits but historically entangled ones.
