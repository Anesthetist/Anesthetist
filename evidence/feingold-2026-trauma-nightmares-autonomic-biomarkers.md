## Summary

Multicenter home-recording study using multi-sensor wearables to identify physiological markers of trauma-related nightmares in military personnel with PTSD.

## Key Findings

- Trauma nightmares show a distinctive autonomic pattern: pre-awakening sudomotor buildup followed by post-awakening cardiovascular-electrodermal surges
- Reduced vagal HRV observed specifically during trauma nightmare episodes
- Home-based multi-sensor wearables successfully captured these signatures in naturalistic settings
- Establishes that trauma processing has detectable autonomic signatures even during sleep

## Relevance to SRL

Demonstrates that vagal tone depression is a biomarker of trauma processing, extending the HRV-as-indicator framework into sleep. Relevant to clinician populations exposed to secondary trauma and moral injury. The home-recording success validates passive wearable monitoring for capturing stress signatures that clinicians may not self-report. Supports the case for overnight HRV as part of a comprehensive clinician durability monitoring system.
