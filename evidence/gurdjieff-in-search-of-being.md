## Summary

Gurdjieff's teachings on the Fourth Way: consciousness work integrated into daily life rather than isolated in monastery, ashram, or fakir practice. Self-remembering as the core technology for awakening.

## Somnistic Relevance

Direct source for the self-remembering concept in the vault. Gurdjieff's self-remembering is the cognitive template for what NeuroMinutes operationalize somatically: a brief, repeated interruption of automatic functioning that restores awareness. The Fourth Way's insistence on practice-in-life (not retreat) maps to Pausality's in-shift design.
