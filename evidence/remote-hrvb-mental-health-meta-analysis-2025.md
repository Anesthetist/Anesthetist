## Citation

(2025). Efficacy and methodology of remote heart rate variability biofeedback interventions for mental health: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-025-09750-w

## Key Findings

- Meta-analysis showed medium effect size of remote HRV-B for improving depression (g = −0.41, p = 0.026)
- Medium effect size for improving HRV (g = 0.443, p = 0.002) compared to control conditions
- Confirms feasibility and efficacy of remotely delivered HRV biofeedback for mental health
- Provides evidence base for scaling HRV-B interventions beyond clinic settings

## Relevance to SRL

Validates the remote delivery model for HRV biofeedback, directly supporting SRL's digital platform strategy for delivering autonomic regulation training at scale. The depression effect size supports the broader wellbeing impact of HRV training beyond performance metrics. Relevant to Pausality's app-based delivery model and the Minimum Effective Dose concept for determining optimal remote intervention protocols.

## Methodology

Systematic review and meta-analysis; included RCTs of remote HRV biofeedback; outcomes included depression, stress, anxiety, sleep, and HRV; calculated effect sizes (Hedges' g) vs. control conditions.
