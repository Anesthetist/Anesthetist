## Summary

How a parent's own attachment history shapes their parenting. Siegel's thesis: understanding your own neural and emotional patterns is the prerequisite for attuned parenting.

## Somnistic Relevance

The recursive self-knowledge loop. Same structure as Colonna's Reboot, same structure as Pausality itself: know yourself first, regulate yourself first, then extend that to others. Siegel grounds it in attachment neuroscience.
