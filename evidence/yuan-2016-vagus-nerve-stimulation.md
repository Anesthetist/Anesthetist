# Vagus Nerve and VNS: Comprehensive Review (Yuan & Silberstein 2016)

## Summary

Two-part comprehensive review covering vagus nerve anatomy, physiology, and stimulation methods. Compares invasive VNS (surgical implantation) with non-invasive transcutaneous approaches (taVNS). Details the mechanisms by which VNS modulates autonomic function, inflammation, and mood regulation.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Non-invasive VNS (transcutaneous auricular) produces similar autonomic effects to surgical VNS
- Breathing-based vagal stimulation is the simplest form of "natural VNS"
- Validates [[ozen-2026-deep-breathing-vs-vns]] — deep breathing matches hardware VNS on most outcomes
- Vagal afferents (80-90% of fibers) carry information brain-ward — the body informs the brain
