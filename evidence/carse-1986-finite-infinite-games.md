## Summary

Carse distinguishes two types of games: finite games, played to win with fixed rules and boundaries, and infinite games, played to continue playing with evolving rules. A finite player plays within boundaries; an infinite player plays with boundaries. Finite games have definite beginnings and endings; infinite games have neither. The purpose of a finite game is to declare a winner; the purpose of an infinite game is to perpetuate play. Key principle: "A finite game is played for the purpose of winning, an infinite game for the purpose of continuing the play."
