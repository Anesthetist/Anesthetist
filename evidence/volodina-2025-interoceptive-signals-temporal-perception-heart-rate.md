## Citation

Volodina, Rusinova, & Terenteva (2025). Interoceptive signals and emotional states shape temporal perception through heart rate modulation. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 16, 1610347. [DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1610347](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1610347)

## Key Findings

- Investigated the relationships between interoceptive signals, emotions, and time perception with a focus on the mediating role of heart rate
- Emotional video stimuli were used to induce different states while measuring temporal judgments and cardiac activity
- Heart rate mediates the relationship between interoceptive/emotional state and temporal perception
- Provides the most recent empirical confirmation of the chain: body state changes heart rate, heart rate changes temporal processing
- Uses formal mediation analysis to establish heart rate as the statistical mediator, not just a correlate

## Relevance to SRL

This is the freshest replication of the core mechanism chain, published in 2025 from a lab independent of both Wittmann and Pollatos. The mediation analysis is methodologically stronger than earlier correlational studies because it establishes the directional pathway: interoceptive state -> heart rate change -> temporal perception shift.

For SRL's evidence claims, this paper confirms that heart rate is not just associated with temporal perception but statistically mediates the relationship. When Pausality measures heart rate change during a NeuroMinute, it is measuring the mediator of temporal perception change. The biometric feedback is not a proxy for the mechanism. It IS the mechanism, measured at the peripheral level.

## Methodology

Experimental study using emotional video stimuli with concurrent cardiac monitoring and temporal estimation tasks. Formal mediation analysis establishing heart rate as the pathway between interoceptive/emotional state and temporal judgment.
