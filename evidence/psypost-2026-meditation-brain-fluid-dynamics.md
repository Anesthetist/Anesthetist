## Citation

PsyPost (2026). Neuroscientists find evidence meditation changes how fluid moves in the brain.

## Key Findings

- When expert meditators switched from mind wandering to focused attention, absolute flow of cerebrospinal fluid decreased
- The reduction was driven by a decrease in backward regurgitant flow
- Fluid movement became more efficient and coherent during focused attention states
- Suggests meditation produces measurable changes in brain physiology beyond neural activity — affecting fluid dynamics

## Relevance to SRL

A novel biomarker dimension for meditation research. If meditation-induced state changes alter CSF dynamics, this opens questions about whether SRL's state-transition concepts (gap moments, neurogating) have corresponding fluid-dynamic signatures. Could inform future measurement approaches for NeuroHarmonics beyond EEG and HRV.

## Methodology

Neuroimaging study of expert meditators comparing mind-wandering vs. focused attention states. Measured cerebrospinal fluid flow dynamics. Published 2026.
