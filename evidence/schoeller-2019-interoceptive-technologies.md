## Summary
Schoeller et al. (2019) introduce the concept of "interoceptive technologies" — devices and systems designed to modulate internal bodily signals to influence emotional states. The review covers two main approaches: direct modulation (e.g., vagus nerve stimulation, thermal feedback, respiratory pacing) and illusory feedback (e.g., false heartbeat feedback altering anxiety perception). The authors argue that targeting interoceptive channels offers a more direct route to emotional regulation than traditional cognitive-behavioral approaches.

## Key Finding for SRL
This paper provides the academic legitimacy for SRL's entire technology thesis. Somnistics is, by definition, an interoceptive technology company. The direct modulation vs. illusory feedback taxonomy helps position SRL's approach (resonant breathing, haptic cues, HRV biofeedback) within a recognized scientific framework, and the paper's forward-looking vision of consumer interoceptive devices maps directly to SRL's product roadmap.
