## Summary

Rinzai Zen practices that integrate energy cultivation (tanden/hara work) with meditation. Moore argues Western Zen lost the embodied dimension. Sudden awakening requires a prepared body, not just a prepared mind.

## Somnistic Relevance

The somatic-contemplative integration that Pausality operationalizes. Moore's critique (Western meditation lost the body) maps exactly to Somnistics' critique (Western healthcare lost the clinician's body). Tanden cultivation is the Zen version of diaphragmatic literacy.
