## Summary

The two-system model of cognition. System 1 (fast, automatic, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, deliberate, analytical). How biases arise from System 1 dominance and when each system serves better.

## Somnistic Relevance

The cognitive architecture beneath clinical decision-making. A CRNA in crisis operates on System 1. A CRNA with autonomic regulation can maintain System 2 access under load. Kahneman provides the decision science framework. Pausality provides the physiological intervention that keeps System 2 online when it matters most.
