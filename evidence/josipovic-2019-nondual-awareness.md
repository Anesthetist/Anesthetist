# Nondual Awareness (Josipovic 2019)

## Summary

Josipovic's research investigates non-dual awareness as a distinct neural state — consciousness without the usual subject-object structure. This state is associated with enhanced well-being, resilience, and adaptability. It represents the deepest level of contemplative development and maps onto the Anandamaya kosha (bliss/integration) in the somnistics framework.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Non-dual awareness is a distinct, measurable neural state (not just a philosophical concept)
- Associated with enhanced well-being, resilience, and adaptive flexibility
- Achievable through progressive contemplative training (not just spontaneous)
- Represents the integration endpoint where subject-object distinction relaxes
