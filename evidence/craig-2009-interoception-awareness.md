# The Anterior Insula and Human Awareness (Craig 2009)

## Summary

Published in Nature Reviews Neuroscience, A.D. Craig proposes that the anterior insula serves as the neural substrate for subjective awareness itself. It re-represents the body's current physiological state (interoception) into a unified "global emotional moment" — the basis of the sentient self. Interoception is not just body awareness; it IS the foundation of consciousness.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Anterior insula = the neural basis of subjective awareness (not just body sensation)
- Interoception is foundational to consciousness, not supplementary to it
- Validates [[anterocept]] as targeting the most fundamental neural process
- Published in Nature Reviews Neuroscience = highest tier evidence
- The "sentient self" arises from interoceptive integration — training interoception IS training self-awareness
