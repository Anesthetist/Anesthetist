# Breathing Interventions + Mouth Taping RCT (Zaliene 2025)

## Summary

6-week RCT in women with dysfunctional breathing using app-guided breathing exercises (iBreathe), with one arm adding nighttime mouth taping. Both groups reduced respiratory rate and perceived stress. The mouth-taping group showed additional HRV improvements (HF component) and better respiratory control.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- App-guided breathing exercises produce measurable autonomic improvement (validates Pausality approach)
- Nighttime mouth taping adds incremental HRV benefit — supports [[mouth-breathing-pathology]] observation
- Nasal breathing emphasis validated by RCT
- 6-week intervention timeline comparable to [[neuro-ouroboros]] 21-day progressive curriculum
