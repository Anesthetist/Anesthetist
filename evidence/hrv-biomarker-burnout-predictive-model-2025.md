## Summary
MedRxiv preprint (Sept 2025) demonstrates that increased LF/HF ratio (sympathetic dominance) is significantly associated with higher emotional exhaustion. A predictive model integrating HRV parameters, psychological distress, and occupational factors achieved **79.1% accuracy** in identifying burnout risk.

## Relevance to Pausality
Directly validates our core thesis: HRV is an objective, predictive biomarker of burnout status. Supports algorithmic risk stratification in real-time to target Gap Moment interventions to at-risk moments. Could be cited in clinical validation materials and investor deck.
