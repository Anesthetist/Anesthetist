# Arena Strive 2024: App-Based Asynchronous Coaching Reduces Clinician Burnout

**Citation:** JAMA Network Open, 2024  
**URL:** https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2844922  
**Study Design:** Prospective cohort, pre-to-post, single-group  
**N:** 192 completers (367 enrolled)

## Study Summary

An 8-week asynchronous, app-based personalized coaching intervention integrated with a wearable photoplethysmography (PPG) sensor was delivered to 367 clinicians and other health care professionals at a large health system. 192 completed the protocol (mean 13.6 lessons, mean 19.6 coach messages exchanged).

**Intervention components:**
- Wearable sensor tracking HRV and bedtime consistency
- Asynchronous coaching via secure chat (not live sessions)
- Brief microlearning modules (<5 minutes each)
- 3 performance domains: [performance, recovery, and resilience — per COM-B framework]

**Primary Outcomes (Stanford Professional Fulfillment Index + MBI-HSS):**
- Statistically significant improvements across all PFI domains at 8 weeks
- **Largest effect: self-valuation** (6.47 → 9.02, ~39% increase)
- **Most important item change:** "I feel in control when dealing with difficult problems at work" — the largest single-item shift in the entire study

| Pausality Feature | JAMA Validation |
|---|---|
| App-based, brief sessions | ✅ Sub-5-minute sessions worked; compliance was high |
| Biometric feedback (HRV) | ✅ HRV + bedtime as the regulatory anchor |
| Autonomic regulation framing | ✅ Paper explicitly names autonomic disruption as the burnout mechanism |
| Self-valuation as key metric | ✅ Largest effect size in study |
| "In control at difficult moments" | ✅ Strongest single-item change — language gold for Pausality |

## What This Does NOT Validate for Pausality

| Pausality Claim | Limitation |
|---|---|
| Breathing as specific mechanism | ❌ Arena Strive used multimodal coaching — specific breathing mechanism unvalidated |
| SpO2 / blood oxygen monitoring | ❌ Study used HRV + bedtime; SpO2 as regulatory signal not addressed |
| "Measurable results in 1 minute" | ⚠️ Outcomes measured at 8 weeks, not per session |
| CRNA-specific efficacy | ❌ Population was mixed (47.9% attending physicians, 24.5% APCs, 19.3% nurses) — no CRNA-specific data |
| 21-session breathing program | ⚠️ Linkage to peer-reviewed research on breathing sessions requires care |
