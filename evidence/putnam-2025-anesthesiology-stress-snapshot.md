# Anesthesiology Residency Stress: 30-Day Snapshot (Putnam 2025)

## Summary

83% of anesthesiology residents reported experiencing a stressful event in the past 30 days, with 20% of those events classified as catastrophic. The study developed a novel Stress Objectivity Scale and identified recurring themes: trainee vulnerability, clinical complications, and interpersonal conflicts with colleagues.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- 83% stress incidence in 30 days confirms the clinical environment is inherently high-stress (not occasional)
- 20% catastrophic events validates [[ptsd-prehabilitation-gap]] — clinicians need pre-trauma resilience
- Stress Objectivity Scale could complement MAIA-2 for outcome measurement
- Supports enterprise sales thesis: anesthesia programs need resilience interventions urgently

## Concepts Derived

- [[clinician-durability]] — 83% stress incidence demands systematic resilience training
- [[gap-moment-training]] — high-frequency stress = high-frequency gap moments needing intervention
- [[error-archetypes-qrc]] — colleague conflicts and clinical complications map to error archetypes 3 and 6
