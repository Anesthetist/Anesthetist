## Summary

Rao et al. (2025) conducted the first large-scale concurrent EEG + EGG study during sleep (60 participants, 3 nights). Published as a *bioRxiv* preprint.

## Key Findings

1. Gastric infraslow amplitude modulations (~0.007 Hz) align with cortical sigma power in NREM sleep
2. Gastric amplitude predicts subjective sleep quality
3. Stomach-brain coupling is dynamic and state-dependent
4. First large-scale concurrent EGG-EEG sleep study
5. Gut signals influence brain states during sleep

## SRL Relevance

Directly relevant to sleep protocols. Gastric interoception during sleep may be a measurable outcome for Somnistics interventions targeting sleep quality. "Somnistics" literally relates to sleep.
