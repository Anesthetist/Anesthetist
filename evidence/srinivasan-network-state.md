## Summary

Thesis that new forms of collective organization can emerge from online communities that share values and eventually acquire physical territory. The progression: network union to network archipelago to network state.

## Somnistic Relevance

The community architecture question. If Somnistics builds a certified professional network (SCPs), what governance structure holds it together? Srinivasan's framework is one model for how a shared-values community scales beyond content into institutional form. Also connects to neural sovereignty as an individual-level parallel to political sovereignty.
