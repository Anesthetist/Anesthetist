# CDISC — Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium

Regulatory standard for clinical trial data exchange. SDTM organizes data into Interventions, Events, and Findings domains. Therapeutic Area (TA) standards extend it for specific diseases. The Operational Data Model (ODM) handles metadata interchange.

## Relevance to SRL

Built for drug trials and regulatory submissions. The "Intervention" domain captures drug dosing, not breathing protocol parameters. No concept of real-time biometric feedback loops, haptic cueing, or progressive 21-session autonomic training curricula. Structured for "Patient received Drug X at Dose Y on Day Z," not "User completed a 60-second cardiac-anchored breathing session at intensity level 4."

## Neurotagging Gap

CDISC will be relevant when SRL runs RCTs — neurotagged data can feed into CDISC submissions. But CDISC itself doesn't define the intervention-target-outcome triad for behavioral micro-interventions.
