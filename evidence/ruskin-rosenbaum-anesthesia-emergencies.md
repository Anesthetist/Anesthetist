## Summary

Management of anesthetic crises. Protocols for when consciousness management fails and the clinical system enters emergency mode.

## Somnistic Relevance

The extreme case. When the provider's own nervous system is most stressed is exactly when clinical decisions carry the highest consequence. This is the use case that validates Pausality's entire thesis: a CRNA who has trained autonomic regulation can access pattern recognition under crisis load. A CRNA who hasn't is running on adrenaline and procedural memory alone. The gap between those two providers is measurable in patient outcomes.
