## Summary

Epothilone B (microtubule stabilizer) delayed anesthetic-induced unconsciousness in rats by approximately 69 seconds (large effect size). Directly tests Orch OR theory: if anesthetics disrupt quantum vibrations in microtubules, then stabilizing microtubules should resist anesthetic effects. That's what they found.

## Significance

First strong behavioral evidence linking microtubules to awareness. Complements Hameroff/Orch OR references and MIT dynamic-stability papers.
