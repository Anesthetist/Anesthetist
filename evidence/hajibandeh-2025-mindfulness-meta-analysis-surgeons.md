## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis quantifying the effect of mindfulness-based interventions (MBIs) on psychological outcomes in surgeons, with pooled effect sizes.

## Key Findings

- **Stress reduction:** MBIs significantly reduced perceived stress (PSS-10; MD: -3.24, p=0.030)
- **Mindfulness improvement:** MBIs significantly improved mindfulness scores (CAMS-R; MD: 3.97, p<0.00001)
- **Burnout reduction:** MBIs significantly reduced burnout (aMBI; MD: -1.98, p=0.020)
- **Evidence quality:** Moderate-to-high certainty across outcomes
- **Lebares group:** Senior author from UCSF Center for Mindfulness in Surgery, continuing to build the evidence base

## SRL Relevance

Provides pooled quantitative effect sizes for mindfulness interventions in surgeons — critical for evidence-based positioning. The stress reduction (PSS-10 MD: -3.24) and burnout reduction (aMBI MD: -1.98) are the kind of numbers needed for enterprise sales decks and clinical credibility arguments.
