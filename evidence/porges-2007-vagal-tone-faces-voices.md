# The Polyvagal Perspective (Porges 2007)

## Summary

Elaborates how the myelinated vagus (ventral vagal complex) simultaneously regulates heart rate AND the muscles of facial expression and vocalization. The "vagal brake" modulates cardiac output while the same neural circuit controls face and voice — explaining why calm breathing produces calm faces and calm voices, and why seeing a calm face calms the observer's autonomic state.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Vagal brake simultaneously controls heart, face, and voice — single mechanism, triple output
- Explains why [[co-regulation]] works: calm breath → calm face → calm observer
- Social engagement system is NOT separate from autonomic regulation — it's the same circuit
- Validates [[script-design-invitational-language]] — voice tone directly modulates listener's autonomic state
