## Summary

Feasibility and acceptability study of the Oura ring for continuous HRV-based stress monitoring in dementia home care workers over 21 days.

## Key Findings

- 88.24% retention rate over 21-day monitoring period
- 94.2% valid data capture rate
- High ease of use reported by participants
- Participants reported enhanced health awareness from seeing their own physiological data
- Demonstrates that consumer-grade wearables are feasible for continuous stress monitoring in healthcare worker populations

## Relevance to SRL

Validates the Oura ring as a feasible, acceptable HRV monitoring platform for healthcare workers. The high data validity rate (94.2%) and enhanced health awareness finding support SRL's approach of using consumer wearables to build interoceptive literacy and track minimum effective dose of interventions.
