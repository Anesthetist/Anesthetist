# Prevention of PTSD by Early Treatment (Shalev et al. 2012)

## Summary

Randomized controlled trial demonstrating that early cognitive-behavioral intervention within one month of traumatic event exposure significantly reduces the development of chronic PTSD. The earlier the intervention, the stronger the prevention effect. This supports the logic of SRL's prehabilitation thesis: if early intervention after trauma prevents PTSD, then building autonomic resilience before trauma should be even more protective.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Early intervention prevents PTSD development (timing matters)
- CBT-based approaches are effective when delivered early
- Supports [[ptsd-prehabilitation-gap]]: if post-trauma intervention works, pre-trauma preparation should be even better
- Published in Archives of General Psychiatry = high clinical credibility
