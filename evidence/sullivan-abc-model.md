Sullivan's ABC Model (Accelerator, Breakthrough, Commitment) replaces linear goal-setting with a dynamic framework where entrepreneurs identify their current accelerating advantages, target breakthrough capabilities, and align commitments accordingly. The model treats strategic planning as iterative rather than deterministic.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The ABC model mirrors the Cap x K functor in the SRL architecture -- capabilities and knowledge combine to produce strategic optionality rather than fixed endpoints. For Pausality's commercialization pathway, the ABC approach frames each product launch as a breakthrough derived from existing accelerators (clinical expertise, research vault, agent infrastructure).
