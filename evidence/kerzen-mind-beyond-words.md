Kerzen explores cognition that operates below and beyond linguistic processing -- the intelligence embedded in bodily sensation, spatial awareness, and intuitive pattern recognition. The work examines how verbal framing both enables and constrains access to deeper knowing.

**Somnistic Relevance**: This is the theoretical foundation of embodied clinical intelligence. CRNAs operate in a pre-verbal cognitive mode during critical events -- reading hemodynamic patterns, sensing airway changes, and making split-second decisions that precede conscious verbal reasoning. Kerzen's framework validates the tacit knowledge dimension that Pausality aims to formalize.
