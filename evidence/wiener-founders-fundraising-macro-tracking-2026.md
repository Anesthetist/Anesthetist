## Core Thesis

Founders who ignore macroeconomic conditions misread investor signals and mistime their raises. Investors constantly optimize across asset classes (bonds, public equities, PE, venture) — your seed round is one line item in a much bigger portfolio game.

## Key Arguments

### Investors live in a different information stream
- Serious investors track interest rates, inflation, conflicts, and credit spreads continuously
- Institutional VCs face upstream LP pressure (pension funds, endowments, family offices) that directly constrains deployment pace and terms
- Current stat: VCs sitting on **$4.5 trillion** in dry powder — high by historical standards but deployed cautiously

### The macro → fundraising transmission mechanism
- **Low rates + calm world** → low opportunity cost of venture → investors reach for risk → founder-friendly terms
- **High/climbing rates + geopolitical shocks** → safer assets offer decent returns → LPs delay commitments, shrink tickets, shift to bonds → VCs become conservative → founders feel "ghosting"
- You're not just competing with other startups for capital — you're competing with the bond market

### How macro shows up at founder level
1. **Slower processes** — funds stretch deployment, more "let's stay close" deferrals
2. **Tougher terms** — valuation compression, more structure, sharper milestones
3. **Preference for durability** — efficient growth and unit economics beat blitzscale narratives when money isn't cheap

### What to track (weekly cadence)
- Policy interest rates from central bank
- Inflation trends (especially energy-driven)
- Major equity indices (proxy for risk appetite)
- Bond yields (low / normal / high regime)

### Three diagnostic questions before pitching
1. Does this environment make my **customers** more cautious or aggressive?
2. Does it make my **supply chain/costs** less predictable?
3. Does it make **investors** more hungry for risk or more interested in safe income?

### Tactical recommendation
Include a single slide acknowledging the macro backdrop:
> "Given current rates and volatility, we are assuming a cautious funding environment — here is how we are planning runway, hiring, and milestones over the next 24 months."

This signals you're managing a business, not just chasing a vision. In choppy times, that difference separates funded companies from "we like it but the timing is tough."
