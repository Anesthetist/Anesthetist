# SRNA Pandemic Burnout (Horvath 2021)

## Summary

Reviews the compounding stress on Student Registered Nurse Anesthetists (SRNAs) from COVID-19 pandemic, social unrest, and economic disruption. Documents burnout manifestations including fatigue, depression, substance abuse, and suicidal ideation. Calls urgently for nurse anesthesiology programs to implement wellness interventions and resilience resources.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- SRNA burnout includes substance abuse and suicidal ideation — not just "feeling stressed"
- Calls for institutional wellness interventions = enterprise sales opportunity
- Published in AANA Journal = validates SRL's target professional organization
- Supports [[ptsd-prehabilitation-gap]] — prevention needed before clinical practice begins

## Concepts Derived

- [[clinician-durability]] — burnout begins in training, not just practice
