## Citation

Scientific Reports (2025). Heart rate variability biofeedback in a global study of the most common coherence frequencies and the impact of emotional states. *Scientific Reports*.

## Key Findings

- Large-scale global study identifying the most common coherence frequencies during HRV biofeedback
- Examined how emotional states influence HRV coherence patterns
- Provides population-level data on resonant breathing frequency distribution
- Demonstrates that emotional context significantly modulates biofeedback effectiveness

## Relevance to SRL

Directly relevant to SRL's resonant breathing frequency concept. Population-level coherence data could refine SRL's approach to individualized breathing protocols and validate the importance of emotional state in achieving optimal coherence — supporting the integration of affective components into NeuroHarmonics training.

## Methodology

Global observational study using HRV biofeedback data. Published 2025 in Nature Scientific Reports.
