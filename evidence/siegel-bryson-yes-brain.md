## Summary

Siegel's interpersonal neurobiology applied to parenting. A "yes brain" state (open, resilient, curious) vs. a "no brain" state (reactive, rigid, fearful). How parents can cultivate the neural conditions for integration.

## Somnistic Relevance

Siegel's yes/no brain maps to sympathetic/parasympathetic dominance. The "yes brain" is the ventral vagal state Pausality trains for. Siegel provides the developmental neuroscience showing these patterns are set early and can be reshaped through relational co-regulation.
