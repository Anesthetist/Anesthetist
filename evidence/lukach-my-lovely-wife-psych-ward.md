Lukach's memoir documents navigating his wife's psychotic episodes and psychiatric hospitalizations. The account provides an unflinching look at how severe mental illness reshapes family systems, identity, and the boundary between caregiver and partner.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The caregiver durability question is central to Pausality. Lukach's experience illustrates how sustained exposure to another's crisis erodes the caregiver's own regulatory capacity -- the same dynamic CRNAs face across decades of managing other people's physiological emergencies. The grief loop and co-regulation concepts both apply.
