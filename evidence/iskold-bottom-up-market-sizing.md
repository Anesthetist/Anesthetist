# Bottom-Up Market Sizing: The Netflix Baseline Hack

## Source
- **Author:** Alex Iskold
- **URL:** startuphacks.vc

## Summary

Framework for pre-seed startups to size their market using bottom-up methodology rather than top-down TAM/SAM/SOM. The "Netflix baseline hack" uses Netflix's subscriber count as an anchor point for consumer app market sizing. Includes specific methodology for B2C health apps: start with addressable population, apply adoption rate assumptions, layer pricing model.

## Somnistic Relevance

Directly applicable to Pausality's market sizing for investor materials. Rather than citing the $322B burnout cost market (top-down), this framework would have Randy build from:

1. **Number of CRNAs** (54,000) × adoption rate × ARPU
2. **Expand to RNs** — broader nursing population
3. **Expand to all healthcare** — full clinical workforce
4. **Enterprise layer** — B2B institutional licensing

This bottom-up sequence is more credible in investor conversations and maps to Pausality's actual go-to-market sequence.
