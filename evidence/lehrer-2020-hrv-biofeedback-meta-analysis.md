# HRV Biofeedback: How and Why Does It Work (Lehrer & Gevirtz 2014)

## Summary

Comprehensive mechanistic review explaining how HRV biofeedback works. Resonance frequency breathing (~0.1 Hz) stimulates the baroreflex, producing large-amplitude HR oscillations that exercise and strengthen autonomic regulatory reflexes over time. Reviews strong effect sizes across anxiety, depression, anger management, athletic performance, and asthma. The baroreflex mechanism explains why effects persist beyond the practice session.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Baroreflex stimulation is the core mechanism (not relaxation response)
- Resonance frequency varies individually (~4.5-6.5 breaths/min)
- Effects compound over sessions — autonomic reflexes strengthen like muscles
- Strong evidence across multiple clinical populations
- Explains why 60-second sessions produce lasting effects (baroreflex training, not state induction)
