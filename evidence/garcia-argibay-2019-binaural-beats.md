# Binaural Auditory Beats (Garcia-Argibay 2019)

## Summary

Meta-analysis demonstrating that binaural beats can influence cognition, reduce anxiety, and alter consciousness states. The alpha frequency range (8-12 Hz) shows the strongest effects on relaxation and calm focus — the frequency range used in SRL's NeuroMinute audio protocols.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Binaural beats at specific frequencies can induce corresponding brainwave entrainment
- Alpha-range binaural beats (8-12 Hz) produce measurable relaxation and enhanced memory
- The effect is subtle but significant — works as a subliminal support layer
- Theta-range beats show effects on creativity and emotional processing
