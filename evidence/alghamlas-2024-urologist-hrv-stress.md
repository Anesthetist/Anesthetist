## Summary

Study assessing physiological stress via HRV in urological surgeons across experience levels during surgical interventions.

## Key Findings

- **Sample:** 171 urological interventions
- **Method:** HRV monitoring with RMSSD as key metric
- **Counterintuitive finding:** Senior surgeons showed HIGHER physiological stress (greater HR elevation) than junior surgeons
- **Implication:** Experience does NOT eliminate surgical stress — it may redistribute it. Senior surgeons may carry greater cognitive burden (responsibility, case complexity awareness) even as technical skill increases
- **RMSSD** validated as key metric for intraoperative stress assessment

## SRL Relevance

Directly challenges the assumption that experience alone builds stress resilience. This supports the Somnistics argument that autonomic regulation is a trainable skill separate from clinical expertise — even 20-year veterans need autonomic fitness training. RMSSD alignment with SRL's preferred HRV metric.
