# Sleep, Cognition and Emotion (Walker 2009)

## Summary

Matthew Walker's review establishes sleep as essential for emotional regulation and cognitive performance. Sleep deprivation amplifies amygdala reactivity by approximately 60% while decoupling prefrontal regulatory control — making the sleep-deprived brain simultaneously more reactive and less regulated. REM sleep specifically processes emotional memories, reducing their affective charge (overnight therapy).

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Sleep deprivation amplifies amygdala by 60% + decouples PFC = dual vulnerability
- Directly explains why [[sleep-debt-clinical-performance]] degrades clinical judgment
- REM processes emotional memories = sleep is an autonomic regulation mechanism
- Validates sleep quality as prerequisite for all other somnistics interventions
