# HRV Biofeedback for Stress and Anxiety: Meta-Analysis (Goessl et al. 2017)

## Summary

Meta-analysis of HRV biofeedback interventions showing significant reductions in self-reported stress and anxiety with moderate-to-large effect sizes. The evidence supports HRV biofeedback as a reliable clinical intervention — not just a wellness tool.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- HRV biofeedback produces reliable, significant stress/anxiety reductions (meta-analytic confirmation)
- Moderate-to-large effect sizes support clinical positioning (not just wellness)
- Autonomic flexibility training via biofeedback has robust evidence base
- Supports Pausality's evidence-based positioning vs. meditation-app competitors
