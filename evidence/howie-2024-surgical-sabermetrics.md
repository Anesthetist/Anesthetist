## Summary

Comprehensive review coining the term "surgical sabermetrics" — the application of technology-enhanced objective metrics to assess nontechnical skills in the operating room.

## Key Findings

- **Scope:** 19,229 articles screened, 81 included
- **Metrics identified:** 122 distinct objective digital metrics for assessing surgical nontechnical skills
- **Cognitive load:** Measured in 57 of the included studies, making it the most commonly assessed domain
- **Critical gap:** NO studies measured intraoperative decision-making objectively — it remains the unmeasured dimension of surgical performance
- **Implication:** The field has extensive tools for measuring stress and cognitive load but lacks methods for capturing the quality of clinical decisions made under that load

## SRL Relevance

The "surgical sabermetrics" framework validates SRL's approach to objective performance measurement. The decision-making gap is precisely where Cognitive Variability Analysis and Gap Moment Detection could position SRL as a category creator. 122 metrics mapped = comprehensive landscape for SRL product positioning.
