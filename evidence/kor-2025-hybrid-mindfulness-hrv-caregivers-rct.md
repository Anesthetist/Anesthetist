## Summary

Randomized controlled trial evaluating a 6-week hybrid (face-to-face + online) mindfulness-based intervention for stress reduction in informal caregivers.

## Key Findings

- 6-week hybrid mindfulness program (90 min/week, face-to-face + online) significantly improved outcomes vs control
- Improvements in: perceived stress, depressive symptoms, trait mindfulness, and HRV
- Critical temporal finding: physiological benefits (HRV improvement) preceded subjective burden reduction
- Hybrid delivery format achieved comparable results to fully in-person programs

## Relevance to SRL

The finding that physiological benefits precede subjective experience is significant — it validates the SRL approach of using HRV as a leading indicator of intervention effectiveness before participants "feel better." The hybrid delivery model (90 min/week) maps to a minimum effective dose framework. Supports the case that autonomic regulation changes are the substrate upon which psychological resilience is built.
