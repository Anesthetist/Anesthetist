## Citation

(Authors, 2025). Heart rate variability biofeedback in a global study of the most common coherence frequencies and the impact of emotional states. *Scientific Reports*. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-87729-7

## Key Findings

- Largest HRV biofeedback dataset ever analyzed: **1.8 million user sessions**.
- Positive emotional states were significantly associated with higher HRV Coherence scores and more stable HRV frequencies.
- Negative emotional states were associated with lower coherence scores and less stable HRV.
- Confirms at population scale that emotional valence and physiological coherence are bidirectionally linked — consistent with the HeartMath coherence model.

## Relevance to SRL

Population-scale validation of the HRV coherence construct that underlies SRL's Resonant Breathing Frequency and NeuroHarmonics frameworks. The emotional ↔ coherence relationship at scale supports the SRL premise that affective state regulation IS physiological regulation — and that training coherence is training emotion. Also relevant to the NeuroMinute as a minimum-effective-dose coherence-inducing intervention.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Retrospective observational analysis of biofeedback session data
- **N:** 1.8 million sessions (global user database)
- **Measures:** HRV Coherence score, HRV frequency stability, self-reported emotional state
- **Analysis:** Correlational; comparison across positive vs. negative emotional state sessions
