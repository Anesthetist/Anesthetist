## Summary

Practical guidance for physician career sustainability. Strategies for maintaining clinical performance while building a sustainable professional life.

The meta-skill of sustaining a clinical career while building something larger. Randy's own trajectory (28 years of clinical practice while founding SRL) is a lived case study of this challenge.
