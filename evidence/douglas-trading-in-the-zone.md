## Summary

Douglas's central argument: consistent trading results come from accepting uncertainty and thinking in probabilities rather than certainties. The market doesn't care about your analysis; your psychology determines whether you can execute it.

## Somnistic Relevance

"The zone" Douglas describes is a physiological state, not just a mindset. It's the calm, responsive, probability-accepting mode that maps to ventral vagal engagement. Douglas knew the what but not the how at the body level. Pausality provides the mechanism.
