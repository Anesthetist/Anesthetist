Scheinfeld presents a radical reframe of financial reality, arguing that money is a consciousness construct rather than an objective resource. The framework challenges scarcity-based financial thinking by repositioning the individual's relationship to abundance as an internal state rather than an external condition.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Whether or not one accepts the metaphysics, the psychological reframe is clinically useful. Scarcity mindset produces measurable physiological stress responses that degrade decision-making. For clinician entrepreneurs transitioning from salary-based to entrepreneurial income, Scheinfeld's framework addresses the somatic dimension of financial anxiety.
