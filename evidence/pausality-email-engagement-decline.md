## Summary

Tracking 8 email campaigns from Aug 2025 to Mar 2026 showing consistent engagement decline.

## Key Metrics

- **Open rates:** 72% (Welcome, Nov 2025) → 53% (v1.0.8 announcement, Mar 2026)
- **Click rates:** 7.8% (Welcome) → 2.1% (v1.0.8)
- **Subscriber count:** 446 → 424 (-22 net, -4.9%)
- **Best performer:** Welcome/onboarding email
- **Worst performer:** v1.0.8 launch email — lowest click rate of any send despite being the most significant product update

Pattern suggests classic list fatigue — early excitement cohort cooling, new subscriber acquisition not replacing churn.

## Somnistic Relevance

Signals that the email list alone cannot sustain Pausality's growth. The early adopter cohort (largely from Randy's CRNA network and initial launch buzz) has normalized. Need new activation mechanisms: in-person demos (Rachitsky Strategy 1), referral loops (Strategy 3), or content-driven re-engagement. The Welcome email's 72%/7.8% performance suggests onboarding is still the highest-engagement touchpoint — any new user acquired through offline channels will see strong initial engagement. The problem isn't the product message; it's channel fatigue.
