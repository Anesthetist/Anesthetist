## Summary

Four principles from Toltec wisdom: be impeccable with your word, don't take anything personally, don't make assumptions, always do your best. Framework for personal freedom through behavioral discipline.

## Somnistic Relevance

"Don't take anything personally" is a cognitive restatement of autonomic regulation. When you're regulated, events don't trigger a threat response. Ruiz's agreements are the behavioral outputs of a well-regulated nervous system.
