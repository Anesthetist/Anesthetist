## Summary

Intelligence beyond the human. Bridle surveys animal cognition, plant signaling, fungal networks, and AI as forms of intelligence that challenge anthropocentric definitions. The argument for a broader, ecological understanding of mind.

## Somnistic Relevance

Expands the consciousness inquiry beyond the human nervous system. If intelligence is distributed across biological and computational substrates, then the Vigil multi-agent architecture and the vault's consilience engine are small-scale instances of the same pattern. Connects to Bratton's social intelligence thesis.
