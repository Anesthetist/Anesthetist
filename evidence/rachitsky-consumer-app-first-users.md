## Summary

Analysis of 20+ major consumer apps identifying 7 strategies that account for virtually all early user acquisition:

1. **Go offline** — physical, in-person distribution (Tinder at sororities, DoorDash Stanford flyers, Uber at Caltrain, Snapchat at malls)
2. **Go online** — seed content yourself (Pinterest, Reddit fake accounts)
3. **Invite friends** — leverage existing social graph (Facebook dorm-to-dorm, WhatsApp contacts, Venmo check-splitting)
4. **Create FOMO** — exclusivity and scarcity (Gmail invites, Clubhouse, early Facebook)
5. **Leverage influencers** — get prominent early adopters (Instagram/Jack Dorsey, Product Hunt/tech bloggers)
6. **Get press** — media-driven launches (Mint/TechCrunch, Superhuman waitlist)
7. **Build pre-launch community** (Notion, Figma)

Most successful apps used only one strategy initially.

## Somnistic Relevance

Maps directly to Pausality's growth strategy:

- **Strategy 1 (go offline)** is Randy's strongest play — 28 years walking hospital corridors, CRNA conferences with Apple Watch demos, Jason at Ferrari events.
- **Strategy 3 (invite network)** is already happening through Massimo and the Ferrari community.
- **Strategy 4 (FOMO/exclusivity)** is built into "we work with a small number of clients by design."
