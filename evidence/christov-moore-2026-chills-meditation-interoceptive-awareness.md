# Chills-Inducing Music Augments Meditation (Christov-Moore et al. 2026)

## Summary

Published in *Frontiers in Psychology* (February 2026), this RCT (n=398) found that trait interoceptive awareness predicted ego-dissolution and connectedness during meditation. Adding chills-inducing music to mindfulness and loving-kindness meditation enhanced self-transcendence, mood improvement, and emotional breakthrough compared to meditation alone. Interoceptive awareness served as a trait-level moderator of meditation depth.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Trait interoceptive awareness predicts meditation depth (ego-dissolution, connectedness)
- Music-augmented meditation enhances self-transcendence beyond meditation alone
- Chills response as a physiological marker of transcendent experience
- Large sample (n=398) provides robust RCT evidence
- Interoceptive awareness is a prerequisite capacity for deeper meditative states
