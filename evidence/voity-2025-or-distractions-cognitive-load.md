## Summary

Observational study quantifying the frequency, severity, and patient outcome associations of operating room distractions during cesarean deliveries.

## Key Findings

- **Distraction frequency:** Mean of 81 distractions per cesarean delivery
- **Severity:** Severe distractions occurred in ALL observed cases — zero cases were distraction-free
- **NICU association:** Cases with NICU admissions had higher distraction scores (98.8 vs lower) and more severe distractions (3.56 vs fewer)
- **Sources:** Door movement and teaching were the most common distraction sources
- **Implication:** The OR is an inherently distraction-saturated environment — 81 distractions per case means approximately one every 30-45 seconds

## SRL Relevance

Quantifies the distraction load that clinicians face — 81 per case with severe distractions universal. This is the environmental context in which Gap Moment Training must operate. The NICU association provides the outcomes link. Every distraction is a potential state transition failure point.
