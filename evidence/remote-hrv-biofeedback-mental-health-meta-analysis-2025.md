## Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of remote/digital HRV biofeedback interventions for mental health. Found medium effect sizes for improving depression and HRV compared to controls. Validates remote delivery model.

## Relevance to SRL
Directly supports Pausality's remote/app-based delivery model. Meta-analytic evidence strengthens claims for scalable digital HRV-BF interventions.

## Key Constructs
- Remote HRV-BF delivery efficacy
- Digital therapeutic validation
- Medium effect sizes for depression and HRV improvement
