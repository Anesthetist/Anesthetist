## Summary

Review article arguing that surgical training addresses knowledge and technique but systematically neglects the "mental pillar" of performance, proposing specific mental skills for orthopaedic surgeons.

## Key Findings

- **Core argument:** Surgery trains two pillars (knowledge + technique) but NEGLECTS the third pillar — mental skills
- **Named skills:** "Performance breathing" explicitly identified as a surgical mental skill, alongside visualization and compartmentalization
- **Performance breathing:** Direct reference to controlled breathing as a deployable performance tool in the OR
- **Framework:** Adapts sport psychology's mental skills training model to surgical performance
- **Implication:** The surgical profession itself is calling for exactly the type of intervention SRL provides

## SRL Relevance
