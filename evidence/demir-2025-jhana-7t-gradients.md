# Jhana Reorganizes Brain Connectivity Gradients (Demir et al. 2025)

## Summary

First 7T fMRI study showing jhana meditation disrupts the brain's hierarchical gradient organization — shifting from segregated sensory/cognitive processing toward global integration. Simultaneously, it creates GREATER differentiation between sensory and attention-modulation regions. This means jhana doesn't blur processing boundaries; it reorganizes them into a more integrated but more differentiated architecture.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Jhana reorganizes cortical hierarchy (not just activates/deactivates regions)
- Integration + differentiation simultaneously (matches Siegel's definition of mental health)
- 7T fMRI resolution reveals gradient-level changes invisible to standard imaging
- First demonstration that meditation can reorganize the brain's fundamental processing architecture
- Sacchet Lab publication — key evidence for Harvard NeuroSkill integration
