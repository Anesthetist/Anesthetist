## Summary

Cross-sectional study examining fatigue prevalence and its physiological and organizational predictors among ICU nurses.

## Key Findings

- 92.9% of ICU nurses reported clinically significant fatigue
- HRV (sympathovagal balance), sleep time, circadian rhythm disruption, and cortisol levels predicted fatigue severity
- Shift length (8h vs 12h) modulated which factors mattered most — different shift structures activate different fatigue pathways
- Demonstrates that fatigue in high-acuity clinical environments has measurable autonomic substrates

## Relevance to SRL

Quantifies the autonomic cost of ICU nursing shifts and identifies HRV as a predictive biomarker. The near-universal fatigue prevalence (92.9%) underscores the urgency of embedded micro-interventions. Shift-length modulation of fatigue pathways suggests protocol titration may need to account for scheduling context.
