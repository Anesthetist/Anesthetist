## Citation

Burnout Trends Among US Health Care Workers. *PMC*, 2025. PMC12013355.

## Key Findings

- 25% of US clinicians are considering switching careers, primarily due to burnout
- In surveys of over 1,100 health workers: 93% reported stress, 86% anxiety, 76% exhaustion/burnout, 41% loneliness
- Burnout remains at crisis levels post-pandemic
- Organizational and individual interventions both show promise, but rigorous studies remain scarce

## Relevance to SRL

**CRITICAL market validation for SRL/Pausality.** These statistics confirm the urgent need for evidence-based clinician performance and resilience tools. The 76% burnout rate and 25% career-switching consideration represent the exact pain point that Clinician Durability, Gap Moment Training, and the Somnistics Certification program are designed to address. ## Methodology

Survey-based epidemiological study; over 1,100 US healthcare workers; self-reported burnout, stress, anxiety, exhaustion, and loneliness measures.
