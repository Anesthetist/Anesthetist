# Cyclic Sighing for Mood Enhancement (Balban et al. 2023)

## Summary

This Stanford RCT showed that 5 minutes of daily structured breathing — particularly cyclic sighing (double inhale through nose, long exhale through mouth) — improved mood and reduced respiratory rate more effectively than an equivalent duration of mindfulness meditation. This validates breath-first approaches over meditation-first approaches.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Structured breathing outperforms mindfulness meditation for mood improvement
- Cyclic sighing specifically effective — extended exhale drives parasympathetic activation
- 5 minutes daily is sufficient for measurable effects (supports MED principle)
- Effects are both subjective (mood) and physiological (respiratory rate reduction)
