## Summary

Practical NLP techniques for communication, state management, and behavioral change. Anchoring, reframing, and modeling excellence.

## Somnistic Relevance

NLP's state-management tools operate at the cognitive-linguistic level. Pausality operates at the somatic-autonomic level. Both target the same outcome (shifting internal state to improve performance) through different mechanisms. NLP anchoring is a cognitive version of what Gap Moment Training does physiologically.
