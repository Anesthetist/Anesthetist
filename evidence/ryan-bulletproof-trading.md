Ryan provides a systems-based approach to building trading resilience -- creating processes that survive drawdowns, volatility spikes, and psychological pressure without requiring heroic discipline in the moment.

**Somnistic Relevance**: "Bulletproof" is clinician-durability language applied to markets. The insight that resilience is structural rather than willpower-based maps directly to the Pausality thesis -- you don't ask CRNAs to be tougher, you redesign their recovery and awareness systems so durability is a byproduct of process.
