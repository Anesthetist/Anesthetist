## Summary

Source of the AQAL (All Quadrants, All Levels) framework — a meta-theoretical model that maps consciousness, development, and human experience across four quadrants: Upper-Left (interior/individual — subjective experience), Upper-Right (exterior/individual — behavioral/physiological), Lower-Left (interior/collective — cultural/shared meaning), Lower-Right (exterior/collective — social systems/structures). Wilber synthesizes over 200 developmental models into a single integrative map, arguing that no perspective is complete without all four quadrants.
