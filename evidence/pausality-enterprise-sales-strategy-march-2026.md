## Enterprise Sales Strategy — Field Update (March 24, 2026)

Current status: Pre-revenue enterprise, pilot-centric. Consumer iOS app live and monetized. F&F round ~$100K of $150K secured, earmarked for enterprise pilot development and clinical validation.

### Core GTM: Pilot-First, Validate-then-Scale

- Hybrid motion: bottom-up (individual CRNA adoption → internal champions using 62%+ burnout stat) + top-down (Randy's 20+ year CRNA network → warm intros to anesthesia departments, wellness programs, C-suite)
- Timeline: 3-6 months for first 3-5 pilots → proprietary ROI data → broader expansion
- Biometric proof measurable in days/weeks (HR drop via Apple Watch/AirPods Pro 3), not quarters

### Enterprise Offering (from /partners.html)

- Customized onboarding, admin dashboards, dedicated support
- ROI framing: reduced turnover, fewer errors, improved team resilience
- Positioning: "preparation, not recovery" — 60-second micro-sessions that fit real clinical shifts
- Differentiation vs Calm Business/Headspace: real-time biometric visibility during actual high-stakes moments

### Compliance Wedge

- Live: AES-256 encryption, on-device/iOS Keychain, HIPAA/PHI-compliant data handling
- Roadmap: SOC 2 for clinical/enterprise partners
- Called out explicitly as foundation for enterprise — de-risks procurement
