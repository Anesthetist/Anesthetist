# Teaching Clinicians Breathing Reduces Patient Pain (Schechter 2010)

## Summary

A brief 1-hour training session teaching clinicians breathing techniques led to measurable reduction in patient pain during pediatric immunizations. The key insight: clinicians who experience the technique on themselves first become more effective at teaching and guiding patients through it.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- 1-hour clinician training → measurable patient outcome improvement
- Validates [[clinician-learns-by-experiencing]] — experience on self transfers to patient care
- Brief training is sufficient (supports minimum effective dose for education)
- Clinician regulation → patient regulation (co-regulation through competence)

## Concepts Derived

- [[clinician-learns-by-experiencing]] — direct evidence that self-experience improves patient care
- [[co-regulation]] — trained clinician regulates patient through technique delivery
- [[minimum-effective-dose]] — 1 hour of training produces measurable outcomes
