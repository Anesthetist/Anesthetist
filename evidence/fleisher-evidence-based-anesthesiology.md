## Summary

Comprehensive evidence-based approach to anesthetic management. Systematic review of clinical decision-making frameworks grounded in outcomes data rather than tradition or authority.

## Somnistic Relevance

The clinical foundation. The standard against which all consciousness interventions are ultimately measured. Pausality's breath-based protocols sit downstream of this evidence base - they need to meet the same evidentiary rigor that governs pharmacological consciousness management. Fleisher's framework models how Somnistics should evaluate its own interventions: mechanism, dosing, outcome measurement, risk stratification.
