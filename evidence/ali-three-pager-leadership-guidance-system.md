## Overview

The ALI Three Pager is the foundational overview document for Brian Whetten's Authentic Leadership Institute methodology. It condenses three interlocking frameworks onto three pages: the Leadership Guidance System, the Genius Making System (with Corporate Compass and Structural Superpowers), and a seven-level Levels of Thinking developmental model.

## Key Frameworks

### 1. Leadership Guidance System

A polarity map distinguishing two operational modes of identity, needs, and power:

**Positive Mode (Authentic):**
- **Identity**: Soul / Intuition → Heart
- **Needs**: Mature Love, Contribution, Creativity, Growth
- **Power**: Receiving / Power Through → Giving

**Negative Mode (Shadow/Reactive):**
- **Identity**: Judgment → Body / Fear
- **Needs**: Conformance, Dominance, Craving, Control
- **Power**: Control Of / Taking Power Over

The system maps the polarity between authentic leadership (operating from soul/intuition with power-through dynamics) and reactive leadership (operating from fear/judgment with power-over dynamics). The central insight is that the same human drives (identity, needs, power) express through radically different channels depending on the leader's state of consciousness.

### 2. Genius Making System

**Corporate Compass™**: A circular model with Growth at top, Mission and Vision on either side, Results below Vision, Stability at bottom, Values and Relationships flanking Stability, and Fit below Relationships. Discernment sits at center.

**Structural Superpowers Table**: Maps four leadership capacities to their light and shadow expressions:

| Superpower | Value | Kryptonite | Antidote | Distortion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vision | Growth | Ungrounded Thinking | One Person With One Problem | Forest Without Trees |
| Realism | Stability | Small Minded Thinking | Yes Yes Hell No Goals | Trees Without Forest |
| Consideration | Relationships | Overcare and People Pleasing | Owning Your Needs | Unfocused Thinking |
| Prioritization | Results | Running Over Others | Deep Listening | Myopic Thinking |

### 3. Levels of Thinking (Seven-Level Developmental Model)

A hierarchical consciousness model mapping brain regions, cognitive capacities, characteristic language, problem types, orienting questions, and emotional signatures:

| Level | Brain | Description | Problems | Feelings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7. Inspired | Neocortex – Whole Person | Seeing tension between opposites; creative third choices | Developmental | Gratitude, Inspiration, Peace |
| 6. Systems | Neocortex – Right Brain | Feedback loops, unintended consequences, leverage points | Developmental | Fulfillment, Love, Joy |
| 5. Analytical | Neocortex – Left Brain | Component parts; modeling and fixing each piece | Technical | Neutrality, Understanding, Acceptance |
| 4. Self Protective | Limbic Brain | Being right; finding the correct way | Technical | Pride, Determination, Courage, Significance |
| 3. Codependent | Limbic Brain | Social expectations; rescuer or victim roles | Moral | Anger, Guilt, Connection, Belonging |
| 2. Us vs. Them | Reptile Brain | Tribal identification; behavioral patterns | Moral | Pleasure, Pain, Fear, Grief |
| 1. All or Nothing | Reptile Brain | Dualistic thinking; black/white, safe/unsafe | Moral | Anxiety, Hatred, Shame, Addiction |

## Somnistic Relevance

### Developmental Staging as Interoceptive Depth Marker

Whetten's seven-level model maps directly onto the SRL thesis that **awareness depth is trainable and stage-dependent**. Levels 1–3 (reptile/limbic) correspond to autonomic-dominant processing where interoceptive signals are either suppressed (Level 1 dissociation) or fused with narrative (Level 3 codependent reactivity). Levels 5–7 represent progressive neocortical integration — precisely the territory where structured interoception training operates.

### Diagnostic-Therapeutic Unity Principle

The Leadership Guidance System's polarity structure embodies Randy's key insight about Whetten's exercises: **the assessment IS the intervention**. When a clinician or leader maps their current position on the Identity-Needs-Power grid, the act of locating oneself in the shadow quadrant (fear/judgment/control) simultaneously reveals the training entry point AND begins the shift toward the authentic quadrant. This mirrors the SRL principle that interoceptive assessment instruments (e.g., MAIA-2-CRNA) function as awareness-training devices, not merely measurement tools.

### Structural Superpowers as Clinician Archetype Detection

The four superpowers (Vision, Realism, Consideration, Prioritization) with their kryptonite/antidote structure parallel the SRL concept of clinician cognitive profiles. A CRNA who over-indexes on Prioritization (results at the expense of relationships) exhibits the same pattern SRL identifies as "force over finesse." The antidote column (Deep Listening, Owning Your Needs) maps to co-regulation and self-remembering practices.

### Brain-Level Mapping to State Transition Architecture

The Levels of Thinking model's explicit brain-region mapping (Reptile → Limbic → Neocortex) provides a simplified but clinically useful framework for understanding state transitions. Level transitions correspond to autonomic state shifts that are detectable via the same physiological markers (HRV, respiratory pattern, EDA) that Pausality's sensor stack monitors. This creates a potential bridge: ALI's developmental levels could be operationalized as biometrically-detectable states within the Pausality platform.
