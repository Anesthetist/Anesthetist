## Citation

(Authors, 2025). Neurofeedback Training Modulates Brain Functional Networks and Improves Cognition in Amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment Patients Aged 60–70 Years. *Brain Sciences*, 15(11), 1243. https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3425/15/11/1243

## Key Findings

- Following neurofeedback training (NFT), aMCI patients showed enhanced connectivity strength, global efficiency, and nodal characteristics within the **delta band**.
- Connectivity was attenuated in theta, alpha, and beta bands — suggesting band-specific reorganization toward more efficient baseline-state processing.
- Cognitive performance improved meaningfully following the NFT protocol in this 60–70 year-old cohort.
- Demonstrates neurofeedback produces measurable neuroplastic changes in brain network organization, not just behavioral outcomes.

## Relevance to SRL

Strengthens the scientific foundation for neurofeedback as a Hemispheric Rebalancing and State Transition tool — particularly for aging populations. Relevant to SRL's contemplative neuroscience and NeuroHarmonics frameworks. For CRNA/clinician populations, shows that EEG-based neurofeedback can produce measurable network-level changes, validating the concept of trainable cognitive architecture.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Pre/post neurofeedback intervention study
- **Population:** Adults aged 60–70 with amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment (aMCI)
- **Intervention:** Neurofeedback training (NFT protocol)
- **Measures:** EEG functional connectivity (delta, theta, alpha, beta), cognitive performance tests
