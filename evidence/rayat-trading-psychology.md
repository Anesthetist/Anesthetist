Rayat examines the psychological dimensions of trading performance -- how cognitive biases, emotional regulation, and identity dynamics shape market outcomes. The work bridges behavioral finance theory with practical self-management techniques.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Trading psychology and clinical decision-making under uncertainty share identical failure modes: confirmation bias, loss aversion, anchoring, and emotional hijacking. The parallels between managing a trading book and managing an anesthetic case are structural, not metaphorical.
