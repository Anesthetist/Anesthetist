Goode presents the Direct Path approach to nondual awareness -- a method that bypasses progressive stages in favor of immediate investigation of experience. The practice involves examining perception, sensation, and thought to reveal the nondual nature of awareness without requiring years of preliminary practice.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The Direct Path is the contemplative analog to the clinical "pattern recognition" that experienced CRNAs develop -- seeing the whole situation immediately rather than building it up from parts. Goode's method maps to nondual-clinical-awareness, where the practitioner's awareness and the clinical field are not separate.
