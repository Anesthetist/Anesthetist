## Summary

Computational model showing propofol, ketamine, and dexmedetomidine all share the same "dynamic instability" signature in neural recordings. Despite different pharmacological mechanisms, they converge on a universal disruption pattern.

## Significance

Suggests a shared computational mechanism for unconsciousness across drug classes. Adds a computational layer to clinical pharmacology.

## SRL relevance

Reinforces the microtubule/consciousness evidence. Adds the computational modeling dimension that a clinical-LLM system could eventually predict or detect. Relevant to attention-as-gain-control concept.
