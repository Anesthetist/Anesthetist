Sullivan examines the behavioral differences between the wealthy who maintain and grow their wealth versus those who lose it. The "thin green line" is the behavioral boundary between financial security and financial fragility -- driven by psychology rather than income level.

**Somnistic Relevance**: The thin line between durability and fragility is the central metaphor of Pausality. In finance as in clinical practice, the margin between thriving and burnout is behavioral, not circumstantial. Sullivan's work reinforces that sustainable performance (financial or clinical) is a function of habits and awareness, not raw capacity.
