## Summary

Trading as a practice of quietness and discipline rather than excitement and prediction. Emphasis on emotional regulation as the primary edge.

## Somnistic Relevance

The parasympathetic trader. Matov's "quiet" maps directly to the regulated state Pausality trains. The best traders, like the best CRNAs, operate from a calm baseline and respond to signals rather than reacting to noise.
