## Citation
Enhancing wellness: a systematic review of biofeedback interventions for healthcare professionals. Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2026. DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1761371

## Structured Abstract

**Background:** Biofeedback interventions are increasingly proposed as tools for healthcare professional wellness, but evidence has not been systematically synthesized.

**Methods:** Systematic review of biofeedback interventions (HRV-biofeedback, RSA training, and other modalities) tested in healthcare professional populations.

**Results:** HRV-biofeedback and respiratory sinus arrhythmia training demonstrated consistent improvements in: perceived stress, anxiety, depressive symptoms, emotional regulation, and resilience. Physiological benefits included increased HRV, decreased sympathetic arousal, and improved autonomic balance.

**Conclusion:** Biofeedback, particularly HRV-biofeedback, shows robust evidence for improving multiple wellness domains in healthcare professionals.
