## Summary

How tech companies extract behavioral data to predict and modify human behavior for profit. Zuboff names the economic logic: human experience as raw material.

## Somnistic Relevance

The ethical counter-model. Pausality collects biometric data (HRV, breathing patterns). Zuboff's framework is the cautionary tale: what happens when biometric data serves the company rather than the user. SRL's compliance metadata layer needs to explicitly address this.
