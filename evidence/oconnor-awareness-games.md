## Summary

Practical exercises for Gurdjieffian self-observation. Structured games for noticing automatic patterns in thought, emotion, and sensation.

## Somnistic Relevance

Companion to the Gurdjieff texts. These are the exercises that operationalize self-remembering. The "game" format is the same design principle behind NeuroMinutes: make the awareness practice brief, structured, and repeatable rather than open-ended.
