## Summary

How the brain processes grief. The neural mapping of attachment and what happens when the person mapped is gone. Why grief is a learning process, not just an emotion.

## Somnistic Relevance

Connects to the constraint-release grief loop concept in the vault. Grief is an autonomic event. The brain's map-updating process during loss produces physiological signatures that Pausality's tools could detect and support. Also relevant to the second-victim phenomenon: clinicians who lose patients undergo a grief process the institution rarely acknowledges.
