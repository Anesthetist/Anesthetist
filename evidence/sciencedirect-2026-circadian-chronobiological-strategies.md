## Citation

Journal of Psychosomatic Research (2026). Circadian rhythms and health: Chronobiological strategies for enhancing physical and mental well-being. *Journal of Psychosomatic Research*.

## Key Findings

- Reviews chronobiological strategies for performance optimization including light exposure management, meal timing, and sleep hygiene
- Emerging precision chronotherapy uses AI-driven circadian health monitoring for personalized approaches
- Circadian misalignment linked to metabolic, cardiovascular, and mental health risks
- Dynamic blue-enriched LED lighting mimicking natural photoperiods can regulate melatonin secretion and enhance alertness in workplace settings

## Relevance to SRL

Circadian optimization is a natural extension of SRL's sleep and recovery science work. The concept of "time therapeutics" — leveraging circadian alignment to optimize when interventions are most effective — could inform SRL's approach to training protocols (e.g., optimal timing for breathwork, neurofeedback, or gap moment training sessions).

## Methodology

Review paper synthesizing chronobiology, sleep science, and performance research. Published 2026 in Journal of Psychosomatic Research.
