# The Games People Play With Cash Flow

## Core Insight

"People with limited understanding of business think that business is all about making profits. But those who actually run businesses know that running a business is all about managing cash flows."

Cash flow — not profit — is the critical variable for business decisions.

## Three Cash Flow Games

### 1. Payment Terms
Adjusting invoice payment terms changes cash flow dynamics and company valuation. Private equity firms exploit this systematically.

### 2. Speed Effects
Fast, reliable delivery reduces inventory requirements for downstream customers, improving their cash positions. This allows **premium pricing** despite competitive pressure. The advantage comes from consequences of speed, not production cost.

### 3. Pre-Payments and Float
**Nick Kokonas (Alinea/Tock):** Collected reservation deposits, creating working capital that allowed negotiating steep supplier discounts. Reduced beef costs by 50% by offering prepayment — eliminated vendor's spoilage risk on dry-aged meat.

## The John Malone Case

When Malone took over TCI (cable) in 1973:
- High upfront capital for infrastructure
- Predictable, monopoly-like subscription revenues
- Favorable tax depreciation mechanics

**Strategy:** Leveraged debt to acquire systems, used depreciation to defer taxes. TCI showed accounting losses for 25 years while generating **30.3% compound annual returns** vs. 14.3% for S&P 500. Malone invented EBITDA as a metric: "cash flow is a fact; profit is an opinion."

## The Meta-Lesson

First-principles reasoning fails not through logical error but through **incomplete foundational understanding**. The solution is continuous reality-checking during execution, not perfect initial analysis.

## SRL Relevance

**CE course pre-payment model:** SRL can structure CE courses as pre-paid cohorts (like Kokonas). This creates float for investment in product development while delivering measurable value.

**Speed effect for enterprise:** Pausality's 60-second delivery unit is the ultimate speed play — no scheduling, no training room, no facilitator required. This reduces the "implementation cost" for enterprise buyers to near zero, which is itself a cash-flow advantage.

**Malone's insight applied:** SRL's current $39 MRR is not the point. Recurring subscription revenue from even small numbers of users creates the predictable cash flow base that enables capital allocation decisions. Revenue rhythm, not revenue size, is the early-stage metric.
