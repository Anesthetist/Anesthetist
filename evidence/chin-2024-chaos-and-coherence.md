# Chaos and Coherence in Business

## Core Concept

**Coherence:** A business where every component reinforces others to exploit a central market insight. "Every part of your business works to strengthen every other part" through tradeoffs that create competitive advantages.

**Strategy** (per Richard Rumelt): diagnosis identifying critical challenges → guiding policy addressing challenges → coordinated actions executing policy.

## The Balance

Chin's key realization: **excessive focus on coherence without enough exploratory chaos creates blind spots.** His boss's seemingly chaotic decision-making (last-minute demos, opportunistic deals) actually generated valuable information for strategic refinement.

High conscientiousness excels at execution but risks missing opportunities without sufficient experimentation. Different founder types require different chaos/coherence ratios.
