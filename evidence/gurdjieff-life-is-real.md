## Summary

Gurdjieff's third series. Autobiographical account of his own struggle with consciousness work. The title encodes the thesis: ordinary life is mechanical sleep; real life begins with self-remembering.

## Somnistic Relevance

Companion to In Search of Being. The title itself is a NeuroMinute: "Life is real only then, when 'I am.'" The gap between automatic functioning and present awareness is what Pausality's tools measure and train.
