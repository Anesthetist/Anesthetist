## Summary

History and science of neurofeedback. How EEG biofeedback evolved from fringe to clinical tool. Case studies of neurofeedback treating ADHD, anxiety, PTSD, and peak performance.

## Somnistic Relevance

The biofeedback lineage. Neurofeedback is the brain-wave cousin of HRV biofeedback. Robbins documents the path from clinical skepticism to evidence-based adoption, which is the same path Pausality walks. Also connects to the alpha-theta crossover concept and closed-loop biofeedback.
