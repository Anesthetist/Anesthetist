## Overview

"Depleted Dad Syndrome" is **not** a peer-reviewed construct. It is a popular term coined by practitioners (Dr. Jason Selk, Dr. Scott Behson) circulating through coaching sites and therapy practices, not journals. The underlying science lives in the **parental burnout (PB)** literature.

## Foundational Researchers

**Isabelle Roskam** and **Moïra Mikolajczak** at Université Catholique de Louvain (Belgium) built the entire empirical framework over the last 15 years.

## Key Papers

### BR2 Etiological Model
Mikolajczak M. &amp; Roskam I. (2018). "A Theoretical and Clinical Framework for Parental Burnout: The Balance Between Risks and Resources (BR2)." *Frontiers in Psychology*, 9:886.
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6006266/
- Parental burnout is a unique, context-specific syndrome from enduring chronic parenting stress. Three symptoms: (1) overwhelming exhaustion in parental role, (2) emotional distancing from children (functional/instrumental interactions only), (3) loss of accomplishment in parental role.

### Consequences Paper
Mikolajczak M., Gross J.J., &amp; Roskam I. (2019). "Parental Burnout: What Is It, and Why Does It Matter?" *Clinical Psychological Science*, 7(6):1319-1329.
- URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/2167702619858430

### 42-Country Prevalence Study
Roskam I. et al. (2021). "Parental Burnout Around the Globe: a 42-Country Study." *Affective Science*, 2(1):58-79.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33758826/
- Western parents 5x more likely to experience PB than non-Western parents, even after accounting for economic disparities. Individualistic cultural values are a significant factor. Emotion regulation appears to be a prominent candidate mechanism.

### Distinctness from Depression and Job Burnout
Mikolajczak M. et al. (2020). "Is Parental Burnout Distinct From Job Burnout and Depressive Symptoms?" *Clinical Psychological Science*, 8(4):673-689.
- URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/2167702620917447

### Measurement Instrument (PBA)
Roskam I., Brianda M.E., &amp; Mikolajczak M. (2018). "A Step Forward in the Conceptualization and Measurement of Parental Burnout: The Parental Burnout Assessment (PBA)." *Frontiers in Psychology*, 9:758.

### Single-Parent Specific Finding
Roskam I. et al. (2023). "Parental Burnout in the Context of Special Needs, Adoption, and Single Parenthood." *Children*, 10(7):1131.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37508628/
- Single parents displayed higher PB than matched controls.

### Biological Marker
Brianda M.E., Roskam I., &amp; Mikolajczak M. (2020). "Hair cortisol concentration as a biomarker of parental burnout." *Psychoneuroendocrinology*, 117:104681.

### Treatment Evidence
Brianda M.E. et al. (2020). "Treating parental burnout: Impact of two treatment modalities on burnout symptoms, emotions, hair cortisol, and parental neglect and violence." *Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics*.

### Longitudinal Process Model
Roskam I. &amp; Mikolajczak M. (2021). "The slippery slope of parental exhaustion: A process model of parental burnout." *Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology*, 77:101354.
- PB is not instantaneous but the result of a prolonged process. Emotional exhaustion is the initial stage.

### 15-Year Meta-Analytic Review
Mikolajczak M. et al. (2023). "15 Years of Parental Burnout Research: Systematic Review and Agenda." *Current Directions in Psychological Science*.
- URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/09637214221142777

### 26-Study Systematic Review
Published in *BMC Public Health* (2024), searching PubMed, Web of Science, and EBSCO from 2010-2023.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38317118/

## Critical Gap: Father-Specific Research

Most PB studies focus on mothers, influenced by traditional thinking that mothers take the main care of children. The field's own founders explicitly call out father-specific PB research as a gap.

The popular 42% prevalence figure for fathers traces to an Ohio State University study finding that 42% of male parents experience parental burnout, with men expressing PB through **anger and frustration** rather than the emotional exhaustion more common in mothers.

## Strategic Relevance to Pausality

The father-specific gap is either a problem or an opportunity:
- Solid peer-reviewed foundation exists for parental burnout generally
- Father-specific PB research is thin and explicitly called out as a gap
- Anger/frustration expression pattern in fathers suggests different intervention design needed
- Biometric markers (hair cortisol, HRV) already validated in the PB literature
- Emotion regulation identified as prominent candidate mechanism — directly aligns with Pausality's Gap Moment Training and autonomic regulation approach
- "Depleted Dad Syndrome" has cultural traction but no clinical backing — an opening for a product that bridges popular awareness to evidence-based intervention
