## Summary

How government policy shaped the American healthcare system. The institutional and regulatory forces that created the environment CRNAs operate within.

## Somnistic Relevance

The system-level context. Pausality doesn't exist in a vacuum - it enters a healthcare system shaped by decades of policy decisions around scope of practice, reimbursement, credentialing, and institutional power. Understanding these forces matters for go-to-market (who can buy, who can prescribe, what gets reimbursed) and for the longer regulatory path if Somnistics seeks clinical validation or FDA clearance.
