## Overview

A 3-page coaching protocol for releasing self-judgment through a structured somatic-relational forgiveness process. The core reframe shifts forgiveness from forgiving others' actions to forgiving one's own judgments — recognizing that judgments, not events, are the root source of suffering.

## Key Framework

### Essence
Our judgment is a core source of our pain, and we release our judgment through the practice of self-forgiveness.

### Core Reframe
- Conventional forgiveness: "I forgive them for [what they did]" — focuses on symptoms (their actions)
- Compassionate self-forgiveness: "I forgive myself for judging [myself/them] as ___" — goes to root cause (the judgment itself)

Projections of judgment onto others are recognized as displaced self-judgments. When someone judges another as "stupid and unpopular," the root is a hidden self-judgment of being stupid and unpopular. Self-forgiveness goes to the roots of suffering rather than its branches.

### Self-Forgiveness as Spiritual Process
Self-forgiveness is not mental or emotional — it is a process of surrender and inviting in grace. It creates healing that goes to the core.

### Seven-Step Coaching Protocol

1. **Center**: Go within, connect with highest wisdom, set intentions, ask Spirit for assistance
2. **Surface the situation**: Coach invites client to share about a frustrating relationship or situation — finding "I'm upset because..."
3. **Name the feelings**: What are you feeling? What emotions are coming up?
4. **Locate in body**: Where do you feel these emotions? Place a hand there. Connect with acceptance and understanding.
5. **Reach beneath upset to fear**: Support the client in connecting to the fear underneath the surface emotion
6. **Surface judgments**: Explore judgments placed on the situation, the other person, and themselves ("I'm upset because...", "They should...", "I should...")
7. **Forgiveness from love**: Connect to a memory of feeling loving and connected. Locate those feelings in the body. From that embodied state, practice: "I forgive myself for judging myself as ___"

## Somnistic Relevance

### Somatic-First Forgiveness Protocol

Steps 4 and 7 are explicitly interoceptive: locate the emotion in the body, place a hand there, connect from a place of acceptance. This is structured interoception training embedded in a forgiveness practice. The hand-on-body gesture creates a haptic anchor point — the physical touch provides a tangible reference for the interoceptive signal, making abstract emotional content concrete and locatable.

### State-Dependent Processing

Step 7's instruction to access a loving/connected state BEFORE attempting forgiveness is autonomic sequencing — the protocol ensures the participant is in a ventral vagal state (safety, connection) before approaching stored judgments. This is identical to the SRL principle that autonomic safety is prerequisite to interoceptive access. You cannot process stored pain from a state of threat.

### Judgment as Autonomic Contraction

The framework's identification of judgment as the root cause of suffering (not the triggering event) maps to the SRL concept of constraint: judgment creates autonomic contraction, narrowing the bandwidth of awareness and perception. Self-forgiveness is constraint release — the letting go of the judgment literally expands the available autonomic bandwidth.

### Second Victim Phenomenon Application

This protocol has direct application to the CRNA second victim phenomenon. After an adverse event, clinicians often carry self-judgment ("I should have...") that creates ongoing autonomic contraction. Compassionate self-forgiveness provides a structured pathway for releasing those judgments — not by condoning the error, but by releasing the self-judgment that perpetuates suffering and, paradoxically, impairs future clinical performance.
