## Summary

Why some Asian economies succeeded (Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, China) and others stalled (Philippines, Thailand, Indonesia). Three-step formula: land reform, export-oriented manufacturing discipline, tightly controlled finance.

## Somnistic Relevance

Systems-level thinking about how institutions scale. The discipline required for national development (long-term investment, delayed gratification, tight feedback loops) parallels the discipline required for building an organization. Also part of the geopolitical literacy stack.
