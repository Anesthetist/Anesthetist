## Citation
Harnessing non-invasive vagal neuromodulation: HRV biofeedback and SSP for cardiovascular and autonomic regulation (Review). PMC12082064, 2026.

## Key Findings

- HRV biofeedback and the Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP) offer innovative non-invasive strategies for promoting health and resilience by bridging cardiovascular, neurological, and psychological domains
- Vagus nerve activity can be non-invasively indexed by HRV parameters, with specific HRV parameters predicting less all-cause mortality, lower risk of myocardial infarction, and better survival in cancer
- Slow-paced breathing while receiving visual HRV feedback (HRV-B) is an effective method for self-activating the vagus
- HRVB interventions are beneficial in reducing pain, depression scores, NSAID use, and in increasing HRV coherence ratio
- Future directions include integrating wearable HRV monitoring, AI-driven adaptive biofeedback, and expanding across diverse populations
