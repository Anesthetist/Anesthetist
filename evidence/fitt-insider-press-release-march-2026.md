## FITT Insider Press Release — March 25, 2026

**Headline:** Pausality Expands Stress Management App With AirPods Pro 3 Heart Support and Shareable Session Data

**Subhead:** 21 clinician-designed breathing sessions with real-time heart rate, SpO2, and respiratory rate on Apple Watch, putting physiological control back in your hands.

**Publication:** FITT Insider (fitness/wellness industry trade press)
**Date:** March 25, 2026
**URL:** https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/pausality-expands-stress-management-app-with-airpods-pro-3-heart-support-and-shareable-session-data/

### Key Product Claims

- Real-time HR, blood oxygen, and respiratory rate visible on wrist DURING breathing sessions (not after)
- 21 progressive one-minute breathing sessions designed from respiratory physiology
- AirPods Pro 3 heart rate integration (new in this update)
- Full offline support with encrypted local storage
- Shareable biometric results cards showing HR chart and session impact
- Pricing: $49.99/year or $12.99/month with free trial
- Requirements: iOS 18+, watchOS 11.1+

### FDA Context

FDA January 2026 General Wellness Policy update explicitly cleared HR, SpO2, and respiratory rate for wellness use without device clearance. Press release positions Pausality as purpose-built for this new category of clinician-designed consumer health tools.

### Key Phrases

- "Physiological sovereignty" — the ability to see, understand, and control your own stress response in the moment
- "Designed from respiratory physiology — not wellness trend culture"
- "21 progressive one-minute breathing sessions built around what actually moves the needle on the human stress response"

### Somnistic Relevance

This is SRL's first major trade press placement. FITT Insider reaches the fitness/wellness industry — adjacent to but not identical to the clinical audience. The press release effectively positions Pausality in the consumer wellness market while maintaining clinical credibility through Randy's CRNA background.
