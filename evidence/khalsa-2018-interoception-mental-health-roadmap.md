## Summary
Khalsa et al. (2018) present a comprehensive roadmap linking interoceptive dysfunction to psychiatric conditions including anxiety disorders, depression, eating disorders, somatic symptom disorders, and addiction. The paper synthesizes evidence from a multidisciplinary workshop, proposing that disrupted interoceptive processing is a transdiagnostic mechanism across mental health conditions. The authors outline a research agenda for developing interoception-based therapeutic interventions and standardized assessment methods.

## Key Finding for SRL
This roadmap validates interoception as a viable and scientifically grounded therapeutic target — the core premise of SRL's entire product line. The transdiagnostic framing supports SRL's strategy of building a single interoceptive training platform applicable across multiple clinical populations, rather than condition-specific interventions. It also provides the authoritative citation chain for investor and clinical audiences.
