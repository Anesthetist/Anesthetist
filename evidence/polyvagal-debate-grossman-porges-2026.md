## Summary

**The Critique (Grossman + 38 co-signatories):**
- Core PVT premises are "not defensible based on existing neurophysiological and evolutionary evidence"
- Three main objections: (1) HRV as a direct measure of vagal tone/safety is oversimplified; (2) No strong human evidence for dorsal vagal "freeze" pathway functioning as PVT claims; (3) Social engagement is not uniquely mammalian — non-mammal species show complex social behavior with similar nerve structures
- This is Grossman's fourth published critique (after 2007, 2016, 2023)

**Porges' Response:**
- PVT has meaningfully advanced understanding, clinical practice, and trauma-informed care over three decades
- The theory's contribution lies in linking brainstem autonomic organization to observable patterns of regulation, vulnerability, and resilience
- Empirically tractable and clinically meaningful framework regardless of phylogenetic precision

**Clinical Educator Perspective:**
- PVT approaches remain "scientifically questionable but useful in practice"
- The relevant question is whether the theory has advanced clinical care for trauma survivors
