## Claim

"Autoresearch" automating scientific discovery won't happen. AutoML made the same promises in 2017 and didn't deliver. PhD students should master fundamentals and learn research from first principles. Trends fade, solid foundations persist.

## Context

Reposted by Jeremy Howard, which signals agreement from a prominent ML practitioner. Hatamizadeh draws a direct parallel between current autoresearch hype and AutoML circa 2017.
