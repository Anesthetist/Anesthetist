## Citation

Neuroscientists find evidence meditation changes how fluid moves in the brain. *PsyPost*, reporting on peer-reviewed MRI study, 2026.

## Key Findings

- MRI study found that when meditators switched from mind wandering to focused attention, absolute flow of cerebrospinal fluid decreased
- The decrease was driven by a reduction in backward, regurgitant flow
- Fluid movement became more efficient and coherent during focused attention states
- Suggests meditation produces measurable changes in brain fluid dynamics, not just neural activity

## Relevance to SRL

A novel biomarker pathway for meditation effects. If meditation produces more efficient CSF flow, this may relate to glymphatic clearance and brain health — relevant to Clinician Durability and long-term cognitive performance. The finding that focused attention creates more coherent fluid dynamics parallels SRL's Coherence Field concept and the NeuroHarmonics principle that physiological coherence spans multiple systems.

## Methodology

MRI-based fluid flow measurement during meditation; comparison of mind-wandering vs. focused-attention states within meditators.
