# Mind-Body Effects of Mindfulness-Based Training in Athletes (Campo et al. 2026)

## Summary

Published in *Frontiers in Psychology* (March 2026), this preliminary RCT examined the effects of an 8-week mindfulness-based training program on athletes. The intervention significantly improved both interoceptive awareness (subjective) and SDNN — a time-domain HRV metric (objective) — simultaneously in the intervention group compared to controls. This dual improvement demonstrates that subjective body-awareness gains are accompanied by measurable autonomic nervous system changes.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Mindfulness training simultaneously improves interoceptive awareness AND HRV (SDNN)
- Co-improvement of subjective and objective metrics validates the interoception-autonomic link
- 8-week protocol is sufficient for measurable dual-channel effects
- Athletic populations show responsiveness to mind-body training
- Supports using HRV as an objective biomarker for interoceptive training progress

## SRL Relevance

Directly validates the SRL approach of pairing subjective interoceptive measures (MAIA-2) with objective autonomic metrics (HRV). Demonstrates that these are not independent domains — they co-improve. Supports the dual-measurement strategy in Somnistics protocols. PMID: 41822416.
