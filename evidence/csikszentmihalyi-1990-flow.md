# Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience

## Key Findings

Csikszentmihalyi's foundational work establishing the concept of "flow" — a state of complete absorption in an activity where challenge and skill are balanced, time perception shifts, and self-consciousness dissolves. Flow is characterized by:

1. Clear goals and immediate feedback
2. Balance between challenge and skill
3. Merging of action and awareness
4. Loss of self-consciousness
5. Transformation of time perception
6. Autotelic experience (intrinsically rewarding)

## Relevance to SRL

Flow state is a key target state within the Somnistics framework. NeuroMinute protocols aim to facilitate transitions toward flow-conducive autonomic states. The Gap Moment Detection Engine monitors for conditions that support or obstruct flow entry.

The concept of "challenge-skill balance" maps directly to Somnistics' titration-to-effect principle: interventions must be calibrated to the practitioner's current capacity, not standardized to a population average.
