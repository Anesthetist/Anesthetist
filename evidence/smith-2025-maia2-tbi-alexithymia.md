# MAIA-2 Deficits in TBI and Alexithymia (Smith 2025)

## Summary

Individuals with traumatic brain injury scored lower on MAIA-2 subscales (Not Distracting, Self-Regulation, Body Listening) compared to controls. Lower Attention Regulation was more strongly associated with alexithymia in the TBI group. Suggests mindfulness-based and somatic-focused therapies targeting interoceptive sensibility may improve psychological well-being after brain injury.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- MAIA-2 detects clinically meaningful interoceptive deficits
- Attention Regulation subscale specifically linked to alexithymia (inability to identify emotions)
- Validates MAIA-2 as both assessment and outcome measure for interoceptive training
- Extends application beyond CRNAs to TBI rehabilitation populations
