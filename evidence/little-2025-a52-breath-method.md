# A52 Breath Method: Breathwork for Mental Health (Little 2025)

## Summary

Narrative review proposing the A52 Breath Method (5s inhale, 5s exhale, 2s hold) as a structured slow diaphragmatic nasal breathing framework. Reviews evidence that slow breathing at 6 breaths/min or fewer significantly improves vagal tone, HRV, and parasympathetic activity while reducing cortisol, anxiety, and PTSD symptoms.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- 6 breaths/min confirmed as threshold for autonomic benefit (matches [[resonant-breathing-frequency]])
- Nasal + diaphragmatic = required elements (not optional preferences)
- Cortisol reduction adds a biomarker dimension beyond HRV
- PTSD symptom reduction supports [[ptsd-prehabilitation-gap]]
- A52 pattern (5-5-2) is similar to SRL's resonant breathing protocols
