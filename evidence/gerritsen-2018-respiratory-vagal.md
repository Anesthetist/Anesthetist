# Respiratory Vagal Stimulation Model (Gerritsen & Band 2018)

## Summary

This paper proposes that the primary mechanism through which contemplative practices (meditation, yoga, tai chi) produce their beneficial effects is respiratory vagal nerve stimulation — not attention regulation or cognitive mechanisms alone. Slow, controlled breathing directly stimulates vagal afferents, producing downstream effects on autonomic regulation, emotional processing, and cognitive function.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Respiratory vagal stimulation is the primary mechanism (not attention or cognition)
- Explains why breath-first approaches (Pausality) outperform meditation-first approaches
- Validates the [[awareness-before-technique]] principle — breath is the mechanism, attention is secondary
- Supports the convergence thesis: [[eight-therapeutic-convergence]] all work through the same vagal pathway
