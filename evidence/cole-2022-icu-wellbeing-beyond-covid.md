# ICU Well-being Beyond COVID (Cole et al. 2022)

## Summary

Review of burnout among critical care personnel examining risk factors: compassion fatigue, lack of environmental control, difficult interpersonal dynamics, and repeated end-of-life exposure. Recommends organizational commitment to efficiency, stressor recognition, community building, and team communication strategies. Published in Anesthesiology Clinics — directly relevant to CRNA target market.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Burnout risk factors are structural/environmental, not just individual resilience deficits
- Compassion fatigue + interpersonal conflict = top drivers (maps to [[error-archetypes-qrc]] archetypes 3 and 6)
- "Community building and team communication" = [[co-regulation]] in clinical language
- Published in Anesthesiology Clinics = high credibility for CRNA audience
