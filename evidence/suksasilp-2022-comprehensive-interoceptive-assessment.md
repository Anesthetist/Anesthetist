## Overview

Suksasilp and Garfinkel (2022) argue that interoception must be assessed comprehensively across all dimensions (accuracy, sensibility, awareness) and across multiple bodily axes (cardiac, respiratory, gastric). Current research largely assesses interoceptive dimensions in isolation, failing to capture the full complexity of interoceptive function. This paper provides a framework for multi-dimensional, multi-modal interoceptive assessment.

## Key Findings

1. Comprehensive assessment requires all three Garfinkel dimensions: interoceptive accuracy, sensibility, and awareness
2. Assessment must span multiple bodily axes (cardiac, respiratory, gastric) — not just cardiac
3. Different interoceptive dimensions map to different cognitive, emotional, and clinical conditions
4. No single measure captures interoception fully; multi-method assessment is the gold standard
5. Interoceptive processes are dissociable across dimensions and bodily axes, with differential clinical mappings

## Connection to Somnistics

This is the assessment framework SRL should adopt for clinical research. It maps directly to [[interoceptive-literacy]] as a multi-dimensional, multi-modal construct. SRL's protocols already span respiratory and cardiac axes through resonant breathing and HRV biofeedback; this paper validates extending assessment beyond heartbeat detection tasks to capture the full interoceptive profile that training aims to reshape.
