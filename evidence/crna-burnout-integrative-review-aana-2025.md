## Citation
Contributing Factors and Associated Outcomes of Burnout Among Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists: An Integrative Review. AANA Journal, June 2025. PMID: 40440194.

## Key Findings

- CRNA burnout prevalence ranges from 12.5% to 72% across studies
- Top contributing factors: lack of autonomy (n=7 studies), lack of leadership support (n=4), moral distress (n=3), poor relations with physicians (n=3)
- Risk factors: younger age, longer work hours, likely female gender
- Emotional intelligence inversely correlates with burnout: 1-unit increase in EI score → 20% reduction in burnout
- Education on coping skills can reduce burnout; one study showed post-intervention emotional exhaustion dropped from extreme levels to only 1% scoring high
- AANA launched Wellness Ambassador micro-credential program in 2025 — CRNAs trained as on-site wellness champions
