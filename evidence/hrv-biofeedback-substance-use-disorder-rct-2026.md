## Citation

Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback for Substance Use Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial. *PubMed ID: 41032322*, 2026.

## Key Findings

- Second-generation HRVB delivered via wearable patch significantly reduced substance use in early recovery
- 64% reduction in alcohol and other drug use days compared to controls
- Reduced negative affect and craving — key triggers for relapse
- Wearable delivery model eliminates need for clinician-administered sessions, improving scalability

## Relevance to SRL

**HIGH IMPACT for SRL.** Validates the core SRL thesis that autonomic regulation training (via HRV biofeedback) can modulate self-regulation in high-stakes populations. The wearable delivery model parallels Pausality's vision for accessible, technology-mediated interoceptive training. The craving/affect reduction pathway directly maps to SRL's concepts of State Transition and Interoceptive Literacy — participants learned to notice and regulate internal states before they escalated.

## Methodology

Randomized clinical trial; individuals in early recovery from substance use disorder; second-generation wearable HRV biofeedback patch vs. control; outcomes measured via self-report and physiological monitoring.
