Varelas traces how the financialization of the American economy transformed money from a medium of exchange into a systemic risk vector. The narrative connects personal stories from Wall Street to structural changes in how capital markets distort incentives.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Understanding systemic financial risk is essential for non-ergodic positioning. Clinician entrepreneurs who build ventures without understanding how financial systems concentrate and distribute risk are operating with incomplete situational awareness -- the financial equivalent of not monitoring end-tidal CO2.
