## Summary

Roman emperor's private journal. Practices for maintaining equanimity, duty, and clear perception under the pressure of governing an empire.

## Somnistic Relevance

The original performance-under-pressure manual. Aurelius governed while managing his own mortality, grief, and the weight of decisions affecting millions. The stoic practice of returning attention to what's within your control is the cognitive parallel to what Pausality teaches at the autonomic level.
