## Summary

The Durants' 100-page distillation of their 11-volume Story of Civilization. Patterns that repeat across cultures and centuries: competition, concentration of wealth, the role of biology, morality, religion, economics, government, and war.

## Somnistic Relevance

The longest-wavelength pattern recognition. If Somnistics operates at the intersection of biology, culture, and institutional behavior, Durant provides the historical frame: these dynamics are old. The human nervous system hasn't changed. What changes is the technology we build around it.
