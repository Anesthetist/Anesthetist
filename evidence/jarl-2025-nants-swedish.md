## Summary

Translation and psychometric validation of a Swedish-language adaptation of NANTS, a structured observer-rated tool for assessing non-technical skills in nurse anesthetists. The parent instrument derives from Rhona Flin's ANTS (Anesthetists' Non-Technical Skills) framework. The Danish version (NANTS-dk, Lyk-Jensen et al.) was the proximal source.

Sixteen experienced nurse anesthetists rated simulated anesthesia scenarios on video using a five-point scale.

## NANTS Framework: Four Categories, 15 Elements

1. **Situational Awareness**
   - Gather information
   - Recognize and understand the situation
   - Anticipate and think ahead
2. **Decision Making**
   - Identify courses of action
   - Assess and balance different courses of action
   - Re-evaluate decisions
3. **Work Task Management**
   - Plan
   - Prioritize
   - Use resources
   - Maintain policies and procedures
4. **Teamwork**
   - Exchange information
   - Value the team's competence
   - Coordinate activities
   - Show authority, when required
   - Demonstrate team-oriented behavior and support team members

## Psychometric Findings

- Internal consistency: Cronbach α 0.78 to 0.86 (acceptable to good across categories)
- Interrater reliability: ICC 0.79 to 0.86 (good)
- Test-retest reliability: ICC 0.41 to 0.68 (poor to moderate)
- Raters reported the tool was initially demanding but appropriate for nurse anesthetist competency assessment
- "Not Relevant" ratings posed challenges, especially with shorter video clips and secondary providers

## Why This Matters for SRL

The closest published instrument to Somnistics' somatic-certification competency domains. NANTS names the behavioral surface (awareness, decision-making, task management, teamwork). It does not measure the autonomic substrate that produces or degrades those behaviors under load.

The weak test-retest reliability (ICC 0.41 to 0.68) is the diagnostic signal. Non-technical skills fluctuate with state. That state variability is where an HRV and interoceptive-literacy measurement layer adds orthogonal signal, capturing the physiological regulation that the observational tool cannot see.

Positioning frame for the certification: NANTS observes what the clinician does. Somnistics measures the regulation that shapes what the clinician can do. The two layers are complementary, not competitive.

## Candidate Outcome Measure for Pausality

NANTS is a plausible secondary outcome measure for Pausality clinical studies targeting anesthetist populations. Primary outcomes (HRV, interoceptive accuracy) capture the substrate; NANTS would capture whether substrate changes translate to observable behavioral competencies under simulation conditions.
