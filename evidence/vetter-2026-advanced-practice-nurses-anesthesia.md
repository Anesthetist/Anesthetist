## Summary

Analysis of gaps in perioperative care and the potential role of advanced practice nurses (APNs) in anesthesia to address workforce sustainability challenges.

## Key Findings

- Identified significant gaps in perioperative care delivery
- Integration of APNs in anesthesia could provide needed clinical leadership
- Aging population and specialist shortage exacerbate existing challenges
- Workforce sustainability requires structural role evolution, not just recruitment
- Positions advanced practice nursing as a solution to systemic healthcare delivery gaps

## Relevance to SRL
