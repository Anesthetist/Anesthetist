## Citation

Emotion-Linked Interoceptive Awareness Scale (ELIA) — Development and Validation. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2026. DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1757948

## Key Findings

- Developed and validated a new self-report measure (ELIA) assessing awareness of bodily sensations under specific emotional contexts
- Extends the attentional style framework by demonstrating that interoceptive awareness is modulated by emotional state
- Validated across four online studies, showing interoception and emotion are closely intertwined
- Emphasizes the foundational role of bodily signals in generating, regulating, and experiencing emotion

## Relevance to SRL

Directly supports SRL's Interoceptive Literacy concept by providing a new measurement tool that captures the emotion–body signal interaction. This aligns with Gap Moment Training's emphasis on noticing internal state shifts during emotional transitions — the ELIA could become a validated assessment instrument for SRL interventions.

## Methodology

Four online validation studies using self-report measures; psychometric analysis of the ELIA scale's factor structure, reliability, and convergent validity with existing interoceptive awareness measures.
