## Summary

Taxonomy of 87 human emotions and experiences. Brown's argument: naming what you feel with precision is the first step to regulating it. Emotional granularity as a skill.

## Somnistic Relevance

Emotional granularity is the cognitive complement to interoceptive literacy. Pausality teaches people to detect physiological states. Brown provides the vocabulary for the emotional layer riding on top of those states. Both are required for full self-regulation.
