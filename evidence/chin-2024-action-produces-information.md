# Action Produces Information

## Core Principle

Brian Armstrong (Coinbase CEO): **"Action produces information."** Classical decision-making frameworks assume perfect information and fixed choices. Real-world decisions require generating new information through experimentation.

## Three Heuristics

### 1. Scott Berkun's Coin Flip
"Often you're better off flipping a coin and moving in any clear direction" when options seem equally attractive. Action reveals data that analysis cannot.

### 2. Gary Klein's Zone of Indifference
When options are comparably attractive, accept that perfect decisions are impossible. Act decisively on a good-enough choice rather than remain paralyzed by analysis.

### 3. Bezos's Decision Types
- **Type 1** (irreversible): Analyze thoroughly
- **Type 2** (reversible): Decide quickly and adapt

Most business decisions are reversible, favoring rapid action with course correction.

## SRL Relevance

- The vault has 1,200+ notes of understanding. The action gap is: how many CRNAs have actually used Pausality this week?
- Shipping the CE course (even imperfect) produces more information than another month of enrichment

- Every enterprise proposal sent produces information about objections, pricing sensitivity, and champion behavior

**The OODA connection:** Action is the "Act" phase that feeds the next "Observe" cycle. Without action, the loop stalls at Orient.
