## Top 3 Stories That Matter for SRL

**1. FDA's 2026 Wellness Device Guidance Reshapes the Wearable Landscape**
The January 2026 FDA guidance significantly broadened what qualifies as a "general wellness product" — noninvasive wearables measuring activity, recovery, sleep, pulse, and fitness-related biomarkers now have a clearer path outside medical device regulation, provided claims avoid disease references. The critical distinction: "signals/patterns" vs. "medical information." A device that detects patterns warranting closer examination (like HRV anomalies suggesting autonomic dysregulation) can operate under wellness framing. **SRL implication:** Pausality's HRV biofeedback and somatic training tools sit squarely in this newly expanded wellness lane. The regulatory moat just got wider for clinician-facing wellness platforms that don't make diagnostic claims but do train interoceptive awareness.

**2. White House AI Framework Proposes Federal Preemption of State AI Laws**
On March 20, the White House released its National Policy Framework for AI, recommending Congress preempt state AI laws imposing "undue burdens." This directly affects the patchwork of state healthcare AI regulations (Texas TRAIGA, California's anti-impersonation rules, Illinois chatbot laws). **SRL implication:** If federal preemption passes, it simplifies Pausality's multi-state go-to-market. But watch the gap — HHS is simultaneously collecting feedback on clinical AI governance. The window between state law freeze and federal rule crystallization is a land-grab opportunity for platforms that can demonstrate responsible AI use in clinical wellbeing.

**3. Neurowellness Goes Mainstream — Nervous System Regulation Is the New Biohack**
"Neurowellness" dominated early 2026 wellness discourse. Consumer neurotech funding is active (Mave Health $2.1M, Audicin $1.9M for real-time nervous system regulation). The Global Wellness Summit named "The Rise of Neurowellness" a top 2026 trend. The narrative is shifting from passive tracking to active nervous system regulation — exactly Pausality's thesis. **SRL implication:** The market is being educated for you. But the consumer neurotech crowd is building expensive hardware. Pausality's edge is software-first somatic training that works with commodity wearables. Position against hardware lock-in.

## EU vs US Sentiment Delta

The transatlantic gap is widening into a strategic opportunity. Europe is in compliance panic mode — a 2026 readiness report found 78% of enterprises unprepared for AI Act obligations hitting in August 2026, with healthcare AI classified as high-risk requiring conformity assessments. Meanwhile, the US is actively deregulating: the FDA loosened wellness device rules, and the White House wants to preempt state-level AI constraints. The sentiment inversion is clear — EU discourse centers on "how do we comply" while US discourse centers on "how fast can we ship." For SRL, this means the US market is where you move fast, but EU compliance rigor (once navigated) becomes a credibility badge. A CE-marked, AI Act-compliant Pausality platform would be a moat in European healthcare systems already desperate for clinician burnout solutions.

## Chaos Signal

**The somatic therapy + AI convergence nobody is pricing in.** A 2026 Triad Summit is exploring the intersection of somatic therapy and AI tools for healthcare transformation. Therapy itself is shifting from cognitive-only to bottom-up, body-based modalities integrating somatic awareness and nervous system regulation. This is moving from fringe to clinical mainstream — but the tech stack doesn't exist yet. Every AI health company is building for diagnostics or administrative automation. Almost nobody is building AI for embodied awareness training. The 90-day risk: a well-funded consumer neurotech startup pivots from hardware to a software-first somatic training platform and occupies the positioning Pausality is building toward. The 90-day opportunity: if Pausality ships a working prototype before this convergence crystallizes, it becomes the reference architecture.
