## Citation

PLOS Biology (2025). Interoception as a central mechanism in Whole Person Health. *PLOS Biology*.

## Key Findings

- Frames interoception as the fundamental biological interface central to whole-person health
- Introduces the "4M Framework": Measure, Map, Monitor, and Modulate
- Measure: develop tools to quantify interoceptive signals
- Map: identify neural correlates of interoceptive processing
- Monitor: track interoceptive states in real time
- Modulate: design targeted interventions to shift interoceptive function
- Positions interoception at the intersection of mind-body medicine

## Relevance to SRL

The 4M Framework maps remarkably well onto SRL's approach — particularly the progression from measurement (CVA, HRV metrics) through mapping (NeuroHarmonics) to modulation (Gap Moment Training, resonant breathing). This framework could serve as external validation for SRL's methodology.
