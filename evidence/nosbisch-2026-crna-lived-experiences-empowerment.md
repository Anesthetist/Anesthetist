## Summary

Heuristic inquiry into the lived experiences of Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists (CRNAs) regarding anesthesia provision, seeking to identify the essential meaning of their practice.

## Key Findings

- "Empowerment" identified as the meaningful essence of CRNA anesthesia provision
- Four key themes emerged: practicing as clinician, developing comprehensive understanding, developing situational awareness, trusting own competence
- Empowerment vs disenfranchisement directly affects professional durability and career longevity
- The ability to trust one's own clinical judgment is foundational to sustained practice

## Relevance to SRL

Maps directly to clinician durability and interoceptive literacy. The theme of "trusting own competence" parallels the somnistics framework of developing internal sensing capacity as a clinical skill. Empowerment as the core essence suggests that interventions must enhance (not replace) clinician agency. The disenfranchisement finding supports the case that systemic factors erode durability even when individual coping skills are strong.
