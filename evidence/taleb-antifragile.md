## Summary

Systems that benefit from volatility, randomness, and stress. Taleb's central distinction: fragile (harmed by disorder), robust (indifferent), antifragile (strengthened by it). Optionality as the key to thriving in uncertain environments.

## Somnistic Relevance

The theoretical backbone of the hormesis-durability model. Pausality's training protocols are designed to produce antifragility in the clinician's nervous system: controlled stress exposure that builds capacity rather than depleting it. Also connects to non-ergodic-risk: Taleb's framework for understanding which risks end the game vs. which make you stronger.
