## Summary
Garfinkel et al. (2015) established a foundational three-dimensional model of interoception, distinguishing interoceptive accuracy (objective performance on heartbeat detection tasks), interoceptive sensibility (self-reported beliefs about body awareness), and interoceptive awareness (metacognitive correspondence between accuracy and sensibility). Across multiple experiments, the authors demonstrated that these three dimensions are empirically distinct and dissociable, challenging the prior assumption that interoception is a unitary construct.

## Key Finding for SRL
This trichotomy (accuracy/sensibility/awareness) is the conceptual backbone of SRL's interoceptive literacy framework. It validates the need for multi-modal assessment — not just asking people how aware they are, but measuring actual performance and the gap between belief and ability. This directly informs how Somnistics designs training protocols and selects outcome measures like MAIA-2 and heartbeat evoked potentials.
