## Summary

Nine-stage hierarchical model of consciousness development drawing on Tibetan Buddhist teachings and transpersonal psychotherapy. Stages progress from Step 0 (Innocence — low self-awareness, group belonging) through Steps 1–3 (ego-driven: dominance, obedience, ambition) to Steps 4–5 (expansion beyond ego: sharing, responsibility) to Steps 6–8 (spiritual development: love, bliss, enlightenment). Each stage is characterized across multiple dimensions: sense of self, worldview, values, behaviors, relationships, work, and money.

Springett also presents a four-phase "ice-to-steam" model of Kundalini awakening as a transformative continuum: Ice (dense/frozen psychic material, trauma, ego defense) → Melted Ice (breakdown of old structures, emotional flooding) → Water (smoother flow, integration, creative emergence) → Steam (sublimation, subtle states, compassion, transpersonal clarity). The model emphasizes that kundalini is not foreign energy entering you but your own psychic system changing state into more fluid forms.

## SRL Relevance

The consciousness gradation model provides a developmental scaffolding for understanding where Somnistics users may be in their awareness journey. The ice-to-steam Kundalini framework maps onto kosha architecture layers (annamaya/frozen → pranamaya/flooding → manomaya/flow → vijnanamaya/integration → anandamaya/field-consciousness) and provides a Western-accessible bridge metaphor for state transitions at the macro-developmental level.
