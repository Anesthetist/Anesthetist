## Summary

Cabrera, Kolacz et al. (2018) developed the Body Perception Questionnaire Short Form (BPQ-SF), a short form of Porges' body perception questionnaire grounded in polyvagal theory. Published in *International Journal of Methods in Psychiatric Research* 27(2):e1596.

## Key Findings

1. 46 items with Body Awareness + Autonomic Reactivity subscales
2. Autonomic Reactivity splits into Supradiaphragmatic (ventral vagal organs) and Subdiaphragmatic (dorsal vagal/GI organs)
3. Grounded in polyvagal theory anatomy
4. Chinese, Persian, Italian validations available
5. Free at traumascience.org

## SRL Relevance

Complements MAIA-2 by measuring autonomic reactivity specifically. The supra/subdiaphragmatic split maps to SRL's interest in both respiratory (above diaphragm) and gastric (below) interoception.
