# Deep Breathing vs. VNS for Caregiver Resilience (Ozen 2026)

## Summary

RCT comparing deep breathing exercises vs. transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS) in mothers of children with cerebral palsy. Both interventions significantly improved sleep quality, depressive symptoms, and caregiver burden. VNS showed stronger antidepressant effects, but deep breathing is simpler, lower-cost, and more scalable.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Deep breathing matches VNS on most outcomes — validates breath-first approach over device-dependent VNS
- Confirms breathing as accessible, scalable intervention for caregiver populations
- Supports [[minimum-effective-dose]] thesis: simple breathing rivals expensive technology
