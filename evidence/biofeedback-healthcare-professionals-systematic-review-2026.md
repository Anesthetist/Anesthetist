## Summary
Systematic review of biofeedback interventions (including HRV-BF) specifically targeting healthcare professionals' wellness. Found consistent improvements in perceived stress, anxiety, emotional regulation, and resilience, with physiological benefits including increased HRV and improved autonomic balance.

## Relevance to SRL
Directly supports Pausality thesis that HRV biofeedback can serve as a scalable clinician resilience tool. Key evidence for enterprise/hospital system pitch.

## Key Constructs
- HRV biofeedback → clinician stress reduction
- Autonomic balance restoration in high-demand clinical settings
- Resilience building through biofeedback
