## Citation

(2026). Effects of heart rate variability biofeedback on cardiac autonomic function in patients with cardiovascular disease: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10484-025-09765-3

## Key Findings

- HRV biofeedback significantly improved SDNN (standard deviation of NN intervals) in CVD patients across 13 RCTs (965 participants).
- Both systolic (MD = −3.08 mmHg) and diastolic (MD = −3.23 mmHg) blood pressure decreased with HRV biofeedback intervention.
- Effects on parasympathetic activity and psychological outcomes remain inconclusive — larger-scale trials are needed.
- Searched 2,402 records from inception to April 2025; 13 RCTs met inclusion criteria.

## Relevance to SRL

Directly supports SRL's Resonant Breathing Frequency and Vagal Tone concepts, confirming that protocol-based HRV biofeedback produces measurable cardiovascular improvements consistent with enhanced autonomic regulation. The blood pressure findings are particularly relevant to clinician health optimization (Clinician Durability) and support the Minimum Effective Dose principle — even brief biofeedback protocols produce clinically meaningful effects.

## Methodology

- Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs.
- 13 RCTs, 965 total participants with cardiovascular disease.
- Outcomes: HRV indices, blood pressure, psychological measures.
- Database search through April 2025.
