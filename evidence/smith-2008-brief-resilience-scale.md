## Overview

The Brief Resilience Scale (BRS) is a 6-item self-report instrument designed to measure the ability to bounce back or recover from stress. Unlike other resilience scales that conflate resilience with protective factors or resources, the BRS directly assesses the core construct of recovery from adversity. It is free to use and takes less than 2 minutes to administer.

## Key Findings

1. 6 items loading on a single factor, providing a clean unidimensional measure of resilience as stress recovery
2. Specifically measures the ability to bounce back from stress, rather than coping resources or personality traits
3. Discriminates from related but distinct constructs including coping style, social support, and personal characteristics
4. Free to use with no licensing requirements, making it accessible for research and clinical deployment
5. Takes less than 2 minutes to administer, minimizing respondent burden

## Connection to Somnistics

The BRS is an ideal brief resilience measure for SRL's enterprise pilots — it is free, contains only 6 items, and is well-validated. Pre/post BRS measurement in CRNA cohorts undergoing [[gap-moment-training|Gap Moment Training™]] would provide a clean, defensible outcome metric for [[clinician-durability]] interventions.
