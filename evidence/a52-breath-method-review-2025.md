## Background

This narrative review examines the primary psychophysiological pathways and mechanisms of breathwork, focusing on the "A52 Breath Method" while surveying the broader breathwork evidence base for mental health and stress resilience.

## Key Findings

- Examines widely practiced breathing techniques and their mechanisms
- Highlights breathwork's influence on the nervous system as central to its effectiveness
- Reviews psychophysiological pathways that mediate breathwork benefits
