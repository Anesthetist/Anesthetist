Davies and Read explore the psychological and cultural dimensions of money -- how it shapes identity, relationships, and decision-making in ways that economic models fail to capture. The book treats money as a social technology with emergent properties beyond its nominal function.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Money as social technology parallels how language functions in clinical settings -- a medium that shapes the reality it describes. Understanding money's psychological architecture helps clinician entrepreneurs navigate the identity transition from salaried professional to capital allocator without losing clinical grounding.
