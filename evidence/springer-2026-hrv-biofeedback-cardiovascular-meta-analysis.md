## Citation

Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback (2026). Effects of Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback on Cardiac Autonomic Function in Patients with Cardiovascular Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback*.

## Key Findings

- Meta-analysis of 13 RCTs involving 965 participants with cardiovascular disease
- HRV biofeedback significantly improved SDNN (standard deviation of NN intervals)
- Increased low-frequency HRV measures (marker of baroreflex activity)
- Both systolic and diastolic blood pressure decreased significantly
- Demonstrates clinical-grade autonomic improvements in a disease population

## Relevance to SRL

Adds to the growing evidence base that HRV biofeedback produces measurable, clinically meaningful shifts in autonomic regulation. The cardiovascular population data strengthens the case for HRV-based interventions across health contexts, including the perioperative settings relevant to SRL's clinician durability work.

## Methodology

Systematic review and meta-analysis. 13 RCTs, 965 participants. Published 2026 in Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback.
