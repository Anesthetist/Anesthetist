## Summary

Gall's laws of systems behavior. "A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that worked." Systems tend to oppose their own proper function. Humor as a vehicle for deep systems truth.

## Somnistic Relevance

The design philosophy for everything SRL builds. Start simple (60-second protocol), prove it works, then add complexity. Don't build the vault architecture and expect it to produce insight. Build one concept note, link it, see what emerges. Gall's laws are the immune system against overengineering.
