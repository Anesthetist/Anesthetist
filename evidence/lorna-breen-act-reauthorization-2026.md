## Summary

The Dr. Lorna Breen Health Care Provider Protection Act was reauthorized in the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2026 (H.R. 7148), extending through September 2030. Signed by President Trump.

## Key Provisions
- Federal grants to health care service providers and medical professional associations for mental health programs
- Supports peer-support programs and mental health care for healthcare professionals
- Prioritizes applicants focusing on reducing administrative burden
- The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) publicly applauded the reauthorization (February 2026)
