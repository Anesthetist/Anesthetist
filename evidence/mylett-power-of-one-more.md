## Summary

The "one more" principle applied across domains: one more rep, one more call, one more attempt. Mylett's argument that the margin between average and exceptional is usually one more effort than you think you have.

## Somnistic Relevance

The performance mindset layer. "One more" is a cognitive tool for pushing past comfort zones. Pausality provides the autonomic support that makes "one more" sustainable rather than depleting.
