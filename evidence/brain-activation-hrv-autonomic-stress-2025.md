## Summary
Demonstrated that breathing-induced relaxation and stress induction significantly affected HR variation and triggered brain activation in central autonomic network (CAN) regions. Brain activation within the CAN predicted changes in HRV, offering novel insights into dynamic brain-heart interactions during stress-related autonomic regulation.

## Relevance to SRL
Translational neuroscience evidence for brain-heart bidirectional signaling. Supports mechanistic claims about breath-based interventions modulating the CAN. Strong citation for scientific credibility deck.
