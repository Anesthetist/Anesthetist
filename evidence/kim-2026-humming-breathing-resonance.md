# Humming Breathing and Resonance Frequency (Kim 2026)

## Summary

Humming breathing (Bhramari Pranayama) naturally drives respiratory frequency to cluster near 0.1 Hz — the resonant frequency — and increases HRV metrics (RMSSD, SDNN, total power, LF power) compared to rest. No significant difference between humming with and without auditory occlusion, indicating benefits arise from the 0.1 Hz resonance frequency mechanism rather than the vibratory component.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Humming naturally achieves ~0.1 Hz resonant frequency without biofeedback device guidance
- RMSSD increase validates vagal tone improvement in a single session
- Device-free alternative for populations without Apple Watch
- Supports [[resonant-breathing-frequency]] as the core mechanism
