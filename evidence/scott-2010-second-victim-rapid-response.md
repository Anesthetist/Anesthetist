## Summary
Scott et al. (2010) defined the "second victim" phenomenon — healthcare providers who are emotionally traumatized by being involved in a patient safety incident — and described the deployment of a systemwide rapid response team at the University of Missouri Health System. The paper outlines a three-tiered support model providing immediate peer support, trained peer mentors, and professional referral pathways. This became the foundational framework for institutional second victim support programs internationally.

## Key Finding for SRL
This seminal paper establishes the clinical need that SRL's clinician durability framework addresses. The second victim phenomenon represents a systemic gap in healthcare workforce sustainability — clinicians lack tools for autonomic recovery after adverse events. SRL's Gap Moment Training and resonant breathing protocols are designed to fill exactly this gap, providing self-administered nervous system regulation at the point of need.
