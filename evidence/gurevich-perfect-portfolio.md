Gurevich presents a macro-driven approach to portfolio construction that prioritizes risk allocation over asset selection. The framework treats portfolios as dynamic systems that must adapt to regime changes in markets and geopolitics.

**Somnistic Relevance**: Portfolio construction as dynamic system management mirrors how a CRNA constructs and adapts an anesthetic plan -- the "portfolio" of drugs, monitoring modalities, and interventions must be rebalanced in real time as the patient's state evolves. Both require reading regime changes before they become crises.
