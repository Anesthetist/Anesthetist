## Summary

Twist's thesis: our relationship to money reflects our deepest beliefs about scarcity and sufficiency. Three toxic money myths: there's not enough, more is better, that's just the way it is. The alternative: sufficiency as a practice.

## Somnistic Relevance

The emotional substrate beneath Randy's $10M goal. Twist reframes wealth not as accumulation but as aligned flow. This connects to the time-wealth concept and the "one system" framing: financial goals serve the mission, not the other way around.
