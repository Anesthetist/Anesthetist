## Citation

Authors (2025). Heart Rate Variability as a Biomarker of Burnout in Healthcare Workers: A Predictive Model Integrating Psychosocial and Occupational Factors. *medRxiv* (preprint).

- **DOI:** 10.1101/2025.09.06.25335221
- **URL:** https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.06.25335221v1

## Background

This preprint explores HRV as an objective physiological marker for early detection of burnout risk in healthcare workers, integrating psychosocial and occupational factors into a predictive model.

## Key Findings

- HRV shows promise as an objective biomarker for burnout risk stratification
- Predictive models that integrate HRV with psychosocial and occupational variables perform better than either domain alone
- Supports the concept of multi-modal assessment approaches

## SRL Relevance

**Directly supports SRL's Demand Biomarkers concept and the Somnistics Readiness Battery (SRB-60).** This paper provides evidence that:

- HRV-based burnout detection is a viable approach (validates Pausality's measurement strategy)
- Multi-modal assessment (physiological + psychosocial) outperforms single-domain approaches (supports SRL's integrated battery approach)
- Early detection before clinical burnout manifests is possible (supports prehabilitative mental hygiene positioning)
