## Summary

Biddulph's model of the mind as a four-story mansion: body (ground floor), emotions (second), thinking (third), and spiritual awareness (fourth). Most people live on the third floor and never visit the others.

## Somnistic Relevance

Another layered model of consciousness that maps to the kosha architecture. Biddulph's "ground floor" (body) is Pausality's entry point. The claim that most people are stuck on the thinking floor explains why breath-based (body-floor) interventions produce outsized results: they activate a floor most people have abandoned.
