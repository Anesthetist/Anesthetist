# Wearable Devices for Anxiety Assessment: Systematic Review (Elgendi 2026)

## Summary

Systematic review of 26 studies (2014-2024) evaluating wearable biosignal approaches for anxiety detection. Multi-signal approaches achieved 81.94% pooled accuracy vs 76.85% for single-signal methods. ECG was the most reliable individual signal (80.34%). PPG and EDA also showed promise as complementary channels.

## Key Claims Relevant to SRL

- Multi-modal sensor fusion outperforms single-signal by ~5 percentage points (validates [[sensor-fusion-optimal-stack]])
- ECG > PPG for accuracy — supports [[hrv-deprecation-measurement-integrity]] (PPG has limitations)
- 82% accuracy achievable with consumer wearables for affective state detection
- Published 2026 in Nature Communications Medicine — current and high-credibility

## Concepts Derived

- [[sensor-fusion-optimal-stack]] — multi-signal superior to single-signal (confirmed by systematic review)
