## Summary

Ariely's behavioral economics made accessible. How irrational forces shape decisions in business and daily life. Illustrated format for broader audience.

## Somnistic Relevance

The decision-making error landscape. Ariely maps the cognitive biases; Pausality addresses the physiological conditions under which those biases worsen (stress, fatigue, sympathetic dominance). Together they explain why clinicians make preventable errors and what can be done about it.
