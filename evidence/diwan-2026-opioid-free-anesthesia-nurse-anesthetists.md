## Summary

National survey of Swedish perioperative healthcare professionals examining knowledge, attitudes, and practice patterns regarding opioid and nonopioid strategies in perioperative care.

## Key Findings

- 39% of perioperative professionals reported uncertainty about evidence for opioid-free approaches
- Nurse anesthetists scored better than anesthesiologists on postoperative pain management knowledge
- Non-pharmacological pain management methods used by only 23% of respondents
- Significant knowledge gap identified that targeted education could address
- Supports the case for CEU programs bridging pharmacological and non-pharmacological approaches

## Relevance to SRL

Identifies a concrete CEU market opportunity: 77% of perioperative clinicians are not using non-pharmacological methods, largely due to knowledge gaps rather than opposition. Nurse anesthetists' superior postoperative knowledge suggests receptivity to evidence-based non-pharmacological training. Directly supports the somnistics certification pathway as filling a documented education gap in perioperative care.
