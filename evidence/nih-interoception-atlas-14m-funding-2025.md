## Summary
NIH-funded $14.2M project launched Oct 2025 (includes Nobel laureate Ardem Patapoutian) to create first detailed atlas of interoceptive pathways — how the brain monitors internal bodily states.

## Relevance to Pausality
Validates federal investment in interoception science — the foundational mechanism behind Gap Moment Training. As this atlas matures, it could provide mechanistic evidence for our approach. Watch for collaboration or citation opportunities.
