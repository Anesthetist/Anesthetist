# Law Student (BLIP Clinic)

## Profile

Law students in the BLIP (Berkeley Law IP) Clinic program are a pilot audience for Pausality in high-pressure academic environments. They face intense cognitive demands -- case reading, exam preparation, moot court, clinic casework -- with limited tools for managing the autonomic toll. The BLIP Clinic engagement includes a 7-day experience design, engagement summaries, and ventures-track integration. This audience validates that SRL's approach works beyond healthcare, demonstrating transferability to knowledge-worker stress. Law students are analytically sharp but not scientifically trained; they need accessible language with enough evidence to satisfy their critical thinking without requiring clinical literacy.

## Language Notes

Frame interventions around cognitive performance and focus, not clinical outcomes. "One minute between study blocks to reset your nervous system" is more compelling than autonomic regulation terminology. Use "exam performance," "sustained focus," and "pressure management" rather than clinical or wellness language. The 7-day progressive structure should feel achievable and low-commitment -- law students are time-scarce and skeptical of anything that adds to their load. Evidence framing: "used by anesthesia clinicians in life-or-death settings" establishes credibility without requiring the student to understand the clinical details.
