# Pediatric Surgeon

## Profile

Pediatric surgeons operate in sustained high-stakes states where cognitive load, emotional strain (working on children), and physiologic dysregulation drive burnout, errors, and long-term health costs.

## Key Characteristics

- **Operational tempo**: Long, complex cases with life-or-death stakes on vulnerable patients
- **Emotional burden**: Operating on children carries unique psychological weight
- **Team dynamics**: Work closely with CRNAs, OR nurses, surgical techs — team co-regulation matters
- **Culture**: Scientific, efficient, skeptical of "soft" interventions — needs credibility framing
- **Decision fatigue**: Sustained vigilance over multi-hour cases

## How They Encounter Pausality

- Through OR team members who are already using it (CRNAs as bridge)
- Via academic medical center pilot programs (e.g., MUSC)
- Through surgical department wellness initiatives
- CEU-eligible modules that count toward continuing education

## Messaging Frame

"Teaching Precision Under Pressure" — position as performance physiology, not wellness/meditation. Emphasize measurable outcomes: HRV optimization, vigilance maintenance, faster emotional baseline recovery.

## Key Differentiator from CRNA Audience

Surgeons have more institutional power to champion adoption but may be harder to initially engage. The entry point is often through their anesthesia partners who are already using it.
