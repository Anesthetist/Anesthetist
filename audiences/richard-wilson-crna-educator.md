## Article Summary: "Catching the Spark Before the Flame"

Key claims:
- Burnout in nurse anesthesia students is preventable if caught early
- Early signals: performance drops, withdrawal from leadership, loss of clinical confidence, emotional changes
- Early burnout = cognitive overload/fatigue, not inability — recoverable
- Educator response: supportive conversation, normalization, specific actionable adjustments, follow-up
- Addresses professional identity formation as a distinct at-risk construct

SRL connection: These early warning signals are precisely the autonomic dysregulation states that Pausality's interoceptive literacy curriculum and gap moment training protocols address. Richard's framework is reactive (identify → intervene); SRL's is preventive (train regulation capacity before burnout initiates).
