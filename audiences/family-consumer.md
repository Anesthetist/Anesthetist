# Family / Consumer (B2C)

## Profile

Families — especially extended families and multi-generational households — seeking shared wellness practices that are fun, brief, and build connection. Primary purchaser is likely a health-conscious parent or grandparent.

## Key Characteristics

- **Time-constrained**: Need practices that fit into existing routines (mealtime, bedtime, parties)
- **Multi-generational**: Must work for kids through elderly
- **Social**: Value shared experience over individual optimization
- **Novelty-seeking**: Attracted to beautiful design, tech integration (AR), "cool factor"
- **Aspirational**: "That's what fighter pilots do" social proof resonates

## How They Encounter Pausality

- NeuroMinute Family Deck (42-card product)
- Word-of-mouth from clinician users who bring it home
- Social media / Instagram-worthy visual breath exercises
- School wellness programs or therapy office waiting rooms

## Messaging Frame

"One Minute Together" — emphasis on connection, play, and shared nervous system regulation. Avoid clinical language entirely. Lead with beauty, simplicity, and fun.

## Revenue Model

- Physical card deck ($29-39)
- Digital companion subscription ($4.99/mo)
- Gift bundles, seasonal editions
- School/therapy licensing
