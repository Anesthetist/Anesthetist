# Yoga / Breathwork Practitioner

## Profile

Yoga and breathwork practitioners represent a community that already understands the transformative power of breath but often lacks the neuroscientific language to explain why their practices work. SRL's framework -- particularly the bridge between Vedantic kosha architecture and modern autonomic neuroscience -- gives these practitioners credibility tools for client-facing communication and insurance/institutional legitimacy. Randy's unique multi-domain expertise (clinical anesthesia + polyvagal theory + Vedantic philosophy) makes SRL a natural authority for this bridge-building work. This audience is an amplification channel: practitioners who understand the science become evangelists to their clients.

## Language Notes

Honor the tradition first, then layer in the science. Never frame it as "science proving yoga right" -- instead, show how neuroscience and contemplative traditions are describing the same phenomena from different vantage points. Use "resonant breathing frequency" rather than clinical jargon like RMSSD. The kosha framework is familiar territory; connect it to interoceptive layers and autonomic regulation in ways that deepen rather than replace their existing understanding. Avoid clinical coldness but do not shy from the evidence -- this audience wants both heart and rigor.
