# Academic / Clinical Researcher

## Profile

Academic and clinical researchers represent both a validation channel and a collaboration opportunity for SRL. The open research questions from the biofeedback tool (e.g., whether 60 seconds is sufficient for measurable EEG shifts via consumer-grade Muse 2, optimal recovery windows between protocol tests, correlation between subjective ratings and biometric scores) are genuine scientific inquiries that could produce publishable findings. Researchers need full provenance chains, reproducible protocols, and transparent methodology. SRL's PROV-O-based knowledge architecture is designed to meet this standard -- every claim traces to its source through the provenance chain.

## Language Notes

Full academic register. Cite specific studies by author and year (Lehrer 2000, Porges 2011, Zaccaro 2018, van der Kolk 2014). Distinguish clearly between established findings, novel synthesis, and speculative hypotheses. Frame SRL's novel concepts (anterocept, polyanchora, neuro-ouroboros) as proposed constructs requiring empirical validation, not settled science. Use standard methodological language: effect sizes, confidence intervals, inclusion/exclusion criteria. Acknowledge limitations proactively. IRB considerations should be front and center for any human-subjects research involving biometric data.

## Example Outputs

- White paper with full citation apparatus and methodology section
- Research collaboration proposal with defined hypotheses and study design
- Data sharing agreement template for biometric research data
- Literature review connecting SRL constructs to existing peer-reviewed work
