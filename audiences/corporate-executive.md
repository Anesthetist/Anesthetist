# Corporate Executive / Enterprise Buyer

## Profile

Corporate executives and enterprise buyers represent the B2B revenue path -- Red Arrow 3 (Enterprise Pilot to Enterprise Contract). These are decision-makers evaluating Pausality as a scalable team resilience tool, often within existing corporate wellness or EAP programs. They care about measurable ROI, deployment friction, and whether the intervention produces results they can report to their board. Mayo Clinic conversations are the current enterprise beachhead. This audience does not need deep clinical evidence but does need enough to trust the science is real and differentiated from generic meditation apps.

## Language Notes

Lead with outcomes and metrics: engagement rates, HR drop per session, conversion percentages, retention data. Frame somnistics as "applied nervous system training" rather than breathwork or meditation. Use "team resilience" and "cognitive performance" rather than "stress reduction." Emphasize differentiation from Calm, Headspace, and other wellness apps -- Pausality is biometric-verified, clinically grounded, and workflow-integrated. Speak to competitive advantage: organizations that train their people's nervous systems outperform those that merely offer relaxation content. Keep clinical detail available but behind a click, not in the pitch.

- Enterprise pilot proposal with KPIs and success criteria
- ROI calculator based on absenteeism, presenteeism, and turnover metrics
- Executive briefing deck (2-3 slides max for C-suite attention span)
- Integration guide for existing wellness platforms
