# Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist

## Profile

CRNAs are the primary market for Pausality and the audience Randy knows most deeply from 28 years of clinical anesthesia experience. They manage 30-40 autonomic state transitions per shift across induction, maintenance, emergence, and handoff -- each one a gap moment where a one-minute evidence-based intervention can measurably shift vagal tone and cognitive readiness. CRNAs are highly trained, evidence-demanding clinicians who will reject anything that smells like wellness fluff. They need clinical-grade language, peer-reviewed citations, and proof that an intervention fits inside OR workflow without disrupting patient care.

## Language Notes

Use clinical and pharmacological framing. "Titration to effect" resonates immediately -- CRNAs titrate drugs for a living. Frame breathing protocols as autonomic interventions, not relaxation techniques. Reference RMSSD, HRV coherence, and BOLT scores as measurable outcomes. Avoid words like "mindfulness," "wellness," or "self-care" -- use "clinician durability," "autonomic regulation," and "neural state training." AANA Category A CEU eligibility is a strong adoption driver; lead with it in any educational offering.
